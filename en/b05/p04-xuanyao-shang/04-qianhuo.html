Part IV · Essentials of the Mystery (Part I)

Song of Lead and Fire · Song of Completing the Way · Song of the Secret Essentials of the Way

Song of Lead and Fire, Song of Completing the Way, Song of the Secret Essentials of the Way — fire phasing of one Celestial Circuit; Spirit not straying and Essence not leaking; gazing at the Mysterious Pass and regulating True Breath

About This Chapter

  • Song of Lead and Fire: Sets out the fire phasing of one "Celestial Circuit" in concrete detail: at the close of Hai and start of Zi, the Medicine arises and one yang returns; one closes the Great Mysterious Pass and leads the Medicine past the Caudal Funnel; rising, the eyes guard the Muddy Pellet until "six times six" is finished and yang reaches its peak and gives birth to yin; then one opens the gate to withdraw the fire, gazing downward to guard the Kun Field (abdomen), completing "three times eight" for one Celestial Circuit. At the close it declares: this is "natural true fire phasing, rising and descending of itself, adding and drawing of itself," sweeping away long treatises on lunar phases and ablution.
  • Song of Completing the Way: "When Spirit does not stray outward and Essence does not leak, when Breath is not dissipated, the numinous sprout takes root"—the opening couplet states the entire foundation. It then describes the return of one yang, the Waterwheel turning in reverse, ten months of warm nurturing, and three years of maturing.
  • Song of the Secret Essentials of the Way: Zhang Sanfeng condenses the "essentials of the Way" into two things: gazing at the Mysterious Pass, and regulating True Breath. The Mysterious Pass is nowhere else—"some say it has a fixed place at the center; some say it has no fixed place and must be grasped on one's own"—in truth it lies "between having a fixed place and having none." What is hardest at the start is "leaving distraction only to fall into torpor"; the remedy is "one breath goes out, one breath comes in, breath resting upon breath, constantly nestled together." This is the most approachable piece in this scroll for beginners.

In the original text, Song of Sitting in Meditation ("When first sitting in meditation, learning to practice Chan") appears at this point; because it is already included in Book 2 with commentary by Qiu Ling, please read it there: Song of Sitting in Meditation.

Safety Note: Song of Lead and Fire describes coordinating eye gaze with intention to guide the ascent and descent of qi. That is a specialized historical technique—do not attempt to "circulate qi" on your own by following it. For beginners, practicing only the part in Song of the Secret Essentials of the Way about "regulating True Breath, breath resting upon breath, neither distracted nor torpid" is more than enough. If you experience head pressure, headache, palpitations, or insomnia, stop practicing.

Original Text and Translation

§1Song of Lead and Fire
Original (Chinese)

（三丰自记：左右二字作前后看，勿误。）

大药之生有时节，亥末子初正半夜；精神相媾合光华，恍恍惚惚生明月；媾罢流下喷泡然，一阳来复休轻泄；急须闭住太玄关，火逼药过尾闾穴。

(Zhang Sanfeng's own note: Read the two characters "left" and "right" as "front" and "back"; do not misunderstand.)

The birth of the Great Medicine has its season: the close of Hai and start of Zi, right at midnight. Essence and Spirit unite and merge in radiance; in the dim and indistinct, a bright moon is born. Once united, it flows downward with a bubbling surge—this is One Yang returning; never let it leak lightly. Immediately seal the Great Mysterious Pass, and let the fire urge the Medicine through the Caudal Funnel cavity.

§2
Original (Chinese)

采时用目守泥丸，垂下左上且凝歇；谓之瞻理脑升玄，右边放下复起折；六六数毕药生乾，阳极阴生往右迁；须开关门以退火，目光下瞩守坤田；右上左下才凝住，三八数了一周天；此是天然真火候，自然升降自抽添；也无弦望与晦朔，也无沐浴共长篇；异名尽除譬喻扫，只斯两句是真诠。

When gathering, keep the eyes guarding the Muddy Pellet, drawing from below up along the back and briefly resting in stillness—this is called "gazing up to guide the brain's ascent into the mystery"; then let it descend down the front, rising and turning again. When the count of six times six (thirty-six) is complete, the Medicine forms the Qian body; yang reaches its peak and yin arises, shifting toward the front. Then one must open the gate to withdraw the fire, directing the gaze downward to guard the Kun Field; ascending in front and descending in back until it settles—when three times eight (twenty-four) is complete, that is one Celestial Circuit. This is natural true fire phasing: rising and descending naturally, drawing and adding naturally. There are no quarter, full, dark, or new moons, nor long treatises on ablution. Strip away all alternate names and sweep clean all metaphors—only those two lines are the true explanation.

§3Song of Completing the Way
Original (Chinese)

神不外游精不泄，气不耗散灵芽植；五行四象入中宫，何虑金丹不自结；内有真神外有应，满目空花降白雪；一阳来复亥子交，当中现出团圞月；急忙下手用功夫，金逢望远不堪摘；呼吸运起玄关火，青天劈破鸿蒙裂；黄河逆转上昆仑，九窍三关都透辟；化为琼浆吞入腹，哑子吃蜜难分说。

When Spirit does not stray outward and Essence does not leak, when Breath is not dissipated, the numinous sprout is planted. When the Five Phases and Four Emblems enter the Central Palace, why worry that the Golden Elixir will not congeal of itself? Within is True Spirit, without is resonance; empty flowers fill the eyes and white snow descends. At the junction of Hai and Zi, One Yang returns, and in the center appears a round full moon. Swiftly set your hand to the work—once Metal passes the full moon, it grows too old to pluck. With breathing, rouse the fire of the Mysterious Pass; the blue sky splits open and primal chaos rends apart. The Yellow River reverses its course and ascends to Kunlun; all nine orifices and Three Passes open clear through. Turning into jade nectar swallowed into the belly, it is like a mute tasting honey—impossible to put into words.

§4
Original (Chinese)

到此功程要谨慎，采取沐浴按时节；二品阴阳物类同，两般内外火符别；灵药得来片晌时，温养还须十个月；老成更要过三年，三三如九面丹壁；丹成长啸出山去，隐显立功著化迹；上帝闻名下紫书，诏我朝天飞空碧；后人依此用勤修，便是三清会上客。

At this stage of the journey, exercise great care: both gathering and ablution must keep to their proper timing. The two grades of yin and yang are of the same kind; the two modes of inner and outer fire-tallies must be clearly distinguished. Obtaining the numinous Medicine takes only a brief moment, yet warm nurturing still requires ten months; maturing takes three more years, and three times three makes nine years facing the cinnabar wall. Once the elixir is complete, with a long whistle one leaves the mountains—now hidden, now manifest, establishing merit and leaving traces of transformation. Hearing of it, the Supreme Sovereign sends down a purple decree, summoning me to court in Heaven, soaring through the azure void. If later people practice diligently according to this, they too will be guests at the assembly of the Three Pure Ones.

§5Song of the Secret Essentials of the Way
Original (Chinese)

道要歌，道要歌，不知道要必遭魔；看玄关，调真息，知斯二要修行毕；以元神，入气海，神气交融默默时，便得一玄真主宰；将元气，入黄庭，气神和合昏昏际，又得一玄最圆明；一玄妙，一玄窍，有欲观窍无观妙；两者玄玄是要机，异名同出谁知道。

Song of the Essentials of the Way, Song of the Essentials of the Way—not knowing the essentials of the Way, one is bound to meet obstacles and demons. Gaze at the Mysterious Pass, regulate True Breath: knowing these two essentials, cultivation is complete. Bring Original Spirit into the Sea of Breath; when Spirit and Breath fuse silently together, you obtain the first mystery—the true sovereign. Bring primordial Breath into the Yellow Court; when Breath and Spirit harmonize in dim quietude, you obtain the second mystery—most round and luminous. One mystery is the Marvel (miao); one mystery is the Opening (qiao): with desire, observe the Opening; without desire, observe the Marvel. Together these two—"mystery upon mystery"—are the essential pivot; different in name yet issuing from the same source, who truly knows it?

§6
Original (Chinese)

看玄关，无他诀，先从窍内调真息；神恬气静极自然，妙自无生现太极；古仙翁，多半语，恐泄真机不妄举；或言有定在中央，或言无定自领取；到如今，我尽言，此在有定无定间；有定曰窍无曰妙，老君所说玄又玄。

Gazing at the Mysterious Pass has no other secret: first regulate True Breath from within the Opening. When Spirit is serene and Breath still to the utmost naturalness, the Marvel arises out of non-being and displays the Supreme Polarity (Taiji). The immortal elders of old mostly spoke only half the truth, fearing to leak the true pivot and unwilling to speak lightly: some said it has a fixed location at the center; some said it has no fixed location and must be realized on one's own. Today I tell it all: it lies between having a fixed place and having none. What is fixed is called the Opening (qiao); what is unfixed is called the Marvel (miao)—this is what Lord Lao meant by "mystery upon mystery."

§7
Original (Chinese)

指分明，度有情，留与吾门作赏音；遇而不修为下鬼，为圣为凡随乎人；初下手，最难行，离了散乱又昏沉；大丈夫，有真学，必将神气分清浊；先天神兮最清明，后天神兮乃浊物；扫除浊物守清明，闭塞三宝居灵谷；这灵谷，即窍儿，窍中调息要深思；一息去，一息来，息息相依时相偎；幽幽细细无人觉，神气团冲九窍开；照此行持得窍妙，昏沉散乱从何来。

I point it out plainly to save sentient beings, leaving it for those of my lineage who truly appreciate the tune. To encounter it and not cultivate is to become a lowly ghost; whether one becomes sage or ordinary depends entirely on oneself. At the very beginning it is hardest to proceed: no sooner do you leave distraction than you slip into torpor. A person of resolve with genuine learning must separate the clear from the turbid in Spirit and Breath: the Spirit of Prior Heaven is clearest and brightest; the spirit of Posterior Heaven is a turbid thing. Sweep away the turbid and guard the clear brightness; seal the Three Treasures (ears, eyes, mouth) and dwell in the Numinous Valley. That Numinous Valley is the Opening itself; regulating the breath inside the Opening demands deep attentiveness. One breath goes out, one breath comes in—breath leaning on breath, constantly nestled together. Faint and subtle, unnoticed by anyone, Spirit and Breath gather into a ball and surge open the nine orifices. Practice steadily like this to gain the marvel of the Opening—from where could torpor and distraction ever arise?

Key Terms

  • Close of Hai and start of Zi: Around 11 p.m., when yin reaches its extreme and yang is born in the daily cycle—the symbol alchemists call the Living Zi Hour.
  • Great Mysterious Pass (taixuanguan): Here refers to the lower gateway; closing it prevents leakage.
  • Caudal Funnel cavity (weilüxue): The tip of the coccyx, starting point of the Governing Vessel; see Caudal Funnel Pass.
  • Muddy Pellet (niwan): The upper Cinnabar Field at the crown of the head.
  • Kun Field (kuntian): The abdomen, the lower Cinnabar Field.
  • Six times six, three times eight: Thirty-six and twenty-four; alchemists pair the yang number 36 with advancing the fire and the yin number 24 with withdrawing the tally.
  • Quarter, full, dark, and new moons: Lunar phases borrowed by alchemists as metaphors for fire phasing.
  • Mysterious Pass (xuanguan): Defined here as "between having a fixed place and having none"—fixed it is called the Opening, unfixed the Marvel; see Mysterious Pass.
  • Three Treasures (sanbao): Here, ears, eyes, and mouth; see the preceding chapter and Commentary on Patriarch Lü's Hundred-Character Tablet in this book.
  • Numinous Valley (linggu): Identified here as "the Opening itself," referring to the Mysterious Pass.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Two essentials: gaze at the Mysterious Pass, regulate True Breath. Zhang Sanfeng says, "Knowing these two essentials, cultivation is complete."
  2. Do not hunt mechanically for the Mysterious Pass. Neither fixate rigidly on a single physical spot nor drift without any anchor—it lies "between having a fixed place and having none."
  3. Countering distraction and torpor. The two main obstacles in quiet sitting are racing thoughts (distraction) and drowsiness (torpor). The remedy is mutual reliance of mind and breath—the mind following the breath, the breath resting with the mind.
  4. No leaking, no scattering, no straying. "Spirit does not stray outward, Essence does not leak, Breath is not dissipated"—a fundamental principle of life-nourishing practice that applies in daily life as well.
  5. Leave specialized guidance techniques for personal instruction. The eye-movement and counting methods in Song of Lead and Fire are historical oral formulas not meant for self-directed experimentation.

Translator's Notes

  1. "Read the two characters 'left' and 'right' as 'front' and 'back'": Zhang Sanfeng's own note. In the poem's phrases "down then up along the left," "release down the right," "shift to the right," and "up on the right, down on the left," "left" stands for the back (spine, Governing Vessel) and "right" for the front (chest and abdomen, Conception Vessel). The translation follows this key.
  2. "Flows downward with a bubbling surge": Describes an experiential sign when the Medicine arises; this site leaves it without further elaboration.
  3. "Gazing up to guide the brain's ascent into the mystery" (zhanli nao sheng xuan): Retained from the original wording; the general sense is directing the gaze upward to lead qi up to the brain.
  4. "Strip away all alternate names and sweep clean all metaphors—only those two lines are the true explanation": "Those two lines" refers to "This is natural true fire phasing: rising and descending naturally, drawing and adding naturally."
  5. "Once Metal passes the full moon, it grows too old to pluck": Wang is the full moon on the fifteenth lunar day; after the full moon, light wanes, symbolizing the Medicine growing past its prime.
  6. "Like a mute tasting honey": An ineffable sweetness that cannot be described in words.
  7. "The two grades of yin and yang are of the same kind": "Two grades" refers to inner and outer medicines; see the seven-character quatrains later in this scroll.
  8. "With desire, observe the Opening; without desire, observe the Marvel": Adapts chapter 1 of the Daodejing: "Ever without desire, one beholds its marvel; ever with desire, one beholds its bounds."
  9. In the original compilation, Song of Sitting in Meditation stands between Song of Completing the Way and Song of the Secret Essentials of the Way; since it appears in Book 2, only a link is provided here.
