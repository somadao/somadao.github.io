Part IV · Essentials of the Mystery (Part I)

Four Songs Commenting on the Nine Sovereigns Elixir Scripture · Song of Sealing the Leaks

Four Songs Commenting on the Nine Sovereigns Elixir Scripture and Song of Sealing the Leaks — likening the Holy Embryo to ten months of gestation; sealing the leaking body and sealing the leaking orifices

About This Chapter

  • Four Songs Commenting on the Nine Sovereigns Elixir Scripture: The Nine Sovereigns Elixir Scripture (Jiuhuang danjing) uses the ten-month development of an infant in the womb to symbolize the growth of the "Holy Embryo" in internal alchemy: in the third month the ethereal soul is born, in the fourth month the corporeal soul is born, in the seventh month the seven orifices of the heart open, and in the tenth month form and spirit are firmly established. Zhang Sanfeng wrote songs for four of those months (Treatise on Dragon, Tiger, Lead, and Mercury in Part II of this book is also a commentary on The Nine Sovereigns Elixir Scripture). Interwoven with mantras and colloquial phrasing, their style is distinctive. The first song states plainly: "No need to set up a furnace or erect a tripod; simply draw and replenish through non-action... no use for 'spirit-intercourse' or 'bodily separation'; use only the two ○s of breathing out above and in below."
  • Song of Sealing the Leaks: "Sealing the leaks" (gulou) means plugging every outlet so that Essence, Breath, and Spirit do not drain away. Written in a lively folk-song voice: "Close the Yellow Chamber, tend the elixir stove, do not let the Six Thieves come make a racket; the Master of the House laughs heartily, guarding the Yellow Court's vital seal," and "reject every bit of impure Essence and impure Breath."

Editorial note: In the original text, seven songs stand between Song of the Secret Essentials of the Way and Four Songs Commenting on the Nine Sovereigns Elixir Scripture: Song of the Great Way, Song of the True Bellows, Song of the Single Opening of the Mysterious Pass, Song of Refining Lead (beginning "The Most High Way"), Song of the Golden Elixir, Song of Dispelling Delusions on the Golden Fluid Reverted Elixir, and Song of Pointing Out Delusions on the Dragon-Tiger Reverted Elixir. Because they rely primarily on yin-yang school formulations, they are omitted under this site's editorial policy.

Original Text and Translation

§1Commentary on the Nine Sovereigns Elixir Scripture — Third Month, Birth of the Ethereal Soul: Song of Three Returns and One Reversal of the Golden Elixir
Original (Chinese)

一月二月，都是少莲花里真精血；不须安炉立鼎，只用无为抽彻；也不候两弦气到，候只候印堂星阙；也不候八两半斤为活子，候只候五千四百正当月；不用神交与体隔，只用上呼下吸两○说；一来浑身煖，二来顖门彻，三来天目开，便见大千世界；此等效验处，方是生胞生胎生魂的的来；分咐修仙子，须向云中跨鹤，切莫沙土中埋。

In the first and second months, there is only true Essence and blood within the tender lotus-womb. No need to set up a furnace or erect a tripod; simply draw and replenish through non-action. Neither wait for the breath of the two quarter moons to arrive—wait only for tidings at the Hall of Impression, the Starry Gate between the brows. Nor wait for "eight ounces and half a catty" as the Living Zi Hour—wait only for the exact month when the count of five thousand four hundred comes full. Do not use "spirit-intercourse" or "bodily separation"; use only the teaching of the two ○s of exhaling above and inhaling below. First, the whole body grows warm; second, the fontanelle opens clear through; third, the Celestial Eye opens and you behold the trichiliocosm. Verified signs such as these are truly and unmistakably how the sac, the embryo, and the ethereal soul come into being. I charge every student of immortality: ride the crane into the clouds, and never end up buried in sand and dirt.

§2Commentary on the Nine Sovereigns Elixir Scripture — Fourth Month, Birth of the Corporeal Soul: Song of Four Returns and Two Reversals of the Golden Elixir
Original (Chinese)

老祖名为降魔护道祖，贯通七魄制于酉；酉内有真金，金能保长生；帝真魄命元，从此妙元元；洞房花烛夜，对境忘情耶；嗒哩哪，嚤哩哪，金吒木吒咤喃哪；唵似我也，似我也，南无般若波罗密耶。

The Ancient Patriarch is named "Patriarch Who Subdues Demons and Protects the Way," penetrating all seven corporeal souls and bringing them under control at You. Within You lies True Metal, and Metal preserves long life. The sovereign true corporeal soul is the origin of life; from this unfolds mystery upon mystery. Even on a wedding night of ornate candles in the bridal chamber, facing the scene one forgets all passion! Ta-li-na, mo-li-na, Jinzha Muzha zha-nan-na; Om, like me, like me, Namo Prajñāpāramitā ye.

§3Commentary on the Nine Sovereigns Elixir Scripture — Seventh Month, Opening the Seven Orifices of the Heart: Song of Seven Returns and Five Reversals of the Golden Elixir
Original (Chinese)

道君坐太微，降生大天尊；垂此真法语，引人上玉清；一个〔符〕字诀，七个月象形；左二右三名为五，太极浑然有五灵；五灵方得七窍开，须把天目运此来；叫人修真的的是，穿破机关上琼台。

The Lord of the Way sits in the Palace of Grand Tenuity and descends to be born as the Great Heavenly Worthy; he hands down these true words of Dharma to guide people up to Jade Purity. A single talisman-character `[talisman]` formula images the form of the seventh month: two on the left and three on the right make five, and within the undifferentiated Supreme Polarity dwell the Five Numina. Only when the Five Numina are complete do the seven orifices open; one must bring the Celestial Eye to operate right here. This is unmistakably where people are told to cultivate truth: pierce through the hidden mechanism and ascend to the Jasper Terrace.

§4Commentary on the Nine Sovereigns Elixir Scripture — Tenth Month, Form and Spirit Firmly Established: Song of Ten Returns of the Golden Elixir
Original (Chinese)

弄玉仙姑吹玉箫，声声吹入元关窍；此窍名为天地根，先天足来百景自生神；八景生了二十四诸天，九窍自通灵；圣胎原借真一炁，王母瑶池泛金液；莲花心，心心含著黄芽生，香在里，锦乾坤，夺得真香妙氤氲，保我劫劫常存；自从纯阳道祖一泄此，万世贤良细用心；蓬莱岛，当留名，瀛洲会，须效许真君，白日间拔宅飞升；三丰今日苦叮咛，但愿人人俱有寿阳合元根。

Immortal Maiden Nongyu plays her jade flute, every note blowing straight into the Opening of the Mysterious Pass. This opening is called the Root of Heaven and Earth; when Prior Heaven is full, the hundred inner landscapes naturally give birth to Spirit. From the Eight Landscapes arise the twenty-four heavens, and the nine orifices open into numinous clarity of themselves. The Holy Embryo originally relies on the Breath of True Unity; on the Queen Mother's Jasper Pool, Golden Fluid brims and ripples. At the heart of the lotus, heart within heart holds the growing Yellow Sprout—fragrance dwells inside, a brocade cosmos; seizing that true fragrance and wondrous mist preserves me across kalpa after kalpa. Ever since Patriarch Chunyang (Lü Dongbin) first revealed this, worthy seekers of ten thousand generations must apply their minds with care. On Penglai Isle leave your name; at the gathering of Yingzhou emulate True Lord Xu, ascending with his whole household in broad daylight. Today I, Sanfeng, earnestly exhort you: my sole wish is that every person may enjoy long life, their yang united with the primordial root.

§5Song of Sealing the Leaks
Original (Chinese)

说玄妙，讲玄妙；运精神，转九窍，三关八门都撞到；闭黄房，修丹灶，休将六贼来喧闹；主人翁，哈哈笑，守住黄庭罩檄要；婴儿来，姹女叫，两家一处成玄妙；结成丹，怀中抱，浑身一片霞光照；坐丹田，清虚号，紫气红光常晃耀；上至须弥泥丸宫，下至涌泉彻幽奥。

Speak of mystery and marvel, expound mystery and marvel: move Essence and Spirit, revolve through the nine orifices, striking through the Three Passes and Eight Gates. Close the Yellow Chamber, tend the elixir stove, and do not let the Six Thieves come make a racket. The Master of the House laughs heartily, guarding the Yellow Court's vital seal. The Infant arrives, the Fair Maiden calls—the two households unite in one place to accomplish the mystery. Congealed into the elixir, held close in the embrace, the whole body shines with one sheet of rosy radiance. Seated in the Cinnabar Field, styled Clear Void, purple vapor and crimson light glow without cease—reaching up to Mount Sumeru, the Muddy Pellet Palace, and down to Bubbling Spring (Yongquan), penetrating every hidden depth.

§6
Original (Chinese)

七宝池内将宝盗，杂精杂气都不要；琼枝玉蕊金花新，珊瑚玛瑙砗磲造；固漏身，固漏窍，不漏铛铛固漏鏊；十二重楼都开导，七经八脉合三焦，百骸四体皆征效；大肠周，小肠烁，上下鹊桥平稳趠；赵州桥上去饮茶，甘河里边听人叫；大运通，小运到，三家相见同欢乐。

Steal the treasure from the Pool of Seven Jewels; reject every bit of impure Essence and impure Breath. Jade boughs, jeweled stamens, Golden Flowers blooming fresh—fashioned of coral, agate, and giant clam shell. Seal the leaking body, seal the leaking orifices—clang, clang, patch the pan so not a drop leaks out! All Twelve Stories of the Tower open and flow clear; the seven conduits and Eight Vessels unite with the Triple Burner; every bone and all four limbs show verified response. The large intestine circulates, the small intestine glows warm, and across the upper and lower Magpie Bridges one steps smoothly and steadily. Go up onto Zhaozhou Bridge to drink tea; beside the Sweet River listen to a voice calling. The Great Circuit flows through, the Small Circuit arrives, and the Three Households meet together in shared joy.

§7
Original (Chinese)

我把一身固漏坚，万载千秋无老少；固漏形躯炼太阳，精炁神全守中黄；铅汞熬煎成至宝，金丹一粒放毫光；婴儿显象长相守，与天同寿免无常；有人学得固漏歌，便是长生不老方。

I seal my entire body solid and leakproof—for ten thousand years and a thousand autumns, beyond old or young. Sealing the leaking frame, I refine the Great Yang; Essence, Breath, and Spirit complete, I guard the Central Yellow. Lead and Mercury simmer into the supreme treasure; a single grain of Golden Elixir radiates fine beams of light. The Infant manifests its form and abides forever, sharing Heaven's lifespan and escaping impermanence. Whoever learns this Song of Sealing the Leaks holds the very prescription for long life without aging.

Key Terms

  • Nine Sovereigns Elixir Scripture (Jiuhuang danjing): An alchemical scripture annotated by Zhang Sanfeng whose exact identity is uncertain; Part II of this book also includes Commentary on the Nine Sovereigns Elixir Scripture: Treatise on Dragon, Tiger, Lead, and Mercury.
  • Birth of the ethereal soul, birth of the corporeal soul, opening the seven orifices of the heart, form and spirit firmly established: Using the month-by-month development of a fetus in the womb as a metaphor for the Holy Embryo.
  • Hall of Impression, Starry Gate (yintang xingque): The space between the two eyebrows.
  • Fontanelle (xinmen): The anterior crown of the skull, soft and unclosed in infancy.
  • Celestial Eye (tianmu): Slightly above the space between the brows, called by alchemists the portal of the upper Cinnabar Field.
  • Spirit-intercourse / bodily separation (shenjiao, tige): "Bodily separation and spirit-intercourse" is a yin-yang school expression, explicitly rejected here ("do not use").
  • Sealing the leaks (gulou): Preventing Essence, Breath, and Spirit from draining outward.
  • Six Thieves (liuzei): Craving and agitation stirred up through the six sense faculties—eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, and mind.
  • Yellow Chamber, Yellow Court, Central Yellow: All refer to the Central Palace.
  • Meeting of the Three Households (sanjia xiangjian): The union of body, mind, and intention (or Essence, Breath, and Spirit) as one.

Putting It into Practice

  1. No external furnace or tripod is needed. "No need to set up a furnace or erect a tripod; simply draw and replenish through non-action... do not use 'spirit-intercourse' or 'bodily separation'"—here Zhang Sanfeng once again rejects yin-yang school methods.
  2. Sealing the leaks is the baseline of cultivation. Essence does not leak, Breath does not scatter, Spirit does not race outward; "reject every bit of impure Essence and impure Breath."
  3. Guard the six senses. "Do not let the Six Thieves come make a racket"—looking less, listening less, and desiring less are themselves sealing the leaks.
  4. Do not cling to mantras. The mantra in the fourth-month song is part of the original text and retained verbatim; grasping its import (subduing demons, protecting the Way, forgetting passion even amid tempting circumstances) matters far more than reciting syllables.

Translator's Notes

  1. The four pieces in this set are originally titled Commentary on the Nine Sovereigns Elixir Scripture — Third Month, Birth of the Ethereal Soul: Song of Three Returns and One Reversal of the Golden Elixir, Fourth Month, Birth of the Corporeal Soul: Song of Four Returns and Two Reversals of the Golden Elixir, Seventh Month, Opening the Seven Orifices of the Heart: Song of Seven Returns and Five Reversals of the Golden Elixir, and Tenth Month, Form and Spirit Firmly Established: Song of Ten Returns of the Golden Elixir. They form the first four sections of this chapter.
  2. "Only true Essence and blood within the tender lotus-womb": Following the ten-month gestation metaphor running through the whole set, in the first and second months the womb holds only the Essence and blood inside the "lotus" (womb), before the ethereal soul has been born.
  3. "The two ○s of exhaling above and inhaling below": In the original edition, a circle symbol follows the character "two" (liang); preserved verbatim.
  4. "Even on a wedding night of ornate candles in the bridal chamber, facing the scene one forgets all passion": Meaning that even in the most emotionally stirring circumstances one remains unmoved and free of passion, echoing "subduing demons and protecting the Way" above.
  5. "Ta-li-na... Namo Prajñāpāramitā ye": Mantras and chanted syllables in the original, preserved without free paraphrase. The final phrase corresponds to the Buddhist Namo Prajñāpāramitā.
  6. "A single `[talisman]` formula": The original prints a special graphic talisman character that cannot be typeset, represented here by `[talisman]`.
  7. "Immortal Maiden Nongyu plays her jade flute": Nongyu, legendary daughter of Duke Mu of Qin, skilled on the flute, who ascended with Xiao Shi astride phoenixes.
  8. "True Lord Xu": Xu Xun, who according to legend ascended in broad daylight together with his entire household of forty-two people.
  9. "Guarding the Yellow Court's vital seal" (shouzhu huangting zhaoxiyao): Retained from the original wording; the general sense is guarding the vital center of the Yellow Court.
  10. "Clang, clang, patch the pan so not a drop leaks out" (bulou dangdang gulou ao): Ao is a flat iron griddle for baking flatbread; dangdang is the clanging sound of mending metalwork—using the voice of a tinker mending pans as a humorous metaphor for sealing leaks.
  11. "Go up onto Zhaozhou Bridge to drink tea": Playfully blends the Chan koan "Zhaozhou: go drink tea" with using Zhaozhou Bridge as a metaphor for the Magpie Bridge.
