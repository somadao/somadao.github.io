Part IV · Essentials of the Mystery (Part I)

Song of Daoist Sentiment · Song of Refining Lead · Song of the One Breath of Prior Heaven

Song of Daoist Sentiment, Song of Refining Lead, Song of the One Breath of Prior Heaven — before refining the Reverted Elixir first refine inner nature; before cultivating the Great Medicine first cultivate the mind

About This Chapter

Three seven-character songs:

  • Song of Daoist Sentiment: Fifty-two lines forming a complete song of practice. Its opening lines are the most famous: "First lay aside all worldly entanglements together, next study the principles with fine precision; before refining the Reverted Elixir first refine inner nature, before cultivating the Great Medicine first cultivate the mind." Once the mind is cultivated, the "tidings of the elixir" arrive naturally; once inner nature is clear, the "ingredients of the Medicine" arise of themselves. The middle section describes the phenomena when the Medicine arises, repeatedly admonishing: "If you reach this point do not be alarmed or afraid," "all manner of visions are not of the same kind," and "the slightest rising thought loses Heaven's truth." At the end, after all yin Essence throughout the body is stripped away and the Yang Spirit appears, one returns to the dusty world to accumulate merit and virtue.
  • Song of Refining Lead: "True Lead is born prior to Heaven and Earth—what use have metals, stones, trees, or herbs?" True Lead is not mineral or botanical substance, but the Breath of Prior Heaven. The remainder discusses the fire phasing of one yang, two yang, and three yang up to the full count of five thousand four hundred.
  • Song of the One Breath of Prior Heaven: "From birth it is inherently the Breath of Prior Heaven, hidden within the Mountain of Form where few can meet it"—the One Breath of Prior Heaven is innate in every person, yet hidden within the body so that few ever encounter it.

Original Text and Translation

§1Song of Daoist Sentiment
Original (Chinese)

道情非是等闲情，既识天机不可轻；先把世情齐放下，次将道理细研精；未炼还丹先炼性，未修大药且修心；心修自然丹信至，性清自然药材生。

Daoist sentiment is no ordinary idle sentiment; once you recognize Heaven's pivot, never take it lightly. First lay aside all worldly entanglements together; next examine the principles carefully until you master them. Before refining the Reverted Elixir, first refine your inner nature; before cultivating the Great Medicine, first cultivate your mind. When the mind is cultivated, tidings of the elixir arrive naturally; when inner nature is clear, the ingredients of the Medicine naturally arise.

§2
Original (Chinese)

药材生，紧加功，雷声隐隐震虚空；电光灼处寻真种，风信来时觅本宗；霞光万道笼金鼎，紫云千丈罩天门；若还到此休惊怕，稳把元神守洞门；如猫捕鼠兔逢鹰，急急着力又加勤；万般景象皆非类，一颗红光是至真。

When the Medicine's ingredients arise, press the work closely: muffled thunder rolls and shakes the empty void. Where lightning flashes bright, seek the true seed; when the herald wind arrives, look for the root source. Ten thousand rays of rosy light enfold the golden tripod; a thousand fathoms of purple cloud canopy the Gate of Heaven. Should you reach this point, do not be startled or afraid—steadily hold Original Spirit guarding the cavern gate. Like a cat catching a mouse, like a hawk seizing a hare, apply swift strength and redouble your diligence. Ten thousand kinds of visions are none of them the real thing; one single orb of crimson light is the ultimate truth.

§3
Original (Chinese)

此个红光是春意，其中有若明窗尘；中悬一点先天药，远似葡萄近似金；到此全凭要谨慎，丝毫念起丧天真；待他一点自归伏，身中化作四时春；一片白云香一阵，一番雨过一番新；终日绵绵如醉汉，悠悠只等洞中春。

This crimson light is the breath of spring; within it float motes like dust in a sunlit window. Suspended at its center is a single speck of Prior-Heaven Medicine—from afar like a grape, up close like gold. Reaching this juncture, everything depends on care and restraint: the slightest rising thought loses Heaven's truth. Wait until that single speck submits and settles of itself, and the body transforms into the spring of all four seasons: one drift of white cloud brings a wave of fragrance; each passing shower leaves everything renewed. All day long, continuous and mellow like a tipsy soul, unhurriedly awaiting the spring within the cavern.

§4
Original (Chinese)

遍体阴精都剥尽，化作纯阳一块金；此时气绝如小死，打成一片是全真；到此功成才了当，却来尘世积功勋；行满功成天命诏，阳神出现了真灵；此言休向非人说，不逢达者莫轻论；其中切切通玄理，此真之外更无真；收拾行囊牢封固，他日成功可印心；可印心，道情歌，五十二句要君寻；若有虚言遭天谴，说与非人鞭丧身。

All yin Essence throughout the body is completely stripped away, transformed into a single ingot of pure yang gold. At this moment ordinary breath ceases as in a "little death"; fused into one seamless whole, this is Complete Truth (Quanzhen). Reaching this stage, the work is accomplished and settled—yet one returns to the dusty world to accumulate meritorious deeds. When deeds are full and practice complete, Heaven sends down its summons; the Yang Spirit emerges, perfecting the true numen.

Do not speak these words to the wrong person; unless you meet one of understanding, do not discuss them lightly. Every phrase here penetrates the mysterious principle directly; beyond this truth there is no other truth. Pack your satchel and seal it tightly—on the day you succeed, it will verify your own heart. Able to verify the heart is this Song of Daoist Sentiment, fifty-two lines for you to ponder carefully; if there is a single false word, may Heaven strike me down—and whoever speaks it to the unworthy shall bring ruin upon themselves.

§5Song of Refining Lead
Original (Chinese)

炼铅之法何人晓，得此便为真仙了；痛嗟老大无觅处，遍游五湖及三岛；六十七岁入终南，得遇真人传至宝；真铅生于天地先，何用金石与木草；炼之九九功若成，杳冥之内生金宝；金骨一根动天地，二八调和生美形。

Who knows the method of refining Lead? Whoever obtains it becomes a true immortal at once. Bitterly I sighed in old age, with nowhere left to search, having roamed the Five Lakes and Three Isles. At sixty-seven I entered Mount Zhongnan and met a Perfected One who passed on the supreme treasure. True Lead is born prior to Heaven and Earth—what use have metals, stones, trees, or herbs? When refined through nine times nine cycles to completion, a golden treasure arises within the dim and obscure. A single golden spine shakes Heaven and Earth; the two eights harmonize to bring forth a radiant form.

§6
Original (Chinese)

美形才生居土釜，须要念念牢封固；一阳火起要温养，二阳火起有神功；若到三阳同泰卦，腾腾猛火烧虚空；退尽阴符生灵质，灵质才生天地毕；若还亏损再调和，再居土釜生灵质；五千四百全其数，方尽炼铅真妙趣；嘱语后来同志者，莫将炼铅看容易。

No sooner is this fine form born than it dwells in the Earthen Cauldron; it must be sealed tightly in every single thought. When the fire of One Yang rises, warm nurturing is needed; when the fire of Two Yang rises, wondrous power unfolds. When Three Yang arrives in accord with the hexagram Tai (Peace), surging fierce fire blazes through the empty void. Withdraw the Yin Tally completely and the numinous substance is born; once that numinous substance arises, the work of Heaven and Earth is complete. If any depletion occurs, harmonize anew and place it back in the Earthen Cauldron to regenerate the numinous substance. Only when the count of five thousand four hundred is complete do you exhaust the true wonder of refining Lead. I admonish fellow seekers who come after: never look upon refining Lead as easy.

§7Song of the One Breath of Prior Heaven
Original (Chinese)

生来本是先天炁，隐在形山人难遇；分明说破君须记，一弦春水包形势；下口将来入口吞，十二雷门都惊惧；醉兮醉兮复醉兮，丹田春透红如玉；蟠桃漫饮瓮头香，巽风鼓动元和气；阴魔战退一腔春，神号鬼哭翻天地；功完行满足三千，乘鸾跨鹤仙飞去。

From birth it is inherently the Breath of Prior Heaven, hidden within the Mountain of Form where people can scarcely encounter it. Clearly I break it open for you—remember it well: one crescent of spring water enfolds the entire frame. Taking it down through the mouth and swallowing it in, all Twelve Thunder Gates tremble in awe. Tipsy, oh tipsy, tipsy once again—spring permeates the Cinnabar Field, rosy as jade. Freely drinking Peach of Immortality wine fragrant at the jar's rim, the Wind of Xun stirs the primordial harmonious Breath. Yin demons are routed, leaving a chest full of spring; spirits wail and ghosts cry as Heaven and Earth overturn. When work is complete and good deeds reach three thousand, mount the phoenix and ride the crane to soar away as an immortal.

Key Terms

  • Daoist sentiment (daoqing): Songs sung by Daoists to exhort and awaken people in the world.
  • Tidings of the elixir / ingredients of the Medicine (danxin, yaocai): Signs of the elixir and intimations of the Medicine arising, referring to the stirring of the Breath of Prior Heaven.
  • Dust in a sunlit window (mingchuang chen): Floating dust motes visible where sunlight streams through a window—an alchemical image for the subtle, barely perceptible shimmer when the Medicine arises.
  • Little death (xiaosi): A state of deep absorption in which ordinary breathing seems to cease and body and mind fall completely still—not physical death.
  • True Lead (zhenqian): The Breath of Prior Heaven; as this song states, "born prior to Heaven and Earth," not metals, stones, or herbs.
  • Earthen Cauldron (tufu): The Central Palace, the Yellow Court.
  • One Yang, Two Yang, Three Yang: The hexagrams Fu (one yang), Lin (two yang), and Tai (three yang), symbolizing fire phasing as yang energy gradually waxes.
  • Five thousand four hundred: A traditional total count for fire phasing; explanations vary across texts, and here it signifies one complete cycle.
  • Mountain of Form (xingshan): Metaphor for the human body.
  • Twelve Thunder Gates (shi'er leimen): Metaphor for the Twelve-Storied Tower (throat), or the passes and apertures throughout the body.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Cultivate mind and inner nature first. "Before refining the Reverted Elixir, first refine inner nature; before cultivating the Great Medicine, first cultivate the mind"—this can be practiced every day: set down worldly entanglements, study sound principles, and let the mind grow clear.
  2. Neither fear nor chase visions. "Should you reach this point, do not be startled or afraid" and "ten thousand kinds of visions are none of them the real thing"—consistent with On Verifications of Reversion in this book.
  3. A single thought loses it. "The slightest rising thought loses Heaven's truth"—the crux of quiet sitting is not doing something extra, but not stirring grasping thoughts.
  4. Even after attainment, accumulate virtue. "Reaching this stage the work is settled—yet one returns to the dusty world to accumulate meritorious deeds."

Translator's Notes

  1. "At sixty-seven I entered Mount Zhongnan": Author's Preface to Essentials of the Mystery in this scroll says "during the Yanyou era I first entered Mount Zhongnan." Legends regarding Zhang Sanfeng's birth year vary; the text is kept as written.
  2. "The two eights harmonize to bring forth a radiant form": "Two eights" refers to eight ounces and eight ounces (half a catty and eight ounces), symbolizing Lead and Mercury matched in equal measure.
  3. "A single golden spine shakes Heaven and Earth": Metaphor for True Breath first arising and resonating throughout the entire body.
  4. "Surging fierce fire blazes through the empty void" / "Withdraw the Yin Tally completely": Advancing Yang Fire and withdrawing the Yin Tally are the two halves of alchemical fire phasing.
  5. "One crescent of spring water enfolds the entire frame": "One crescent" refers to the waxing or waning quarter moon, symbolizing the time when the Medicine arises; "spring water" symbolizes True Breath.
  6. "Tipsy, oh tipsy, tipsy once again": Describes the mellow, suffused warmth and ease of body and mind when the Medicine arises.
  7. "Good deeds reach three thousand": Daoists commonly speak of "three thousand meritorious works and eight hundred virtuous deeds," referring to accumulated charitable conduct.
  8. "Speak it to the unworthy and bring ruin upon oneself": A traditional oath of restraint in transmission, warning against reckless disclosure.
