V · Essentials of the Mystery (Part Two)

Sweeping All Clear Daoist Songs · Partridge Sky · Five Watches Daoist Songs (VI) · Questions and Answers on the Mysterious Mechanism

Twelve Songs of Sweeping All Clear, Partridge Sky, Set VI of the Five Watches Daoist Songs, and Questions and Answers on the Mysterious Mechanism — all with image and form is false; only what is soundless and scentless is true

About This Chapter

The final chapter of Essentials of the Mystery (Part Two), and the conclusion of the entire inner-alchemy selection of this book.

  • Twelve Daoist Songs of Sweeping All Clear: Each lyric begins with “Sweep all clear, shine forth the…”—True, Sovereign, Emperor, Master, Life, Numinous, One, Mind, Brightness, Immortal, Buddha, Sage. “Sweeping all clear” means sweeping everything empty: clearing away words, images, body and mind, scriptures, and idols alike so that the original source shines forth. “All with image and form is false; only what is soundless and scentless is true”; “do not mistake body and mind for the True Person”; “cling not to traces, stick not to words”; “do not mistake Spirit Mountain for the Buddha-land.” This is the most empty and crystalline cycle in the entire book, reading very much like Chan gāthās.
  • Partridge Sky: “To fight demons, first fight the demon of sleep—this is the very first whip of cultivation.” Overcoming sleepiness is the opening step of practice; in laying the foundation (zhuji) one’s resolve must be firm, allowing Spirit to return and Breath to restore until the Elixir body forms, and only then speaking of refining Lead.
  • Five Watches Daoist Songs (Set VI): Five songs, each concluding with the refrain “in an instant water and fire meet in Jiji (Already Across).”
  • Questions and Answers on the Mysterious Mechanism: A question-and-answer exchange attributed to Zhang Sanfeng. Question: who gathers and closes the breath? Before one’s parents were born, where does that single point of True Yang cultivate its root and establish life? Answer: the breath gathers and closes of itself, lifts and raises of itself; that single point of True Yang “cultivates its root and nourishes its stem within emptiness, and establishes life and settles its base within emptiness.”

Original Text and Translation

§1Sweeping All Clear · No. 1
Original (Chinese)

一扫光，照见真，拍拍满怀都是春；玉非宝，珠非珍，北邙路儿不随身；有象有形皆是假，无声无臭始为真；身非道，道非心，莫把身心当真人。

Sweep all clear, shine forth the True—brimming full, your whole bosom is springtime! Jade is no treasure, pearls are no rarity; none of them follows you down the road to Mount Beimang. Whatever has image and form is false; only what has neither sound nor scent is true. The body is not the Way, the Way is not the thinking mind—never mistake body and mind for the True Person.

§2Sweeping All Clear · No. 2
Original (Chinese)

一扫光，照见君，花前月下醉醺醺；文非字，学非文，东君笑人寡见闻；桃红柳绿李花白，一挥而就不思寻；人纷纷，物纭纭，无异于人自超群。

Sweep all clear, shine forth the Sovereign—amid blossoms and beneath the moon, gently tipsy with joy! Literature is not mere characters, learning is not mere texts; the Lord of Spring laughs at how narrow people’s vision is. Peach blossoms red, willows green, plum blossoms white—all dashed off in a single stroke without plotting or search. People bustle and things throng in confusion; differing in nothing from others, you naturally rise above the crowd.

§3Sweeping All Clear · No. 3
Original (Chinese)

一扫光，照见帝，神化机关一旦契；鱼自跃，鸢自飞，造化何常潜算计；气化形生基始兆，一点冲和归根蒂；包虚空，生天地，一见百事自如意。

Sweep all clear, shine forth the Emperor—in one instant you tally with the pivot of spirit-transformation! Fish leap of themselves, kites soar of themselves; when did Creation ever scheme in secret? As Breath transforms and form arises, the foundation first takes omen, and a single point of harmonious blending returns to root and stem. Encompassing empty space and giving birth to Heaven and Earth—seen once, a hundred affairs unfold at will.

§4Sweeping All Clear · No. 4
Original (Chinese)

一扫光，照见主，豁然洞观来时路；陆乘车，水舟渡，不移一步天堂处；顷刻而成理最真，立跻圣域语非误；不窥牖，不出户，便知天下有把握。

Sweep all clear, shine forth the Master—suddenly you see clear through the road by which you came! On land take a carriage, on water cross by boat; without shifting a single step, this very spot is Heaven. Perfected in an instant—this principle is truest of all; stepping straight into the realm of sages—those words are no mistake. Without peering out the window or stepping beyond the door, you know all under Heaven with quiet certainty.

§5Sweeping All Clear · No. 5
Original (Chinese)

一扫光，照见生，一点冲和二五精；性即理，命即情，氤氲妙用一时成；迷时取之头头错，悟后拈来处处神；死自死，生自生，培由栽兮覆由倾。

Sweep all clear, shine forth Life—a single point of harmonious blending, the essence of the Two and the Five! Inner nature (xing) is principle, life (ming) is vitality’s feeling, and their swirling, pervasive wonder matures in a single moment. In delusion, every grasp misses the mark; after awakening, whatever you pick up is marvelous everywhere. Death dies of itself, life lives of itself—what stands planted is nourished, what leans over topples.

§6Sweeping All Clear · No. 6
Original (Chinese)

一扫光，照见灵，朝游暮宿玲珑亭；醒也宁，睡也宁，春来无处不青青；达聪不听有弦琴，明目须读无字经；毋恍惚，毋窈冥，常宁常静常惺惺。

Sweep all clear, shine forth the Numinous—roaming by dawn and resting by dusk in the Exquisite Pavilion! Awake you are tranquil, asleep you are tranquil; when spring arrives, no place fails to turn green. Truly keen ears do not listen to a stringed zither; truly clear eyes must read the wordless scripture. Neither dim nor obscure, stay ever tranquil, ever still, ever awake.

§7Sweeping All Clear · No. 7
Original (Chinese)

一扫光，照见一，一贯一毕一归一；圣也一，贤也一，天地万物无非一；人间百虑皆一致，天下殊途同归一；仙也一，凡也一，一了百当自简易。

Sweep all clear, shine forth the One—one threads all through, one finishes all, one returns to One! The sage is One, the worthy is One; Heaven, Earth, and the ten thousand things are nothing other than One. A hundred human cares converge on one goal; all diverse paths under Heaven return to One. Immortal is One, ordinary mortal is One—settle the One and a hundred matters fall cleanly and effortlessly into place.

§8Sweeping All Clear · No. 8
Original (Chinese)

一扫光，照见心，儒书佛典仙子经；尽赘瘤，皆附瘿，都是各道其道人；立图设象枉费心，巧譬曲喻徒敲唇；不执迹，不泥文，即是神而明之人。

Sweep all clear, shine forth the Mind: Confucian books, Buddhist canons, and immortal scriptures are all excess wens and dangling tumors—each author merely preaching a Way of his own. Drawing diagrams and setting up symbols wastes the mind in vain; clever parables and convoluted metaphors merely wear out the lips. Cling not to traces, stick not to words—that very person is one who “makes it bright through spirit.”

§9Sweeping All Clear · No. 9
Original (Chinese)

一扫光，照见明，混沌初分焉有文；秦焚书，汉注经，注经经亡焚书存；心口相传文字授，总是无事惹事情；无衣钵，无剑琴，才算一尘不染人。

Sweep all clear, shine forth Brightness—when primordial chaos first parted, where were any written texts? Qin burned the books and Han wrote commentaries on the classics; yet commenting killed the classics while burning kept the books alive! Handing down mouth-to-ear formulas and written transmissions is forever stirring up trouble where none existed. With neither robe nor bowl, neither sword nor zither—only then can one be called untouched by a single speck of dust.

§10Sweeping All Clear · No. 10
Original (Chinese)

一扫光，照见仙，始信通字是强言；窍非妙，牝非玄，非水非火非汞铅；也无无极合有极，也无先天与后天；性归命，命归天，复命归根混沌前。

Sweep all clear, shine forth the Immortal—only now do you trust that even the word “penetrate” is forced speech! The opening is not “wondrous,” the female is not “mysterious”; it is neither water nor fire, neither Mercury nor Lead. There is no “Limitless joining the Supreme Ultimate,” nor any Prior Heaven and Posterior Heaven. Inner nature returns to life, life returns to Heaven—returning to destiny and reverting to the root, prior to primordial chaos.

§11Sweeping All Clear · No. 11
Original (Chinese)

一扫光，照见佛，须知彼岸无有佛；佛非心，心非佛，枉自持斋念弥勒；莫把灵山当佛地，除却灵山别有佛；圆陀陀，光灼灼，千圣不传这一着。

Sweep all clear, shine forth the Buddha—know that on the “farther shore” there is no Buddha either! Buddha is not mind, mind is not Buddha; in vain do people keep fasts and chant Maitreya’s name. Never mistake Spirit Mountain for the Buddha-land—apart from Spirit Mountain there is another Buddha! Perfectly round and glowing bright, this single move not a thousand sages have ever transmitted.

§12Sweeping All Clear · No. 12
Original (Chinese)

一扫光，照见圣，通天彻地一轮镜；静中动，动中静，一任万物不相侵；花开花落地自贞，云来云去天常净；正大光，光大正，分而能合定能应。

Sweep all clear, shine forth the Sage—piercing Heaven and plumbing Earth, one great round mirror! Motion within stillness, stillness within motion, let the ten thousand things come and go without ever encroaching. Flowers bloom and flowers fall, yet Earth remains steadfast; clouds drift in and clouds drift away, yet Heaven stays ever clear. Upright, vast, and luminous—luminous, vast, and upright—able to divide yet reunite, settled in stillness yet ready to respond.

§13Partridge Sky
Original (Chinese)

战魔须战睡魔先，此是修行第一鞭；目下辛勤熬一夜，壶中日月换千年；筑基事，志要坚，须凭玄牝觅玄玄；神归气复成丹体，然后开炉讲炼铅。

To battle demons, battle the demon of sleep first—this is the very first whip of cultivation. Endure one night of diligent wakefulness right now, and the sun and moon inside the gourd trade it for a thousand years. In laying the foundation (zhuji), your resolve must be firm: rely upon the Mysterious Female to seek the mystery beyond mystery. Once Spirit returns and Breath restores to form the Elixir body, only then open the furnace and speak of refining Lead.

§14Five Watches Daoist Songs (VI) · First Watch
Original (Chinese)

一更里，万事休，抖精神，坐床头；巍巍不动主人守，偃月炉中黄芽逗，七宝林中玉液流，真阳一撞三关透；顷刻间水火既济，灌泥丸降下重楼。

In the first watch, lay ten thousand affairs to rest; rouse your spirit and sit at the head of the bed. Towering and unmoved, the true Master keeps watch; in the Crescent-Moon Furnace the Yellow Sprout stirs, in the Seven-Jewel Grove Jade Liquid flows, and a single surge of True Yang pierces all Three Passes. In an instant water and fire meet in Jiji (Already Across), irrigating the Muddy Pellet (niwan) and descending down the Twelve-Storied Tower.

§15Five Watches Daoist Songs (VI) · Second Watch
Original (Chinese)

二更里，心要坚，三尸神，在目前；七情六欲来磨炼，目前仗起青锋剑，倒跨白牛走上山，阳神去把阴兵战；顷刻间水火既济，一霎时撞过三关。

In the second watch, keep the mind steadfast—the Three Corpse spirits stand right before your eyes! The seven emotions and six desires come to temper and test you; right here lift the gleaming blue-steel sword, mount the white ox backward and ride up the mountain, as the Yang Spirit goes forth to battle the hosts of yin. In an instant water and fire meet in Jiji (Already Across), and in the blink of an eye surge straight through the Three Passes.

§16Five Watches Daoist Songs (VI) · Third Watch
Original (Chinese)

三更里，要持行，采先天，兔逢鹰；杳冥之内来真信，铅汞相投归炉鼎，取坎填离入内庭，黄婆用意相勾引；顷刻间水火既济，六月天井底成冰。

In the third watch, hold steady to your practice—gathering Prior Heaven is swift and unerring as a falcon seizing a hare. Out of the dim and obscure comes the true message; Lead and Mercury plunge into each other and return to furnace and cauldron. Draw from Kan to fill Li and enter the inner court, while the Yellow Dame gently guides them with intention. In an instant water and fire meet in Jiji (Already Across), and at the bottom of a well in high summer, ice forms cool and clear.

§17Five Watches Daoist Songs (VI) · Fourth Watch
Original (Chinese)

四更里，用功勤，要高提，智慧灯；方把虎龙擒得定，紫阳双修性与命，散则成炁聚成形，九还七返真人认；顷刻间水火既济，退阴符炼就阳神。

In the fourth watch, work with diligence and lift high the lamp of wisdom. Only then can you capture Tiger and Dragon securely—what Master Ziyang called “dual cultivation” is cultivating inner nature (xing) and life (ming). Dispersed it is Breath (qi), gathered it takes form; through nine returns and seven reversals, recognize the True Person. In an instant water and fire meet in Jiji (Already Across), withdrawing the yin tally to refine the Yang Spirit to completion.

§18Five Watches Daoist Songs (VI) · Fifth Watch
Original (Chinese)

五更里，莫放参，将白牛，赶上山；婴儿出见明珠现，金丹一粒如珠点，收来放去任回还，尸贼锁在空王殿；顷刻间水火既济，百日功老变童颜。

In the fifth watch, never slacken your inquiry; drive the white ox straight up the mountain. The Infant steps forth and the bright pearl appears—one grain of Golden Elixir like a dot of pearl, gathered in or sent out at will, returning freely while the Corpses and Thieves lie locked inside the Hall of the King of Emptiness. In an instant water and fire meet in Jiji (Already Across), and in a hundred days of practice an elder’s face turns youthful as a child’s.

§19Questions and Answers on the Mysterious Mechanism
Original (Chinese)

〔原按〕前见羽流抄本，有三丰先生自问自答二条，极为玄妙，或以为永乐遥问而先生遥答之者，是亦一说也。

(Original Note) Formerly I saw a Daoist manuscript containing two passages of Master Sanfeng asking and answering himself, exceedingly subtle and mysterious. Some hold that the Yongle Emperor posed the question from afar and Master Sanfeng answered from afar—that too is one account.

§20Questions and Answers on the Mysterious Mechanism · The Question
Original (Chinese)

者道人，开口就讲道；我问你，者呼吸谁收谁闭，谁举谁提；父母未生时，者一点真阳动静，在那里修根养蒂，在那里立命安基；你与我讲个元音，明个道理，切莫要错讲糊支。

Hey Daoist, the moment you open your mouth you talk about the Way! Let me ask you: who gathers and closes this breath, who lifts and raises it? Before your parents were born, where does that single point of True Yang in motion and stillness cultivate its root and nourish its stem, where does it establish life and settle its foundation? Speak to me the primal note and make the principle clear—whatever you do, do not misspeak or hem and haw!

§21Questions and Answers on the Mysterious Mechanism · The Answer
Original (Chinese)

那道人，稽首便答道；你看我，者呼吸自收自闭，自举自提；父母未生时，那一点真阳动静，在虚无内修根养蒂，虚无内立命安基；者是咱讲的元音，明的道理，又何必指东猜西。

That Daoist bowed his head and answered: Look at me—this breath gathers and closes of itself, lifts and raises of itself! Before your parents were born, that single point of True Yang in motion and stillness cultivates its root and nourishes its stem within emptiness, and establishes life and settles its foundation within emptiness. This is the primal note I speak and the principle I make clear—what need is there to point east and guess west?

Key Terms

  • Sweeping all clear (yisaoguang): Sweeping everything completely empty, clearing away every clinging and attachment.
  • Mount Beimang: A hill north of Luoyang famous as an ancient burial ground, symbolizing death.
  • Lord of Spring (Dongjun): The deity of spring, or the sun.
  • Fish leap and kites soar: From the Book of Songs (“the kite flies up to Heaven; the fish leaps in the deep”), cited in the Doctrine of the Mean to illustrate that the Way pervades everywhere.
  • Essence of the Two and the Five: The essence of the two breaths (yin and yang) and the Five Phases.
  • Making it bright through spirit (shen er ming zhi): From the Book of Changes, Great Treatise Part I: “Making it bright through spirit depends upon the person.”
  • Limitless and Supreme Ultimate (wuji, taiji): From Zhou Dunyi’s Explanation of the Diagram of the Supreme Ultimate: “The Limitless and yet the Supreme Ultimate.”
  • Spirit Mountain (Lingshan): Vulture Peak, where the Buddha preached, standing for the Buddha-realm.
  • Demon of sleep: Torpor and drowsiness during quiet sitting.
  • Hall of the King of Emptiness: “King of Emptiness” is an epithet of the Buddha; a metaphor for the realm of serene emptiness.
  • Primal note (yuanyin): The fundamental, root principle.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Do not mistake body and mind for the True Person. Both body and thoughts are constantly changing; the true “I” is not located inside them—this is the very first sweep of Sweeping All Clear.
  2. Cling not to traces, stick not to words. Every scripture, diagram, and metaphor is a finger pointing at the moon; once you see the moon, put down the finger. This is also the finest attitude with which to read this book: never cling literally to all its Lead, Mercury, Dragons, and Tigers.
  3. Tranquil awake, tranquil asleep. The real fruit of cultivation shows up ultimately in abiding tranquility and constant wakefulness.
  4. Battle the demon of sleep first. The most common obstacle in quiet sitting is torpor; Partridge Sky calls overcoming it “the very first whip of cultivation.”
  5. The breath gathers and closes of itself. The answer in Questions and Answers on the Mysterious Mechanism is wonderfully plain: do not forcefully manipulate the breath—let it move naturally; resting quietly within emptiness is itself cultivating the root and establishing life.

Translator's Notes

  1. The twelve lyrics of Sweeping All Clear are titled Twelve Daoist Songs of Sweeping All Clear in the original edition.
  2. “Brimming full, your whole bosom is springtime”: paipai (拍拍) describes the feeling of overflowing fullness.
  3. “The Lord of Spring laughs at how narrow people’s vision is”: rendered verbatim from the original text.
  4. “Know all under Heaven with quiet certainty”: beneath 把握 (“certainty/grasp”) the original prints a pronunciation note reading 握 as wù so that it rhymes with 误 (wù) and 户 (hù).
  5. “Qin burned the books and Han wrote commentaries… commenting killed the classics while burning kept the books alive”: means that an overgrowth of scholastic commentary buries the living meaning of the classics.
  6. “Inner nature is principle, life is vitality’s feeling”: this volume compares inner nature (xing) to principle (li) and life (ming) to feeling/vital impulse (qing).
  7. “In vain do people keep fasts and chant Maitreya’s name”: the original carries a note reading 弥勒 (Mílè) as Míliù to rhyme with 佛 (fó/fú).
  8. “What Master Ziyang called ‘dual cultivation’ is inner nature and life”: Zhang Boduan (Perfected Person Ziyang) championed the dual cultivation of inner nature and existence (xing and ming); this line states explicitly that “dual cultivation” means inner nature and life.
  9. Questions and Answers on the Mysterious Mechanism: the original note is by Li Xiyue. Throughout the text, 者 (zhě) stands for 这 (“this”), as noted in the original edition.
  10. “At the bottom of a well in high summer, ice forms”: symbolizes the cool, lucid clarity of mind when fire phasing reaches maturity.
  11. “Drive the white ox straight up the mountain”: the white ox draws on the “great white-ox cart” parable of the Lotus Sutra, signifying the supreme vehicle.
  12. This chapter closes Volume Four of the Complete Works of Zhang Sanfeng. Volume Five (Cloud and Water Collection) and subsequent literary volumes are not included in this selection.
