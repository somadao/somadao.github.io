V · Essentials of the Mystery (Part Two)

Song of Conjunction at the Mysterious Pass · Song of the Meeting of Yin and Yang · Clear Chants of the Grotto-Heaven · Moon Over the West River

Song of Conjunction at the Mysterious Pass, Song of the Meeting of Yin and Yang, Clear Chants of the Grotto-Heaven, and Moon Over the West River — inverting Kan and Li; hiding in the market to practice stillness; the Way lies in the single Mysterious Pass

About This Chapter

  • Song of Conjunction at the Mysterious Pass and Song of the Meeting of Yin and Yang: Two short lyrics whose titles use “conjunction” (jiaogou) and “meeting” (jiaohui) to speak entirely of the union of Kan and Li, Lead and Mercury within one’s own body: “inverting Li within Kan,” “Li returns to Kan, Kan returns to Li,” and “yin draws yang Essence to take Kan’s fullness; yang subdues yin Essence to replenish Li’s emptiness”—what inner alchemists call “taking Kan to fill Li.”
  • Clear Chants of the Grotto-Heaven (Dongtian qingchang): The tune title comes from Qiu Chuji’s Song of the Azure Sky: “Day and night, clear tones fill the grotto-heaven.” Five stanzas (out of six originally; the second is omitted) describe splitting open primordial chaos, subduing the Five Thieves, hiding in the dusty market to practice quiet work silently, gathering Lead to pair with Mercury, and completing the Seven Returns and Nine Reversions. The fourth stanza—“I only wait to hide in the dusty market and silently practice quiet work; I only wait to choose a fitting place before putting it into action”—again states the sequence of refining the self amid ordinary life before choosing a secluded spot for deeper work.
  • Moon Over the West River (Xijiang yue): A short lyric that summarizes the entire Way in interlocking chain-verse (dingzhen): “The Way lies in the single Mysterious Pass; the Pass encloses Original Breath and Original Essence; Original Essence and Original Breath nourish Original Spirit; when Spirit is full, movement and stillness arise naturally…”

Note: In the original text, A Formula of Heaven’s Secret follows Fine Golden Flower, Clear Chants of the Grotto-Heaven includes a second stanza, and two Lyrics of the Hall of Spring Beauty precede Moon Over the West River. Because these are framed primarily in the idiom of the yin-yang dual-cultivation school, they are omitted in keeping with this site’s editorial policy.

Original Text and Translation

§1Song of Conjunction at the Mysterious Pass
Original (Chinese)

大道本无说，妙理话难彻，玄关一点达摩诀；上至昆仑泥丸顶，下至重渊涌泉穴；铅为母，汞为爹，协的铅汞阴阳把子结，姹女婴儿一处歇。

The Great Way is essentially beyond words; its wondrous principle cannot be exhausted in speech—the single point of the Mysterious Pass is Bodhidharma’s oral secret. Above, it reaches the Kunlun summit of the Muddy Pellet; below, it descends to the deep abyss of the Bubbling Spring cavity. Lead is mother, Mercury is father; harmonizing Lead and Mercury, yin and yang, they conceive the child, and Fair Maiden and Infant rest together in one place.

§2Song of the Meeting of Yin and Yang
Original (Chinese)

颠倒坎中离，龙虎风云会；妙玄一点包天地，毫厘大小人不知，返三回五透天机；离归坎，坎归离，坎离水火运东西，婴儿姹女作夫妻；阴盗阳精取坎实，阳伏阴精补离虚，三三六六分天地；这些功夫非容易，变乾坤，漏泄先天炁。

Inverting Li within Kan, Dragon and Tiger meet like wind and clouds. One wondrous, mysterious point encloses heaven and earth—so tiny or vast that people do not know it; returning three and turning five reveals Heaven’s mechanism. Li returns to Kan, Kan returns to Li; water and fire of Kan and Li circulate between east and west, and Infant and Fair Maiden become husband and wife. Yin draws out yang Essence, taking the fullness within Kan; yang subdues yin Essence, replenishing the emptiness within Li; three-three and six-six divide heaven and earth. Such work is no easy matter—it transforms Qian and Kun and discloses the Breath of Prior Heaven.

§3Clear Chants of the Grotto-Heaven · Six Stanzas
Original (Chinese)

〔原按〕邱祖青天歌云「昼夜清音满洞天」，盖指独露全真时也，调名取此。

(Original Note) Patriarch Qiu’s Song of the Azure Sky says, “Day and night, clear tones fill the grotto-heaven,” referring to the moment when Complete Reality stands alone and revealed. The tune title is taken from this.

§4Clear Chants of the Grotto-Heaven · First Stanza
Original (Chinese)

俺则待剖开混沌包，劈破鸿蒙窍；俺则待觅一滴续命浆，寻一枝返魂草；俺只见虎啸炁来潮，癸动药生苗；会黄婆将琴鼓教，见金公把竹板敲；不觉的丹田热烧，原来是命宝归黄道；那时节把神息匀调，静观水火交。

I only wait to split open the pouch of primordial chaos and cleave apart the opening of undifferentiated antiquity; I only wait to seek one drop of life-extending nectar and find one sprig of soul-returning herb. I only see the Tiger roar as Breath surges like the tide, and Gui-water stir as the Medicine sprouts its seedling. Meeting the Yellow Dame who teaches while strumming the zither, seeing the Metal Lord tapping bamboo clappers—unawares, the Cinnabar Field burns warm: so the treasure of life has returned to the Yellow Path! At that moment regulate Spirit and breath evenly, and quietly watch water and fire unite.

§5Clear Chants of the Grotto-Heaven · Third Stanza
Original (Chinese)

炼己将五贼平，换景把七情并；制伏的六欲宁，扫荡的三尸净；性定自通灵，心虚内照明；配刚柔，金水称；会阴阳，颠倒行；既济功成，万神悉听命；闻诏飞升，丹台阶已注名。

In refining the self, pacify the Five Thieves; transforming the scene, gather in the seven emotions; subdue the six desires until they are calm; sweep out the Three Corpses until all is clean. When inner nature is settled, spiritual penetration comes of itself; when the mind is empty, inner illumination shines bright. Pairing the firm and the yielding, Metal and Water are evenly matched; uniting yin and yang, move them in inversion. When the work of Already Across (Jiji) is accomplished, ten thousand spirits all obey command; hearing the summons, soar upward—your name is already inscribed upon the Cinnabar Terrace.

§6Clear Chants of the Grotto-Heaven · Fourth Stanza
Original (Chinese)

俺只待隐市尘，默静功；俺只待择善地，方作用；俺只待仗慧剑，将白雪培，凭匠手把黄芽种；俺只待搬火炼真空，寻光破鸿蒙；俺只待摄二气归离户，采一阳入震宫；学旌阳行踪，冲举乘丹凤；倣轩辕神通，飞升跨火龙。

I only wait to hide in the dusty market and silently practice quiet work; I only wait to choose a fitting place before putting it into action. I only wait to wield the sword of wisdom to bank up the White Snow, and by a craftsman’s hand plant the Yellow Sprout. I only wait to move the fire to refine True Emptiness, seeking the Light to shatter primordial chaos. I only wait to gather the two breaths back to the Gate of Li, and harvest the single yang into the Palace of Zhen. Following the footsteps of Xu Jingyang, I ascend in broad daylight riding a cinnabar phoenix; emulating the spiritual power of the Yellow Emperor, I soar aloft astride a fire dragon.

§7Clear Chants of the Grotto-Heaven · Fifth Stanza
Original (Chinese)

采铅精，配汞苗，立炉鼎，修玄要；夺乾坤造化机，会日月盈亏妙；合水火左右烧，使魂魄往来交；按四时不失序，顺八节应卦爻；辨昏朝，明消长，观天道，须教周天十二遭。

Gather the Essence of Lead, pair it with the seedling of Mercury, set up furnace and tripod, and cultivate the essentials of the mystery. Seize the creative mechanism of Qian and Kun; comprehend the wonder of sun and moon waxing and waning. Unite water and fire to heat from left and right; make ethereal soul (hun) and corporeal soul (po) go back and forth in union. Follow the four seasons without losing order; accord with the eight solar nodes to match the hexagram lines. Discern dusk and dawn, understand waxing and waning, observe the Way of Heaven—and make the Celestial Circuit turn twelve full rounds.

§8Clear Chants of the Grotto-Heaven · Sixth Stanza
Original (Chinese)

七返火珠明了性，九还金液大丹成；霹雳一声，早见真人出顶。

At the Seven Returns, the fiery pearl illuminates inner nature completely; at the Nine Reversions, the Great Elixir of Golden Fluid is accomplished. With a single clap of thunder, the True Person is already seen emerging from the crown of the head.

§9Moon Over the West River
Original (Chinese)

道在玄关一窍，窍包元炁元精；元精元炁养元神，神满自然动静；动静三回九转，九转变化乾坤；乾坤颠倒种仙根，根发西江月正。

The Way lies in the single opening of the Mysterious Pass; the opening encloses Original Breath and Original Essence. Original Essence and Original Breath nourish Original Spirit; when Spirit is full, movement and stillness arise naturally. Through movement and stillness, three turns and nine cycles; nine cycles transform Qian and Kun. Inverting Qian and Kun plants the immortal root—and the root sprouts just as the moon stands full over the West River.

Key Terms

  • Taking Kan to fill Li (取坎填离): The single yang inside Kan is fullness, and the single yin inside Li is emptiness; taking the yang of Kan to replenish the yin of Li restores pure Qian. See conjunction of Kan and Li.
  • Bodhidharma’s oral secret (达摩诀): Borrows the name of the Chan patriarch Bodhidharma to refer to the mind-method.
  • Yellow Dame and Metal Lord (黄婆、金公): True Intention and True Lead; see Yellow Dame (True Intention).
  • Gui (癸): Gui-water, symbolizing the first stirring of Prior Heaven water.
  • Gate of Li and Palace of Zhen (离户、震宫): Li stands for the heart-mind; Zhen stands for eastern wood, where the first yang is born.
  • Jingyang (旌阳): Xu Xun (Xu Jingyang), who according to legend ascended to heaven together with his entire household.
  • Moon Over the West River (西江月): A lyric tune title; here it also alludes to western metal-water and the moment when the moon is full.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The conjunction of Kan and Li happens within a single body. “Conjunction” and “husband and wife” in these songs are metaphors for the water and fire of Kan and Li—the union of heart-spirit and kidney-breath.
  2. First hide in the market, then choose a fitting place. The order in the fourth stanza matches the whole book: begin by practicing quietly in the midst of ordinary life.
  3. Original Essence, Original Breath, and Original Spirit interlock ring by ring. The chain-verse of Moon Over the West River shows the natural sequence: Essence nourishes Breath, Breath nourishes Spirit, and once Spirit is full, movement and stillness unfold of themselves—with no need to force anything.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Song of Conjunction at the Mysterious Pass” and “Song of the Meeting of Yin and Yang”: The titles employ standard inner-alchemical metaphors whose substance concerns Kan-water and Li-fire within the body; this site translates them accordingly.
  2. “Lead is mother, Mercury is father”: So in the original text, reversing the more usual alchemical convention that calls Lead the father (Metal Lord) and Mercury the mother (Fair Maiden); retained verbatim.
  3. “Harmonizing Lead and Mercury, yin and yang, they conceive the child”: The characters xie de (协的) are printed as small padding characters in the original, meaning to bring Lead and Mercury, yin and yang, into union.
  4. “Patriarch Qiu’s Song of the Azure Sky”: Qiu Chuji (Perfected Master Changchun), one of the Seven Masters of Quanzhen, author of the Song of the Azure Sky (Qingtian ge).
  5. “Three-three and six-six divide heaven and earth”: Standard inner-alchemical numerology; retained literally.
  6. “Emulating the spiritual power of the Yellow Emperor, I soar aloft astride a fire dragon”: The original writes 倣 as a variant of 仿 (“to emulate”).
