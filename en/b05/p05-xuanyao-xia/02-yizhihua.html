V · Essentials of the Mystery (Part Two)

A Spray of Blossoms · Chant of the Hibernating Dragon · Fine Golden Flower

A Spray of Blossoms, On the Hibernating Dragon Method, Chant of the Hibernating Dragon, and Fine Golden Flower — an autobiographical account of seeking the Way, sleep cultivation, and the Golden Flower

About This Chapter

  • A Spray of Blossoms (Yizhi hua): The tune title comes from Bai Yuchan’s line, “Yellow Sprout at midnight—a single spray of spring.” The first piece describes regulating Original Breath, piercing the Three Passes, ascending to the Muddy Pellet, descending the Twelve-Storied Tower, and returning to the root and restoring life: “all that is needed is to gather that single grain of millet before primordial chaos was divided.” The fourth piece is Zhang Sanfeng’s autobiographical account: casting aside fame and abandoning family estate, entering Mount Zhongnan in his sixties or seventies to receive direct oral transmission from Master Fire Dragon, descending the mountain to seek companions, meeting Shen Wansan, refining the elixir through nine cycles, gazing at the wall on Mount Wudang for nine years, and later—when summoned by the imperial court—“riding the wind and mounting a simurgh up to the Nine Heavens.”
  • On the Hibernating Dragon Method and Chant of the Hibernating Dragon: “Hibernating Dragon” refers to sleep cultivation (shuigong), traditionally traced to Chen Tuan (Chen Tunan). Zhang Sanfeng claims to inherit this lineage: “Sleeping immortal, sleeping immortal, lying high on a stone root, forgetting the years.” Though seemingly asleep, in truth “I sleep, yet in sleep I have not slept”—the body sleeps while the mind remains awake, “though the hibernating dragon sleeps, in sleep it is awake.”
  • Fine Golden Flower (Mei jinhua): The tune title derives from the Cantong qi, “The Yellow Emperor praised the Golden Flower.” “Golden Flower” is a metaphor for True Lead. This piece shows that the true Golden Fluid Reverted Elixir has nothing to do with blind cultivation; one must “slay all the seven emotions, six desires, and entanglements of affection, and sweep away ten thousand miles of floating clouds until not a speck of dust remains.”

Note: The second and third pieces of A Spray of Blossoms and the first piece of Fine Golden Flower are framed primarily in the idiom of the yin-yang dual-cultivation school and are omitted in keeping with this site’s editorial policy.

Original Text and Translation

§1A Spray of Blossoms
Original (Chinese)

〔原按〕一作美金花，按：玉蟾翁快活歌云「黄芽半夜一枝春」，本集后五更云「一枝春色金花丽」，盖取此为调名也，词照彭好古选本、张邓刻本及神仙鉴参订。

(Original Note) Also titled Fine Golden Flower. Master Bai Yuchan’s Song of Carefree Joy says, “Yellow Sprout at midnight—a single spray of spring,” and the Five Watches songs later in this collection say, “A single spray of spring splendor, the Golden Flower radiant”—the tune title was likely taken from these lines. The text has been collated against Peng Haogu’s anthology, the Zhang and Deng editions, and the Mirror of Immortals (Shenxian jian).

§2
Original (Chinese)

行持造化功，下手调元气；自从师传后，独自守无为；玉液长提，元炁归真位；透三关，过尾闾，逆转河车上泥丸，撞昆仑发震如雷；甘泉香生甜如蜜，入玉池化作金液，逍遥饮甘露自垂；下重楼十二阶梯，牢封固护守坚持；原来是精炁神，三般儿归根复命，原来是金木水火土，五行攒簇；玄中玄，有不死还丹；妙中妙，有接命的根基。

Practicing the work of creation, begin by regulating Original Breath. Ever since receiving the master’s transmission, I have kept alone to non-action (wuwei). Continuously drawing up the Jade Fluid, Original Breath returns to its true seat. Piercing the Three Passes and crossing the Caudal Funnel, reversing the River Chariot up to the Muddy Pellet, it strikes Kunlun with a rumble like thunder. A fragrant sweet spring wells up as sweet as honey, enters the Jade Pool and turns into Golden Fluid; carefree, I drink the Sweet Dew as it descends of itself. Down the twelve steps of the Twelve-Storied Tower, seal it fast, guard it securely, and hold firm with perseverance. So it is simply Essence, Breath, and Spirit—these three returning to the root and restoring life; so it is simply metal, wood, water, fire, and earth—the Five Phases gathered into one cluster. Mystery within mystery: here lies the deathless Reverted Elixir; wonder within wonder: here lies the foundation for prolonging life.

§3
Original (Chinese)

谁不行，谁不会，谁不做，都只在采取先天一窍迷；怎肯胡为，俺也向花丛中，敲竹鼓琴心似水；从今参透真消息，忘物忘形，子前午后可为持，卯酉之中沐浴时；讲甚么生死轮回，说甚么姹女婴儿，都只要采取，鸿蒙未判一粒黍米。

Who does not practice, who does not claim to know, who does not make the attempt? Yet all go astray solely over “gathering from the single Prior Heaven opening.” How could I ever act recklessly? Even amid a cluster of blossoms, tapping bamboo and strumming the zither, my mind remains like still water. From this day on, having pierced through to the true tidings, forgetting things and forgetting form, before Zi and after Wu one may maintain the work, while Mao and You are the hours of ablution. Why speak of birth, death, and rebirth? Why talk of Fair Maiden and Infant? All that is needed is to gather that single grain of millet from before primordial chaos was divided.

§4A Spray of Blossoms · Fourth Piece
Original (Chinese)

因求大道玄，走尽天涯畔；撇功名势利，弃家业田园；万般辛苦，衣破鞋穿师难面；愁则愁，六七十年光阴短，入终南，感得火龙亲口传；命玄出山，觅侣求铅；遍访名贤，尽是些诡计悭贪窃道玄；也是俺该有那出世因缘，幸遇着仗义疏财沈万三，争奈他力薄难全。

Seeking the mystery of the Great Way, I walked to the very edges of the earth, casting aside fame, rank, and worldly power, abandoning family estate and fields. Through ten thousand hardships, with tattered clothes and worn-through shoes, a true master was hard to meet. My one sorrow was how short sixty or seventy years of time can be—until I entered Mount Zhongnan and moved Master Fire Dragon to impart his direct oral transmission. The master bade me leave the mountains to seek Dao-companions and pursue True Lead. Visiting famed worthies everywhere, I found only scheming, stingy, greedy folk trying to steal the mysteries of the Way. Yet it was my destiny to have the karmic affinity for transcendence: happily I met Shen Wansan, generous with wealth and loyal to righteousness—alas, his strength was too slight to see it through completely.

§5
Original (Chinese)

俺只得，把炉火烹煎九转完，向丽春院，采药行符经五载，入武当山，面壁调神又九年；猛听得朝命宣，欲待要不觐君王面，又恐怕胡尚书性命难全；驾云直上朝阳殿，官家见喜悦龙颜；俺欲待口口相传，恐违了玉皇命言，俺只得，跨风乘鸾上九天。

I had no choice but to simmer the furnace fire until all nine cycles were complete: at the Hall of Spring Beauty I gathered the Medicine and practiced the tallies for five years, then entered Mount Wudang to gaze at the wall and regulate Spirit for nine more years. Suddenly came word of an imperial summons. I wished not to appear before the sovereign’s face, yet feared that Minister Hu’s life might not be spared. Mounting a cloud straight up to the Chaoyang Hall, I made the Emperor’s dragon countenance beam with delight. I wished to pass it on word for word, yet feared transgressing the Jade Emperor’s decree—so I could only stride the wind, mount a simurgh, and ascend to the Nine Heavens.

§6On the Hibernating Dragon Method · Fisherman's Lyric
Original (Chinese)

蛰法无声却有声，声声说与内心听；神默默，气冥冥，蛰龙虽睡睡还醒。

The hibernating method is soundless, yet has a sound—every note spoken for the inner heart to hear. Spirit is silent, Breath is dim and deep; though the hibernating dragon sleeps, in sleep it is still awake.

§7Chant of the Hibernating Dragon
Original (Chinese)

睡神仙，睡神仙，石根高卧忘其年，三光沉沦性自圆；气气归玄窍，息息任天然；莫散乱，须安恬，温养得汞性儿圆，等待他铅花儿现；无走失，有防闲，真火候，运中间；行七返，不艰难，炼九还，何嗟叹；静观龙虎战场战，暗把阴阳颠倒颠；人言我是朦胧汉，我却眠兮眠未眠。

Sleeping immortal, sleeping immortal, lying high upon a stone root, forgetting the years; as the Three Lights sink down, inner nature becomes whole of itself. Breath upon breath returns to the Mysterious Opening; inhalation and exhalation are left to spontaneity. Do not scatter in distraction; remain serene and tranquil. Gently nurture the nature of Mercury until it is full and round, and await the appearance of the Lead blossom. With no leakage and with careful guard, True fire phasing turns right in the center. Performing the Seven Returns is not hard; refining the Nine Reversions—why sigh over it? Quietly watch Dragon and Tiger contend on the field of battle, secretly inverting yin and yang upside down. People call me a drowsy, muddled fellow—yet I sleep, and in sleeping have not slept.

§8
Original (Chinese)

学就了，真卧禅，养成了，真胎元，卧龙一起便升天；此蛰法，是谁传，曲肱而枕自尼山，乐在其中无人谙；五龙飞跃出深潭，天将此法传图南；图南一派俦能继，邋遢道人张丰仙。

Having mastered true reclining meditation and nourished the true embryonic origin, the reclining dragon rises once and ascends to heaven. Who handed down this hibernating method? “Bending an arm for a pillow” came from Confucius at Mount Ni—the joy within it no one fathoms. Five dragons leaped forth from a deep pool, and Heaven transmitted this method to Chen Tunan. Who can carry on Tunan’s lineage? None other than the disheveled Daoist, Immortal Zhang Sanfeng.

§9Fine Golden Flower · Two Pieces
Original (Chinese)

〔原按〕题义：按参同云「古记题龙虎，黄帝美金华。」龙虎、金华，皆古圣垂训书也，丹家以金华比真铅，故契云「卒得金华，转而相因；化为白液，凝而至坚。」盖言汞得真铅而化液，入黄庭而成至宝也，又云「铅体外黑，内怀金华。」盖以造丹者，于黑铅之中取出白金，制成戊土名曰美金华，则金华之贵重极矣！调名取此。

(Original Note) On the title: The Cantong qi says, “Ancient records are titled Dragon and Tiger; the Yellow Emperor praised the Golden Flower.” Dragon and Tiger and Golden Flower were both books of instruction handed down by ancient sages. Inner alchemists use “Golden Flower” as a metaphor for True Lead, hence the Cantong qi says: “Suddenly obtaining the Golden Flower, they turn and depend upon one another; transforming into white fluid, it congeals to utmost firmness.” This means that Mercury obtains True Lead, transforms into fluid, and enters the Yellow Court to become the supreme treasure. It also says, “The body of Lead is black outside, yet harbors the Golden Flower within”—meaning that the alchemist extracts white metal from black lead and fashions it into Wu Earth, calling it the “Fine Golden Flower.” How exceedingly precious the Golden Flower is! The tune title is taken from this.

§10Fine Golden Flower · Second Piece
Original (Chinese)

金华玉蕊鲜，世人难分辨；长生须伏气，栽接要真铅，筑基炼己采后天；玉液还丹，纵得了住世延年，还要图金液九还；堪嗤大众学神仙，一个个尽都是盲修瞎炼，一程程谁晓得实究真参；天地里黄芽长遍，满乾坤白华开绽；总待他水尽金生，月正圆，我这里手提宝剑挂南山，诛尽了七情六欲，恩爱牵缠，扫尽了万里浮云，一尘不染，諕的那五鬼三尸心胆寒。

The Golden Flower and jade stamens shine fresh, hard for worldly people to discern. Long life requires subduing the breath; grafting and renewal require True Lead—laying the foundation, refining the self, and gathering from Posterior Heaven. With the Jade Fluid Reverted Elixir, even if you gain long years in the world, you must still aspire to the Golden Fluid Nine Reversions. Laughable are the crowds who study immortality: every single one engages in blind cultivation and reckless practice; stage by stage, who understands real investigation and genuine inquiry? Across heaven and earth Yellow Sprouts grow everywhere; throughout Qian and Kun white blossoms burst open. Simply wait until water is exhausted, metal is born, and the moon is perfectly full. Here I lift my precious sword and hang it on South Mountain, slaying all seven emotions, six desires, and entanglements of affection, sweeping clear ten thousand miles of floating clouds until not a single speck of dust remains—terrifying the Five Ghosts and Three Corpses until their hearts and gallbladders run cold.

§11
Original (Chinese)

我把阴阳颠倒颠，用神机暗合周天；戒身心防危虑险，轻轻的摇动地轴，慢慢的拨转天关，霹雳一声天外天。

I invert yin and yang upside down, using the spiritual mechanism to accord secretly with the Celestial Circuit. Guarding body and mind, vigilant against danger and peril, I gently rock the Earth’s axis and slowly turn the Celestial Pass—one clap of thunder, and heaven opens beyond heaven!

Key Terms

  • Jade Fluid and Golden Fluid (玉液、金液): The Jade Fluid Reverted Elixir corresponds to the stage of laying the foundation and refining the self, while the Golden Fluid Reverted Elixir is the further stage of the Great Elixir; see Jade Fluid (Sweet Dew) and Golden Fluid (Sweet Dew).
  • Twelve-Storied Tower (重楼): The trachea/throat, said by inner alchemists to have twelve rings; see the Twelve-Storied Tower.
  • Grain of Millet (黍米): The elixir-seed condensed from the One Breath of Prior Heaven; see Golden Flower (jinhua).
  • Hibernating Dragon Method (蛰龙法): Sleep cultivation (shuigong), traditionally attributed to Chen Tuan; like a dragon hibernating in sleep, it symbolizes the body at rest while Spirit is gathered within.
  • Bending an arm for a pillow (曲肱而枕): From the Analects (“Shu’er”): “Eating coarse food, drinking water, and bending an arm for a pillow—joy is right in the midst of this.”
  • Golden Flower (金华): Metaphor for True Lead, from the Cantong qi.
  • Five Ghosts and Three Corpses (五鬼三尸): Metaphors for the various desires and cravings within the body.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Sleep too is cultivation. The essence of the Hibernating Dragon Method is “body asleep, mind awake”: even when lying down, let the breath remain natural and the mind free from distraction—“breath upon breath returns to the Mysterious Opening; inhalation and exhalation are left to spontaneity.”
  2. Blind cultivation is a major taboo. “Every single one engages in blind cultivation and reckless practice”—practicing by imagination alone without understanding the principles or seeking a true teacher is something Zhang Sanfeng criticizes again and again.
  3. Being unstained by a single speck of dust is real work. Sever the seven emotions, six desires, and entanglements of affection so that no floating clouds linger over the mind.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Master Bai Yuchan’s Song of Carefree Joy”: Bai Yuchan, Fifth Patriarch of the Southern Lineage, author of the Song of Carefree Joy (Kuaihuo ge).
  2. “Even amid a cluster of blossoms, tapping bamboo and strumming the zither, my mind remains like still water”: Meaning to remain amid bustling worldly places while keeping the mind like still water—the same idea as “refining the self amid the dusty world” in Pointing Out Delusions on Ascending to Heaven.
  3. “Happily I met Shen Wansan, generous with wealth and loyal to righteousness”: Shen Wansan, a legendary Jiangnan millionaire of the late Yuan and early Ming, said in folklore to have supported Zhang Sanfeng.
  4. “At the Hall of Spring Beauty I gathered the Medicine and practiced the tallies for five years”: Lichun yuan (“Hall of Spring Beauty”) originally referred to bustling pleasure quarters or brothels. The pure-stillness school generally understands this as mingling with the ordinary world in the most distracting environments in order to refine the mind—matching “amid a cluster of blossoms, tapping bamboo and strumming the zither, my mind remains like still water” above; this site follows that reading.
  5. “Yet feared that Minister Hu’s life might not be spared”: Refers to the legend that the Yongle Emperor (Ming Chengzu) sent Minister of Rites Hu Ying for many years to search for Zhang Sanfeng.
  6. “From Confucius at Mount Ni”: Mount Ni was Confucius’s birthplace and serves as a metonym for Confucius.
  7. “Five dragons leaped forth from a deep pool, and Heaven transmitted this method to Chen Tunan”: Refers to the legend that Chen Tuan received the sleep-cultivation method from five dragons.
  8. “Suddenly obtaining the Golden Flower, they turn and depend upon one another; transforming into white fluid, it congeals to utmost firmness”: From the Zhouyi cantong qi.
  9. “Terrifying the Five Ghosts and Three Corpses until their hearts and gallbladders run cold”: The character 諕 (xià) is a variant of 吓 (“to frighten”).
