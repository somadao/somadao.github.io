Complete Works of Zhang Sanfeng (Selected Neidan Writings)

三丰全集（丹道选） · Re-edited by Li Hanxu (Qing) · Religious Culture Publishing House (Cai Congzhe punctuated edition) · 2013

The Complete Works of Zhang Sanfeng is an eight-volume collection of Zhang Sanfeng’s writings re-edited in the Daoguang reign of the Qing dynasty by Li Hanxu (Li Xiyue, 1806–1856), founder of the Western school of inner alchemy. This site selects only the sections on inner alchemy: from Volume Three, the Treatise on the Great Way, Plain Talks on the Mysterious Mechanism, and Sayings on the Way; and from Volume Four, the Essentials of the Mystery (including the famous twenty-four songs of the Rootless Tree), together with a biographical introduction to Zhang Sanfeng written by this site. The two essays on fire phasing, the Song of Quiet Sitting, the Plain Words on the Way, and the Collected Sayings of Master Sanfeng are already included in Book Two (Essential Guide to Entering Alchemy Work) and are linked here.

How to read

  1. Every chapter follows the same order: About This Chapter → Original Text and Translation → Key Terms → Putting It into Practice → Translator's Notes. The classical Chinese original is folded away; you can read the translation alone and follow everything.
  2. This book has no notes by Qiu Ling; the chapter introductions and modern translations are by this site. The closing commentaries from the original edition are translated as “Original Commentary” blocks.
  3. The source edition is printed in vertical traditional Chinese; this site converts the original text to simplified Chinese while following the punctuated edition.
  4. Terms such as “of the same kind” or “self and other” in Zhang Sanfeng’s writings have long been read in different ways. Consistent with the earlier books on this site, they are translated according to the solitary pure-stillness method (yin and yang within one’s own body). Passages marked with a safety note are for understanding only; do not imitate them on your own.
