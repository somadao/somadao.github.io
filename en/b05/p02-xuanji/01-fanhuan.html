II · Plain Talks on the Mysterious Mechanism

On the Verifications of Reversion and Return

On the Verifications of Reversion and Return — the experiential signs along the path of Seven Reversions and Nine Returns, from the Lesser Celestial Circuit of the Jade Liquid Returning Elixir to the Golden Liquid Great Elixir and the departure of the Yang Spirit

About This Chapter

Plain Talks on the Mysterious Mechanism is the second section of Volume Three of the Complete Works of Zhang Sanfeng, comprising ten pieces in all. The opening two pieces (On Fire Phasing in Refining the Elixir) and the appended Song of Sitting in Meditation are already included in Book Two with Qiu Ling’s commentary, so they are not repeated here; please read them directly:

  • On Fire Phasing in Refining the Elixir (I) — from sweeping away stray thoughts to the work of ten months
  • On Fire Phasing in Refining the Elixir (II) — three stages of work within a quarter of an hour
  • Song of Sitting in Meditation — when you first sit in meditation and learn to practice Chan, the news lies in the Mysterious Pass

This piece, On the Verifications of Reversion and Return, follows immediately after the essays on fire phasing and deals with “verifications” (zhengyan): what phenomena appear in the body at each stage of the work. The entire text may be read in three parts:

  1. Jade Liquid Returning Elixir (laying the foundation and refining the self): Send the Original Spirit of the upper cavity down into the Sea of Breath below to unite with the Original Breath of the lower cavity. Kan and Li then conjoin spontaneously and Embryonic Breathing settles of itself; this is the fire phasing of the Lesser Celestial Circuit. Once the work matures, even the five grains one eats are transformed into breath to generate essence; finally essence is full, breath is full, and spirit is whole—this is “the initial stage of long life and enduring vision.”
  2. Golden Liquid Great Elixir (entering the chamber): One must first refine the self amid worldly life, and only then seek a secluded place with fellow cultivators standing guard before entering the chamber. The phenomena described in this section are the most intense: the whole body feels as though burned by fire or bursting open like parched beans; water wells up from the Lianquan points beneath the tongue; visions of dragon and tiger, immortals and buddhas appear in the void; and for three days “spirits wail and ghosts weep” on all sides. Zhang Sanfeng repeats his warning: do not identify with any phenomenon, and do not be afraid.
  3. Shedding the embryo and spiritual transformation: Once the Great Medicine enters the mouth, “only then do you know that my destiny rests not with Heaven”; thereafter come the transformations at a hundred days, six months, ten months, and twelve months, all the way to the Yang Spirit going out and returning and walking beneath sun and moon without casting a shadow.

Safety Note: Most of what this essay describes belongs to the highest stages that the ancients called the Golden Liquid Great Elixir and entering the chamber, and several expressions (such as “three thousand jade maidens waiting in attendance” or “a chicken eating it becomes a phoenix”) are clearly figurative and hyperbolic. Read this only for understanding; do not take these phenomena as goals to pursue, and never attempt solitary seclusion, fasting, or intense practices on your own. If palpitations, visual or auditory hallucinations, or prolonged insomnia occur during quiet sitting, stop immediately and seek help from an experienced teacher and a physician. Zhang Sanfeng himself insists repeatedly here: when phenomena appear, “you must never take them for real.”

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

七返九还之法，下手兴功，先将上窍阳里真阴，入内金鼎气海之中，与下窍真阳配合，阳里真阴即是自家元神，属三魂，下窍真阳即是身中元气，属七魄，其先后二气一合，则坎离自交，魂魄混合，神气凝结，胎息自定，每日如外夫妇交情美快，切不可着他，水火自然既济，发运四肢，如外火之生，焰焰相似，只要水火均平，此是小周天火候，调和薰蒸，喉息倒回元海，则外阳自然入内，真火自然上冲，浑身酥软，美快无穷，腹内如活龙动转升降，一日有数十样变化，婴儿姹女自然成合，此是采阴补阳一节，修炼玉液还丹，即筑基炼己积内法财，终日逍遥，昼夜常明，乃长生久视之初阶也。

In the method of Seven Reversions and Nine Returns, when first putting your hand to the work, first send the “True Yin within Yang” of the upper cavity down into the inner golden tripod, the Sea of Breath, to unite with the True Yang of the lower cavity. The “True Yin within Yang” is one’s own Original Spirit, which belongs to the three ethereal souls (hun); the “True Yang of the lower cavity” is the Original Breath within the body, which belongs to the seven corporeal souls (po). As soon as these two breaths—Prior Heaven and Posterior Heaven—unite, Kan and Li conjoin spontaneously, the ethereal and corporeal souls mingle as one, spirit and breath congeal, and Embryonic Breathing settles of itself.

At this time there arises each day a sensation of deep delight, like the union of husband and wife in the outer world—yet you must never cling to it. Water and fire naturally achieve mutual completion (jiji), and warmth radiates into the four limbs like an outer fire blazing up in flames; you need only keep water and fire evenly balanced. This is the fire phasing of the Lesser Celestial Circuit. With such harmonizing and steaming, the breath in the throat turns back into the Original Sea, outer yang naturally withdraws inward, and True Fire naturally surges upward. The whole body feels meltingly supple and filled with boundless bliss; inside the abdomen it is as though a living dragon turns, rises, and descends, undergoing dozens of shifts within a single day. The Infant and Maiden unite spontaneously—this is the stage called “gathering yin to replenish yang” (both yin and yang here being within one’s own body), which is the cultivation of the Jade Liquid Returning Elixir: laying the foundation, refining the self, and accumulating inner “dharma wealth.” From this point on one is carefree all day long and bright day and night; this is the initial stage of long life and enduring vision.

Original (Chinese)

世人常借五谷养命，数日不食则气饥死矣。若人年老下元亏损，骨髓俱空，不能胜五谷之气，是五谷能养人亦能杀人也。若会内外交接，水火既济，气血逆流，五脏气合，脾胃开畅，食入腹中亦能化气生精养神，人果能得下手天机，直候骨髓盈满，腹脐如孕妇人一般，却不是有胎形相，不过是炁满精盈神全而已，果如三全，则真火煅炼，调神炼虚，大丈夫功成名遂之时也。

People in the world always rely on the five grains to sustain life; go a few days without eating and they starve to death. Yet when a person grows old, with the lower origin depleted and the bone marrow emptied out, they can no longer bear even the qi of the five grains—showing that the five grains can nourish a person and can also kill a person. If one knows how to join inner and outer so that water and fire complete each other, qi and blood reverse their flow, the breaths of the five viscera unite, and the spleen and stomach open smoothly, then even the food entering the belly transforms into breath, generates essence, and nourishes spirit.

If one truly obtains the celestial mechanism for beginning the work and continues until the bone marrow is brimming full and the abdomen around the navel swells like that of a pregnant woman—yet there is no physical embryo at all, merely breath full, essence brimming, and spirit whole—once these “Three Completions” are truly attained, one tempers them with True Fire, harmonizes the spirit, and refines emptiness. That is the hour when a person of resolve achieves full accomplishment.

Original (Chinese)

奉劝学人，参访宗师苦求至人，抉破一身内外，天机明白，下手速修炼己待时，候一阳至，择地入室，炼此龙虎大丹，必要僻静，须鸡犬不闻之处，外边又要知音道友，不要一个闲杂人来到，恐防惊散元神，先言和光同尘，今言僻静处，何也？盖炼己于尘俗，养炁是于山林，入室兴功，下手之时也。要超凡入圣，岂是小可的事，必须要一尘不染，万虑俱忘，丝毫无挂，一刀两断，永作他乡之客，终无退悔之心，须先持空炼神，守虚炼性，浑身五脏筋骨气血，都化成青气，专心致志，演神纯熟成形受使，星回斗转随心所变，直养得浑身无有皱纹如蜘蛛相似，上七窍生光，昼夜常明，身如太虚，才是正时候，方可求仙道，这应验炁满神全，法财广大，方可炼大丹，才叫做一个丹客。

I earnestly urge students of the Way: seek out true masters and earnestly entreat realized sages until the celestial mechanism of both inside and outside your single body is laid bare and clear. Then lose no time in putting your hand to practice—first refine the self, then await the season. When the single yang arrives, choose a site and enter the chamber to refine this Dragon-and-Tiger Great Elixir. The chamber must be secluded, a place where not even the sound of cocks or dogs is heard; outside, you must have kindred Daoist companions standing watch, allowing not a single idle bystander to approach, lest the Original Spirit be startled and scattered.

Earlier I spoke of “tempering one’s light and mingling with the dust”; now I speak of a secluded place—why? Because refining the self is done amid worldly dust, whereas nourishing breath is done in mountain forests; entering the chamber to launch the great work is the moment to act. To transcend the ordinary and enter sagehood—how could that be a trifling affair! One must be unstained by a single speck of dust, utterly forgetful of ten thousand cares, free of the slightest attachment, severing everything with a single stroke of the knife, living forever as a traveler in a foreign land, with never a thought of retreat or regret.

First one must uphold emptiness to refine spirit and guard vacuity to refine inner nature, until the five viscera, sinews, bones, qi, and blood throughout the body all dissolve into green-blue breath. With single-minded devotion, exercise the spirit until it is thoroughly mature, able to take form and obey command, turning stars and revolving the Dipper at the mind’s bidding; nourish it until the skin of the whole body is free of wrinkles and smooth like a spider, the seven upper orifices radiate light, day and night remain ever bright, and the body is like the Great Void. Only then is it the right time; only then may one seek the Way of the immortals. With these verifications—breath full, spirit whole, and dharma wealth abundant—one is finally ready to refine the Great Elixir and worthy of being called a true alchemist.

§4
Original (Chinese)

工夫既得，时候自至，七窍光明，三阳开泰，神剑成形，趁水顺风发火，雷轰电闪，方夺外天机，下手擒拏采吾身外真铅，以龙嫁虎，驱虎就龙，若会攒簇，不失时节，湛然摄起海底之金，即开夹脊，上泥丸，落入水晶宫之内与木汞配合，不过半刻攒簇已定，真火冲入四肢，浑身骨肉，火烧刀割相似最难禁受，就是十分好汉，到此无一分主张，须防危虑险，沐浴身心，水火既济，顷刻间，浑身如炒豆子一般相似一齐爆开，浑身气血都会成形说话，就在身上闹成一堆，舌根下又有两穴，左为丹井，右为石泉，此正是廉泉穴，随骨脉一齐开下，肾水上涌到如外水泉一般，咽纳不完，滋味甚异，比糖蜜更不相同。

Once the work is attained, the hour arrives of itself: the seven orifices shine brightly, the Three Yang open in peace, and the spirit sword takes form. Riding the water and following the wind, one ignites the fire amid thunderclaps and flashes of lightning, and only then seizes the outer celestial mechanism—reaching out to capture and gather the True Lead beyond one’s physical body, marrying the dragon to the tiger and driving the tiger to meet the dragon. If one knows how to gather and cluster without missing the exact season, one serenely draws up the gold from the bottom of the sea, opens the Spine-Straddling Pass, ascends to the Muddy Pellet, and lets it descend into the Crystal Palace to unite with Wood-Mercury. In less than half a double-hour the clustering is settled.

At this point True Fire surges into the four limbs; flesh and bones throughout the body feel as though scorched by fire and sliced by knives, most difficult to endure. Even the staunchest hero has not an ounce of control at this juncture. One must guard against danger and peril, bathe body and mind, and bring water and fire into mutual completion. In an instant, the whole body cracks open all at once like parched beans popping in a pan; qi and blood throughout the body seem to take shape and speak, raising a clamor all over oneself.

Beneath the root of the tongue are two cavities—on the left the Elixir Well, on the right the Stone Spring—which are none other than the Lianquan point. Opening together with the bones and channels, kidney water wells upward like an outdoor spring, more than one can swallow, with an extraordinary savor unlike honey or sugar.

Original (Chinese)

又其至妙者，临炉下手之初，地将产其金莲，天亦垂乎宝露，忽然一点真汞下降，透心如冰凉，即运一点神火，随之攒簇于交感宫内，浑然湛然，如千千战鼓之鸣，万万雷声之吼，又即是自己一身百脉气血变化，休要惊怕，只要踏罡步斗，执剑掌印，这里正是凶恶处，三回九转，降帅召将，如此防顾，于虚空中或见龙虎相交、天地交泰、日月交宫、见众仙诸佛，工夫到此诸境发现，切不可认他，恐著外邪，既认元神，汞铅相投，三日才生大药。

More marvelous still: at the very beginning of approaching the furnace to start the work, the earth is about to bring forth its golden lotus and heaven sends down its precious dew. Suddenly a single drop of True Mercury descends, piercing the heart with an icy chill. Immediately move a spark of spirit-fire to follow it and cluster them together within the Palace of Mutual Resonance, whole and limpid. In the ears it sounds like thousands upon thousands of war drums booming and ten thousand peals of thunder roaring—yet all of this is nothing other than the transformation of the qi and blood in the hundred channels of your own body. Do not be startled or afraid. Simply “pace the Dipper and tread the stars, grasp the sword and hold the seal” (keep the mind-spirit firmly anchored). This is indeed a perilous pass: through three turns and nine revolutions, subduing marshals and summoning generals, watch and guard in this way.

In the void you may see dragon and tiger mating, heaven and earth uniting in peace, sun and moon exchanging palaces, or throngs of immortals and buddhas. When the work reaches this point and all manner of visions appear, you must never take them for real, lest you fall prey to external deviations. Acknowledge only the Original Spirit. Thus mercury and lead join together, and after three days the Great Medicine is born.

Original (Chinese)

三日里最难过，遍世界都是邪境，四面神号鬼哭，八方杀气狼烟，此正是大开关，工夫到此十个九个都吓杀了，心不可有恐怖。盖己虽化成神，却是阴神，阴神最灵能千变万化，诸境为害，他岂肯善善降伏，前人说得好「你会六神通，方才脱生死；你若不会六神通，休想成道混沌。」又至三十时辰二日半，气气相通，气满至极，忽然活泼泼地逆出太阳流珠，脱壳入口，百万龙神尽皆失惊，此是元阳真丹，药入口始知我命不由天也。

Those three days are the hardest to pass through: the whole world is filled with delusive scenes, spirits wailing and ghosts weeping on all four sides, killing airs and beacon smoke in all eight directions—this is precisely the “Great Opening of the Passes.” Nine out of ten practitioners who reach this stage are frightened out of their wits; let there be no terror in your heart.

For although you have already transformed into spirit, it is still the Yin Spirit; the Yin Spirit is exceedingly clever and capable of myriad transformations, conjuring every sort of harmful apparition—how would it meekly submit? As the ancients put it well: “Only when you master the six spiritual penetrations do you escape birth and death; if you do not master the six spiritual penetrations, do not dream of attaining the Dao in primal chaos.”

After another thirty double-hours—two and a half days—breath communicates with breath until breath reaches its utmost fullness. Suddenly, vibrantly alive, the Flowing Pearl of the Great Yang rises in reverse, sheds its shell, and enters the mouth, astonishing a million dragon-deities. This is the True Elixir of Original Yang; the moment the medicine enters the mouth, one knows for the first time that “my destiny rests not with Heaven.”

古仙云「这回大死今方活」，又云「一战而天下平」，即是此等地位，这才是天地交泰，日月交宫，真阳之药到口，顷刻周天火发，骨胎化作一堆肉泥，阳神脱体，撒手无碍，专心致志，持空养虚，以空养神，以虚养心，随心变神。夫万物皆天地生发，吾万神朝拜而宾服，厌居尘世，逍遥蓬岛，自有三千玉女奉侍，终日蟠桃会上饮仙酒、戴仙花，四大醺醺，浑身彻底玲珑，海底龟蛇出现，万神受使，才是真铅真汞颠倒，浑身紫雾毫光，瑞气千条，此是五龙大蛰法也。

The ancient immortals said, “Having died the great death this time, only now do I live,” and again, “In a single battle the world is pacified”—they were speaking of this very station. Only now do heaven and earth truly unite in peace and sun and moon exchange palaces. The moment the medicine of True Yang reaches the mouth, the Celestial Circuit fire blazes up in an instant, the skeletal frame melts like a heap of soft clay, and the Yang Spirit leaves the body, letting go without hindrance.

Thereafter, with single-minded devotion, uphold emptiness and nourish vacuity: nourish spirit with emptiness, nourish mind with vacuity, and transform spirit at the mind’s will. All things are generated by heaven and earth, while my myriad inner spirits come to pay homage and submit. Weary of dwelling in the dusty world, roaming freely on the isle of Penglai, three thousand jade maidens naturally attend one; all day at the Peach Banquet one drinks immortal wine and wears immortal blossoms, the four elements gently tipsy, the whole body crystal-clear through and through. The tortoise and serpent of the sea bottom appear and myriad spirits obey one’s command. Only then are True Lead and True Mercury truly inverted, the whole body wrapped in purple mist and radiant hair-light with a thousand auspicious rays—this is the “Great Hibernation Method of the Five Dragons.”

Refined for a hundred days, the Mysterious Pass opens of itself, the Infant reveals its form, and the tortoise and serpent appear, coiling naturally together. Only when the student reaches this station is the outer mercury dried up in the mouth. Refined for six months, the body resembles silver paste, blood turns to white nectar, fragrance wafts from the whole frame, and breath exhaled from the mouth forms clouds—the numinous elixir has ripened into a single piece of dried mercury: whoever ingests it never dies, and it can even restore the dead to life. Refined for ten months, the Yang Spirit departs the body, a single body able to transform into tens of thousands of bodies. Wait only until the twelfth month, seizing the complete number of heaven and earth: the Yang Spirit is accomplished, eighty-four thousand Yang Spirits go out and enter throughout the body; walking under sun and moon one casts no shadow, entering metal and stone one meets no obstruction, entering water one does not drown, entering fire one does not burn, blades and weapons cannot wound one, ghosts and spirits cannot fathom one, transformations are boundless—one has become a True Person (zhenren). Every breath and seasonal pulse of the body is True Medicine; a chicken eating it becomes a phoenix, a dog eating it becomes a dragon. This principle even ghosts and spirits can scarcely fathom; had one never seen such words, one would surely never believe in the Great Medicine and Golden Elixir.

造化工夫、三回九转、七返八还、火候细微、攒簇口诀，只三五日间把天地都颠倒过，都是自然，人身造化阴阳亦是自然，却要体天地造化方可成就，事从做过见过，从试应验到自然处，工夫虽是一年，火候细微只在百日之内，动静凶恶只在几日，一时里得内外攒簇，顷刻湛然，圣胎成就，产黍米之珠，吞入腹内，周天火足，脱胎换骨，只是要持空养虚，余皆自然。今人果得明师指示，先炼己于尘俗，积铅于廛市，攒年簇月，攒日簇时，大定之中只在一刹那间，不出半个时辰，把天地都颠倒过，运火十月之工，体天地自然之法，若不能死中求活，焉能逃出三灾八难哉！

The work of creation and transformation, the three turns and nine revolutions, the seven reversions and eight returns, the subtleties of fire phasing, and the oral formulas of gathering and clustering—in merely three to five days they turn heaven and earth upside down, yet all of it is natural. The yin-yang creativity of the human body is likewise natural, yet one must embody the creative workings of heaven and earth to bring it to completion. These things are spoken from having done them and seen them, verified by trial all the way to spontaneity.

Though the work is said to take a year, the subtle fire phasing lies within a hundred days, and the perilous turbulence of movement and stillness lasts only a few days. Within a single hour inner and outer are gathered and clustered, limpid in an instant; the Holy Embryo is accomplished, bringing forth the pearl of millet grain, swallowed into the belly; once the Celestial Circuit fire is complete, one sheds the mortal frame and exchanges the bones—all that is required is to uphold emptiness and nourish vacuity, and all the rest happens naturally.

If people today truly receive the pointing of an enlightened master, first refining the self amid worldly dust and accumulating lead in the marketplace, compressing years into months and days into hours, then in great absorption—within a single instant, in less than half a double-hour—they invert heaven and earth, carry out the ten months of fire work, and embody the natural law of heaven and earth. If one cannot seek life in the midst of death, how could one ever escape the three calamities and eight hardships!

Key Terms

  • Seven Reversions and Nine Returns: An alchemical term. Seven is the completion number of fire, nine is the completion number of metal; “reversion” and “return” refer to recovering scattered Original Breath and Original Spirit back to their source. In this essay it serves as a general name for the entire process from laying the foundation to shedding the embryo.
  • True Yin within Yang / True Yang of the lower cavity: Explained by the text itself as Original Spirit (belonging to the three ethereal souls) and Original Breath (belonging to the seven corporeal souls); their union is the conjunction of Kan and Li and the merging of hun and po.
  • Jade Liquid Returning Elixir: The work of laying the foundation and refining the self, called in this essay “the initial stage of long life and enduring vision.”
  • Golden Liquid Great Elixir: The advanced work after entering the chamber, uniting the “True Lead beyond the body” with Wood-Mercury within the body, described in the second half of this essay.
  • Three Completions (sanquan): Essence brimming, breath full, and spirit whole.
  • Dharma wealth (facai): An alchemical term for both the inner accumulation (“dharma”) and outer conditions (“wealth,” including protectors, dwelling place, and provisions) needed for cultivation.
  • Lianquan: Points beneath the tongue, called in this essay the Elixir Well on the left and Stone Spring on the right; when the work matures, saliva wells up abundantly.
  • Yin demons: The “spirits wailing and ghosts weeping” during the three days described here—phenomena arising from transformations of yin breath within the body, neither to be identified with nor feared.
  • Great Hibernation Method of the Five Dragons (wulong dazhe fa): Traditionally attributed to Chen Tuan (Chen Xiyi) as a sleep-meditation method; here borrowed to refer to the great absorption of upholding emptiness and nourishing vacuity after the Great Elixir.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Do not identify with phenomena. The single most important line in the entire essay is “you must never take them for real.” Whether bliss and light or terror and apparitions, all are passing phenomena of bodily and mental change, not goals. Clinging to bliss breeds greed; fearing visions breeds disorder.
  2. Refine the self first, enter the chamber later. Zhang Sanfeng lays out the order unmistakably: first refine the self amid worldly life (“tempering one’s light and mingling with the dust”), and only when the work has matured speak of secluded chamber practice. Without the foundation of self-refinement, the higher the state one chases, the greater the danger.
  3. The body is verification, not spectacle. Smooth digestion, food nourishing vitality, steady energy—these plain, grounded shifts are far more reliable than any extraordinary vision.
  4. Read hyperbole as metaphor. Expressions like “three thousand jade maidens” or “a chicken eating it becomes a phoenix” are traditional literary figures for ultimate attainment—do not take them literally, still less seek them out.
  5. Have reliable support. The ancients never entered the chamber without kindred companions watching over them. The same holds for quiet sitting today: do not undertake prolonged, high-intensity sessions alone; make sure people around you know what you are doing.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text comes from Volume Three, Plain Talks on the Mysterious Mechanism, of the Complete Works of Zhang Sanfeng. The first two essays (On Fire Phasing in Refining the Elixir) and the Song of Sitting in Meditation appear in Part Eight of Book Two and are not duplicated here.
  2. “A sensation of deep delight, like the union of husband and wife in the outer world—yet you must never cling to it”: The original uses ordinary marital union as a simile for the pleasurable feeling when spirit and breath unite, and immediately warns against attachment. This site translates it strictly as written without further extrapolation.
  3. “This is the stage called ‘gathering yin to replenish yang’”: The essay itself specifies that “yin” here means the “True Yin within Yang of the upper cavity” (Original Spirit) and “yang” means the “True Yang of the lower cavity” (Original Breath), both within one’s own body. It has nothing to do with sexual gathering practices; Zhang Sanfeng explicitly condemns using women as vessels in both the Treatise on the Great Way and Pointing Out Delusions on the Ascent to Heaven in this same volume.
  4. “Gather the True Lead beyond one’s physical body” / “marrying the dragon to the tiger, driving the tiger to meet the dragon”: “Beyond the body” (shenwai) has historically been interpreted in differing ways. Following the pure-stillness method, this site understands it as “outside the physical frame,” namely the One Breath of Prior Heaven arriving from emptiness rather than something sought from another person—see the next essay, On Ingesting the Great Elixir: “Gold seeds gold, silver seeds silver; outside there is no other numinous spirit.”
  5. “Pace the Dipper and tread the stars, grasp the sword and hold the seal, subduing marshals and summoning generals”: Originally Daoist liturgical terminology, used metaphorically here for holding the mind-spirit steady and governing the shifting phenomena within the body.
  6. “Only when you master the six spiritual penetrations… do not dream of attaining the Dao in primal chaos”: Cited in the original as “a saying of the ancients”; exact source unidentified, retained as printed.
  7. “Having died the great death this time, only now do I live”: A classic Chan expression meaning that once the deluded mind dies completely, true nature comes alive.
  8. “Great Hibernation Method of the Five Dragons”: Chen Tuan of the Five Dynasties and early Song was famed for sleep cultivation, traditionally associated with the “Five Dragons Hibernation Method.”
  9. “Walking under sun and moon one casts no shadow”: A traditional image signifying that the Yang Spirit is pure yang without yin, hence shadowless.
  10. “Seven reversions and eight returns” (qifan bahuan): So printed in the original, varying from “seven reversions and nine returns” at the opening; preserved as written.
