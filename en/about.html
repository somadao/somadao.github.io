What this is

Immortality, Grounded is the English edition of 凡人修仙·落地版, a project that renders the classics of Daoist inner alchemy (neidan) into plain language, passage by passage. Each chapter gives the classical Chinese original, a translation, the commentators’ notes, explanations of difficult terms, practical pointers that can be brought into everyday practice, and the translator’s notes. The English and French versions are translated from the project’s modern Chinese rendering and checked against the classical original; the classical Chinese is kept on the page, folded, for comparison. Existing published translations (Wilhelm and Baynes, Cleary, Pregadio and others) were consulted for understanding and terminology only; the translations here are the project’s own.

This is a personal, non-commercial project of cultural study.

Safety (please read)

  • This site translates and explains ancient texts for cultural study and research. It is not medical advice and cannot replace a doctor’s diagnosis or treatment.
  • Everything marked “Safety note” — holding the breath, contracting the anus, forcing passes open with intention, fasting, stopping menstruation, breast massage, long retreats and the like — is a record of what the ancients did. Read it for understanding only; do not imitate it on your own.
  • People with high blood pressure or a tendency to rising heat should not fix their attention between the eyes or on the crown of the head.
  • The healing methods in the old books are no substitute for proper treatment. If you are ill, see a doctor first.
  • Nurturing the embryo, sending out the spirit and ascending as an immortal belong to the ideals of the Daoist tradition and to ancient records; the commentators themselves repeatedly say these “remain to be verified”.
  • If persistent headaches, palpitations, chest tightness, insomnia or unusual moods appear during practice, or experiences such as leaving the body or seeing and hearing things that are not there, stop practising and, if needed, seek help from a doctor or mental-health professional.
