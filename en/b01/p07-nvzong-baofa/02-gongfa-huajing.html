2 · Method and Transformed Scenes

2 · Method and Transformed Scenes — from the Breast Stream to the Southern Ocean, the navel wheel and the Muddy Pellet

Safety note (translator): This section includes methods such as massaging the breasts and lifting and contracting the two lower orifices. During pregnancy, menstruation or after childbirth, or if you have any breast or gynecological condition, please consult a doctor first; even in good health, massage should be gentle, and lifting and contracting should be slight and brief — do not hold it with force.

About This Chapter

Women’s cultivation begins with yang becoming strong and ends with yin being expelled — the author says this method is extremely secret and few know it. Women take blood as their foundation: when blood is strong, essence is full; when the heart is cool, blood is produced. So one must first still the mind; when the mind is still and settled and the spirit clear, the heart becomes cool. When the heart is cool and saliva wells up, fix the thought on the “Breast Stream” (between the breasts) and massage it in circles with the hand, so that the movement of breath overflows; then raise both hands and massage each breast in circles, until the rich, dense breath circulates all around, and one feels warm breath radiating backward, and something like smoke and flame at the “Double Pass” (behind the heart and in front of the Spine-Straddling Pass), pressing to break through the pass; then the muddy fluid in the pass flows out on both sides to the Breast Stream, like a spring. Then lead it with the True Intention into the “Southern Ocean” (the Crimson Palace inside the Chest Center point) and guard it in stillness for about thirty-six breaths; then relax, and the warm breath descends to the two sides of the waist and circles left and right; then draw it together deep into the navel wheel, turning it slowly forty-nine times and quickly forty-nine times; when warm primordial breath is felt passing backward through the Caudal Funnel, it rises along the spine to the Muddy Pellet, then descends to the Flowery Pool and the Crimson Gate… After this various transformed scenes appear: the body empty as a valley, the sea of nature with blue waves, ten thousand rays of golden light, a bright moon floating and sinking. Finally one concludes with movements such as rubbing the hands, rubbing the face, twisting the waist and swinging the legs. Three times a day for a hundred days, and the foundation is established.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

女子精修，以阳旺为始，而以阴格为终。此法至秘，知者鲜矣！盖女以血为本者，血旺则精盈，心凉则生血。古云：液血之炼，血精之化，还仗神清。血无液化，液失神烘，液泥成痰，流注脾胃，蒸升著肺，散流经络，百病猬生，五脏被灾，六腑遭厄。故丹诀必先息心，心息定而神清，心斯凉矣！

In women’s earnest cultivation, one begins with yang becoming strong and ends with yin being expelled. This method is extremely secret, and few know it! For women take blood as their foundation: when blood is strong, essence is full; when the heart is cool, blood is produced. The ancients said: the refining of fluid into blood and the transformation of blood into essence still depend on a clear spirit. If blood is not produced from fluid, and fluid loses the warming of the spirit, the fluid turns to mud and becomes phlegm, flowing into the spleen and stomach, steaming up and settling in the lungs, spreading through the meridians; a hundred illnesses spring up like a hedgehog’s spines, the five viscera suffer disaster and the six bowels meet misfortune. Therefore the alchemical formula must first still the mind; when the mind is still and settled, the spirit is clear, and the heart becomes cool!

§2
Original (Chinese)

故必当俟心凉液涌，然后念注乳溪，加以用手旋摩，务使气机洋溢。次举两手分旋其房，亦惟俟此烟缊周绕，更觉暖气后烘，双关得有烟焰，势逼透关，满关泥液，分沛乳溪，一如泉涌。

So one must wait until the heart is cool and saliva wells up, then fix the thought on the Breast Stream and massage it in circles with the hand, making sure the movement of breath overflows. Next raise both hands and massage each breast in circles, again only waiting until this rich, dense breath circulates all around; then one also feels warm breath radiating backward, and the Double Pass has something like smoke and flame, its force pressing to break through the pass, and the muddy fluid filling the pass flows out on both sides to the Breast Stream, just like a spring welling up.

§3
Original (Chinese)

旋以真意导入南洋，寂而守之，约有四九之息，舍意一松，觉此个中，油然而降，分注两腰，左右盘旋，各约神息四九之数，乃一意引聚脐轮深处，缓旋四十九，急旋四十九，察吾尾闾，暖炁后穿，如或势缓，可用提缩二便法，自得穿尾升脊，上过昆仑，降注泥丸，觉此泥丸宽广如海，自可停留涵育。

At once lead it with the True Intention into the Southern Ocean and guard it in stillness for about thirty-six breaths; then let go of the intention and relax, and one feels what is within descend abundantly, pouring separately into the two sides of the waist and circling left and right, each for about thirty-six breaths of the spirit. Then, with a single intention, draw it together deep into the navel wheel, turning it slowly forty-nine times and quickly forty-nine times. Observe my Caudal Funnel: warm primordial breath passes backward through it. If its force is slow, one may use the method of lifting and contracting the two lower orifices; it will naturally pass through the Caudal Funnel, rise along the spine, go up over Kunlun and descend into the Muddy Pellet; one feels this Muddy Pellet as vast as the sea, and naturally it can stay there to be nourished and nurtured.

§4
Original (Chinese)

既而降注华池、绛阙，大地阎浮，露珠沛洒，混忘所事，但觉恍焉惚焉，不呼自呼，不吸自吸，不提自提，不咽自咽，此中滋味甘香，气神充和，三田一贯。

Then it descends into the Flowery Pool and the Crimson Gate; the whole great earth of the human world seems to float, dewdrops are scattered abundantly, one blends in and forgets what one is doing, feeling only a vague dimness; without breathing out one breathes out, without breathing in one breathes in, without lifting one lifts, without swallowing one swallows. The taste within is sweet and fragrant, breath and spirit are full and harmonious, and the three fields are joined as one.

§5
Original (Chinese)

已而玄况四塞，急须内顾。顺将万缘放下，旋觉身虚若谷，大地亦无，隐隐凉气袭人，烟缊四塞，忽复雾散云收，下现性海，碧波澄如，我总一念不动，忘境忘情，忽现金光万道，细雨如珠，随光下注，左旋右转，化成皓月，浮沉晶海，遽然如梦而醒。

Soon mysterious states fill all around; one must quickly look within. Let go of all ties as they come, and at once one feels the body empty as a valley, and even the great earth is gone; faintly a cool breath touches one, and rich, dense breath fills all around. Suddenly the mist disperses and the clouds withdraw, and below appears the sea of nature, its blue waves limpid. I keep not a single thought moving, forgetting the scene and forgetting the feelings. Suddenly ten thousand rays of golden light appear, and a fine rain like pearls pours down with the light, turning left and right, and becomes a bright moon floating and sinking in a crystal sea; suddenly it is like waking from a dream.

§6
Original (Chinese)

此际即须内省此身，以气爽神清、遍体和畅为得。得则全身照凝片时，以意注牝，觉得此中恬泰，是矣！遂复摩手摩面，运神绕腹，双耸辘轳，俱各共行四十九息，徐徐扭腰、摆洒膝腿、坐点趾尖，各行二十四息而止。行之百日，日行三次无间，根基立矣！

At this point one must examine this body within, and take clear breath, a clear spirit and harmony throughout the body as the sign of attainment. Having attained it, let the whole body shine and congeal for a moment, and with the intention pour into the pin; when one feels peace and ease within, that is right! Then rub the hands, rub the face, move the spirit around the abdomen, and shrug both shoulders like a windlass, each for forty-nine breaths; then slowly twist the waist, swing and shake the knees and legs, and while sitting tap the tips of the toes, each for twenty-four breaths, and stop. Practice this for a hundred days, three times a day without interruption, and the foundation is established!

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§6
Original note (Chinese)

此段叙述女功具体功法颇详，描写功中玄况（化景）亦多，学者务先熟读，息心默会，则行功有本，不为境迷，纵或现象稍异，而层次亦须井然，方可得效。

女子修行与男子有别。女子乃阴体，须用乳房灵脂，变化气质。故女功先炼形质，后炼本元。形指两乳，质指月经。本元指先天炁。

乳溪在两乳之间若溪处，古云乳溪通心通肾又通脾，其中有膻中穴，对人身至关重要。《黄帝内经》云：膻中为气海。又云：膻中者臣使之宫，喜乐出焉。又云：膻中者心之宫城也。故宜特别观注，加意按摩，盖女子命根根于心。

房即指两乳房。古人云：乳房上通心肺之津液，下徹血海之真汁。又按乳之左房通肝，右房通肺。

双关位于夹脊之前心之后夹缝中。夹脊在脊椎第七、八胸椎之间，前对膻中穴。传为孙不二述《西王母女修正途十则》一书有注云：“谨按双关位于脊前宫心后，关内有二穴，人身泥液之所踞，左曰膏，右曰肓。药力不能到，真炁不自至者。泥液踞祸一身，造化生人，乃为设关以护心，故名其处曰双关。”

南洋，乃以人身前后为南北，当是两乳之间的膻中穴深入一寸三分绛宫处。

三田以泥丸（在百会穴内）为上田，中黄（心后双关前）为中田，脐内一寸三分处为下田。

牝指内牝，即子宫。丹书云：阳曰玄，阴曰牝。

此节功法惟能虚寂心念，故得神清炁旺，聚神烘关。加以注乳溪揉乳房功法，始得泥液溶涌出关，达南洋锻炼，是为有无相济之功。

This passage describes the concrete method of women’s practice in considerable detail and also depicts many mysterious states (transformed scenes) during practice. Students must first read it thoroughly and silently understand it with a still mind; then their practice will have a foundation and they will not be deluded by the scenes. Even if the phenomena differ somewhat, the stages must still be in good order for results to be obtained.

Women’s cultivation differs from men’s. Women have a yin body and must use the numinous fat of the breasts to transform their constitution. So women’s practice first refines form and substance, and then refines the original root. “Form” means the two breasts, “substance” means the menses, and the “original root” means the primordial breath of Prior Heaven.

The Breast Stream is the stream-like place between the two breasts. The ancients said that the Breast Stream connects with the heart, the kidneys and the spleen; within it is the Chest Center point (danzhong), which is of the utmost importance to the human body. The Yellow Emperor’s Inner Classic says: “The Chest Center is the sea of breath.” And: “The Chest Center is the office of the envoy; joy and delight come forth from it.” And: “The Chest Center is the palace wall of the heart.” So it should be specially contemplated and carefully massaged, for the root of women’s life is rooted in the heart.

“Breasts” (fang 房) means the two breasts. The ancients said: the breasts connect above with the fluids of the heart and lungs and penetrate below to the true juices of the sea of blood. Also: the left breast connects with the liver, the right with the lungs.

The Double Pass is in the gap in front of the Spine-Straddling Pass and behind the heart. The Spine-Straddling Pass is between the seventh and eighth thoracic vertebrae, facing the Chest Center point in front. In the book Ten Rules of the Correct Path of Women’s Cultivation from the Queen Mother of the West (Xiwangmu nüxiu zhengtu shize 西王母女修正途十则), said to have been recounted by Sun Bu’er, a note says: “Respectfully noted: the Double Pass lies in front of the spine and behind the heart. Within the pass are two points, where the muddy fluid of the body lodges; the left is called gao, the right huang. The power of medicines cannot reach there, and the true primordial breath does not come there of itself. The muddy fluid lodging there harms the whole body; when creation brought forth humans, it set up a pass here to protect the heart, so the place is called the Double Pass.”

The “Southern Ocean” takes the front and back of the body as south and north; it should be the Crimson Palace, one cun three fen inward from the Chest Center point between the breasts.

The three fields: the Muddy Pellet (inside the Hundred Meetings point) is the upper field, the Central Yellow (behind the heart and in front of the Double Pass) is the middle field, and the point one cun three fen inside the navel is the lower field.

Pin means the inner pin, that is, the womb. The alchemical books say: “Yang is called xuan (the mysterious), yin is called pin (the female).”

In this method, only by making the mind and thoughts empty and still can one obtain a clear spirit and strong primordial breath, gather the spirit and warm the pass. Adding the methods of fixing the thought on the Breast Stream and kneading the breasts, the muddy fluid can dissolve, well up out of the pass and reach the Southern Ocean to be smelted — this is the work in which being and non-being help each other.

Key Terms

  • Beginning with yang becoming strong, ending with yin being expelled: the general outline of women’s cultivation.
  • Refining fluid into blood, transforming blood into essence.
  • Fluid turning to mud and becoming phlegm: fluid that loses the warming of the spirit stagnates into mud and becomes phlegm.
  • Breast Stream: the stream-like place between the breasts, containing the Chest Center point; Qiu Ling’s note: it connects with the heart, the kidneys and the spleen.
  • Breasts (fang): the two breasts; the left connects with the liver, the right with the lungs.
  • Double Pass: in the gap in front of the Spine-Straddling Pass and behind the heart, containing the two points gao and huang (its location is close to the “Double Pass of the Spine-Straddling Pass” of Part One).
  • Muddy fluid: turbid fluid lodging in the Double Pass.
  • Southern Ocean: taking the front of the body as south, the Crimson Palace one cun three fen inside the Chest Center point.
  • Thirty-six breaths (si jiu zhi xi 四九之息): literally “four nines,” thirty-six breaths.
  • Method of lifting and contracting the two lower orifices: lightly lifting and contracting the front and back lower orifices; see the safety note.
  • Kunlun: the crown.
  • Yanfu 阎浮: Jambudvīpa, the Buddhist name for the human world.
  • The three fields joined as one: the upper, middle and lower fields joined by a single breath; Qiu Ling’s note: the Muddy Pellet is the upper field, the Central Yellow the middle field, the point one cun three fen inside the navel the lower field.
  • Sea of nature: a limpid, sea-like realm seen in stillness.
  • Pin 牝: the inner pin, that is, the womb; “yang is called xuan, yin is called pin.”
  • Shrugging both shoulders like a windlass.
  • Tapping the tips of the toes while sitting.
  • Form and substance, original root: Qiu Ling’s note: form means the two breasts, substance the menses, the original root the primordial breath of Prior Heaven.

Putting It into Practice

  1. First a cool heart, then massage: the order in the text is very clear — first still the mind, wait until the heart is cool and saliva wells up in the mouth, and only then begin massage.
  2. Where to massage: first between the breasts (the Breast Stream, the Chest Center point), then each breast, always with gentle circular massage. The aim is to let the movement of breath “overflow,” not to stimulate.
  3. The route of the attention (for understanding): Breast Stream → Southern Ocean (inside the chest) → the two sides of the waist → deep in the navel wheel → Caudal Funnel → spine → Muddy Pellet → Flowery Pool → Crimson Gate.
  4. When transformed scenes appear: “one must quickly look within; let go of all ties as they come,” “I keep not a single thought moving, forgetting the scene and forgetting the feelings” — exactly the same principle as in men’s practice.
  5. The standard for concluding practice: clear breath, a clear spirit, harmony throughout the body; then conclude with rubbing the hands, rubbing the face, circling the abdomen, shrugging the shoulders, twisting the waist, swinging the legs and tapping the toes. This set of concluding movements is very simple, and anyone can do it after quiet sitting.
  6. A hundred days, three times a day: the time the text gives for establishing the foundation.

Translator's Notes

  1. “This rich, dense breath” (yanyun 烟缊): that is, yinyun 氤氲.
  2. “The whole great earth of the human world” (dadi Yanfu 大地阎浮): Yanfu is the Buddhist name for the human world (Jambudvīpa); here it describes the whole world seeming to float.
  3. “The Double Pass lies in front of the spine and behind the heart”: the original quoted in Qiu Ling’s note (双关位于脊前宫心后) seems to contain an extra character; the meaning is that the Double Pass is in front of the spine and behind the heart.
  4. Qiu Ling did not divide the original text of this section into paragraphs; for ease of comparison, it is divided by content into §1–§6.
