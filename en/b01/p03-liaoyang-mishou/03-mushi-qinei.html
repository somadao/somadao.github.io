3 · Looking into the Navel — cool fluid and warm breath meet: the Lesser Celestial Circuit

Listen to this chapterENEnglish

About This Chapter

After illuminating the Root of the Mountain and the Chamber of Spirit for a while (the text says seven days; Qiu Ling’s note says it need not be limited to seven days), bring the gaze down and look into the navel (the lower Cinnabar Field). This section describes the bodily signs in great detail: first there is warmth; the lower Cinnabar Field gradually becomes wide, and the warmth gradually becomes like boiling. If the mind is still like still water, a drop of cool fluid will emerge from the Crimson Palace; then hot breath comes up from below to meet it and circles left and right below the stomach; soon cool fluid wells up from behind the heart and meets the heat of the kidneys — “a delight beyond words.” Then the two eyes and the mind’s intention guide the movement of breath to turn in the abdomen, and finally it returns to the Sea of Breath — this is the meeting of Kan and Li, also called the Lesser Celestial Circuit. Qiu Ling’s note explains clearly two kinds of meeting, “the lower meeting the upper” and “the upper meeting the lower,” and says that in fact cool fluid begins to appear already while holding the intention on the opening of the Spine-Straddling Pass.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

如是行去，总以止念返照，刻刻如是。行满七日，乃可自此景中移目下视脐内，自得直入下田，便有暖气。随目下注，须更用目内旋，初则自中至外，旋旋如此，自觉下田渐宽，乃复令目自外至中，旋旋如之。此中自觉渊如，而觉暖气内发，渐至沸如。此心仍如止水，则自得有凉液一点，点出绛宫，乃是退符之真验。

Continue in this way, always with stopping thoughts and turning the light inward as the main thing, at every moment. After a full seven days, you may move the gaze down from this state and look into the navel; you will naturally go straight into the lower field, and there will be warmth. As the gaze pours downward, also turn it within: at first from the center outward, round and round like this, and you will feel the lower field gradually becoming wider; then let the gaze go from the outside toward the center, round and round in the same way. Within, you will feel a depth like an abyss, and feel warmth arising within, gradually becoming like boiling. If at this time the mind is still like still water, a drop of cool fluid will naturally emerge from the Crimson Palace — this is the true sign of “withdrawing the response.”

§2
Original (Chinese)

于斯时也，必有热气自穴上迎，至于脘下左右盘旋。其气腾如，若有所待。吾则一意守之以目，迎之以心，如是以应之。少顷便有凉液涌自心后，与肾热气相接，现有吞吐莫释，畅快难言之景。切莫贪恋，即须收住。徐以两目内视渊深之处，继以心意导之，使此气机旋转腹中，初则自小而大，后则复收为小，仍导二炁一归气海，乃为坎离交，又名小周天，实为退符之大用。不知者误谓进火，而进火之机却基于此。

At this time, hot breath will certainly come up from the cavity to meet it, and circle left and right below the stomach. This breath rises as if waiting for something. With a single intention I guard it with the eyes and welcome it with the mind, responding to it in this way. Soon cool fluid wells up from behind the heart and meets the hot breath of the kidneys; there appears a state of swallowing and releasing that cannot be explained, a delight beyond words. On no account cling to it; you must gather it in at once. Slowly look with both eyes into that deep place, then guide it with the mind’s intention, letting this movement of breath turn in the abdomen — at first from small to large, then gathered back to small — and still guide the two breaths to return together into the Sea of Breath. This is the meeting of Kan and Li, also called the Lesser Celestial Circuit; it is in fact the great function of “withdrawing the response.” Those who do not understand mistake it for “advancing the fire,” yet the mechanism of advancing the fire is based on this.

§3
Original (Chinese)

故我于斯时，一呼一吸，满口津液如饴，便宜烹炼，或留绛阙，或散经络，或注心后而入肾，各有妙用，故必当连咽，将此心火存烘，俟有化机洋溢，随义施行。终则导归气海，以火逼入，所谓液于是化血，血于是化精，精于是化气，气于是冲关透后者。

So at this time, with each exhalation and inhalation, the mouth is full of saliva sweet as malt sugar, just right for cooking and refining. It may be kept in the Crimson Palace, or spread through the meridians, or poured behind the heart and into the kidneys; each has its wondrous use. So you must swallow it repeatedly and keep it warm with the fire of the heart, wait until the impulse to transform overflows, and act accordingly. In the end guide it back into the Sea of Breath and drive it in with fire — this is what is meant by “fluid then turns into blood, blood into essence, essence into breath, and breath then breaks through the passes and passes up the back.”

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§3
Original note (Chinese)

如此行功，虽说行满七日便可转入下视脐内，但实不限于七日。脐内即下田，指脐轮后一寸二分处，此窍又叫神炉，坤炉。《寥阳殿问答编》第一篇说：“脐轮之后一寸二分，囫地一声，真元落于此处，号曰天心，一名气海，又曰神炉，乃胎仙元命之根，是故又号天根，为炼精化气，炼气上升之地，是故又号坤炉，按即丹书之下田。”《皇极阖辟仙经》曰：“盖常人呼吸，皆从咽喉而下，至中脘而回，不能与祖气相通，所谓众人之息以喉也。若至人呼吸，直贯明堂而上，盖切切然以守夹脊双关，自然通于天心一窍，得与元始祖气相连，如磁吸铁，而同类相亲，即庄子所谓真人之息以踵也。踵者深也。”

内视下田，实际上是凝神入气穴。行功既久而纯熟，回光返照山根，就会自然而然地渐渐达于中黄夹脊一窍，意守中黄，就会自然而然地渐渐通达天心下田。当呼吸入于气穴（即下田），便会自然进入胎息的境界。这种自然而然之妙，只有行功者才会体会得到。

当得到凉液一点出自绛宫，而与下田上升热气相迎，此为下交上，即下田与绛宫相交；得到凉液自心后关前涌出，而与肾热气相接，此为上交下，即心肾相交，都是很好的效应，使人浑身轻松，畅快难言。其实，在意守夹脊一窍时，即开始有此现象。故常操此心藏于夹脊之窍，则常感此窍涌出凉液一片，感到身心舒畅，亦为得效之验。

此种退符（前降）现象，只有持久的胎息出现，方为坎离交媾，又名小周天之验，古人说是乃得“真金”而非“黄叶”。

胎息发生之际，必满口津液，自上腭下降，香如醍醐，味似甘露，当咽下重楼，以目送之，此乃“气化”作用，小还丹之妙。

In practicing like this, although it is said that after a full seven days one may turn to looking down into the navel, in fact it is not limited to seven days. “Into the navel” means the lower field, the point one cun two fen behind the navel; this opening is also called the Furnace of Spirit or the Furnace of Kun. The first section of the Questions and Answers in the Liaoyang Hall says: “One cun two fen behind the navel: with a cry at birth, the true origin falls here. It is called the Celestial Heart, also the Sea of Breath, also the Furnace of Spirit; it is the root of the original life of the Embryonic Immortal, and so it is also called the Root of Heaven; it is the place where essence is refined into breath and breath is refined to rise, and so it is also called the Furnace of Kun — note: that is, the lower field of the alchemical books.” Perfected Yin’s Immortal Scripture of Opening and Closing says: “The breathing of ordinary people always goes down from the throat and turns back at the Central Stomach Duct; it cannot communicate with the ancestral breath — this is what Zhuangzi calls ‘the multitude breathe with their throats.' The breathing of the accomplished person goes straight through the Bright Hall and upward, because he earnestly and intently guards the Spine-Straddling Pass, and so naturally communicates with the single opening of the Celestial Heart and is able to join the primordial ancestral breath, like a magnet attracting iron, like things of the same kind drawn to each other — this is what Zhuangzi calls ‘the Perfected breathe with their heels.' ‘Heels’ means depth.”

Looking within at the lower field is in fact concentrating the spirit and entering the Cavity of Breath. When practice has gone on long and become thoroughly familiar, Reverting the Light and illuminating the Root of the Mountain will quite naturally and gradually reach the opening of the Central Yellow, the Spine-Straddling Pass; holding the intention on the Central Yellow will quite naturally and gradually reach the Celestial Heart, the lower field. When the breathing enters the Cavity of Breath (the lower field), one naturally enters the state of embryonic breathing. Only those who practice can experience this wonder of what happens naturally.

When a drop of cool fluid emerges from the Crimson Palace and meets the hot breath rising from the lower field, this is “the lower meeting the upper,” that is, the lower field meeting the Crimson Palace. When cool fluid wells up from behind the heart and in front of the pass and meets the hot breath of the kidneys, this is “the upper meeting the lower,” that is, the meeting of heart and kidneys. Both are very good effects, making the whole body feel light and relaxed, a delight beyond words. In fact, this phenomenon begins already when one holds the intention on the opening of the Spine-Straddling Pass. So if one constantly keeps the mind stored in the opening of the Spine-Straddling Pass, one will often feel a sheet of cool fluid welling up from this opening, and body and mind will feel at ease; this too is a sign of having gained results.

This phenomenon of “withdrawing the response” (descending in front) is the sign of the conjunction of Kan and Li, also called the Lesser Celestial Circuit, only when lasting embryonic breathing appears; the ancients said that this is obtaining “real gold,” not “yellow leaves.”

When embryonic breathing arises, the mouth will certainly be full of saliva, descending from the palate, fragrant as ghee and tasting like sweet dew; it should be swallowed down the Twelve-Storied Tower (the throat), sent down with the gaze. This is the action of “transformation by breath,” the wonder of the Lesser Returned Elixir.

Key Terms

  • Looking down into the navel: the gaze looks down at the lower Cinnabar Field.
  • Turning the gaze within: rotating the gaze (the intention) within the lower Cinnabar Field, first from the center outward, then from the outside toward the center.
  • Like an abyss, like boiling: deep as an abyss; the warmth gradually growing until it is like boiling water.
  • Cool fluid: a sensation of coolness that appears in stillness, welling up from the Crimson Palace or from behind the heart.
  • Withdrawing the response / descending in front: the breath descending along the front of the body, as opposed to “advancing the fire / rising behind.”
  • Crimson Palace: one cun three fen inward from the Chest Center point between the nipples.
  • Below the stomach: below the stomach duct, that is, the upper abdomen.
  • The lower meeting the upper / the upper meeting the lower: the hot breath of the lower Cinnabar Field meeting the cool fluid of the Crimson Palace; the cool fluid from behind the heart meeting the heat of the kidneys (the meeting of heart and kidneys).
  • Sea of Breath / Celestial Heart / Furnace of Spirit / Furnace of Kun / Root of Heaven: all refer to the lower Cinnabar Field.
  • The two breaths returning together to the Sea of Breath: the cool and the hot currents returning together to the lower Cinnabar Field.
  • Conjunction of Kan and Li / Lesser Celestial Circuit: this section’s definition — cool fluid and warm breath meet, turn in the abdomen and return to the Sea of Breath.
  • Saliva sweet as malt sugar: see Sweet Dew.
  • Fluid turns into blood, blood into essence, essence into breath: the order of transformations described in this part; see the next section.
  • Lesser Returned Elixir: the action of “transformation by breath” in which saliva descends during embryonic breathing and is sent down with the gaze.
  • Yellow leaves, real gold: see Part Two, Chapter 2.

Putting It into Practice

  1. When to shift: after illuminating the Root of the Mountain and the Chamber of Spirit until the mind is settled and the breath regulated (the text says seven days; Qiu Ling says it need not be limited to seven days), move the gaze down to the lower abdomen.
  2. How to turn the gaze within: with the gaze (in fact, the intention), lightly draw circles inside the lower abdomen, first from the center outward; when the abdomen feels wider, from the outside toward the center. Lightly and slowly.
  3. The order of signs (for comparison, not to be pursued): warmth in the lower abdomen → gradually wider and deeper → warmth like boiling → a drop of cool fluid emerging from the chest (the Crimson Palace) → hot breath rising to meet it and circling in the upper abdomen → cool fluid welling up from behind the heart and meeting the heat of the lower back and kidneys → a delight beyond words.
  4. The most important sentence: “On no account cling to it; you must gather it in at once.” When pleasant sensations appear, do not become absorbed in them; gather them in at once and slowly guide the breath back to the lower abdomen.
  5. Saliva: when the mouth is full of sweet saliva, swallow it in several mouthfuls and send it down to the lower abdomen with the gaze (the intention).

Translator's Notes

  1. “Those who do not understand mistake it for ‘advancing the fire,' yet the mechanism of advancing the fire is based on this”: that is, this step looks like “advancing the fire” but is in fact “withdrawing the response”; yet the later mechanism of “advancing the fire” (breaking through the passes and rising behind) is based precisely on this step.
  2. “With a cry at birth”: the base text of the passage quoted in Qiu Ling’s note reads 囡地一声, as in Part Two; the Chinese edition unifies it as 囫地.
  3. “Because he earnestly and intently guards the Spine-Straddling Pass”: Qiu Ling’s quotation of the Scripture of Opening and Closing here lacks one character, 意 (“intention”), found in the original (“holds his intention on the Spine-Straddling Pass”); it is reproduced as in the base text.
