6 · Nurturing the Holy Embryo

6 · Nurturing the Holy Embryo — “collect the Medicine in non-movement, carry out the fire phasing in non-action”

Listen to this chapterENEnglish

About This Chapter

This last section is broadly the same as “Nurturing the Holy Embryo,” Chapter 7 of Part Two, but with two noteworthy differences. First, it states clearly that the “Elixir Bolt” into which the numinous elixir descends is the middle Cinnabar Field. Second, it says that when the embryo is complete after ten months and the spirit moves to the upper palace, “there is no need for a clap of thunder” — no need, as in Part Two, Chapter 9, for “samādhi fire” and “fire of the sun” to blast open the gate at the crown, because “it can pass through metal and stone; why trouble to split open the gate at the crown?” The best sentence of this section comes from Bai Yuchan: “Collect the Medicine in non-movement; carry out the fire phasing in non-action.” Qiu Ling’s note points out that this passage has already entered the stages of refining Breath into Spirit and refining Spirit and returning to Emptiness, and, quoting Zhang Ziyang and Zhao Bichen, explains four expressions common in the alchemical books but rarely made clear: harmonizing the four images, assembling the five phases, the five breaths returning to the origin, and the three flowers gathering at the summit.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

长养圣胎，又当另做，非可容易。既做了卯酉周天，火逼金行，一点乾金，遂上乾宫，渐采渐积，日烹日熔，损之又损，到得炼无可炼，此时药也不生，轮也不转，液也不降，火也不炎，五气俱朝于上阳，三花皆聚于乾顶。然经云：“鼎中有宝非真宝，重结灵胎是圣胎”。但珠在昆仑，何由得下？必假神炉窃灵阳真气以催之，太阳真火以逼之，催逼既久，灵丹应手脱落，化为金液，吞入口中，直射丹扃之内，此即中丹田也。

Nurturing the Holy Embryo is a separate task again, and not an easy one. Once the mao-you Celestial Circuit has been done, fire drives the metal to move, and a point of the metal of Qian rises to the palace of Qian (the crown). Gradually collected and gradually accumulated, cooked day by day and smelted day by day, reduced and reduced again, it reaches the point where there is nothing left to refine. At this time the Medicine is no longer born, the wheel no longer turns, the liquid no longer descends, the fire no longer flares; the five breaths all pay court to the Upper Yang, and the three flowers all gather at the summit of Qian. Yet the scripture says: “The treasure in the cauldron is not yet the true treasure; the numinous embryo formed anew is the Holy Embryo.” But the pearl is at Kunlun (the crown) — how can it come down? One must rely on the numinous yang true breath stolen in the Furnace of Spirit (the lower Cinnabar Field) to urge it on, and on the true fire of the sun to press it. When it has been urged and pressed for a long time, the numinous elixir drops off at a touch, turns into golden liquid, is swallowed into the mouth, and shoots straight into the Elixir Bolt — this is the middle Cinnabar Field.

§2
Original (Chinese)

于一切时中，时时照顾，念兹在兹，混混沌沌，不即不离，所谓时时照丹扃，刻刻守黄庭是也。又云：“漫守药炉看火候，但安神息任天然。”陈虚白曰：“念不可起，念起则火燥。意不可散，意散则火冷”。只要一念不起，一意不散，含光默默，真息绵绵，此长养圣胎之真火候也。故白玉蟾曰：“采药物于不动之中，行火候于无为之内”。如此十月，圣胎成矣。

At all times, watch over it constantly, every thought here, in an undifferentiated state, neither clinging nor leaving — this is what is meant by “at every moment illuminate the Elixir Bolt, at every instant guard the Yellow Court.” It is also said: “Leisurely guard the furnace of the Medicine and watch the fire phasing; simply settle spirit and breath and leave all to nature.” Chen Xubai said: “No thought may arise — when a thought arises, the fire becomes dry; the intention may not scatter — when the intention scatters, the fire grows cold.” Simply let not a single thought arise and not a single intention scatter; hold in the light in silence, the True Breath continuous and soft — this is the true fire phasing for nurturing the Holy Embryo. That is why Bai Yuchan said: “Collect the Medicine in non-movement; carry out the fire phasing in non-action.” After ten months like this, the Holy Embryo is complete.

§3
Original (Chinese)

气足胎完，脱出其胞，移神上宫，无须霹雳一声。盖金石能透，何劳顶门开裂哉！盖其末后重结之胎，乃以天地至先之元为法身，以其自得之一为法心。究其妙用，乃以三才为药物，而以太虚为炉鼎，感致无极之真，与我法心合而成一也。故其末后行功，鬼神莫得而窥，岂仅出入自如，变化无方已哉！必与天合体，与地合用，行全道备，方不负为形神俱妙，与道合真之人。噫！学者要知人之得与天地并列为三者，天地为大造，人身亦一大造也。人之足重如此。

When the breath is sufficient and the embryo complete, it comes out of its womb and the spirit moves to the upper palace (the upper Cinnabar Field) — with no need for a clap of thunder. For it can pass through metal and stone; why trouble to split open the gate at the crown! The embryo formed anew at the end takes the primal origin that preceded Heaven and Earth as its Dharma Body, and the One it has itself obtained as its Dharma Mind. If we examine its wondrous function, it takes the Three Powers (Heaven, Earth and Humanity) as its ingredients and the Great Void as its furnace and cauldron, calling forth the reality of the Ultimateless and uniting it with my Dharma Mind as one. So in its final practice, not even ghosts and spirits can catch a glimpse of it — how could it be merely a matter of going out and coming in freely and transforming without limit! One must share one’s substance with Heaven and one’s function with Earth, with practice complete and the Way fully realized; only then does one live up to the name of a person “whose form and spirit are both wondrous, united with the Dao in reality.” Ah! Students should know that the reason humans can stand alongside Heaven and Earth as the third of the Three Powers is that Heaven and Earth are a great work of creation, and the human body too is a great work of creation. So precious is the human being.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§3
Original note (Chinese)

此段功法已进入炼炁化神及炼神还虚阶段，带有丹功的最高理想境界。

请参看本书《皇极阖辟仙经》第七、八、九、十章注释及《道乡集》养胎功法、出神功法部分。

关于和合四象、攒簇五行、五炁朝元及三花聚顶的解释，张紫阳在《金丹四百字序》中说：“含眼光，凝耳韵，调鼻息，缄舌气，是为和合四象”。“东魂之木，西魄之金，南神之火，北精之水，中意之土，是为攒簇五行”。他又说：“以眼不视而魂在肝，耳不闻而精在肾，舌不声而神在心，鼻不香而魄在肺，四肢不动而意在脾，故名五炁朝元。以精化为炁，以炁化为神，以神化为虚，故名三花聚顶。”

千峰老人赵避尘说：“非是金花、银花、红花为三花，实则是精气神聚于顶内，恍恍惚惚，金光发献，为三花聚于顶内。”他又说，五气朝元指全身真炁发动，其炁升，五脏之炁皆升，其炁降，五脏之炁皆降。既降之后，五炁合而为一，丹田五炁发生金光，现于目前性光之内，全身万脉归宗，而阴阳之气化成纯阳之光，似这个○，才是五炁朝元之功。五三花聚顶，发不出五炁朝元之景。

This method has already entered the stages of refining Breath into Spirit and refining Spirit and returning to Emptiness, and carries the highest ideal of alchemical practice.

Please refer to the notes to Chapters 7, 8, 9 and 10 of the Scripture of Opening and Closing in this book, and to the sections on nurturing the embryo and sending out the spirit in the Collection of Daoxiang.

On the meaning of harmonizing the four images, assembling the five phases, the five breaths returning to the origin and the three flowers gathering at the summit, Zhang Ziyang says in his Preface to the Four Hundred Words on the Golden Elixir (Jindan sibaizi xu 金丹四百字序): “Holding in the light of the eyes, congealing the hearing of the ears, regulating the breath of the nose, sealing the breath of the tongue — this is harmonizing the four images.” “The wood of the hun soul in the east, the metal of the po soul in the west, the fire of the spirit in the south, the water of essence in the north, the earth of intention in the center — (gathered together) this is assembling the five phases.” He also says: “When the eyes do not look, the hun soul rests in the liver; when the ears do not listen, essence rests in the kidneys; when the tongue makes no sound, the spirit rests in the heart; when the nose does not smell, the po soul rests in the lungs; when the four limbs do not move, intention rests in the spleen — hence the name ‘the five breaths return to the origin.' Essence is transformed into breath, breath into spirit, spirit into emptiness — hence the name ‘the three flowers gather at the summit.'”

The Old Man of a Thousand Peaks, Zhao Bichen, says: “The three flowers are not a golden flower, a silver flower and a red flower; in reality they are essence, breath and spirit gathered inside the crown, vague and indistinct, with golden light appearing — this is the three flowers gathering inside the crown.” He also says that the five breaths returning to the origin means the true breath of the whole body being set in motion: when this breath rises, the breaths of the five viscera all rise; when it descends, the breaths of the five viscera all descend. Once they have descended, the five breaths unite into one; the five breaths in the Cinnabar Field give off golden light, which appears within the light of inner nature before the eyes; all the vessels of the body return to their source, and the breaths of yin and yang are transformed into a light of pure yang, like this ○ — only this is the work of the five breaths returning to the origin. Without the three flowers gathering at the summit, the scene of the five breaths returning to the origin cannot appear.

Key Terms

  • Metal of Qian: a point of true yang, likened to metal.
  • The five breaths returning to the origin (the five breaths all paying court to the Upper Yang): see the explanations of Zhang Ziyang and Zhao Bichen quoted in Qiu Ling’s note.
  • Three flowers gathering at the summit: the three flowers of essence, breath and spirit gather at the crown; Zhang Ziyang: “essence is transformed into breath, breath into spirit, spirit into emptiness — hence the name ‘the three flowers gather at the summit.'”
  • Elixir Bolt: this section states clearly that it “is the middle Cinnabar Field.”
  • “Leisurely guard the furnace of the Medicine and watch the fire phasing; simply settle spirit and breath and leave all to nature”: from Zhang Ziyang’s Awakening to Reality.
  • “Collect the Medicine in non-movement; carry out the fire phasing in non-action”: the words of Bai Yuchan.
  • Upper palace: the upper Cinnabar Field (the Muddy Pellet).
  • Dharma Body, Dharma Mind: the primal origin that preceded Heaven and Earth as the Dharma Body, the One one has obtained as the Dharma Mind.
  • Three Powers: Heaven, Earth and Humanity.
  • Harmonizing the four images: holding in the light of the eyes, congealing the hearing of the ears, regulating the breath of the nose, sealing the breath of the tongue.
  • Assembling the five phases: the hun soul (wood, east), the po soul (metal, west), spirit (fire, south), essence (water, north) and intention (earth, center) gathered into one.
  • Holy Embryo, the three transmutations.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The sentence most worth remembering: collect the Medicine in non-movement; carry out the fire phasing in non-action (Bai Yuchan). The higher one goes, the more it is “non-movement” and “non-action.”
  2. Everyone can practice harmonizing the four images: hold in the light of the eyes (the eyes do not look outward), congeal the hearing of the ears (the ears do not listen outward), regulate the breath of the nose (fine and even breathing), seal the breath of the tongue (no speaking, the tongue against the palate). These four things are the most basic bodily preparation for every session of quiet sitting.
  3. A plain understanding of the five breaths returning to the origin: the eyes do not look, the ears do not listen, the tongue makes no sound, the nose does not smell, the four limbs do not move — when all five outward activities are drawn back in, the breaths of the five viscera naturally return to their origin. This is the same thing as “Reverting the Light” in Part One and “the six roots returning to the source” in Part Two.
  4. The fire phasing for nurturing the embryo: as in Part Two, Chapter 7 — not a single thought arising, not a single intention scattering, holding in the light in silence, the True Breath continuous and soft.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Leisurely guard the furnace of the Medicine and watch the fire phasing; simply settle spirit and breath and leave all to nature”: from Awakening to Reality by Zhang Boduan (Ziyang); the source is noted under Key Terms.
  2. “When a thought arises, the fire becomes dry”: the saying of Chen Xubai quoted in Part Two, Chapter 7, reads “the fire flares.” The two places differ by one character, with the same meaning; each follows its base text.
  3. The last sentence of Qiu Ling’s note reads 五三花聚顶 in the base text; 五 is an error for 无 (“without”), and the translation reads “without the three flowers gathering at the summit.”
  4. “The reason humans can stand alongside Heaven and Earth as the third”: that is, the reason humans can stand alongside Heaven and Earth as one of the Three Powers.
