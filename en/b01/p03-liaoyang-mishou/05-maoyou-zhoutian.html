5 · The Mao-You Celestial Circuit

5 · The Mao-You Celestial Circuit — the route of the left–right circuit, and the Bridge for Governing Life

Listen to this chapterENEnglish

About This Chapter

The rising behind and descending in front of the previous section is “the union of Qian and Kun,” along the north–south axis. People know only this and do not know that there is also an east–west “mao-you Celestial Circuit” — like having a cart without wheels or a boat without a rudder. This section gives a route far more detailed than that of Part Two, Chapter 6: a fiery pearl emerges from the Cavity of Breath and rises along the left side, past the left of the navel, the left of the Crimson Palace, the left ribs, the left shoulder, the root of the left ear and the left eye, to the Root of the Mountain; it pauses there briefly, then turns to the right eye, behind the root of the right ear, the right shoulder, the right of the heart and the navel, and returns to the Cinnabar Field. Thirty-six times is advancing the yang fire; twenty-four times in the opposite direction is withdrawing the yin response. This section also mentions for the first time a place the alchemical scriptures rarely speak of, the “Bridge for Governing Life”: it lies crosswise inside the navel, connecting the Cinnabar Field in front with the Gate of Life behind. Qiu Ling’s note says that the route of the true breath described here is very clear, a collection of the experience of earlier practitioners, “rarely found in ordinary alchemical books, and truly precious.”

Safety note (translator): This section involves guiding operations such as pressing “an object” against the perineum and using the gaze to lead the movement of breath along a fixed route; the text itself admits that “at the beginning one cannot avoid applying a little deliberate intention.” Practice only under the guidance of an experienced teacher.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

行之既熟，更须急行卯酉周天之法。世人止知乾坤交媾，而不知卯酉周天。是有南北而无东西，如有车无轮，有舟无舵，其欲至远，不亦难乎？第预清净其心，空洞其念，虚寂其机，湛如油如。外用一物，顶住太玄关。时至发动，寂体以随，中无后天参杂，用目守住泥丸，下照坤脐。良久，自从气穴中透出火珠一粒，自左边升起，至脐左边，次到绛宫，从绛宫之左，忽折入左胁下，而后透入左肩，上左耳根，入左目，到山根，略存一顷，即转右目，从右耳根后，下右肩，绕而前转心之右，下至脐，仍还丹田。如是者三十六次，为进阳火。又从右边升起，左边降下，二十四次，为退阴符。

Once the preceding work has become familiar, you must quickly practice the method of the mao-you Celestial Circuit. People know only the union of Qian and Kun (rising and descending behind and in front) and do not know the mao-you Celestial Circuit. That is having north and south but no east and west — like having a cart without wheels or a boat without a rudder; if you wish to go far, will it not be hard? Simply first make your mind pure and still, empty your thoughts, let the movement of breath be empty and quiet, limpid and at ease. Outside, use an object to press against the Pass of Great Mystery (the Perineum). When the time comes and the movement of breath starts, let the still substance follow it, with no admixture of Posterior Heaven in between; guard the Muddy Pellet with the eyes and illuminate downward on the navel of Kun (the lower Cinnabar Field). After a good while, a single fiery pearl will emerge by itself from the Cavity of Breath and rise on the left side to the left of the navel, then to the Crimson Palace; from the left of the Crimson Palace it suddenly turns in below the left ribs, then passes into the left shoulder, rises to the root of the left ear, enters the left eye and reaches the Root of the Mountain. It pauses there for a moment, then turns to the right eye, goes down from behind the root of the right ear to the right shoulder, circles round to the front, turns to the right of the heart, descends to the navel and returns again to the Cinnabar Field. Thirty-six times like this is “advancing the yang fire.” Then rising on the right and descending on the left, twenty-four times, is “withdrawing the yin response.”

§2
Original (Chinese)

但初时入手，未免略略著意，到纯熟地位，自然左右俱升，且或分从治命桥前后，俱不知其然而然者。人之一身皆属阴，惟目属阳。盖目者，阳窍也。故目之所到，即心之所到。心之所到，即气之所到。此收内药之妙也。治命桥，丹经未有及之者，独《金笥宝箓》云：内有栖神窟，横安治命桥。言之甚明。工夫行到纯熟，气穴中自然元气升起，如喷泡然，入于脐轮，横过治命之桥。此一桥也，前通丹田，后通命门，中空如管，乃元气往来之所。忽然两肾如汤煎，若尚有阴火，小觉微痛，盖龙战于野之义。若阴火已消尽者，不痛也。

At the very beginning, however, one cannot avoid applying a little deliberate intention; when one reaches the stage of full familiarity, it naturally rises on both left and right, and sometimes goes separately in front of and behind the Bridge for Governing Life — all without knowing how it happens. The whole human body belongs to yin; only the eyes belong to yang, for the eyes are yang openings. So where the eyes go, the mind goes; where the mind goes, the breath goes. This is the wonder of gathering in the inner Medicine. The Bridge for Governing Life has never been mentioned in the alchemical scriptures, except in the Precious Register of the Golden Casket (Jinsi baolu 金笥宝箓), which says: “Within there is the Cave Where the Spirit Dwells, and across it lies the Bridge for Governing Life.” It puts it very clearly. When the work becomes fully familiar, the original breath naturally rises in the Cavity of Breath, welling up like bubbles, enters the navel wheel and crosses this Bridge for Governing Life. This bridge connects the Cinnabar Field in front with the Gate of Life behind; it is hollow inside like a tube, the place where the original breath goes back and forth. Suddenly the two kidneys feel like simmering broth; if there is still yin fire, one feels a slight pain — this is the meaning of “dragons fight in the wilds” in the Book of Changes. If the yin fire is already exhausted, there is no pain.

§3
Original (Chinese)

徐徐滚上昆仑之顶，此时下而尾闾，中而二十四骨节，都不经历。且更有一种妙处，并不由玉枕关，忽从两腮边上元始宫中，自慢慢降入山根到鼻准，入人中。浓液凝入雀卵，从鹊桥入舌下，历十二重楼，徐徐咽入中宫，则先天一立，后天退藏矣。所过之穴，有阴气者，未免相战，微微作痛。盖战尽群阴，始完全先天也。一正至而百邪难容，一窍开则万孔生春。铅气上升，汞气下降。铅汞之气，浑圆于丹鼎之外，却病延年。金丹之道，思过半矣。《圭旨》云：“大道分明见此元，璇玑卯酉法天然。由中达外中全外，自后推前后即前。阳火进来从右转，阴符退后往西旋。霎时火候周天毕，炼颗明珠似月圆”。此明珠，即婴儿种子也。

(The breath) slowly rolls up to the summit of Kunlun (the crown). At this time it passes neither the Caudal Funnel below nor the twenty-four vertebrae in the middle. And there is a further wonder: it does not go through the pass of the Jade Pillow either, but suddenly rises along both cheeks into the Palace of Primal Beginning, then of itself slowly descends to the Root of the Mountain, to the tip of the nose, and into the philtrum. Thick fluid congeals like a sparrow’s egg, enters below the tongue by the Magpie Bridge, passes the Twelve-Storied Tower (the throat), and is slowly swallowed into the Central Palace; then Prior Heaven is established and Posterior Heaven withdraws and hides. Where there is yin breath in the points it passes, there cannot fail to be a battle, and a slight pain. For only when the host of yin has been defeated is Prior Heaven fully restored. When a single correct breath arrives, a hundred pathogens find no room; when one opening opens, ten thousand pores come alive with spring. The lead breath rises, the mercury breath descends; the breaths of lead and mercury encircle the elixir cauldron in a perfect round, dispelling illness and prolonging life. Of the way of the Golden Elixir, one has now grasped more than half. The Principles of Inner Nature and Existence says: “The Great Way is clear; here one sees the origin. The Jade Pivot turns; the mao-you method is natural. From the center reaching outward, the center keeps the outside whole; from behind pushing to the front, behind is the front. The yang fire advancing turns to the right; the yin response withdrawing circles toward the west. In an instant the fire phasing of the circuit is complete, and a bright pearl is refined, round as the moon.” This bright pearl is the seed of the infant (the Holy Embryo).

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§3
Original note (Chinese)

行卯酉周天之法，志在收取大药归壶不致耗散，故曰“收功”。《性命圭旨》云：“前段乾坤交媾，收外药也，此段卯酉周天，收内药也。外交媾者后上前下，一升一降也，内交媾者左旋右转，一起一伏也。两者循环状似璇玑。”

此段功法妙处，在于开始时即要求“寂体以随，中无后天参杂”，则此种气功态为真意主宰之自然状态，初学者未免略略著意，到纯熟地步，就“不知其然而然”了。但所述真气运行路线十分清楚，这正是集合了前人行卯酉周天的经验所得，其具体程度乃为一般丹书所罕见，诚足珍贵。

行卯酉周天之功还在于战退身上群阴，使成乾健之体。在这里，两眼之功特别重要，所谓“含光便是长生药”，所谓“机在目”是也。所以凡真气所到之处，如有阴气者，则感到微微作痛。两眼之神光必须随真气之运行而从下至上，从左至右旋转不离，使阳道日长，阴道日消，以达到阳纯阴剥的地步。

The aim of practicing the mao-you Celestial Circuit is to gather in the Great Medicine and return it to the vessel so that it is not dissipated; hence it is called “concluding the work.” The Principles of Inner Nature and Existence says: “The preceding union of Qian and Kun gathers in the outer Medicine; this mao-you Celestial Circuit gathers in the inner Medicine. The outer union goes up behind and down in front, one rising and one descending; the inner union turns left and right, one rising and one falling. Both circulate like the Jade Pivot (the Dipper) turning.”

The wonder of this method is that from the very beginning it requires “letting the still substance follow it, with no admixture of Posterior Heaven in between”; thus this state of practice is a natural state governed by the True Intention. Beginners cannot avoid applying a little deliberate intention, but when they reach full familiarity, it happens “without knowing how it happens.” Moreover, the route of the true breath described here is very clear; it is a collection of the experience of earlier practitioners of the mao-you Celestial Circuit, and its level of detail is rarely found in ordinary alchemical books — truly precious.

The effect of practicing the mao-you Celestial Circuit also lies in defeating the host of yin in the body and making it a strong body of Qian. Here the work of the two eyes is especially important — as the sayings go, “holding in the light is the medicine of long life,” and “the mechanism is in the eyes.” So wherever the true breath goes, if there is yin breath, one feels a slight pain. The spiritual light of the two eyes must follow the movement of the true breath, turning from below to above and from left to right without leaving it, so that the way of yang grows day by day and the way of yin wanes day by day, until yang is pure and yin is stripped away.

Key Terms

  • Mao-you Celestial Circuit: the circuit in the left–right (east–west) direction; the union of Qian and Kun is in the front–back (north–south) direction.
  • Limpid and at ease (zhan ru you ru 湛如油如): clear and limpid, naturally at ease.
  • An object: something pressed against the perineum; the text does not say what it is (compare the “ritual implement” of Part Two, Chapter 6).
  • Letting the still substance follow: the still original substance follows the movement of breath, without admixture of Posterior-Heaven intention.
  • Navel of Kun: the lower Cinnabar Field.
  • Fiery pearl: a point of true yang emerging from the Cavity of Breath.
  • Advancing the yang fire, withdrawing the yin response: rising on the left and descending on the right thirty-six times is advancing the yang fire; rising on the right and descending on the left twenty-four times is withdrawing the yin response.
  • Bridge for Governing Life: lying crosswise inside the navel, connecting the Cinnabar Field in front with the Gate of Life behind, “hollow inside like a tube, the place where the original breath goes back and forth.” From the Precious Register of the Golden Casket: “Within there is the Cave Where the Spirit Dwells, and across it lies the Bridge for Governing Life.”
  • “Dragons fight in the wilds”: the top line of the hexagram Kun in the Book of Changes, a metaphor for the struggle of yin and yang.
  • Summit of Kunlun, Palace of Primal Beginning: the crown of the head.
  • Magpie Bridge: where the tongue meets the palate.
  • Sparrow’s egg: describing the congealed fluid.
  • Twelve-Storied Tower: the throat (windpipe).
  • Inner Medicine, outer Medicine: the Principles of Inner Nature and Existence says that the union of Qian and Kun (rising behind and descending in front) gathers in the outer Medicine, and the mao-you circuit (turning left and right) gathers in the inner Medicine.
  • Jade Pivot (xuanji 璇玑): the Dipper; a metaphor for turning.
  • “Holding in the light is the medicine of long life”: the spiritual light of the two eyes is held in and not let out.

Putting It into Practice

  1. “Letting the still substance follow” is the general principle: it is not dragging the breath along with the intention, but letting the still mind follow the movement of breath. Qiu Ling also says that once it is familiar it happens “without knowing how it happens.”
  2. The value of the route: this description of the route rising on the left and descending on the right is a concrete record rarely found in alchemical books. Readers can take it as a reference for “roughly what the mao-you circuit is like,” not as a map that must be traced exactly.
  3. About the “slight pain”: the text says that where there is yin breath there will be a slight pain. Modern readers should distinguish: a light, brief sensation that disappears once passed can be observed; persistent, marked pain or discomfort means you should stop practicing and consult a doctor.
  4. The role of the eyes: “Where the eyes go, the mind goes; where the mind goes, the breath goes.” This is exactly why all guiding must be gentle: as soon as the eyes and the intention use force, the breath is dragged along forcibly.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Outside, use an object to press against the Pass of Great Mystery”: Part Two, Chapter 6, has “press the ritual implement against the opening of the Pass of Great Mystery.” Neither text says what the object is; no conjecture is offered here.
  2. “Pauses there for a moment” (lüe cun yi qing 略存一顷): yi qing means “a moment.”
  3. “The yang fire advancing turns to the right”: here the Principles of Inner Nature and Existence says “turns to the right,” which differs in its description of direction from this section’s “rises on the left side.” This may reflect a difference of point of view (seen from oneself or from opposite); the translation follows the texts without reconciling them.
  4. “Of the way of the Golden Elixir, one has now grasped more than half” (si guo ban yi 思过半矣): an echo of the “Appended Statements” of the Book of Changes, “if the wise look at the judgments, they will grasp more than half”; that is, one has understood most of it.
