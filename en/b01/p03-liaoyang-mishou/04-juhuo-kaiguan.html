4 · Gathering the Fire and Opening the Passes

4 · Gathering the Fire and Opening the Passes — when the three treasures are full, they break through the passes by themselves: the Greater Celestial Circuit

Listen to this chapterENEnglish

About This Chapter

The previous section ended by saying: fluid turns into blood, blood into essence, essence into breath. This section continues: fluid turns into blood in the Crimson Gate, blood into essence in the two kidneys, essence into breath in the Sea of Breath. As these transformations are stored up, the three treasures of essence, breath and spirit in the lower Cinnabar Field become full and there is “a force that has nowhere to go”; it naturally breaks through the Caudal Funnel, travels up the spine, passes the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow, reaches the Muddy Pellet, then comes down through the face and the inside of the nose to below the tongue, and through the windpipe, the Florid Canopy and the Crimson Palace straight to the Cinnabar Field — this is the River Chariot turning by itself, the Greater Celestial Circuit. Compared with Part Two, Chapter 4, the tone of this section is much gentler: “on no account be agitated and wish to hurry,” “use the method of lifting and contracting the grain path slightly,” “neither be alarmed nor delighted.” Qiu Ling’s note explains one by one the exact locations of the Caudal Funnel, the Pass of Great Mystery, the Kidney Hall, the Hundred Meetings point, the Mysterious Breast, the Florid Canopy and the Yellow Court.

Safety note (translator): This section still contains guiding actions such as lifting and contracting the grain path, looking upward with the eyes closed, and lowering the head while using the intention to pass through the passes. Practice only under the guidance of an experienced teacher; it is unsuitable for people with high blood pressure, glaucoma and similar conditions.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

然液之化血，必于绛阙。血之化精，必于两肾。精之化气，必于气海。如是化存，则此下田三宝充盈，乃有无路可走之势，自然冲开尾闾，从太玄关逆流。隐隐觉有两股暖气，上肾堂，过夹脊，历二十四骨节。《圭旨》有云：“火逼金行颠倒转，自然鼎内大丹凝。”此之谓也。

Fluid turns into blood, necessarily in the Crimson Gate. Blood turns into essence, necessarily in the two kidneys. Essence turns into breath, necessarily in the Sea of Breath. As these transformations are stored up, the three treasures in the lower field become full, and there is a force that has nowhere to go. It naturally breaks through the Caudal Funnel and flows upward against the current from the Pass of Great Mystery (the Perineum). One dimly feels two currents of warmth rising to the Kidney Hall, passing the Spine-Straddling Pass and going through the twenty-four joints of the spine. The Principles says: “Fire drives the metal to move and turn upside down; naturally the Great Elixir congeals in the cauldron.” This is what is meant.

§2
Original (Chinese)

但至夹脊，学者道浅，宝积未富，那能一时便得直透而上，切勿烦躁欲速，惟自日日行之，意中须以心目相送，微用提撮谷道法，如辘轳循环，自然志至气次，时候一到，片响之间，夹脊自开，双关自辟，直上玉枕。玉枕一关，名曰铁壁，居头凹之处，有一高骨，尤难过此。必须闭目上视，低头用意，直透此关，上至泥丸，与神交合。

But when it reaches the Spine-Straddling Pass, the student’s attainment is shallow and the stored treasure not yet rich — how could it go straight through and up all at once? On no account be agitated and wish to hurry; simply practice day after day. In your intention, send it on with mind and eyes, and use the method of lifting and contracting the grain path slightly, circulating like a windlass; naturally, where the will goes the breath follows. When the time comes, in a moment the Spine-Straddling Pass opens by itself, the Double Pass opens by itself, and it goes straight up to the Jade Pillow. The pass of the Jade Pillow is called the “Iron Wall”; it is in the hollow at the back of the head, where there is a high bone, and it is particularly hard to pass. One must close the eyes and look upward, lower the head and use the intention to go straight through this pass up to the Muddy Pellet, where it unites with the spirit.

§3
Original (Chinese)

此时景象，有如雷轰电掣，火焚波沸，种种形声，似真似幻。切须牢守身心，勿惊勿喜，徐徐自定，清馨冷然渗入两耳，泥丸昆仑之间，如火珠一颗，盘旋不已，隐现于眉间，觉从鼻内而下向玄膺，仍自降入气管，乃由华盖、绛宫，直抵丹田。此时身心快乐，难以言喻。所谓“乾坤交媾罢，一点落黄庭”是也。学者如或真火稍微，再加微火吹动，微微抽添，又复如前，从后而上，从前而下，环绕不已，此即河车自转也。然后升前降，俱在骨节内行，不在皮肤内行。诗云：“丹田直透泥丸顶，自在河车几百遭”。则铅枯而汞干矣！此乃大周天火候。

The scenes at this time are like thunder roaring and lightning flashing, fire burning and waves boiling — all kinds of shapes and sounds, seemingly real, seemingly illusory. You must firmly guard body and mind, neither alarmed nor delighted, and slowly it settles by itself. A clear, cool fragrance seeps into both ears; between the Muddy Pellet and Kunlun something like a fiery pearl circles without stopping, appearing and disappearing between the eyebrows. One feels it come down from inside the nose to the Mysterious Breast (under the tongue), then descend of itself into the windpipe, and through the Florid Canopy and the Crimson Palace straight to the Cinnabar Field. The joy of body and mind at this time is beyond words. This is what is meant by “when Qian and Kun have finished their union, a single point falls into the Yellow Court.” If the student’s true fire is a little weak, add a little gentle fire to blow it, withdrawing and adding slightly, and again, as before, it rises behind and descends in front, circling without end — this is the River Chariot turning by itself. Rising behind and descending in front, it travels within the bones and joints, not within the skin. A poem says: “From the Cinnabar Field straight through to the summit of the Muddy Pellet, the River Chariot turns freely hundreds of times.” Then the lead is exhausted and the mercury dried! This is the fire phasing of the Greater Celestial Circuit.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§3
Original note (Chinese)

此段为大周天功法。尾闾在尾骶骨（尻脊）上第三节，丹书说是阴阳受化之乡，任督交会之处。太玄关即会阴穴，在两阴之间。肾堂在骶椎上五分之一处，相当于第一骶椎处，又名精门、肾关。泥丸即百会穴内。百会在两耳廓尖连线之中点，即头顶中央旋毛中。玄膺在舌下金津、玉液穴之间。华盖在胸骨内面，胸骨柄与胸骨体相交界水平。黄庭在腹内，相当于脐内一寸二分下田之上。

上等根器之人，行功到此，自然冲开尾闾；但中、下等根器者则未必能自然开关，须行聚火开关之法。而只有守胎息才能开关。《皇极阖辟仙经》说：“开关乃修真要务，胎息即证道根基。未有不守胎息而望开关，不待开关而能长生在世者”。古人云：“关未开，休打坐，如无麦子空挨磨”。盖只有关窍已开，才能行采药之事，否则，纵然遇到大药，而关窍不开，也是徒费精神，采亦全无应验。

开关亦有徵象，这就是丹田真气产生，俗称神炉药生，从而丹田火炽，两肾汤煎。必待至有此徵象，方可行开关之功。

This passage is the method of the Greater Celestial Circuit. The Caudal Funnel is at the third segment up from the tailbone (sacrum); the alchemical books call it the place where yin and yang receive transformation, the meeting point of the Conception and Governing vessels. The Pass of Great Mystery is the Perineum point, between the two lower orifices. The Kidney Hall is about one fifth of the way up the sacrum, corresponding to the first sacral vertebra; it is also called the Gate of Essence or the Kidney Pass. The Muddy Pellet is inside the Hundred Meetings point (baihui 百会). The Hundred Meetings point is at the midpoint of the line joining the tips of the two ears, that is, at the whorl of hair in the middle of the crown. The Mysterious Breast is under the tongue, between the Golden Fluid and Jade Liquid points. The Florid Canopy is on the inner surface of the breastbone, level with the junction of the manubrium and the body of the sternum. The Yellow Court is inside the abdomen, roughly above the lower field one cun two fen inward from the navel.

People of superior capacity naturally break through the Caudal Funnel when their practice reaches this point; but those of middling and lower capacity may not open the passes naturally and must use the method of gathering the fire and opening the passes. And only by guarding the embryonic breathing can the passes be opened. Perfected Yin’s Immortal Scripture of the Opening and Closing of the Supreme Ultimate says: “Opening the passes is the essential task of cultivating reality; embryonic breathing is the foundation for realizing the Dao. No one can hope to open the passes without guarding the embryonic breathing, and no one can live long in the world without first opening the passes.” The ancients said: “While the passes are not open, do not (merely) sit in meditation — it is like pushing an empty millstone with no wheat.” For only when the passes and openings are open can one proceed to collecting the Medicine; otherwise, even if one meets the Great Medicine, if the passes and openings are not open, one only wastes one’s energy, and the collecting will have no effect at all.

Opening the passes also has its signs: the true breath is produced in the Cinnabar Field, commonly called “the Medicine is born in the Furnace of Spirit,” and then the fire blazes in the Cinnabar Field and the broth simmers in the two kidneys. Only when these signs have appeared may one do the work of opening the passes.

Key Terms

  • Fluid into blood, blood into essence, essence into breath: the order of transformation in this part — into blood in the Crimson Gate, into essence in the two kidneys, into breath in the Sea of Breath.
  • Three treasures: essence, breath and spirit.
  • A force that has nowhere to go: when the essence and breath of the lower Cinnabar Field are full to the utmost, they naturally seek a way out and break through the passes.
  • Pass of Great Mystery: the Perineum point.
  • Kidney Hall: roughly at the first sacral vertebra; also called the Gate of Essence or the Kidney Pass.
  • Twenty-four joints: the spine.
  • Three Passes: the Caudal Funnel, the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow; the Jade Pillow is also called the “Iron Wall.”
  • Lifting and contracting the grain path: lifting the anus; this section says to use it “slightly.”
  • Windlass (lulu 辘轳): the winch over a well for drawing water, a metaphor for the circulating movement of breath.
  • Where the will goes the breath follows (zhi zhi qi ci 志至气次): from the Mencius, “where the will arrives, the breath follows.”
  • Mysterious Breast: under the tongue, between the Golden Fluid and Jade Liquid points.
  • Florid Canopy (huagai 华盖): the inner surface of the breastbone at the junction of the manubrium and the body of the sternum.
  • “When Qian and Kun have finished their union, a single point falls into the Yellow Court”: after Qian (the head) and Kun (the abdomen) meet, a point of true essence falls into the Yellow Court.
  • River Chariot turning by itself: the breath rising behind and descending in front, circulating naturally.
  • Greater Celestial Circuit: both the text of this section and Qiu Ling’s note call this the “Greater Celestial Circuit” (the meeting of cool fluid and warm breath in Section 3 is called the “Lesser Celestial Circuit”). Different alchemical books divide the greater and lesser circuits differently; see Celestial Circuit.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The precondition is “a force that has nowhere to go”: breaking through the passes is the natural result of the lower Cinnabar Field becoming full, not something pushed through by force. Qiu Ling’s note stresses again: wait for the signs of the Medicine born in the Furnace of Spirit, the fire blazing in the Cinnabar Field and the broth simmering in the two kidneys.
  2. “On no account be agitated and wish to hurry; simply practice day after day”: if it does not pass the Spine-Straddling Pass, simply practice as usual every day, without hurrying.
  3. On lifting and contracting the grain path: this section only says to use it “slightly.” Even if it is used, it is only a very light lift and release, in time with the breath — never holding the breath and lifting hard.
  4. When intense scenes appear: “Firmly guard body and mind, neither alarmed nor delighted, and slowly it settles by itself.” This is the most valuable sentence of the section.
  5. The breath travels within the bones and joints: the text stresses that rising behind and descending in front “travels within the bones and joints, not within the skin” — a deep, natural sensation, not a line drawn on the surface by the mind.

Translator's Notes

  1. “In a moment” (pianxiang 片响): should read 片晌, “a moment.”
  2. “Rising behind and descending in front, it travels within the bones and joints”: the Chinese 然后升前降 should probably be read as 然 (a conjunction, “and so”) + 后升前降 (“rising behind and descending in front”) rather than 然后 (“afterward”); the translation follows this reading.
  3. “The Principles”: that is, the Principles of Inner Nature and Existence.
  4. The “Greater Celestial Circuit” of this section differs in meaning from the “Greater Celestial Circuit” of Part One, The Secret of the Golden Flower, Chapter 12 (yin and yang in harmony, the Central Palace in its right place, all things flourishing), and also from the Greater Celestial Circuit of the Wu–Liu school, which comes “after the Great Medicine passes the gates.” The glossary entry “Celestial Circuit” notes this.
