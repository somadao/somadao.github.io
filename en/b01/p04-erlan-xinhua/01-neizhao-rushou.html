1 · Beginning Inner Illumination

1 · Beginning Inner Illumination — illuminating downward station by station until each is “pure white”

Listen to this chapterENEnglish

About This Chapter

Where does inner illumination begin? First close the eyes and regulate the breath for a while; only when the breath is regulated, use the intention to concentrate the spirit in the brain and glance lightly with the eyes toward the crown; you will feel a faint brightness, like moonlight on a dark night. Then with the intention lead this light to shine on the Muddy Pellet, until the brain is full of light and the head is clear as crystal; then lead the light through the throat to the Crimson Palace and illuminate it until it is pure white; then to the Central Yellow, until it is pure white; the brightness descends with the breath, and the lower Cinnabar Field gradually becomes wide and deep. After a long time, when you feel breath moving within, keep a single thought clear and empty, and with the intention lead the gaze around from the Sea Bottom to the back; the light passes through the Caudal Funnel and rises all the way to the crown. Finally, use “the method of watching over head and tail”: the light of the two eyes hangs in mid-air like sun and moon, shining down through the crown and the three passes all the way to the deepest Sea Bottom, where the reflection of a moon appears, mirroring the moon above — at this point, “I no longer feel that I have a body.” Qiu Ling’s note matches each step with a term from the alchemical scriptures; it is the most useful part of this section.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

内照从何下手？冥目调息片时，觉息调矣，始以意凝神于脑，以目光微向巅顶一看，觉有微明，如黑夜月色然，随即用意引此光映泥丸，待得脑中光满，而头若水晶然。

Where does inner illumination begin? Close the eyes and regulate the breath for a while. When you feel that the breath is regulated, only then use the intention to concentrate the spirit in the brain, and glance lightly with the eyes toward the crown; you will feel a faint brightness, like moonlight on a dark night. Then at once use the intention to lead this light to shine on the Muddy Pellet, and wait until the brain is full of light and the head is like crystal.

§2
Original (Chinese)

久之，乃引此明由重楼达绛宫，存之片晌，觉我绛宫纯白，随以意引到中黄，亦如上法存之，觉中黄纯白，其光明自觉随气下降，便觉下田渐渐宽阔而更幽深焉。内照至此，愈久愈明，而愈宽愈广。

After a while, lead this brightness through the Twelve-Storied Tower (the throat) to the Crimson Palace and keep it there for a moment, until you feel that my Crimson Palace is pure white. Then with the intention lead it to the Central Yellow and keep it there in the same way, until you feel that the Central Yellow is pure white. You will feel that the brightness descends with the breath, and the lower field gradually becomes wide and still deeper. When inner illumination has reached this point, the longer it goes on the brighter it becomes, and the wider and vaster.

§3
Original (Chinese)

久之又久，觉有气动于中，我则一念清虚，微以意引目光从海底兜照后去，未几觉此光明已透尾闾，渐渐有光自下升上，竟透达巅。

After a long, long time, when you feel breath moving within, keep a single thought clear and empty, and with a light intention lead the gaze around from the Sea Bottom (the perineum) to illuminate the back. Before long you will feel that the brightness has passed through the Caudal Funnel; gradually light rises from below until it reaches right through to the crown.

§4
Original (Chinese)

我于斯时用首尾照顾法。其法为何？我之两目光存在半天空，如日如月，下照巅顶，直透三关，照至极深海底，几然现有一轮月影，沉于海底，与上半天空月轮上下相映。我于斯际万籁皆空，惟用一意上冲下透并行不悖之诀。行之久久，觉此清光上透九霄，下破九渊。斯时我身已不觉有焉。内照入手如此。

At this time I use “the method of watching over head and tail.” What is this method? I visualize the light of my two eyes in mid-air, like the sun, like the moon, shining down on the crown, straight through the three passes, all the way to the deepest Sea Bottom; faintly there appears the reflection of a round moon, sunk at the Sea Bottom, mirroring the moon in mid-air above. At this moment all sounds are empty for me, and I use only the formula of “a single intention charging upward and passing downward, both going together without conflict.” After practicing this for a long time, I feel this clear light pierce the nine heavens above and break through the nine abysses below. At this time I no longer feel that I have a body. This is how inner illumination begins.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

此段功法，主要运用目光行之。盖古云：“机在目”。又云，人身遍体属阴，赖以化阴还阳者，靠两目。

引光照泥丸（在百会内）而使头若水晶，此即洗髓法。觉绛宫（在两乳之间膻中穴内一寸三分）纯白，此即洗心法。觉中黄（心后夹脊双关前夹缝中）纯白，此即净土法。觉下丹田（在脐内一寸三分处）渐宽，此即靖海法。觉有气动于中，此即龙从海底（即会阴穴）现。光明透尾闾，此即虎从水底翻。觉渐有光自下升上，此即黄河水逆流。竟透达巅，此即还精补脑。照至极深海底，此即日月耀金庭。月轮月影上下相映，此即水在长江月在天。光之所到，即变纯白，此部分豁然若失，最后我身已不觉有。然亦不是一朝一夕之功可以达此境界。高血压患者不宜练此功。

This method is carried out mainly with the gaze. For the ancients said: “The mechanism is in the eyes.” They also said: the whole human body belongs to yin, and what it relies on to transform yin and return to yang is the two eyes.

Leading the light to shine on the Muddy Pellet (inside the Hundred Meetings point) until the head is like crystal — this is the method of “washing the marrow.” Feeling the Crimson Palace (one cun three fen inward from the Chest Center point between the nipples) pure white — this is the method of “washing the heart.” Feeling the Central Yellow (in the gap behind the heart and in front of the Spine-Straddling Pass) pure white — this is the method of “purifying the land.” Feeling the lower Cinnabar Field (one cun three fen inward from the navel) gradually widen — this is the method of “calming the sea.” Feeling breath moving within — this is “the dragon appears from the Sea Bottom (the Perineum point).” The brightness passing through the Caudal Funnel — this is “the tiger turns over from the bottom of the water.” Feeling light gradually rise from below — this is “the Yellow River flows backward.” Reaching right through to the crown — this is “returning the essence to replenish the brain.” Shining all the way to the deepest Sea Bottom — this is “sun and moon shine on the Golden Court.” The moon and its reflection mirroring each other above and below — this is “the water is in the Long River, the moon is in the sky.” Wherever the light reaches becomes pure white, and that part suddenly seems to vanish, until finally I no longer feel that I have a body. But this state cannot be reached by the work of a day or a night. People with high blood pressure should not practice this method.

Key Terms

  • Inner illumination (neizhao 内照): that is, Reverting the Light, reverting the gaze and illuminating within — illuminating inward with the light of awareness.
  • Closing the eyes (mingmu 冥目).
  • Crown (dianding 巅顶): the top of the head.
  • Muddy Pellet: Qiu Ling’s note: inside the Hundred Meetings point.
  • Twelve-Storied Tower: the throat.
  • Crimson Palace: one cun three fen inward from the Chest Center point between the nipples.
  • Central Yellow: in the gap behind the heart and in front of the Spine-Straddling Pass.
  • Lower Cinnabar Field: Qiu Ling’s note here says “one cun three fen inward from the navel” (the preceding parts mostly say one cun two fen).
  • Sea Bottom (haidi 海底): the Perineum point.
  • Leading around (douzhao 兜照): illuminating by circling around from below.
  • Method of watching over head and tail: the light of the two eyes hangs in mid-air and shines down all the way to the Sea Bottom, watching over top and bottom, head and tail, at the same time.
  • Nine heavens, nine abysses: the highest heaven and the deepest abyss.
  • Washing the marrow, washing the heart, purifying the land, calming the sea: Qiu Ling’s names for the four stations of inner illumination (the Muddy Pellet, the Crimson Palace, the Central Yellow, the lower Cinnabar Field).
  • The dragon appears from the Sea Bottom, the tiger turns over from the bottom of the water, the Yellow River flows backward, returning the essence to replenish the brain, sun and moon shine on the Golden Court, the water is in the Long River and the moon is in the sky: common veiled expressions of the alchemical scriptures, which Qiu Ling matches one by one with the steps of this method.

Putting It into Practice

  1. First regulate the breath, then use the intention: “When you feel that the breath is regulated, only then use the intention to concentrate the spirit in the brain” — before the breathing is even, do not hurry into any visualization.
  2. Four stations, illuminating downward: the Muddy Pellet (the head clear as crystal) → the Crimson Palace (the chest pure white) → the Central Yellow (the middle between chest and back pure white) → the lower Cinnabar Field (gradually wider and deeper). Stay at each station until it feels “pure white” before going down; there is no need to do it all in one session.
  3. The light is light: the text says “glance lightly,” “with a light intention” — the gaze is only a light glance. Staring hard at the crown is the surest way to get a swollen head.
  4. The standard of this method is “pure white” and “wide”: wherever it shines becomes clear and spacious, as if washed with clean water.
  5. People with high blood pressure should not practice it (Qiu Ling). Beginners can also do only the second half: start illuminating downward from the Crimson Palace and the Central Yellow, without dwelling on the head.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Faintly there appears” (ji ran xian you 几然现有): ji ran means faintly, as if; the translation renders it “faintly.”
  2. “The lower Cinnabar Field (one cun three fen inward from the navel)”: Qiu Ling says “one cun three fen” here, while the preceding parts mostly say “one cun two fen”; it is given as in the base text.
  3. Qiu Ling did not divide the original text of this part into sections, giving only three passages; for ease of reading and recitation, the first passage is further divided here into §1–§4, and Qiu Ling’s note is placed after §4.
  4. The last suggestion (that beginners may start illuminating downward from the Crimson Palace and the Central Yellow) is the translator’s advice, not part of the original.
