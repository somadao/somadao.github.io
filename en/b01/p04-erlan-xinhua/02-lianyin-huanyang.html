2 · Refining Yin and Returning to Yang — what to do when the lower body is hot and cool breath flows down

About This Chapter

This section deals with a very practical situation that many practitioners meet: the lower body becomes hot, and at the height of the heat a few wisps of cool breath suddenly flow down from the chest and abdomen, or from behind the viscera and in front of the spine, and circle to the left and right of the genitals. The text says that this is the yin essence of the lower body beginning to transform on meeting the true breath, but the true breath is still weak; if it transforms but is not refined, it will combine with ordinary breath and become the essence of sexual intercourse — that is, it is about to leak. The remedy: concentrate the spirit, gather the breath at the Sea Bottom (the perineum), and push and stir it with the light of the two eyes, like turning a millstone; the stiller the mind becomes, the more the breathing stops, and the true breath stays in the lower body; the lower body becomes hot as a boiling cauldron, the yin essence turns into breath, attacks backward through the Caudal Funnel, rises to the Muddy Pellet, turns into true fluid and descends … This is the formula of “refining yin and returning to yang.” Qiu Ling’s note stresses that this is precisely a wonderful state, and that one must on no account stop practicing at this moment — stopping would do harm.

Safety note (translator): Qiu Ling’s note says that “advancing the yang fire” in this section is “holding the breath and visualizing.” Practice breath-holding methods only under the guidance of an experienced teacher; do not force yourself to hold your breath; they are unsuitable for people with high blood pressure or cardiovascular or cerebrovascular disease.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

如行功时，下部腾腾气热，忽于热极之际，得有几缕凉气或自胸腹下降，或自腑后脊前流下，溯洄于男根左右，此下部阴精遇炁而化，真炁力微，化而失炼，则与凡气合，将成交感之精。不进阳火，此物必将夺关而出。

If during practice the lower body becomes hot with rising breath, and suddenly, at the height of the heat, a few wisps of cool breath descend, some from the chest and abdomen, some flowing down from behind the viscera and in front of the spine, and circle to the left and right of the male root (the genitals), this is the yin essence of the lower body beginning to transform on meeting the true breath. But the true breath is still weak; transformed but not refined, it will combine with ordinary breath and is about to become the essence of sexual intercourse. If the yang fire is not advanced, this substance will surely force its way out through the pass (leak out).

§2
Original (Chinese)

法惟凝神集炁于海底，以两目光推而荡之，如转磨然。我于此际，此心愈加宁静，则呼吸气停而真炁得注留下部。下部斯得热如鼎沸，而阴精化气随炁，后攻穿尾闾，升至泥丸，化为真液，下降重楼，润绛宫，从心后脊前分达两肾。

The only method is to concentrate the spirit and gather the breath at the Sea Bottom (the perineum), and push and stir it with the light of the two eyes, like turning a millstone. If at this moment my mind becomes stiller and stiller, the breathing stops and the true breath can pour into and remain in the lower body. The lower body then becomes hot as a boiling cauldron, and the yin essence turns into breath; following the true breath, it attacks backward through the Caudal Funnel, rises to the Muddy Pellet, turns into true fluid, descends the Twelve-Storied Tower (the throat), moistens the Crimson Palace, and from behind the heart and in front of the spine reaches the two kidneys separately.

§3
Original (Chinese)

我则以两目光降送至肾，左右分旋，急旋急转，便热如火，由两肾热至脐轮。此一热也，须比前热数倍，斯物由真精化而为炁矣！从此不住手，其热复降至海底而仍行其存注之功，则如前云之阴精，又得化气而后升矣。炼阴还阳之诀，不外乎此。其效验可时见，而要妙在能恒久焉。果能循环无间，日行时作，何愁不如前贤所许，计月而成者哉。

With the light of my two eyes I then send it down to the kidneys and turn it separately on the left and right, turning it fast, and it becomes hot as fire; from the two kidneys the heat reaches the navel wheel. This heat must be several times greater than the earlier heat — the substance has turned from true essence into breath! From then on, do not stop; the heat descends again to the Sea Bottom, where one continues the work of keeping and pouring, and the yin essence spoken of before can again turn into breath and rise behind. The formula of refining yin and returning to yang is nothing other than this. Its effects can be seen at any time, but the key lies in being able to persevere. If one can truly keep it circulating without interruption, practicing it every day and at all times, why worry about not achieving, as the earlier worthies promised, success counted in months?

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§3
Original note (Chinese)

练功之时觉几缕凉气（即阴精）由体内四周流下，此正是妙境，切勿终止练功，终止则有弊。惟凝神集炁于海底，此为进阳火之秘诀，进阳火即是闭息存思。二气到泥丸，即是还精补脑，化为真液，乃后天甘露，古人认为是化血之物，肾热如火即是炼血化精，热至脐轮，乃炼气返炁。如此循环行功不可稍住，则不断炼精化炁，要炼到周身纯阳之后方无阴精，此乃炼阴还阳之诀。

When during practice one feels a few wisps of cool breath (that is, yin essence) flowing down from all around inside the body, this is precisely a wonderful state; on no account stop practicing, for stopping would do harm. Simply concentrate the spirit and gather the breath at the Sea Bottom: this is the secret formula for advancing the yang fire, and advancing the yang fire is holding the breath and visualizing. The two breaths reaching the Muddy Pellet is returning the essence to replenish the brain; it turns into true fluid, which is the sweet dew of Posterior Heaven, regarded by the ancients as something that turns into blood. The kidneys hot as fire is refining blood into essence; the heat reaching the navel wheel is refining breath and returning it to primordial breath. Circulating the practice like this without the slightest pause, one continuously refines essence into breath, until the whole body is pure yang and there is no more yin essence. This is the formula of refining yin and returning to yang.

Key Terms

  • Yin essence: the Posterior-Heaven essence of the lower body (the reproductive system), which begins to transform on meeting the true breath; Qiu Ling’s note: the cool breath is the yin essence.
  • Ordinary breath: the breath of Posterior Heaven.
  • Essence of sexual intercourse: the reproductive essence of Posterior Heaven; see Essence.
  • Forcing its way out through the pass: breaking through and leaking.
  • Advancing the yang fire: Qiu Ling’s note to this section explains it explicitly as “holding the breath and visualizing”; see fire phasing.
  • Sea Bottom: the perineum.
  • Pushing and stirring it, like turning a millstone: pushing and rotating at the Sea Bottom with the gaze (the intention).
  • True fluid / sweet dew of Posterior Heaven: see Sweet Dew.
  • Turning separately on the left and right: the gaze rotating separately at the left and right kidneys.
  • Refining yin and returning to yang: continuously refining yin essence into breath until the whole body is pure yang.
  • Success counted in months: success within a matter of months (as the ancients said).

Putting It into Practice

  1. Recognize the phenomenon: during practice the lower abdomen and lower body become hot, and at a certain point cool breath flows downward, with a tendency toward sexual arousal — the ancients recorded this phenomenon explicitly; there is no need to panic or to be embarrassed about facing it.
  2. The key is “the mind becoming stiller and stiller”: the heart of the text is not an action but that at this moment the mind becomes stiller. As soon as the mind is disturbed and thoughts run after it, it is “transformed but not refined.”
  3. Do not break off at this moment: Qiu Ling’s note: “This is precisely a wonderful state; on no account stop practicing, for stopping would do harm.”
  4. Keep the intention at the Sea Bottom, turn the gaze lightly: the spirit is concentrated in the perineum area and the gaze (the intention) turns lightly like a millstone — not contracting with force.
  5. About “holding the breath”: the text says “if my mind becomes stiller and stiller, the breathing stops” — the breathing stopping is the result of a still mind, not deliberate breath-holding. Please understand it in this order and do not reverse it by holding your breath to try to become still.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Flowing down from behind the viscera and in front of the spine” (fu hou ji qian 腑后脊前): behind the viscera and in front of the spine, that is, the rear part of the middle of the body.
  2. “Circle to the left and right of the male root”: the text is written from the point of view of a male practitioner. Women’s cultivation has its own system; Part Seven of this book, The Precious Raft of the Women’s School of Dual Cultivation, and the appendix, Commentary on Sun Bu’er’s Poems on the Stages of Women’s Alchemy, deal specifically with women’s alchemy and can be compared.
  3. The explanation of “holding the breath” in point 5 is the translator’s remark, based on the word order of the text: “if my mind becomes stiller and stiller, the breathing stops” expresses cause and effect.
