Chapter 6 · The Mao-You Celestial Circuit

Chapter 6 · The Mao-You Celestial Circuit — turning left and right, and a table of the many names of inner nature and existence

About This Chapter

The previous chapter was “collecting the Medicine”; this chapter is “concluding the work.” Once the Medicine has been collected and returned to the cauldron, if one does not practice the mao-you Celestial Circuit, it is like having a cart without wheels or a boat without a rudder, and the Golden Elixir that has formed will be dissipated. The method of the mao-you circuit is this: with the “master that moves the breath” (the gaze and the intention), start from the lower Cinnabar Field, illuminate up the left side to the crown, then descend on the right side back to the lower Cinnabar Field — that is one degree; thirty-six such degrees are “advancing the yang fire.” Then, in the opposite direction, twenty-four degrees are “withdrawing the yin response.” Day by day and month by month, “mercury gradually increases and lead gradually decreases,” until finally “yin is stripped away and yang is pure,” and the precious pearl of Muni is formed. The most precious part of Qiu Ling’s note is his full quotation of the “table of the different names of inner nature and existence” from the Principles of Inner Nature and Existence: dozens of names from the alchemical scriptures — dragon and tiger, sun and moon, Qian and Kun, the infant and the girl and so on — are assigned to two sides, inner nature at the crown and existence at the navel, sorting at a stroke a page full of veiled language.

Safety note (translator): This chapter involves pressing a “ritual implement” against the perineum and using the gaze and intention to guide the movement of the breath in regular rotations. This is still a deliberate guiding method; practice it only under the guidance of an experienced teacher. The approach of Part One, The Secret of the Golden Flower, explicitly rejects deliberately guiding the Celestial Circuit.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

前章先天大药入于黄庭，采药也。此章卯酉周天，左右旋转，收功也。张全一《铅火秘诀》云：“大药之生有时节，亥末子初正二刻。精神交媾含光华，恍恍惚惚生明月。媾毕留下喷泡然，一阳来复休轻泄。急须闭住太玄关（编者按：即会阴），火逼金过尾闾穴。采时用目守泥丸，垂于左上且凝歇。谓之专理脑生玄，右边放下复旋折。六爻数毕药升乾，阳极阴生往右迁。须开关门以退火，目光下瞩守坤田。右上左下六凝住，三八数了一周天。此是天然真火候，自然升降自抽添。也无弦望与晦朔，也无沐浴达长篇。异名剪除譬喻扫，只斯数语是真诠。”此于采药归壶后行之，则所结金丹不致耗散。大药采来归鼎，若不行卯酉周天之功，如有车无轮，有舟无舵，欲求远载其可得乎？

In the previous chapter the Great Medicine of Prior Heaven entered the Yellow Court — that was collecting the Medicine. This chapter is the mao-you Celestial Circuit, turning left and right — this is concluding the work. Zhang Quanyi (Zhang Sanfeng) says in his Secret Formula of Lead and Fire: “The birth of the Great Medicine has its season: at the end of hai and the beginning of zi, in the second quarter. Essence and spirit join, holding a radiant light; in vagueness and indistinctness a bright moon is born. When the joining is over, there remains a feeling like bubbles welling up — this is the return of the one yang; do not let it leak lightly. At once close the Pass of Great Mystery (editor’s note (Qiu Ling): that is, the Perineum); with fire drive the metal past the cavity of the Caudal Funnel. While collecting, guard the Muddy Pellet with the eyes; let them rest toward the upper left and pause there, congealed. This is called ‘attending solely to the brain to produce the mystery.' Then let it down on the right and turn back again. When the number of the six lines is complete, the Medicine has risen to Qian (the crown); yang at its extreme gives birth to yin, and it moves over to the right. Now open the gate of the pass to withdraw the fire; let the gaze look downward and guard the field of Kun (the lower Cinnabar Field). Upper right, lower left — congeal and pause at six points; when the number of three times eight is done, that is one Celestial Circuit. This is the natural, true fire phasing; it rises and descends by itself, withdraws and adds by itself. There is no waxing and waning, no full moon and new moon, nor long discourses on ablution. Cut away the different names, sweep away the metaphors: only these few lines are the true explanation.” If this is practiced after collecting the Medicine and returning it to the vessel, the Golden Elixir that has formed will not be dissipated. When the Great Medicine has been collected and returned to the cauldron, if one does not do the work of the mao-you Celestial Circuit, it is like having a cart without wheels or a boat without a rudder — how could one hope to carry it far?

Qiu Ling's noteon §1
Original note (Chinese)

本章后原有南樵子及闵小良氏之注。南樵子注曰：“后升前降，采外药也。左旋三十六以进阳火，右转二十四以退阴符，皆收内药而使来归壶，不致耗散也。日积月久，炼成一黍米之珠，以成真人者即此也”。闵小良注曰：“谨按此章，乃就一身中采取坎一以为种子，与上章经义不一，上章得自虚空，此章成自神功者也”。

Originally this chapter was followed by notes by Nanqiaozi and by Min Xiaoliang. Nanqiaozi’s note says: “Rising behind and descending in front is collecting the outer Medicine. Turning left thirty-six times to advance the yang fire and turning right twenty-four times to withdraw the yin response are both for gathering in the inner Medicine and making it return to the vessel so that it is not dissipated. Day by day and month by month, one refines a pearl the size of a grain of millet and becomes a Perfected — this is how.” Min Xiaoliang’s note says: “Respectfully noted: this chapter collects the one [yang] within Kan inside one’s own body as the seed, which differs from the meaning of the previous chapter; the previous chapter’s Medicine is obtained from empty space, while this chapter’s is formed by one’s own spiritual work.”

§2
Original (Chinese)

其法先以法器顶住太玄关口，次以行气主宰，下照坤脐，良久，徐徐从左上照乾顶，少停，从右下降坤脐为一度。如此三十六转，为进阳火。三十六度毕，去了法器，开关退火，亦用行气主宰，下照坤脐，良久，徐徐从右上照乾顶，少停，从左下降坤脐为一度。如此二十四，为退阴符。纯阳云：“有人问我修行法，遥指天边月一轮。”此即行气主宰之义也。

The method: first press the “ritual implement” against the opening of the Pass of Great Mystery (the Perineum); next, with the “master that moves the breath,” illuminate downward on the navel of Kun (the lower Cinnabar Field) for a good while; then slowly illuminate up the left side to the summit of Qian (the crown), pause a little, and descend on the right side back to the navel of Kun — that is one degree. Turn like this thirty-six times: this is “advancing the yang fire.” When the thirty-six degrees are complete, remove the ritual implement, open the pass and withdraw the fire. Again with the master that moves the breath, illuminate downward on the navel of Kun for a good while; then slowly illuminate up the right side to the summit of Qian, pause a little, and descend on the left side back to the navel of Kun — that is one degree. Turn like this twenty-four times: this is “withdrawing the yin response.” Lü Chunyang said: “Someone asked me the method of practice; I pointed far off to the round moon at the edge of the sky.” This is the meaning of the “master that moves the breath.”

Qiu Ling's noteon §2
Original note (Chinese)

《性命圭旨》云：“然而金丹大道之秘，秘在性命两字。性者天也，常潜于顶，故顶者性之根也。命者海也，常潜于脐，故脐者命之蒂也”。又云：丹经对此巧喻多端，万字千名不可胜教，“如论顶中之性者，喻之曰汞也、龙也、火也、根也、日也、魂也、离也、乾也、己也、天也、君也、虚也、兔也、无也、主也、浮也、朱砂也、扶桑也、姹女也、昆仑也。如论脐中之命者，喻之曰铅也，虎也、水也、蒂也、月也、魄也、坎也、坤也、戊也、地也、臣也、实也、乌也、有也、宾也、沉也、水银也、华岳也，婴儿也、曲江也。”又云：“故盲修者歧而二之，若真修者合而一之。合一者，炼炁而凝神，尽性而至命，烹铅而乾汞，取坎而填离。”

一升一降，左旋右转，抽铅添汞，均与乾顶（泥丸）有密切关系。抽铅添汞实是还精补脑。经云：“欲得不老，还精补脑”。《黄庭经》云：“泥丸百节皆有神”。又云：“脑神精根字泥丸”。《性命圭旨》说：“真铅入鼎之时，须要驱除杂念，奋速精神，目视顶门，用志不分。”

The Principles of Inner Nature and Existence says: “The secret of the Great Way of the Golden Elixir lies in two words: inner nature and existence. Inner nature is Heaven; it always lies hidden at the crown, so the crown is the root of inner nature. Existence is the sea; it always lies hidden at the navel, so the navel is the stem of existence.” It also says that the alchemical scriptures have so many clever metaphors for these two that their thousands of names and words could never all be taught: “When speaking of the inner nature at the crown, they call it mercury, dragon, fire, root, sun, hun soul, Li, Qian, ji, Heaven, sovereign, empty, hare, non-being, host, floating, cinnabar, Fusang, the girl, Kunlun. When speaking of the existence at the navel, they call it lead, tiger, water, stem, moon, po soul, Kan, Kun, wu, Earth, minister, full, crow, being, guest, sinking, quicksilver, Mount Hua, the infant, the Winding River.” It also says: “So those who cultivate blindly divide them into two, while those who truly cultivate unite them into one. To unite them into one is to refine the breath and congeal the spirit, to fulfill inner nature and reach existence, to cook the lead and dry the mercury, to take from Kan and fill Li.”

One rising and one descending, turning left and turning right, withdrawing lead and adding mercury — all are closely connected with the summit of Qian (the Muddy Pellet). Withdrawing lead and adding mercury is in fact “returning the essence to replenish the brain.” A scripture says: “If you wish not to grow old, return the essence to replenish the brain.” The Scripture of the Yellow Court (Huangting jing 黄庭经) says: “The Muddy Pellet and all the hundred joints have their spirits.” And: “The spirit of the brain is called Essence Root; its style is Muddy Pellet.” The Principles of Inner Nature and Existence says: “When true lead enters the cauldron, you must drive away stray thoughts, rouse your spirit, look with the eyes at the gate at the crown, and keep your will undivided.”

§3
Original (Chinese)

此功与采药归壶之功共是一连，采取药物于曲江之下，聚火载金于乾顶之上，乾坤交媾于九宫，周天运转而凝结，故清者凝结于乾顶，浊者流归于坤炉。逐日如此抽添，如此交媾，汞渐多而铅渐少，久而铅枯汞乾，阴剥阳纯，结成牟尼宝珠，是为金液大还丹也。盖坎中之铅，原是九天之真精。离中之汞，原是九天之真气。始因乾体一破，二物遂分两弦，常人日离日分，分尽而死；而至人法坤乾之体，效坎离之用，夺神功，改天命，而求坎中之铅制离中之汞，取坎中之阳制离中之阴，盖阳纯而复成乾元之体也。紫阳曰：“取将坎位中心实，点化离宫腹内阴，自此变成乾健体，潜藏飞跃尽由心。”

This work and the work of collecting the Medicine and returning it to the vessel form one continuous whole. The Medicine is collected below the Winding River (the lower Cinnabar Field); the fire is gathered and the metal carried up to the summit of Qian (the Muddy Pellet); Qian and Kun join in the nine palaces; the Celestial Circuit turns and congeals. So the clear congeals at the summit of Qian, and the turbid flows back to the furnace of Kun. Withdrawing and adding like this every day, joining like this, mercury gradually increases and lead gradually decreases; in time the lead is exhausted and the mercury dried, yin is stripped away and yang is pure, and the precious pearl of Muni is formed — this is the Great Returned Elixir of Golden Liquid. For the lead within Kan was originally the true essence of the Nine Heavens, and the mercury within Li was originally the true breath of the Nine Heavens. Only because the body of Qian was once broken did these two things divide into the two quarters of the moon. Ordinary people separate and divide further day by day, and when the division is complete they die. But the accomplished person takes as model the substance of Kun and Qian and imitates the function of Kan and Li; he seizes the work of the spirits and changes his heavenly destiny, seeking the lead within Kan to subdue the mercury within Li, taking the yang within Kan to subdue the yin within Li, so that yang becomes pure and again forms the substance of the originating Qian. Zhang Ziyang said: “Take the full line at the heart of the Kan position, and with it transform the yin within the belly of the Li palace; from then on it becomes the strong body of Qian — hiding deep or soaring high, all depends on the mind.”

Qiu Ling's noteon §3
Original note (Chinese)

张紫阳《悟真篇》曰：“取将坎位中心实，点化离宫腹内阴，从此变成乾健体，潜藏飞跃总由心。”董德宁于《悟真篇正义》中注曰：“先天卦位乾南而坤北，后天卦位坎北而离南，盖先天之乾坤交，则乾中虚而为离（即☲），其坤中实而为坎（编者按：即☵），于是乾坤退居于西之南北，而坎离进位于正之南北，则谓之后天矣！故先天以乾坤为尊，是乃体也；而后天以坎离为贵，是乃用也。今修丹之道，是将后天之用以复其先天之体，故炼取坎中之阳气（即☵之中爻），以点化离中之真阴（即☲之中爻），乃运行交会，而复其乾健纯阳之体，则吾身修炼之造化毕矣！潜藏者，沉伏也。飞跃者，升举也。谓此之采取点化，乃升沉举伏之法，而总在吾之心意，以为运用之枢机耳！”

行卯酉周天须运用两眼神光。张紫阳说：“只要两眼皎，上下交相用。须向静中行，莫向忙里送。”眼者阳窍也，人之一身皆属阴，惟有这点阳，以这点阳从下至上，从左至右转而又转，就能战退群阴，使阳道日长，阴道日消，使真气上下循环，此眼之功用也。

Zhang Ziyang’s Awakening to Reality says: “Take the full line at the heart of the Kan position, and with it transform the yin within the belly of the Li palace; from then on it becomes the strong body of Qian — hiding deep or soaring high, all depends on the mind.” Dong Dening comments in his Correct Meaning of Awakening to Reality (Wuzhen pian zhengyi 悟真篇正义): “In the arrangement of the trigrams of Prior Heaven, Qian is in the south and Kun in the north; in the arrangement of Posterior Heaven, Kan is in the north and Li in the south. When Qian and Kun of Prior Heaven joined, the middle line of Qian became empty and it became Li (☲), and the middle line of Kun became full and it became Kan (editor’s note (Qiu Ling): ☵). Then Qian and Kun withdrew to the north and south of the west (northwest and southwest), and Kan and Li advanced to the true south and north — and this is called Posterior Heaven! So Prior Heaven honors Qian and Kun: this is the substance; and Posterior Heaven values Kan and Li: this is the function. Now the way of cultivating the elixir is to use the function of Posterior Heaven to restore the substance of Prior Heaven: so one refines and takes the yang breath within Kan (the middle line of ☵) to transform the true yin within Li (the middle line of ☲), lets them move and meet, and restores the strong, pure-yang body of Qian — and then the creative work of cultivating my body is complete! ‘Hiding deep’ means sinking and lying low; ‘soaring high’ means rising and lifting. That is, this collecting and transforming is a method of rising and sinking, lifting and lying low, and it all lies in my mind and intention, which serve as the pivot and mechanism of its operation!”

The mao-you Celestial Circuit must use the spiritual light of the two eyes. Zhang Ziyang said: “Just let the two eyes be bright, used alternately above and below. You must do it in stillness, not push it in a hurry.” The eyes are yang openings. The whole human body belongs to yin; only this point is yang. When this point of yang turns from below to above and from left to right, again and again, it can defeat the host of yin, so that the way of yang grows day by day and the way of yin wanes day by day, and the true breath circulates up and down — this is the function of the eyes.

Key Terms

  • Mao-you Celestial Circuit: the circuit that turns left and right (as opposed to the “zi-wu Celestial Circuit,” which rises behind and descends in front).
  • Zhang Quanyi: Zhang Sanfeng (personal name Quanyi).
  • Pass of Great Mystery: the Perineum (Qiu Ling’s note).
  • Ritual implement (faqi 法器): the text does not say what it is; see the Translator’s Notes.
  • Master that moves the breath: the gaze and the True Intention as the master that guides the movement of breath; “I pointed far off to the round moon at the edge of the sky.”
  • Navel of Kun / field of Kun / furnace of Kun: the lower Cinnabar Field; summit of Qian: the Muddy Pellet (the crown).
  • Advancing the yang fire, withdrawing the yin response: turning left thirty-six times is advancing the yang fire, turning right twenty-four times is withdrawing the yin response; see fire phasing.
  • Six lines, three times eight: veiled expressions for the numbers thirty-six and twenty-four.
  • Waxing and waning, full and new moon: phases of the moon, often used in the alchemical scriptures as metaphors for the stages of fire phasing.
  • Outer Medicine, inner Medicine: in Nanqiaozi’s note, rising behind and descending in front collects the outer Medicine; turning left and right gathers in the inner Medicine.
  • Lead and Mercury / withdrawing lead and adding mercury: reducing lead (existence, breath) and increasing mercury (inner nature, spirit); see the table of different names from the Principles of Inner Nature and Existence quoted by Qiu Ling.
  • Returning the essence to replenish the brain: an ancient saying of nourishing life, meaning that essence and breath travel upward to nourish the brain.
  • Precious pearl of Muni / Great Returned Elixir of Golden Liquid: metaphors for the completed elixir.
  • Taking from Kan to fill Li: filling the yin within Li with the yang within Kan to restore the body of Qian; see Part One, Chapter 8.
  • Trigrams of Prior Heaven and Posterior Heaven: the arrangement of Fu Xi (Qian south, Kun north) and that of King Wen (Li south, Kan north).

Putting It into Practice

  1. The real value of this chapter is the “table of different names”: the Principles of Inner Nature and Existence divides dozens of the most common veiled terms of the alchemical scriptures into two groups, “inner nature at the crown” and “existence at the navel.” From now on, whenever you read an alchemical book and meet dragon and tiger, sun and moon, crow and hare, the infant and the girl, cinnabar and quicksilver, Kunlun and the Winding River … first see which group it belongs to, and you will know whether it is talking about spirit (inner nature) or breath (existence). This table has been included in this site’s glossary.
  2. “Those who cultivate blindly divide them into two; those who truly cultivate unite them into one”: all the paired veiled terms must finally return to “refining the breath and congealing the spirit, fulfilling inner nature and reaching existence” — the union of spirit and breath.
  3. Treat the method of the mao-you circuit as information only: pressing a ritual implement against the perineum and guiding with a fixed number of rotations is a deliberate guiding exercise; Qiu Ling’s note to Chapter 4 of this same part explains that methods of this kind are supplements for people of middling and lower capacity.
  4. The principle for using the eyes: “You must do it in stillness, not push it in a hurry” — even when guiding, it must be done quietly and gently.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Ritual implement”: the text does not say what it is. Some lineages say it means pressing the heel against the perineum; others say it is a specially made object. The book does not explain, so the translation keeps the original term without speculating.
  2. “When the joining is over, there remains a feeling like bubbles welling up”: describing the sensation, after the joining, of something like bubbles surging up inside the body; translated literally.
  3. “Nor long discourses on ablution”: that is, there is no need for long treatises on ablution; the translation follows this sense.
  4. “Qian and Kun withdrew to the north and south of the west”: that is, Qian withdrew to the northwest and Kun to the southwest (the positions of the Posterior Heaven arrangement); the translation adds this in parentheses.
  5. “Cook the lead and dry the mercury”: gan 乾 here is read “dry,” meaning to dry and fix it.
