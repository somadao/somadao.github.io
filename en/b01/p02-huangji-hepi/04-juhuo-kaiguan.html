Chapter 4 · Gathering the Fire and Opening the Passes

Chapter 4 · Gathering the Fire and Opening the Passes — breaking through the three passes (the Lesser Celestial Circuit)

Listen to this chapterENEnglish

About This Chapter

This chapter is about “opening the passes.” On the basis of the previous three chapters — “the Medicine is born, the fire blazes, the broth simmers” — the breath is sunk with the intention to the Caudal Funnel, twisted together with “the true fire within water” into a single strand, and made to break through in turn the three passes of the Caudal Funnel, the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow, straight up to the Muddy Pellet at the crown. Then the Conception and Governing vessels meet naturally, and the breath “descends in front and rises behind,” turning like a wheel — this is what is usually called the Lesser Celestial Circuit. Qiu Ling’s note sums it up as the four-word formula “inhale, lick, contract, close,” and states clearly that people of superior capacity need only keep the spirit on the Spine-Straddling Pass and practice without stopping, and when the breath is full it will break through the passes by itself; the forceful method of this chapter is “devised for people of middling and lower capacity.”

Safety note (translator): This chapter contains forceful operations such as holding the breath, tightly contracting the anus (lifting the anus), guiding the breath with the intention to charge through, and continuing to charge even when “stabbing pain” appears. These are recognized as the points in inner alchemy where deviations most easily occur, and the approach clearly differs from that of Part One, The Secret of the Golden Flower (“the Celestial Circuit is not led by the breath”; “if you add the slightest deliberate intention, it becomes a heterodox way”). This site only translates the texts. Without a reliable teacher guiding you in person, please do not follow these instructions; they are especially unsuitable for people with high blood pressure, cardiovascular or cerebrovascular disease, glaucoma, hernia or hemorrhoids, during pregnancy, or with any chronic illness.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

开关乃修真首务，胎息即证道根基。未有不守胎息而望开关，不待开关而能得长生者。许旌阳曰：“关未开，休打坐，如无麦子空挨磨。”

Opening the passes is the first task in cultivating reality; embryonic breathing is the foundation for realizing the Dao. No one can hope to open the passes without guarding the embryonic breathing, and no one can attain long life without first opening the passes. Xu Jingyang said: “While the passes are not open, do not (merely) sit in meditation — it is like pushing an empty millstone with no wheat.”

§2
Original (Chinese)

开关之法，择日入室静定，开天门以采先天，闭地户以守胎息，谨候神炉药生，丹田火炽，两肾汤煎。见此功效，上闭巽窍，塞兑垂帘，神息归根，以意引气沉于尾闾，自与水中真火纽作一股，直撞三关。当此之时，切勿散漫，倒提金锁锁，以心役神，以神驭气，以气冲火，火炽金熔，默默相冲，自一息至数百息，必要撞开尾闾。火逼金过太元关，而闾口内觉刺痛，此乃尾闾关开之验。一意后冲，紧撮谷道，以鼻息在闾抽吸，内提上去，如推车上高坡陡处，似撑船到急水滩头，不得停篙住手，猛烹急炼，直逼上升，再经夹脊双关，仍然刺痛，此又二关开通之验。以神合气，以气凝神，舌柱上腭，目视顶门，运过玉枕，直达泥丸顶上，融融温暖。

The method for opening the passes: choose a day, enter the quiet room and settle into stillness; open the Gate of Heaven to collect Prior Heaven, close the Door of Earth to guard the embryonic breathing, and wait attentively until the Medicine is born in the Furnace of Spirit, the fire blazes in the Cinnabar Field and the broth simmers in the two kidneys. When you see these effects, close the opening of Xun above, shut the mouth and lower the curtains of the eyes, let spirit and breath return to the root, and with the intention lead the breath down to sink into the Caudal Funnel; there it will naturally twist together with the true fire within water into a single strand and charge straight at the three passes. At this time, on no account let yourself become slack. “Lift the golden lock upside down” (tighten the lower body); use the mind to command the spirit, the spirit to drive the breath, the breath to stir the fire; the fire blazes and the metal melts. Charge silently, from one breath to several hundred breaths, until you have broken through the Caudal Funnel. The fire drives the metal past the Pass of Great Origin (the Perineum), and a stabbing pain is felt inside the opening of the Caudal Funnel — this is the sign that the pass of the Caudal Funnel has opened. Then, with a single intention, charge backward; tightly contract the “grain path” (lift the anus); with the breath of the nose draw and suck at the Caudal Funnel, lifting it up from within, like pushing a cart up the steepest part of a high slope, or poling a boat to the head of a rapid — you must not stop poling or let go. Cook fiercely and refine urgently, driving it straight upward. Passing through the Spine-Straddling Pass, there is again a stabbing pain — this is the sign that the second pass has opened. Then join breath with spirit and congeal spirit with breath, press the tongue against the palate, look with the eyes toward the gate at the crown, move it past the Jade Pillow and straight up to the top of the Muddy Pellet, where there is a melting warmth.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§2
Original note (Chinese)

聚火开关，即聚火撞开尾闾关，并上冲夹脊、玉枕（在后脑骨下）二关，谓之三关。何谓聚火之法？此法即吸、舐、撮、闭四字诀。吸者鼻中吸气以接先天；舐者舌柱上腭以迎甘露，撮者紧撮谷道内提顶上；闭者闭目垂帘兼逆听神水落黄庭也。此章功法妙处在神气纽作一股，默默透后上冲，次而如推车上半山，似渡过急流水，必须登巅达岸而后已。

Gathering the fire and opening the passes means gathering the fire to break through the pass of the Caudal Funnel and then charging up through the two passes of the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow (below the occipital bone); together they are called the Three Passes. What is the method of gathering the fire? It is the four-word formula: inhale, lick, contract, close. “Inhale” means drawing breath in through the nose to receive Prior Heaven; “lick” means pressing the tongue against the palate to welcome the Sweet Dew; “contract” means tightly contracting the grain path and lifting it from within up to the crown; “close” means closing the eyes and lowering the curtains, while also listening inward as the divine water falls into the Yellow Court. The wonder of this chapter’s method lies in spirit and breath being twisted into a single strand that silently passes through the back and charges upward — then it is like pushing a cart halfway up a mountain, or like crossing a rushing stream: you must reach the summit or the far bank before you stop.

§3
Original (Chinese)

息数周天数足，以目左旋三十六转，铅与汞合，真气入脑而化为髓，再候药生，仍行前功。每日昼夜，或行五、七、九次，行之百日，任督自然交会，一元上下，旋转如轮，前降后升，络绎不绝，内有一股氤氲之气，如云如雾，腾腾上升，冲透三关，直达紫府。渐采渐凝，久之金气布满九宫，补脑之余，化而为甘露，异香异美，降入口中，以意送入黄庭土府，散于百络，否则送炉。如是三关透彻，百脉调和，一身畅快，上下流通，所谓醍醐灌顶得清凉，同入混炉大道场者此也。百日之功，无间时刻，关窍大开，方可行采药归壶之事。不然，纵遇大药，而关窍不开，徒费神机，采亦全无应验。

When the number of breaths has completed the number of a circuit, rotate the eyes to the left thirty-six times; lead and mercury join, and the true breath enters the brain and turns into marrow. Then wait for the Medicine to be born again, and repeat the previous work. Each day and night, practice five, seven or nine times. After a hundred days of this, the Conception and Governing vessels will meet naturally; the one primal breath goes up and down, turning like a wheel, descending in front and rising behind, continuously without end. Within there is a dense, rich breath, like cloud and mist, rising and rising, charging through the three passes straight up to the Purple Palace (the Muddy Pellet). Gradually collected and gradually congealed, in time the golden breath fills the nine palaces of the head; what is left over after replenishing the brain turns into sweet dew, of extraordinary fragrance and taste, which descends into the mouth. With the intention, send it into the earthen residence of the Yellow Court and let it spread through all the network vessels; otherwise, send it to the furnace (the lower Cinnabar Field). In this way the three passes are thoroughly opened, all the vessels are in harmony, the whole body feels free and comfortable, and above and below flow freely. This is what is meant by “the ghee poured on the crown brings coolness; together we enter the great place of practice of the mixing furnace.” The work of a hundred days, without a moment’s interruption, opens the passes and openings wide; only then may one proceed to “collecting the Medicine and returning it to the vessel.” Otherwise, even if one meets the Great Medicine, if the passes and openings are not open, one only wastes one’s spiritual energy, and the collecting will have no effect at all.

§4
Original (Chinese)

张三丰云：“不炼还丹先炼性，未修大道且修心，修心自然丹性至，性至然后药材生”。还虚子曰：“开关之法，妙在神守双关一窍”。此窍能通十二经络，善透八万四千毛窍，神凝于此，闭息行持，久之自然精满气化，气满自然冲开三关，流通百脉，畅于四肢，窍窍光明，此为上根利器也。然于中下之士，或又行功怠缓，则关窍难开，必得丹田火炽，两肾汤蒸，依法运行，方能开通。故经云：“天之神栖于日，人之神栖于目”。古人谓：目之所至，神亦至焉，神之所至气亦至焉。又云：神行则气行，神住则气住。开关功夫，不外乎此。

Zhang Sanfeng said: “Before refining the Returned Elixir, first refine inner nature; before cultivating the Great Dao, first cultivate the mind. When the mind is cultivated, the nature of the elixir arrives of itself; when inner nature arrives, then the ingredients of the Medicine are born.” Huanxuzi said: “The wonder of the method for opening the passes lies in keeping the spirit on the single opening of the Double Pass.” This opening communicates with the twelve meridians and readily reaches the eighty-four thousand pores. When the spirit is concentrated here and one holds the breath and perseveres, in time essence naturally becomes full and transforms into breath, and when the breath is full it naturally breaks through the three passes, flows through all the vessels, spreads to the four limbs, and every opening becomes bright — this is for people of superior capacity, sharp instruments. But for people of middling and lower capacity, or those who are slack and slow in practice, the passes and openings are hard to open; only when the fire blazes in the Cinnabar Field and the broth steams in the two kidneys, and they proceed according to the method above, can they open them. That is why the scripture says: “The spirit of Heaven dwells in the sun; the spirit of a person dwells in the eyes.” The ancients said: where the eyes go, the spirit goes too; where the spirit goes, the breath goes too. And also: when the spirit moves, the breath moves; when the spirit stops, the breath stops. The work of opening the passes is nothing other than this.

Qiu Ling's noteon §3–§4
Original note (Chinese)

上根利器之人神守夹脊双关一窍，练功不辍，久之气满，自然能冲开三关；但中下之士，又行功怠缓，则关窍难开，必须依此法运行，方能开通。故此章功法乃为中下之士而设者也。本功法亦补第一章功法所未示，恐学者专事中透捷法，即循穷想山根，意守中黄而通天心之黄道，而置任脉督脉而不顾，则未免功缺。

People of superior capacity, sharp instruments, keep the spirit on the single opening of the Spine-Straddling Pass and practice without stopping; in time the breath becomes full and can naturally break through the three passes. But people of middling and lower capacity, who are also slack and slow in practice, find the passes hard to open, and must proceed according to this method to open them. So the method of this chapter is devised for people of middling and lower capacity. This method also supplements what the method of Chapter 1 did not show: there is a fear that students will practice only the “shortcut of passing through the center” — the Yellow Path that runs from thinking intently of the Root of the Mountain and holding the intention on the Central Yellow through to the Celestial Heart — and leave the Conception and Governing vessels aside, in which case their work would inevitably be incomplete.

Key Terms

  • Opening the passes: breaking through the three passes and achieving the Lesser Celestial Circuit.
  • Three Passes: the pass of the Caudal Funnel (between the third vertebrae counting up from the tailbone), the Spine-Straddling Pass (between the seventh and eighth thoracic vertebrae) and the Jade Pillow (below the occipital bone).
  • Xu Jingyang: Xu Xun, a Daoist of the Jin dynasty, patriarch of the Jingming (Pure Brightness) school.
  • Opening of Xun (xunqiao 巽窍): not explained in the text. Xun is wind, and it is generally understood as the nostrils, connected with breathing.
  • Shutting the mouth (sai dui 塞兑): dui is the mouth; from the Laozi: “Block the openings, shut the doors.”
  • “Lift the golden lock upside down”: tighten the perineum and anus and lift upward.
  • Pass of Great Origin (taiyuan guan 太元关) / Pass of Great Mystery: the Perineum (Qiu Ling’s note in Chapter 6).
  • True fire within water: the single point of true yang within the water of Kan.
  • Contracting the grain path: contracting the anus (lifting the anus).
  • Tongue pressed against the palate: the tip of the tongue touching the palate, that is, “building the Magpie Bridge.”
  • Rotating the eyes to the left thirty-six times: turning the eyes (the gaze, the intention) to the left thirty-six times.
  • Purple Palace, nine palaces: the head, that is, the Muddy Pellet and its surroundings.
  • Sweet Dew / ghee poured on the crown: the cool fluid that descends from the head.
  • Earthen residence of the Yellow Court: the middle Cinnabar Field.
  • Four-word formula: inhale, lick, contract, close.
  • Shortcut of passing through the center: the shortcut that goes straight along the “Yellow Path” on the midline of the body (the method of Chapter 1).
  • Conception and Governing vessels: the Conception vessel runs along the midline of the front of the body, the Governing vessel along the midline of the spine at the back.

Putting It into Practice

  1. First look at the precondition: the precondition for opening the passes in this chapter is the three signs of Chapter 3 — the Medicine born in the Furnace of Spirit, the fire blazing in the Cinnabar Field, the broth simmering in the two kidneys. If these signs have not appeared, there is no question of opening the passes.
  2. Keep the two routes apart:
    • The route of superior capacity: keep the spirit on the Spine-Straddling Pass, practice without stopping, and when the breath is full it breaks through the passes by itself (the “shortcut of passing through the center” of Chapter 1, consistent with The Secret of the Golden Flower).
    • The route of middling and lower capacity: this chapter’s forceful method of “inhale, lick, contract, close” to charge through the passes.
  3. Treat the forceful actions of this chapter as information only: holding the breath, lifting the anus, charging fiercely with the intention, and continuing to charge despite stabbing pain all carry a risk of deviation (a swollen or aching head, rising blood pressure, stagnant breath and tightness in the chest, insomnia, local pain and so on). The old book describes “stabbing pain” as a sign of progress; modern readers should not take this as a reason to endure pain.
  4. The most valuable sentence: “When the spirit moves, the breath moves; when the spirit stops, the breath stops.” Wherever the intention goes, the breath follows — which is exactly why the intention must be light and steady.
  5. Common basic skills: the tongue against the palate, lowering the curtains of the eyes, a mind that is not slack — these are safe and shared by the methods of all schools.

Translator's Notes

  1. “While the passes are not open, do not sit in meditation”: this does not mean one should not sit; it means that without opening the passes one merely sits in vain and makes no progress. The translation adds “(merely)” in parentheses.
  2. “Opening of Xun”: the book does not explain it. Chapter 5 has the phrase “thunder roars at the door of Xun, lightning flashes from the gate of Kun.” The translation keeps the original name and notes the usual understanding under Key Terms.
  3. “Lift the golden lock upside down”: the base text reads 倒提金锁锁, probably for 倒提金锁, with the second 锁 a verb, “to lock.” The translation renders it “lift the golden lock upside down (tighten the lower body).”
  4. “Rotate the eyes to the left thirty-six times”: the text does not say whether the eyeballs are turned or whether the gaze (the intention) rotates. Schools differ; the translation follows the wording without elaborating.
  5. “Otherwise, send it to the furnace”: that is, if it is not sent into the Yellow Court, it is sent into the Furnace of Spirit, the lower Cinnabar Field.
