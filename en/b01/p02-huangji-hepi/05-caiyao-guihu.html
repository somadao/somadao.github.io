Chapter 5 · Collecting the Medicine and Returning It to the Vessel — the zi hour, and what true lead and true mercury feel like

About This Chapter

Once the passes have opened, one can “collect the Medicine.” This chapter says that the Medicine should be collected at midnight, at the zi hour, when the one yang first stirs; at that moment the yin and yang of Heaven and Earth are meeting, the human body corresponds to Heaven and Earth, and one can “steal the mechanism of Heaven and Earth and seize the wonder of yin and yang.” The method is to sit quietly with a clear mind at the end of the hai hour and the beginning of the zi hour, with not a single thought arising, in utmost emptiness and steadfast stillness; then the Great Medicine of Prior Heaven enters the Yellow Court along with the breath. The chapter describes this moment with a long string of splendid images — thunder roars at the door of Xun, lightning flashes from the gate of Kun, yellow sprouts cover the ground, white snow fills the sky — and ends with “the essential formula of sealing.” Qiu Ling’s note is long; its most valuable part is a full quotation from Huang Yuanji of the Qing dynasty explaining “lead and mercury.” Huang says very concretely what true lead and true mercury are and what they feel like in the body; Qiu Ling calls it “real experience from practice.”

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

采药必用夜半子时一阳初动者，其时太阳正在北方，而人身之气在尾闾，正与天地相应，乃可以盗天地之机，夺阴阳之妙，炼魂魄而为一，合性命以双修。盖此时乃坤复之际，天地开辟于此时，日月合璧于此时，草木萌蘖于此时，人身阴阳交会于此时。至人于此时而采药，则内征外应，若合符节，乃天人合发之机，至玄而至妙者也。经云：“食其时，百骸理。盗其机，万化安。”又云：“每当天地交合时，盗取阴阳造化机。”

The Medicine must be collected at midnight, at the zi hour, when the one yang first stirs. At that time the sun is due north (below the earth) and the breath of the human body is at the Caudal Funnel, in exact correspondence with Heaven and Earth; only then can one steal the mechanism of Heaven and Earth, seize the wonder of yin and yang, refine the hun and po souls into one, and unite inner nature and existence in dual cultivation. For this moment is the juncture between Kun ䷁ and Fu ䷗ — the first birth of one yang within pure yin. Heaven and Earth open at this moment, sun and moon join at this moment, plants sprout at this moment, and the yin and yang of the human body meet at this moment. When the accomplished person collects the Medicine at this moment, the signs within and the responses without match like the two halves of a tally. This is the moment when Heaven and humanity are set in motion together, most mysterious and most wonderful. The scripture says: “Eat at the right time and all the bones are in order; steal the mechanism and all transformations are at peace.” It also says: “Whenever Heaven and Earth join, steal the creative mechanism of yin and yang.”

Qiu Ling's noteon §1
Original note (Chinese)

此章论夜半正子时采药法。亥末子初之时，即晚间十一时，此时乃天人合发之机。药即先天一炁。《仙佛合宗》云：“金丹根本喻药物，”“即吾身中一点真阳之精炁，号曰先天祖炁者是也”。

This chapter discusses the method of collecting the Medicine at the exact zi hour of midnight. The end of the hai hour and the beginning of the zi hour is eleven o’clock at night; this is the moment when Heaven and humanity are set in motion together. The Medicine is the One Breath of Prior Heaven. The Common Source of Immortals and Buddhas (Xianfo hezong 仙佛合宗) says: “The root of the Golden Elixir is likened to the Medicine,” and “it is the single point of the essence-breath of true yang in my body, called the ancestral breath of Prior Heaven.”

§2
Original (Chinese)

于亥末子初之时，清心静坐，凝神定息，收视返听，一念不生，万缘尽息，浑沦如太极之未分，溟涬如两仪之未兆，湛然如秋江之映月，寂然似止水之无波，内不知乎吾身，外则忘乎宇宙，虚极静笃，心与天通，先天大药随我呼吸而入于黄庭，周天数足，铅汞交结，天然真火，薰蒸百脉，周流六虚，冲和八表，一霎时雷轰巽户，电发坤门，五蕴空明，九宫透彻，玉鼎汤煎，金炉火炽，黄芽遍地，白雪漫天，铅汞髓凝，结如黍珠，三十六宫花似锦，乾坤无处不春风。诀曰：“存神惟在肾，水火养潜龙。含光须默默，调息顺鸿濛”。此乃封闭之要诀也。

At the end of the hai hour and the beginning of the zi hour, clear the mind and sit quietly; concentrate the spirit and settle the breath; withdraw the sight and turn the hearing inward; let not a single thought arise and all external ties come to rest. Undifferentiated, like the Great Ultimate before it divided; boundless and dim, like the Two Modes before the slightest sign of them appeared; limpid, like an autumn river reflecting the moon; still, like water at rest without a ripple. Within, not knowing that I have a body; without, forgetting the whole universe; emptiness at its limit, stillness complete, the mind communicating with Heaven — then the Great Medicine of Prior Heaven enters the Yellow Court along with my breathing. When the number of the circuit is complete, lead and mercury join; the natural true fire steams through all the vessels, circulates through the six directions and spreads its harmony beyond the eight bounds. In an instant, thunder roars at the door of Xun and lightning flashes from the gate of Kun; the five aggregates become empty and bright, the nine palaces are clear right through; in the jade cauldron the broth simmers, in the golden furnace the fire blazes; yellow sprouts cover the ground, white snow fills the sky; lead and mercury congeal like marrow and form a pearl like a grain of millet; the thirty-six palaces of the body bloom like brocade, and there is no place between Qian and Kun where the spring wind does not blow. The formula says: “Keep the spirit only in the kidneys (the lower Cinnabar Field); with water and fire nourish the hidden dragon. Hold in the light, in silence; regulate the breath in accord with the primal haze.” This is the essential formula of sealing.

Qiu Ling's noteon §2
Original note (Chinese)

此段功夫先要凝神中黄一窍，意守天心，虚极静笃，即所谓凝神入坤脐（下田）而生药，作为丹本，然后行子午周天之功，闭任开督，猛烹极炼，即所谓凝神入乾顶（泥丸）而成丹。《性命圭旨》说：“其恍恍惚惚是采取时候，猛烹极炼是采取功夫，吸、舐、撮、闭是烹炼之的旨也。”

清代黄元吉《道德经注释》一书云：“学人欲修性命，先明铅汞”，“然灵液即真水，真水即汞也。真阳即真气，真气即铅也。汞为精，铅为气，二者皆后天有形有象之物，只可顺而生男育女，不可为长生大药。必从此汞之下降，铅之上升。会合中宫，凝神调息，片刻间兀兀腾腾如雾如烟，如潮如海，才算是真铅，可作为炼丹之本，所谓坎离交而得药是也，于是运起阳火阴符，逆从尾闾直上泥丸，泥丸久积阴精与我这点真铅之气配合为一，即所谓乾坤交而结丹是也。阳气上升泥丸有何景象？觉得头首爽利，非等平日之昏晕，有如风吹云散而天朗气清，另有一番气象，才算是真汞。以前之汞还是凡汞，不可以养成仙胎。铅汞会于泥丸，斯时之凡精凡气合同而化，不见有铅，不见有汞，只有一清凉恬淡之味，化为甘露神水，香甜可口，不似平日粗精浊气，即古人谓醍醐灌顶是，从上腭落下吞而服之，送入黄庭温养，即封固矣！此个真精一生，浑身苏软如绵，欲睡不睡，欲醒不醒，而平日动荡之身心，至此浑然湛然不动不摇，自安所止而得所止。此境非大静大定不能，若夫采取之法，即一意凝注，毫不分散，古人谓不采之采甚于采，是所谓交媾者，即神入气中，气包神外，两两不分。”此段乃实际心得体会，故录于此。一意凝结之法，最好是凭目力从后透而升，遂无逐情外漏之弊。

《尹真人寥阳殿问答编》云：“汝于亥末子初观一阳初动之际，虚极静笃，心与天同，神息合一，先天之炁随我呼吸，入于黄庭，周天数足，丹田汤沸，此真验也。百脉如虫行，四肢如火炽，此采药归壶之秘诀也。”《性命圭旨》又道：“盖此时天地一阳来复，而吾身之天地亦然，内以采取吾身之阳，外以盗取天地之阳，则天地之阳有不悉归于我之身中而为我之药物乎?!”故古人有此段功夫所得真阳，多来自虚空，即大自然空间之说。学者若能得此，必自顶门而下，且必滴顶应阙霎时清凉，所谓乾元得自顶，三界立清凉是也。

至药既入于黄庭，封闭之诀，则取文火温养之义。

For this stage of the work, one must first concentrate the spirit in the single opening of the Central Yellow and hold the intention on the Celestial Heart, in utmost emptiness and steadfast stillness; this is what is called “concentrating the spirit in the navel of Kun (the lower field) to produce the Medicine,” which serves as the root of the elixir. Then one practices the zi-wu Celestial Circuit, closing the Conception vessel and opening the Governing vessel, cooking fiercely and refining to the utmost; this is what is called “concentrating the spirit in the summit of Qian (the Muddy Pellet) to form the elixir.” The Principles of Inner Nature and Existence says: “That vague and indistinct moment is the time of collecting; fierce cooking and utmost refining is the work of collecting; inhale, lick, contract and close are the exact key to the cooking and refining.”

Huang Yuanji of the Qing dynasty says in his Commentary on the Daodejing: “Students who wish to cultivate inner nature and existence must first understand lead and mercury.” And: “The numinous fluid is true water, and true water is mercury. True yang is true breath, and true breath is lead. Mercury is essence, lead is breath; both are things of Posterior Heaven with form and appearance, which, if they follow their natural course, can only produce sons and daughters and cannot be the Great Medicine of long life. One must make this mercury descend and this lead rise, meet in the Central Palace, concentrate the spirit and regulate the breath; when in a moment there is a dense, surging rising, like mist and smoke, like tide and sea, only then is it true lead, which can serve as the root for refining the elixir — this is what is called ‘obtaining the Medicine through the meeting of Kan and Li.' Then one sets in motion the yang fire and the yin response, going against the current from the Caudal Funnel straight up to the Muddy Pellet; the yin essence long accumulated in the Muddy Pellet combines with my single point of true-lead breath into one — this is what is called ‘forming the elixir through the meeting of Qian and Kun.' What happens when the yang breath rises to the Muddy Pellet? The head feels clear and sharp, not dull and dizzy as usual; it is as if the wind had blown the clouds away and the sky were bright and the air clear — a quite different atmosphere. Only then is it true mercury. The mercury before this was still ordinary mercury, which cannot nourish the immortal embryo. When lead and mercury meet in the Muddy Pellet, the ordinary essence and ordinary breath of that moment blend and transform together; one no longer sees lead, nor mercury, but only a cool, light, tranquil taste, which turns into sweet dew, divine water, fragrant and sweet, unlike the coarse essence and turbid breath of ordinary days — this is what the ancients called ‘ghee poured on the crown.' It falls from the palate; swallow it and send it into the Yellow Court to be warmly nourished — that is sealing it! When this true essence is born, the whole body becomes soft as cotton, as if about to sleep yet not sleeping, about to wake yet not waking; the body and mind that were agitated in ordinary days now become whole, limpid, unmoving and unshaken, resting of themselves where they should rest and finding where they should rest. This state cannot be reached without great stillness and great absorption. As for the method of collecting, it is simply concentrating with a single intention, without the least dispersion; the ancients said ‘collecting without collecting is better than collecting.' What is called intercourse is the spirit entering the breath and the breath enfolding the spirit, the two inseparable.” This passage is real experience from practice, which is why I record it here. For the method of congealing with a single intention, it is best to rely on the power of the eyes to pass through the back and rise; then there will be no fault of chasing the passions and leaking outward.

Perfected Yin’s Questions and Answers in the Liaoyang Hall (Yin zhenren Liaoyang dian wenda bian 尹真人寥阳殿问答编) says: “At the end of the hai hour and the beginning of the zi hour, observe the moment when the one yang first stirs; in utmost emptiness and steadfast stillness, the mind one with Heaven, spirit and breath united, the breath of Prior Heaven follows my breathing into the Yellow Court; when the number of the circuit is complete, the Cinnabar Field is like boiling broth — this is the true sign. All the vessels feel as if insects were crawling in them, the four limbs as if fire were blazing — this is the secret formula of collecting the Medicine and returning it to the vessel.” The Principles of Inner Nature and Existence also says: “At this time the one yang returns in Heaven and Earth, and so it is in the Heaven and Earth of my body. Within, collect the yang of my body; without, steal the yang of Heaven and Earth — then how could the yang of Heaven and Earth fail to return entirely into my body and become my Medicine?” That is why the ancients said that the true yang obtained in this stage of the work mostly comes from empty space, that is, from the space of nature. If students obtain it, it will certainly come down from the gate at the crown, and it will certainly drip on the crown and respond at the palate, bringing an instant coolness — this is what is called “the originating power of Qian is obtained from the crown, and the three realms at once become cool.”

As for the formula of “sealing” after the Medicine has entered the Yellow Court, it means nourishing it warmly with a gentle fire.

Key Terms

  • Collecting the Medicine / collecting the Medicine and returning it to the vessel: collecting the One Breath of Prior Heaven and returning it to the Cinnabar Field (the vessel).
  • Zi hour / end of hai and beginning of zi: around eleven o’clock at night.
  • Juncture of Kun and Fu: the turning point from the hexagram Kun (pure yin) to the hexagram Fu (the first birth of one yang).
  • Stealing the mechanism: from the Scripture of the Hidden Accordance (Yinfu jing 阴符经); following and making use of the timing of the movements of Heaven and Earth.
  • Undifferentiated, boundless and dim (hunlun, mingxing 浑沦、溟涬): the primal chaos before Heaven and Earth divided.
  • Six directions, eight bounds: up, down and the four directions; and beyond the eight directions.
  • Door of Xun, gate of Kun: not explained in the text; both are veiled names for places in the body. The gate of Kun is generally understood to be below (the abdomen, the perineum area).
  • Five aggregates: form, sensation, perception, mental formations, consciousness; see Part One, Chapter 5.
  • Jade cauldron, golden furnace: the cauldron is above (the Muddy Pellet), the furnace below (the Cinnabar Field).
  • Yellow sprouts, white snow: common symbols in the alchemical scriptures for the first birth of the Medicine and the purity of the true breath.
  • Pearl like a grain of millet: see Part One, Chapter 3.
  • “Keep the spirit only in the kidneys”: “kidneys” here means the area of the lower Cinnabar Field, not the organs themselves.
  • Hidden dragon: from the first line of the hexagram Qian in the Book of Changes, “hidden dragon, do not act”; a metaphor for the yang breath hidden below and not yet set in motion.
  • Primal haze (hongmeng 鸿濛): the original breath before Heaven and Earth divided; by extension, a state of natural primal undifferentiation.
  • Sealing: after the Medicine enters the Yellow Court, nourishing and securing it with a gentle fire.
  • Zi-wu Celestial Circuit: the circuit rising along the Governing vessel and descending along the Conception vessel (the Lesser Celestial Circuit).
  • Closing the Conception vessel and opening the Governing vessel: rising along the Governing vessel, for the time being not letting the breath go along the Conception vessel.
  • Lead and Mercury: Huang Yuanji says that mercury is essence (numinous fluid, true water) and lead is breath (true yang, true breath).
  • Yang fire and yin response: in the circuit, “advancing the yang fire” (rising) and “withdrawing the yin response” (descending).
  • Sweet Dew / ghee poured on the crown: the cool, sweet fluid formed when lead and mercury meet in the Muddy Pellet.
  • Collecting without collecting: obtaining it naturally without deliberately trying to collect it; see Min Yide’s commentary in Part One, Chapter 2.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The heart of collecting the Medicine is “stillness,” not “collecting”: the longest passage of the text is entirely a description of the state in which not a single thought arises, in utmost emptiness and steadfast stillness; “the Great Medicine of Prior Heaven enters along with my breathing” is the result of this state. Huang Yuanji also says: “As for the method of collecting, it is simply concentrating with a single intention, without the least dispersion … collecting without collecting is better than collecting.”
  2. About the zi hour: the text emphasizes midnight, the zi hour (about 11 pm to 1 am). In his note to Part One, Chapter 8, Qiu Ling also introduced the “Living Zi Hour” — not tied to the clock: whenever yang is born at the extreme of stillness, that is the zi hour. Modern readers can put the emphasis on “the extreme of stillness” rather than on staying up late.
  3. The bodily sensations of true lead and true mercury (Huang Yuanji):
    • True lead: when one concentrates the spirit and regulates the breath, in a moment there is in the abdomen “a dense, surging rising, like mist and smoke, like tide and sea.”
    • True mercury: after the breath rises to the head, “the head feels clear and sharp … as if the wind had blown the clouds away and the sky were bright and the air clear.”
    • Sweet dew: cool, light, fragrant and sweet; it falls from the palate and is swallowed and sent to the Yellow Court.
    • Afterward: the whole body soft as cotton, about to sleep yet not sleeping, about to wake yet not waking; body and mind limpid and unmoving.
  4. The four lines of “the essential formula of sealing” are worth learning by heart: “Keep the spirit only in the kidneys; with water and fire nourish the hidden dragon. Hold in the light, in silence; regulate the breath in accord with the primal haze” — the spirit guards the lower Cinnabar Field, the light is not let out, the breathing follows its natural course.
  5. After the Medicine enters the Yellow Court: nourish it warmly with a gentle fire, that is, continuously and lightly, neither forgetting nor forcing, with no more effort.

Translator's Notes

  1. “The scripture says: ‘Eat at the right time and all the bones are in order; steal the mechanism and all transformations are at peace’”: from the Yellow Emperor’s Scripture of the Hidden Accordance (Huangdi yinfu jing 黄帝阴符经); the Chinese edition adds the title. The source of the next quotation, “Whenever Heaven and Earth join, steal the creative mechanism of yin and yang,” is not known.
  2. “At that time the sun is due north”: the ancients explained the birth of yang at the extreme of yin by the sun reaching due north (below the earth) at the zi hour — part of the theory of correspondence between Heaven and humanity.
  3. The string of images beginning with “thunder roars at the door of Xun, lightning flashes from the gate of Kun” depicts dramatic changes of body and mind within deep stillness. Explanations differ from school to school; the translation follows the wording without forced interpretation.
  4. The work of Huang Yuanji quoted in Qiu Ling’s note is titled in his note Commentary on the Daodejing; the title is kept as Qiu Ling gives it. Huang Yuanji’s teachings are the subject of the third book of this project, Huang Yuanji’s Quiet-Sitting Methods for Nourishing Life, Annotated (Qing Huang Yuanji yangsheng jinggong xinfa zhushi 清·黄元吉养生静功心法注释), and the two can be compared.
  5. “Drip on the crown and respond at the palate” (di ding ying que 滴顶应阙): the sweet dew drips on the crown and responds at the palate (que); the translation follows this sense.
