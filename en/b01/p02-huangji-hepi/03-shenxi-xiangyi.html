Chapter 3 · Spirit and Breath Relying on Each Other

Chapter 3 · Spirit and Breath Relying on Each Other — the Medicine is born, the fire blazes, the broth simmers

About This Chapter

The spirit in the Celestial Valley (the Muddy Pellet) at the crown, clear and still, is true inner nature; the true breath in the Furnace of Spirit (the lower Cinnabar Field), rich and unceasing, is true existence. These two are what the alchemical scriptures call true water and fire, the true crow and hare, the true husband and wife. This chapter asks that the two be “knotted into one mass, blended in one place,” so that through all twelve double-hours of the day spirit does not leave breath and breath does not leave spirit. Every day, before the zi hour and after the wu hour, sit quietly, “opening the Gate of Heaven to collect Prior Heaven, closing the Door of Earth to guard the embryonic breathing,” until three signs appear: “the Medicine is born in the Furnace of Spirit, the fire blazes in the Cinnabar Field, the broth simmers in the two kidneys.” These are the precondition for “opening the passes” in the next chapter. Qiu Ling’s note explains the principle of embryonic breathing very thoroughly and points out that this stage of the work lies entirely in one word: “illuminating.”

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

天谷之神，湛然寂然，真性也。神炉之中，真气氤氲而不息者，真命也。他两个才是真水火，真乌兔，真夫妇，真性命。使二者纽结一团，混合一处，炼在一炉，二六时中神不离气，气不离神，性不离命，命不离性，二者则二而一，一而二者也。

The spirit in the Celestial Valley (the Muddy Pellet), clear and still, is true inner nature. The true breath in the Furnace of Spirit (the lower Cinnabar Field), rich and unceasing, is true existence. These two alone are true water and fire, the true crow and hare (sun and moon), the true husband and wife, true inner nature and existence. Make the two knot into one mass, blend in one place, be refined in one furnace; through all twelve double-hours, let spirit not leave breath and breath not leave spirit, inner nature not leave existence and existence not leave inner nature. The two are two yet one, one yet two.

§2
Original (Chinese)

其功与前章之功一贯而下，每日子前午后，定息静坐，开天门以采先天，闭地户以守胎息，纳四时之正气，以归正室，以养胎真，渐采渐炼，以完乾体，以全亲之所生。天之所赋真汞八两、真铅半斤，气若婴儿，阴阳吻合，混沌不分，出息微微，入息绵绵，内气不出，外气反入，久之神炉药生，丹田火炽，两肾汤煎。此胎息还元之初，众妙归根之始也，则一刻功夫，可夺天地一年之节候。璇玑停轮，日月合璧，真是万里阴沉春气到，九霄清沏露华凝。妙矣哉！真阳交感之候欤？盖神入气中，犹天气之降于地，气与神合，犹地道之承于天。《易》曰：“大哉乾元，万物资始也。”盖一阳不生于复，而生于坤。坤虽至阴，然阴里藏阳，大药之生，实根底于此。

The work of this chapter continues directly from that of the previous one. Every day, before the zi hour and after the wu hour, settle the breath and sit quietly; open the Gate of Heaven to collect Prior Heaven, close the Door of Earth to guard the embryonic breathing; take in the correct breath of the four seasons and let it return to the true chamber, to nourish the true essence of the embryo. Collect a little at a time and refine a little at a time, so as to make the body of Qian complete again and preserve whole the body given by your parents. What Heaven bestows is eight ounces of true mercury and half a pound of true lead, in balance; the breath is like an infant’s, yin and yang fit together perfectly, undivided in primal chaos. The out-breath is faint and slight, the in-breath soft and continuous; the inner breath does not go out, while the outer breath instead comes in. After a long time, the Medicine is born in the Furnace of Spirit, the fire blazes in the Cinnabar Field, and the broth simmers in the two kidneys. This is the beginning of embryonic breathing returning to the origin, the start of all wonders returning to the root. At this time, a quarter-hour of work can seize a whole year’s seasons of Heaven and Earth. The Jade Pivot of the Dipper stops turning, sun and moon join like a perfect jade disc — truly, “Over ten thousand miles of gloom the breath of spring arrives; in the ninefold clear sky the dew condenses.” How wonderful! Is this not the moment when true yang responds and joins? For spirit entering breath is like the breath of Heaven descending to Earth; breath joining with spirit is like the way of Earth receiving Heaven. The Book of Changes says: “Great indeed is the originating power of Qian; the ten thousand things owe their beginning to it.” For the one yang is not born in Fu ䷗ (Return) but in Kun ䷁ (the Receptive). Although Kun is utmost yin, yang is hidden within yin, and the birth of the Great Medicine is in fact rooted here.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§2
Original note (Chinese)

此章承上章而有所发挥，功法亦一贯而下，即定息静坐，开天门（顶门，在泥丸之上）以采先天真气，闭地户（会阴穴）以守胎息，从而达到神炉（天心，下田）药生，丹田火炽，两肾汤煎，是为证验。

胎息是由天谷（泥丸）元神与神炉（天心）真气，亦即性与命，互相结合的条件下自然而然形成的。《性命圭旨》说，只要凝神入气穴，蛰神于其中，藏气于其内，以空空之心合真人深深之息，则心息相依，息调心净。《天仙正理》说，人在胎时不由口鼻呼吸，只因与母脐相通，故随母呼吸而为呼吸，能似无气息一般，此真胎息景也；离胎而息即断，无母脐与子脐相通，不得不向自身口鼻起呼吸。胎息者，逆修返还之理，以我今呼吸之息而返还为胎中之息也，即以口鼻呼吸之气而归于胎息之所（即丹田之所），如处胎息之时，渐渐炼至胎息亦真无。《抱朴子内篇》说：“得胎息者，能不以鼻口嘘吸，如在胞胎之中，则道成矣！”“以鸿毛著鼻口之上，吐炁而鸿毛不动为候也。”

This chapter continues the previous one and develops it further, and the method also continues directly: settle the breath and sit quietly, open the Gate of Heaven (the gate at the crown, above the Muddy Pellet) to collect the true breath of Prior Heaven, close the Door of Earth (the Perineum point) to guard the embryonic breathing, and so reach the point where the Medicine is born in the Furnace of Spirit (the Celestial Heart, the lower field), the fire blazes in the Cinnabar Field and the broth simmers in the two kidneys. These are the signs.

Embryonic breathing forms quite naturally under the condition that the Original Spirit of the Celestial Valley (the Muddy Pellet) and the true breath of the Furnace of Spirit (the Celestial Heart) — that is, inner nature and existence — join with each other. The Principles of Inner Nature and Existence says that one need only concentrate the spirit and enter the Cavity of Breath, let the spirit hibernate there and the breath be stored within, and join an empty mind with the deep breathing of the Perfected; then mind and breath rely on each other, the breath is regulated and the mind is pure. The Correct Principles of Celestial Immortality (Tianxian zhengli 天仙正理) says that in the womb a person does not breathe through mouth and nose; because the navel communicates with the mother’s, the child breathes as the mother breathes, as if it had no breath at all — this is the true scene of embryonic breathing. Once out of the womb, this breathing is cut off; since the mother’s navel no longer communicates with the child’s, the child has no choice but to start breathing through its own mouth and nose. Embryonic breathing is the principle of cultivating in reverse and returning: returning my present breathing to the breathing in the womb, that is, bringing the breath of mouth and nose back to the place of embryonic breathing (the Cinnabar Field), as when one breathed in the womb, and gradually refining it until even the embryonic breathing truly returns to nothing. The Inner Chapters of the Master Who Embraces Simplicity (Baopuzi neipian 抱朴子内篇) says: “One who attains embryonic breathing can breathe without nose and mouth, as if in the womb — then the Dao is accomplished!” And: “Place a goose feather on the nose and mouth; when one breathes out and the feather does not move, that is the sign.”

§3
Original (Chinese)

药将产时，就与孕妇保胎一般，一切饮食起居俱要小心谨慎。诗云：“潮来水面侵堤岸，风定江心绝浪波。性寂情空心不动，坐无昏愦睡无魔。”此惟凝神气穴，定心觉海，元神与真气相依相恋，自然神满不思睡，而真精自凝，铅汞自投，胎婴自栖，三尸自灭，九虫自出，其身自觉安而轻，其神自觉圆而明。若此便是长生路，休问道之成不成。此境必待神炉药生，丹田火炽，两肾汤煎，方见此效，方可行开关之功。

When the Medicine is about to be produced, it is just like a pregnant woman protecting the fetus: everything in eating, drinking and daily life must be done with care. A poem says: “When the tide comes in, the water rises over the banks; when the wind settles, the middle of the river has no more waves. Inner nature still, emotions empty, the mind unmoved — sitting without dullness, sleeping without demons.” This can be achieved only by concentrating the spirit in the Cavity of Breath and settling the mind in the sea of awareness, so that the Original Spirit and the true breath rely on and cling to each other. Then naturally the spirit is full and one does not wish to sleep; the true essence congeals of itself, lead and mercury meet of themselves, the embryo-infant settles of itself, the Three Corpses are destroyed of themselves, the Nine Worms leave of themselves; the body feels peaceful and light, and the spirit feels whole and bright. This is the road to long life; do not ask whether the Dao will be accomplished or not. This state is seen only after the Medicine is born in the Furnace of Spirit, the fire blazes in the Cinnabar Field and the broth simmers in the two kidneys; only then may one do the work of opening the passes.

§4
Original (Chinese)

修真之士果能将夹脊双关所凝之神，藏于气穴，守而不离，则一呼一吸，夺先天元始祖气，尽入气穴之中，久而真气充满，畅于四肢，散于百骸，无有阻滞，则自然而肾汤煎，丹田火热而开关也。

If those who cultivate reality can truly store in the Cavity of Breath the spirit concentrated in the Spine-Straddling Pass, and guard it without leaving it, then with every inhalation and exhalation they will seize the primordial ancestral breath of Prior Heaven, and all of it will enter the Cavity of Breath. In time the true breath fills the body, flows through the four limbs and spreads through all the bones without any obstruction; then naturally the broth simmers in the two kidneys, the Cinnabar Field becomes hot, and the passes open.

Qiu Ling's noteon §3–§4
Original note (Chinese)

丹田火炽，两肾汤煎，为开关之候。开关即冲开尾闾关（在尾骶向上数第三椎间），实现小周天。未有不守胎息而望开关者。

此段功夫全在照之一字。古语云：“照者慧日也，慧日照破昏衢，能见本来面目。”常要回光内照，照顾不离，则真息自然旋转。

“The fire blazes in the Cinnabar Field, the broth simmers in the two kidneys” — this is the time for opening the passes. Opening the passes means breaking through the pass of the Caudal Funnel (between the third vertebrae counting up from the tailbone) and achieving the Lesser Celestial Circuit. No one can hope to open the passes without guarding the embryonic breathing.

This stage of the work lies entirely in one word: “illuminating.” An old saying goes: “Illuminating is the sun of wisdom; the sun of wisdom shines through the dark road, and one can see one’s original face.” Constantly Revert the Light and illuminate within, watch over it without leaving, and the True Breath will turn of itself.

Key Terms

  • Celestial Valley (tiangu): that is, the Muddy Pellet, the upper Cinnabar Field.
  • Furnace of Spirit (shenlu 神炉): that is, the lower Cinnabar Field (in this part also called the Celestial Heart).
  • True water and fire, true crow and hare, true husband and wife: the various metaphors of the alchemical scriptures for the yin–yang pair; this chapter says plainly that they are spirit (inner nature) and breath (existence). See the entry Lead and Mercury.
  • Twelve double-hours (er liu shi zhong 二六时中): all twelve double-hours of the day, that is, the whole day.
  • Before zi and after wu: before the zi hour (11 pm–1 am) and after the wu hour (11 am–1 pm); generally, fixed times of sitting each day.
  • Gate of Heaven, Door of Earth: according to this chapter’s note, the Gate of Heaven is the gate at the crown (above the Muddy Pellet) and the Door of Earth is the Perineum point.
  • Eight ounces of true mercury, half a pound of true lead: in the old system half a pound equals eight ounces; a metaphor for yin and yang being equal in weight and in balance.
  • The Medicine is born in the Furnace of Spirit, the fire blazes in the Cinnabar Field, the broth simmers in the two kidneys: the three signs of this part, and the precondition for “opening the passes.”
  • The Jade Pivot stops turning, sun and moon join like a jade disc: the Dipper stops, sun and moon are in conjunction — a metaphor for the moment when yin and yang meet within the body.
  • “The one yang is not born in Fu but in Kun”: the hexagram Fu (Return) is the first birth of a single yang line; Kun is pure yin. The meaning is that yang is in fact conceived within utmost yin (utmost stillness).
  • Three Corpses, Nine Worms: harmful “corpse spirits” and nine kinds of worms that Daoism holds to live in the body, which cultivation can expel.
  • Opening the passes: breaking through the pass of the Caudal Funnel and achieving the Lesser Celestial Circuit.
  • Illuminating (zhao 照): Reverting the Light and illuminating within.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The key sentence: spirit does not leave breath, breath does not leave spirit. The concrete method is still that of Chapter 2, “illuminating the Celestial Heart with the Dao mind” — clearly illuminating the lower Cinnabar Field without leaving it.
  2. How to understand “opening the Gate of Heaven, closing the Door of Earth”: in the context of the whole book, it can be understood as the crown of the head relaxed, empty and alive (receiving Prior Heaven), and the perineum naturally held (keeping the lower body from leaking). No effort is required here; the forceful method comes in Chapter 4, which is a different matter.
  3. The signs are signposts: a feeling of “birth” in the lower abdomen (the Furnace of Spirit), warmth in the Cinnabar Field (the fire blazing), and a sensation like warm water simmering in the lower back and kidney area (the broth simmering). Only when all three have appeared can one speak of the next step, “opening the passes.” If they have not appeared, continue with this step and do not hurry.
  4. Be careful in daily life: “When the Medicine is about to be produced, it is just like a pregnant woman protecting the fetus” — at this stage pay even more attention to regular routines, moderate your desires, and avoid overwork and great swings of emotion.
  5. Everything lies in “illuminating”: the conclusion of Qiu Ling’s note — constantly Revert the Light and illuminate within, watch over it without leaving, and the True Breath will turn of itself.

Translator's Notes

  1. “In the ninefold clear sky”: the Chinese 清沏 should read 清澈 (“clear”); the translation follows the corrected reading.
  2. “Then naturally the broth simmers in the two kidneys”: the Chinese 则自然而肾汤煎 appears to have lost the character 两 (“two”) after 而; the translation follows the sense, “the two kidneys.”
  3. “Eight ounces of true mercury, half a pound of true lead”: in the old system a pound (jin 斤) had sixteen ounces (liang 两), so half a pound is eight ounces; the alchemical books often use this to express the balance of yin and yang.
  4. On the “goose feather that does not move” in embryonic breathing: this is an ancient criterion given in the Baopuzi. It describes the result of breathing that becomes naturally faint in deep stillness; it is not an instruction to hold the breath. This is the translator’s remark.
