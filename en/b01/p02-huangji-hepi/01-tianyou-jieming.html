Chapter 1 · Adding Oil to Prolong Life

Chapter 1 · Adding Oil to Prolong Life — finding again the “old road” we had before birth

About This Chapter

In the mother’s womb a person breathes along with the mother through the umbilical cord. At that time my breath, my mother’s breath, the breath of Heaven and Earth and the breath of the Great Void all communicate, opening to opening. Once we are born and the cord is cut, this channel is broken, and for the rest of our life not a single breath ever enters there again — we “cannot find the old road.” This chapter teaches how to find that old road again: first think intently of the Root of the Mountain, then hold the intention on the Spine-Straddling Pass, so that the breath gradually reaches the ancestral opening of the Celestial Heart and joins again with the primordial ancestral breath. It is like adding oil to a lamp that is about to run dry, hence the name “adding oil to prolong life.” Qiu Ling’s note matches the “Spine-Straddling Pass” of this chapter with the “Central Yellow” of The Secret of the Golden Flower and confirms this with quotations from the Principles of Inner Nature and Existence (Xingming guizhi 性命圭旨); it is the key to understanding the whole part.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

原人生受气之初，在胞胎内随母呼吸，受气而成。此缕与母相连，渐推渐开，中空如管，气通往来，前通于脐，后通于肾，上通夹脊，由明堂至山根而生双窍，由双窍下至准头，而成鼻之两孔，是以名曰鼻祖。斯时我之气通母之气，母之气通天地之气，天地之气通太虚之气，窍窍相通，无有阂隔，及乎数足，裂胞而出，剪断脐带，囫地一声，一点元阳落于脐轮之后，号曰天心，虚灵一点是也。自此后天用事，虽有呼吸往来，不得与元始祖气相通，人生自幼至老，断未有一息注于其中，尘生尘灭，万死万生，皆为寻不着旧路耳。

When we trace a human life back to the moment it first receives breath, it is formed in the womb, breathing along with the mother and receiving breath. This strand is joined to the mother; gradually it pushes outward and opens, hollow in the middle like a tube, and breath passes back and forth through it. In front it reaches the navel, behind it reaches the kidneys, above it reaches the Spine-Straddling Pass; from the Bright Hall (mingtang 明堂) it goes to the Root of the Mountain and produces two openings, and from these two openings it goes down to the tip of the nose and forms the two nostrils — that is why the nose is called the “ancestor” (bizu 鼻祖, literally “nose-ancestor”). At this time my breath communicates with my mother’s breath, my mother’s breath with the breath of Heaven and Earth, and the breath of Heaven and Earth with the breath of the Great Void; opening communicates with opening, with no barrier at all. When the months are complete, the child breaks out of the womb, the umbilical cord is cut, and with a cry a single point of original yang falls behind the navel. This is called the “Celestial Heart” (tianxin 天心); it is that single point of empty numinosity. From then on Posterior Heaven takes charge. Although breath still goes in and out, it can no longer communicate with the primordial ancestral breath. From childhood to old age, not one breath ever enters there. People are born and perish in the dusty world, die ten thousand times and are born ten thousand times — all because they cannot find the old road.

§2
Original (Chinese)

所以太上立法教人修炼，由其能夺先天之正气。所以能夺者，由其有两孔之呼吸也。所呼者自己之元气从中而出，所吸者天地之正气从外而入。人若使根源牢固，呼吸之间亦可夺天地之正气，而寿命绵长；若根源不固，所吸天地之正气，恒随呼吸而出，元气不为己有，反为天地所得，亦只为不得其门而入耳。

So the Most High established a method to teach people to cultivate themselves, because people are able to seize the correct breath of Prior Heaven. They can seize it because they breathe through the two nostrils. What is breathed out is one’s own original breath coming out from within; what is breathed in is the correct breath of Heaven and Earth entering from outside. If people make their root firm, then between one breath and the next they too can seize the correct breath of Heaven and Earth, and their life will be long. If the root is not firm, the correct breath of Heaven and Earth that they breathe in always goes out again with the breath; their original breath does not remain their own but is instead taken by Heaven and Earth. This too is only because they cannot find the door to go in.

§3
Original (Chinese)

盖常人呼吸皆从咽喉而下，至中脘而回，不能与祖气相通，所谓众人之息以喉也。若至人呼吸，直贯明堂而上，盖切切然以意守夹脊双关，自然通于天心一窍，得与元始祖气相连，如磁吸铁，而同类相亲，即庄子所谓真人之息以踵也。踵者深也。即真人潜深渊，浮游守规中之意。此窍初凝，即生两肾，次而生心，其肾如藕，其心如莲，其梗中空外直，柱地撑天，心肾相去八寸四分，中余一寸二分，谓之腔子里，乃心肾往来之路，水火既济之乡。欲通此窍，先要穷想山根，则呼吸之气方渐次而通夹脊，透泥丸，以达于天心祖窍，而子母会合，破镜重圆，渐渐扩充，则根本定固，救住命宝，始可言修炼功夫。行之既久，一呼一吸入于气穴，乃自然而然之妙也。

The breathing of ordinary people always goes down from the throat and turns back at the Central Stomach Duct (zhongwan 中脘); it cannot communicate with the ancestral breath. This is what Zhuangzi calls “the multitude breathe with their throats.” The breathing of the accomplished person, however, goes straight through the Bright Hall and upward, because he earnestly and intently holds his intention on the Spine-Straddling Pass, and so naturally communicates with the single opening of the Celestial Heart and is able to join the primordial ancestral breath — like a magnet attracting iron, like things of the same kind drawn to each other. This is what Zhuangzi calls “the Perfected breathe with their heels.” “Heels” (zhong 踵) means depth. It is the meaning of the line in the Token for the Agreement of the Three: “The Perfected sinks into the deep abyss, floating and roaming, guarding the compass center.” When this opening first congeals, it first produces the two kidneys and then the heart. The kidneys are like a lotus root, the heart like a lotus flower; the stem between them is hollow inside and straight outside, standing on the earth and holding up the sky. The heart and kidneys are eight cun four fen apart (a cun is a “Chinese inch,” about 3 cm; a fen is a tenth of a cun), and in the exact middle there remain one cun two fen, called the “inside of the cavity” (qiangzi li 腔子里). It is the road along which heart and kidneys go back and forth, the place where water and fire complete each other. To open this opening, first think intently of the Root of the Mountain; only then will the breath gradually pass through the Spine-Straddling Pass, penetrate the Muddy Pellet and reach the ancestral opening of the Celestial Heart. Then child and mother meet again, like a broken mirror made whole. As this is gradually expanded, the root becomes firm and the treasure of life is saved; only then can one speak of the work of cultivation. When this has been practiced for a long time, every inhalation and exhalation enters the Cavity of Breath — this is the wonder of what happens of itself.

Qiu Ling's noteon §3
Original note (Chinese)

欲通此窍，先要穷想山根。按山根在两眼之间鼻梁上，于此想到无可想，想到一念不生，自然豁然内辟，神由黄道直达中黄，自觉宽广高深无际，自然通于天心（编者按：本文所谓天心乃脐内一寸二分处）一窍，与原始祖气相连，真息自现。古人说：丹道之妙，始基于此。第一步已直贯到性命双修全功。《性命圭旨》有诗云：“一条直路少人寻，寻到山根始入门。坐定更知行炁主，真人之息自深深。”此之谓也。

黄道者自会阴直达泥丸自然升降之道也，由此道升降纯是先天，求其自然。穷想山根，元神自然到达中黄，意守中黄，自然通于天心，形成一条黄道。学者妄用心意，不从自然，强引之升降，每有“后天闹黄”之弊。中黄未开谓之玄关，既开谓之玄窍，学者行到虚极静笃时，此窍乃现，胎息息于此，元神升降于此。

“To open this opening, first think intently of the Root of the Mountain.” Note: the Root of the Mountain is on the bridge of the nose between the two eyes. Think here until there is nothing left to think, until not a single thought arises, and it will naturally open inward all at once. The spirit goes along the “Yellow Path” straight to the Central Yellow; one feels within a space vast, high and deep, without limit, and it naturally communicates with the single opening of the Celestial Heart — editor’s note (Qiu Ling): the Celestial Heart in this chapter is the point one cun two fen inward from the navel — and joins with the primordial ancestral breath, and the True Breath appears of itself. The ancients said: the wonder of the alchemical Way has its foundation here. This first step already runs straight through to the whole work of the dual cultivation of inner nature and existence. A poem in the Principles of Inner Nature and Existence says: “Few seek the one straight road; only on reaching the Root of the Mountain does one enter the gate. Once seated in stillness, know further that the True Intention is master of moving the breath, and the breathing of the Perfected becomes deep of itself.” That is what is meant here.

The “Yellow Path” is the road that runs straight from the Perineum to the Muddy Pellet, along which things rise and descend naturally. What rises and descends along this road is purely Prior Heaven, and it must be allowed to happen naturally. When one thinks intently of the Root of the Mountain, the Original Spirit naturally reaches the Central Yellow; when one holds the intention on the Central Yellow, it naturally communicates with the Celestial Heart, and so a Yellow Path is formed. If students use the mind and intention recklessly, not following nature, and forcibly lead the breath up and down, they often suffer from the fault called “the Posterior Heaven making trouble in the Yellow” (houtian naohuang 后天闹黄). When the Central Yellow has not yet opened it is called the “Mysterious Pass”; once it has opened it is called the “Mysterious Opening.” Only when students reach the utmost emptiness and steadfast stillness does this opening appear; embryonic breathing rests here, and the Original Spirit rises and descends here.

§4
Original (Chinese)

按了真子曰：“欲点长明灯，须用添油法”。若非此窍，则必不能添油，必不能接命。故吕祖曰：“塞精宜急早，接命莫教迟”。然于此着功法，最是简易，但行、住、坐、卧常操此心藏于夹脊之窍，则天地真气随鼻呼吸，以扯而进，自与己之混元真精凝结丹田，而为吾养生之益。盖此窍之气，上通天谷，下达尾闾，周流百节之处。以天地无涯之元气，续我有限之形躯，自是容易。学者诚能凝神夹脊之窍，守而不离，久久纯熟，则里面皎皎明明，如月在水相似，自然散其邪火，消其杂虑，降其动心，止其妄念。妄念止则真息自现，真息现而真念无念，真息无息。息无则命根永固，念无则性体常存。性存命固，息念俱消，即性命双修之第一步功也。

Note: Liaozhenzi said: “To light the ever-burning lamp, you must use the method of adding oil.” Without this opening it is impossible to add oil and impossible to prolong life. That is why Patriarch Lü said: “Stop the leaking of essence early; do not delay in prolonging life.” Yet the method for this step is the simplest and easiest of all. Simply, whether walking, standing, sitting or lying down, constantly keep this mind stored in the opening of the Spine-Straddling Pass. Then the true breath of Heaven and Earth will be drawn in with the breathing through the nose, will naturally congeal in the Cinnabar Field together with your own primordial true essence, and will benefit the nourishing of your life. For the breath of this opening reaches up to the Celestial Valley (the Muddy Pellet) and down to the Caudal Funnel; it is the place from which breath circulates through all the joints. Using the boundless original breath of Heaven and Earth to sustain my limited bodily form is naturally easy. If students can truly concentrate the spirit in the opening of the Spine-Straddling Pass and guard it without leaving it, then after long practice, when it has become thoroughly familiar, within there will be a clear brightness, like the moon in water. Naturally this disperses the pathogenic fire, dissolves stray thoughts, subdues the agitated mind and stops deluded thinking. When deluded thinking stops, the True Breath appears of itself; when the True Breath appears, the true thought is no-thought and the True Breath is no-breath. When breath returns to nothing, the root of life is forever firm; when thought returns to nothing, the substance of inner nature always remains. Inner nature remaining and existence firm, breath and thought both dissolved — this is the first step of the dual cultivation of inner nature and existence.

Qiu Ling's noteon §4
Original note (Chinese)

添油接命之功在于先要穷想山根，然后意守夹脊双关，而通于天心一窍。此章则著重论述夹脊双关。

《吕祖金华宗旨》说，夹脊双关一窍名曰中黄，位于心后关前。夹脊双关在人背脊二十四椎上下之正中，即脊椎第七、八胸椎之间，正对胸部膻中穴（两乳头之间）。心后关前，心后易明，关前即夹脊双关在背内面向心部夹缝之处。回光意守此窍，元神注入，自觉此中大无外细无内，皎明如月在水。故丹书名之曰神室，又名黄房。《易经》曰：“君子黄中通理，正位居体，美在其中，而畅于四肢，发于事业，美之至也。”故此窍又名黄中。

《性命圭旨》一书据传出于尹真人弟子手笔，故该书第一节口诀《涵养本原救护命室》内附《退藏沐浴工夫》内容基本上与本章相同。《易经》云：“圣人以此洗心退藏于密”，古人谓此语为沐浴，或谓之为艮背（艮，限止之意）。《性命圭旨》云：“这个窍妙原夫心属乎火，而藏之以背之水者，洗之之义也。心居乎前而藏之以背之后者，退之之义也，故初机之士降伏其心，束之太紧，未免有烦躁火炎之患，是以暂将心火之南而藏背水之北，水火互相交养，自然念虑不生，即白玉蟾所谓洗心涤虑为沐浴是也。然沐浴虽为洗心之法，艮背虽有止念之功，二理是则是矣，皆未到实际之地，此向上一着千圣秘而不传。”这“向上一着”是什么呢？就是懂得运用夹脊双关一窍。《性命圭旨》继续说：“人若明得此窍，真可以夺神功改天命。古圣有言曰：夹脊双关透顶门，修行径路此为尊。以其上通天谷（编者按：即泥丸），下达尾闾，中通心肾，召摄灵阳救护命宝，此非修行径路而何？”又说：“但能培养本原，观照本窍，久则油然心新，浩然气畅，凝然不动，寂然无思，豁然知空，了然悟性，此所谓皮肤剥落尽，一真将次见矣！功夫至此，自然精神朗发，知慧日生，心性灵通，隐显自在，自然有一段清宁阖辟之机，自然有一段飞跃活动之趣，自然有一点元阳真炁从中而出，降黄庭，入土釜，贯尾闾，穿夹脊，上冲天谷，下达曲江（喻坎水），流通百脉，溉灌三田，驱逐一身百窍之阴邪，涤荡五脏六腑之浊秽”。

The work of adding oil to prolong life lies first in thinking intently of the Root of the Mountain and then in holding the intention on the Spine-Straddling Pass, so as to reach the single opening of the Celestial Heart. This chapter concentrates on the Spine-Straddling Pass.

The Secret of the Golden Flower of Patriarch Lü says that the opening of the Spine-Straddling Pass is called the “Central Yellow” and is located “behind the heart and in front of the pass.” The Spine-Straddling Pass is in the exact middle of the twenty-four vertebrae of the back, that is, between the seventh and eighth thoracic vertebrae, directly opposite the Chest Center point (danzhong 膻中, between the nipples) on the chest. “Behind the heart and in front of the pass”: “behind the heart” is easy to understand; “in front of the pass” means the gap on the inner side of the back, facing the heart, at the Spine-Straddling Pass. When one Reverts the Light and holds the intention on this opening, the Original Spirit pours into it, and one feels it to be so vast that it has no outside and so fine that it has no inside, clear and bright like the moon in water. That is why the alchemical books call it the “Chamber of Spirit” and also the “Yellow Chamber.” The Book of Changes says: “The noble one, with yellow in the center, penetrates the pattern of things; he occupies the correct position and rests in his place. The beauty is within; it flows out into the four limbs and is expressed in his undertakings. This is beauty at its highest.” So this opening is also called the “Yellow Center.”

The Principles of Inner Nature and Existence is said to have been written by a disciple of Perfected Yin. That is why the “Work of Withdrawing, Storing and Ablution” appended to its first formula, “Nourishing the Root and Source, Rescuing and Protecting the Chamber of Life,” is basically the same as this chapter. The Book of Changes says: “With this the sage cleanses his heart and withdraws into what is hidden.” The ancients called this phrase “ablution,” and some called it “stilling the back” (genbei 艮背; gen means to stop, to limit). The Principles of Inner Nature and Existence says: “The wonder of this opening is this: the heart belongs to fire, yet it is stored in the back, which belongs to water — this is the meaning of ‘washing.' The heart is in front, yet it is stored behind, in the back — this is the meaning of ‘withdrawing.' When beginners subdue their minds, they often restrain them too tightly, and cannot avoid the trouble of irritability and flaring fire. So for a while they take the fire of the heart from the south and store it in the water of the back in the north, so that water and fire nourish each other; naturally thoughts and worries no longer arise. This is what Bai Yuchan meant by ‘cleansing the heart and washing away worries is ablution.' But although ablution is a method for cleansing the heart, and stilling the back has the effect of stopping thought, and these two principles are right as far as they go, neither has yet reached the real ground. There is still ‘one move further up,' which the thousand sages have kept secret and not transmitted.” What is this “one move further up”? It is knowing how to use the single opening of the Spine-Straddling Pass. The Principles of Inner Nature and Existence continues: “If people understand this opening, they can truly seize the work of the spirits and change their heavenly destiny. An ancient sage said: ‘The Spine-Straddling Pass goes through to the gate at the crown; among the paths of cultivation this is the most honored.' Because it reaches up to the Celestial Valley (editor’s note (Qiu Ling): that is, the Muddy Pellet), down to the Caudal Funnel, and in the middle connects heart and kidneys, it can summon and gather the numinous yang and rescue and protect the treasure of life — what is this if not the path of cultivation?” And again: “If you can simply cultivate and nourish the root and source and contemplate this opening, then in time your heart will be freshly renewed, your breath will flow vast and free; you will be congealed and unmoving, silent and without thought; suddenly you will know emptiness and clearly awaken to your nature. This is what is called ‘when the skin has all peeled away, the One Reality is about to appear!' When the work reaches this point, the spirit naturally shines forth, wisdom grows day by day, mind and nature become numinous and penetrating, free to be hidden or manifest. Naturally there is a movement of pure and tranquil opening and closing; naturally there is a delight of soaring and lively movement; naturally a point of original yang true breath comes forth from the center, descends to the Yellow Court, enters the earthen crucible, goes through the Caudal Funnel, passes the Spine-Straddling Pass, rushes up to the Celestial Valley and reaches down to the Winding River (a metaphor for the water of Kan); it flows through all the vessels, irrigates the three fields, drives out the yin pathogens from all the openings of the body, and washes away the turbid filth from the five viscera and six bowels.”

§5
Original (Chinese)

嗟夫！人生如无根之树，全凭气息以为根株，一息不来，即命非我有。故修长生者，首节专以保固真精为本，精旺自然精化为气，气旺自然充满四肢，四肢充满则身中之元气不随呼而出，天地之正气恒随吸而入，久之胎息安，鄞鄂固，斯长生有路矣！夫添油入窍，种种玄况不一，总以造有热汤倾注之验，觉极通畅，自有天然妙处。

Alas! Human life is like a tree without roots; it relies entirely on the breath as its root and trunk. If one breath fails to come, life no longer belongs to me. Therefore those who cultivate long life take as their first step, and as their foundation, the preservation and strengthening of the true essence. When essence is abundant, it naturally transforms into breath; when breath is abundant, it naturally fills the four limbs; when the four limbs are filled, the original breath within the body no longer goes out with the exhalation, and the correct breath of Heaven and Earth constantly comes in with the inhalation. In time the embryonic breathing becomes settled and the yin’e (the foundation and outline of the elixir) becomes firm — then there is a road to long life! When oil is added and enters the opening, all kinds of mysterious states appear, each different; the general sign is a sensation like hot broth pouring down, with a feeling of complete openness and ease — naturally there is a heavenly wonder in it.

Key Terms

  • Nose-ancestor (bizu): this chapter’s explanation — the fetus’s breath goes from the navel, kidneys, Spine-Straddling Pass, Bright Hall and Root of the Mountain and produces the nostrils, so the nose is called the “ancestor.”
  • Celestial Heart: in this chapter (and throughout this part) the “Celestial Heart” is the point one cun two fen inward from the navel, that is, the lower Cinnabar Field; it differs from the “Celestial Heart” of The Secret of the Golden Flower, which is the Mysterious Opening.
  • Primordial ancestral breath / ancestral breath: the original breath of Prior Heaven, that is, the One Breath of Prior Heaven.
  • Central Stomach Duct (zhongwan): a point four cun above the navel.
  • Bright Hall (mingtang): the point one cun two fen inward from the exact middle between the eyebrows.
  • “The multitude breathe with their throats; the Perfected breathe with their heels”: from the Zhuangzi, chapter “The Great Ancestral Teacher.”
  • “The Perfected sinks into the deep abyss, floating and roaming, guarding the compass center”: from the Token for the Agreement of the Three According to the Book of Changes.
  • Inside of the cavity (qiangzi li): the one cun two fen in the exact middle between heart and kidneys, “the road along which heart and kidneys go back and forth, the place where water and fire complete each other.”
  • Root of the Mountain, thinking intently of the Root of the Mountain: concentrating the mind on the Root of the Mountain between the eyes.
  • Spine-Straddling Pass: the central opening of this part; Qiu Ling’s note identifies it with the Central Yellow of The Secret of the Golden Flower; also called the Chamber of Spirit, the Yellow Chamber, the Yellow Center.
  • Yellow Path: the road from the Perineum straight to the Muddy Pellet, along which things rise and descend naturally.
  • “The Posterior Heaven making trouble in the Yellow”: the deviation produced by forcibly leading the breath up and down with the intention.
  • Adding oil to prolong life: replenishing the original breath and prolonging life.
  • Liaozhenzi: the Daoist name of Xiao Tingzhi, an inner alchemist of the Song dynasty.
  • Celestial Valley (tiangu 天谷): that is, the Muddy Pellet.
  • Stilling the back, withdrawing into what is hidden: that is, “ablution” — storing the mind in the quiet place behind.
  • Earthen crucible, Winding River, three fields: the earthen crucible is the Yellow Court; the Winding River is a metaphor for the water of Kan (the lower body); the three fields are the upper, middle and lower Cinnabar Fields.
  • Yin’e 鄞鄂: the foundation and outline of the elixir; see Part One, Chapter 1.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Two steps to begin: first think intently of the Root of the Mountain (between the eyes), until “there is nothing left to think, and not a single thought arises”; then hold the intention on the Spine-Straddling Pass (in the middle of the body, between the seventh and eighth thoracic vertebrae, opposite the Chest Center point).
  2. “Constantly keep this mind stored in the opening of the Spine-Straddling Pass”: this can be done while walking, standing, sitting or lying down; the book considers it “the simplest and easiest” method — the mind is gently stored in the middle between chest and back, and does not run outward.
  3. Do not force it: Qiu Ling’s note specially warns that forcibly guiding the breath up and down with the intention produces the fault of “the Posterior Heaven making trouble in the Yellow.” From the Root of the Mountain to the Central Yellow, and from the Central Yellow to the Celestial Heart, everything “naturally reaches” and “naturally communicates.”
  4. Signs of progress: within, “a clear brightness, like the moon in water”; in the body, a sensation like hot broth pouring down, with a feeling of complete openness and ease.
  5. The root is preserving essence: “the first step, and the foundation, is the preservation and strengthening of the true essence” — moderating sexual desire, sleeping enough and not overworking are the preconditions for adding oil.
  6. Compare with Part One: holding the intention on the Spine-Straddling Pass = “the light gathers in the Central Yellow” in The Secret of the Golden Flower; thinking intently of the Root of the Mountain = “fixing the thought on the point level with and between the two eyes.” The two books describe the same road.

Translator's Notes

  1. “With a cry” (hudi yisheng 囫地一声): the base text here appears to read 囡地一声; Qiu Ling’s note in Part One quotes the same sentence as 囫地一声, which is clearly correct, and the Chinese edition follows it.
  2. “The Perfected sinks into the deep abyss, floating and roaming, guarding the compass center”: a line from the Token for the Agreement of the Three; the source is added in the translation.
  3. “Drawn in” (yi che er jin 以扯而进) and “the root becomes firm” (genben dinggu 根本定固): when Qiu Ling’s notes in Part One (Chapters 7 and 3) quote these two phrases, they read 以扯以进 and 根本完固 respectively. This chapter follows its own base text; the meaning is the same.
  4. “The heart and kidneys are eight cun four fen apart, and in the middle there remain one cun two fen”: this is a traditional statement of the alchemical books, meant to show that there is a “center” between heart and kidneys; it should not be measured against modern anatomy. This is the translator’s remark.
  5. The “Celestial Heart” means different things in this part and in Part One; see the separate senses listed under “Mysterious Opening” in the glossary.
