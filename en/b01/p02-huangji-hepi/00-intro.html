What this book is

Perfected Yin’s Immortal Scripture of the Opening and Closing of the Supreme Ultimate for Realizing the Dao, of the Orthodox Eastern Florescence Lineage (Yin zhenren Donghua zhengmai Huangji hepi zhengdao xianjing 尹真人东华正脉皇极阖辟证道仙经), usually shortened to Huangji hepi xianjing or Hepi xianjing, is attributed to a “Perfected Yin” (who exactly this was has long been disputed). It was originally a manuscript copy transmitted at the Qingyang Temple (Qingyang gong 青羊宫) in Chengdu, and was printed in 1834 (the jiawu year of the Daoguang reign) by the Longjiao Hermitage on Mount Jingai near Huzhou, Zhejiang; its editor was the same Min Yide, eleventh-generation master of the Longmen lineage. It comes from the same transmission as the Taiyi jinhua zongzhi of Part One, and the two books illuminate and complement each other: The Secret of the Golden Flower teaches only one entry method, “Reverting the Light,” while the Scripture of Opening and Closing goes through an entire system of inner alchemy from beginning to end.

Qiu Ling's editorial note

《尹真人东华正脉皇极阖辟证道仙经》一书源出于成都青羊宫传抄本，由浙湖金盖山龙峤山房于清道光甲午年镌刻，金盖山人龙门第十一代闵一得所编订。此书共十章，介绍全套道教气功功法，与《太乙金华宗旨》互相发明，相为表里，现摘其精要并略加注释如下：

Perfected Yin’s Immortal Scripture of the Opening and Closing of the Supreme Ultimate for Realizing the Dao, of the Orthodox Eastern Florescence Lineage derives from a manuscript copy transmitted at the Qingyang Temple in Chengdu. It was engraved and printed by the Longjiao Hermitage on Mount Jingai, Huzhou, Zhejiang, in the jiawu year of the Daoguang reign of the Qing, and edited by Min Yide, man of Mount Jingai and eleventh-generation master of the Longmen lineage. The book has ten chapters and presents a complete set of Daoist qigong methods; it and the Taiyi jinhua zongzhi illuminate each other and are like the outside and inside of one thing. Below I extract its essentials and add brief notes.

What this part is about (in one sentence)

Starting from the foundation of “adding oil to prolong life,” and passing through concentrating the spirit and entering the cavity, spirit and breath relying on each other, opening the passes, collecting the Medicine, the mao-you Celestial Circuit, nurturing the Holy Embryo, suckling the infant and moving the spirit up to the upper field, all the way to refining emptiness and uniting with the Dao — this is a complete road map of inner alchemical cultivation.

Reading tips

  1. Read it alongside Part One. The phrases “adding oil to prolong life” and “spirit and breath relying on each other” quoted in The Secret of the Golden Flower all come from this book. The “Spine-Straddling Pass” of Chapter 1 is the “Central Yellow” of The Secret of the Golden Flower.
  2. The two books differ greatly in style. The Secret of the Golden Flower insists that everything be natural and rejects guiding the breath with the intention (“the Celestial Circuit is not led by the breath”). This book, from Chapter 4 on, contains many explicit actions of intention and effort, such as contracting the anus, holding the breath, guiding the breath to break through the passes and rotating the gaze. These are two different paths; keep them apart when reading and do not mix them in practice.
  3. Be very careful with the methods from Chapter 4 on. Forceful actions such as breaking through the passes, holding the breath and lifting the anus are recognized, now as in the past, as the points where deviations most easily occur. Qiu Ling himself explains that the method of that chapter is a supplementary means “devised for people of middling and lower capacity.” This site only translates and organizes the texts; it does not constitute practice instruction. Without a reliable teacher guiding you in person, please do not practice the forceful actions of Chapters 4 to 6; they are especially unsuitable for people with high blood pressure, cardiovascular or cerebrovascular disease, glaucoma or hemorrhoids, and for pregnant women.
  4. Chapters 7 to 10 deal with very advanced stages. Qiu Ling says frankly that much of this is “a religious ideal,” that Chapter 9 “has much abstract theory and lacks concrete methods,” and that Chapter 10 “already belongs to religious ideal or fantasy.” We translate it as it is; readers can take it as material for understanding the full picture of this system.
