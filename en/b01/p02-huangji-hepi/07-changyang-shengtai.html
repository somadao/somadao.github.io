Chapter 7 · Nurturing the Holy Embryo

Chapter 7 · Nurturing the Holy Embryo — the ten months: only one word, “nourish”

About This Chapter

From this chapter on we enter the stage of “refining Breath into Spirit,” that is, “ten months of nurturing the embryo.” The earlier collecting of the Medicine and the Celestial Circuit refined in the Muddy Pellet a single grain of “Dragon-and-Tiger Golden Elixir.” Now it must come down from the Muddy Pellet, turn into golden liquid, fall into the Central Palace (the Elixir Bolt), and unite there with a point of true yang coming from the Great Void — this is “the Holy Embryo first congealing.” From then on the work comes down to one word: nourish. As a young dragon nourishes its pearl, as a young woman carries a child, one illuminates and is aware at all times, every thought here, in an undifferentiated state. The chapter ends by quoting Chen Xubai on the fire phasing of nurturing the embryo: no thought may arise — when a thought arises the fire flares; the intention may not scatter — when the intention scatters the fire grows cold. Qiu Ling’s note quotes at length various authors’ explanations of the “Holy Embryo” and the “infant” (the conclusion: the Holy Embryo and the infant are nothing but one breath), and also cites the concrete description of “nourishing the śarīra” in Clear Directions on the Methods and Formulas of Inner Nature and Existence (Xingming fajue mingzhi 性命法诀明指) by the Old Man of a Thousand Peaks, Zhao Bichen.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

始初那点金精，浑然在矿，因火相逼，遂上乾宫，渐采渐积，日烹日熔，损之又损，炼之又炼，直至烟消火灭，方才成一粒龙虎金丹，圆陀陀，活泼泼，辉煌闪灼，光耀昆仑，放则迸开天地窍，归复隐于翠微宫。此时药也不生，轮也不转，液也不降，火也不炎。五气俱朝于上阳，三花皆聚于乾顶，阳纯阴剥，丹熟珠灵，此其候也。

At first that point of golden essence lies undifferentiated in the ore. Pressed by the fire, it rises to the palace of Qian (the crown); gradually it is collected and accumulated, cooked day by day and smelted day by day, reduced and reduced again, refined and refined again, until the smoke has cleared and the fire died down. Only then does it become a single grain of Dragon-and-Tiger Golden Elixir: perfectly round, vividly alive, radiant and sparkling, its light shining over Kunlun; when released, it bursts open the openings of Heaven and Earth; when gathered back, it hides again in the Emerald Palace. At this time the Medicine is no longer born, the wheel no longer turns, the liquid no longer descends, the fire no longer flares. The five breaths all pay court to the Upper Yang, the three flowers all gather at the summit of Qian; yang is pure and yin stripped away, the elixir is ripe and the pearl numinous — these are the signs.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1
Original note (Chinese)

从此章开始论述炼炁化神即十月养胎阶段。练功至此，泥丸中已结金丹，眼前呈现丹之外象，即圆陀陀，活泼泼，辉煌闪灼的一粒圆光，即这个○。《大成捷要》描写为“此玄珠似乎在外，闭目却分明；似乎在内，开目极清白，真有不内不外者存。”此乃神炁即体内能量流的凝结物、结晶体。

From this chapter on, the text discusses the stage of refining Breath into Spirit, that is, ten months of nurturing the embryo. When practice reaches this point, the Golden Elixir has already formed in the Muddy Pellet, and before the eyes there appears the outer image of the elixir: a round light, perfectly round, vividly alive, radiant and sparkling — this ○. The Essentials of the Great Accomplishment describes it: “This mysterious pearl seems to be outside, yet when you close your eyes it is distinct; it seems to be inside, yet when you open your eyes it is perfectly clear and white — truly there is something neither inside nor outside.” This is spirit-breath, that is, a condensation, a crystal, of the flow of energy within the body.

§2
Original (Chinese)

然鼎中有宝非真宝，欲重结灵胎，而此珠尚在昆仑，何由得下而结耶？必假我灵，申透真阳之气以催之，太阳真火以逼之，催逼久则灵丹自应时而脱落，降入口中，化为金液而直射于丹扃之内，霎时云腾雨施，雷轰电掣，鏖战片响之间，而消尽一身阴滓，则百灵遂如车之辏毂，七宝直如水之朝宗矣。许宣平曰：“神居窍而千智生，丹入鼎而万种化。”

Yet the treasure in the cauldron is not yet the true treasure. One wishes to form the numinous embryo anew, but this pearl is still at Kunlun (the crown) — how can it come down and form the embryo? One must rely on one’s own numinous clarity, extending and pervading it with the breath of true yang to urge it on, and pressing it with the true fire of the sun. When it has been urged and pressed for a long time, the numinous elixir will drop off when its time comes, descend into the mouth, turn into golden liquid, and shoot straight into the Elixir Bolt (the Central Palace). In an instant clouds rise and rain falls, thunder roars and lightning flashes; in a moment’s fierce battle all the yin dregs of the body are wiped out. Then the hundred numinous powers of the body converge like spokes on a hub, and the seven treasures flow like rivers to the sea. Xu Xuanping said: “When the spirit dwells in the opening, a thousand wisdoms arise; when the elixir enters the cauldron, ten thousand transformations take place.”

Qiu Ling's noteon §2
Original note (Chinese)

为了移胎换鼎，使此灵丹从泥丸而下降黄庭，结成圣胎，置于中宫温养，须使“灵丹应时脱落，吞入口中，化为金液而直射丹扃之内”，这就要实行金液大还丹之功，使真炁经三关入泥丸，化为金液自上腭下降，香似醍醐，味如甘露，即所谓“醍醐灌顶”，而用真意引下重楼，送入中宫，中宫即丹扃也。这就是丹书所谓“一粒灵丹吞入腹，始知我命不由天。”

To “move the embryo and change the cauldron,” that is, to make this numinous elixir descend from the Muddy Pellet to the Yellow Court, form the Holy Embryo, and be placed in the Central Palace to be nourished warmly, one must make “the numinous elixir drop off when its time comes, be swallowed into the mouth, turn into golden liquid and shoot straight into the Elixir Bolt.” This requires the work of the Great Returned Elixir of Golden Liquid: the true breath passes through the three passes into the Muddy Pellet and turns into golden liquid, which descends from the palate, fragrant as ghee and tasting like sweet dew — this is what is called “ghee poured on the crown.” Then, with the True Intention, it is led down the Twelve-Storied Tower (the throat) and sent into the Central Palace, which is the Elixir Bolt. This is what the alchemical books mean by “once a grain of numinous elixir is swallowed into the belly, one knows that one’s life is no longer determined by Heaven.”

§3
Original (Chinese)

然我既得灵丹入鼎，内外交修，炼之又炼，至与天地合德，则太虚中自有一点真阳，从鼻窍（编者按：指玄窍）而入于中宫，与我之灵丹合而为一。盖吾身之灵，感天地之灵，内徵外应，浑然混合。老子云：“人能常清净，天地悉皆归。”当此两阳乍合，圣胎初凝，必须时常照觉，谨慎护持，如小龙之初养珠，如幼女之初怀孕，牢关圣室，不可使之渗漏，更于一切时中，四威仪内，时时照顾，念念在兹，混混沌沌，如子在抱，终日如愚，不可须臾间断也。葛仙翁曰：“息息归中无间断，天真胎里自坚凝。”陈虚白曰：“念不可起，念起则火炎；意不可散，意散则火冷。”惟要不起不散，含光默默，真息绵绵，此长养圣胎之火候也。

Once I have obtained the numinous elixir and it has entered the cauldron, I cultivate within and without, refining and refining again, until I share the virtue of Heaven and Earth. Then, from the Great Void, a point of true yang naturally enters the Central Palace through the opening of the nose (editor’s note (Qiu Ling): meaning the Mysterious Opening) and unites with my numinous elixir as one. For the numinous power of my body resonates with the numinous power of Heaven and Earth; signs within and responses without blend into one. Laozi says: “If a person can always be pure and still, Heaven and Earth will all return to him.” When these two yangs have just joined and the Holy Embryo has just congealed, one must constantly illuminate and be aware, guard and protect it with care, like a young dragon beginning to nourish its pearl, like a young girl newly with child; firmly close the holy chamber and do not let anything leak. Moreover, at all times, in all four postures, watch over it constantly, every thought here, in an undifferentiated state, like a child held in the arms, all day long like a simpleton, without a moment’s interruption. Immortal Elder Ge said: “Every breath returns to the center without interruption; the embryo of heavenly truth becomes firm and congealed of itself.” Chen Xubai said: “No thought may arise — when a thought arises, the fire flares; the intention may not scatter — when the intention scatters, the fire grows cold.” Only let thought not arise and intention not scatter; hold in the light in silence, with the True Breath continuous and soft — this is the fire phasing for nurturing the Holy Embryo.

Qiu Ling's noteon §3
Original note (Chinese)

圣胎、胎仙、婴儿、赤子乃一物，这是珠落黄庭后的称呼。张紫阳《悟真篇》说：“三家相见结婴儿，婴儿是一含真气，十月胎圆入圣基，”清代刘明一《修真辩难参证》说：“圣胎之婴儿是先天之气，自虚无中来与真汞配合而成象者，所谓阳神者是也。”明代伍伍虚《天仙正理》说：“胎即神炁耳，非真有婴儿也，非有形有象也。”所以说圣胎、婴儿不过一炁而已。

养胎之法在于主静。钟离权曰：“胎内婴儿就，勤加温养功，时时照丹扃，刻刻守中黄。”其火候即所谓未得丹时须藉武火，既得丹时须藉文火以养之，绵绵若存，勿忘勿助之意是也。王重阳曰：“圣胎既凝，养以文火，安神定息，任其自如。”清代李德洽《上品丹法节次》说：“得丹之后，为十月养胎之始。此后当刻意操持，时时照顾，如龙养珠，如鸡抱卵，暖气不绝，始得灵胎日渐坚固，一意温和为主，念不可起，念起则火炎，意不可散，意散则火冷。第令无过不及，惟以炉里温温为是，别无他法也。若有作为，危险立至”。又说：“如遇丹火发热，存想两眉间有一黑球，如碗大，收摄入于神室，其热自退，不可不知。”

千峰老人赵避尘在《性命法诀明指》一书径直指出，长养圣胎即养舍利子，并泄露了此中法诀。他说：“将精炁神炼足，聚于顶内，总得以真气真神炼之。催逼既久，灵丹脱落，吞入口中，化为津液，腹响如雷，滋养舍利子，后由炁穴生出舍利之光，能虚室生白，圆圆陀陀，此即舍利子成也，百脉呼吸气长停。这段功夫，全以至静为主，如龙养珠，如幼女怀孕，要自知静心养之，舍利子方足也。”他说：“精不足不能生慧光，舍利子不足，不能生蟾光。慧光如如月光，蟾光如金光。”又说：“蟾光者，乃取三足金蟾之意，是精气神三品合一之理。”他指出：“舍利子不足，还得加功细炼，每日参禅打坐时，两眼归并合一，神气下照丹田，是助舍利子发生足满。顶有颠鸾之状，耳发龙吟虎啸之声，其身如在云端，通身酥麻发痒，如凭虚御风，快乐无边，满面如珠网罩面，又如蚁行，痒痒欲搔，散之印堂，次到鼻柱眼眶，两颧两腮牙关口中，津液升满，咽纳不尽。此时口闭懒开，身沉懒动，入于混沌，化为无有，并不知身在何处，自然息住脉停，真气充满，滋养舍利子也。”

The Holy Embryo, the Embryonic Immortal, the infant and the newborn child are one and the same thing; these are the names for it after the pearl has fallen into the Yellow Court. Zhang Ziyang’s Awakening to Reality says: “When the three families meet, the infant is formed; the infant is the One, holding true breath; when the embryo is complete in ten months, one enters the foundation of sagehood.” Liu Yiming of the Qing dynasty says in his Verifications of the Doubts on Cultivating Reality (Xiuzhen biannan canzheng 修真辩难参证): “The infant of the Holy Embryo is the breath of Prior Heaven, which comes from emptiness and non-being and combines with true mercury to take shape — it is what is called the Yang Spirit.” Wu Chongxu of the Ming dynasty says in The Correct Principles of Celestial Immortality: “The embryo is simply spirit and breath; there is no real infant, nothing with form or appearance.” So the Holy Embryo and the infant are nothing but one breath.

The method of nurturing the embryo lies in making stillness primary. Zhongli Quan said: “When the infant within the embryo is formed, diligently add the work of warm nourishing: at every moment illuminate the Elixir Bolt, at every instant guard the Central Yellow.” Its fire phasing is what is meant by “before obtaining the elixir one must rely on the martial fire; once the elixir is obtained one must rely on the civil fire to nourish it” — that is, the sense of “continuous and barely perceptible, neither forgetting nor forcing.” Wang Chongyang said: “Once the Holy Embryo has congealed, nourish it with the civil fire; calm the spirit, settle the breath, and let it be as it is.” Li Deqia of the Qing dynasty says in his Stages of the Superior Alchemical Method (Shangpin danfa jieci 上品丹法节次): “After obtaining the elixir begin the ten months of nurturing the embryo. From then on one should hold to it attentively and watch over it at all times, like a dragon nourishing its pearl, like a hen brooding an egg, the warmth never interrupted; only then does the numinous embryo grow firmer day by day. Gentle warmth with a single intention is the main thing: no thought may arise — when a thought arises the fire flares; the intention may not scatter — when the intention scatters the fire grows cold. Simply let there be neither excess nor deficiency, and take a gentle warmth in the furnace as the standard; there is no other method. If one acts deliberately, danger comes at once.” He also says: “If the fire of the elixir produces heat, visualize a black ball between the eyebrows, as big as a bowl, and draw it into the Chamber of Spirit; the heat will subside of itself. One must know this.”

The Old Man of a Thousand Peaks, Zhao Bichen, states plainly in Clear Directions on the Methods and Formulas of Inner Nature and Existence that nurturing the Holy Embryo is nourishing the śarīra (shelizi 舍利子), and he reveals the formula for it. He says: “Refine essence, breath and spirit until they are sufficient and gather them inside the crown; they must always be refined with true breath and true spirit. When they have been urged and pressed for a long time, the numinous elixir drops off, is swallowed into the mouth and turns into saliva; the belly rumbles like thunder, and it nourishes the śarīra. Afterward the light of the śarīra comes forth from the Cavity of Breath and can make ‘the empty chamber produce whiteness,' perfectly round — this means the śarīra is complete, and the breathing of all the vessels stops for long periods. This stage of the work is entirely based on utmost stillness, like a dragon nourishing its pearl, like a young girl with child; one must know oneself how to nourish it with a still mind, and only then will the śarīra be sufficient.” He says: “If essence is insufficient, the light of wisdom cannot arise; if the śarīra is insufficient, the toad light cannot arise. The light of wisdom is like moonlight; the toad light is like golden light.” And: “The toad light takes its meaning from the three-legged golden toad; it is the principle of the three grades, essence, breath and spirit, united as one.” He points out: “If the śarīra is insufficient, one must add work and refine it finely. Every day, when sitting in meditation, bring the two eyes together into one and let spirit and breath illuminate the Cinnabar Field below; this helps the śarīra to arise and become full. Then at the crown there is a feeling like a phoenix tumbling in flight; in the ears there are sounds like a dragon’s chant and a tiger’s roar; the body seems to be on the clouds; the whole body tingles, numb and itchy, as if riding the wind through the void, with boundless joy; the whole face feels as if covered with a net of pearls, or as if ants were crawling on it, so itchy one wants to scratch. This spreads to the Hall of Impression (between the eyebrows), then to the bridge of the nose and the eye sockets, the cheekbones, the cheeks, the jaw and the mouth; saliva rises and fills the mouth, more than one can swallow. At this time the mouth stays closed and one is too lazy to open it, the body is heavy and one is too lazy to move; one enters the undifferentiated state and dissolves into nothing, without knowing where the body is; naturally the breath stops and the pulse halts, the true breath fills everything and nourishes the śarīra.”

Key Terms

  • Refining Breath into Spirit / ten months of nurturing the embryo: the second great stage of inner alchemy; see the three transmutations.
  • Dragon-and-Tiger Golden Elixir: the elixir formed by the union of dragon (inner nature, mercury) and tiger (existence, lead).
  • Kunlun, Emerald Palace, palace of Qian, summit of Qian: all refer to the area of the Muddy Pellet at the crown.
  • The five breaths paying court to the Upper Yang, the three flowers gathering at the summit: the breaths of the five viscera and the three flowers of essence, breath and spirit all converge at the crown — a sign that the elixir is ripe.
  • Moving the embryo and changing the cauldron: making the elixir descend from the Muddy Pellet (the upper cauldron) into the Central Palace (the lower cauldron).
  • Elixir Bolt (danjiong 丹扃): the Central Palace, that is, the Yellow Court, the middle Cinnabar Field.
  • Twelve-Storied Tower: the throat (the windpipe).
  • Great Returned Elixir of Golden Liquid: the work by which the true breath turns into golden liquid and descends from the palate.
  • Sweet Dew / ghee poured on the crown.
  • Holy Embryo / Embryonic Immortal / infant / newborn child / Yang Spirit: different names for the same thing; Wu Chongxu says, “The embryo is simply spirit and breath; there is no real infant.”
  • The two yangs just joined: the yang of the Great Void and the yang of my numinous elixir meeting for the first time.
  • Four postures: walking, standing, sitting, lying down.
  • Civil fire, martial fire: see fire phasing.
  • Śarīra (shelizi): the Qianfeng school’s name for the elixir (the Holy Embryo).
  • Light of wisdom, toad light: see Qiu Ling’s note in Part One, Chapter 1; Zhao Bichen says the toad light takes its meaning from the “three-legged golden toad,” a metaphor for the union of the three grades, essence, breath and spirit.

Putting It into Practice

  1. This stage has only one word: nourish. No more collecting, no more turning, no more guiding; “if one acts deliberately, danger comes at once” (Li Deqia).
  2. The standard sentence on fire phasing (worth learning by heart): no thought may arise — when a thought arises the fire flares; the intention may not scatter — when the intention scatters the fire grows cold. Neither arising nor scattering, holding in the light in silence, the True Breath continuous and soft. In fact this sentence applies to quiet sitting at any stage.
  3. “A gentle warmth in the furnace”: below, there is always a mild, gentle warmth, neither cold nor dry — that is right.
  4. A practical remedy (Li Deqia): if during quiet sitting “the fire of the elixir produces heat” — a dry, uncomfortable heat in the body — you can visualize a black ball as big as a bowl between the eyebrows and draw it into the Chamber of Spirit, and the heat will subside. This is an old method of adjustment, offered for reference.
  5. Do not seek extraordinary phenomena: the tingling, the crawling ants, the dragon’s chant and tiger’s roar described by Zhao Bichen are records of his experience. Everyone’s experience differs; if they do not appear, it does not mean you are practicing wrongly; if they do appear, do not chase them.

Translator's Notes

  1. “In a moment’s fierce battle”: the Chinese 片响 should read 片晌, “a moment.”
  2. “Wu Chongxu of the Ming dynasty”: the base text reads 伍伍虚, an error for 伍冲虚 (Wu Shouyang); the translation corrects it.
  3. “Liu Yiming of the Qing dynasty”: here the base text reads 刘明一, while Chapter 9 has 刘一明; 刘一明 (Liu Yiming) is correct, and the translation corrects it.
  4. “Laozi says: ‘If a person can always be pure and still, Heaven and Earth will all return to him’”: this line comes from the Scripture of Constant Purity and Stillness Spoken by the Most High Lord Lao (Taishang Laojun shuo chang qingjing jing 太上老君说常清静经), attributed to Laozi.
  5. The last sentence of the first paragraph of Qiu Ling’s note, “This is spirit-breath, that is, a condensation, a crystal, of the flow of energy within the body,” is Qiu Ling’s own explanation in modern language; it is translated as it stands.
