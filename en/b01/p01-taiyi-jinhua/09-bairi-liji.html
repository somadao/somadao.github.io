Chapter 9 · Laying the Foundation in a Hundred Days

Chapter 9 · Laying the Foundation in a Hundred Days — the first hundred days

Listen to this chapterENEnglish

About This Chapter

A very short chapter that gives a time scale: only after a hundred days of devoted Reverting the Light is there true light; essence and breath naturally become abundant, true yang is naturally born, and within the water there is naturally a true fire. After that, all the conjunction and the forming of the embryo happen by themselves — in a state of “not knowing, not perceiving,” the infant is already complete. The last sentence is the iron rule of the whole part: add even the slightest contrivance, and you are on a heretical path.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

《心印经》云：迴风混合，百日功灵。总之立基百日，方有真光。回之百日，则精气自足，真阳自生，水中自有真火。以此持行，自然交媾，自然结胎，吾方在不识不知之天，而婴儿已成矣。若略作意，便是外道。

The Scripture of the Mind Seal says: “Turn the wind and blend them; in a hundred days the work becomes effective.” In short, only after laying the foundation for a hundred days is there true light. Revert the light for a hundred days, and essence and breath naturally become abundant, true yang is naturally born, and within the water there is naturally a true fire. Keep practicing like this, and the conjunction happens by itself, the embryo forms by itself; while I am still in the natural state of “not knowing, not perceiving,” the infant is already complete. Add even the slightest contrivance, and you are on a heretical path.

Qiu Ling's noteon the whole chapter
Original note (Chinese)

此示人迴旋其呼吸之气，方得神气归根复命；而混合之，方得神宰于气而合一。若用于百日之功，则灵验可见。

The phrase “turn the wind and blend them” teaches people to turn their breath around; only then can spirit and breath return to the root and go back to life. Then blend them together; only then does the spirit govern the breath and become one with it. If this method is applied over a hundred days of practice, its efficacy can be seen.

Key Terms

  • Scripture of the Mind Seal (Xinyin jing 心印经): the Wondrous Scripture of the Mind Seal of the Exalted Jade Emperor, a scripture commonly recited in Daoism, which contains the line “turn the wind and blend them; in a hundred days the work becomes effective.”
  • Turning the wind and blending: “turning the wind” means circulating the breath (wind standing for breathing); “blending” means letting spirit and breath merge into one.
  • Laying the foundation in a hundred days: commonly called “building the foundation in a hundred days” (bairi zhuji 百日筑基) in alchemy — the first, foundational stage.
  • True fire within the water: the point of true yang hidden in the water of Kan, that is, the One Breath of Prior Heaven.
  • Forming the embryo / the infant: images for spirit and breath congealing — the Holy Embryo.
  • Not knowing, not perceiving: from the Book of Odes, “Great Elegantiae,” “Huang yi”: “not knowing, not perceiving, following the laws of the Lord”; meaning without contrivance, in complete naturalness.
  • Heretical path (waidao 外道): a Buddhist term for practices that deviate from the right way.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Take a hundred days as one unit: keep up your daily Reverting the Light and regulating the breath for a hundred days without interruption, then look at the results. This is far more practical than chasing some special experience.
  2. Naturalness is the only standard: the conjunction and the forming of the embryo are both described as happening “by themselves.” Do not “do” them; simply keep reverting the light, regulating the breath and letting go.
  3. “Add even the slightest contrivance, and you are on a heretical path”: when you notice yourself imagining, guiding, or chasing a particular sensation, that is contrivance — gently let it go and return to the simplest Reverting the Light and listening to the breath.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Laying the foundation for a hundred days”: alchemical books commonly say “building the foundation in a hundred days.” The “hundred days” is an approximate unit of time; people progress at different speeds, and there is no need to expect a particular result on the hundredth day. This is the translator’s remark.
