Chapter 12 · The Celestial Circuit

Chapter 12 · The Celestial Circuit — led by the mind, not by guiding the breath; a summary of the whole part

About This Chapter

Many methods describe the Celestial Circuit (zhoutian 周天) as making the breath travel in a loop along the Conception and Governing vessels. The Celestial Circuit of The Secret of the Golden Flower is entirely different: it is not led by the breath; its secret is “the mind arriving.” Deliberately “doing” the circuit is like pulling up seedlings to help them grow. The mind is like the pivot of the Dipper, which does not move; the breath is like the stars, which revolve by themselves. Each time Kan and Li join, that is one circuit; when yin and yang are in harmony, the Central Palace is in its right place and all things flourish, that is the Greater Celestial Circuit. The chapter — and the whole part — ends with a summary: a still mind and settled breath are the foundation; a forgetting mind and congealed breath are the sign; ceased breath and an empty mind are the completed elixir; mind and breath merged into one is nurturing; illuminating the mind and seeing one’s nature is completing the Dao.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

周天非以气作主，以心到为妙诀。若毕竟如何周天，是助长也。无心而守，无意而行，仰观乎天，三百六十五度，刻刻变迁，而斗枢终古不移，吾心亦犹是也。心即斗枢，气即群星，吾身之气，四肢百骸原是贯通，不要十分著力，于此锻炼识神，断除妄见，然后药生。药非有形之物，此性光也，而即先天之真炁，然必于大定后方见，并无采法，言采者大谬矣！见之既久，心地光明，自然心空漏尽。若今日龙虎，明日水火，终成妄想。

The Celestial Circuit is not led by the breath; its wondrous secret is “the mind arriving.” If you insist on working out exactly how to perform the circuit, you are pulling up the seedlings to help them grow. Guard it without mind, practice it without intention. Look up at the sky: its three hundred and sixty-five degrees change from moment to moment, yet the pivot of the Dipper has never moved since ancient times. My mind should be the same. The mind is the pivot of the Dipper; the breath is the host of stars. The breath of my body already flows freely through the four limbs and hundred bones; do not apply too much force. Simply train the cognitive spirit here and cut off deluded views, and then the Medicine is born. The Medicine is not a thing with form: it is the light of nature, which is also the true breath of Prior Heaven. But it appears only after great absorption, and there is no method for “collecting” it — those who speak of collecting it are greatly mistaken! When you have seen it for a long time, the ground of the mind becomes bright, and naturally the mind is empty and all outflows are exhausted. If today you work on dragon and tiger and tomorrow on water and fire, it will all end in deluded imaginings.

§2
Original (Chinese)

一日有一周天，一刻有一周天，坎离交处，便是一周。我之交，即天之回旋也，未能休歇，所以有交之时，即有不交之时，然天之回旋未尝少息。果能阴阳交泰，大地阳和，我之中宫正位，万物一时畅遂，即丹经沐浴法也，非大周天而何。

There is a Celestial Circuit in a day, and a Celestial Circuit in a quarter-hour: wherever Kan and Li meet, that is one circuit. The joining within me is the turning of Heaven. It is only because I have not yet truly come to rest that there are times when they join and times when they do not; but the turning of Heaven never stops for a moment. If yin and yang can truly join in harmony, the great earth is warm with yang, my Central Palace is in its right place, and all things at once flourish freely — this is the method of “ablution” spoken of in the alchemical scriptures. What is this if not the Greater Celestial Circuit?

§3
Original (Chinese)

此中火候，实实有大小不同，究竟无大小可别。到得功夫自然，不知坎离为何物，天地为何等，孰为交，孰为一周两周，何处觅大小之分别耶？总之，一身旋运难真。不真，见得极大亦小；真，则一回旋，天地万物悉与之回旋，即在方寸处，极小亦为极大。

The fire phasing here really does have greater and lesser forms, yet in the end there is no great or small to distinguish. When the practice becomes natural, you no longer know what Kan and Li are, or what Heaven and Earth are like; which is a joining, which is one circuit or two — where would you look for a distinction between great and small? In short, a rotation that turns within one body alone is hard to make genuine. If it is not genuine, even what seems enormous is small; if it is genuine, then at a single turn Heaven, Earth and all things turn with it, and even within the Square Inch the smallest becomes the greatest.

§4
Original (Chinese)

故金丹火候，全要行归自然，不自然，天地自还天地，万物各归万物，若欲强之使合，终不能合，即如天时亢旱，阴阳不和，乾坤未尝一日不周，然终见得有多少不自然处。我能转运阴阳，调摄自然，一时云蒸雨降，草木酣适，山河流畅，纵有乖戾，亦觉顿释，此即大周天也。

So the fire phasing of the Golden Elixir must be entirely returned to naturalness. Without naturalness, Heaven and Earth remain Heaven and Earth, and all things remain each in its own place; if you try to force them together, they will never join. It is like a time of severe drought, when yin and yang are out of harmony: Qian and Kun have not stopped revolving for a single day, yet one can see many things that are not natural. If I can turn yin and yang and adjust and nourish them naturally, then at once clouds rise and rain falls, plants and trees are refreshed, mountains and rivers flow freely; even if something is out of tune, I feel it dissolve at once. This is the Greater Celestial Circuit.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

性光即先天真炁，即药物，要在虚极静笃、心地光明后才看到，故有“明心见性”之说。周天亦以心到为妙诀。坎离交处便是一周，功夫自然，则可以天时验内功，我感觉云蒸雨降，草木酣适，山河流畅，即周天也。

The light of nature is the true breath of Prior Heaven, that is, the Medicine; it is seen only after emptiness reaches its limit and stillness becomes complete, when the ground of the mind is bright — hence the saying “illuminate the mind and see one’s nature.” The secret of the Celestial Circuit, too, is “the mind arriving.” Where Kan and Li meet is one circuit. When the practice becomes natural, one can verify the inner work by the weather: when I feel within my body clouds rising and rain falling, plants and trees refreshed, mountains and rivers flowing freely — that is the Celestial Circuit.

§5
Original (Chinese)

回光在纯心行去，只待真意凝照于中宫，久之自能通灵达变也。总是心静气定为基，心忘气凝为效，气息心空为丹成，心气浑一为温养，明心见性为了道。

Reverting the Light consists in practicing wholeheartedly, simply waiting for the True Intention to congeal and shine on the Central Palace; in time you will naturally attain spiritual insight and mastery of transformation. In sum: a still mind and settled breath are the foundation; a forgetting mind and congealed breath are the sign of progress; ceased breath and an empty mind are the completion of the elixir; mind and breath merged into one is nurturing; illuminating the mind and seeing one’s nature is the completion of the Dao.

Qiu Ling's noteon §5
Original note (Chinese)

此节总结全篇。金华回光功法虽简，但功理却颇深奥，大有诀窍在焉。学者若能识其诀窍，按功法自然行之，则可自然而然地实现胎息、坎离交媾及周天，使天心玄窍开启，从而取得开天目、见法像、结金丹等效验。

This section sums up the whole part. The Golden Flower method of Reverting the Light is simple, but its principles are quite profound and full of subtle keys. If students can recognize these keys and practice the method naturally, they can quite naturally attain embryonic breathing, the conjunction of Kan and Li and the Celestial Circuit, so that the Mysterious Opening of the Celestial Heart opens, and they obtain such signs as the opening of the Celestial Eye, the seeing of the Dharma image and the forming of the Golden Elixir.

Key Terms

  • Celestial Circuit (zhoutian): as this book defines it — wherever Kan and Li meet is one circuit; it is led by the mind arriving, not by guiding the breath.
  • Pulling up seedlings to help them grow: from Mencius 2A, the story of the farmer who pulled on his seedlings to make them grow faster.
  • Handle of the Dipper / pivot of the Dipper: the mind is like the unmoving pivot of the Dipper, the breath like the revolving stars.
  • Medicine: as this book defines it — not a thing with form; it is the light of nature, the true breath of Prior Heaven; it appears only after great absorption, and there is no method for collecting it.
  • The mind empty and all outflows exhausted: “outflows” (lou 漏) are, in Buddhism, the afflictions; their exhaustion means the afflictions are completely cut off.
  • Dragon and tiger, water and fire: common symbols of yin and yang in the alchemical scriptures; here they stand for methods that cling to names and forms.
  • Ablution: yin and yang joined in harmony, the Central Palace in its right place, all things flourishing.
  • Greater Celestial Circuit: this book takes the natural harmony of yin and yang as the Greater Celestial Circuit, which is not what the Wu–Liu school means by the Greater Celestial Circuit after “the Great Medicine passes the gates.”
  • Fire phasing: “must be entirely returned to naturalness.”
  • Central Palace: the same as the Central Yellow.
  • Spiritual insight and mastery of transformation: penetrating spiritual clarity and attaining mastery of change.
  • Completing the Dao: accomplishing the cultivation of the Dao.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Do not deliberately circulate the breath in a loop: The Secret of the Golden Flower explicitly rejects “dragon and tiger today, water and fire tomorrow,” and rejects using the intention to guide the breath along the Conception and Governing vessels. Let the mind rest (the pivot of the Dipper does not move), and the breath flows through naturally (the stars revolve by themselves).
  2. What the Celestial Circuit really means: each time spirit and breath join (concentrating the spirit and gathering in the breath) is one Celestial Circuit. There can be many in a day.
  3. Do not “collect” the Medicine: it appears by itself after great absorption. If during practice the thought “I must grab it” arises, the cognitive spirit has risen again.
  4. Feel it through the weather: Qiu Ling says that when the practice is natural, you can verify the inner work by the weather: when you feel within your body something like clouds rising and rain falling, plants and trees refreshed, mountains and rivers flowing freely — that is the Celestial Circuit.
  5. The five-line summary (worth learning by heart, as a long-term check on yourself):
    • A still mind and settled breath — the foundation
    • A forgetting mind and congealed breath — the sign of progress
    • Ceased breath and an empty mind — the completed elixir
    • Mind and breath merged into one — nurturing
    • Illuminating the mind and seeing one’s nature — completing the Dao

Translator's Notes

  1. “If you insist on working out exactly how to perform the circuit”: bijing 毕竟 here means “insisting on pursuing, determined to have it a certain way.”
  2. “Ceased breath and an empty mind are the completion of the elixir”: xi 息 here means “ceasing,” that is, the breath has settled and stopped, and the mind is empty and clear.
  3. This book’s view of the Celestial Circuit is very different from that of the Wu–Liu school, which describes in detail the procedures of the Lesser Celestial Circuit — advancing the yang fire, withdrawing the yin response, ablution, collecting the Medicine and so on (see the entries “Lesser Celestial Circuit” and “River Chariot” in Jingxuzi’s book). The glossary records the two schools’ explanations separately; readers should not confuse them. This is the translator’s remark.
