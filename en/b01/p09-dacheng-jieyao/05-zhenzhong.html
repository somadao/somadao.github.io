The Concise Essentials of Great Accomplishment, Selected and Annotated · 一·5 产真种不老不嫩景象

I.5 · Signs of the True Seed Produced Neither Too Old nor Too Young

Records of Experience in Refining Essence into Breath, Section 5 · Signs of the True Seed Produced Neither Too Old nor Too Young — the timing of the medicine’s production and the four-word formula for gathering it

Listen to this chapterENEnglish

Safety note (translator): This section speaks of “the breathing suddenly breaking off” and “lying hidden for eighty or ninety breaths, or one or two hundred breaths.” This describes the phenomenon, recorded by the ancients, of the breath becoming naturally very fine and even seeming to stop in deep stillness; it is by no means an instruction to hold the breath, and deliberately holding the breath does harm and no good. In the four-word formula “inhale, lick, contract, close” at the end of this section, “contract” means tightly contracting the anus and lifting it upward, a forceful ancient method that should not be practiced on one’s own for long periods or with great force; it is especially unsuitable for those with hemorrhoids, pelvic floor disorders, or during pregnancy.

About This Chapter

This section is the most detailed account of “inner scenes” in the whole text: when the interaction of spirit and breath reaches its extreme, the Cinnabar Field becomes melting and warm, the whole body soft and blissful, the pores itch, the outer kidney rises of itself, it is as if cool air were blowing in at the crown, there is suddenly a “roar” in the Cinnabar Field, the breathing of mouth and nose seems to break off, the breath flows back into the Original Sea… this repeats three or five, even six or seven times; the Cinnabar Field becomes empty and boundless, holding a thing within — this is the “sprout of the medicine.”

The author stresses two things:

  • Timing: while the sprout of the medicine is still “young,” one must not stir a thought to gather it, but return to emptiness and wait; when the mind and nature become numinous again, the breathing starts again, the outer kidney rises again, the breath comes out of the nose again — “when the time comes, the breath transforms; when the medicine is produced, the spirit knows” — only then is it neither too old nor too young, just right for gathering.
  • Method: apply in succession the four-word formula “inhale, lick, contract, close,” gather the medicine and return it to the furnace, seal it tightly, and go on to the fire phasing of the Celestial Circuit.

Qiu Ling’s note sets aside the details that differ from person to person and distills the signs common to all; it explains what is meant by “young” and what by “old,” and finally explains the four-word formula word by word.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

先天真种，本来无相，因神气交感混合已极，不知不觉忽然丹田融融洽洽，周身苏绵快乐，痒生毫窍，身心无主，丹田温暖，气机渐渐流动，阳物勃然兴起，顶门有冷气吹入，而气穴之中忽然一吼，呼吸顿断，离于口鼻，倒回元海，只觉得下田之中冬冬有声，忽又停住不动，炁息浮起或透出鼻息，或未透出鼻息，或至喉而返，如此一连三次五次或六、七次而后已。

The true seed of Prior Heaven originally has no form. Because the interaction and mingling of spirit and breath has reached its extreme, without one knowing it the Cinnabar Field suddenly becomes melting and harmonious, the whole body soft and blissful, itching arises in the pores, body and mind seem to have no master, the Cinnabar Field is warm, the mechanism of breath gradually begins to flow, the penis rises vigorously, it is as if cool air were blowing in at the crown gate, and suddenly there is a “roar” in the Cavity of Breath; the breathing suddenly breaks off, leaves the mouth and nose, and flows back into the Original Sea; one feels only a “dong-dong” sound in the lower field, then it suddenly stops and does not move; then the breath floats up, sometimes coming out through the nose, sometimes not coming out through the nose, sometimes turning back at the throat. This happens three times, five times, or six or seven times in a row before it ceases.

§2
Original (Chinese)

此时丹田空洞无际，上无覆，下无基，中含一物，此乃是药苗。气机仍嫩之时，切不可动念采取，当还虚以待之。此时，心息如磁石之相翕，神气如虫蛰之相含，恍恍惚惚，我自不肯舍彼，彼自不肯离我，相亲相恋，纽成一团，不知不觉入于混沌。

At this time the Cinnabar Field is empty and boundless, with no cover above and no base below, holding a thing within — this is the sprout of the medicine. While the mechanism of breath is still young, one must never stir a thought to gather it, but should return to emptiness and wait for it. At this time mind and breath attract each other like a magnet, spirit and breath contain each other like hibernating insects; vague and indistinct, I am unwilling to let it go, and it is unwilling to leave me; cleaving to and loving each other, twisted into one mass, without knowing it one enters chaos.

§3
Original (Chinese)

其中景象，似施似翕，而实未见其施翕。似泄似漏，而实未见其泄漏。此时四肢百骸，皆不能转动。少焉痒生毫窍，肢体如绵，恍惚之间心性复灵，上则呼吸复起，下则阳物复兴，丹田之气自下往后而行肾管之根。所谓气满任督自开。琼钟一扣，玉洞双吹，鼻息复出。时至气化，药产神知。

The scene within seems to release and seems to gather, yet in fact no releasing or gathering is seen; it seems about to leak and seems about to escape, yet in fact no leaking or escaping is seen. At this time none of the four limbs or hundred bones can move. After a while itching arises in the pores, the limbs are soft as cotton, and in the vagueness the mind and nature become numinous again: above, the breathing starts again; below, the penis rises again; the breath of the Cinnabar Field goes from below toward the back, to the root of the kidney duct. This is what is called “when the breath is full, the Conception and Governing vessels open of themselves.” “The jade bell is struck once, the jade caves blow as a pair” — the breath comes out of the nose again. When the time comes, the breath transforms; when the medicine is produced, the spirit knows.

§4
Original (Chinese)

这才是玄关窍开，真种产出正是不老不嫩。此时千万不可错过，连用吸、舐、撮、闭四字真口诀，凝神入气穴，采药归炉，封固严密，运动周天火符之玄机，转大法轮之妙用，以烹以炼，结成金丹大药，三百周天数足，而为超凡入圣之基。此精满而返到乾体未破之时也。

Only this is the opening of the Mysterious Pass, and the true seed produced at this moment is just neither too old nor too young. At this time one must on no account miss it: apply in succession the four-word true oral formula “inhale, lick, contract, close,” concentrate the spirit in the Cavity of Breath, gather the medicine and return it to the furnace, seal it tightly, set in motion the mysterious mechanism of the fire tallies of the Celestial Circuit and the wondrous use of turning the great Dharma wheel, cooking and refining it to form the great medicine of the golden elixir; when the count of three hundred circuits is complete, it becomes the foundation for transcending the ordinary and entering sagehood. This is the time when, the essence being full, one returns to the state in which the Qian body is not yet broken.

§5
Original (Chinese)

又云，呼吸倒回元海，蛰藏八、九十息或一、二百息，方有气息冲出，此是真正火候。

It is also said: when the breathing flows back into the Original Sea and lies hidden there for eighty or ninety breaths, or one or two hundred breaths, before a breath bursts out, this is the true fire phasing.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§5
Original note (Chinese)

此写真种产生景象颇详。细微处或者人各不同，其共通处则为气机氤氲发动，肾间微痒，周身踊跃，肢体如绵，从十指以至一身，跳动不止，身如壁立，意若寒灰，丹田气暖，此即药之不老不嫩合中之时。呼吸倒回元海之际便即采药，谓之嫩，则气微而不灵，不能结丹；阳物兴起已衰才采药，谓之老，则气散而不灵，亦不能结丹。

吸、舐、撮、闭乃采药之诀。吸者，鼻中吸气以接先天；舐者，舌舐上腭以迎甘露；撮者，紧撮谷道内中上提；闭者，塞兑垂帘，闭住三关耳、目、口。

This describes in considerable detail the signs of the true seed being produced. In the fine details each person may differ, but what is common is this: the mechanism of breath stirs in a dense haze, there is a slight itching between the kidneys, the whole body leaps, the limbs are soft as cotton, from the ten fingers to the whole body there is ceaseless throbbing, the body stands like a wall, the intention is like cold ashes, and the breath in the Cinnabar Field is warm — this is the moment when the medicine is neither too old nor too young, just right. If one gathers the medicine as soon as the breathing flows back into the Original Sea, it is called “young”: the breath is then still faint and not potent, and cannot form the elixir; if one gathers the medicine only after the rising of the penis has already waned, it is called “old”: the breath has then scattered and is not potent, and cannot form the elixir either.

“Inhale, lick, contract, close” is the formula for gathering the medicine. “Inhale”: draw in breath through the nose to receive Prior Heaven. “Lick”: the tongue licks the upper palate to welcome the Sweet Dew. “Contract”: tightly contract the grain path and lift it upward from within. “Close”: block the dui (the mouth) and lower the curtains, closing the three passes of ear, eye, and mouth — the Three Lights.

Key Terms

  • True seed, sprout of the medicine: names for the true breath of Prior Heaven at its first arising; see Medicine.
  • Original Sea: the lower Cinnabar Field (Sea of Breath).
  • Penis: that is, the outer kidney.
  • Crown gate: the fontanel at the top of the head.
  • Young, old: Qiu Ling’s note — gathering as soon as the breathing flows back into the Original Sea is “young”; gathering only after the rising of the penis has waned is “old”; neither can form the elixir. See the entry Medicine, “old and young.”
  • Returning to emptiness and waiting: when the medicine is young, not stirring a thought but returning to empty stillness to wait.
  • “The jade bell is struck once, the jade caves blow as a pair”: describing the scene of the mechanism of breath giving one jolt and the breath coming out again through both nostrils.
  • “When the time comes, the breath transforms; when the medicine is produced, the spirit knows”: a famous saying of the Wu–Liu school.
  • Four-word formula: inhale, lick, contract, close.
  • Turning the great Dharma wheel: a borrowed Buddhist expression, meaning running the Celestial Circuit.
  • Three hundred circuits: an approximate figure; see Qiu Ling’s note to the next section.
  • The Qian body not yet broken: returning to the pure and complete state of the virgin body before any leakage.
  • Grain path: the anus. Blocking the dui: closing the mouth (dui is the mouth).

Putting It into Practice

  1. The most valuable thing in this section is its view of timing: “neither too old nor too young.” Stirring a thought to grab it too early (young) and missing it too late (old) both fail. Applied to everyday quiet sitting, this means: when a pleasant sensation appears, do not rush to “grab it and strengthen it,” nor regret it only after it has scattered — keep it company quietly, and it will ripen of itself.
  2. “While the mechanism of breath is still young, one must never stir a thought to gather it, but should return to emptiness and wait for it.” This sentence is worth copying out. Many deviations come from “stirring a thought to gather.”
  3. None of the bodily scenes is a goal of practice. Qiu Ling says that “in the fine details each person may differ.” There is no need to look for them by the book; if they come, let them be; if not, do not be anxious.
  4. Of the four-word formula, “lick” (tongue against the upper palate) and “close” (lowering the curtains, withdrawing sight and hearing) are gentle and can be used every day; “inhale” should be natural, not forceful; “contract” should be light, not heavy, and not prolonged.

Translator's Notes

  1. “A ‘dong-dong’ sound”: describing the sound of vibration in the lower Cinnabar Field, like a drumbeat.
  2. “The breath of the Cinnabar Field goes from below toward the back, to the root of the kidney duct”: “the root of the kidney duct” means the area from the perineum to the Caudal Funnel, the starting point of the Governing Vessel.
  3. “The jade bell is struck once, the jade caves blow as a pair”: a common expression in the alchemical books. The jade bell is a metaphor for a jolt in the head or the Cinnabar Field; the jade caves are the two nostrils.
  4. “Grain path”: the anus. “Dui”: among the eight trigrams Dui stands for the mouth; “blocking the dui” means closing the mouth, from the Laozi, “block the openings, shut the doors.”
  5. The original text of this section is one passage; we have divided it into five short passages.
