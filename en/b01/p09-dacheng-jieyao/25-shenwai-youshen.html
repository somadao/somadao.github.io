III.3 · A Body beyond the Body

Records of Experience in Refining Spirit to Return to Emptiness, Section 3 · A Body beyond the Body — the Yang Spirit’s first going out, and gathering in the golden light

Listen to this chapterENEnglish

Note (translator): This section is an ancient record of the stage of “sending out the spirit.” Qiu Ling gives no notes; it is for understanding the whole picture of the alchemical method only. Please do not attempt to practice it.

About This Chapter

The Yang Spirit has moved to above the crown gate — the crown gate is like the Gate of Heaven, the brain palace is the Heavenly Palace. The author says: never be frightened; simply concentrate the spirit with single-minded resolve, visualize the Dharma-body, and with one thought think of going out beyond the Gate of Heaven; then, with the eyes closed, give a light jump downward, like waking from a great dream, and there is “a body beyond the body.”

When the Yang Spirit first goes out, it stays about three or four feet beside the physical body. At this time, toward everything outside the body, one must on no account give rise to the wish to look or listen; whether the realms are true or illusory, all should be disregarded; simply keep the mind dead and unmoving, and single-mindedly enter absorption and wait quietly. In an instant a ray of golden or white light, as big as a cartwheel, issues from one’s own body and appears before the eyes; then with the True Intention move the Dharma-body before the light, and visualize the golden light gradually contracting to the size of about an inch, like a gold coin; with the intention draw it in and take it into the Dharma-body; the Dharma-body then returns into the sea of nature within the body of flesh, is taken back to its own palace, and through cessation absorption goes deep into great absorption. The ancients said: “Golden light is the wondrous medicine for transforming form”; by no means miss it — if it is missed, the golden light scatters and does not come again.

Qiu Ling gives no notes on this section.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

神既迁到顶门之上，顶门如同天门，脑宫即是天宫。切勿惊怖，只管一志凝神，存想法身，一念思出天门之外。随闭目往下轻轻一跳，如梦初醒，而身外有身矣。

Once the spirit has moved to above the crown gate, the crown gate is like the Gate of Heaven, and the brain palace is the Heavenly Palace. Never be frightened; simply concentrate the spirit with single-minded resolve, visualize the Dharma-body, and with one thought think of going out beyond the Gate of Heaven. Then, with the eyes closed, give a light jump downward, as if waking from a dream, and there is a body beyond the body.

§2
Original (Chinese)

阳神初出之时，居于色身之旁三、四尺许。凡身外所有一切，万不可起视听之心，无论一切或真或幻境界，皆当置之度外，只死心不动，绝虑忘情，一味入定，静以待之。

When the Yang Spirit first goes out, it stays about three or four feet beside the physical body. Toward everything outside the body, one must on no account give rise to the wish to look or listen; whatever the realms, true or illusory, all should be disregarded; simply keep the mind dead and unmoving, cut off thought and forget feeling, single-mindedly enter absorption, and wait quietly.

§3
Original (Chinese)

顷刻之间，自己身中即透出一道金光或白光，大如车轮，现于眼前，即用真意将法身移到光前，凝聚留恋，存想金光渐渐收敛，金光即缩小如寸许，状似金钱，即将此光用意一吸，收入法身之中，而法身即入于凡躯性海之内，收回本宫，仍依灭尽定而寂灭之，深入大定。古云：‘金光为化形之妙药。‘千万不可错过。此时金光散去，再无有矣！

In an instant, a ray of golden or white light issues from one’s own body, as big as a cartwheel, and appears before the eyes. Then with the True Intention move the Dharma-body before the light, concentrate and linger on it, and visualize the golden light gradually contracting; the golden light then shrinks to about an inch, shaped like a gold coin; then with the intention draw this light in and take it into the Dharma-body; the Dharma-body then enters the sea of nature within the ordinary body, is taken back to its own palace, and again through cessation absorption is stilled and extinguished, going deep into great absorption. The ancients said: “Golden light is the wondrous medicine for transforming form.” By no means miss it. (If it is missed,) at this time the golden light scatters and is never there again!

Key Terms

  • Crown gate: the crown of the head; see Gate of Heaven / Door of Earth.
  • Brain palace, Heavenly Palace: the brain; see Muddy Pellet.
  • Dharma-body, a body beyond the body: the Yang Spirit going out beyond the body of flesh; see spontaneous Dharma Body and sending out the spirit.
  • Physical body, ordinary body: the body of flesh.
  • Cutting off thought and forgetting feeling: ceasing all thinking, forgetting emotion and consciousness.
  • Sea of nature: the original nature like a sea; here, the place in the body to which the Yang Spirit returns.
  • Golden light is the wondrous medicine for transforming form: golden light taken into the Dharma-body enables the Dharma-body to transform and take form.

Putting It into Practice

  1. This section is for understanding only.
  2. Even in such an advanced record, what the author urges again and again is still “never be frightened,” “one must on no account give rise to the wish to look or listen,” “keep the mind dead and unmoving, single-mindedly enter absorption.” The right attitude toward all strange scenes is the same from the first step to the highest stage: not alarmed, not chasing, not clinging.

Translator's Notes

  1. “At this time the golden light scatters and is never there again”: following on from “by no means miss it,” it means that if this moment is missed, the golden light scatters and does not appear again. The translation supplies “if it is missed.”
  2. The original text of this section is one passage; we have divided it into three short passages.
