I.2 · Reverting the Light to Shine Within

Records of Experience in Refining Essence into Breath, Section 2 · Reverting the Light to Shine Within — bringing the heart’s fire down into the Cinnabar Field

About This Chapter

The principle of inner refining, the author says, is “utterly simple and utterly easy”: it is only bringing the heart’s fire down into the Cinnabar Field. The heart belongs to fire (Li), the Cinnabar Field belongs to water (Kan); when fire enters water, water and fire unite, and true yang is produced. The author quotes in succession the words of five patriarchs — Zhongli Quan, Shi Xinglin, Liu Haichan, Xu Jingyang, and Wang Chongyang — all speaking of this one matter.

Wang Chongyang’s passage is the most concrete: first sink the spirit of the “upper opening” into the Cavity of Breath of the lower opening, mind and breath relying on each other, illuminating this mass of “black” day and night; in time, white arises within the black and fire breaks out in the water, a warm breath appears in the Cinnabar Field — this is the first stirring of true yang.

Qiu Ling’s note supplies a key order of steps: the “upper opening” is the Ancestral Opening between the two eyes; at the start one should first revert the light to shine on the upper opening, and only then concentrate the spirit in the Cavity of Breath — “first cultivate absorption in the palace of Li, then seek the mystery in the watery storehouse.” He also gives a very plain definition of “the opening of the Mysterious Pass”: in the midst of the turmoil of distracting thoughts, suddenly to step out of it in a flash of awareness — that is the opening of the Mysterious Pass, as in “turn your head, and there is the shore.”

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

内炼之道，至简至易，惟欲降心火入于丹田耳。盖丹田乃坎宫属水，心乃离宫属火，火入水中，则水火交，而真阳产矣。

The way of inner refining is utterly simple and utterly easy: it is only a matter of bringing the heart’s fire down into the Cinnabar Field. For the Cinnabar Field is the palace of Kan and belongs to water; the heart is the palace of Li and belongs to fire. When fire enters water, water and fire unite, and true yang is produced.

§2
Original (Chinese)

正阳真人曰：降心火，是南辰移入北辰位。石杏林云：收神皈气内，丹道自然成。刘海蟾曰：我悟长生理，太阳伏太阴。许旌阳云：与君说破我家风，太阳移在月明中。

The Perfected Zhengyang (Zhongli Quan) said: “Bringing down the heart’s fire is moving the southern star into the place of the northern star.” Shi Xinglin said: “Gather the spirit back within the breath, and the alchemical Way completes itself.” Liu Haichan said: “I have awakened to the principle of long life: the great yang lies hidden in the great yin.” Xu Jingyang said: “Let me reveal to you the tradition of my house: the sun moved into the bright moon.”

§3
Original (Chinese)

王重阳曰：初筑基之时，先将上窍之神，沉潜在下窍炁穴之中，心息相依，使真意常觉，一呼一吸往来造化炉中，久久纯熟，自然火从脐下发，虎向水中生，不归中而自归中。初守脐下一窍，回光返照原是一团黑球，如月之阴魄，纯阴之地。日夕被此离火凝照，巽风吹嘘，自然黑中生白，水里火发，温暖之气旋绕气穴，而为真阳初动，无中生有。又曰，纯阴之下，须用风火锻炼，方得阳炁发生。

Wang Chongyang said: When first laying the foundation, first sink the spirit of the upper opening into the Cavity of Breath of the lower opening, mind and breath relying on each other, letting the True Intention remain constantly aware, each exhalation and inhalation going back and forth within the furnace of creation; when this has long become pure and practiced, fire naturally issues from below the navel, the tiger is born in the water, and without being returned to the center it returns to the center by itself. When first guarding the opening below the navel, reverting the light to shine within, what one sees is originally a black ball, like the dark side of the moon, a place of pure yin. Day and night, concentrated on and illuminated by this fire of Li, gently blown by the Wind of Xun, naturally white arises within the black and fire breaks out in the water; a warm breath circles around the Cavity of Breath — this is the first stirring of true yang, something arising out of nothing. He also said: under pure yin, one must refine with wind and fire to obtain the arising of the yang breath.

§4
Original (Chinese)

此皆发明存养一点虚无元神，下藏于气穴之内，谓之送归土釜牢封固是也。先天元神虚灵性光，安于其中，如龟之藏，如蛇之蛰，勿忘勿助，若存若忘，久而呼吸相含，神气相抱，自然玄关窍开而真种产矣！

All of these explain preserving and nourishing that one point of empty Original Spirit, hiding it below within the Cavity of Breath — what is called “sending it back to the earthen pot and sealing it firmly.” The empty, numinous light of nature of the Original Spirit of Prior Heaven rests within, like a tortoise withdrawn, like a snake hibernating; neither forgetting nor helping, as if holding and as if forgetting; in time, the breaths contain each other, spirit and breath embrace each other, and naturally the opening of the Mysterious Pass opens and the true seed is produced!

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

上文王重阳所云“上窍”，当指两眼之间的祖窍，即上丹田。入手功夫当是首先回光返照上窍。上文各家之说似已隐喻此点。盖上窍代表离宫、心、神、火、日、龙等，而下窍指下丹田，古称气穴，则代表坎宫、肾、气、水、月、虎等。古语云，先从离宫修定，再向水府求玄。这才是以性摄情。用两眼神光汇合性光，凝神入气穴，于是坎离相接、日月交光、神气相恋、水火相济、心肾相交、阴阳和合，虚极静笃，恍恍惚惚，杳杳冥冥，从而静极生动，真种（真精真气）遂产。此时古称为“玄关窍开”，即一阳生、活子时。

“玄关窍开”在这里为打开了至玄至妙机关之意。语云，尘情杂虑纷纷扰扰时，从中一觉而出，即是玄关窍开，所谓回头是岸。又曰：彼岸非遥，回光返照即是。

The “upper opening” of which Wang Chongyang speaks above should mean the Ancestral Opening between the two eyes, that is, the upper Cinnabar Field. The work of beginning should be first to revert the light to shine on the upper opening. The sayings of the various masters above seem already to hint at this. For the upper opening stands for the palace of Li, the heart, spirit, fire, the sun, the dragon, and so on, while the lower opening means the lower Cinnabar Field, anciently called the Cavity of Breath, and stands for the palace of Kan, the kidneys, breath, water, the moon, the tiger, and so on. An old saying goes: “First cultivate absorption in the palace of Li, then seek the mystery in the watery storehouse.” Only this is governing the emotions by nature. Uniting the spirit-light of the two eyes with the light of nature, one concentrates the spirit in the Cavity of Breath; then Kan and Li join, sun and moon exchange their light, spirit and breath cleave to each other, water and fire assist each other, heart and kidneys unite, yin and yang harmonize; reaching utmost emptiness and steadfast stillness, vague and indistinct, dim and dark, movement then arises from the extreme of stillness, and the true seed (true essence and true breath) is produced. This moment the ancients called “the opening of the Mysterious Pass,” that is, the arising of the one yang, the Living Zi Hour.

“The opening of the Mysterious Pass” here means the opening of the most mysterious and wondrous mechanism. A saying goes: when worldly feelings and distracting thoughts are in turmoil, to step out of them in a flash of awareness — that is the opening of the Mysterious Pass, what is called “turn your head, and there is the shore.” It also says: the other shore is not far; reverting the light to shine within is it.

Key Terms

  • Reverting the Light to shine within: the overall theme of this section. The order in Qiu Ling’s note is: first illuminate the upper opening (the Ancestral Opening), then concentrate the spirit in the Cavity of Breath.
  • Heart’s fire, Cinnabar Field: the heart is the palace of Li and belongs to fire, the Cinnabar Field is the palace of Kan and belongs to water; fire entering water is the union of water and fire; see the conjunction of Kan and Li.
  • Moving the southern star into the place of the northern star; the great yang hidden in the great yin; the sun moved into the bright moon: all metaphors for “bringing the heart’s fire down into the Cinnabar Field.”
  • Upper opening, lower opening: the upper opening is the Ancestral Opening, the upper Cinnabar Field; the lower opening is the Cavity of Breath, the lower Cinnabar Field.
  • Mind and breath relying on each other: the mind and the breathing attached to each other; see regulating the breath.
  • Furnace of creation: another name for the lower Cinnabar Field.
  • White arising within the black, fire breaking out in the water: a place of pure yin, long illuminated by the spirit-light, gives rise to warm yang breath; see the empty chamber produces whiteness.
  • Earthen pot: another name for the central palace, the Yellow Court; “sending it back to the earthen pot and sealing it firmly” comes from Awakening to Reality.
  • Neither forgetting nor helping, as if holding and as if forgetting: the general principle of the fire phasing — neither letting go nor using force.
  • Opening of the Mysterious Pass: Qiu Ling’s note — stepping out of worldly feelings and distracting thoughts in a flash of awareness; see Mysterious Pass.
  • Arising of the one yang, Living Zi Hour: the moment when movement arises from the extreme of stillness and the true seed is produced.

Putting It into Practice

  1. First illuminate above, then below. Qiu Ling’s order is very practical: first gently draw the gaze back to the space between the two eyes (reverting the light to shine on the upper opening); when the mind is settled, slowly sink this attention down to the lower abdomen (concentrating the spirit in the Cavity of Breath). Fixing rigidly on the lower abdomen right from the start often leads either to too much effort or to drowsiness.
  2. The “black ball” is not something you are meant to imagine. When you close your eyes and look within, the lower abdomen is simply a dim darkness; just keep illuminating it continuously and gently, without hurry or agitation, and in time you will naturally feel warmth. When the warmth appears, that is the body’s response to you.
  3. “Neither forgetting nor helping, as if holding and as if forgetting” is the general rule of the whole fire phasing. Forgetting is scatteredness; helping is tension.
  4. The most practical line: when distracting thoughts are flying about, being able to notice “I am thinking wildly” — that very noticing is already turning your head. There is no need to do battle with thoughts; the awareness itself is reverting the light.

Translator's Notes

  1. The Perfected Zhengyang is Zhongli Quan; Shi Xinglin is Shi Tai, second patriarch of the Southern School; Liu Haichan is Liu Cao; Xu Jingyang is Xu Xun (Jin dynasty), later revered as the patriarch of the Way of Pure Brightness.
  2. “Gather the spirit back within the breath” (shou shen gui qi nei 收神皈气内): 皈 is the same as 归, “to return.”
  3. “The tiger is born in the water”: the alchemical books often say “the dragon comes out of the fire, the tiger is born in the water.” The tiger here means the true yang within Kan.
  4. “Sending it back to the earthen pot and sealing it firmly”: from Zhang Boduan’s Awakening to Reality, “send it back to the earthen pot and seal it firmly; next let the flowing pearl enter and be matched with it.”
  5. The original is one long passage; we have divided it by content into four short passages.
