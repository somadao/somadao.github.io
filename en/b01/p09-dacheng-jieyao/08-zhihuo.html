I.8 · The Signs for Stopping the Fire

Records of Experience in Refining Essence into Breath, Section 8 · The Signs for Stopping the Fire — stop the fire at the second appearance of the yang light, gather the Great Medicine at the third

About This Chapter

When the Lesser Celestial Circuit has been run until the fire phasing is sufficient, one must “stop the fire” — no longer pressing on with the fire of respiration. When should one stop? The author gives a very concrete standard: stop the fire when the tortoise retracts and does not rise and the yang light appears for the second time; gather the Great Medicine when the moon’s radiance is bright and clear and the yang light appears for the third time. Stopping too early (as soon as the sign first appears) errs by haste; stopping too late (waiting for the fourth appearance) errs by delay.

Qiu Ling’s note sums up the markers for stopping the fire in three points: completing the count of three hundred circuits, the tortoise retracting and not rising, and the second appearance of the yang light; and he explains that the “yang light” is a light appearing between the eyebrows, like a flash of lightning. He also explains what the first, second, third, and fourth appearances each mean — by the fourth, the Great Medicine will overflow outward with the fire, turn into the tangible essence of Posterior Heaven, and all the previous work is lost.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

不知止火之候，亦不知采大药之景，故曰万无一成。止火之候正在龟缩不举，阳光二现之时。采大药之诀正在月华明净、阳光三现之时。

Not knowing the signs for stopping the fire, nor the scenes of gathering the Great Medicine — this is why it is said there is not one chance in ten thousand of success. The time for stopping the fire is precisely when the tortoise retracts and does not rise and the yang light appears for the second time. The formula for gathering the Great Medicine applies precisely when the moon’s radiance is bright and clear and the yang light appears for the third time.

§2
Original (Chinese)

不可误于景初至而即止火，而失之速。亦不可误于景四至而后止火，而失之迟。不速不迟之机，二现止火，三现采大药，断无不得龙虎大丹者。

One must not err by stopping the fire as soon as the sign first arrives, and so fail through haste; nor err by stopping the fire only after the sign has arrived a fourth time, and so fail through delay. The moment that is neither hasty nor late is this: stop the fire at the second appearance, gather the Great Medicine at the third — doing so, one will surely not fail to obtain the great elixir of dragon and tiger.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§2
Original note (Chinese)

古云行小周天止火之候：一为行满三百周天之限数；二为龟缩不举；三为阳光二现。阳光发现在两眉间，恍如掣电。阳光一现之时，多在炼精过程中，当时火候未全，淫根未缩。阳光二现多在行满三百周天限数之后的静定中，当时龟缩不举之景，次第呈现，正是止火之候。迄至静定中阳光三现，乃真阳团聚，大药纯乾，是采大药之景。若待至阳光四现，则大药随火之不定而外溢，化为后天有形之精，遂致倾危。

The ancients said there are three signs for stopping the fire in running the Lesser Celestial Circuit: first, completing the prescribed count of three hundred circuits; second, the tortoise retracting and not rising; third, the second appearance of the yang light. The yang light appears between the two eyebrows, flickering like a flash of lightning. The first appearance of the yang light mostly occurs during the process of refining the essence, when the fire phasing is not yet complete and the root of desire has not yet retracted. The second appearance mostly occurs in settled stillness after the prescribed count of three hundred circuits has been completed, when the signs of the tortoise retracting and not rising appear one after another — precisely the time for stopping the fire. When, in settled stillness, the yang light appears for the third time, true yang has gathered into a mass and the Great Medicine is pure Qian — this is the sign for gathering the Great Medicine. If one waits until the yang light appears a fourth time, the Great Medicine overflows outward with the unsteadiness of the fire and turns into the tangible essence of Posterior Heaven, and so one falls into danger.

Key Terms

  • Stopping the fire: once the fire phasing is sufficient, no longer pressing on with the Celestial Circuit by the fire of respiration.
  • The tortoise retracting and not rising: that is, the horse-like retraction.
  • The yang light appearing three times: Qiu Ling’s note — the yang light appears between the eyebrows like a flash of lightning; at the first appearance the fire phasing is not yet complete, at the second one stops the fire, at the third one gathers the Great Medicine, at the fourth the Great Medicine overflows. Compare Celestial Eye.
  • The moon’s radiance bright and clear: describing the clarity and brightness between the eyebrows.
  • Great elixir of dragon and tiger: that is, the Great Medicine, the golden elixir; see Lead and Mercury.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The spirit of stopping the fire is “stop when you have arrived.” For today’s practitioner, the most useful thing in this section is not how many times the yang light appears, but this attitude: when the work has produced results, do not greedily add more, but turn in good time to warm nourishing. Craving achievement and clinging to scenes only lose them.
  2. Qiu Ling uses the word “mostly.” How many times the yang light appears and when are all approximate descriptions; do not grade yourself by them.

Translator's Notes

  1. “The root of desire has not yet retracted”: meaning the male genitals have not yet shown the retraction of “the horse-like retraction.”
  2. “The Great Medicine is pure Qian”: true yang pure, without admixture of yin; see Qian and Kun.
  3. This section agrees completely with the Collection of Daoxiang‘s statement, “the yang light appears three times; one should stop from the second appearance, and after the third it is fitting to gather the Great Medicine”; see Part 8, Section 7.
