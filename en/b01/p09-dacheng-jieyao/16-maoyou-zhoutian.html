II.4 · The Celestial Circuit of Mao and You

Records of Experience in Refining Breath into Spirit, Section 4 · The Celestial Circuit of Mao and You — rising and descending left and right, gathering in the inner medicine to make the embryo round and firm

Listen to this chapterENEnglish

About This Chapter

The preceding sections have repeatedly mentioned “running the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You to gather it in”; this section is devoted to it. Once the golden liquor and jade dew have fallen from the upper field into the Yellow Court, one must quickly use the True Intention and the light of the eyes, from the Kun navel (the lower Cinnabar Field) to the Qian summit (the crown), rising on the left and descending on the right thirty-six times, rising on the right and descending on the left twenty-four times. This is called “gathering in the inner medicine to make the embryo round and firm” — holding the Returned Elixir in place so that the embryo becomes complete and firm.

Qiu Ling quotes the Clarifications on Nature and Life to explain how it differs from the earlier “union of Qian and Kun”:

  • The union of Qian and Kun (the Zi–Wu circuit) gathers the outer medicine: up behind and down in front, one rising and one descending.
  • The Celestial Circuit of Mao and You gathers the inner medicine: turning left and revolving right, one rising and one sinking.

The two circulate in turn like the turning of the armillary (the Northern Dipper). Knowing only the union of Qian and Kun and not the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You is like having a cart without wheels, a boat without a rudder.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

一点金液玉露自上田落于黄庭，急行卯酉周天以收之，须用真意眸光，从坤脐至乾顶，左升右降，四九三十六而定，右升左降，四六二十四而定。此卯酉周天之度数。日月之升降，收尽真阳，战退群阴，名曰收内药以固胎圆。

When a point of golden liquor and jade dew falls from the upper field into the Yellow Court, quickly run the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You to gather it in. One must use the True Intention and the light of the eyes, from the Kun navel to the Qian summit, rising on the left and descending on the right, four nines, thirty-six times, and then settle; rising on the right and descending on the left, four sixes, twenty-four times, and then settle. These are the measures of the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You. Rising and descending like sun and moon, gathering in all the true yang and driving back the host of yin — this is called gathering in the inner medicine to make the embryo round and firm.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1
Original note (Chinese)

行卯酉周天主要收内药不致耗散也。《性命圭旨》曰：“前段乾坤交媾收外药也，此段卯酉周天收内药也。外交媾者后上前下，一升一降也。内交媾者左旋右转，一起一伏也。两者循环状似璇玑。故魏伯阳云：‘循据璇玑，升降上下，周流六爻，难以察睹。‘世人只知有乾坤交媾，而不知卯酉周天，是犹有车而无轮，有舟而无舵，欲望远载其可得乎？”

Running the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You is mainly to gather in the inner medicine so that it is not dissipated. The Clarifications on Nature and Life says: “The earlier union of Qian and Kun gathers the outer medicine; this Celestial Circuit of Mao and You gathers the inner medicine. The outer union goes up behind and down in front, one rising and one descending. The inner union turns left and revolves right, one rising and one sinking. The two circulate in turn, in the manner of the armillary. So Wei Boyang said: ‘Following the armillary, rising and descending above and below, flowing around the six lines, it is hard to observe.' People only know that there is the union of Qian and Kun, and do not know the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You; this is like having a cart without wheels, a boat without a rudder — how could one hope to carry a load far?”

Key Terms

  • Celestial Circuit of Mao and You: a circuit turning left and right, used to gather in the inner medicine; contrasted with the Zi–Wu circuit rising and descending front and back; see Celestial Circuit.
  • Kun navel, Qian summit: the lower Cinnabar Field and the crown.
  • Four nines, thirty-six; four sixes, twenty-four: the yang number and the yin number; Qiu Ling’s note to Group I, Section 6, has already made clear that these are figurative.
  • Gathering in the inner medicine to make the embryo round and firm: congealing the Returned Elixir so that the Holy Embryo becomes complete.
  • Union of Qian and Kun: the Zi–Wu circuit, gathering the outer medicine.
  • Armillary (xuanji 璇玑): the first four stars of the Northern Dipper, a metaphor for the turning of the circuit.
  • Six lines: the six lines of a hexagram, a metaphor for the positions up and down the circuit.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The method of this section belongs to an advanced stage; ordinary practitioners need not “turn” anything. As Qiu Ling already said in the first group: thirty-six and twenty-four are figurative, and one should “never be bound by these figurative comparisons.”
  2. What can be borrowed is its intent: when something has been gained (an experience, a sense of stability), “gather it in” — do not let it scatter. Before ending a session of quiet sitting, letting your attention rest lightly in the lower abdomen for a moment is also a kind of “gathering in.”

Translator's Notes

  1. “Following the armillary, rising and descending above and below, flowing around the six lines, it is hard to observe”: from Wei Boyang’s Cantong qi.
  2. The specific method of the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You has already been explained in Qiu Ling’s note to Group I, Section 10, quoting the Clarifications on Nature and Life: with the True Intention and the light of the eyes, shine down on the Kun navel, shine up on the left to the Qian summit, and descend on the right to the Kun navel — that is one round.
