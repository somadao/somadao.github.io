II.6 · The Pearl Falls into the Yellow Court

Records of Experience in Refining Breath into Spirit, Section 6 · The Pearl Falls into the Yellow Court — the mysterious pearl the size of a millet grain gradually grows

About This Chapter

After the Returned Elixir of golden liquor and jade dew, “a mysterious pearl the size of a millet grain” naturally appears. Preserved and nourished for a long time, it gradually grows, like a red tangerine. One must use the inner and outer true fires to urge it down into the Yellow Court (the earthen pot) — from then on the stirrings settle and the illusory transformations subside.

The author says that the mysterious pearl congeals in the upper field: the dragon sings and the tiger roars, yin cleaves to the yang hun, yang embraces the yin po, the essence of lead and the marrow of mercury congeal like a red tangerine; outwardly borrowing the divine fire of the great sun and inwardly gathering the Samādhi True Fire, attacking from within and without, a point of golden liquor falls back into its old place in the Yellow Court. Then one runs the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You to gather it in and concentrates the spirit in absorption — this is the beginning of “the embryo forming in embryonic breathing.” Thereafter the mysterious pearl gradually grows larger, ever brighter and more radiant — it “seems to be outside, yet with eyes closed it is distinct; seems to be inside, yet with eyes open it is utterly clear and white,” neither inside nor outside. The author’s conclusion: the pearl falling into the Yellow Court is the true verification of illuminating the mind and seeing one’s nature.

Qiu Ling’s note says: the mysterious pearl the size of a millet grain is the golden elixir, nothing but the true breath of Prior Heaven congealed into a mass, which after long refining takes on an image before the eyes — “in truth it is the outer image of the elixir.” He also quotes two passages from the Clarifications on Nature and Life describing the process by which the mysterious pearl takes on its image and the practitioner’s feelings at that time.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

金液玉露还丹以后，自然出现一粒黍米玄珠。存养久之，渐渐长大，宛如丹橘。必须借内外真火，催落黄庭，降下土釜之中。动机由此而定，幻化由此而安。

After the Returned Elixir of golden liquor and jade dew, there naturally appears a mysterious pearl the size of a millet grain. Preserved and nourished for a long time, it gradually grows, just like a red tangerine. One must use the inner and outer true fires to urge it down into the Yellow Court, descending into the earthen pot. From this the stirrings are settled, and from this the illusory transformations are at peace.

§2
Original (Chinese)

宝珠者何也？龙吟虎啸，阴恋阳魂，阳抱阴魄，合一于上田，铅精汞髓凝结如丹橘。此时乾鼎之中，玄珠成象，矿去金存，外借太阳神火，内聚三昧真火，内外夹攻，而一点金液复落于黄庭旧处。见此实效，当行卯酉周天以收之。收讫，即凝神入定，而为胎息凝胞之始也。

What is the precious pearl? The dragon sings and the tiger roars, yin cleaves to the yang hun, yang embraces the yin po, uniting as one in the upper field; the essence of lead and the marrow of mercury congeal like a red tangerine. At this time, within the Qian cauldron, the mysterious pearl takes on its image — the ore removed, the gold remaining; outwardly borrowing the divine fire of the great sun and inwardly gathering the Samādhi True Fire, attacking from within and without, a point of golden liquor falls back into its old place in the Yellow Court. On seeing this actual result, one should run the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You to gather it in. When it has been gathered, at once concentrate the spirit and enter absorption — this is the beginning of the embryo forming in embryonic breathing.

§3
Original (Chinese)

厥后此珠渐生渐大，其色渐明渐赫。惟定之一机，机由我立，化则机生，一机万化。玄珠，变象之祖也，似乎在外，闭目却分明，似乎在内，开目极清白，真有不内不外者存。珠落黄庭，乃是明心见性之真功实验也。

Thereafter this pearl gradually grows larger, its color gradually brighter and more radiant. There is only the one pivot of absorption; the pivot is established by me; when transformation occurs, the pivot is born; one pivot, ten thousand transformations. The mysterious pearl is the ancestor of all changing images: it seems to be outside, yet with eyes closed it is distinct; it seems to be inside, yet with eyes open it is utterly clear and white — there truly exists something that is neither inside nor outside. The pearl falling into the Yellow Court is the true work and true verification of illuminating the mind and seeing one’s nature.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§3
Original note (Chinese)

此段描绘结丹景象。黍米玄珠即金丹，不过是凝结成一团之先天真炁，经过久久锻炼，遂成象于眼前，渐生渐大，渐明渐赫，实丹之外象也。

《性命圭旨》有两段文字描写玄珠成象的经过和练功者当时的感受，特引录如下：“当其真铅入鼎之时，须要驱除杂念，奋速精神，目视顶门，用志不分。霎时龙虎交战，造化争驰，雷轰电掣，撼动乾坤，百脉悚然，九宫透彻，金晶灌顶，银浪冲天，紫阳所谓’以黑而变红，一鼎云气浓’是也。少顷玉鼎汤温，金炉火散，黄芽遍地，白雪漫天，夫唱妇随，龙吟虎啸，阴恋阳魂，阳抱阴魄，铅精汞髓凝结如珠。”又云：“此际玄珠成象，矿去金存，而一点金液复落于黄庭旧处矣！斯时也，溶溶然如山云之腾太虚，霏霏然似膏雨之遍原野，滔滔然若春雨之满泽，液液然象河水之将释，百脉冲和而畅于四体，真个是拍拍满怀都是春也。”

This passage depicts the scene of forming the elixir. The mysterious pearl the size of a millet grain is the golden elixir, nothing but the true breath of Prior Heaven congealed into a mass, which after long refining takes on an image before the eyes, gradually growing larger, gradually brighter and more radiant — in truth it is the outer image of the elixir.

The Clarifications on Nature and Life has two passages describing the process by which the mysterious pearl takes on its image and the practitioner’s feelings at that time, which are quoted here: “When the true lead enters the cauldron, one must drive away distracting thoughts, quickly rouse the spirit, look at the crown gate, and keep one’s resolve undivided. In an instant dragon and tiger do battle, creation races, thunder roars and lightning flashes, shaking Qian and Kun; the hundred vessels start, the nine palaces are penetrated through, golden essence pours over the crown, silver waves surge to the sky — this is what Ziyang meant by ‘from black it changes to red; the cauldron is thick with cloud and vapor.' After a while the liquid in the jade cauldron is warm, the fire in the golden furnace disperses, yellow sprouts cover the ground, white snow fills the sky, the husband sings and the wife follows, the dragon sings and the tiger roars, yin cleaves to the yang hun, yang embraces the yin po, and the essence of lead and the marrow of mercury congeal like a pearl.” It also says: “At this moment the mysterious pearl takes on its image, the ore removed and the gold remaining, and a point of golden liquor falls back into its old place in the Yellow Court! At this time it is flowing like mountain clouds rising into the Great Void, falling softly like nourishing rain spreading over the plains, brimming like spring waters filling the marshes, loosening like river ice about to thaw; the hundred vessels are in harmony and it spreads through the four limbs — truly, all one embraces is spring.”

Key Terms

  • Mysterious pearl the size of a millet grain: Qiu Ling’s note — that is, the golden elixir; “in truth it is the outer image of the elixir”; see Golden Flower.
  • Red tangerine: describing the mysterious pearl after it has grown.
  • Inner and outer true fires: outwardly borrowing the divine fire of the great sun, inwardly gathering the Samādhi True Fire; see Samādhi True Fire.
  • Earthen pot: the Yellow Court, the central palace.
  • Yin cleaving to the yang hun, yang embracing the yin po: hun and po uniting; see hun and po.
  • Essence of lead, marrow of mercury: the quintessence of lead and mercury; see Lead and Mercury.
  • Qian cauldron: the Muddy Pellet in the head.
  • The ore removed, the gold remaining: impurities entirely removed and only pure gold left, a metaphor for yin dross dissolved and true yang alone remaining.
  • The embryo forming in embryonic breathing: beginning to form the embryo in embryonic breathing.
  • One pivot, ten thousand transformations: the single pivot of “absorption” gives rise to all kinds of transformations.
  • Illuminating the mind and seeing one’s nature: a Chan expression; here, the pearl falling into the Yellow Court is the real proof of seeing one’s nature.
  • Yellow sprouts, white snow: symbols in the alchemical books of the medicine ripening.

Putting It into Practice

  1. “There is only the one pivot of absorption.” The root of all scenes is “absorption.” However marvelous the descriptions you read, returning to practice there is only one thing: let the mind settle.
  2. “It seems to be outside, yet with eyes closed it is distinct; it seems to be inside, yet with eyes open it is utterly clear and white” — this description of “neither inside nor outside” points to a clear awareness that does not depend on the senses. For ordinary practitioners it can be understood as maintaining the same stability and wakefulness whether the eyes are open or closed.
  3. Qiu Ling calls it “the outer image of the elixir” — an image, not the elixir itself. Do not cling to seeing anything.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Brimming like spring waters filling the marshes, loosening like river ice about to thaw”: the Clarifications on Nature and Life originally reads 淫淫然若春水之满泽，液液然象河冰之将释. Qiu Ling’s quotation differs slightly from the original book (滔滔然若春雨之满泽，液液然象河水之将释); the translation follows the original sense of the Clarifications (“river ice about to thaw”), while the original text of the note keeps the reading of the base text.
  2. “From black it changes to red; the cauldron is thick with cloud and vapor”: a line from Zhang Boduan’s (Ziyang’s) Awakening to Reality.
  3. “Quickly rouse the spirit” (fensu jingshen 奋速精神): rouse the spirit swiftly.
  4. The original text of this section is one passage; we have divided it into three short passages.
