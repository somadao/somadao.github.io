II.3 · The Returned Elixir of Golden Liquor and Jade Liquor

Records of Experience in Refining Breath into Spirit, Section 3 · The Returned Elixir of Golden Liquor and Jade Liquor — the lesser and greater conjunctions of Kan and Li

Listen to this chapterENEnglish

About This Chapter

Nurturing the embryo rests mainly on entering absorption. When absorption has lasted long, two “returns of the elixir” appear:

  • The Returned Elixir of golden liquor (the lesser conjunction of Kan and Li): a point of hot breath within Kan (the lower Cinnabar Field) rushes up to the position of the heart; guide it with the True Intention through the Caudal Funnel, the Spine-Straddling Pass, and the Jade Pillow into the Muddy Pellet, where it feels as if something touched the brain; then from the upper palate it descends drop by drop into the mouth, shaped like sparrow’s eggs, and turns into “golden liquor,” fragrant as ghee and sweet as dew; swallow it slowly down the Storied Tower and send it with the gaze into the furnace and cauldron. If for nine days in a row it cannot all be swallowed, run the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You to gather it in, then concentrate the spirit and enter absorption again.
  • The Returned Elixir of jade liquor (the greater conjunction of Kan and Li): one again gradually enters great absorption; sun and moon stop turning; at the extreme of dim darkness and chaos, there is suddenly a sound like thunder in the palace of Kun (the belly), like silk being torn; the true breath rushes upward and flies up to the Muddy Pellet, a round light wells up between the eyebrows and turns into “jade liquor” descending into the mouth, fragrant as borneol and cool as mint; swallow it as you become aware of it and send it into the central palace. After seven days of this, run the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You to gather it in, and again guard the center and embrace the One.

The author stresses: the scenes of the Returned Elixir of golden liquor and jade dew all arise from the extreme of stillness.

Qiu Ling’s note says that these two passages are largely the same with minor differences; this is what is popularly called “ghee poured through the crown” or “the Great Returned Elixir,” an important step in the numinous elixir’s “moving the embryo and changing the cauldron”; and he quotes a passage from the Clarifications on Nature and Life to show that this is the “transforming of breath” effected by the Returned Elixir of golden liquor.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

养胎以入定为主，若静定日久，自然觉得坎中一点热气上冲心位，当用真意引过尾闾，由夹脊透玉枕入泥丸，如有物触脑，自上腭颗颗下降口中，状如雀卵，化为金液，香似醍醐，味似甘露，当速以意迎之，徐徐咽下重楼，以目送之于炉鼎之内，畅于四肢，美在其中矣！一连九日，咽纳不尽，即行卯酉周天以收之。收取净尽，速凝神入定，静养胎圆。此是小坎离交媾之妙也。

Nurturing the embryo rests mainly on entering absorption. If settled stillness has lasted many days, one naturally feels a point of hot breath within Kan rushing up to the position of the heart. One should then guide it with the True Intention past the Caudal Funnel, through the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow into the Muddy Pellet, where it feels as if something touched the brain; from the upper palate it descends drop by drop into the mouth, shaped like sparrow’s eggs, and turns into golden liquor, fragrant as ghee and tasting like sweet dew. One should quickly welcome it with the intention, swallow it slowly down the Storied Tower, and send it with the gaze into the furnace and cauldron, where it spreads through the four limbs, and all beauty lies within! For nine days in a row it cannot all be swallowed; then run the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You to gather it in. When it has been entirely gathered, quickly concentrate the spirit and enter absorption, nourishing in stillness to make the embryo complete. This is the wonder of the lesser conjunction of Kan and Li.

§2
Original (Chinese)

自此以后，渐渐又入大定，有时日月停轮，璇玑不行，每到杳冥混沌之极，天机自动，坤宫忽然有雷震之声，腹中如裂帛之状，真气上冲，周流六虚，飞上泥丸，月窟生风，眉涌圆光，化为玉液，降在口中，如冰片之香，似薄荷之凉，随觉随咽，沥沥降下重楼，当用真意送入中宫，如此七日咽纳不尽，即运卯酉周天以收之。

From then on, one gradually again enters great absorption; at times sun and moon stop turning and the armillary no longer moves. Each time the extreme of dim darkness and chaos is reached, the mechanism of Heaven stirs of itself; in the palace of Kun there is suddenly a sound like thunder, and the belly feels as if silk were being torn; the true breath rushes upward, flows around the six voids, and flies up to the Muddy Pellet; wind arises in the Moon’s Cave, a round light wells up between the eyebrows and turns into jade liquor, which descends into the mouth, fragrant as borneol and cool as mint; swallow it as you become aware of it, and as it trickles down the Storied Tower, send it with the True Intention into the central palace. If for seven days it cannot all be swallowed, run the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You to gather it in.

§3
Original (Chinese)

待玉液降完，即凝神入定，守中抱一。金液玉露还丹之景，皆从静极而生，千万不可当面错过。此大坎离交媾之妙也。

When the jade liquor has all descended, concentrate the spirit and enter absorption, guarding the center and embracing the One. The scenes of the Returned Elixir of golden liquor and jade dew all arise from the extreme of stillness; by no means miss them face to face. This is the wonder of the greater conjunction of Kan and Li.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§3
Original note (Chinese)

此二则大同小异，即俗称所谓醍醐贯顶，大还丹是也，为灵丹脱落移胎换鼎的一个重要步骤。《性命圭旨》云：“然而珠在昆仑何由得下而结圣胎？必假神庐窃灵阳真气以催之，太阳真火以逼之，催逼既久，则灵丹应时脱落，吞入口中，化为金液而直射丹扃之内。霎时云腾雨施，电掣雷轰，鏖战片饷之间，销尽一身阴滓，则百灵如车之辏毂，七宝若水之朝宗，皆聚于此矣！”这是指金液玉液还丹所起的“气化”作用。丹扃指中宫即中丹田。

These two passages are largely the same with minor differences; this is what is popularly called “ghee poured through the crown,” or “the Great Returned Elixir,” an important step in which the numinous elixir drops down, moving the embryo and changing the cauldron. The Clarifications on Nature and Life says: “Yet the pearl is on Kunlun (the crown) — how can it come down to form the Holy Embryo? One must borrow the numinous yang true breath stolen from the hut of the spirit to urge it on, and the true fire of the great sun to press it; when it has been urged and pressed for a long time, the numinous elixir drops down at the right moment, is swallowed into the mouth, turns into golden liquor, and shoots straight into the elixir gate. In an instant clouds rise and rain falls, lightning flashes and thunder roars; in a moment’s fierce battle all the yin dross of the whole body is dissolved, and then the hundred numinous powers gather here like spokes converging on the hub, and the seven treasures like rivers flowing to the sea!” This refers to the “transforming of breath” effected by the Returned Elixir of golden liquor and jade liquor. The elixir gate means the central palace, that is, the middle Cinnabar Field.

Key Terms

  • Golden liquor, jade liquor: sweet, delicious saliva descending from the upper palate into the mouth; see Sweet Dew.
  • Lesser conjunction of Kan and Li, greater conjunction of Kan and Li: the Returned Elixir of golden liquor and of jade liquor; see the conjunction of Kan and Li.
  • Within Kan, palace of Kun: the lower Cinnabar Field, the belly.
  • Sparrow’s eggs: describing drops of liquid each distinct.
  • Ghee: the essence refined from milk, a metaphor for the most exquisite taste.
  • Sun and moon stopping their turning, the armillary no longer moving: in deep absorption both breathing and the turning of the circuit stop.
  • Six voids: above, below, and the four directions.
  • Moon’s Cave: by one explanation the head (as opposed to the “Root of Heaven”); used here together with “a round light welling up between the eyebrows,” it means the area of the head and face.
  • Ghee poured through the crown, Great Returned Elixir: Qiu Ling’s terms.
  • Moving the embryo and changing the cauldron: the elixir moving from the upper field to the central palace to form the embryo.
  • Elixir gate (danjiong 丹扃): the central palace, the middle Cinnabar Field.
  • Spokes converging on the hub (cougu 辏毂): the spokes of a wheel gathering toward the center of the axle.

Putting It into Practice

  1. “They all arise from the extreme of stillness.” However marvelous the scene, it is a by-product of stillness. Pursuing scenes is leaving stillness.
  2. More saliva in the mouth during quiet sitting, cool and sweet, is a common, normal phenomenon after relaxing and entering stillness. The ancients extolled it as “sweet dew” and “jade liquor”; we need only swallow it naturally, without reading more into it.
  3. After both returns of the elixir, the instruction is “quickly concentrate the spirit and enter absorption.” Having had an experience, put it down at once and return to guarding the center — this order is itself a key formula of cultivation.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Golden liquor and jade dew” and “golden liquor and jade liquor”: both forms occur in the original and refer to the same thing.
  2. “The numinous yang true breath stolen from the hut of the spirit”: so in the original Clarifications on Nature and Life; the hut of the spirit generally means the nose (by another explanation the brain), and the whole sentence means using stolen yang breath to urge the mysterious pearl to descend.
  3. “A moment’s fierce battle” (aozhan pianxiang 鏖战片饷): pianxiang is the same as pianshang, “a moment.”
  4. The original text of this section has two passages; we have divided them into three short passages.
