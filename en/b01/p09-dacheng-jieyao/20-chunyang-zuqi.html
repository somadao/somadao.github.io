II.8 · The Ancestral Breath of Pure Yang Helps Complete the Embryo

Records of Experience in Refining Breath into Spirit, Section 8 · The Ancestral Breath of Pure Yang Helps Complete the Embryo — refining the body in true emptiness

Listen to this chapterENEnglish

About This Chapter

After the union of sun and moon, the author records two further scenes in which “the original breath of Heaven and Earth” helps nurture the embryo:

  • Entering from below: at the extreme of stillness, one suddenly feels two streams of something of pure yang issuing from the Bubbling Spring points on the soles of the feet, passing through the knees and shins, surging and vigorous, rising up and gathering in the lower Cinnabar Field. Attack it with the Samādhi True Fire, and it goes along the Governing Vessel through the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow straight up to the Muddy Pellet, then through the Storied Tower down to the central palace, mingling into the immortal embryo and becoming one with it — the whole body becomes harmonious, and cold and heat cannot invade it.
  • Entering from above: every morning before the sun has risen, when the sky is faintly light, concentrate the spirit and sit quietly, waiting in emptiness: within, give up thoughts; without, cut off worldly ties; suddenly forget heaven and earth and shatter the bodily frame. Naturally, from the Great Void, a point of yang light like dew, like lightning, bursts into the Mysterious Pass, passes through the Muddy Pellet, and turns into sweet rain descending into the mouth; answer it with the breathing (the Wind of Xun), letting it drive out the evils of the Three Passes and Nine Openings and sweep away the filth of the five viscera and six bowels, until finally one becomes a body of pure yang.

Qiu Ling calls this “the art of refining the body in true emptiness”: its purpose is to steal the boundless original breath of Heaven and Earth to sustain my limited bodily frame, so that Heaven and the human share one breath, connected and flowing. The key is to “put the body outside and be empty,” which is what the Scripture of Purity and Stillness means by “look inward at the mind, and the mind has no mind; look outward at the form, and the form has no form” — body and mind both empty, finally reaching the unity of Heaven and the human.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

日月合璧以后，静极之际，忽觉有两道纯阳之物，从涌泉穴透出，穿膝过胫，蓬蓬勃勃，滚滚上腾，至于下田，会聚一处。我发三昧真火以攻之，由背后督脉穿夹脊，过玉枕直贯泥丸，过重楼降下中宫，混入仙胎之中，相亲相恋，合为一体，自然真炁薰薰，周身融和，寒暑不侵。

After the union of sun and moon, at the extreme of stillness, one suddenly feels two streams of something of pure yang issuing from the Bubbling Spring points, passing through the knees and the shins, surging and vigorous, rolling and rising, until they reach the lower field and gather in one place. I put forth the Samādhi True Fire to attack it; from the Governing Vessel at the back it passes through the Spine-Straddling Pass, past the Jade Pillow straight through to the Muddy Pellet, then through the Storied Tower down to the central palace, where it mingles into the immortal embryo, the two cleaving and loving each other and uniting as one body; naturally the true breath steams warmly, the whole body becomes harmonious, and neither cold nor heat can invade it.

§2
Original (Chinese)

每于晨曦未升，旸谷晞微之时，凝神静坐，虚而待之，内舍意念，外绝尘缘，顿忘天地，粉碎形骸，自然太虚空中，有一点如露如电之阳光，勃然入我玄关，透泥丸，化为甘霖下降口中。

Each time, before the morning sun has risen, while the Valley of Sunrise is faintly light, concentrate the spirit and sit quietly, waiting for it in emptiness: within, give up thoughts; without, cut off worldly ties; suddenly forget heaven and earth and shatter the bodily frame. Naturally, from within the Great Void, a point of yang light like dew, like lightning, bursts into my Mysterious Pass, passes through the Muddy Pellet, and turns into sweet rain descending into the mouth.

§3
Original (Chinese)

我即鼓动巽风以应之，使其驱逐三关九窍之邪，扫荡五脏六腑之垢，焚身炼质，煅滓消霾，抽尽秽浊，变为纯阳之躯矣！

I then stir up the Wind of Xun to answer it, letting it drive out the evils of the Three Passes and Nine Openings, sweep away the filth of the five viscera and six bowels, burn the body and refine its substance, forge away the dregs and dispel the haze, draw out all impurity — and one becomes a body of pure yang!

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§3
Original note (Chinese)

此乃真空炼形之术也。意在窃取天地无涯之元炁，续我有限之形躯，使天人一气，联属流通。练此功时要做到“外其身而虚空”，即《清静经》所云：“内观其心，心无其心；外观其形，形无其形。”这样身心皆空。心空无碍，则神愈炼而愈灵，身空无碍，则形愈炼而愈清，直炼到形神相涵，身心合一，而进入天人合一之境。

此功前半段乃天地元炁从下入体内；下半段为从上入体内，以育我身中圣胎。

This is the art of refining the body in true emptiness. Its purpose is to steal the boundless original breath of Heaven and Earth to sustain my limited bodily frame, so that Heaven and the human share one breath, connected and flowing. In practicing this method one must “put the body outside and be empty,” which is what the Scripture of Purity and Stillness says: “Look inward at the mind, and the mind has no mind; look outward at the form, and the form has no form.” In this way body and mind are both empty. When the mind is empty and unobstructed, the more the spirit is refined, the more numinous it becomes; when the body is empty and unobstructed, the more the form is refined, the clearer it becomes, until form and spirit contain each other, body and mind are united as one, and one enters the realm of the unity of Heaven and the human.

In the first half of this method the original breath of Heaven and Earth enters the body from below; in the second half it enters from above, to nurture the Holy Embryo in my body.

Key Terms

  • Ancestral breath of pure yang: the original breath of Prior Heaven between Heaven and Earth; see Breath.
  • Bubbling Spring point: an acupoint on the sole of the foot.
  • Before the morning sun has risen, the Valley of Sunrise faintly light: before sunrise, when the sky is just growing light. The Valley of Sunrise (Yanggu 旸谷) is where, in ancient legend, the sun rises.
  • Waiting for it in emptiness: from the Zhuangzi, “The Human World”: “Breath is empty and waits for things.”
  • Sweet rain: that is, Sweet Dew.
  • Burning the body and refining its substance, forging away the dregs and dispelling the haze: using true fire to refine away the yin turbidity in the body.
  • Refining the body in true emptiness: Qiu Ling’s term — refining the bodily form through the emptiness of body and mind.
  • Putting the body outside: from the Laozi, “puts his body outside, and his body is preserved.”
  • Unity of Heaven and the human: the highest realm, in which form and spirit contain each other and body and mind are one.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Quiet sitting in the early morning, when it is not yet fully light, is a time esteemed by practitioners ancient and modern. At this time the outside world is quiet, and one has just woken from sleep; the mind empties most easily.
  2. “Within, give up thoughts; without, cut off worldly ties; suddenly forget heaven and earth and shatter the bodily frame” — the key is “forgetting” and “emptiness,” not “receiving” anything. Although this section describes yang light entering the body, Qiu Ling’s note places the emphasis on “body and mind both empty”: when the mind is empty the spirit is numinous; when the body is empty the form is clear.
  3. These two lines from the Scripture of Purity and Stillness can serve as a daily contemplation: look at the mind, and you cannot find a fixed “mind”; look at the body, and you cannot feel its boundaries — relaxing to this degree is “emptiness.”

Translator's Notes

  1. “The Valley of Sunrise faintly light” (Yanggu ximei 旸谷晞微): 晞 originally means “to dry” or “daybreak”; 晞微 means the faint light of dawn.
  2. “Forge away the dregs and dispel the haze” (duan zi xiao mai 煅滓消霾): 煅 is the same as 锻, to forge with fire.
  3. The original text of this section is one passage; we have divided it into three short passages.
