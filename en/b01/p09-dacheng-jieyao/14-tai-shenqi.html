II.2 · The Embryo Is Spirit and Breath

Records of Experience in Refining Breath into Spirit, Section 2 · The Embryo Is Spirit and Breath — the Original Spirit is the bones, the Great Medicine the flesh

About This Chapter

What, after all, is the “Holy Embryo”? This section gives a very clear answer: the Original Spirit is the bones of the embryonic immortal, the Great Medicine its flesh; the Original Spirit is the master of the Great Medicine, the Great Medicine the dwelling of the Original Spirit.

Neither can be lacking:

  • With only the Original Spirit presiding and illuminating but no Great Medicine — even if one can enter absorption and send out the spirit, there can be no “leaving the embryo”;
  • With only the Great Medicine but no Original Spirit presiding and illuminating — though one can open the passes and penetrate the openings, one will surely lose what one has gained.

Only after the Great Medicine has been ingested, when the Original Spirit and the Great Medicine rely on and return to each other, blending without separating, can ears, eyes, mouth, nose, the four limbs and hundred bones all return to emptiness, and one point of numinous light dwell peacefully in the central palace, ever still and ever illuminating. The ancients said: “The fire phasing is non-action in accord with nature; the natural true fire nurtures the embryonic immortal.” With no stray thoughts in the mind, guarding the center and embracing the One, the yang breath arises day by day and the yin breath wanes day by day, until finally even drowsiness is gone.

Qiu Ling’s note concludes with a sentence of Wu Chongxu: “The embryo is only spirit and breath; there is no real infant, nothing with form or image.” The work of nurturing the embryo rests entirely on “utmost stillness.”

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

养胎之功，当以元神为胎仙之骨，以大药为胎仙之肉。以元神为大药之主人，以大药为元神之宅舍。若有元神主照而无大药服食，纵能入定出神，而不能有脱胎现象。若得大药服食，而无元神主照，虽能通关透窍，势必旋得而旋失。

In the work of nurturing the embryo, take the Original Spirit as the bones of the embryonic immortal and the Great Medicine as its flesh; take the Original Spirit as the master of the Great Medicine, and the Great Medicine as the dwelling of the Original Spirit. If there is the Original Spirit presiding and illuminating but no Great Medicine ingested, then even if one can enter absorption and send out the spirit, there can be no phenomenon of leaving the embryo. If one has ingested the Great Medicine but there is no Original Spirit presiding and illuminating, then though one can open the passes and penetrate the openings, one will surely lose it as soon as it is gained.

§2
Original (Chinese)

只有在大药服食以后，以元神为大药之主，以大药为元神之依归，相与混合而不离，才能把耳、目、口、鼻、四肢百骸尽归于虚，一点虚灵性光安居于还丹之中宫内，以温以养，若存若亡，不有不无，常寂常照，炯炯不昧，巍然独存。

Only after the Great Medicine has been ingested, taking the Original Spirit as the master of the Great Medicine and the Great Medicine as the refuge of the Original Spirit, blending with each other and not separating, can one return ears, eyes, mouth, nose, the four limbs and hundred bones entirely to emptiness, and let one point of empty, numinous light of nature dwell peacefully in the central palace where the Returned Elixir lies, warming and nourishing it, as if present and as if gone, neither existent nor nonexistent, ever still and ever illuminating, shining bright and not obscured, standing alone in majesty.

§3
Original (Chinese)

古云：“火候无为合自然，自然真火养胎仙。”心无妄想，久而守中抱一，自然阳气日日发生，运行于正路，神中之阴自然渐渐消灭，而念虑不起，使阳神愈旺而愈明，以致昏睡全无。

The ancients said: “The fire phasing is non-action in accord with nature; the natural true fire nurtures the embryonic immortal.” With no stray thoughts in the mind, guarding the center and embracing the One for a long time, naturally the yang breath arises day by day and moves along the right path; the yin within the spirit naturally wanes away little by little, and thoughts and worries do not arise, so that the Yang Spirit grows ever stronger and ever brighter, until drowsiness is entirely gone.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§3
Original note (Chinese)

大药即先天真炁。此言元神为胎之骨，大药为胎之肉，即是说神炁凝成胎。伍冲虚《天仙正理》说：“胎即神炁耳，非真有婴儿也，非有形有象也。”神为炁之主，炁为神之宅，只有神炁相依，才能入于虚无静定之境，而此段养胎功夫全以至静为主。

The Great Medicine is the true breath of Prior Heaven. Saying here that the Original Spirit is the bones of the embryo and the Great Medicine its flesh means that spirit and breath congeal into the embryo. Wu Chongxu’s True Principles of the Celestial Immortals says: “The embryo is only spirit and breath; there is no real infant, nothing with form or image.” Spirit is the master of breath, breath is the dwelling of spirit; only when spirit and breath rely on each other can one enter the realm of empty, settled stillness, and this work of nurturing the embryo rests entirely on utmost stillness.

Key Terms

  • Embryonic immortal: that is, the Holy Embryo.
  • Bones and flesh, master and dwelling: the relationship between the Original Spirit and the Great Medicine.
  • Leaving the embryo: the Holy Embryo, once nurtured to completion, the Yang Spirit leaving the physical body; see sending out the spirit.
  • Lost as soon as gained: obtained and then lost again at once.
  • The central palace where the Returned Elixir lies: the middle Cinnabar Field to which the Great Medicine returns after ingestion; see Central Yellow.
  • Ever still and ever illuminating, shining bright and not obscured: absorption (stillness) and wisdom (illumination) are not two.
  • The fire phasing is non-action in accord with nature: in the stage of nurturing the embryo, the fire phasing is non-action.
  • Great Medicine: Qiu Ling’s note — the true breath of Prior Heaven; see One Breath of Prior Heaven.

Putting It into Practice

  1. “Spirit is the master of breath, breath is the dwelling of spirit.” This applies to practitioners at every level: attention (spirit) and the felt sense of energy in the body (breath) should be together. The mind within the body, the body illuminated by the mind — this is the basic state of quiet sitting.
  2. Only “illuminating” without “nourishing,” or only “nourishing” without “illuminating,” is incomplete. Clarity alone without a relaxed body easily becomes dry and agitated; relaxation alone without awareness easily becomes dull and sinking.
  3. Wu Chongxu’s words dispel all mystifying imagination: the embryo is only spirit and breath relying on each other; “there is no real infant.”

Translator's Notes

  1. “The fire phasing is non-action in accord with nature; the natural true fire nurtures the embryonic immortal”: an oral formula often quoted by the Wu–Liu school; its source is unknown.
  2. The original text of this section is one passage; we have divided it into three short passages.
