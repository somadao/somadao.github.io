I.11 · False Passing of the Gates

Records of Experience in Refining Essence into Breath, Section 11 · False Passing of the Gates — true and false passing of the gates

Listen to this chapterENEnglish

About This Chapter

This is a very short section on how to tell whether the Great Medicine has truly passed the gates and whether the ingesting has been genuine.

  • False passing, vague ingesting: the true breath is diffuse and the Original Spirit has nothing to rely on. There is no embryonic breathing in the central palace, no transformed scenes in the Conception and Governing vessels, and no “great realm of empty space” in the middle and lower Cinnabar Fields — silent, nothing heard; blank, nothing seen.
  • Genuine passing and ingesting: there is certainly embryonic breathing in the central palace, with unusual things seen and heard; in settled stillness the Conception and Governing vessels show all kinds of “transformed scenes,” such as the Governing Vessel turning into a heavenly ladder, the Conception Vessel into a jeweled pagoda, the central palace into an elixir furnace, the middle and lower Cinnabar Fields into a great realm of empty space, a heaven and earth apart, with dozens of transformations in a single day.

Qiu Ling’s note has only two sentences, but they are very sober: embryonic breathing, and the middle and lower Cinnabar Fields turning into a great realm of empty space, are real; as for the Conception and Governing vessels turning into a heavenly ladder or a jeweled pagoda, not necessarily.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

过关不真，服食模糊，则有真气散漫、元神无依之患。盖过关服食真切，中宫必有胎息、异常闻见。静定之中任督二脉必有化景，或督脉化为天梯，或任脉化为宝塔，或中宫化为丹炉，或中下田化为虚空大界，别有天地。即一日之间有数十种变幻，奇见奇闻等等不一。

If the passing of the gates is not genuine and the ingesting is vague, there is the trouble of the true breath being diffuse and the Original Spirit having nothing to rely on. For if the passing of the gates and the ingesting are genuine, there is certainly embryonic breathing in the central palace, with unusual things seen and heard. In settled stillness the Conception and Governing vessels certainly show transformed scenes: the Governing Vessel may turn into a heavenly ladder, or the Conception Vessel into a jeweled pagoda, or the central palace into an elixir furnace, or the middle and lower fields into a great realm of empty space, a heaven and earth apart. Even within a single day there are dozens of transformations, marvelous sights and sounds of every kind.

§2
Original (Chinese)

若过关渺茫，服食恍惚，而中宫必无胎息，任督二脉必无化景，中、下田必无天地、虚空大界之景象，势必寂然无所闻，茫然无所见。

If the passing of the gates is hazy and the ingesting indistinct, then there is certainly no embryonic breathing in the central palace, certainly no transformed scenes in the Conception and Governing vessels, and certainly no scene of heaven and earth or a great realm of empty space in the middle and lower fields; it will inevitably be silent, with nothing heard, and blank, with nothing seen.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§2
Original note (Chinese)

过关真切，必有胎息及中、下丹田化为虚空大界是也。至任督二脉化为天梯、宝塔则未必。

When the passing of the gates is genuine, there will certainly be embryonic breathing and the middle and lower Cinnabar Fields turning into a great realm of empty space — this is so. As for the Conception and Governing vessels turning into a heavenly ladder or a jeweled pagoda, not necessarily.

Key Terms

  • False passing of the gates, vague ingesting: the Great Medicine has not truly passed the gates and returned into the central palace.
  • Embryonic breathing: an extremely subtle inner breathing; see True Breath.
  • Transformed scenes: the various changing scenes that appear in absorption.
  • Great realm of empty space: the feeling of the middle and lower Cinnabar Fields as a vast, open, boundless expanse.
  • Heavenly ladder, jeweled pagoda, elixir furnace: imaginative transformed scenes of the Conception and Governing vessels and the central palace, which Qiu Ling considers “not necessarily” real.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Qiu Ling’s selectivity is the way to read texts of this kind: separate things that many practitioners experience, such as “embryonic breathing” and “a sense of vast openness,” from scenes colored by personal imagination, such as “heavenly ladders” and “jeweled pagodas.” The former can serve as a reference; the latter need not be taken seriously.
  2. “Silent, with nothing heard, and blank, with nothing seen” is here a mark of “false passing of the gates,” but for beginners, to have nothing at all happening in quiet sitting, simply peace and quiet, is in itself very good — do not measure yourself by the standards of an advanced stage.

Translator's Notes

  1. The first sentence of the original, “If the passing of the gates is not genuine and the ingesting is vague, there is the trouble of the true breath being diffuse and the Original Spirit having nothing to rely on,” is a general statement; what follows first describes the genuine case and then the false case, in contrast.
  2. The original text of this section is one passage; we have divided it into two short passages.
