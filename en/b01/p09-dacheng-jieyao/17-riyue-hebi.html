II.5 · The Union of Sun and Moon

Records of Experience in Refining Breath into Spirit, Section 5 · The Union of Sun and Moon — jade stamens and golden flowers, the sacred sun and the sacred moon

Listen to this chapterENEnglish

Note (translator): The beginning of this section describes “losing consciousness of everything, the breath entirely gone, the six pulses all stopped,” and “the joints as if broken, the heart cold as ice.” These are records by the ancients of extremely deep states of absorption; they are not goals of practice, nor conditions that ordinary people will experience. If in quiet sitting you experience marked discomfort such as palpitations, tightness in the chest, chills, or numbness of the limbs that does not go away, stop practicing and seek medical care if necessary.

About This Chapter

After the Returned Elixir has returned into the central palace and been warmed and nourished for a long time, one sits until the extreme of settled stillness: losing consciousness of everything, the breath entirely gone, the six pulses all stopped. In great absorption, one only feels as if a wind were descending from the sky, pouring into the Muddy Pellet and the two eyes, passing through the whole body, all the joints opening together. The author urges: never be afraid; only concentrate the spirit and enter absorption.

At the extreme of absorption, the breath of true lead turns into a bright full moon and the breath of true mercury into a red sun; the sunlight and moonlight unite, and together put forth two golden flowers, golden red, with five distinct petals, called “jade stamens and golden flowers.” At this time one must use the True Intention to concentrate and gather them in, draw them through the nose into the lower Cinnabar Field and the Xuwei point, rising behind and descending in front, returning into the central palace and becoming one with the immortal embryo, then run the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You to gather it in; continuing in settled stillness, the elixir light shines constantly day and night at the Hall of Impressions, called “the sacred sun and the sacred moon illuminating the golden court.”

Qiu Ling’s note is very sober: the Daoist school regards this as the manifestation of the true yang original breath growing ever more abundant through the light of wisdom before the eyes; but whether these transformed scenes are the same for every practitioner remains to be verified. When the practice reaches this point, the elixir light should shine constantly at the Hall of Impressions.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

还丹以后，归入中宫温养成胎。久久薰蒸，坐至静定之极，不省人事，气息全无，六脉皆住。大定之中，只觉得有风似从天降下，灌入泥丸、两目之中，迳透全身，百节齐开，骨节如断，心冷如冰，丹田似火，切勿恐怖，只有凝神入定。

After the Returned Elixir, it returns into the central palace and is warmed and nourished to form the embryo. After long steaming, one sits until the extreme of settled stillness, losing consciousness of everything, the breath entirely gone, the six pulses all stopped. In great absorption, one only feels as if a wind were descending from the sky, pouring into the Muddy Pellet and the two eyes, passing straight through the whole body, all the joints opening together; the joints feel as if broken, the heart cold as ice, the Cinnabar Field like fire. Never be afraid; only concentrate the spirit and enter absorption.

§2
Original (Chinese)

此是金木交合之际，三宫气满，二炁俱足，神炁大定，恍如醉梦，久而定极，真铅之气，化为一轮明月之象，真汞之气，化为一轮红日之形，这是铅汞相投。

This is the moment of the union of metal and wood; the three palaces are full of breath, the two breaths are both sufficient, spirit and breath are in great absorption, as if drunk or dreaming. In time, at the extreme of absorption, the breath of true lead turns into the image of a bright full moon, and the breath of true mercury into the form of a red sun — this is lead and mercury meeting.

§3
Original (Chinese)

当一轮明月缭绕不定现于眼前之际，须用真意留而待之，后又现有日光来与月光相合，日月交光之中，合发金花二朵，金红赤色，五瓣分明，此名曰玉蕊金花。

When the bright full moon appears before the eyes, circling unsteadily, one must use the True Intention to keep it there and wait; afterwards sunlight also appears and unites with the moonlight, and in the mingling of the light of sun and moon two golden flowers are put forth together, golden red, with five distinct petals; these are called jade stamens and golden flowers.

§4
Original (Chinese)

是时日光与月光伴归一处，悬于印堂之上，当运动真意，凝聚收敛而蛰藏之，以鼻吸入下丹田及虚危穴，自然后升前降，归入中宫，与仙胎混而为一，急行卯酉周天以收之。仍须静定，深入寂灭，使丹光在印堂昼夜常明，名曰圣日圣月，照耀金庭者也。

At this time the sunlight and the moonlight return together to one place and hang above the Hall of Impressions; one should set the True Intention in motion, concentrate and gather them in, and store them away in hibernation, drawing them through the nose into the lower Cinnabar Field and the Xuwei point; naturally they rise behind and descend in front, return into the central palace, and merge into one with the immortal embryo; then quickly run the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You to gather it in. One must still remain in settled stillness, going deep into silent extinction, so that the elixir light shines constantly day and night at the Hall of Impressions; this is called “the sacred sun and the sacred moon illuminating the golden court.”

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

道门认为，日月合璧及金花合并是真阳元炁通过眼前慧光或蟾光愈来愈充足的表现，但对练功者来说，是否化景都是一样，则待证。练功至此，丹光当在印堂常明。

The Daoist school holds that the union of sun and moon and the joining of the golden flowers are manifestations of the true yang original breath growing ever more abundant through the light of wisdom or toad light before the eyes; but for practitioners, whether the transformed scenes are all the same remains to be verified. When the practice reaches this point, the elixir light should shine constantly at the Hall of Impressions.

Key Terms

  • Union of sun and moon: the overall theme of this section; the bright moon is the image of true lead, the red sun the image of true mercury.
  • The six pulses all stopped: in Chinese medicine, the six pulse positions (cun, guan, chi on left and right); here, the pulse seeming to stop.
  • Union of metal and wood: another pair of metaphors, metal for lead (emotion) and wood for mercury (nature); see Lead and Mercury.
  • Three palaces: the upper, middle, and lower Cinnabar Fields.
  • Jade stamens and golden flowers: the two golden flowers born in the mingling of the light of sun and moon.
  • Xuwei point: that is, the perineum; see Perineum.
  • Rising behind and descending in front: rising along the Governing Vessel and descending along the Conception Vessel.
  • Immortal embryo: that is, the Holy Embryo.
  • Sacred sun and sacred moon, golden court: the elixir light shining constantly at the Hall of Impressions; the golden court means the head and face.
  • Light of wisdom, toad light: light appearing before the eyes in settled stillness; see light of Nature / light of cognition.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Qiu Ling’s sentence, “whether the transformed scenes are all the same remains to be verified,” is the key to reading this section. The scenes are the ancients’ records, varying from person to person; they are not a standard answer.
  2. “Never be afraid; only concentrate the spirit and enter absorption” means for beginners: whatever unfamiliar sensation appears in quiet sitting, neither fear it nor chase it; keep watching it calmly. But if it is persistent discomfort, stop; do not force yourself to go on.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Passing straight through the whole body” (jing tou quanshen 迳透全身): 迳 is the same as 径, “directly.”
  2. “Return together to one place” (ban gui yi chu 伴归一处): accompanying each other back to one place.
  3. The original text of this section is one passage; we have divided it into four short passages.
