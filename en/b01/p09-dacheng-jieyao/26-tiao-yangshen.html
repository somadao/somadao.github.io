III.4 · Regulating the Going Out and Coming In of the Yang Spirit

Records of Experience in Refining Spirit to Return to Emptiness, Section 4 · Regulating the Going Out and Coming In of the Yang Spirit — from seven days to seven sevens, gradually regulating and nourishing for three years

Listen to this chapterENEnglish

Note (translator): This section is an ancient record of the stage of “sending out the spirit.” Qiu Ling gives no notes; it is for understanding the whole picture of the alchemical method only. Please do not attempt to practice it.

About This Chapter

The last section of the whole text describes the order of steps in regulating the Yang Spirit’s going out and coming in:

  • When first regulating the Yang Spirit to leave the shell, it goes out and comes back at once; on coming back, one enters absorption for seven days through cessation absorption; it goes out again and comes back, and one remains in absorption for fourteen days (two sevens); it goes out again and comes back, and one remains in absorption for three sevens, four sevens… up to seven sevens. Regulated and nourished step by step, it is complete only after three years.
  • The overall rule is “according to the gathering in and letting out of the yang light, one gauges the going out and coming in of the regulated spirit”: in going out, first let out the yang light through the crown gate, then with one thought think of going out, and following the light transcend to outside the body; in gathering in, first take the hair-fine light into the Dharma-body, then the Yang Spirit returns by the old road to the upper field of the physical body and enters absorption through cessation absorption. Only after three years, when the nature is seasoned, is one assured that there is no danger of fright.
  • Even after the going out and coming in, rising and descending have become practiced, one must still choose a good day and leave the shell only when the sky is bright and the air clear, as one cares for a small child: in thick fog do not go out the door, in heavy rain do not travel the road; regulate it at every moment, and never let the mind slacken even for a moment, lest the Yang Spirit go out once and not return.

Qiu Ling gives no notes on this section. With it, the selections from the Concise Essentials of Great Accomplishment come to an end.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

初调阳神出壳，旋出而旋入，依灭尽定而寂灭之。一定七日，再调出而旋入，仍依灭尽定而寂灭之。一定二七始，再调出而旋入。一定三七始，调出旋入。一定四七始，以至五七、六七、七七始，调出而旋入。渐次调养，三年而后已也。

When first regulating the Yang Spirit to leave the shell, it goes out and at once comes back in, and through cessation absorption is stilled and extinguished. After one absorption of seven days, it is again regulated to go out and at once comes back in, and again through cessation absorption is stilled and extinguished. Only after one absorption of two sevens is it again regulated to go out and at once come back in. Only after one absorption of three sevens is it regulated to go out and come back in. Only after one absorption of four sevens, and so on to five sevens, six sevens, and seven sevens, is it regulated to go out and at once come back in. Regulated and nourished step by step, it is complete only after three years.

§2
Original (Chinese)

总依阳光之收放，准调神之出入。当神居上田，灭尽定极之余，总先佈阳光，透出顶门，然后一念思出，随光超脱于身外。如欲敛神入壳，先将毫光收入法身之内，然后阳神由旧路复归色身上田之中，仍依灭尽定而寂灭之。调出旋入，灭尽寂灭，三载以后，性体老练，方保无惊怖之患也。

Always, according to the gathering in and letting out of the yang light, one gauges the going out and coming in of the regulated spirit. When the spirit dwells in the upper field, after cessation absorption has reached its extreme, always first let out the yang light through the crown gate, then with one thought think of going out, and following the light transcend to outside the body. If one wishes to gather the spirit back into the shell, first take the hair-fine light into the Dharma-body, then the Yang Spirit returns by the old road into the upper field of the physical body, and again through cessation absorption is stilled and extinguished. Regulated to go out and at once come back in, extinguished and stilled — only after three years, when the nature is seasoned, is one assured that there is no trouble of fright.

§3
Original (Chinese)

阳神出入升降纯熟，须择良辰，天朗气清方可出壳，如护小儿一般。大雾莫出门，大雨莫行路，时刻调理，不可一时心有懈怠，恐阳神一出而不回也。

When the Yang Spirit’s going out and coming in, rising and descending have become practiced, one must still choose a good hour; only when the sky is bright and the air clear may it leave the shell, as one cares for a small child. In thick fog do not go out the door; in heavy rain do not travel the road; regulate it at every moment, and never let the mind slacken even for a moment, lest the Yang Spirit go out once and not return.

Key Terms

  • Regulating the spirit to leave the shell, going out and at once coming back in: going out and returning immediately; see sending out the spirit.
  • Cessation absorption, stilled and extinguished: the deep absorption entered after the spirit is taken back.
  • Seven days, two sevens… seven sevens: lengthening the time in absorption step by step in units of seven days.
  • Letting out the yang light (bu yangguang 佈阳光): emitting the yang light (佈 is the same as 布).
  • Hair-fine light: the light on the Yang Spirit’s body.
  • The old road: the crown gate and upper field passed through on going out.
  • As one cares for a small child: as carefully as one looks after an infant.

Putting It into Practice

  1. This section is for understanding only.
  2. In the last passage the author still says “never let the mind slacken even for a moment” and “as one cares for a small child.” From the “neither forgetting nor helping” of the first group to the final “as one cares for a small child,” the same attitude runs through the whole text: careful, continuous, gentle, and never rough.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Always first let out the yang light” (总先佈阳光): the original book has 佈, a variant of 布, meaning to emit, to spread.
  2. “Only after one absorption of two sevens” (一定二七始): meaning that only after one absorption has lasted fourteen days (is it regulated to go out again). 始 is read as “only then.”
  3. With this, the twenty-six sections of the Concise Essentials of Great Accomplishment selected by Qiu Ling come to an end. The book also contains a part on “refining Emptiness to unite with the Dao” (the nine years of facing the wall), which Qiu Ling did not select.
  4. The original text of this section has two passages; we have divided them into three short passages.
