II.1 · The Essentials of Refining Breath into Spirit

Records of Experience in Refining Breath into Spirit, Section 1 · The Essentials of Refining Breath into Spirit — the general outline of the ten months of nurturing the embryo

Listen to this chapterENEnglish

Note (translator): From this section we enter the second group, “refining Breath into Spirit” (the ten months of nurturing the embryo). This is an advanced stage of the alchemical method, and of the many scenes the book describes (the mysterious pearl descending, golden flowers floating upward, golden lotuses welling up from the ground, white snow flying in the sky, and so on), Qiu Ling points out in several places that they “await verification.” Please read it as a document for understanding the whole picture of the alchemical method, not as a goal of practice.

About This Chapter

As at the beginning of the first group, this section summarizes the whole course of the ten months of nurturing the embryo in a passage of parallel prose:

  • The general outline: guarding the center and embracing the One is the core of nurturing the embryo, practiced day and night together; responding in movement and storing in stillness is the divine mechanism of refining the elixir, so from beginning to end.
  • The course: nourish at every moment, neither forgetting nor helping; warm continuously and densely, constantly settled and constantly illuminating; one will see the true breath moving upward and the Sweet Dew descending; run the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You to gather in the inner medicine, and divide the rising and descending into left and right to make the embryo round and firm. Of the various scenes, “whether true or illusory, the mind should always be dead to what is seen and heard” — pay no attention to any of them.
  • The result: the lead exhausted and the mercury dried, breathing stops and the armillary ceases to turn, sun and moon unite, the breath turns into pure yang, the body withstands cold and heat, drowsiness is entirely gone; finally, at the extreme of cessation absorption, golden lotuses well up from the ground and white snow flies across the sky, and with the Samādhi True Fire one strikes at the Hundred Meetings point to bring out the Yang Spirit in absorption.

Qiu Ling’s note explains this stage very clearly: refining Breath into Spirit is the stage of passing from action to non-action; in fact it is entering the state of great absorption. The so-called “Holy Embryo” or “infant” is only a metaphor for the congealing of spirit and breath. At this time one runs the Greater Celestial Circuit: the cauldron moves down from the Muddy Pellet to the Yellow Court, with the Yellow Court as cauldron and the lower field as furnace, and one guards only the space between the two fields.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

次节炼炁化神而行十月养胎之功，守中抱一乃养胎之主脑，不分昼夜而并进；动应静蓄，是炼丹之神机，无论始终而皆然。时时刻刻勿忘勿助而养，绵绵密密常定常照而温，将见真炁上运，甘露下降。

The next stage, refining Breath into Spirit, carries out the work of the ten months of nurturing the embryo. Guarding the center and embracing the One is the core of nurturing the embryo, practiced day and night together without distinction; responding in movement and storing in stillness is the divine mechanism of refining the elixir, so from beginning to end. Nourish at every moment, neither forgetting nor helping; warm continuously and densely, constantly settled and constantly illuminating; and one will see the true breath moving upward and the Sweet Dew descending.

§2
Original (Chinese)

行卯酉之周天以收内药，分左右之升降以固胎圆，或太阳当空，催迫久而玄珠下降炉中，或丹光如莲，开放极而金花上飘鼎外，愈出愈奇，要皆金丹之变化，或真或幻，总宜心死于见闻。

Run the Celestial Circuit of Mao and You to gather in the inner medicine, and divide the rising and descending into left and right to make the embryo round and firm. Perhaps the great sun stands in the sky, and when it has pressed long, the mysterious pearl descends into the furnace; perhaps the elixir light is like a lotus, and when it has opened to the full, golden flowers float upward out of the cauldron — the more appears, the more marvelous; all are transformations of the golden elixir. Whether true or illusory, the mind should always be dead to what is seen and heard.

§3
Original (Chinese)

直至铅尽汞乾，二炁住则璇玑停轮，阳长阴消而日月合璧，自然气化纯阳，身能耐寒却暑，昏睡全无而丹光常明，心必达圣通灵。

Until the lead is exhausted and the mercury dried, the two breaths come to rest and the armillary ceases to turn; yang grows and yin wanes, and sun and moon unite; naturally the breath turns into pure yang, the body can withstand cold and ward off heat, drowsiness is entirely gone and the elixir light is ever bright, and the mind will surely reach sagely wisdom and numinous penetration.

§4
Original (Chinese)

由是灭尽定极，心空性现，金莲匝地涌，白雪满天飞，聚三昧真火，攻百会乾鼎，调出入定阳神。此十月养胎之功已返到乾元面目，固有真我之位。

From this, at the extreme of cessation absorption, the mind is empty and the nature appears, golden lotuses well up all over the ground, white snow flies all across the sky; gather the Samādhi True Fire, strike at the Qian cauldron of the Hundred Meetings point, and bring out the Yang Spirit in absorption. When the work of these ten months of nurturing the embryo is done, one has returned to the original face of Qian the Origin, to the place of the true self that was there from the beginning.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

炼炁化神乃道教气功的第二阶段，以炁与神合炼，使炁归神，实际上是进一步的炼药功夫，是丹法中由有为到无为的阶段，称为中关，也叫十月养胎。这个胎叫“圣胎”，亦称“婴儿”，实际上是神炁凝结的比喻。

炼炁化神阶段实行的是大周天功夫。小周天是采小药运河车，经过上乾顶（泥丸）而下坤炉（下田）而封存。大周天则将鼎下移，以黄庭（在中田）为鼎，下田为炉，元炁氤氲于二田之虚境，修持只守此二田之间，所谓一念不起，一意不散，含光默默，真息绵绵，圆明觉照，常自惺惺，此是长养圣胎之真火候，实际上是进入大定状态。丹书说养胎必须常常觉照，谨谨护持，如小龙之乍养珠，似少女之始怀孕，牢关神室（即中宫、中丹田），不可使之渗漏。静定中行金液、玉液还丹，灵丹化液，吞入口中，直落黄庭，勤加温养，种种化景随之出现，于是丹熟珠灵，阴制阳纯，炼成纯阳的乾健体，也就能返老还童，延长寿命。

Refining Breath into Spirit is the second stage of Daoist qigong: breath and spirit are refined together, so that breath returns to spirit. In fact it is a further stage of the work of refining the medicine, the stage in the alchemical method of passing from action to non-action; it is called the “middle pass,” and also the ten months of nurturing the embryo. This embryo is called the “Holy Embryo,” also called the “infant”; in fact it is a metaphor for the congealing of spirit and breath.

In the stage of refining Breath into Spirit, the work of the Greater Celestial Circuit is carried out. The Lesser Celestial Circuit is gathering the lesser medicine and turning the River Chariot, passing up through the Qian summit (the Muddy Pellet) and down to the Kun furnace (the lower field), where it is sealed and stored. The Greater Celestial Circuit, however, moves the cauldron down, taking the Yellow Court (in the middle field) as the cauldron and the lower field as the furnace; the original breath swirls in the empty realm between these two fields, and cultivation guards only the space between them — what is called not a single thought arising, not a single intention scattering, containing the light in silence, the True Breath continuous, round and bright awareness, ever wakeful. This is the true fire phasing for nurturing the Holy Embryo; in fact it is entering the state of great absorption. The alchemical books say that in nurturing the embryo one must constantly be aware and carefully protect it, like a young dragon just beginning to nurture its pearl, like a young woman just become pregnant; one must firmly close the chamber of the spirit (that is, the central palace, the middle Cinnabar Field) and not let it leak. In settled stillness one carries out the Returned Elixir of golden liquor and jade liquor; the numinous elixir turns into liquid, is swallowed into the mouth, and falls straight into the Yellow Court; diligently warmed and nourished, all kinds of transformed scenes appear in turn, until the elixir ripens and the pearl becomes numinous, yin is exhausted and yang pure, and one refines a strong Qian body of pure yang, and so can reverse old age, return to youth, and prolong one’s life.

Key Terms

  • Refining Breath into Spirit: the second stage of the alchemical method, also called “the middle accomplishment of nurturing the embryo,” “the middle pass,” or “the ten months of nurturing the embryo”; see the three transmutations.
  • Guarding the center and embracing the One: the core of nurturing the embryo.
  • Responding in movement, storing in stillness: following it when it moves, storing and nourishing it when it is still.
  • Inner medicine: the medicine after the Returned Elixir; see Medicine.
  • Mysterious pearl: an image of the golden elixir; see Golden Flower; see Section 6 for details.
  • The mind dead to what is seen and heard: not stirring the mind or a thought at anything seen or heard.
  • The lead exhausted and the mercury dried: lead and mercury having blended to the utmost, the traces of each disappear; see Lead and Mercury.
  • The two breaths at rest, the armillary ceasing to turn: the breathing of mouth and nose comes to rest, and the turning of the Celestial Circuit stops. The armillary (xuanji 璇玑) originally means the first four stars of the Northern Dipper, a metaphor for the turning wheel of the circuit.
  • Union of sun and moon: see Section 5 for details.
  • Cessation absorption: a Buddhist term, deep absorption in which mental activity has entirely ceased.
  • Golden lotuses welling up all over the ground, white snow flying all across the sky: the scenes when the embryo is complete after ten months and the spirit is about to go out; see Section 10 for details.
  • Samādhi True Fire, the Qian cauldron of the Hundred Meetings: the gathered spirit-fire and the crown of the head.
  • Yang Spirit: the name of the Holy Embryo once it has been nurtured to completion; see Holy Embryo, sending out the spirit.
  • Original face of Qian the Origin, the true self: the original nature of pure yang.
  • Greater Celestial Circuit: Qiu Ling’s note — with the Yellow Court as cauldron and the lower field as furnace, guarding only the space between the two fields.
  • Chamber of the spirit: the central palace, the middle Cinnabar Field.

Putting It into Practice

  1. “Whether true or illusory, the mind should always be dead to what is seen and heard.” This is the sentence most worth remembering in the whole second group. If advanced practitioners pay no attention to marvelous scenes, beginners have even less need to chase any experience.
  2. Qiu Ling reduces “nurturing the embryo” to “great absorption”: not a single thought arising, not a single intention scattering, containing the light in silence, the True Breath continuous, round and bright awareness, ever wakeful. Remove the metaphors, and these lines describe the state of deep quiet sitting — calm, and at the same time clear.
  3. “Like a young dragon just beginning to nurture its pearl, like a young woman just become pregnant” describes the attitude of protection: careful, gentle, continuous, neither rough nor careless.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Yin is exhausted and yang pure” (阴制阳纯): 制 should probably be 尽, “exhausted” (阴尽阳纯 is a common alchemical expression); the translation follows “yin exhausted and yang pure,” while the original text keeps the reading of the base text.
  2. “Just beginning to nurture its pearl” (zha yang zhu 乍养珠): zha means “just, at first.”
  3. “The two breaths come to rest”: generally understood as the breath of the mouth and nose coming to rest, that is, embryonic breathing; some also understand it as the two breaths of lead and mercury congealing.
  4. The original text of this section is one passage; we have divided it into four short passages.
