II.10 · Heavenly Flowers Falling in Profusion: Stopping the Fire

Records of Experience in Refining Breath into Spirit, Section 10 · Heavenly Flowers Falling in Profusion — stopping the fire; the embryo complete after ten months, leaving absorption and moving to the upper field

About This Chapter

The last section of the second group speaks of the signs for stopping the fire when the embryo is complete after ten months. After the embryo is complete, at the extreme of cessation absorption, the light of nature is revealed: looking outward in stillness, purple clouds and auspicious mists fill the eyes; looking down from the crown, a mass of golden light envelops the body. One must not cling to it, but with the mind dead enter absorption, and naturally “golden lotuses rise from the ground, white snow flies across the sky” — golden lotuses well up from the ground, pass up through the ninth heaven, turn into snowflakes falling in profusion; keep them with the intention, and they return into the body to nurture the infant. This “heavenly flowers falling in profusion” is the time for stopping the fire when the embryo is complete after ten months.

If the heavenly flowers fall in profusion and one still does not know to stop the fire, there is the danger of “burning by fire.” The method of stopping the fire is: no longer use the work of still illumination, but simply return to emptiness, the mind dwelling in nothingness, everything entering emptiness, and the fire stops of itself.

The author also explains what “leaving absorption” and “entering absorption” mean: leaving absorption is leaving the middle field and moving to the upper field; entering absorption is concentrating the spirit in the Celestial Valley (the Muddy Pellet), so that the ears and eyes regain their keenness. The time for leaving absorption is the day the fire is stopped — too early, and the spirit will wander off and stray far; too late, and the spirit becomes dull and not numinous.

Qiu Ling notes: the work of refining Breath into Spirit (the ten months of nurturing the embryo) ends with this section. The alchemical scriptures hold that when practice reaches this point, one has returned to the original face of Qian the Origin; after stopping the fire and leaving absorption, one moves on to refining Spirit to return to Emptiness, that is, the stage of the three years of suckling.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

胎圆以后，灭尽定极之际，露出性光。静中外视，紫霞祥烟满目，顶中下视，一团金光罩体。不可着他，死心入定，自有金莲从地起，白雪满天飞。

After the embryo is complete, at the extreme of cessation absorption, the light of nature is revealed. Looking outward in stillness, purple clouds and auspicious mists fill the eyes; looking down from within the crown, a mass of golden light envelops the body. One must not cling to it; with the mind dead, enter absorption, and naturally golden lotuses rise from the ground and white snow flies across the sky.

§2
Original (Chinese)

金莲从地涌出，上透九霄，自然化为雪片，从天飞来，缤纷而下，以意留之，仍归身中，育我婴儿，乃十月胎圆止火之候。一但天花乱坠不知止火，便有火焚之危。

Golden lotuses well up from the ground and pass up through the ninth heaven, naturally turning into snowflakes that fly down from the sky, falling in profusion; keep them with the intention, and they return again into the body to nurture my infant — this is the time for stopping the fire when the embryo is complete after ten months. Once the heavenly flowers fall in profusion, if one does not know to stop the fire, there is the danger of burning by fire.

§3
Original (Chinese)

天花乱坠是出定中宫，迁入上田之景到也，必须止火以化神，不须再用寂照之功，只用一味还虚心住于无，俱入于虚无，而火得止。

Heavenly flowers falling in profusion means that the scene of leaving absorption in the central palace and moving to the upper field has arrived; one must stop the fire to transform into spirit, no longer using the work of still illumination, but simply returning to emptiness, the mind dwelling in nothingness, everything entering emptiness, and so the fire is stopped.

§4
Original (Chinese)

又入定出定之意，出定者离中田迁入上田；入定者凝神于天谷（泥丸），使耳目重开聪明。出定之时在止火一日。倘在止火前出定，神必迷荡而远矣！若在止火后出定，神又顽纯而不灵矣！

As for the meaning of entering and leaving absorption: leaving absorption is leaving the middle field and moving to the upper field; entering absorption is concentrating the spirit in the Celestial Valley (the Muddy Pellet), so that the ears and eyes are opened again to keenness. The time for leaving absorption is on the day the fire is stopped. If one leaves absorption before the fire is stopped, the spirit will surely wander off and stray far! If one leaves absorption after the fire is stopped (when the time has passed), the spirit will again be dull and not numinous!

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

炼炁化神（即十月养胎）之功到此节而止。丹经认为练功至此，已返到乾元面目。止火出定，功法将转入炼神还虚即三年乳哺阶段。

The work of refining Breath into Spirit (that is, the ten months of nurturing the embryo) ends with this section. The alchemical scriptures hold that when practice reaches this point, one has returned to the original face of Qian the Origin. After stopping the fire and leaving absorption, the method moves on to refining Spirit to return to Emptiness, that is, the stage of the three years of suckling.

Key Terms

  • Embryo complete: the Holy Embryo complete; see Holy Embryo.
  • Cessation absorption: deep absorption in which mental activity has entirely ceased.
  • Purple clouds and auspicious mists, golden light enveloping the body: the scenes when the embryo is complete.
  • Golden lotuses rising from the ground, white snow flying across the sky: the marks of the embryo complete after ten months, echoing Section 1’s “golden lotuses well up all over the ground, white snow flies all across the sky.”
  • Heavenly flowers falling in profusion: the scene of snowflakes falling in profusion, the time for stopping the fire in the ten months of nurturing the embryo; see stopping the fire.
  • Danger of burning by fire: harming the embryo by not stopping the fire in time.
  • Returning to emptiness, the mind dwelling in nothingness: the method of stopping the fire.
  • Leaving absorption, entering absorption: here specifically leaving the middle field to move to the upper field, and concentrating the spirit in the Celestial Valley; see sending out the spirit.
  • Celestial Valley: the Muddy Pellet.
  • Original face of Qian the Origin: see Qian and Kun.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The principle of stopping the fire appears once more: in the first group the fire was stopped at the second appearance of the yang light; here it is stopped when the heavenly flowers fall in profusion. The principle is the same — stop when you have arrived, turning from “doing” to “not doing.”
  2. “No longer using the work of still illumination, but simply returning to emptiness.” At the height of cultivation even “illumination” must be put down, and only emptiness remains. The lesson for ordinary practitioners is: do not grip any method too tightly; a method is a boat for crossing the river.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Once” (一但): should be written 一旦.
  2. “Dull and not numinous” (顽纯而不灵): 纯 should be 钝, “dull”; the translation follows “dull,” while the original text keeps the reading of the base text.
  3. “Leaving absorption in the central palace”: leaving absorption from the central palace.
  4. The original text of this section is one passage; we have divided it into four short passages.
