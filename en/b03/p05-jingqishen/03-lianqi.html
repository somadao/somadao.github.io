Chapter Five · Essence, Breath and Spirit · 炼气

Refining the Breath

Refining the breath — the Prior Heaven original breath is the root, Posterior Heaven breathing the branch, and neither can be lacking; regulate the breathing while walking, standing, sitting and lying, and when the Posterior Heaven breath is strong, the Prior Heaven is naturally there; the spirit-light shines down, is stirred and then penetrates

About This Chapter

This section speaks of “breath”; its core is the relationship between the Prior Heaven original breath and the Posterior Heaven breath of breathing.

  1. The Prior Heaven breath is the root, the Posterior Heaven breath the branch. The Prior Heaven breath is the breath of the Supreme Ultimate, existing before Heaven and Earth; with it a person lives, without it a person dies — it is a person’s life. The Posterior Heaven breath is breathing, the doorway of the original breath. Huang Yuanji gives two comparisons: the Prior Heaven is the strings and pipes (the instrument), the Posterior Heaven the sound of the strings and pipes; the Prior Heaven is the orchid and the cassia, the Posterior Heaven their fragrance.
  2. But neither can be lacking. Without the Posterior Heaven breath with form, one cannot glimpse the Prior Heaven original breath. If the disciples cannot recognize the Prior Heaven breath truly, it is mostly because their Posterior Heaven physical bodies are too weak. A person is healthy and vigorous precisely because the Posterior Heaven breath is ample: each breath in and out goes back and forth to the central yellow palace, reaching everywhere, the viscera within and the limbs without.
  3. The most practical passage: there is no need to seek mysteries; simply, while walking, standing, sitting and lying, regulate the breathing evenly at all times, following its natural course, neither adding nor subtracting, nor letting it run loose; in daily life, do not stir the mind, do not stir the breath, neither overwork nor overrest — and when the Posterior Heaven breath is strong, the Prior Heaven original breath naturally returns. Not preserving the Posterior Heaven and only seeking the Prior Heaven is like cooking sand to make rice.
  4. The one sign of the arrival of the Prior Heaven original breath: the mind like a lively spring, the body like a steep, unmoving rock, the whole body inside and out at ease and harmonious everywhere. Whatever can be described or likened to an image is the Posterior Heaven breath.
  5. The one breath of emptiness neither increases nor decreases: it is there when the breath is clear, and there when the breath is turbid. The fault lies in lingering when the breath is clear and fretting when it is turbid — and that itself is the floating cloud.
  6. What older people should do: use the spirit-light shining down to stir the true metal in the watery storehouse (the lower elixir field) — “still and unmoving, it is stirred and then penetrates”; begin from the “penetrating”.

Qiu Ling’s note: spirit and essence use only the Prior Heaven, but breath must use both Prior and Posterior Heaven — using the true breathing of the Posterior Heaven to seek where the true person breathes. Lü Chunyang too awakened to the Way only after reading “Prior Heaven breath, Posterior Heaven breath” in the Mirror for Compounding the Medicine. The second note describes the process of refining breath into spirit, quoting Zhang Ziyang’s “all day long, soft as a drunkard” to describe this state.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

气者体之充也。人所受之以生者也。而气亦有先后天之分。先天之气元气也。先天者何？盖此气为太极之气，先乎天地而有者也，故曰先天。人得气于天地，实得此先乎天地之气也。有此气则生，无此气则死。是气也即人之命也。人欲固命，不可不固此气。而气有后天者何？呼吸之气是也。呼吸是元气之门户，有元气而后开呼吸之窍，是之谓后天之气。盖以受天之气而有于后焉者也。先天之气本也，后天之气末也。先天之气源也，后天之气流也。先天之气丝竹也，后天之气丝竹之音而已，丝竹坏而音杳矣！先天之气兰桂也，后天之气特兰桂之香而已，兰桂凋而香息矣！人恐断此呼吸之气，不可不培养本源，以固此太极之元气，此神气性命之辨也。

Breath is what fills the body, what a person receives in order to live. Breath too is divided into Prior Heaven and Posterior Heaven. The Prior Heaven breath is the original breath. Why is it called Prior Heaven? Because this breath is the breath of the Supreme Ultimate, existing before Heaven and Earth, hence “Prior Heaven”. What a person obtains as breath from Heaven and Earth is in fact this breath prior to Heaven and Earth. With this breath one lives, without it one dies — this breath is a person’s life. If one wants to secure life, one must secure this breath. What then is the Posterior Heaven breath? It is the breath of breathing. Breathing is the doorway of the original breath: first there is the original breath, and then the openings of breathing open; this is called the Posterior Heaven breath, because it exists only after receiving the breath of Heaven. The Prior Heaven breath is the root, the Posterior Heaven breath the branch; the Prior Heaven breath is the source, the Posterior Heaven breath the stream. The Prior Heaven breath is like the zither and the strings and pipes, the Posterior Heaven breath only the sound they make — when the strings and pipes are broken, the sound is gone too! The Prior Heaven breath is like the orchid and the cassia tree, the Posterior Heaven breath only their fragrance — when the orchid and cassia wither, the fragrance vanishes too! If a person fears that this breath of breathing will be cut off, he must cultivate the root and source to consolidate this original breath of the Supreme Ultimate — this is the distinction between spirit and breath, nature and life.

§2
Original (Chinese)

炼丹之道，虽曰先天元气酝酿而成，其实非后天有形之气不能瞥见先天元气，是知先后二气两不可无者也。若无后天滓质之气，则先天一气无自而生。若非先天清空一气，则后天尸气概属幻化之具，终不足以结成胎仙。吾观诸子于先天真一之气，不能实实在在认得真修得足者，皆由后天色身太弱，无以勃勃蓬蓬而活见本来虚无妙相也。今为诸子再言后天之气。夫人之身所以健爽者，无非此后天之气足也。气何在？即身间一呼一吸出入往来，絪缊内藏者是。此气即肾间气动，肺主之而出，肾迎之而入。一出一入往还于中黄宫内，则内而脏腑，外而肢体，无处不运，即无处不充，所谓身心两泰，毛发肌肤皆精莹矣！

Although the way of refining the elixir is said to be brewed from the Prior Heaven original breath, in fact, without the Posterior Heaven breath with form, one cannot catch even a glimpse of the Prior Heaven original breath — from this we know that the two breaths, Prior and Posterior Heaven, cannot do without each other. Without this Posterior Heaven breath with dregs and form, the Prior Heaven one breath has no way to arise; without the clear, empty Prior Heaven one breath, this Posterior Heaven breath of the flesh (the “corpse breath”) is only something that will vanish like an illusion, never enough to form the embryo of immortality. I see that you cannot truly recognize and fully cultivate the Prior Heaven true and single breath, all because your Posterior Heaven physical bodies are too weak to show forth, vigorous, surging and lively, the wondrous form of original emptiness. Now let me speak to you again of the Posterior Heaven breath. The reason a person’s body is healthy and vigorous is nothing but that this Posterior Heaven breath is ample. Where is the breath? It is what goes out and in, back and forth, with each breath in the body, intermingling and stored within. This breath is the stirring of the breath between the kidneys: the lungs govern its going out, the kidneys receive its coming in. Going out and coming in, back and forth to the central yellow palace (in this book, the lower elixir field), so that there is no place, the viscera within or the limbs without, that it does not reach, and therefore no place that is not filled — this is what is called “body and mind both at ease”, until even the hair and skin are spirited and lustrous!

§3
Original (Chinese)

诸子欲收先天元气蕴于中宫，生生不已，化化无穷，离不得一出一入之呼吸，息息归根，神气两相融结，和合不解，然后后天气足，先天之气之生始有自也。若不于后天呼吸之息向中宫吹嘘，则金无所生，水不能足，一身内外多是一团燥灼之气，犹之天气亢阳而无润泽之气，万物之枯焦不待言。此一呼一吸所以为人生生之本也。

If you want to store the Prior Heaven original breath in the central palace (the lower elixir field), letting it be born without cease and transform without end, you cannot do without the going out and coming in of breathing: every breath returning to the root, spirit and breath melting and binding together, harmonized and undivided; then, when the Posterior Heaven breath is ample, the production of the Prior Heaven breath has a cause. If one does not use the Posterior Heaven breath of breathing to blow into the central palace, then “metal” (the Prior Heaven true yang) has no way to arise, and “water” (the essence-breath of the kidneys) cannot be ample either; the whole body inside and out is mostly a mass of dry, hot breath, like a drought with no moistening breath, not to mention the withering and scorching of all things. This breathing in and out is the root of a person’s ceaseless life.

§4
Original (Chinese)

诸子如今用功，不必寻奥妙，但于行、住、坐、卧之时，常常调其呼吸顺其自然，任其天然，毫无加损于其间，亦不纵放于其际，一切日用云为，总总一个不动心，不动气，不过劳过逸，自然后天气旺，先天元气自回还于五官之地。不必问先天何在而先天之气自然在是矣！若不知保养后天，徒寻先天元气，势如炊沙求饭，万不可得。

In your work now there is no need to seek any mysteries; simply, while walking, standing, sitting and lying, harmonize the breathing at all times, following its natural course, letting it be as it naturally is, neither adding nor subtracting anything within it, nor letting it run loose; in all daily affairs, always keep to not stirring the mind, not stirring the breath, not overworking, not overresting, and naturally the Posterior Heaven breath grows strong, and the Prior Heaven original breath naturally returns to the five senses. There is no need to ask where the Prior Heaven is; the Prior Heaven breath is naturally right here! If one does not know how to preserve the Posterior Heaven and only seeks the Prior Heaven original breath, it is like cooking sand hoping to make rice — it can never be obtained.

§5
Original (Chinese)

到得后天尸气一聚于中，先天之气自在于内，絪絪缊缊，兀兀腾腾，莫可名状而亦无可名状者。若曰可名，皆是后天之气。诸子知否？若先天元气到时，只有一点可验之处，心如活泼之泉，体似峻峋之石，自然一身内外无处不爽快，无处不圆融，非可意想作为而得者也。故先天一气名曰虚无元气。以此思之，足见先天一气无可名无可指，后人强名之曰先天一气，既属强名，实无所有。学者于此元和内蕴之时，而犹欲于身心内实实在在拟一个色相出来，错矣！且此模拟之心，即是后天之意。有此一意，而先天淳朴之气必为后天之气打散，虽曰先天犹是后天也。

When this Posterior Heaven breath of the flesh gathers in the central palace, the Prior Heaven breath is naturally within it: intermingling and diffuse, rising and surging, indescribable, with nothing to describe it by. If it can be described, it is all Posterior Heaven breath. Do you know? When the Prior Heaven original breath arrives, there is only one point by which it can be verified: the mind like a lively spring, the body like a steep, unmoving rock, and naturally no place in the whole body, inside or out, that is not at ease and harmonious — this cannot be obtained by imagining or doing. So the Prior Heaven one breath is called “the empty original breath”. Thinking of it this way, one knows that the Prior Heaven one breath can neither be named nor pointed to; later people reluctantly named it “the Prior Heaven one breath”; since the name is reluctant, there is in fact nothing there. If the student, while this original, harmonious breath is stored within, still wants to liken it to some solid image within body and mind, he is wrong! Moreover, this likening mind is the Posterior Heaven intention. With this one intention, the simple Prior Heaven breath is sure to be scattered by the Posterior Heaven breath — though called Prior Heaven, it is in fact still Posterior Heaven.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§5
Original note (Chinese)

黄氏于此解释先天元气与后天之气之不可分离，可谓详矣！神与精只用先天，忌至后天，盖后天者乃思虑之神、交感之精，无神通变化。但气则不然，不能无先后天之二用，盖先天元气必用呼吸之气助之，所谓用后天之真呼吸去寻真人呼吸处。《天仙正理》云，吕纯阳初闻道而未甚精明，及见《入药镜》中“先天炁，后天炁，得之者，常似醉”之说，而后深悟成道。吕纯阳自作诗曰：“因看崔公《入药镜》，令人心地转分明。”可见悟得先后天气运用之重要。

Here Huang explains in great detail why the Prior Heaven original breath and the Posterior Heaven breath cannot be separated! Spirit and essence use only the Prior Heaven and must avoid the Posterior Heaven, because the Posterior Heaven spirit is the spirit of thinking and the Posterior Heaven essence is the essence of intercourse, without spiritual powers or transformation. But breath is different: it cannot do without both the Prior and the Posterior Heaven functions, because the Prior Heaven original breath must be helped by the breath of breathing — this is what is called “using the true breathing of the Posterior Heaven to seek where the true person breathes”. The Correct Principles of Celestial Immortality says that when Lü Chunyang first heard the Way he was not yet very clear about it; only when he saw the saying “Prior Heaven breath, Posterior Heaven breath — whoever obtains them is always as if drunk” in the Mirror for Compounding the Medicine did he awaken deeply and accomplish the Way. Lü Chunyang himself wrote in a poem: “From reading Master Cui’s Mirror for Compounding the Medicine, the ground of my mind grew clear.” We can see how important it is to understand the use of the Prior and Posterior Heaven breaths.

§6
Original (Chinese)

虚无一气，又谓真一之气，又曰真一之精，又曰天然元气，又曰天空一气，种种名色不一而足。要无非无声无臭、无思无虑之真，却不在内不在外，隐在色身之中，谓之法身。然而，此难思量，难揣度，却远在天边，近在咫尺，孔子所谓“我欲仁斯仁至矣”！足见此个元气天然自然，未尝一息偶离，离此即不得生，又何以成人耶？然必如何而后可觅哉？虽然著一觅字，又千差万错，增数重障蔽。惟有如生等所说，一切放下，一丝不挂，万缘不染，此个虚无之气即在个中生，积久功深，谅已明白无疑。

The one breath of emptiness is also called “the breath of the true One”, “the essence of the true One”, “the natural original breath”, “the one breath of the empty sky” — all sorts of names. It is nothing but that true substance without sound or scent, without thought or worry: it is not inside, nor outside; hidden in the physical body, it is called the Dharma-body. Yet, hard to think of and hard to fathom, it is both far away at the edge of the sky and right before one’s eyes — just as Confucius said, “I desire humaneness, and humaneness arrives”! So we see that this original breath is natural and spontaneous, never for a single breath apart from me; apart from it one cannot live — how could one be a person? How, then, must one find it? But as soon as the word “find” is used, there are a thousand errors and ten thousand mistakes, adding layer upon layer of veils. Only as you have said: put everything down, without a thread of attachment, untouched by any tie, and this empty breath is born within; with long accumulation and deep work, I think you already understand this beyond doubt.

§7
Original (Chinese)

要知此个虚无一气，天地人物同是一般，富贵贫贱均是一理，极之生死患难亦不为之改移。气息有盈虚消长，而此个元气无有盈虚消长。第后学浅见，不知人有清浊明暗，皆是气机运行，而专以气之清明寻虚无一气，而于昏浊之际，则以为不在也。讵知此个元气不因清明而有，亦不为昏浊而无，只怕不知去欲存理，闭邪归正，于气清时有一流连顾盼之意，于气浊时又加一忧菀烦恼之心。明明元气当前，如日丹之照临，无不光明洞达，反因此障碍心起，遂加浮云遮蔽而日月无光矣。

You must know that this one breath of emptiness is the same for Heaven, Earth, people and things, one principle for the rich and noble and the poor and lowly, and even life and death, trouble and hardship do not change it. The breath has fullness and emptiness, waning and waxing, but this original breath has no fullness or emptiness, no waning or waxing. Only later students, shallow in understanding, do not know that people’s being clear or turbid, bright or dark, is all the movement of the breath’s dynamism, and seek the one breath of emptiness only when the breath is clear and bright, and when they are dull and turbid, think it is not there. How little they know that this original breath does not exist only because of clarity, nor cease to exist because of turbidity; the only fear is not knowing how to remove desire and preserve principle, shut out the deviant and return to the upright: when the breath is clear, there is a lingering, reluctant attachment; when the breath is turbid, a further mind of gloom and vexation is added. The original breath is clearly right before one’s eyes, like the sun and moon shining down, bright and penetrating everywhere; but because this obstructing mind arises, it is as if floating clouds were added to cover them, and the sun and moon lose their light.

§8
Original (Chinese)

尤要明得此个元气本无朕兆，亦无形色，实为后天精气神之根本，先天精气神之主宰。故虚无一气在先天而生乎阴阳，落后天而藏于阴阳。总之，人能打扫得闲思杂虑、一切起心动念的障碍，干干净净不染纤尘足矣！

Above all, understand: this original breath originally has no omen and no form or color; it is truly the root of the Posterior Heaven essence, breath and spirit, and the master of the Prior Heaven essence, breath and spirit. So the one breath of emptiness, in Prior Heaven, gives birth to yin and yang; fallen into Posterior Heaven, it is hidden within yin and yang. In short, if a person can only sweep away idle thoughts and stray worries, every obstacle of the mind arising and thoughts stirring, letting not a speck of dust cling — that is enough!

§9
Original (Chinese)

然而后生小子气息壮旺，易得会其真际；而在年华已迈者犹难调和气血，保养灵光，采此一点至阳之精，此又将奈何哉？吾再示生一个采炼法程。《易》曰：“寂然不动，感而遂通”。生等于元气未见时，不妨以神光下照，将此神火去感动水府所陷之金，久久自然水中火发，而真金出矿矣！此感而彼应，其机有捷于影响者，故古人教后学，于寂然不动中无可采取，教以神光下照之法，而于通处下手，以采取先天一味至真之气出来，以为丹本者此也。

However, young people, whose breath is strong and abundant, easily experience its true reality; but what are those already old to do, who find it hard to harmonize breath and blood, preserve the numinous light and gather this point of utmost yang essence? Let me teach you another method of gathering and refining. The Book of Changes says: “Still and unmoving, it is stirred and then penetrates.” Before the original breath has appeared, you may as well let the spirit-light shine downward, using this spirit-fire to stir the “metal” (the Prior Heaven true yang) sunk in the watery storehouse (the lower elixir field); in time, fire will naturally come forth in the water, and the true metal will come out of the ore! Stir here, and it responds there, faster than shadow following form or echo answering sound — so the ancients, teaching later students that in stillness and non-movement there is nothing to gather, gave them the method of the spirit-light shining down, beginning from the “penetrating” (stirred and then penetrating), to gather the single, utmost true breath of Prior Heaven as the root of the elixir — this is what they meant.

§10
Original (Chinese)

亦非此个动气即元气也。要知此个元气方其未形之时，未尝不在，然而清空之气不可见也。及其既形之际，又非此个有形者即是真一之气，而要不过此真一之气之所发所也。当其发时恍惚杳冥，略有所以认识者，在此亦犹见影知形之意，其实形无所见耳。到此发见昭著，放之则弥六合，即天地亦不能载，所谓生天、生地、生人、生物之本者即此是也。然虽无量无边而仍不离方寸，所谓卷之则退藏于密者是其义矣！由此以思，絪缊者仍是阴阳真气，而主宰此真气者始是至真之元气也。

But it is not that this breath which has been stirred up is the original breath. You must know that this original breath, before it has traces of form, is not absent; it is only that clear, empty breath cannot be seen; when it has traces of form, it is not that this thing with form is the breath of the true One, but only the place from which the breath of the true One issues. When it stirs, dim and indistinct, deep and obscure, there is a slight point by which it can be recognized, as seeing a shadow one knows there is a form, though the form itself is not seen. When it appears clearly, released it fills the six directions (Heaven, Earth and the four quarters), and even Heaven and Earth cannot contain it — what is called the root of the birth of Heaven, Earth, people and things is this. Yet though it is measureless and boundless, it is still not apart from the square inch (the mind); what is called “rolled up, it withdraws into secrecy” means just this! Thinking of it this way, that intermingling breath is still the true breath of yin and yang; what presides over this true breath is the utmost true original breath.

Qiu Ling's noteon §6–§10
Original note (Chinese)

先天虚无一气无声无臭，亦无形色，如何觅寻？惟用神光下照之法，即回光返照可以寻得。此气一到，忽有浩浩渊渊刚健中正之象，与后天之气不同。后天之气但见其暖，不见逍遥自在之处。真一之气一来，使我身苏软如绵，美快无比，恍惚似有所见，又似无可象，只觉清净恬淡，不知有天地人我，是为明验。

上文谈到过炼元精以化之气，精已化气，不再有生精之时。我用真意引元气自会阴穴起从背后直上泥丸，再从前面降下丹田，使气入神中，神包气外，久之浑然无气息往来，而胎息绵绵，惟觉一点灵光隐约在灵台之上，则元气已化为元神。如此炼气化神，为大周天火候。张紫阳云：“终日绵绵如醉汉，悠悠只守洞中春”，即炼气化神之态。

The Prior Heaven one breath of emptiness has no sound or scent, nor form or color — how is it to be found? Only by the method of the spirit-light shining down, that is, reverting the light to shine within, can it be found. When this breath arrives, there is suddenly a scene vast and deep, firm and strong, central and upright, different from the Posterior Heaven breath. The Posterior Heaven breath feels only warm, without that free and easy quality; when the breath of the true One comes, it makes my whole body soft as cotton, delightful beyond compare, dim and indistinct, as if seeing something, yet as if there were nothing to describe, feeling only pure, clear and tranquil, not knowing Heaven, Earth, others or self — this is the clear proof.

It was mentioned above that the original essence is refined and transformed into breath; once the essence has transformed into breath, there is no longer any time for producing essence. I use the true intention to guide the original breath, starting from the perineum point, straight up the back to the Muddy Pellet, then down the front to the elixir field, so that the breath enters the spirit and the spirit wraps the breath; in time, undividedly, there is no coming and going of breath, while embryonic breathing is continuous, and one feels only a point of numinous light dimly above the numinous terrace (the heart) — this is the original breath having transformed into the Original Spirit. Refining breath into spirit like this is the fire phasing of the Great Celestial Circuit. Zhang Ziyang says: “All day long, soft as a drunkard, leisurely guarding only the spring within the cave.” This is the state of refining breath into spirit.

Key Terms

  • Prior Heaven breath, Posterior Heaven breath: the Prior Heaven breath is the original breath (the breath of the Supreme Ultimate), the Posterior Heaven breath is the breath of breathing; see Prior Heaven and Posterior Heaven.
  • Breathing is the doorway of the original breath: first there is the original breath, then the openings of breathing open.
  • Strings and pipes and their sound, orchid and cassia and their fragrance: the Prior Heaven is the substance, the Posterior Heaven its expression.
  • Corpse breath: the breath of the Posterior Heaven body of flesh.
  • Embryo of immortality: the holy embryo; see Holy Embryo.
  • Central yellow palace, central palace: in this book, the lower elixir field.
  • Every breath returning to the root: every breath returning to the place of the root; see concentrating the spirit in the Cavity of Breath.
  • Metal, water: metal is the Prior Heaven true yang, water the essence-breath of the kidneys.
  • Cooking sand to make rice: a metaphor for futile effort.
  • The mind like a lively spring, the body like a steep rock: the one verifiable sign of the arrival of the Prior Heaven original breath.
  • The one breath of emptiness: also called the breath of the true One, the essence of the true One, the natural original breath, the one breath of the empty sky; see One Breath of Prior Heaven.
  • Dharma-body: the one breath of emptiness hidden in the physical body; see spontaneous Dharma Body.
  • Removing desire and preserving principle, shutting out the deviant and returning to the upright: removing selfish desire and keeping the principle of Heaven, shutting out deviant thoughts and returning to right thought.
  • Still and unmoving, it is stirred and then penetrates: from the “Xici” (Appended Statements), part one, of the Book of Changes.
  • Watery storehouse: the lower elixir field.
  • The six directions: Heaven, Earth and the four quarters.
  • Refining breath into spirit: the second step of the three transmutations; see the three transmutations.
  • Numinous terrace: the heart.

Putting It into Practice

  1. First nourish the Posterior Heaven, then speak of the Prior Heaven. If the body is weak and the breathing disordered, the Prior Heaven cannot be seen. Regularity in eating, drinking and daily life, neither overworking nor overresting, is itself refining the breath.
  2. Regulate the breath while walking, standing, sitting and lying: whether walking, standing, sitting or lying, let the breathing be even, smooth and natural — neither adding, nor subtracting, nor letting it run loose. Do not stir the mind, do not stir the breath.
  3. Do not try to picture it: when the Prior Heaven breath arrives, the mind is simply like a lively spring and the body like a rock, the whole body at ease and harmonious. Whatever can be clearly described or imagined as an image is still Posterior Heaven.
  4. Do not linger when it is clear, do not fret when it is turbid: when the sitting goes well, do not cling to it; when the sitting is dull, do not be vexed — the original breath is always there; it is vexation and lingering that are the floating clouds.
  5. For older people: first let the spirit-light gently shine down on the lower elixir field and wait for it to be “stirred and then penetrate”; do not hurry to look for breath sensations.
  6. Safety note: “using the true intention to guide the original breath from the perineum point straight up the back to the Muddy Pellet” in Qiu Ling’s note is work for after the essence has transformed into breath and the true breath-energy is ample. Beginners should not forcibly lead the breath up the spine to the head with the mind; if a swollen feeling in the head, headache, a flushed face or palpitations appear, stop and return to natural relaxation.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text comes in two places: the first consists of two paragraphs (§1–§5), followed by a note by Qiu Ling; the second is one paragraph (§6–§10), followed by a note by Qiu Ling.
  2. “Posterior Heaven corpse breath” (后天尸气): so in two places in the original book; “corpse breath” refers to the breath of the Posterior Heaven body of flesh; compare Chapter Two, section four: “the human body without water and fire is no more than a corpse”.
  3. “Like the sun-cinnabar shining down” (如日丹之照临): 日丹 is probably a mistake for 日月, “sun and moon” (the following text has “the sun and moon lose their light”); the translation follows “sun and moon”.
  4. “Gloom and vexation” (忧菀烦恼): 菀 stands for 郁, “gloom”.
  5. “The place from which the breath of the true One issues” (此真一之气之所发所也): so in the original book; the second 所 is probably 处, “place”; the translation renders “the place from which it issues”.
  6. “Released, it fills the six directions; rolled up, it withdraws into secrecy”: these two lines are Cheng Yi’s words on the Doctrine of the Mean, adapted by Huang Yuanji.
  7. “I desire humaneness, and humaneness arrives”: Analects, “Shu er”.
