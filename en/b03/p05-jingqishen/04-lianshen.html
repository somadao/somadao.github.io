Chapter Five · Essence, Breath and Spirit · 炼神

Refining the Spirit

Refining the spirit — what acts with purpose is the cognitive spirit, what acts without purpose the Original Spirit; the Original Spirit is the master mechanism of cultivating the elixir; still and unmoving, it is stirred and then penetrates, and that sudden leap of movement is the true Original Spirit

About This Chapter

This section speaks of “spirit”: the Original Spirit and the cognitive spirit, and the master key of cultivating the elixir — the Original Spirit.

I. The Original Spirit and the cognitive spirit (§1–§2) 1. Spirit is the awareness in the mind. The Prior Heaven spirit is the Original Spirit, and spirit is the nature: when a child is small it knows to love, when a little older it knows to respect — the “knowing” is spirit, the “love” is nature, and the two are originally undivided. Later, veiled by selfish desire, the spirit loses its beginning, and what it expresses is often contrary to the nature — this is the Posterior Heaven spirit. 2. Contrast: the Prior Heaven spirit is still, whole and bright, one with the nature; the Posterior Heaven spirit is moving, deficient and dark, apart from the nature. Cultivating the nature is nourishing this Prior Heaven spirit. 3. What acts with purpose is the cognitive spirit; what acts without purpose is the Original Spirit. When the cognitive spirit is in charge, the Original Spirit steps back and obeys; when the Original Spirit presides, the cognitive spirit is wholly transformed into the Original Spirit — they cannot stand side by side. 4. What the Original Spirit is like: like an empty valley answering a sound or a shadow following a form, aware naturally, knowing naturally, without the slightest arrangement — as Mencius says, on suddenly seeing a child about to fall into a well, everyone has a heart of alarm and compassion. What is intentional belongs to the cognitive spirit; what is without intention belongs to the Original Spirit.

II. The Original Spirit is the master mechanism of cultivating the elixir (§3–§6) 5. When the medicine is born without the Original Spirit, it is ordinary essence and cannot form an embryo; when the elixir returns without the Original Spirit, it is an illusion and cannot form an infant. What is called “cooking and refining the yang spirit” is gathering this Original Spirit and accumulating it day by day and month by month. 6. The Original Spirit is the Limitless becoming the Supreme Ultimate: in empty stillness with nothing to do, undivided and whole, suddenly something is stirred and leaps into movement — the moment of this leap is the true Original Spirit. 7. Three minds: anticipating before anything stirs is the future mind; recalling after it has stirred is the past mind; calculating a little while responding to affairs, unable to respond and forget, is the present mind. Suddenly stirred and suddenly responding, suddenly responding and suddenly stopping — only this is the working of the Original Spirit. 8. The Mysterious Pass, the Mysterious Female and the Original Spirit: the single opening of the Mysterious Pass is this yang spirit, stirred by chance; the gate of the Mysterious Female is when, after yin and yang unite, the breath of the one origin first shows signs, diffuse and mingling; the Original Spirit is what is numinous and aware within that diffuse, lively breath — two, yet truly one. 9. The Original Spirit is the true intention: in stillness it is the master, in movement it is the master, and in gathering the medicine it is what operates. Throughout the twelve double-hours of the day, it is always moving and always aware, always responding and always still.

Qiu Ling’s note is rather long: it introduces the descriptions of “the emergence of the yang spirit” in alchemical books (quoting the True Words of Chanting the Way), and points out that Huang Yuanji’s works do not teach the method of the emergence of the yang spirit; everything depends on refining the mind in ordinary times — only if the ground of the mind is empty and bright is the yang spirit pure; otherwise the spirit that emerges is mixed, frightened at the sight of anything fearful, greedy at the sight of anything desirable, and may linger without returning and fall into demonic paths.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

神者心中之知觉也。以其灵明故谓之神。而神有先后天之分。先天神元神也。神即性也。盖神为心中之知觉，而性即心中至善之理，其始浑于一元。有生之初，知觉从性分而出，如孩提知爱，稍长知敬，知即神，爱即性也。见神即以见性，神与性未尝分也，是以为先天之元神。性原不在神外也，自蔽于私欲，而神失其初矣，性亦为神所蔽矣，神之所发，常与性反，此为后天之神，盖先其天而配于后焉者也。先天之神静，后天之神动。先天之神完，后天之神亏。先天之神明，后天之神昏。先天之神神与性合，后天之神神与性离。道之修性去其蔽性之私，绝其梏性之欲，寂之又寂，归于至静；洗其心于至清，涤其虑于至静，所以有清静因也。所谓修性者即以养此先天之神而已。

Spirit is the awareness in the mind; because it is numinous and bright, it is called spirit. Spirit too is divided into Prior Heaven and Posterior Heaven. The Prior Heaven spirit is the Original Spirit; spirit is also the nature. For spirit is the awareness in the mind, and the nature is the utmost good principle in the mind; at the beginning the two are undividedly one. When a person is first born, awareness separates out from the nature — for example, a small child knows to love its parents, and when a little older knows to respect its elder brother: the “knowing” is spirit, the “love” is nature. Seeing the spirit, one sees the nature as well; spirit and nature have never been apart, so it is called the Prior Heaven Original Spirit. The nature is originally not outside the spirit; ever since it was veiled by selfish desire, the spirit lost its beginning, the nature was veiled by the spirit, and what the spirit expresses is often contrary to the nature — this is the Posterior Heaven spirit, because it arises after the Prior Heaven, matched with the Posterior Heaven. The Prior Heaven spirit is still, the Posterior Heaven spirit moving; the Prior Heaven spirit is whole, the Posterior Heaven spirit deficient; the Prior Heaven spirit is bright, the Posterior Heaven spirit dark; the Prior Heaven spirit is one with the nature, the Posterior Heaven spirit apart from the nature. The Daoist cultivation of the nature is removing the selfishness that veils the nature and cutting off the desires that bind it, silent and silent again, returning to utmost stillness; washing the mind in utmost clarity, cleansing thoughts in utmost stillness — hence the expression “clarity and stillness”. What is called cultivating the nature is simply nourishing this Prior Heaven spirit.

§2
Original (Chinese)

有为而为者识神也，无为而为者元神也。识神用事，元神退听。元神作主，识神悉化为元神。此理欲之关不容并立也。尤要知元神无迹，元气中之至灵处即元神也。然必如谷之应声，影之随形，自然而觉，自然而知，不假一毫安排，无容一丝拟议，如孟子谓乍见孺子将入于井，皆有怵惕恻隐之心，是元神也。由此推之，视听言动，日用事为，无在不有元神作用。但有意者属识神，无心者属元神，能以元神为主返入虚无境地，欲一则一，欲万则万，神通无外，法力无边，岂但入水不溺、入火不焚者哉！

What acts with purpose is the cognitive spirit; what acts without purpose is the Original Spirit. When the cognitive spirit is in charge, the Original Spirit steps aside and obeys; when the Original Spirit presides, the cognitive spirit is wholly transformed into the Original Spirit. This is the pass between the principle of Heaven and human desire, and the two cannot stand side by side. Above all, know this: the Original Spirit has no traces of form; the most numinous point within the original breath is the Original Spirit. It must be like a valley answering a sound or a shadow following a form, aware naturally and knowing naturally, without borrowing the slightest arrangement, without allowing the slightest calculation — as Mencius says, on suddenly seeing a child about to fall into a well, everyone feels a heart of alarm and compassion — this is the Original Spirit. Reasoning from this, seeing, hearing, speaking and moving, all daily affairs — there is nowhere the Original Spirit is not at work. Only: what is intentional belongs to the cognitive spirit; what is without intention belongs to the Original Spirit. If one can take the Original Spirit as master and return to the realm of emptiness, wanting one, there is one; wanting ten thousand, there are ten thousand; spiritual powers with nothing outside them, Dharma-power without limit — far more than merely not drowning in water and not burning in fire!

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§2
Original note (Chinese)

先天之神为元神，后天之神为识神，二者不容并立。混混沌沌中忽有一点灵光发现，此即为我之元神。我若认得元神，由元神作为我身的主宰，就不会为外来事物所纷扰。勿令我之灵阳被物牵引而去，即炼我之元神。张三丰说，人能以清净为体，镇定为基，天心为主，元神为用，巧使盗机，返还造化，何患功之不成。

The Prior Heaven spirit is the Original Spirit, the Posterior Heaven spirit is the cognitive spirit, and the two cannot stand side by side. In the midst of chaos and undifferentiation, suddenly a point of numinous light appears — this is my Original Spirit. If I recognize the Original Spirit and let it be the master of my body, I will not be disturbed by things coming from outside. Not letting my numinous clarity be drawn away by outer things is refining my Original Spirit. Zhang Sanfeng said: if a person can take clarity and stillness as substance, calm and settledness as foundation, the heart of Heaven as master and the Original Spirit as function, and skillfully use the method of stealing the vital spring of Heaven and Earth to return to creation, why worry that the work will not succeed?

§3
Original (Chinese)

元神者修丹之总机括也。药生无此元神，是为凡精无用，不能结胎。还丹无此元神，是为幻相不能成婴。吾窃怪世之修士徒知精气为宝，不知元神为主，总说成药亦不过保固色身而已，乌能成圣胎哉！吾今为生道破，夫所谓烹炼阳神者，即此元神，采而服之，日积月累，日充月盛而成之者也。不然，何不曰阳精、阳气而必曰阳神哉！可知炼丹者即炼此元神一味为之主也。然此是上上乘法以成金液大还之丹者，若中下两品，虽不全用阳神，亦却离不得阳神。若无阳神，凡精凡气亦不能凝结于身心。若最上乘法纯是阳神一件，虽不离精气二者，然不过为之补助而已。

The Original Spirit is the master mechanism, the master pivot, of cultivating the elixir. When the medicine is born without this Original Spirit, it is useless ordinary essence and cannot form an embryo; when the elixir returns without this Original Spirit, it is an illusion and cannot form an infant. I have privately found it strange that practitioners in the world know only that essence and breath are treasures, and do not know that the Original Spirit is the master; even if they say they have formed the medicine, it only serves to preserve the physical body — how could it form the Holy Embryo! Today I tell you plainly: what is called “cooking and refining the yang spirit” is this Original Spirit, gathered and taken in, accumulated day by day and month by month, growing fuller day by day and more abundant month by month. Otherwise, why is it not called yang essence or yang breath, but necessarily yang spirit? We can see that refining the elixir takes the refining of this one ingredient, the Original Spirit, as the main thing. However, this is the supreme vehicle, used to accomplish the great returning elixir of golden fluid; the methods of the middle and lower grades, though they do not use the yang spirit entirely, cannot do without it either — without the yang spirit, even ordinary essence and ordinary breath cannot condense in body and mind. The supreme vehicle is purely and solely the yang spirit; though it cannot do without essence and breath either, essence and breath are only auxiliaries.

§4
Original (Chinese)

生须要认得无神清楚以后才有作用。夫元神即无极而太极也。当其虚静无事，浑浑沦沦，无可名状，及忽焉感孚，跃然而动。此跃然一动之际即是真正元神。《易》曰寂然不动，感而遂通天下之故是也。若未动时先存逆料，是未来心。若已动后犹怀追忆，是过去心。忽感忽应，忽应忽止，是即元神作用，其中稍有计较，不能随应随忘，是谓现在心。若不明元神，由此采取即带浊秽，即使养成，难以飞腾变化，去来自如。

You must first recognize the Original Spirit clearly; only then will it have a function. The Original Spirit is “the Limitless, and then the Supreme Ultimate”: when it is empty and still with nothing to do, undivided and whole, indescribable, suddenly something is stirred and it leaps into movement — the moment of this leap of movement is the true Original Spirit, which is what the Book of Changes calls “still and unmoving, it is stirred and then penetrates all the affairs under Heaven”. If one anticipates before the affair has come, this is the “future mind”; if one keeps recalling after the affair has passed, this is the “past mind”; suddenly stirred and suddenly responding, suddenly responding and suddenly stopping — this is the working of the Original Spirit; if there is the slightest calculation in it, unable to respond and forget, this is called the “present mind”. If one does not understand the Original Spirit and gathers from here, one carries turbidity and filth; even if it is nourished to completion, it will hardly be able to soar and transform, coming and going at will.

§5
Original (Chinese)

吾今略为抉破，生好好用功以行采取焉。然微乎微乎！非上根法器，加以学问优，见识到，则不可语此也。又云，玄关一窍即此偶然感动之阳神。又云，玄牝之门亦此阳神之触发。然有分别：玄牝之门是阴阳交媾之后，一元之气絪絪缊缊，始有朕兆；若元神则是氤氲活泼之气中灵而觉者是。虽然是二，究竟一也。故老子曰：“谷神不死，是谓玄牝”。炼丹无此阳神，其所汩没者大矣！

Today I have laid it bare for you a little; you must work hard to gather it. Yet how subtle, how subtle! Unless a person is of the highest capacity, with good learning and deep insight besides, one cannot speak of this to him. It is also said: the single opening of the Mysterious Pass is this yang spirit stirred by chance; the gate of the Mysterious Female is also the triggering of this yang spirit. But there is a distinction: the gate of the Mysterious Female is when, after yin and yang unite, the breath of the one origin, intermingling and diffuse, first shows signs; while the Original Spirit is what is numinous, bright and aware within that diffuse, lively breath. Though spoken of as two, they are in the end one. So Laozi says: “The spirit of the valley does not die; it is called the Mysterious Female.” Refining the elixir without this yang spirit, too much would be buried!

§6
Original (Chinese)

虽然此元神者亦即清清净净、无杂无染、一心一德之真意也。其静也元神主之；其动也元神主之；及其采而为药也，亦元神为之运用而转旋也。元神之用诚大矣哉！生善会之，切莫加一念生一意，一日十二时中常动常觉，常应常静，不怕他万感纷投，俱是此个元神作用，否则落于后天甲里，那一点灵光反隐而不见矣！

Even so, this Original Spirit is also the true intention, clear and pure, unmixed and unstained, with one mind and one virtue: in stillness the Original Spirit presides; in movement the Original Spirit presides; when it comes to gathering the medicine, it is also the Original Spirit that operates and manages. How great is the working of the Original Spirit! Understand it well: never add one more thought or give rise to one more intention; throughout the twelve double-hours of the day, always moving and always aware, always responding and always still — never fear the ten thousand contacts pouring in one after another; all of it is this Original Spirit at work; otherwise one falls into the rut of the Posterior Heaven, and that point of numinous light is instead hidden from sight!

Qiu Ling's noteon §3–§6
Original note (Chinese)

烹炼阳神，为道家功法炼神还虚阶段的最高理想，即从泥丸炼出阳神。《仙佛合宗》、《唱道真言》、《道乡集》、《大成捷要》等丹经道书多有叙述。《唱道真言》云：“阳神之脱胎也，有光自脐轮外注，有香自鼻口中出，此脱胎之先兆也。既脱之后，则金光四射，毛窍晶融，如日之初升于海，珠之初见于渊，而香气氤氲满室矣！一声霹雳，金火交流，而阳神已出于泥丸矣！既出之后，全看平日功夫，吾所言炼心正为此际也。平日心地养得虚明，则阳神纯是先天灵气结成，本来无思无为，遇境不染，见物不迁，收纵去我，去来自如。一进泥丸，此身便如火热，金光复从毛窍间出，香气亦复氤氲，顷刻间返到黄庭，虽有如无，不知不觉，此真境也。若心地未能虚明，所结之胎决非圣胎，所成之神原带几分驳杂，犹人气禀昏浊，多以气质之性用事。其神虽出，一见可惧则怖生，一见可欲则爱生，殆将流连忘返，随入魔道，此身虽死，不知者以为得仙坐化，谁知阳神一出而不复者矣！”黄元吉氏著作则未及出阳神功法。

鹤臞子在《唱道真言》中谈到烹炼阳神全看平日功夫，即其所言炼心之说。平日心地养得虚明，则阳神方纯。黄氏于此所言亦即此意，认清元神，把它炼成我身动静之主宰，日积月累，日充月盛，则把此元神烹炼而成阳神。盖元神与阳神在本质上都是氤氲活泼气中之灵觉，元神乃阳神之前身，二而一也。

煅炼元神到无作无为、无思无虑、纯乎天然自然之极，而无迹象可寻，即是炼神还虚，亦可说此时是烹炼阳神。

Cooking and refining the yang spirit is the highest ideal of the stage of “refining spirit to return to emptiness” in Daoist practice, that is, refining out the yang spirit from the Muddy Pellet. Alchemical scriptures and Daoist books such as The Common Origin of Immortals and Buddhas, the True Words of Chanting the Way, the Collection of Daoxiang and the Essentials of Great Accomplishment give many accounts of it. The True Words of Chanting the Way says: “When the yang spirit leaves the embryo, light flows outward from the navel wheel and fragrance comes out of the mouth and nose — these are the omens of leaving the embryo. After it leaves the embryo, golden light shines in all directions, the pores are crystalline and penetrating, like the sun just rising over the sea, like a bright pearl just emerging from the abyss, and fragrance fills the room! With a clap of thunder, metal and fire flow together, and the yang spirit has already emerged from the Muddy Pellet! After it emerges, everything depends on the work of ordinary times — what I call refining the mind is precisely for this moment. If the ground of the mind has been nourished to be empty and bright in ordinary times, the yang spirit is formed purely of Prior Heaven numinous breath, originally without thought or action, unstained by circumstances, unmoved by things, gathered and released at my will, coming and going freely; once it returns to the Muddy Pellet, this body becomes hot as fire, golden light again comes out from the pores, fragrance again diffuses, and in an instant it returns to the Yellow Court, present as if absent, unknowing and unaware — this is the true realm. If the ground of the mind has not been nourished to be empty and bright, the embryo formed is certainly not the holy embryo, and the spirit formed is already somewhat mixed, like a person of dull, turbid endowment who acts mostly from the nature of temperament: though this spirit has emerged, at the sight of anything fearful it is terrified, at the sight of anything lovable it is greedy, and it may well linger without returning and fall into demonic paths; though this body has died, those who do not know think he has become an immortal and passed away in meditation — who knows that the yang spirit, once out, could not come back!” Huang Yuanji’s works do not speak of the method of the emergence of the yang spirit.

What Heququzi says in the True Words of Chanting the Way — that cooking and refining the yang spirit depends entirely on the work of ordinary times — is what he calls “refining the mind”: only if the ground of the mind is nourished to be empty and bright in ordinary times is the yang spirit pure. What Huang says here has the same meaning: recognize the Original Spirit clearly, refine it into the master of my body’s movement and stillness, accumulate it day by day and month by month, growing fuller and more abundant, and this Original Spirit is cooked and refined into the yang spirit. The Original Spirit and the yang spirit are in essence both that point of numinous awareness within the diffuse, lively breath; the Original Spirit is the forerunner of the yang spirit — two, yet one.

Refining the Original Spirit to the extreme of non-doing and non-acting, without thought or worry, purely natural and spontaneous, with no traces to be found, is “refining spirit to return to emptiness”; one may also say that this is when the yang spirit is being cooked and refined.

Key Terms

  • Original Spirit: the Prior Heaven spirit, one with the nature, still, whole and bright; see Original Spirit.
  • Cognitive spirit: the Posterior Heaven spirit, acting with purpose, apart from the nature.
  • Knowing to love, knowing to respect: Mencius, “Jinxin I”: “There is no small child who does not know to love its parents, and when grown, none who does not know to respect its elder brother.”
  • Clarity and stillness: washing the mind in utmost clarity, cleansing thoughts in utmost stillness.
  • Suddenly seeing a child about to fall into a well: Mencius, “Gongsun Chou I”, a metaphor for the Original Spirit arising naturally.
  • Master mechanism: the master switch, the master pivot.
  • Yang spirit: formed by cooking and refining the Original Spirit; see Holy Embryo and sending out the spirit.
  • The great returning elixir of golden fluid: the elixir accomplished by the supreme vehicle.
  • The Limitless, and then the Supreme Ultimate: the moment of a sudden leap of movement in empty stillness, that is, the Original Spirit.
  • Future mind, past mind, present mind: anticipating, recalling and calculating — none of these is the Original Spirit.
  • Jiali: a term in the original book, roughly meaning a rut or trap.
  • Refining spirit to return to emptiness: the last step of the three transmutations; see the three transmutations.
  • True Words of Chanting the Way: an alchemical book transmitted by Heququzi of the Qing dynasty, already seen in Chapter One.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Distinguish the Original Spirit from the cognitive spirit: what is intentional and calculating is the cognitive spirit; what is without intention and naturally aware is the Original Spirit.
  2. Respond and forget: when an affair comes, respond; when it has passed, stop — no anticipating, no recalling, no calculating. This is refining the spirit in daily life.
  3. The Original Spirit is not apart from the true intention: in stillness it guards, in movement it gathers; beyond this, add not a thought or an intention.
  4. On the emergence of the yang spirit: this belongs to a very advanced stage, and Huang Yuanji’s works do not teach the concrete method. On this site it is for understanding only; do not try practices such as “sending out the spirit” on your own; Qiu Ling, quoting the True Words of Chanting the Way, also stresses that if the ground of the mind is not empty and bright, sending out the spirit carries the danger of falling into demonic paths.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text comes in two places (each followed by a note by Qiu Ling in the original book); the original book does not give the source. The first place has two paragraphs; the second has two paragraphs, the first of which is divided into three short passages.
  2. “You students must recognize the ‘no-spirit’ clearly” (生须要认得无神清楚): 无神 should be a mistake for 元神, “Original Spirit”; the translation follows 元神.
  3. “Otherwise one falls into the Posterior Heaven jiali” (否则落于后天甲里): jiali is so in the original book, roughly meaning a rut; probably a misprint.
  4. “Hence there is the cause of clarity and stillness” (所以有清静因也): so in the original book, meaning “hence the expression ‘clarity and stillness’”.
  5. “Not letting my numinous yang be drawn away by things” (勿令我之灵阳被物牵引而去): the original book has 灵阳 (“numinous yang”), recorded as is; it means the numinous clarity of the mind.
