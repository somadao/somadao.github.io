Chapter Five · Essence, Breath and Spirit · 三件功夫

The Three Stages of Work

The three stages of work — refining essence into breath, refining breath into spirit, refining spirit to return to emptiness can all be done and reached in one sitting; do not get off the seat before reaching great stillness

About This Chapter

This is the last section of Chapter Five. Huang Yuanji combines the three stages of refining essence into breath, refining breath into spirit and refining spirit to return to emptiness into “three stages of work” that can be completed in a single sitting, and sets them out in one continuous flow — this is the most concrete method of beginning in the chapter.

  1. Preparation: first concentrate the spirit, then regulate the breath. Once the spirit is concentrated, there is no need to seek a flood-like breath; it is enough that the mind is free of vexation and the intention free of attachment, the mind like an empty vessel, the intention like melted ice, the body standing unmoving like a mountain.
  2. First stage: refining essence into breath. Let the spirit-light shine down into the Cavity of Breath, silently watching the breath of the yinqiao (the perineum) and the breath of the Crimson Palace (the heart) meet in the elixir tripod; cook and refine finely with the gentle warmth of the spirit-fire, blow slowly with the faint Wind of Xun (breathing), and naturally the essence melts and the breath transforms. Sign: the breaths of heart and kidneys unite, the Crimson Palace is at ease, the kidneys are at peace, the outer breathing and the inner true breath merge into one breath, gathering without scattering, and true yang appears.
  3. Second stage: refining breath into spirit. Breathing joined, spirit and breath meeting and gathering in the elixir field, winding and flowing like a living dragon swimming. I simply concentrate the spirit within and pour the breath without, letting it be natural; stillness reaches its extreme and moves, movement reaches its extreme and becomes still.
  4. Third stage: refining spirit to return to emptiness. After long stillness and concentration, the flowing in the elixir field seems present and absent, unnoticed at a rough look, known only on fine attention. At this point dissolve the mind of awareness altogether, let spirit and breath stir, harmonize, warm and refine themselves, and simply reach emptiness and keep to silence. First a fragrant wind rushes upward and the hundred vessels are steamed (spirit giving birth to breath); then one feels one’s vitality growing and wisdom opening day by day, a breath rising from the compass center, crystalline like jade (breath giving birth to spirit). This is the very time to let go into emptiness, as if with mind, as if without intention.
  5. Two prohibitions: do not get off the seat when spirit and breath have met only roughly and great stillness has not been reached; do not leave the seat when spirit and breath have merged into one piece but the realm of emptiness, purity and naturalness has not yet been reached. One must also add returning to the furnace and sealing, in accord with the fullness and emptiness of Heaven and Earth and the atmosphere of the four seasons, to complete one full round.
  6. The River Chariot opens of itself: practicing like this, the true breath naturally rises to pay homage at the Muddy Pellet and flows down to the elixir field, passing through the hundred vessels and pervading the skin — the road of the River Chariot opens naturally, and I merely follow where it opens and guide it slightly.

Qiu Ling’s note: this fuses the three stages in one furnace and completes them in one flow; it is truly a workable shortcut; but without a certain foundation, the student will not find this method easy to practice well either.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

今特详下手之功，如打坐之时先凝神继调息。到得神已凝了，不必有浩然正气至大至刚立塞天地，只要心无烦恼意无牵挂，觉得心如空器一点不有，意若冰融片念不生，此身耸立恍如山岳，静镇不动不摇，由是以神光下照于气穴之中，默视吾阴跻之气与绛宫之气两相会于丹鼎之中，我即以温温神火细细烹炼，微微巽风缓缓吹嘘，自然精融气化。此即是炼精化气也。何以知其炼精化气哉？前此未采外来之气与吾心内之神两相配合，会成一家，此个坎离各自分散全不相依，呼吸亦不相调，到得收回外气以制内里阴精，气到之时阴精自化，上下心肾之气既合为一，自然绛宫安闲，肾府自在，外之呼吸与内之真息合而为一气，浑如夫妇配成，聚而不散，日充月盛，真阳从此现象矣！此即化气之明徵也。

Now let me speak in special detail of the work of beginning. For example, when sitting, first concentrate the spirit, then regulate the breath. When the spirit is concentrated, there is no need to have the feeling of a flood-like, upright breath, utmost in greatness and firmness, filling Heaven and Earth; it is enough that the mind is free of vexation and the intention free of attachment, feeling the mind like an empty vessel with nothing at all in it, the intention like melted ice with not a thought arising, and the body standing tall like a mountain, quiet and settled, unmoving and unshaken. Then use the spirit-light to shine down into the Cavity of Breath (the lower elixir field), silently watching the breath of my yinqiao (the perineum) and the breath of the Crimson Palace (the heart) meet in the elixir tripod (the lower elixir field); I use the gentle warmth of the spirit-fire to cook and refine finely, the faint Wind of Xun (breathing) to blow slowly, and naturally the essence melts and the breath transforms — this is refining essence into breath. How do we know that this is refining essence into breath? Before this, the breath from outside had not yet been gathered to match and unite with the spirit in my mind into one family; Kan (the kidneys) and Li (the heart) were each scattered, not relying on each other at all, and the breathing was not harmonized; when the outer breath is drawn back to subdue the yin essence within, as soon as the breath arrives, the yin essence naturally transforms, and the breaths of heart and kidneys above and below unite into one; naturally the Crimson Palace is at ease and the kidneys at peace, the outer breathing and the inner true breath merge into one breath, undivided like husband and wife made a pair, gathering without scattering, growing fuller day by day and more abundant month by month, and from then on true yang appears — this is the clear proof of transforming into breath.

§2
Original (Chinese)

既已化气，再行向上之事。何谓向上之事？斯时呼吸合，神气交，凝聚丹田，宛转悠扬几如活龙游泳，一日有无数变化。我惟凝神于中，注息于外，听其天然，自然静极而动，动极而静。此即炼气化神也。

Once it has transformed into breath, do the work of going upward. What is the work of going upward? At this time the breathing is joined, spirit and breath meet, gathering in the elixir field, winding and flowing, almost like a living dragon swimming, with countless transformations in a day. I simply concentrate the spirit within it and pour the breath outside, letting it be natural; naturally stillness reaches its extreme and moves, movement reaches its extreme and becomes still — this is refining breath into spirit.

§3
Original (Chinese)

到得静定久久，我气益调。前此宛转流行于丹田者，此时烹炼极熟，觉得似有似无，若动若静，粗看不觉，细会始知。此际务将知觉之心一齐泯去，百想无存，万虑全消，即丹田交会之神气听它自鼓、自调、自温、自煅，我惟致虚守寂，纯任自然，神入气中而不知，气周神外而不觉。如此烹炼一阵，自有一阵香风上冲，百脉遍体薰蒸，此所谓神生气也。又觉精神日长，智慧日开，一心之内但觉一气从规中起，清净微妙，精莹如玉，此所谓气生神也。如此神气交养，两两相生，斯时正宜撒手成空，不粘不脱，若有心若无意。此炼神还虚之实际也。

When stillness and concentration have lasted long, my breath becomes even more harmonious. What was winding and flowing in the elixir field before is now cooked and refined to full ripeness, feeling as if present and absent, as if moving and still, unnoticed at a rough look, known only on fine attention. At this time one must dissolve the mind of awareness altogether, keeping not one of a hundred thoughts, all worries dissolved; the spirit and breath meeting in the elixir field are left to stir themselves, harmonize themselves, warm themselves and refine themselves; I simply reach emptiness and keep to silence, wholly following nature, the spirit entering the breath unawares, the breath circulating around the spirit unnoticed. After cooking and refining like this for a while, naturally a fragrant wind rushes upward, and the hundred vessels throughout the body are steamed — this is what is called “spirit giving birth to breath”. One also feels one’s vitality growing day by day and wisdom opening day by day, and feels in the mind only a breath rising from the compass center (the elixir field), pure, subtle and wondrous, crystalline like jade — this is what is called “breath giving birth to spirit”. With spirit and breath nourishing each other and giving birth to each other like this, this is the very time to let go into emptiness, neither clinging nor detached, as if with mind, as if without intention — this is the reality of refining spirit to return to emptiness.

§4
Original (Chinese)

此三件功夫一时可行可到，学人须遵道而行。不可但到神气粗交未至大静，即行下榻。又不可但到神气大交凝成一片两不分明，未到虚无、清净、自然之境，速离坐地。必须照此行持，从炼精起至于气长神旺，久久化为清净自然，再加归炉封固功法，然后合乎天地盈虚消息与一年春夏秋冬气象。如此始完全一周功夫。照此修持，自然我气益调，我神益静，中有无穷变化，不尽生机。由是日夜勤功，绵绵密密，寂照同归，自有真气薰蒸上朝泥丸，下流丹田，透百脉而贯肌肤，勃然有不可遏之状，此河车之路自然而通，我不过顺其所通而略为引之足矣！

These three stages of work can all be done in a single sitting, and the student must follow the right path in doing them. One must not get off the seat when spirit and breath have met only roughly and great stillness has not yet been reached; nor hurry to leave the seat when spirit and breath have met greatly and merged into one piece, indistinguishable, but the realm of emptiness, purity and naturalness has not yet been reached. One must practice like this: starting from refining the essence, until the breath grows and the spirit flourishes, transforming over time into purity and naturalness, then adding the method of returning to the furnace and sealing, in accord with the news of the fullness and emptiness, waning and waxing of Heaven and Earth, corresponding to the atmosphere of spring, summer, autumn and winter in a year — only so is one full round of the work completed. Practicing like this, naturally my breath grows more harmonious and my spirit more still, with endless transformations and inexhaustible vitality within. From then on work diligently day and night, continuous and dense, silence and illumination returning together as one, and naturally the true breath will steam, rising to pay homage at the Muddy Pellet, flowing down to the elixir field, passing through the hundred vessels and pervading the skin, surging with an irresistible force — this is the road of the River Chariot opening naturally, and I need only follow the direction in which it opens and guide it slightly!

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

黄氏于此将炼精化气、炼气化神、炼神还虚三个阶段，即他说的三件功夫，并在一起，融为一炉，一气呵成介绍给学者。正如他说的“此三件功夫一时可行可到”，“河车之路自然而通”，诚为一可行的练功捷径。黄氏又循循交代说，不可未到大静及虚无清净之境而即行下榻，以免如入宝山空手回，此亦久坐必有禅之旨。

但学人若无一定功底，此一气呵成之法亦不易练好。

Here Huang puts together the three stages of refining essence into breath, refining breath into spirit and refining spirit to return to emptiness — what he calls the “three stages of work” — fuses them in one furnace, and presents them to students in one continuous flow. As he says, “these three stages of work can all be done and reached in one sitting” and “the road of the River Chariot opens naturally”; it is truly a workable shortcut in practice. Huang also patiently instructs: one must not get off the seat before reaching great stillness and the realm of emptiness and purity, lest one enter a mountain of treasure and come back empty-handed — this is also the meaning of “sit long enough and there will surely be chan”.

But if the student does not have a certain foundation, this method of completing everything in one flow is not easy to practice well either.

Key Terms

  • The three stages of work: refining essence into breath, refining breath into spirit, refining spirit to return to emptiness; see the three transmutations.
  • The mind like an empty vessel, the intention like melted ice: the state of mind and intention once the spirit is concentrated.
  • Yinqiao: the perineum; see perineum.
  • Crimson Palace: the heart, the middle elixir field.
  • Elixir tripod: the lower elixir field.
  • Wind of Xun: breathing.
  • The outer breathing and the inner true breath merging into one: the clear proof of transforming into breath; see True Breath.
  • Concentrating the spirit within, pouring the breath without: the measure of effort in refining breath into spirit.
  • Spirit giving birth to breath, breath giving birth to spirit: spirit and breath nourishing each other and giving birth to each other.
  • Letting go into emptiness: putting down all use of intention, refining spirit to return to emptiness.
  • Compass center: the central, upright place on which the intention is fixed; here, the elixir field.
  • Getting off the seat: ending the sitting.
  • Returning to the furnace and sealing: gathering spirit and breath back into the elixir field and sealing them in.
  • Silence and illumination returning together: stillness and awakened illumination as one.
  • Sit long enough and there will surely be chan: sitting long enough and still enough, one naturally gains something.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The complete procedure of each sitting: concentrate the spirit → regulate the breath → let the spirit-light shine down on the elixir field, cooking and refining with gentle warmth (transforming into breath) → concentrate the spirit within and let it move or be still as it will (transforming into spirit) → dissolve awareness and let go into emptiness (returning to emptiness) → gather back into the elixir field and seal → get off the seat.
  2. Do not hurry to get off the seat: getting up before you are thoroughly still is like entering a mountain of treasure and coming back empty-handed.
  3. Do the closing: before ending, gather spirit and breath back into the elixir field, and stay quiet for a moment before getting off the seat.
  4. The River Chariot opens naturally: the true breath steaming and flowing above and below comes naturally when the work is ripe; I only “follow where it opens and guide it slightly”; do not pull and turn it forcibly with the mind.
  5. Do what you can (Qiu Ling): without a certain foundation, there is no need to force yourself to complete all three steps in one sitting; first make the first step solid.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is in four paragraphs; the original book does not give the source.
  2. “Blow slowly with the faint Wind of Xun” (微微巽风缓缓吹嘘): Xun is wind, and alchemists use the Wind of Xun as a metaphor for breathing.
  3. “This is the clear proof of transforming into breath” (此即化气之明徵也): 徵 is the same as 征, proof.
