Chapter Five · Essence, Breath and Spirit · 炼精

Refining the Essence

Refining the essence — the original essence has no form and lodges within spirit and breath; nourish the spirit in silence and the breath in stillness, and the essence has no way to leak; a cool, clear mind and sweet saliva filling the mouth are true signs of the production of the original essence

About This Chapter

This section answers a most basic question: in refining the essence, what essence is refined?

  1. Two kinds of essence: when ordinary people speak of essence, they think only of the essence of intercourse — that is the Posterior Heaven essence with form, not the original essence. The original essence has no form and lodges within spirit and breath, pervading the ears, eyes and all parts of the body, yet one cannot point out where it is; it is the quintessence formed by the brewing and flowing of the Original Spirit and the original breath.
  2. The key to preserving the essence does not lie in the essence itself: even the essence of intercourse is formless essence gradually turned into form and expelled when spirit and breath are stirred. So nourish the spirit in silence and the breath in stillness, and the essence naturally has no way to leak; once spirit and breath have stirred, wanting the essence alone not to leave cannot be done. There is nowhere to apply force to the essence; one simply works on spirit and breath.
  3. Producing essence: essence is born only after stillness. Shaking it by moving it around or by massage is wrong; Guangchengzi says “do not shake your essence, do not tire your form”. Feeling arises from what the eyes see; as soon as feeling arises, close the eyes — and the way of preserving the essence is complete. Concentrate the spirit and regulate the breath, above and below melting together, like the breaths of Heaven and Earth meeting to form rain, and the essence is born.
  4. The signs of the production of the original essence: in the midst of not knowing and not perceiving, suddenly the mind feels cool and clear, or sweet saliva fills the mouth — this is the original essence being produced. At this moment, gather it as soon as you notice it, refine it as soon as you draw it in, letting the wind of breathing go up and down — this is refining the essence.
  5. What true essence is: the sweet dew and numinous fluid in the mouth are still Posterior Heaven essence with form; the true original essence is that point of “clear, bright true knowing” when, in the midst of chaos, not knowing and not perceiving, suddenly there is awareness. Take this as the elixir head and the embryonic breath as the fire, burn and refine it slowly, and a diffuse, mingling breath will surge up in the elixir field — this is essence transforming into breath.

Qiu Ling’s note: essence includes the Prior Heaven original essence and the essence of intercourse; “refining the essence” refers to refining the Prior Heaven original essence. The original essence is in fact produced when stillness reaches its extreme and turns to movement — one awakening and it stirs at the extreme of stillness is the single breath in which the Mysterious Pass opens.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

修炼之家常以精与神气配说。至叩其何者为精？则茫无以应。即诸书亦有言精者，然而情词恍惚，并无确据，间有执交媾之精对者。至叩此精藏于何所？则又茫无以应、不知此特后天有形之精，非元精也。元精无形，即寓于神气之中，贯乎耳目百体而无可指。

Practitioners often speak of essence together with spirit and breath. But if you ask them what essence is, they are at a loss for an answer. Various books also speak of essence, but their words are vague and indistinct, without a firm basis; occasionally someone answers with the essence of intercourse. Ask further where this essence is stored, and again they are at a loss — not knowing that this is only the Posterior Heaven essence with form, not the original essence. The original essence has no form; it lodges within spirit and breath, pervading the ears, eyes and all the parts of the body, yet one cannot point out where it is.

§2
Original (Chinese)

夫精者粗之对也，如日者阳之精，月者阴之精。先天之神为离中之空阴，则元神即阴之精也。先天之气为坎中之空阳，则元气即阳之精也。又如髓者骨之精也，脂者肉之精也。而尤有贯乎髓与脂之内者。髓与脂乃流而不息，润而不枯，则所谓元精者即元神元气酝酿流行之精华也。脏腑配五行之气，阴阳寓焉，浊气为粗，清气为精，即所谓二五之精也。而坎离之神气即寓于其内。五官百骸皆元神之气之所统，亦即元精之所贯，则但言神气而不必言精也。即如交媾之精，则神与气感化通体，无形之精徐而成形以出者也。故养神于寂，养气于静，精无由泄矣！倘神与气交感而动，而独责精以不走，能乎不能？则所谓精者无可著力，惟加意于神气而已矣！

“Essence” (jing) is the opposite of “coarse”; it means quintessence, refined purity: the sun, for example, is the quintessence of yang, the moon the quintessence of yin. The Prior Heaven spirit is the one yin hidden in the middle of the trigram Li (☲), so the Original Spirit is the quintessence of yin; the Prior Heaven breath is the one yang hidden in the middle of the trigram Kan (☵), so the original breath is the quintessence of yang. Again, marrow is the quintessence of bone, fat the quintessence of flesh; and there is yet something that pervades marrow and fat — it is by this that marrow and fat flow without cease and moisten without drying. What is called the original essence is the quintessence formed by the brewing and flowing of the Original Spirit and the original breath. The five viscera and six bowels are matched with the breaths of the five phases, and yin and yang lodge within them: the turbid breath is the coarse, the clear breath the essence — this is what is called “the essence of the two and the five” (the quintessence of the two breaths of yin and yang and of the five phases). The spirit and breath of Kan and Li lodge within it. The five senses and hundred bones are all governed by the Original Spirit and original breath, which is also where the original essence pervades — so it is enough to speak of spirit and breath, and there is no need to speak separately of essence. Even the essence of intercourse is formless essence that, when spirit and breath stir and transform throughout the body, gradually turns into form and is expelled. So nourish the spirit in silence and the breath in stillness, and the essence has no way to leak! If spirit and breath have already been stirred by mutual contact, and one merely demands that the essence not leave, can it be done? So it is said: there is nowhere to apply force to the essence itself; one need only work on spirit and breath!

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§2
Original note (Chinese)

古书对精之概念多未能讲清，黄氏于此一语道破。盖精有先天元精与交媾之精，元精无形，寓于神气之中，为我凝炼所得，温养有方，便无渗漏而化为元气；交媾之精有形，乃后天之物，为感化所成，即泄漏之精。炼精乃指煅炼先天元精而言。

The ancient books mostly failed to explain the concept of “essence” clearly; here Huang settles it in one sentence. There are two kinds of essence: the Prior Heaven original essence and the essence of intercourse. The original essence has no form and lodges within spirit and breath; it is obtained by my concentrating and refining; when warmly nourished in the right way, it does not seep away but transforms into original breath. The essence of intercourse has form; it is a Posterior Heaven thing, formed by the stirring of desire, and it is the essence that leaks. “Refining the essence” refers to refining the Prior Heaven original essence.

§3
Original (Chinese)

炼精化气虽是下手初基，要知人无精则无气无神，亦犹灯之无油，则无火无光也。但云炼精而不知生精，又将何以为用哉？黄帝云：“精不足者补之以味”。后人解释有节饮食、薄滋味之说。又古人云：“精以静而后生。”术家以搬运按摩动摇其精误矣！广成子云：“毋摇尔精，毋劳尔形，毋俾尔思虑营营，乃可长生”。此可见保精之道又在乎身无摇动，心无杂妄矣！

Although refining essence into breath is the first basic step of beginning, you must know that without essence a person has no breath and no spirit, just as a lamp without oil has no fire and no light. If one only speaks of refining essence without knowing how to produce essence, what is there to use? The Yellow Emperor said: “Where essence is insufficient, replenish it with flavor.” (Where the essence is insufficient, nourish it with the flavors of food.) Later commentators also speak of moderation in eating and plainness of flavor. The ancients also said: “Essence is born only after stillness.” (Essence can only be produced in stillness.) The masters of techniques shake their essence by moving it around and by massage — this is wrong! Guangchengzi said: “Do not shake your essence, do not tire your form, do not let your thoughts be busy and agitated, and you can live long.” From this we can see that the way of preserving the essence also lies in the body not moving rashly and the mind being free of stray thoughts and delusions!

§4
Original (Chinese)

古人云：“精由情感而动”。情欲动而窒其情，情由目见而生，情一生而冥其目，保精之道于此完矣！人果能凝神调息于方寸，一心不散，一息不出，犹天之气下，地之气上，上下相融，自然成雨。精之生也又何异是？只怕心不静而息不调，上下不相混合，斯精所以日消也。至于心中灵液下降，则无形色可见。而泥丸阴精化为甘露，此有可以窥者，但要勤修炼耳！否则著有著无，皆耗精也。至于精已化气，则神气混合，心息相依，其身体内外泰然融然，有苏软如绵之意，此即气生之兆也。

The ancients said: “Essence stirs because of feeling.” (Essence stirs because of the touch of desire.) So as soon as desire stirs, stop that feeling; feeling arises from what the eyes see, so as soon as feeling arises, close the eyes — the method of preserving the essence is complete here! If a person can truly concentrate the spirit and regulate the breath within the square inch (the mind), the mind not scattering and the breath not racing outward, it is like the breath of Heaven descending and the breath of Earth rising, above and below melting together and naturally forming rain — how is the production of essence any different from this? The only fear is that the mind is not still and the breath not regulated, so that above and below cannot mix; this is why the essence is used up day by day. As for the numinous fluid of the heart descending, it has no form or color to be seen; but the yin essence of the Muddy Pellet (the crown) transforming into Sweet Dew can be perceived — one need only cultivate diligently! Otherwise, clinging to being or clinging to non-being, both use up the essence. When the essence has transformed into breath, spirit and breath mix, mind and breath rely on each other, the whole body inside and out is at peace and melting, with a feeling of softness like cotton — this is the sign of breath being born.

§5
Original (Chinese)

广成子曰：“毋摇尔精”。精即汞，汞即心中之灵液，元神之所依托者也。油干灯息，汞竭人亡，此机不可不慎也！所望诸子于无知无觉时，或忽焉心地清凉，或时而甘津满口，皆产元精之真验也。能于此觉之即取，收之即炼，鼓橐籥之风，一上一下，听其往来，即炼精，即前行短，二候采牟尼之法也。吾道最重者在此一刻间呼吸之息，不失其机，此玄关窍开水源至清之时也。从此一生一采，毛窍疏通，适有晶莹如玉之状，此即精化气时也。急忙采取，运行河车，切勿失其机焉！

Guangchengzi said: “Do not shake your essence.” The essence here is “mercury”, and mercury is the numinous fluid in the heart, on which the Original Spirit relies. When the oil runs dry the lamp goes out; when the mercury is exhausted the person dies — this key point must be treated with care! I hope that in the midst of not knowing and not perceiving, you will sometimes suddenly feel the mind cool and clear, or at times find sweet saliva filling your mouth — these are all true signs of the production of the original essence. If at this moment you can gather it as soon as you notice it and refine it as soon as you draw it in, working the wind of the “bellows” (a metaphor for breathing), up and down, letting it come and go — this is refining the essence, and also the method of “the forward course is short”, “gathering the muni in two stages”. What we Daoists value most lies in the breath of breathing in this one moment, not losing its timing — this is the time when the Mysterious Pass opens and “the source of the water is utterly clear”. From then on, as soon as it is born, gather it, and the pores of the whole body open up, with just a crystalline, jade-like state; this is the time of essence transforming into breath. Gather it quickly, turn the River Chariot, and never miss the moment!

§6
Original (Chinese)

灵液滋生，口有甘露，俱是后天有形之精，算不得真精。惟精明之精庶几近道。然精生有时，知真时者便知真精。究竟精生之时在人为何时哉？盖精者，其静而寂寂也则为先天之元气，及静养久久忽焉而有动机，此即鸿蒙未判将判之时，元气已有动机。元气之动，即静为元气，动化元精。此时之精非交感之物事也，亦非有形之精周身踊跃也。必从混混沌沌中，无知无觉时忽焉有知觉，是元精化生也，又谓真知灵知也。总之，元精无形，惟此万念齐捐，一灵独运，炯然朗抱，浑然而知，即为精生，即为水源至清。

The numinous fluid arising and sweet dew in the mouth are all Posterior Heaven essence with form, and do not count as true essence; only the “essence of clear brightness” (that point of clear, bright awareness) is close to the Way. Yet the production of essence has its proper time; know the true time, and you know the true essence. When, then, is the time of the production of essence in the human body? Essence, when it is still and silent, is the Prior Heaven original breath; when it has been nourished in stillness for a long time and suddenly there is a stirring — this is the time when the primal chaos is not yet divided and about to divide, and the original breath already has a stirring. When the original breath stirs, it is this: in stillness it is the original breath, in movement it transforms into the original essence. Essence at this time is not the thing of intercourse, nor essence with form surging through the body. It must be that, out of chaos and undifferentiation, in the midst of not knowing and not perceiving, suddenly there is awareness — this is the original essence being born, also called “true knowing” or “numinous knowing”. In short, the original essence has no form; it is only at the time when all thoughts are abandoned, one numinous point moves alone, shining brightly, knowing undividedly — that is the birth of essence, that is “the source of the water is utterly clear”.

§7
Original (Chinese)

从此一念不纷，即以此个真意主宰，督精为丹头，又以一呼一吸之胎息为火，以慢慢的呼吸神火，烧灼此个元精于丹田之中，久之，火力到时，即变化生焉。何也？精生无形，不过一个精明之真知，只一心无两念，从此以神主宰，以息吹嘘，不久丹田中忽有一股氤氲之气，蓬勃之机，从下元涌起，渐渐至于身体，始犹似有似无，不大有力，孟子谓平旦之气是。久则油然心安，浩然气畅，至大至刚，有充塞天地之状。自亦不知此气从何而始，从何而终，此即精化气时也。是气也虽有形可知可见，然元精元气分之则二，合之仍一。以其动言之则为精，以其静言之则为气。

From then on, with not a single thought in disorder, use this True Intention to preside, take the essence as the elixir head (the root of refining the elixir), and take the embryonic breathing of each breath in and out as the fire; with the slow fire of the spirit in breathing, burn and refine this original essence in the elixir field; in time, when the fire’s strength is sufficient, a transformation will occur. Why? When essence is born it has no form; it is only a clear, bright true knowing, only one mind and no second thought; from then on, with the spirit presiding and the breath blowing, before long a diffuse, mingling breath, a surging impulse, suddenly rises from the lower origin in the elixir field and gradually reaches the whole body; at first it seems present and absent, not very strong — this is what Mencius calls “the breath of the early morning” (the clear breath of early morning before contact with things); in time, the mind is naturally at ease, the breath flows vast and free, utmost in greatness and firmness, as if filling Heaven and Earth. One does not know where this breath begins or ends — this is the time of essence transforming into breath. Although this breath has form and can be known and seen, the original essence and the original breath, spoken of separately, are two, and taken together are still one: from its moving side it is called essence; from its still side it is called breath.

Qiu Ling's noteon §3–§7
Original note (Chinese)

虽说精从静生，但元精（即真精、真种）实从静极而动中产。静极一觉而动之时，即玄关窍开一息之顷，我应不失时机以真意主宰，煅炼元精于丹田，时机成熟，即运行河车炼精化气。

前文已介绍了玄关窍开种种景象，黄氏于此处又指出了心中灵液下降（即心地清凉）和甘露满口两景，作为元精生产之真验，学者宜证验之。

Although it is said that essence is born from stillness, the original essence (that is, the true essence, the true seed) is in fact produced when stillness reaches its extreme and turns to movement. The moment of one awakening and it stirs at the extreme of stillness is the single breath in which the Mysterious Pass opens; I should not miss the moment, but let the true intention preside and forge the original essence in the elixir field; when the time is ripe, turn the River Chariot and refine essence into breath.

The various scenes of the opening of the Mysterious Pass have already been introduced above; here Huang further points out two scenes, “the numinous fluid of the heart descending” (that is, the mind feeling cool and clear) and “sweet dew filling the mouth”, as true signs of the production of the original essence, which students should verify for themselves.

Key Terms

  • Original essence: formless essence lodging within spirit and breath, the quintessence formed by the brewing and flowing of the Original Spirit and original breath; see Essence.
  • Essence of intercourse: the Posterior Heaven essence with form, formed by the stirring of desire.
  • The yin within Li, the yang within Kan: the one yin in the middle of the trigram Li ☲, the one yang in the middle of Kan ☵; the Original Spirit is the quintessence of yin, the original breath the quintessence of yang.
  • The essence of the two and the five: the quintessence of the two breaths of yin and yang and of the five phases.
  • Where essence is insufficient, replenish it with flavor: from the Inner Classic of the Yellow Emperor, Suwen.
  • Essence is born only after stillness: essence is produced in stillness.
  • Essence stirs because of feeling: essence stirs because of the touch of desire.
  • Numinous fluid: the fluid of the heart; when it descends, the mind feels cool and clear.
  • Sweet Dew: transformed from the yin essence of the Muddy Pellet; sweet saliva filling the mouth.
  • Mercury: here, the numinous fluid in the heart, on which the Original Spirit relies; see Lead and Mercury.
  • Bellows (tuoyue): a metaphor for breathing.
  • The forward course is short, gathering the muni in two stages: the method of gathering the original essence and returning it to the furnace for warm nourishing; see Chapter One, section six.
  • The source of the water is utterly clear: the time when the Mysterious Pass opens and the original essence is first born.
  • Essence of clear brightness: that point of clear, bright awareness, that is, the true essence.
  • Elixir head: the root of refining the elixir.
  • Breath of the early morning: from Mencius, “Gaozi I”, the clear breath of early morning before contact with things.
  • True seed: that is, the original essence; see Medicine.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The essence does not need to be “managed” separately. Nourish the spirit in silence and the breath in stillness, and the essence naturally does not leak; once spirit and breath are disordered, trying to guard the essence alone will not hold it.
  2. Do not shake it: do not stimulate it by massage, moving it around and the like; with the body not moving rashly and the mind free of stray thoughts, essence is naturally born.
  3. Two signs: during quiet sitting, the mind suddenly feels cool and clear, or sweet saliva fills the mouth — these are signals of the production of the original essence. The saliva can be swallowed naturally, without special effort.
  4. True essence lies in “knowing”, not in “things”: sweet dew and saliva are still Posterior Heaven things; the true original essence is that point of clarity when one suddenly awakens within chaos. Recognize it, let the true intention preside, and nourish it slowly and warmly with continuous breathing.
  5. Safety note: “gather it quickly, turn the River Chariot” presupposes that a diffuse, mingling breath truly rises naturally in the elixir field and gradually reaches the whole body; without such natural movement of the breath’s dynamism, do not forcibly move it with the mind.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text comes in two places: the first paragraph (§1–§2) is followed by a note by Qiu Ling; the second place consists of two paragraphs (§3–§7), followed by a note by Qiu Ling.
  2. “The Prior Heaven spirit is the empty yin in the middle of Li … the Prior Heaven breath is the empty yang in the middle of Kan” (先天之神为离中之空阴……先天之气为坎中之空阳): so in the original book. “Empty” (kong) expresses that it is void and invisible; the translation renders it “the one yin (yang) hidden in the middle”.
  3. “Where essence is insufficient, replenish it with flavor”: from the Inner Classic of the Yellow Emperor, Suwen, “Great Treatise on the Correspondences of Yin and Yang”.
  4. Guangchengzi’s words: based on what Guangchengzi says to the Yellow Emperor in Zhuangzi, “Letting Be”, with slight differences in wording.
  5. “Bellows” (tuoyue 橐籥): from chapter 5 of the Daode jing, “The space between Heaven and Earth — is it not like a bellows?”
  6. “Govern the essence as the elixir head” (督精为丹头): so in the original book, meaning taking the original essence as the elixir head and governing it.
  7. “Shining brightly, embracing clearly” (炯然朗抱): so in the original book; 抱 (“embrace”) is probably 照 (“shine”), and the translation renders “shining brightly”.
