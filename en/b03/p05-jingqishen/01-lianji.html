Chapter Five · Essence, Breath and Spirit · 炼己之功

The Work of Refining the Self

The work of refining the self — unmoved before beautiful scenes, not the least shaken amid splendor; only when refining the self is mature are there true spirit and true breath

About This Chapter

Chapter Five speaks of the three treasures, “essence, breath and spirit”. Yet its first section does not speak of essence, breath and spirit themselves, but of something that must be done before refining them — refining the self.

  1. Essence, breath and spirit must merge into one piece: if essence is not treasured and breath not made ample, the spirit cannot enter the breath and the breath cannot subdue the spirit — like a man’s essence and a woman’s blood each staying in its own place, which can produce no child.
  2. What refining the self looks like: recognize the Prior Heaven essence, breath and spirit, and refine them — unmoved before beautiful scenes, not the least shaken amid splendor and bustle. This is the essential learning of refining the self, and the real work of “transforming one’s temperament”.
  3. Why refining the self matters: if refining the self is mature in ordinary times, then when one enters the room to sit, the six faculties are in great concentration and not a thought arises; one naturally reaches chaos and the realm of the dim and obscure, the Prior Heaven one breath comes out of emptiness, and the single opening of the Mysterious Pass arises out of nothing — exactly as when one first entered the womb and received breath.
  4. True intention and true breath: the true intention is the master of my coming into life (obtaining the principle from Heaven to form the nature); the true breath is the dwelling of my coming into life (obtaining the mandate from Heaven to form the body).
  5. Warning: not knowing how great a matter refining the self is, and hoping to obtain the medicine and form the elixir after only a moment’s work, is not only useless — the mind runs like a horse and a monkey, the Posterior Heaven fire flares up, damaging the elixir and burning the body, and instead increasing illness and shortening life.

Qiu Ling’s note makes clear what “refining the self” is: “refining” is training with diligent, earnest effort; the “self” (ji) is the nature, the intention, the Original Spirit — the point of numinosity in the mind. The method of refining the self: when the ears chase sounds, refine them to not hearing; when the eyes chase colors, refine them to not seeing; when the spirit chases contacts, refine it to not thinking.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

修炼以精气神为主。如不宝精裕气，则神不入气，气不伏神，不能打成一片。犹男精女血各居其所，两不相合，安有生男育女之时？学道人欲求一元真气，始也水火不交，安有真铅之产？及真阳一动，不行河车功法交媾乾坤，安得成丹？如此神了神，气了气，不相凝聚，焉得无息之息，以成先天法身，不神之神，以配两大乾坤乎！生等须认取先天之精气神，于是加以锻炼，对美景而依然不动，任纷华而不稍改移。只有进火行符，水中金生；进火有度，火里水发；退符有功，日运己汞，己固阳精。此炼己之要学，亦变化气质之实功也。

Cultivation takes Essence, breath and Spirit as its main concern. If essence is not treasured and breath not made ample, then the spirit cannot enter the breath, nor can the breath subdue the spirit, and the three cannot merge into one piece. It is like a man’s essence and a woman’s blood each staying in its own place without uniting — how could there ever be the birth of sons and daughters? Those who learn the Way seek the one original true breath: if at first water and fire (kidney-water and heart-fire) do not meet, how could true lead (the Prior Heaven true yang breath) be produced? When true yang stirs, if one does not use the method of the River Chariot to unite Qian and Kun (letting the true breath circulate along the Conception and Governing vessels so that above and below meet), how could the elixir be formed? With spirit returning only to spirit and breath only to breath, neither gathering with the other, how could one obtain “the breath that is no breath” to accomplish the Prior Heaven Dharma-body, or obtain “the spirit that is not spirit” to match Heaven and Earth, Qian and Kun! You must recognize the Prior Heaven essence, breath and spirit and then refine them — unmoved before beautiful scenes, not the least shaken amid splendor and bustle. As long as one advances the fire and applies the tally (adjusting effort according to the fire phasing), metal will be born in water (the Prior Heaven true yang produced in the lower elixir field); when advancing the fire has its measure, water will come forth in the fire (heart-fire descending and transforming into moistening yin essence); when withdrawing by the tally is effective, one can daily turn “ji mercury” (the mind-spirit governed by the true intention), using ji (the true intention) to secure the yang essence. This is the essential learning of refining the self, and also the real work of transforming one’s temperament (changing the Posterior Heaven disposition).

§2
Original (Chinese)

吾愿生初行炼己不辞劳瘁，庶入室之时六根大定，一念不生，自能到混混沌沌之候，有恍惚杳冥之机，此即先天一气从虚无中来，亦即玄关一窍从无生有，庶与我当日生身受气之初一般无二。何也？先年投胎夺舍从恍惚中一念而来，与父母精血吻合，今不顺而逆，乃合阴阳坎离，团聚一区，以寻我先天真意真气。夫真意即我投生之主宰，真气即我投生之庐舍。真意即我得天之理以成性，真气即我得天之命以成形者。

I hope that when you first do the work of refining the self, you will not shrink from toil. Then, when you enter the room to sit, the six faculties (eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body and mind) will be in great concentration, not a thought will arise, and you will naturally reach the time of chaos and undifferentiation, when the opportunity of the dim and obscure appears — this is the One Breath of Prior Heaven coming out of emptiness, and the single opening of the Mysterious Pass arising out of nothing, exactly as when I was first born and received breath. Why do I say this? Because when I entered the womb back then, I came from one thought out of the dim and indistinct, matching the essence and blood of my father and mother; now, instead of going along the forward course, one turns back, joining yin and yang, Kan and Li, gathering them in one place, in order to seek my Prior Heaven true intention and true breath. The true intention is the master of my coming into life; the true breath is the dwelling of my coming into life. The true intention is the nature I formed by obtaining “principle” from Heaven; the true breath is the bodily form I formed by obtaining “mandate” from Heaven.

§3
Original (Chinese)

炼己纯熟，方有真神真气，得与天地清空灵阳之气浑合为一，于是进退温养，日夜不息，久则化形而道成矣！如今学人不知炼己事大，乃行一时半刻之功，希图得药成丹，不惟无益，且意马心猿，妄动妄走，后天火起，必伤丹而焚身，不惟不能却病延年，而反增病促命也。生等勉之！

Only when refining the self is mature are there true spirit and true breath, and only then can one merge undividedly with the clear, empty, numinous yang breath between Heaven and Earth; then, advancing the fire and withdrawing by the tally, nourishing warmly, day and night without stopping, in time one can transform form and substance and accomplish the Way! Students today do not know how great a matter refining the self is; they do only a moment’s work and hope to obtain the medicine and form the elixir — not only is this of no benefit, but the mind and intention run about like a monkey and a wild horse, and once the Posterior Heaven fire flares up, it will surely damage the elixir and burn the body; not only can it not ward off illness and lengthen life, it instead increases illness and shortens life. Strive, all of you!

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§3
Original note (Chinese)

精气神为人身之三宝，练气功必须锻炼精气神，调剂和发挥精气神的各自机制和相互关系，以达到保健延年的目的。但要锻炼精气神，必先炼己。

何谓炼己？炼者，以勤苦心力精进励志进行锻炼也。己者与性、意、元神之义相同，实即心中之一灵性。伍冲虚《天仙正理》云：“己者即我静中之真性，动中之真意，为元神之别名也。”因此，必先炼己，才能以我之真性真意来主宰精气。炼己之法为：当耳逐声而用听，则炼之于不闻；目逐色而用观，则炼之于不见，神逐感而用交，则炼之于不思。欲得元精之生、气之清真、神之静定，非先炼己不可。

Essence, breath and spirit are the three treasures of the human body. To practice qigong one must refine essence, breath and spirit, regulating and bringing into play their respective mechanisms and their relations with one another, so as to achieve the aim of preserving health and lengthening life. But to refine essence, breath and spirit, one must first refine the self.

What is refining the self? “Refining” is training with diligent, earnest effort and a resolute will to advance. The “self” (ji) means the same as the nature, the intention, the Original Spirit — in fact it is that point of numinosity in the mind. Wu Chongxu’s Correct Principles of Celestial Immortality says: “The self is my true nature in stillness and true intention in movement; it is another name for the Original Spirit.” So one must first refine the self, and only then can one use one’s true nature and true intention to preside over essence and breath. The method of refining the self is: when the ears chase sounds and want to listen, refine them to not hearing; when the eyes chase colors and want to look, refine them to not seeing; when the spirit chases contacts and wants to engage, refine it to not thinking. If one wants the birth of original essence, the clarity and truth of breath, and the stillness and concentration of spirit, one must first refine the self.

Key Terms

  • Essence, Breath, Spirit: the three treasures of the human body.
  • Treasuring essence and making breath ample: valuing the essence and filling out the breath.
  • True lead: the Prior Heaven true yang breath; see Lead and Mercury.
  • River Chariot: the circulation of the true breath rising along the Governing vessel and descending along the Conception vessel; see Celestial Circuit.
  • Uniting Qian and Kun: making above and below (the crown and the lower elixir field), yin and yang, meet.
  • The breath that is no breath, the spirit that is not spirit: the true breath and the true spirit; see Chapter Two, section five.
  • Refining the self: training the “self” (the true nature and true intention, the Original Spirit) so that it is unmoved by outer scenes.
  • Advancing the fire and applying the tally: increasing and decreasing the fire phasing; see fire phasing.
  • Metal born in water, water coming forth in fire: the spirit-light shining down, the Prior Heaven true yang born in the lower elixir field; heart-fire descending and transforming into moistening yin.
  • Ji mercury: the mind-spirit governed by the true intention; mercury is the mind-spirit; see Lead and Mercury.
  • Transforming one’s temperament: changing the Posterior Heaven disposition.
  • The six faculties: eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body and mind.
  • Entering the womb and taking a dwelling: being born into a womb.
  • The mind running like a horse and a monkey: the mind and intention as restless as a galloping horse or a monkey.
  • Correct Principles of Celestial Immortality: an alchemical book by Wu Chongxu of the Ming dynasty.

Putting It into Practice

  1. First refine the self, then refine essence, breath and spirit. The work of beginning is not first looking for breath sensations, but first refining this mind: unmoved by beautiful scenes, unshaken by bustle.
  2. Daily life is the place for refining the self: when the ears are pulled away by sounds, bring them back to “not hearing”; when the eyes are pulled away by colors, bring them back to “not seeing”; when the mind is drawn by affairs, bring it back to “not thinking”.
  3. When refining the self is mature, sitting naturally goes deep: only if the mind does not run about in ordinary times can the six faculties be in great concentration and not a thought arise when you sit down.
  4. Safety note: Huang Yuanji warns explicitly that with insufficient refining of the self, merely wanting to obtain the medicine and form the elixir quickly, “the Posterior Heaven fire flares up, damaging the elixir and burning the body” — showing as restlessness, dry heat, insomnia, a swollen head and so on. If these appear, stop the hurried work and return to relaxation and an ordinary mind.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is one paragraph; the original book does not give the source. It is divided into three short passages.
  2. “Daily turn ji mercury, with ji secure the yang essence” (日运己汞，己固阳精): so in the original book — that is, using the true intention to move the mind-spirit and secure the yang essence.
  3. “Unmoved before beautiful scenes, not the least shaken amid splendor”: fenhua refers to the splendor and bustle of the world.
