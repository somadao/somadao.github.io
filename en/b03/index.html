Huang Yuanji’s Heart-Method of Quiet Practice for Nourishing Life, Annotated

清·黄元吉养生静功心法注释 · Edited and annotated by Qiu Ling · China Literature and History Press · 1990

In the Daoguang and Xianfeng reigns of the Qing dynasty, Huang Yuanji taught for more than ten years around Fushun in Sichuan, lecturing on the Daode jing and on quiet practice for nourishing life. His disciples recorded his talks as the Sayings from the Hall of Joyful Nurture, and he himself wrote a Commentary on the Daode jing; Chen Yingning called the two books required reading for Daoists. Qiu Ling took the Sayings as his main source and the Commentary as a supplement, regrouped Huang Yuanji's scattered talks by subject into ten chapters — the single opening of the Mysterious Pass, the gate of the Mysterious Female, the true intention, clarifying the mind and seeing the nature, essence, breath and spirit, the union of Kan and Li and of Qian and Kun, the River Chariot, gathering, refining and fire phasing, other secret formulas, and a conclusion — and added notes drawn from his own practice to each passage. Huang Yuanji is known for plain reasoning and few coded terms: "his reasoning is plain and not abstruse; his meaning is deep yet easy to grasp."

How to read

  1. Every section follows the same order: About This Chapter → Original Text and Translation → Key Terms → Putting It into Practice → Translator's Notes. The classical Chinese original is folded away; you can read the translation alone and follow everything.
  2. In the original book every passage is a saying of Huang Yuanji followed by Qiu Ling's note. Qiu Ling's notes come right after the passages they explain; the Chinese of each note can be unfolded, and the full translation is shown below it.
  3. All chapter and section titles were added by Qiu Ling; the texts themselves come from the Sayings from the Hall of Joyful Nurture and the Commentary on the Daode jing.
  4. Passages marked "Safety note" record vigorous methods or very advanced stages described by the ancients. Read them for understanding only; do not try them on your own.
