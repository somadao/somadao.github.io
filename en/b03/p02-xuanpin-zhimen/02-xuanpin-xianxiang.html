Chapter Two · The Gate of the Mysterious Female · 玄牝之门现象

The Appearance of the Gate of the Mysterious Female

The appearance of the gate of the Mysterious Female — breathing breaks off, and the original breathing begins to move; only by refining from breathing to no breathing can the gate of the Mysterious Female appear

About This Chapter

The previous section explained “what” the gate of the Mysterious Female is; this one explains “what it looks like” and how it comes to appear:

  1. Without cultivation, you will not see it. Only when the outer breathing breaks off does the original breathing (the true breath) begin to move; with long warm nourishing, the opening between the navel and the abdomen opens, and every pore of the whole body opens too — this is embryonic breathing, just as a baby in its mother’s womb breathes together with its mother.
  2. Beginning: start by gently drawing in the breath of the mouth and nose, until the breath seems present and absent; then the gate of the Mysterious Female opens and the original breathing appears. This point of original breathing is the innate capacity of human life.
  3. The gate of the Mysterious Female is not “breathing out is the mysterious, breathing in is the female”, nor is it the two breaths of water and fire of the palace of Li and the palace of Kan. It lies between being and non-being, in a place neither inside nor outside.
  4. A gradual process: at all times let the spirit-light pour straight into the lower field, gathering spirit and breath into the single opening of the Mysterious Pass — at first the breathing is coarse and strong; in time it becomes fine (a little stillness); later still it seems present and absent (deeper concentration); finally there is no breath to be detected at all (great concentration, great stillness). Only then is there medicine to gather.
  5. As long as one breath has not stopped, the spirit moves with the breath, and there can be no talk of the Mysterious Female; working only on spirit and breath that still have thoughts and worries, one cannot obtain the medicine, nor easily get rid of illness.

Qiu Ling’s note puts it very bluntly: people who practice quiet work today mostly work on the breath of the mouth and nose; very few can let the outer breathing turn quite naturally into inner breathing. Without obtaining the gate of the Mysterious Female, one has in fact not yet entered the gate of qigong. He also describes the appearance of the gate of the Mysterious Female, that is, the scene of embryonic breathing.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

古云：“凝神于虚，合气于漠。”此个虚无窟子，古人谓不在身中，又却离不得身中，此即太上所谓“谷神不死，是谓玄牝。”此个玄牝门，不先修炼则不见象。必要呼吸息断，元息始行，久久温养，则玄牝出入，外接天根，内接地轴，绵绵密密，于脐腹之间一窍开时，而周身毛窍无处不开，此即谓胎息。即赤子未离母腹，与母同呼吸之气一般。生能会得此窍，较从前炼口鼻之气大有不同。生自今后须从口鼻之气微微收敛，敛而至于气息若无，然后玄牝门开，元息见焉。此点元息即人生之本能，从此采取，庶得真精真气真神。

The ancients said: “Concentrate the spirit in emptiness; merge the breath in vastness.” (Gather the spirit in the place of emptiness, and let the breath melt into the formless and indifferent.) Of this Cave of Emptiness (the empty, hollow opening) the ancients said that it is not inside the body, yet cannot be separated from the body — this is what the Most High Laozi called “the spirit of the valley does not die; it is called the Mysterious Female”. Without first putting in the work of cultivation, one cannot see the scene of this gate of the Mysterious Female. Only when the breathing of the mouth and nose “breaks off” (becomes so naturally fine that it seems to have stopped) does the original breathing (the Prior Heaven true breath) begin to move; with long warm nourishing, the Mysterious Female goes out and comes in, connecting outwardly with “the root of Heaven” (communicating with the breath of Heaven and Earth) and inwardly with “the axis of Earth” (connecting with the root within the body), continuous and dense, unbroken and unscattered; when this opening between the navel and the abdomen opens, there is not a single pore in the whole body that does not open too — this is called embryonic breathing, just like a baby not yet out of its mother’s womb, which does not breathe through mouth and nose but shares one breath with its mother through the umbilical cord. If you can grasp this opening, you will find it very different from refining the breath of mouth and nose as you did before. From now on, you must first start by gently drawing in the breath of the mouth and nose, drawing it in until the breath seems present and absent; then the gate of the Mysterious Female opens of itself, and the original breathing appears. This point of original breathing is the capacity people have from birth (the instinct of life); only by gathering from here is there hope of obtaining true essence, true breath and true spirit.

§2
Original (Chinese)

古云：“玄牝之门世罕知，休将口鼻妄施为。饶君吐纳经千载，争得金乌搦兔儿。”是知玄牝之门，非如今之时师传人以出气为玄，入气为牝之谓也。又非在离宫在坎宫水火二气之谓也，盖在有无之间，不内不外之地，父母媾精时一点灵光堕入胞胎内，是为玄牝之的旨。尔学人细心自辨。若说是出玄入牝，是浑浑沦沦，毫无踪迹，又堕于顽空。在他初学之徒，吾亦不过于形色间指出一个实迹，若诸子工夫已有进步，可以抉破其微。

An ancient poem says: “The gate of the Mysterious Female is rarely known in the world; do not act rashly with mouth and nose. Though you breathe out and in for a thousand years, how could the golden crow catch the hare?” That is: few in the world know the gate of the Mysterious Female; do not work blindly on the breathing of mouth and nose; even if you practice expelling the old and taking in the new for a thousand years, how could you make the golden crow catch the jade hare? (The golden crow is the sun, standing for yang; the jade hare is the moon, standing for yin; the union of sun and moon is the union of yin and yang that forms the elixir.) From this we know that the gate of the Mysterious Female is not what some teachers of the day teach — that breathing out is called the mysterious and breathing in is called the female; nor does it refer to the two breaths of the fire of the palace of Li (the heart) and the water of the palace of Kan (the kidneys). It lies between being and non-being, in a place neither inside nor outside — the place where, when father and mother united and conception took place, that point of numinous light fell into the womb; this is the exact purport of the Mysterious Female. You students must discern it carefully for yourselves. Yet if one merely says “going out by the mysterious and in by the female” in the abstract, it is all vague and undifferentiated, without a trace to be found, and the student will again fall into dull emptiness (pure voidness, with nothing to hold on to). So for beginners I can only point out a real position on the body that has form and color (the place below the navel spoken of in the next section); if your work has already progressed, the subtlety within can be laid bare, and there is no need to be tied to a position any longer.

§3
Original (Chinese)

吾闻昔人云：“念有一毫之不止，息不能定；息有一毫之未定，命非我有。”是知玄牝者，从有息以炼至无息，至于大定大静之候，然后见其真也。近日用功，虽气息能调，然未归于虚极静笃，则玄牝之门犹不能现象。惟于日夜之际，不论有事无事，处变处常，时时以神光直注下田，将神气二者收敛于玄关一窍之中，始则一呼一吸，犹觉粗壮，久则觉其微细，则少静矣！又久则觉其若有无，则更定矣！迨至气息纯返于神，全无气息之可窥，斯时方为大定大静，炼丹则有药可采。此可悟玄牝之门，此可见生身受气之初，是即真正玄牝之消息。以之修炼，可以得药成丹也。不然有一息之未止，则神随气动，气与神迁，有何玄牝之可言！不知定息静神，从有思有虑之神气上用功，莫说丹不能成，即药亦不可得。莫说命不我立，即病亦难除。此玄牝所以为炼丹之本也。

I have heard earlier people say: “As long as a single thought has not stopped, the breath cannot be settled; as long as the breath is not settled in the slightest, nature and life are not under my own command.” From this we know that the Mysterious Female must be refined from “having breath” (still breathing through mouth and nose) to “having no breath” (breathing so fine it seems absent); only at the time of great concentration and great stillness can one see its true face. In your recent work, although you have been able to regulate the breath, you have not yet returned to utmost emptiness and steadfast stillness (emptiness to the extreme, stillness to the full), and so the gate of the Mysterious Female cannot yet appear. Simply, day and night, whether busy or idle, whether facing upheaval or ordinary times, at all times pour the spirit-light (that light of the two eyes shining inward) straight toward the lower field (the lower elixir field), and gather both spirit and breath into the single opening of the Mysterious Pass: at first each breath in and out still feels coarse and heavy; in time you feel it become fine — this is a little stillness! Later still, you feel it seem present and absent — this is deeper concentration! When the breath has wholly returned into the spirit and no breathing can be perceived any longer, then at last it is great concentration and great stillness, and in refining the elixir there is medicine to gather. From here you can awaken to the gate of the Mysterious Female; from here you can see the state of a person when first conceived and first receiving breath — this is the true news of the Mysterious Female (the message of its appearing). Use it to cultivate, and you can obtain the medicine and form the elixir. Otherwise, as long as a single breath has not stopped, the spirit moves along with the breath, and the breath runs off along with the spirit; spirit and breath both wander outside — what Mysterious Female is there to speak of! If one does not know how first to settle the breathing and still the mind and spirit, but works only on a mind and breath that still have thoughts and worries, not only will the elixir not be refined — not even the medicine can be gathered; not only will one fail to take nature and life into one’s own hands — even illness will be hard to get rid of. This is why the Mysterious Female is the root of refining the elixir.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§3
Original note (Chinese)

今人习练养生静功，多炼口鼻之气，能得玄牝之门，把外呼吸自自然然变为内呼吸者少矣！行功如不得玄牝之门，实未进入气功之门。

玄牝之门现象，亦即胎息之景。虚极静笃中，口鼻呼吸渐渐收敛，由粗而细而若无，而脐腹内则真息绵绵，在下田一起一伏，一升一降，氤氤氲氲，神融气畅，周身苏软如绵，美快无比，并恍觉一股清凉之气上升于泥丸内，周身毛窍齐开，耳、目、口鼻亦放光明。此正是玄牝之门现象，往往随之恍惚杳冥中一觉而动，续见玄关窍开。

Most people who practice quiet work for nourishing life today refine the breath of the mouth and nose; very few obtain the gate of the Mysterious Female and let the outer breathing turn quite naturally into inner breathing! If in practice one does not obtain the gate of the Mysterious Female, one has in fact not yet entered the gate of qigong.

The appearance of the gate of the Mysterious Female is also the scene of embryonic breathing: in utmost emptiness and steadfast stillness, the breathing of mouth and nose is gradually drawn in, from coarse to fine, until it seems present and absent; while within the navel and abdomen the true breath is continuous, rising and falling, ascending and descending in the lower elixir field, diffusing and mingling like a mass of warm mist; spirit and breath merge into one piece, flowing freely without obstruction; the whole body is soft as cotton, delightful beyond compare; and one dimly feels a cool breath rising into the Muddy Pellet, every pore of the body opening at once, and light shining from the ears, eyes, mouth and nose. This is precisely the appearance of the gate of the Mysterious Female, and it is often followed, in the dim and obscure, by one awakening and it stirs, and then one sees the opening of the Mysterious Pass.

Key Terms

  • Concentrate the spirit in emptiness, merge the breath in vastness: the key method for the gate of the Mysterious Female, explained in detail in the next section.
  • Root of Heaven, axis of Earth: the Mysterious Female goes out and in, connecting outwardly with the root of Heaven and inwardly with the axis of Earth — a metaphor for linking above and below.
  • Original breathing: the Prior Heaven breath, that is, the true breath and embryonic breathing; see True Breath.
  • Embryonic breathing: the opening between the navel and abdomen opens, every pore of the body opens, like a baby breathing in its mother’s womb.
  • Sheng, “you students”: Huang Yuanji’s way of addressing the disciples attending his talks.
  • The golden crow catching the hare: the golden crow is the sun, the jade hare the moon; the sun seizing the moon is a metaphor for the union of yin and yang; see Lead and Mercury.
  • Teachers of the day: ordinary teachers of the time.
  • Breathing out is the mysterious, breathing in is the female: the mistaken understanding Huang Yuanji criticizes.
  • Exact purport: the precise meaning.
  • Laying bare the subtlety: bringing out the fine points within.
  • Great concentration, great stillness: when the breath has wholly returned into the spirit and no breathing can be detected.
  • Lower field: that is, the lower Cinnabar Field.
  • The beginning of being conceived and receiving breath: when father and mother unite and a point of numinous light falls into the womb.
  • Yinyun: the appearance of breath diffusing and mingling.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Do not work on the mouth and nose. Do not seek deep or long breaths; simply draw in the breath of mouth and nose gently, letting it grow ever finer until it turns of itself into the true breath in the abdomen.
  2. A natural progression: the breathing coarse and strong → fine (a little stillness) → seeming present and absent (deeper concentration) → no breath to be detected at all (great concentration, great stillness). Each step comes from stillness, not from doing.
  3. You can practice at any time: busy or idle, at all times let the spirit-light pour straight into the lower field, gathering spirit and breath in one place.
  4. When the phenomena come, neither be alarmed nor chase them: the lower abdomen rising and falling, the whole body soft, a cool breath rising, the pores opening — these are the appearance of the gate of the Mysterious Female; afterward there is often one awakening and it stirs, and the opening of the Mysterious Pass. As Chapter One says, simply let the true intention preside.
  5. Safety note: when the book says “breathing breaks off” or “the breath seems absent”, this is a state that appears naturally after extreme stillness; it is not forcibly closing off or holding the breath. Deliberately holding the breath easily causes tightness in the chest, a swollen head or palpitations; if these appear, return to natural breathing and relax.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is in two paragraphs; the original book does not give the source. The second paragraph is divided into two short passages.
  2. “If you students can grasp this opening”, “from now on, you students”: sheng refers to the disciples attending the talks.
  3. “How could the golden crow catch the hare”: this section reads 争得 (“how could”), while the next section, quoting the same poem, reads 怎得; the meaning is the same, and each is recorded as in the original book. 搦 is read nuò, “to grasp, to catch”.
  4. “Later still, you feel it seem present and absent” (又久则觉其若有无): so in the original book; a character 若 seems to be missing, and it should read 若有若无, “seeming present and absent”; the translation follows this.
  5. “If one merely says ‘going out by the mysterious and in by the female’… one will again fall into dull emptiness”: the meaning is that if one only speaks of “going out by the mysterious and in by the female” in the abstract, the student has nowhere to begin, so Huang Yuanji first “points out a real trace among form and color” for beginners — the position given in the next section.
