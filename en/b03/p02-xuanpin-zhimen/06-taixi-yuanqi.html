Chapter Two · The Gate of the Mysterious Female · 胎息与元气

Embryonic Breathing and the Original Breath

Embryonic breathing and the original breath — two, yet truly one; the spirit is the master of the embryo, the breath its helper, the breathing assists its formation

About This Chapter

The last section of this chapter speaks of “embryonic breathing” and the “original breath” together, as the conclusion of the whole chapter:

  1. The original breath and embryonic breathing are two, yet truly one. Without the Prior Heaven original breath, the Posterior Heaven ordinary breath could not arise; without the Posterior Heaven ordinary breath, the Prior Heaven original breath would have nowhere to lodge.
  2. The way to summon the original breath: concentrate the spirit and regulate the breath → the Posterior Heaven breath becomes even → the Prior Heaven embryonic breath appears, and within its seeming presence and absence the original breath lodges → in time the breathing suddenly ceases, embryonic breathing is naturally there, and the creative transformation of yin and yang fills the whole body inside and out.
  3. In the end the method is a single sentence: with the bright shining of the spirit-light, regulate the Posterior Heaven breathing and draw forth the Prior Heaven embryonic breath.
  4. The use of embryonic breathing distinguishes effort from naturalness, and civil from martial; but what matters most is that all ties are empty and not a speck of dust clings, with the Original Spirit transformed into the true intention presiding and directing, never letting a thought drift unnoticed or a breath grow dull and unclear — like the sun coming and going, ceaseless day and night, shining everywhere.
  5. The three-sentence summary: the spirit is the master of the embryo, the breath its helper, the breathing assists its formation. When the spirit is concentrated, the breath regulated, and spirit and breath embrace in the elixir field, the furnace and tripod are set up; when embryonic breathing is calm and even, the spirit concentrated and the breath gathered, it is the right moment to gather the original yang true breath.

Qiu Ling’s note is a single sentence: to obtain the original yang true breath for cooking and refining, one must pass through embryonic breathing — “the gate of the Mysterious Female is called the root of Heaven and Earth”, and this is the reason.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

元气与胎息虽二，而实一也。若无先天元气，则后天之凡息无以生。无后天凡息，则先天之元气无由寄。欲招先天元气伏养于身中，必凝其神，调其息，迨至后天息平，先天胎息见，似有似无之内，先天元气寓焉。久之，息顿灭，先天胎息自在个中，一往一来，阴阳造化充满于一身内外，有不知其何自而起，何由而止者。人能于此直养无害，则跳出乾坤之外，包罗日月之中，较诸天地为尤大也。此岂别有法哉，要不外一神光之朗照，调后天呼吸引起先天胎息，而元神元气自寓个中，为我身之主也。

The original breath and embryonic breathing, though two names, are in fact one thing. Without the Prior Heaven original breath, the Posterior Heaven ordinary breath (breathing through mouth and nose) would have no way to arise; without the Posterior Heaven ordinary breath, the Prior Heaven original breath would have nowhere to lodge. If one wants to summon the Prior Heaven original breath and keep it hidden and nourished in the body, one must concentrate one’s spirit and harmonize one’s breath; when the Posterior Heaven breathing has become calm and even, the Prior Heaven embryonic breath appears, and within this seeming presence and absence the Prior Heaven original breath lies lodged. In time the breathing suddenly seems to stop, the Prior Heaven embryonic breath is naturally there within, going and coming, and the vital impulse of the creative transformation of yin and yang fills the whole body inside and out, so that you do not know where it starts or where it ends. If at this time one can nourish it straightforwardly, following its nature, and do it no harm, one can leap beyond the limits of Heaven and Earth, enfold the sun and moon within oneself, and be vaster than Heaven and Earth. Is there some other method for this? In the end it is nothing but the bright shining of the spirit-light, using it to harmonize the Posterior Heaven breathing and draw forth the Prior Heaven embryonic breath; then the Original Spirit and the original breath naturally lodge within, and become the master of this body of mine.

§2
Original (Chinese)

是知凡息一停，胎息自动，而生死由我矣！生等须知胎息之用，有勉强、自然之分，为文为武之用，而其要紧者，惟在万缘皆空，一尘不染，如如自如，朗朗常明，我惟以元神化为真意主宰之而运用之，母令一念游移不觉，一息昏怠不明，常惺惺天、活泼泼地如太阳之往来无停，日夜不息，而光之所照，无一处有遗，无一刻不在也。如此久久，胎息常住于金鼎之中，不从口鼻出入，亦无明暗起灭，一息如斯，始而结成刀圭妙药，渐而凝成玄黄至宝，终则大而化，化而神。要不外以神为胎之主，以气为胎之辅，以息助胎之成。人能凝神调息，注意规中，呼吸绵绵，不徐不疾，神与气两相抱合，凝于丹田之中，即炉鼎安立矣！及至胎息和平，神凝气聚，即阴阳持平，二八平分，正宜采取元阳真气，以收回玄宫。

From this we know that as soon as the ordinary breath stops, embryonic breathing stirs of itself, and life and death are in my own hands! You must know that the use of embryonic breathing distinguishes “effort” (guiding with a little intention) from “naturalness” (using no intention at all), and also has different uses of civil and martial fire; but what matters most lies only in all ties being empty and not a speck of dust clinging, at ease and natural, bright and ever clear; I simply transform the Original Spirit into the True Intention and let it preside and direct, never letting a single thought drift off unnoticed, never letting a single breath grow dull and slack without clarity, alert and lively at every moment, like the sun moving back and forth without stopping, ceaseless day and night, its light reaching everywhere, missing no place and absent at no moment. In time, embryonic breathing dwells constantly in the golden tripod (the elixir field), not going in and out through mouth and nose, with no change of light and dark, arising and ceasing, every breath like this: first it forms the wondrous medicine of the knife-point (the first bit of elixir medicine), gradually it condenses into the supreme treasure of the dark and the yellow (the precious elixir formed by yin and yang), and finally it becomes great and transforms, and transforming becomes spirit (reaching a state of unfathomable wonder). In sum, it comes down to this: the spirit is the master of the embryo, the breath is the helper of the embryo, and the breathing assists the formation of the embryo. If a person can concentrate the spirit and regulate the breath, fix the intention on the compass center (the elixir field), the breathing continuous, neither slow nor fast, with spirit and breath embracing each other and gathering in the elixir field, then the furnace and tripod for refining the elixir are set up! When embryonic breathing is calm and even, the spirit concentrated and the breath gathered — that is, when yin and yang are balanced, half and half (“two eights evenly divided”) — it is the right moment to gather the original yang true breath and bring it back into the mysterious palace.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§2
Original note (Chinese)

欲得元阳真气以烹炼，非经胎息不可。“玄牝之门，是谓天地之根”，其理在此。

To obtain the original yang true breath for cooking and refining, one must pass through embryonic breathing. “The gate of the Mysterious Female is called the root of Heaven and Earth” — this is the reason.

Key Terms

  • Original breath: the Prior Heaven original breath, lodged within the Posterior Heaven ordinary breath; see Breath.
  • Embryonic breathing: the Prior Heaven breath that appears once the Posterior Heaven breath is even, seeming present and absent; see True Breath.
  • Nourishing straightforwardly without harm: from Mencius, “Gongsun Chou I”, “nourish it with uprightness and do it no harm”; nourishing it in accordance with its nature without harming it.
  • Effort and naturalness: the use of embryonic breathing distinguishes using intention (effort) from not using it (naturalness), corresponding to civil and martial fire; see fire phasing.
  • Ever alert, lively: always awake and never darkened, the vital impulse flowing.
  • Golden tripod: the elixir field.
  • Knife-point: the true intention, also the first-formed medicine; see True Intention.
  • The supreme treasure of the dark and the yellow: the treasure formed by yin and yang, that is, the elixir.
  • Compass center (guizhong): the central, upright place on which the intention is fixed; here, the elixir field.
  • Setting up the furnace and tripod: when spirit and breath embrace in the elixir field, the furnace and tripod for refining the elixir are set up.
  • Two eights evenly divided: “two eights” is half a jin and eight liang, a metaphor for yin and yang balanced half and half.
  • Original yang true breath: the Prior Heaven true yang breath; see One Breath of Prior Heaven.
  • Mysterious palace: where the original yang true breath is stored.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Do not set the original breath against breathing. The original breath lodges within the ordinary breath; first make the Posterior Heaven breathing even, and only then will the Prior Heaven embryonic breath appear.
  2. The whole method in one sentence: with the bright shining of the spirit-light, regulate the Posterior Heaven breathing and draw forth the Prior Heaven embryonic breath.
  3. What matters most is not technique but the ground of the mind: all ties empty, not a speck of dust clinging; let only the true intention preside, never letting a thought drift unnoticed or a breath grow dull and unclear.
  4. Remember the three-sentence summary: the spirit is the master of the embryo, the breath its helper, the breathing assists its formation.
  5. The standard for breathing: continuous, neither slow nor fast.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is one paragraph; the original book does not give the source. It is divided into two short passages.
  2. “Never letting a single thought drift off unnoticed” (母令一念游移不觉): 母 (“mother”) should be 毋 (“do not”).
  3. “Ever alert, lively” (常惺惺天、活泼泼地): 天 should be 地, pairing with 活泼泼地 that follows.
  4. “You students must know” (生等须知): sheng deng refers to the students attending the talks.
  5. “Two eights evenly divided”: a common expression of the alchemists; “two eights” is half a jin and eight liang (the same weight), a metaphor for yin and yang in balance.
  6. “Bring it back into the mysterious palace”: the original text does not say concretely where the mysterious palace is; we record it as is without speculating.
