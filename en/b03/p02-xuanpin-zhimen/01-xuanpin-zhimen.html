Chapter Two · The Gate of the Mysterious Female · 何谓玄牝和玄牝之门

What Are the Mysterious Female and Its Gate

What are the Mysterious Female and its gate — to keep the spirit of the valley from ever dying, one must lay the foundation from the Mysterious Female; neither inside nor outside, observe its rising and falling, coming and going

About This Chapter

This section is taken from Huang Yuanji’s Commentary on the Daode jing, chapter 6, the chapter “The spirit of the valley does not die; it is called the Mysterious Female”. The previous chapter spoke of “the single opening of the Mysterious Pass”; this chapter takes another angle and speaks of “the gate of the Mysterious Female”:

  1. The spirit of the valley is the empty, numinous, never-darkened substance. “Valley” speaks of its emptiness, “spirit” of its numinosity, and “does not die” means ever awake and never darkened. Nourish it as the elixir head, and cultivation becomes easy; but it has neither form nor shadow, so one must begin from the “Mysterious Female” — “to keep the spirit of the valley from ever dying, one must lay the foundation from the Mysterious Female”.
  2. The mysterious is Heaven, the female is Earth. When Heaven and Earth come together, the Mysterious Female appears; when the Mysterious Female appears, opening and closing take shape. Going up and down, coming and going, revolving within the Cave of Emptiness — this is the gate of the Mysterious Female. Ordinary people use it in the forward direction, and it is the door of death; sages reverse it, and it is the gate of life.
  3. Beginning: lower the curtains and contemplate within; when all is chaotic and without knowing, the ordinary mind dies; suddenly an awakening and it stirs, and the mind of the Way is born. When the embryonic breath stirs, do not cling rigidly to the elixir field; between inside and outside, observe its rising and falling, coming and going.
  4. “Going out by the mysterious and in by the female”: the going out is not one’s original face, nor is the coming in; only between one going out and one coming in lies the wondrous truth — that is, numinous emptiness.
  5. State of mind: there must not be the slightest impatience or urgency, nor the slightest carelessness — neither clinging nor leaving, neither forgetting nor forcing. “Continuous, as if present; used without effort.”

Qiu Ling’s note distinguishes the Mysterious Pass from the gate of the Mysterious Female: the two come out together but have different names; the opening of the Mysterious Pass is “one awakening and it stirs, and the true seed is produced”; the opening of the gate of the Mysterious Female is “the true breath is born”. He also says straightforwardly: the mysterious is the opening of the Mysterious Pass between the two eyes, and the female is the lower elixir field; the gate of the Mysterious Female has no fixed position in ordinary times, and can be seen only in utmost emptiness and steadfast stillness, when the true breath and the true breath-energy circle within the Cave of Emptiness.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

太上示人，下手之功曰：“谷神不死。”何以为谷神？山穴曰谷，言其虚也。变动不拘曰神，言其灵也。不死，即惺惺不昧之谓也。人能直养此虚灵不昧之体以为丹头，则修炼自易。然而无形无影，不可捉摸，必于有声有色者而始得其端倪。古云：“要得谷神长不死，须从玄牝立根基。”何以谓之玄？玄即天也。何以谓之牝？牝即地也。天地合而玄牝出，玄牝出而阖辟成。其间一上一下，一往一来，旋循于虚无窟子，即玄牝之门也。孔子曰：“乾坤其易之门”，不诚然乎？！

The Most High Lord Lao (Laozi), teaching people the work of beginning, says simply: “The spirit of the valley does not die.” What is the “spirit of the valley”? A hollow between two mountains is called a “valley”; it is used to describe the emptiness of this substance: empty and hollow within, with nothing in it, yet able to contain everything. What changes unpredictably, bound by no pattern and following no fixed rule, is called “spirit”; it is used to describe the numinosity of this substance: subtle and mysterious, beyond your guessing, yet able to respond at any time and place. “Does not die” means “alert and never darkened”: always awake and clear, never dull or muddled. If a person can directly nourish this empty, numinous, ever-awake substance and take it as the starting point and root of refining the elixir (the elixir head), cultivation will naturally be easy. But this substance has neither form nor shadow; it cannot be grasped or touched, and one must first begin from what has sound and color, what can be felt, to get a thread of it. So the ancients said: “To keep the spirit of the valley from ever dying, one must lay the foundation from the Mysterious Female.” What is the “mysterious”? The mysterious is Heaven. What is the “female”? The female is Earth (the basic meaning of pin 牝 is female; here it takes Earth’s sense of yielding, bearing and nurturing). As soon as Heaven and Earth come together, the Mysterious Female appears; as soon as the Mysterious Female appears, a movement of opening and closing takes shape (the ancients called it hepi: he is closing, pi is opening). Within this opening and closing, the breath goes up and down, comes and goes, revolving and circling in that empty, hollow Cave of Emptiness — this place is the gate of the Mysterious Female. Confucius said: “Qian and Kun — are they not the gate of the Changes?” (Qian is Heaven, Kun is Earth; as Heaven and Earth open and close, the transformations of all things go in and out from here.) Is this not exactly the principle he meant!

§2
Original (Chinese)

第此门也，阴阳往来之路，天地造化之乡，人物发生之地，得之则生，失之则死。凡人顺用之则为死户，圣人颠倒之，则为生门。人欲炼丹，以成长生久视之道，舍此玄牝之门，别无径也，非天地之根而何？

Only, this gate is the road by which the two breaths of yin and yang come and go, the land where Heaven and Earth create all things, the place from which people and things arise: obtain it and you live, lose it and you die. Ordinary people use it in the forward direction — the breath following desire, scattering and leaking outward — and it becomes the door to death; sages use it reversed — gathering back what scatters outward and nourishing it within — and it becomes the gate of long life. If people want to refine the elixir and accomplish the Way of “long life and lasting vision” (living long and staying clear without dimming), apart from this gate of the Mysterious Female there is no other road — what is this if not what Laozi calls “the root of Heaven and Earth”?

§3
Original (Chinese)

修士垂帘观照，浑沌无知时，死凡心也。忽焉一觉而动，生道心也。所谓静则为元神，动则为真意，是其中胎息一动，不要死死执著丹田，必于不内不外之间，观其升降往来，悠扬活泼，即得真正胎息矣！

The practitioner lowers the curtains of the eyes (the eyelids drooping naturally, leaving only a thread of faint light) and contemplates within. When stillness reaches the point of chaos and undifferentiation, where one knows nothing at all, the ordinary mind, with its usual tangle of thoughts, dies; and within this chaos, suddenly a perception stirs (one awakening and it stirs) — this awakening is the birth of the mind of the Way. This is what the ancients meant by “in stillness it is the Original Spirit, in movement it is the True Intention” — the same substance of mind is called the Original Spirit when still, and when it stirs and can take charge it is called the true intention. In the midst of this, when the embryonic breathing stirs, do not nail your attention rigidly to the elixir field; you must, between inside and outside (neither guarding rigidly deep in the lower abdomen, nor putting it outside the body), watch it rise and fall, come and go of itself, moving freely and lively — and so you obtain the true embryonic breathing!

§4
Original (Chinese)

古人云：出玄入牝。是出，非我本来面目，入，亦非我本来面目，惟一出一入间，中含妙谛，即虚灵也。所谓真阴真阳，形而为真一之气是也。天地之根岂外此哉？要之，谷神者太极之理，玄牝者阴阳之气，其在先天理气原是合一，其在后天理气不可并言。修道人欲寻此妙窍，著不得一燥切心，起不得一忽略念，惟借空洞之玄牝，养虚灵之谷神，不即不离，勿忘勿助，斯得之矣！故曰：“绵绵若存，用之不勤。”

The ancients said: “Going out by the mysterious and in by the female” (the breath goes out from the mysterious and in toward the female). But this “going out” is not my original face (the true self I have from Prior Heaven), and this “coming in” is not my original face either; only at the juncture between one going out and one coming in lies a wondrous principle — that is, “numinous emptiness” (the substance that is empty yet numinous, numinous yet never darkened). What is called true yin and true yang uniting and taking shape as “the breath of the true One” (that point of original breath of Prior Heaven, not yet divided) is just this. Could the root of Heaven and Earth lie outside it? In short: the spirit of the valley is the principle of the Supreme Ultimate (the principle that is the source of all things, leaning toward the side of “spirit”); the Mysterious Female is the breath of yin and yang (the breath through which the principle moves, leaning toward the side of “breath”). In Prior Heaven (before a person is born, before thoughts arise), principle and breath are originally one and cannot be separated; in Posterior Heaven, once a person has a body and thoughts, principle and breath separate and can no longer be spoken of together. If the practitioner wants to find this wondrous opening, there must not be the slightest impatience or urgency in the mind (always wanting to get it quickly), nor the slightest carelessness (drifting off as one sits); simply use the empty, hollow Mysterious Female to nourish the numinous, empty spirit of the valley, neither clinging nor leaving (not holding too tight, nor leaving it), neither forgetting nor forcing (neither forgetting it nor pushing it with effort), and so you obtain it! That is why Laozi says: “Continuous, as if present; used without effort.” (Kept continuously, as if present and absent; used without strain or forcing.)

§5
Original (Chinese)

谷神者，空谷之神，问之若答应焉，如响即不死也。其在人身，总一虚灵不昧之真。自人丧厥天良，谷神之汩没者久矣！后之修士欲得谷神长存，虚灵不昧，以为金丹之本，从空际盘旋，无有把柄。惟从无欲观妙、有欲观窍下手，有无一立，妙窍齐开，而玄牝立焉。故曰：“此窍非凡窍，乾坤共合成，名为神气穴，内有坎离精。”总要精、气、神三者打成一片，方名得有无窍、生死门，否则为凡窍，而无先天一元真气存乎其中。虚则落顽空，实则拘形迹，皆非虚灵不昧之体。惟此玄牝之门，不虚不实，即虚即实，真有不可名言者。

The “spirit of the valley” is the spirit in the empty valley: when you call out in a valley, it answers you at once — answering like an echo, never failing; this is called “not dying”. In the human body, it is in short a true substance that is empty, numinous and never darkened (the original mind, empty yet numinous, always clear). Ever since people lost their heavenly conscience (the original mind endowed by Heaven), this spirit of the valley has been buried for too long! Later practitioners who want the spirit of the valley to last forever, empty, numinous and never darkened, as the root of the Golden Elixir, but merely circle around in emptiness, are like something hovering in mid-air with no handle to grasp. Only by beginning from “without desire, observe its wonder; with desire, observe its opening”: when the mind is without desire or thought, contemplate the formless “wonder”; when something is stirred, contemplate the “opening” that leaves traces — “non-being” and “being” established together, “wonder” and “opening” opening together, and the Mysterious Female is established. So it is said: “This opening is no ordinary opening; it is formed by Qian and Kun together; it is called the cavity of spirit and breath; within it is the essence of Kan and Li.” (This opening is not an ordinary orifice of the body; it is formed jointly by Qian and Kun, Heaven and Earth; it is called the cavity where spirit and breath meet, and within it is the essence produced by the union of Kan and Li.) Only when essence, breath and spirit have merged into one piece can it be called “the opening of being and non-being” (the opening where being and non-being meet) and “the gate of life and death” (the gate that leads to death when used forward and to life when reversed); otherwise it is just an ordinary opening, without the Prior Heaven true breath of the one origin within it. If in practice one leans toward “emptiness” — attending to nothing, seeking only voidness — one falls into dull emptiness (dead emptiness, empty but not numinous); if one leans toward “substance” — staring rigidly at some position in the body — one is bound by traces of form — neither is the empty, numinous, never-darkened substance. Only this gate of the Mysterious Female is neither purely empty nor purely substantial; call it empty, and yet it truly exists; call it substantial, and yet it is empty — there is truly something wondrous in it that cannot be put into words.

§6
Original (Chinese)

静则无形，动则有象。静不是天地之根，动亦非人物之本。惟动静交关处，乃是坎离颠倒之所，日月交光之乡，真所谓天根地窟也。学人到得真玄真牝，一升一降，此间之气凝而为性，发而为情，所以由虚极静笃中生出法相来。其曰绵绵若存者，明调养必久而胎息乃能发动也。曰用之不勤者，言抽添有时，而符火不妄加减也。人能顺天地自然之道，则金丹得矣！

When still, it has no form to be seen; only when it moves is there an image to be perceived. But “stillness” itself is not the root of Heaven and Earth, and “movement” itself is not the origin of people and things. Only at the juncture where stillness reaches its extreme and is about to move, and movement reaches its extreme and is about to be still, is the place where Kan and Li are reversed (heart-fire descending, kidney-water rising), the place of the sun and moon joining their light (spirit and breath, yin and yang meeting) — truly deserving the name “root of Heaven and cave of Earth” (the root of Heaven, Earth and all things). When the student’s cultivation reaches the point where the true mysterious and the true female appear, rising and falling, the breath within, when it gathers, becomes “nature” (the substance), and when it is released, becomes “feeling” (the function); and so out of utmost emptiness and steadfast stillness all kinds of scenes are born (Dharma-forms). Saying “continuous, as if present” makes clear that the nourishing must be long and unbroken before the embryonic breathing can stir; saying “used without effort” means that the withdrawing and adding of the fire phasing has its proper times, and the strength of the fire (the tally-fire) must not be increased or decreased at random. If a person can follow the natural way of Heaven and Earth, the Golden Elixir is obtained!

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§6
Original note (Chinese)

以上为黄元吉氏《道德经注释》第六章注文。此章老子原文为：“谷神不死，是谓玄牝。玄牝之门，是谓天地之根。绵绵若存，用之不勤。”

张紫阳《金丹四百字自序》云：“要知夫身中一窍，名曰玄牝。此窍者非心、非肾、非口、非鼻、非脾胃、非谷道、非膀胱、非丹田、非泥丸。能知此一窍，则冬至在此矣，药物在此矣，火候在此矣，沐浴亦在此矣，脱体亦在此矣！夫此一窍，亦无边旁，更无内外，乃神气之根，虚无之谷，在身中求之，不可求于他也。此之一窍，不可以私意揣度，是必心传口授”。古今气功家对玄牝及玄牝之门说法不一，使人无所适从，而黄元吉氏之说则较为合理。

玄关一窍，道家亦曰玄牝之门，此两者实同出而异名。但玄关窍开，一觉而动，真种产焉；玄牝之门开，真息生焉。两者角度实有不同。“谷神不死”，意为虚灵，而虚灵必须玄牝以形之。玄即天，亦属乾、阳、离宫、神；牝即地，亦属坤、阴、坎户、气。直接了当地说，玄即两眼之间的玄关窍，牝即下丹田。故天地合而玄牝出，玄牝出而阖辟成，阖辟成而才有玄牝之门。

玄牝之门平时并无定位，但在气功态中可于人身求之，即虚极静笃、混沌无知时，凡息停而真息现，真息与真气于虚无窟子中一上一下、一往一来盘旋，悠扬活泼，虚灵不昧，此即玄牝之门。

老子说：“玄牝之门，是谓天地之根。”天地之根亦即人身之根。盖出玄入牝，非虚灵不能形成玄牝之门，非有玄牝之门，不能出现真正的胎息，非有真胎息不能形成真一之气。故曰人身之根。

何以得出玄入牝之间之虚灵？观照下田切忌死死执着，要不即不离，勿忘勿助，则可得之。

The above is Huang Yuanji’s commentary on chapter 6 of his Commentary on the Daode jing. Laozi’s original text for this chapter is: “The spirit of the valley does not die; it is called the Mysterious Female. The gate of the Mysterious Female is called the root of Heaven and Earth. Continuous, as if present; used without effort.”

Zhang Ziyang’s preface to the Four Hundred Characters on the Golden Elixir says: “Know that there is one opening in the body, called the Mysterious Female. This opening is not the heart, not the kidneys, not the mouth, not the nose, not the spleen and stomach, not the grain path (the anus), not the bladder, not the elixir field, not the Muddy Pellet. If one can know this one opening, then the winter solstice (the moment one yang is first born, the Living Zi Hour) is here, the medicine (the Prior Heaven true breath that is gathered) is here, the fire phasing is here, the ablution (stopping the fire and nourishing warmly) is here too, and the shedding of the body (the final birth of the spirit out of the embryo) is here too — every step of cultivation from beginning to end depends on this one opening! This one opening has no edges, still less an inside or outside; it is the root of spirit and breath, the valley of emptiness; seek it in your own body, not outside it. This one opening cannot be guessed at by private intention; it must be transmitted mouth to ear by a teacher in person.” Qigong masters ancient and modern disagree about the Mysterious Female and its gate, leaving people at a loss, and Huang Yuanji’s account is the more reasonable.

The single opening of the Mysterious Pass is also called the gate of the Mysterious Female by the Daoists; the two in fact come out together but have different names. But the opening of the Mysterious Pass is one awakening and it stirs, and the true seed is produced; the opening of the gate of the Mysterious Female is the True Breath being produced — the angles of the two truly differ. “The spirit of the valley does not die” speaks of numinous emptiness; but numinous emptiness itself has no form, and must rely on the Mysterious Female to show traces. The mysterious is Heaven, belonging in alchemy to the side of Qian, yang, the palace of Li and spirit; the female is Earth, belonging to the side of Kun, yin, the door of Kan and breath. To put it straightforwardly: the mysterious is the opening of the Mysterious Pass between the two eyes, and the female is the lower Cinnabar Field. So, when Heaven and Earth (the upper and lower ends) come together, the Mysterious Female appears; when the Mysterious Female appears, the movement of opening and closing takes shape; and only with this opening and closing is there what is called the gate of the Mysterious Female.

The gate of the Mysterious Female has no fixed position in ordinary times, but in the qigong state it can be found in the body: in utmost emptiness and steadfast stillness, chaotic and without knowing, the ordinary breath stops and the true breath appears; the true breath and the true breath-energy circle up and down, coming and going, within the Cave of Emptiness, free and lively, empty, numinous and never darkened — this is the gate of the Mysterious Female.

Laozi says: “The gate of the Mysterious Female is called the root of Heaven and Earth.” The root of Heaven and Earth, placed in the human body, is the root of the body. Why? Because here each link holds the next: the breath goes in and out between the mysterious and the female, and without numinous emptiness the gate of the Mysterious Female cannot form; without the gate of the Mysterious Female, true embryonic breathing cannot appear; without true embryonic breathing, the breath of the true One (the Prior Heaven original breath) cannot form. That is why it is called the root of the body.

How does one obtain the numinous emptiness between going out by the mysterious and coming in by the female? In contemplating the lower field, above all do not cling rigidly; be neither clinging nor leaving, neither forgetting nor forcing, and you can obtain it.

Key Terms

  • Spirit of the valley: the spirit in the empty valley, a metaphor for the empty, numinous, never-darkened substance; “does not die” means alert and never darkened.
  • Elixir head: the starting point and root of refining the elixir.
  • Mysterious Female: the mysterious is Heaven, the female is Earth; Qiu Ling’s note: the mysterious is the opening of the Mysterious Pass between the two eyes, the female is the lower elixir field. See Mysterious Opening / Mysterious Pass.
  • The gate of the Mysterious Female: the place where the Mysterious Female opens and closes, going up and down, coming and going; it has no fixed position in ordinary times and can be seen when the true breath appears in utmost emptiness and steadfast stillness.
  • Opening and closing (hepi): closing and opening.
  • Cave of Emptiness: where the gate of the Mysterious Female revolves.
  • Door of death, gate of life: the same gate — used forward by ordinary people it leads to death, reversed by sages it leads to life; see the method of reversal.
  • One awakening and it stirs: a sudden awakening in the moment of chaos without knowing, when the mind of the Way is born; see Chapter One, section three.
  • Going out by the mysterious and in by the female: the numinous emptiness between going out and coming in; the going out and coming in themselves are not the original face.
  • Breath of the true One: the breath formed by the union of true yin and true yang; see One Breath of Prior Heaven.
  • Impatient mind, careless thought: impatience and urgency, carelessness and negligence — the two things to avoid in cultivating the Way.
  • Continuous, as if present; used without effort: the nourishing must be long; withdrawing and adding have their times, and the tally-fire is not increased or decreased at random.
  • Opening of being and non-being, gate of life and death: the Mysterious Female once essence, breath and spirit have merged into one piece, as opposed to an “ordinary opening”.
  • Dull emptiness: leaning toward emptiness, empty of everything; see realm of yin / realm of the aggregates.
  • Root of Heaven and cave of Earth: the juncture of movement and stillness, that is, the gate of the Mysterious Female.
  • Withdrawing and adding: increasing and decreasing the fire phasing; see fire phasing.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The gate of the Mysterious Female is not a position you can “find”. Qiu Ling says it has no fixed position in ordinary times; only when stillness reaches the point where the ordinary breath stops and the true breath appears does “the gate open”. Seek stillness first, not a position.
  2. When the embryonic breath stirs, do not guard the elixir field rigidly. Between inside and outside, it is enough to watch it rise and fall, come and go of itself, free and lively.
  3. Both kinds of mind must go: impatience and urgency (wanting scenes to appear quickly), and carelessness (drifting off as you sit). Neither clinging nor leaving, neither forgetting nor forcing.
  4. “Continuous, as if present”: embryonic breathing stirs only through long nourishing; you will not see it after a few sittings. “Used without effort”: do not add or reduce effort at random.
  5. On “the mysterious is the opening of the Mysterious Pass between the two eyes”: this is Qiu Ling describing the correspondence of the two ends of the Mysterious Female, not telling people to fix the mind between the two eyes. Chapter One has already warned that fixing the mind on the ancestral opening is not suitable for people with yin deficiency and yang excess or high blood pressure; the way of beginning in this book is still the spirit-light shining down on the elixir field, and at the end of his note to this section Qiu Ling also says “contemplating the lower field”.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is in two paragraphs, both from chapter 6 of Huang Yuanji’s Commentary on the Daode jing; the first is divided into four short passages, the second into two.
  2. “Qian and Kun — are they not the gate of the Changes?”: from the “Xici” (Appended Statements), part two, of the Book of Changes.
  3. “Is this not exactly…!”: the original punctuation is “?!”, which we keep.
  4. “Without desire, observe its wonder; with desire, observe its opening”: the received text of chapter 1 of the Daode jing reads “ever without desire, one observes its wonder; ever with desire, one observes its boundary”; Huang Yuanji explains “boundary” (jiao 徼) as “opening” (qiao 窍), as already seen in Chapter One, section two.
  5. The four lines “This opening is no ordinary opening; it is formed by Qian and Kun together…”: the original only says “so it is said”, without giving the source.
  6. Zhang Ziyang, whose preface to the Four Hundred Characters on the Golden Elixir Qiu Ling quotes, is Zhang Boduan, author of the Awakening to Reality.
