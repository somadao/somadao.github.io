Chapter Two · The Gate of the Mysterious Female · 玄牝相交

The Meeting of the Mysterious and the Female

The meeting of the mysterious and the female — the mysterious is the gate of Li, the female the door of Kan; with the outer breath regulated, Kan and Li meet and true yang is born

About This Chapter

This section brings the “Mysterious Female” down to Kan and Li:

  1. The mysterious is the gate of Li, the female is the door of Kan. The true yin within Li descends to the palace of Kan, the true yang within Kan rises, and the two meet in the true central yellow position (in this book, the lower elixir field); in time they condense into a single breath — Li spouts jade stamens of itself, and Kan puts forth golden blossoms of itself.
  2. The jade stamens and golden blossoms are not real things; they are the state after Kan and Li unite, when body and mind are both at ease, there is the light of a pearl of wisdom in the eyes, and endless delight within.
  3. The outer breathing is indispensable: if one does not use the outer breathing to regulate at all times and nourish warmly throughout, the spirit and breath within have difficulty uniting. The original breath within must rely on the outer breathing to be drawn in; at first each goes its own way, but in time they gather into one mass, and true yang is born.
  4. Tell true and false birth of yang apart: spirit and breath not yet refined at all, one suddenly sits in meditation and sees the outer yang rising, and takes it for the birth of yang and the production of the medicine — this is stirred up by Posterior Heaven perception and ordinary fire, and cannot be used as medicine. When true yang stirs, essence, breath and spirit are all involved.
  5. Setting up the furnace and tripod and carrying out the fire phasing before the ordinary breath has stopped and embryonic breathing has appeared is merely setting up an empty furnace and tripod and wasting fire and tally; even if there is an elixir, it is an illusory elixir. In the end one must sweep away all yin breath and let the yang light alone appear, like a blue sky and bright sun — not a hair of dullness or a speck of distraction will do.

Qiu Ling’s note holds that by explaining the meeting of the mysterious and the female as the union of Kan and Li, water and fire in use, and spirit and breath uniting, Huang solved the age-old riddle of how to explain the Mysterious Female. He stresses in particular that although the appearance of the gate of the Mysterious Female lies in turning the ordinary breath of outer breathing into the true breath of inner breathing, the role of the outer breathing cannot be denied — only by adjusting the outer breathing can one promote the meeting of the mysterious and the female, which is why there is such a thing as “regulating the breath”. This leads into the next section.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

吾前言玄牝之门，其实玄即离门，牝即坎户。惟将离中真阴下降坎宫，真阳上升，两两相会于中黄正位，久久凝成一气，则离之中自喷玉蕊，坎之中自吐金英。玉蕊金英，亦非实有其物，不过言坎离交媾，身心两泰，眼中有智珠之光，内心有无穷之趣，如金玉之清润缜密，无可测其罅漏者。然非以外之呼吸时时调停，周遍温养，则内之神气难以交合。

I spoke earlier of the gate of the Mysterious Female; in fact, the mysterious is the gate of Li, and the female is the door of Kan (in the human body Li refers to the heart, belonging to fire and spirit; Kan refers to the kidneys, belonging to water and breath). One only has to bring the true yin within Li (the heart) down to the palace of Kan (the lower elixir field), and let the true yang within Kan rise, the two meeting in the “true central yellow position” (in this book, the lower elixir field) and condensing in time into a single breath; then Li will naturally spout “jade stamens”, and Kan will naturally put forth “golden blossoms”. The so-called jade stamens and golden blossoms (stamens like jade, flowers like gold) are not really such things; they merely describe the state after the conjunction of Kan and Li (heart and kidneys, spirit and breath uniting): body and mind are both at ease and comfortable, there is a brightness in the eyes like a pearl of wisdom, there is endless delight within, and the whole person is clear, smooth and close-knit like gold and jade, without the slightest crack or leak. But if one does not use the outer breathing to regulate it at all times and nourish it warmly throughout, the spirit and breath within have difficulty uniting.

§2
Original (Chinese)

古云：“玄黄若也无交媾，怎得阳从坎户飞。”是知天地无功，以日月为功。人身无用，以水火为用。天地无日月，天地一死物而已。人身无水火，人身一尸壳而已。日月者，天地之精神。水火者，人身之元气。惟能交会于中，则内之元气假外之呼吸以为收敛。始而觉其各别，久则会萃一团，而真阳自此生矣！倘阴阳不交，则氤氲元气不合，而欲阳之生也其得乎？

An ancient poem says: “If the dark and the yellow did not unite, how could yang fly up from the door of Kan?” (Heaven is dark and Earth yellow; if the two breaths of yin and yang did not unite, how could the yang breath fly up from the door of Kan — the lower elixir field?) From this we know that Heaven and Earth themselves do nothing; their working is shown through the movement of the sun and moon; the human body itself does nothing either; its function is accomplished through water and fire (kidney-water and heart-fire). Heaven and Earth without the sun and moon would be no more than a dead thing; the human body without water and fire would be no more than a corpse. The sun and moon are the spirit of Heaven and Earth; water and fire are the original breath of the human body. As long as water and fire can meet in the middle, the original breath within will be drawn in by means of the outer breathing. At first one feels that each goes its own way (the inner breath is the inner breath, the breathing is the breathing); in time they gather into one mass, and true yang is born from here! If yin and yang do not meet, the diffuse, mingling original breath cannot come together — how then could one hope for the yang breath to be born?

§3
Original (Chinese)

可笑世之凡夫，以全未煅炼之神气，突然打坐，忽见外阳勃举，便以为阳生药产，岂知此是后天之知觉为之。凡火激之而动者，何可入药？生须知真阳之动，不止一个精生，气与神皆有焉。必须澄神汰虑，寡欲清心，将口鼻之呼吸一齐屏息，然后真息见焉，胎息生焉，元神出焉。由此再加进火，退符、沐浴、温养之功法，自有先天一点真阳发生，灵光现象，以之为药，可以驱除一身之邪私。以之为丹，可以成就如来之法相。

It is laughable how ordinary people in the world, with spirit and breath not refined at all, suddenly sit in meditation and, seeing the outer yang (the genitals) rise, take it for “the birth of yang and the production of the medicine” (true yang born and ready to be gathered), not knowing that this is caused by Posterior Heaven perception (desire and the senses). How can something stirred up by ordinary fire (the fire of lust) be used as medicine? You must know: when true yang stirs, it is not only essence that is produced; breath and spirit change at the same time. One must clarify the spirit and wash away stray thoughts entirely, lessen desire and clear the mind, and draw in the breathing of mouth and nose all together (until stillness makes the breathing seem absent); only then does the True Breath appear, does embryonic breathing arise, does the Original Spirit reveal itself. On this basis, add the practices of advancing the fire and withdrawing the tally (increasing and decreasing the fire phasing), ablution (stopping the fire to rest) and warm nourishing, and naturally a point of Prior Heaven true yang will arise and the numinous light will appear. Use it as medicine, and it can drive out the evil breath and selfish desires of the whole body; use it to refine the elixir, and one can accomplish a Dharma-form like that of the Tathāgata (the majestic form of a Buddha, borrowed here as a metaphor for the highest attainment of cultivation).

§4
Original (Chinese)

古云：“勿助勿忘妙呼吸，须从此处用功夫。调停一气生胎息，始向中间设鼎炉。”是知安炉立鼎以锻炼真药，未到凡息停而胎息见之时，则空安炉鼎，枉用火符，终不能成丹。即说有丹，亦幻丹矣！不但无以通灵，以之却病延年，亦有不能者。总之玄牝相交，元黄相会，无非扫尽阴气，独露阳光，有如青天白日，方是坎离交，真阳现。有一毫昏怠之心，则阴气未消；有一点散乱之心，则阳神未老，究不可谓为阳纯。

An ancient poem says: “Neither forcing nor forgetting — the wonder of breathing; it is here that you must put in the work. Regulate the one breath until embryonic breathing is born; only then set up tripod and furnace in the middle.” (The wonder of breathing lies in neither pushing it with effort nor forgetting it; the work must begin here; only when one breath has been regulated until embryonic breathing is born can one set up the tripod and furnace for refining the elixir in the body.) From this we know that in setting up the furnace and tripod to refine the true medicine, if one has not yet reached the point where the ordinary breath stops and embryonic breathing appears, one has set up the furnace and tripod in vain and wasted the fire phasing, and in the end the elixir cannot be refined; even if one says an elixir has been refined, it is an empty, unreal illusory elixir! Not only can it not open up numinous clarity — even for warding off illness and lengthening one’s years there are things it cannot do. In short, what is called the meeting of the mysterious and the female, the meeting of the dark and the yellow, is nothing but sweeping away the yin breath completely so that only the yang light shows, like a blue sky and bright sun; only this is Kan and Li meeting and true yang appearing. As long as there is a hair of a torpid, slack mind, the yin breath has not been fully dissolved; as long as there is a speck of a distracted mind, the yang spirit is not yet seasoned and mature — in the end it cannot be called pure yang.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

玄牝相交，实即出玄入牝。黄元吉氏将玄牝相交解释为坎离交媾、水火为用、神气交合，揭示了对玄牝解释之千古之谜，使学者更容易理解。

虽然，玄牝相交而至玄牝之门显象，在于使有息炼到无息，即使外呼吸之凡息转化为内呼吸之真息，返回人之未生在胞胎内的内呼吸状态，但外呼吸之作用，却不容否认。盖只有调剂外呼吸，才能促进玄牝相交，亦即坎离交媾，故有所谓调息之举。此理汉代崔希范《入药镜》说得最精辟。他说：“先天炁，无天炁，得之者，常似醉。”非有后天之呼吸不能助先天之真息出现。若得胎息出现，玄牝之门露象，即觉丹田火暖，美在其中，畅于四肢，如痴如醉。而此又是真阳产生之际，安炉设鼎之时。

The meeting of the mysterious and the female is in fact going out by the mysterious and in by the female. By explaining the meeting of the mysterious and the female as the union of Kan and Li, water and fire in use, and spirit and breath uniting, Huang Yuanji solved the age-old riddle of how to explain the Mysterious Female, making it easier for students to understand.

Although the meeting of the mysterious and the female, and indeed the appearance of the gate of the Mysterious Female, lies in refining from breath to no breath — that is, turning the ordinary breath of outer breathing into the true breath of inner breathing, returning to the state of inner breathing a person had in the womb before birth — the role of the outer breathing cannot be denied. Only by adjusting the outer breathing can one promote the meeting of the mysterious and the female, that is, the union of Kan and Li; that is why there is the practice called “regulating the breath”. Cui Xifan’s Mirror for Compounding the Medicine states this principle most incisively. He says: “Prior Heaven breath, Posterior Heaven breath — whoever obtains them is always as if drunk.” Without Posterior Heaven breathing, one cannot help the Prior Heaven true breath to appear. If one obtains the appearance of embryonic breathing and the gate of the Mysterious Female shows itself, one feels the fire in the elixir field warm, beauty within, spreading to the four limbs, as if entranced or drunk. And this is also the moment when true yang is produced, the time to set up the furnace and tripod.

Key Terms

  • Gate of Li, door of Kan: the mysterious is the gate of Li (heart, spirit), the female the door of Kan (kidneys, breath); see conjunction of Kan and Li.
  • True central yellow position: in this book, the lower elixir field.
  • Jade stamens and golden blossoms: Li spouting jade stamens, Kan putting forth golden blossoms — a metaphor for the scene of body and mind at ease after Kan and Li unite, not real things; see Union of sun and moon.
  • The light of a pearl of wisdom: the clear light that appears in the eyes.
  • Crack or leak (xialou): gaps and leaks.
  • The dark and the yellow (xuanhuang): Heaven dark and Earth yellow, a metaphor for Qian and Kun, yin and yang; yuanhuang 元黄 in the text is the same.
  • Heaven and Earth have no work of their own and work through the sun and moon; the body has no function of its own and functions through water and fire: Heaven and Earth show their work through the movement of the sun and moon, the human body functions through the meeting of water and fire (spirit and breath).
  • The outer yang rising: see outer kidney; when it appears suddenly before the work has matured, it is stirred up by Posterior Heaven perception and ordinary fire, and must not be taken for the production of the medicine.
  • Advancing the fire and withdrawing the tally: see fire phasing.
  • Setting up the furnace and tripod: establishing the furnace and tripod for refining the elixir, that is, concentrating the spirit in the elixir field with spirit and breath embracing.
  • Illusory elixir: carrying out the fire phasing before the ordinary breath has stopped and embryonic breathing has appeared yields only an illusory elixir.
  • The yang spirit not yet seasoned: the yang spirit not yet mature and practiced.
  • Mirror for Compounding the Medicine: an alchemical scripture by Cui Xifan, written in rhymed lines of three characters.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The outer breathing is a helper, not an enemy. The spirit and breath within can unite only with the outer breathing regulating at all times and nourishing warmly throughout. Do not try to “abolish breathing” from the start — first make the breathing even, and let it become fine of itself.
  2. Tell true and false birth of yang apart. If, before the work has matured, the outer yang rises as soon as you sit, it is stirred up by Posterior Heaven perception and ordinary fire; it is not the production of the medicine, and do not take it as the moment for gathering; simply let it go calmly.
  3. The signs of true yang stirring: not just one place in the body moving, but essence, breath and spirit all involved — the mind clear, delight within, body and mind at ease, the eyes bright.
  4. Do not rush to talk about fire phasing. Talking of advancing the fire and withdrawing the tally, of setting up the furnace and tripod, before the ordinary breath has stopped and embryonic breathing has appeared, will only yield an illusory elixir.
  5. Both faults must be swept away: a hair of torpor and slackness, a speck of distraction — neither is yet pure yang.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is in one paragraph; the original book does not give the source. It is divided into four short passages.
  2. “The meeting of the dark and the yellow” (yuanhuang xianghui 元黄相会): yuan 元 stands for xuan 玄. To avoid the taboo on the name of the Kangxi emperor, Xuanye 玄烨, Qing authors often replaced 玄 with 元; the translation uses “the dark and the yellow”.
  3. “A hair of a dull, slack mind” (有一毫昏怠之心): hundai is torpor and slackness. “In the end it cannot be called pure yang” (究不可谓为阳纯): it still cannot be called pure yang.
  4. “Draw in the breathing of mouth and nose all together” (将口鼻之呼吸一齐屏息): like “breathing breaks off” in the previous section, this refers to the outer breathing becoming naturally fine to the point of seeming absent in extreme stillness, not deliberately holding the breath (see Qiu Ling’s note in the next section, “must not be forced down”).
  5. “A Dharma-form like that of the Tathāgata”: a Buddhist expression, used as a metaphor for attainment in cultivation.
  6. The two poems “If the dark and the yellow did not unite” and “Neither forcing nor forgetting — the wonder of breathing” are only introduced as “the ancients said”, without a source.
  7. Qiu Ling’s note quotes Cui Xifan’s Mirror for Compounding the Medicine as “Prior Heaven breath, no-Heaven breath” (先天炁，无天炁): 无天炁 should be a mistake for 后天炁, “Posterior Heaven breath”, and the translation follows this. Also, Qiu Ling calls him “Cui Xifan of the Han dynasty”; Cui Xifan is generally thought to have lived in the late Tang and Five Dynasties.
  8. Qiu Ling’s note, “solved the age-old riddle of how to explain the Mysterious Female” (揭示了对玄牝解释之千古之谜): so in the original book, recorded as is.
