Chapter Two · The Gate of the Mysterious Female · 篇首导读

Introduction to Chapter Two

What this chapter covers

Chapter One spoke of “the single opening of the Mysterious Pass” from the side of the “spirit”: within chaos, suddenly an awakening and it stirs. This chapter speaks of “the gate of the Mysterious Female” from the side of the “breath”: the outer breathing gradually becomes fine and subtle, and the true breath, the embryonic breath, appears in the abdomen.

Qiu Ling says it very clearly in his note to the first section: the two come out together but have different names, yet the angle differs — when the opening of the Mysterious Pass opens, one awakening and it stirs, and the true seed is produced; when the gate of the Mysterious Female opens, the true breath is produced. He also says: people who practice quiet work today mostly work on the breath of the mouth and nose, and very few can let the outer breathing turn quite naturally into inner breathing; without obtaining the gate of the Mysterious Female, one has in fact not yet entered the gate of qigong.

The six sections of this chapter form a complete sequence:

What the Mysterious Female and its gate are (definition) → what it looks like when it appears (appearance) → where it is and how to begin (the place of going out by the mysterious and in by the female) → its principle (the meeting of the mysterious and the female, that is, the meeting of Kan and Li) → how exactly to regulate the breathing (regulating the breath and listening to the breath) → the result (embryonic breathing and the original breath)

What this chapter is about (in one sentence)

Make the outer breathing even, without forcing it down; let the mind and spirit open out, without guarding rigidly, only listening lightly to the breath coming and going; concentrate the spirit below the navel, not far from the skin and flesh, neither inside nor outside — the ordinary breath gradually stops and the embryonic breath stirs of itself: this is the opening of the gate of the Mysterious Female; the original breath lodges within the embryonic breath, and it is the root of refining the elixir.

Reading tips

  1. The most practical sections of this chapter are the third and the fifth. Section three gives the position and the way to begin (concentrate the spirit below the navel, not far from the skin and flesh, without the gaze staring rigidly inward); section five gives the procedure for regulating the breathing (make it even → count the breaths for a while → open out the mind and spirit and listen to the breath).
  2. “Breathing breaks off” and “holding the breath” never mean holding one’s breath. Such expressions occur many times in the book; they refer to the outer breathing becoming naturally fine after extreme stillness, until it seems present and absent. In his note to section five Qiu Ling stresses that the drawing-in of the outer breathing “comes about entirely naturally, and must not be forced down”; forcing it down instead chokes the breath and disturbs the breath’s dynamism.
  3. When Qiu Ling says “the mysterious is the opening of the Mysterious Pass between the two eyes, the female is the lower elixir field”, he is describing the correspondence of the two ends of the Mysterious Female, not telling people to fix the mind between the two eyes. The way of beginning in this book is still the one given in Chapter One, the spirit-light shining down on the elixir field; “concentrating the spirit in emptiness” in section three also shines toward the area below the navel.
  4. The outer breathing is a helper. Qiu Ling’s note to section four says: the role of the outer breathing cannot be denied; only by adjusting the outer breathing can one promote the meeting of the mysterious and the female. Do not try to “abolish breathing” from the start.
  5. In the original text, yuanhuang 元黄 is xuanhuang 玄黄 (“dark and yellow”), written this way by Qing authors to avoid the taboo on the name of the Kangxi emperor, Xuanye 玄烨.
