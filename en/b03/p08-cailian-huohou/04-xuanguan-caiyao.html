Chapter Eight · Gathering, Refining and Fire Phasing · 玄关采药

Gathering the Medicine at the Mysterious Pass

Gathering the medicine at the Mysterious Pass — the Mysterious Pass where essence is born and the Mysterious Pass where breath stirs; tender medicine and old medicine: gathering before the muddled state is tender, running outward after awareness is old; if one cannot enter the muddled state, only let stillness and illumination return together

About This Chapter

This section speaks of how to gather the medicine when the opening of the Mysterious Pass opens, and how to judge whether the medicine is “tender” or “old”.

  1. There is more than one Mysterious Pass: the Mysterious Pass of the Prior Heaven breath of primordial unity is without sound or smell, without color or form — the heavenly secret of “refining emptiness”, the very highest vehicle. Besides this, in refining essence into breath there is “the Mysterious Pass where essence is born”, and in refining breath into spirit there is “the Mysterious Pass where breath stirs”.
  2. The Mysterious Pass where essence is born: sitting, concentrating the spirit and regulating the breath, one reaches a dim, indistinct state; the spirit has concentrated, the breath is regulated, and a point of true essence is hidden at the yinqiao (the perineum). Out of the moment of awareness within the muddled state, quickly draw the breath of the yinqiao back to the proper position of the Central Yellow (the lower elixir field), to be refined into one with the yin essence long accumulated within Li.
  3. Tender medicine, old medicine:
    • Not yet in the muddled state — there is no medicine;
    • Already in the muddled state, but spirit and breath have not yet merged into one, and one goes to gather — the medicine is tender and not fit for refining;
    • Awareness arises in the muddled state, but one cannot recognize it clearly and draw it in at once, and after the awareness thinks of other things, after the stirring runs outward again — the medicine is old, and even less usable.
  4. The Mysterious Pass where breath stirs: after essence is born and drawn back, it is matched with the spiritual fluid of the Li palace; the spirit enters the breath and the breath surrounds the spirit; at first they still now join, now part; when spirit and breath have merged into one piece, turning and floating in the elixir field, the spiritual breath of one’s own body and the yang breath coming from heaven and earth outside become one without one’s noticing — this is “breath-yang arising”, the phenomenon of the Mysterious Female, “heaven and earth uniting to send down sweet dew”. At this time, awakening out of the muddled state, the source of the water is utterly clear, and one gathers — this is “two parts of fire refining two parts of fresh, tender water”. Gathering before the great union of the Mysterious Female is tender; not gathering quickly once the great union has come, the newly born spiritual breath has already dispersed — that is old.
  5. A shortcut: being able to enter the muddled state is of course good; if one cannot, as long as one is continuous and close-knit, stillness and illumination returning together, with images in the dimness and knowing in the obscurity, without raising a “mind of clear awareness”, spirit and breath gathering together, harmonious and undivided — and one also sees the breath in the body and the breath from outside mingling richly and surging vigorously, the whole body leaping, melting and soft and joyful — this is just the time.

Qiu Ling’s note: these are two levels of practice. In refining essence into breath, the Mysterious Pass stirs with a moment of awareness, and one draws true essence from the perineum; when spirit and breath have merged in the muddled state, the lesser medicine gathered is neither old nor tender and can transform into true breath-energy. In refining breath into spirit, the Mysterious Pass stirs with a moment of awareness, Kan and Li join, the phenomenon of the Mysterious Female appears, the original breath unites with the yang breath of heaven and earth, the whole body leaps, melting and soft and joyful; the great medicine gathered is neither old nor tender and can transform into spirit.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

夫玄关一窍，是先天混元一气之玄关，了无声臭可扪，色相可见。此为最上上乘炼虚一着天机。若论玄关不止一端，如炼精化气之时则有精生之玄关；炼气化神之时则有气动之玄关。

The one opening of the Mysterious Pass is the Mysterious Pass of the Prior Heaven breath of primordial unity; not the slightest sound or smell can be touched, and there is no color or form to be seen. This is the heavenly secret of “refining emptiness”, the very highest of the highest vehicles. But if we speak of the Mysterious Pass, there is more than one kind: for example, when refining essence into breath, there is “the Mysterious Pass where essence is born”; when refining breath into spirit, there is “the Mysterious Pass where breath stirs”.

§2
Original (Chinese)

何谓精生之玄关？如下手打坐即便凝神调息，到得恍惚之间，神已凝了，息已调了，斯时一点真精即藏于阴跻一穴之处。我从混沌一觉中急忙摄收阴跻之气，归于中黄正位，与离中久积阴精煅炼为一。斯亦有药嫩药老之说。何谓嫩？如未混沌，斯为无药；若已混沌，未能使神气融和混化为一，即便去阴跻采取，斯为药嫩，不堪入炼；若混沌一觉，我不能辨认清白即时提摄，待至一觉之后又复觉及他事，一动之后又复动而外驰，斯为药老，更不可用。

What is “the Mysterious Pass where essence is born”? For example, when you begin to sit, you concentrate the spirit and regulate the breath, and when you reach a dim, indistinct state, the spirit has already concentrated and the breath has already been regulated; at this time a point of true essence is hidden at the point of the yinqiao (the perineum). Awakening out of the muddled state, I quickly draw in the breath of the yinqiao and bring it back to the proper position of the Central Yellow (the lower elixir field), to be forged into one together with the yin essence long accumulated within Li. Here too there is talk of “tender medicine” and “old medicine”. What is tender? If one has not yet entered the muddled state, there is no medicine; if one has already entered the muddled state but has not yet been able to make spirit and breath merge and transform into one, and goes to gather at the yinqiao, this is tender medicine, which cannot be refined; if awareness arises in the muddled state and I cannot recognize it clearly and draw it in at once, and after the awareness I think of other things, after the stirring I move and scatter outward again — this is old medicine, and even less usable.

§3
Original (Chinese)

若气阳生，药物之老嫩又在何时？盖从此精生，摄之而归，与我离宫灵液两相配合，斯时神入气中，气周神外，其始神与气犹有时合时分之状，不能合为一区。神即离宫之神火，气即坎中之神水。迨至神与气融成一片，宛转于丹田中，悠扬活泼，吾身灵气与天地外来之阳气不觉合而为一，此即气阳生玄牝现象，所谓天地相合以降甘露。露即外来灵阳之气，是此时须从混沌中一觉，方是水源至清，不染纤尘。如此采取，斯为二分火炼二分新嫩之水，正是药苗新生，又谓离喷玉蕊，坎吐金英，是二家交媾而成丹。否则未能大静无以为大定也。若未到玄牝大交而采，是为药嫩；既已大交犹不急采，则新生之灵气已散，是为药不堪用。

If it is “breath-yang arising”, when then is the age of the medicine judged? After essence is born, it is drawn in and brought back, and paired with the spiritual fluid of my Li palace; at this time the spirit enters the breath and the breath surrounds the spirit; at first, spirit and breath still seem now to join and now to part, unable to come together in one place. The spirit is the spirit-fire of the Li palace; the breath is the spirit-water within Kan. When spirit and breath have merged into one piece, turning and floating lively in the elixir field, and the spiritual breath of my body and the yang breath coming from heaven and earth outside become one without my knowing it — this is the phenomenon of the Mysterious Female in “breath-yang arising”, what is called “heaven and earth uniting to send down sweet dew”. The dew is the spiritual yang breath coming from outside. At this time one must awaken out of the muddled state; only then is the source of the water utterly clear, untouched by a speck of dust. Gathering in this way is “two parts of fire refining two parts of fresh, tender water” (the fire matched to the tenderness of the medicine); it is just when the shoots of the medicine are newly born, also called “Li sprays out jade stamens, Kan puts forth golden blossoms”, the two houses uniting to form the elixir. Otherwise, without great stillness there is no great stability. If one goes to gather before the great union of the Mysterious Female has come, the medicine is tender; if the great union has come and one does not gather quickly, the newly born spiritual breath has already dispersed, and the medicine cannot be used.

§4
Original (Chinese)

吾再示一捷法，能混沌固佳，如不能混沌，只要自家绵绵密密，寂照同归，恍惚之而有象，杳冥之而有知，不起一明觉心，两两会萃，和畅不分，又复见吾身之气与外来之气细细缊缊，蓬蓬勃勃，周身踊跃，苏软快乐，此当其时也，急运河车，大丹在指顾间矣！

Let me teach you another shortcut: being able to enter the muddled state is of course good; if you cannot enter it, as long as you yourself are continuous and close-knit, with stillness and illumination returning together (stillness and illuminating awareness as one), with images in the dimness and knowing in the obscurity, without raising a mind of “clear awareness” (not deliberately trying to perceive and discriminate), spirit and breath gathering together, harmonious and undivided, and you also see the breath of your body and the breath from outside mingling finely and richly, surging vigorously, the whole body leaping, melting and soft and joyful — this is just the time: quickly turn the River Chariot, and the great elixir is within a finger’s point and a glance!

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

此为两层功法。炼精化气时，玄关窍开一觉而动，我即向会阴穴摄取真精，若混沌中神气融和，所采之药（小药）则为不老不嫩，可以煅化为真气。炼气化神时，玄关窍开一觉而动，我即急运河车，若坎离交媾出现玄牝现象，混沌中我之元气与天地外来之阳气相合，周身踊跃，苏软快乐，则所采之药（大药）为不老不嫩，可煅化为神。

These are two levels of practice. When refining essence into breath, the opening of the Mysterious Pass opens and stirs with a moment of awareness, and I draw true essence from the perineum point; if spirit and breath have already merged in the muddled state, the medicine gathered (the lesser medicine) is neither old nor tender, and can be forged and transformed into true breath-energy. When refining breath into spirit, the opening of the Mysterious Pass opens and stirs with a moment of awareness, and I quickly turn the River Chariot; if Kan and Li unite and the phenomenon of the Mysterious Female appears, and in the muddled state my original breath unites with the yang breath coming from heaven and earth outside, the whole body leaping, melting and soft and joyful, then the medicine gathered (the great medicine) is neither old nor tender, and can be forged and transformed into spirit.

Key Terms

  • The Mysterious Pass of the Prior Heaven breath of primordial unity: without sound or smell, without color or form; refining emptiness, the highest vehicle.
  • The Mysterious Pass where essence is born: in refining essence into breath, true essence hidden at the yinqiao is drawn back to the elixir field upon a moment of awareness.
  • The Mysterious Pass where breath stirs: in refining breath into spirit, spirit and breath merge into one piece and become one with the yang breath of heaven and earth.
  • Not the slightest sound or smell can be touched: nothing at all to grasp of sound or smell.
  • Proper position of the Central Yellow: in this book, the lower elixir field.
  • Tender medicine: gathering before spirit and breath have merged.
  • Old medicine: running and scattering outward again after the moment of awareness.
  • Two parts of fire refining two parts of fresh, tender water: the fire matched to the medicine.
  • Li sprays out jade stamens, Kan puts forth golden blossoms: the union of Kan and Li; see union of sun and moon.
  • Stillness and illumination returning together: stillness and illuminating awareness as one.
  • Mind of clear awareness: a mind that deliberately perceives and discriminates.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Watch the timing: in the muddled state, if spirit and breath have not yet merged into one piece — do not hurry; if after the moment of awareness the mind runs off again — it is too late. The key is in the very moment of that awareness: recognize it clearly and draw it back.
  2. The gathering is very light: after the moment of awareness, gently bringing the mind back to the elixir field is “drawing in” — it is not forcefully pulling up the perineum.
  3. The shortcut is the most practical: it does not matter if you cannot enter the muddled state — be continuous and close-knit, let stillness and illumination return together, do not raise a deliberately discriminating mind, and wait for spirit and breath to harmonize by themselves.
  4. Safety note: “quickly turn the River Chariot” refers to the time after spirit and breath have already merged into one piece and the whole body is melting and soft and joyful; without these signs, do not turn it.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is one paragraph; the original book does not give the source. It is divided into four short passages.
  2. “Not the slightest sound or smell can be touched” (了无声臭可扪): 扪, read mén, means to touch.
  3. “If it is breath-yang arising” (若气阳生): meaning “as for the yang arising of the stirring breath”.
