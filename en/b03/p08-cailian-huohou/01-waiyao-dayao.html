Chapter Eight · Gathering, Refining and Fire Phasing · 外药、大药和圣胎

The Outer Medicine, the Great Medicine and the Holy Embryo

The outer medicine, the great medicine and the Holy Embryo — the ordinary father and ordinary mother join and produce the outer medicine; the spiritual father and holy mother join and nurture the great medicine; Prior Heaven yang energy subdues Posterior Heaven yin essence and transforms into the Holy Embryo

About This Chapter

Chapter Eight speaks of “gathering, refining and fire phasing” — when the medicine is born, how to gather it, and with what fire to refine it. The first section begins by distinguishing three levels: the outer medicine (lesser medicine), the inner medicine (great medicine) and the Holy Embryo.

  1. The outer medicine (lesser medicine): following the ordinary way of husband and wife joining, fire enters the land of water — the spirit enters the breath — this is “the ordinary father and ordinary mother join and produce the medicine”. When the fire steams and the water boils, metal is born at the bottom of the water, the mysterious opening opens and the true sign arrives: true yang is born and the medicine is produced. It stirs outside the kidney channel, and its breath is small.
  2. The inner medicine (great medicine): once the metal breath is born, true lead is naturally sufficient; the fire urges the water to rise, wood carries metal upward, ascending to the Qian tripod (the head), where true lead is matched with true mercury; then, with true fire and true intention, it is led down to the elixir field and gathered back into the Kun furnace to be cooked and refined; then wait again for true yang to stir — this is “nurtured by the union of the spiritual father and holy mother”. It stirs within the root of the breath, and when it is born there are signs of “heaven answering with stars, earth answering with tides, the six roots shaking”.
  3. The Holy Embryo: then, with the Golden Elixir, the River Chariot is set going; Prior Heaven yang energy subdues Posterior Heaven yin essence, and yin essence joins with true breath-energy and transforms into the Holy Embryo — the child comes because it longs for the mother, the mother stays because she longs for the child; child and mother embrace, spirit and breath rely on each other.
  4. A one-sentence summary: the great way of the Golden Elixir differs from begetting people only in reversing the workings of creation at every point and turning Qian and Kun upside down.

Qiu Ling’s note: “the medicine is at root one breath” — the differences between the outer medicine, the inner medicine and the Holy Embryo are only differences in the quality of the breath. What the method of guarding the center (the joining of Kan and Li) refines is the lesser, outer medicine, which can only strengthen the lower origin and cannot form the elixir; what the method of the River Chariot (the joining of Qian and Kun) refines is the great, inner medicine, which alone can form the elixir; the Holy Embryo takes the great medicine as the elixir-mother and is refined by turning the River Chariot again, and is also called the infant. Only by understanding the distinction between these three does one know the order of gathering and refining.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

始用顺道之常，效夫妇交媾之法，以火入水乡，即是以神入气中，此为凡父凡母交而产药。迨火蒸水沸，水底金生，斯时玄窍开而真信至，是为真阳生而药产，此为外药。

At the beginning, using the ordinary method of the forward way, imitating the way husband and wife join, fire enters the land of water — that is, the spirit enters the breath — this is “the ordinary father and ordinary mother join and produce the medicine” (the ordinary father and ordinary mother are the Posterior Heaven spirit and breath). When the fire steams and the water boils and metal is born at the bottom of the water (Prior Heaven true yang arises in the lower elixir field), the mysterious opening opens and the true sign (a reliable confirmation) arrives — this is true yang being born and the medicine produced, and it is called the “outer medicine”.

§2
Original (Chinese)

金气既生，真铅自足，于是火促水腾，木载金升，切切催之，款款运之，上升乾鼎以真铅配真汞，以真火真意引之下入丹田，即入坤腹，以炉鼎和药炉炼丹。此返坎为男，复离为女，颠倒女男迭为宾主，收归坤炉烹炼一晌，再候真阳火动以为金丹大药，此为内药生，又曰大药。产此为灵父圣母交媾而育者也。

Once the metal breath (the breath of Prior Heaven true yang) is born, true lead is naturally sufficient; then the fire urges the water to rise, and wood (mercury) carries metal (lead) upward; urge it on earnestly, move it along unhurriedly, raising it to the Qian tripod (the head), where true lead is matched with true mercury; then, with true fire and true intention, lead it down into the elixir field (the Kun belly), harmonizing the ingredients in the furnace and tripod to refine the elixir. This is returning Kan to the male (Qian) and restoring Li to the female (Kun), turning female and male upside down, each in turn host and guest; gathered back into the Kun furnace and cooked for a while, one again waits for the fire of true yang to stir, as the great medicine of the Golden Elixir — this is the birth of the inner medicine, also called the “great medicine”. What produces it is the union of the “spiritual father and holy mother” (the Prior Heaven spirit and breath), which nurtures it.

§3
Original (Chinese)

前小药之生，动在肾管外，其气小，故曰小药、外药。此则动于气根之内，生时有天应星、地应潮、六根震动之状，故曰内药、大药，又曰金丹。

The lesser medicine produced earlier stirs outside the kidney channel, and its breath is small, so it is called the lesser medicine or outer medicine. The great medicine here stirs within the root of the breath (deep in the elixir field), and when it is produced there are the phenomena of “heaven answering with stars, earth answering with tides, the six roots shaking”, so it is called the inner medicine or great medicine, and also the Golden Elixir.

§4
Original (Chinese)

再以此金丹运起河车，鼓动巽风，施用坤火合离宫真精而煅之，真气合真精，即以先天阳气制伏后天阴精，阴精亦合真气而化为圣胎。夫真气自真精而生者也，为气复精归，故喻子投母胎，所谓子恋母而来，母恋子而住，子母相抱，神气相依，即内燃真火外用阴符阳火，内外交炼，即结为圣胎，所谓铅将尽汞亦乾，化为一块紫金霜。金丹大道与生人异者只此处处逆施造化、颠倒乾坤耳。

Then, with this Golden Elixir, the River Chariot is set going, the Wind of Xun (the breathing) is stirred up, and the Kun fire, joined with the true essence of the Li palace, is used to forge it: true breath-energy joins with true essence — that is, Prior Heaven yang energy subdues Posterior Heaven yin essence, and yin essence, joining with true breath-energy, transforms into the Holy Embryo. True breath-energy is born out of true essence, and now the breath returns to the essence, so it is likened to “the child entering the mother’s womb” — as it is said, “the child comes because it longs for the mother, the mother stays because she longs for the child”; child and mother embrace, spirit and breath rely on each other; within, the true fire burns, and without, the yin tally and yang fire are used; refined within and without together, the Holy Embryo is formed — this is what is meant by “the lead nearly spent, the mercury dried up, they transform into a block of purple-gold frost”. The great way of the Golden Elixir differs from begetting people (bearing sons and daughters) only in reversing the workings of creation at every point and turning Qian and Kun upside down.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

古人云：“药本一炁也。”外药、内药（即小药、大药）与圣胎之不同，不过气质之不同而已。

用顺道之常，效夫妇交媾之法，神气相交，玄关窍开所产之药，为小药、外药。而逆施造化，颠倒乾坤，由灵父圣母而育，取坎填离，抽铅添汞，真铅配真汞再加烹炼而生者，为大药、内药，又名金丹。

扩而言之，用守中之法，坎离交媾炼出者为小药、外药。用运行河车之法，乾坤交媾炼出者为内药、大药。外药只足以壮旺下元，冲举肾气，不能结丹，只有大药才能成丹。

至于圣胎，则是把大药作为丹母，再运河车使真气合真精炼成。圣胎又名婴儿。《悟真篇》说：“三家相见结婴儿，婴儿是一含真气，十月胎圆入圣基”，则知圣胎为精气神凝结而成，实亦先天一气。

明白外药、大药与圣胎之区别，乃知采炼次序工程。

The ancients said: “The medicine is at root one breath.” (The medicine is at root just the one Prior Heaven breath.) The differences between the outer medicine, the inner medicine (that is, the lesser and great medicines) and the Holy Embryo are no more than differences in the quality of the breath.

The medicine produced by using the ordinary method of the forward way, imitating the joining of husband and wife, with spirit and breath meeting and the opening of the Mysterious Pass opening, is the lesser, outer medicine. What is nurtured by the spiritual father and holy mother by reversing the workings of creation and turning Qian and Kun upside down — taking from Kan to fill Li, drawing out lead and adding mercury, true lead matched with true mercury and further cooked and refined — is the great, inner medicine, also called the Golden Elixir.

To put it more broadly: what is refined by the method of guarding the center, through the union of Kan and Li, is the lesser, outer medicine; what is refined by the method of turning the River Chariot, through the union of Qian and Kun, is the inner, great medicine. The outer medicine can only strengthen the lower origin and lift the kidney breath; it cannot form the elixir; only the great medicine can become the elixir.

As for the Holy Embryo, it takes the great medicine as the elixir-mother, and is refined by turning the River Chariot again, so that true breath-energy joins with true essence. The Holy Embryo is also called the “infant”. Awakening to Reality says: “When the three families meet, the infant is formed; the infant is one holding of true breath; in ten months the embryo is complete and enters the foundation of sagehood.” (When the three families of essence, breath and spirit meet, the infant is formed; the infant is a mass of true breath; when the embryo is complete in ten months, one enters the foundation of the sage.) From this we know that the Holy Embryo is formed by the congealing of essence, breath and spirit, and is in fact also the one Prior Heaven breath.

Only by understanding the distinction between the outer medicine, the great medicine and the Holy Embryo does one know the order and the work of gathering and refining.

Key Terms

  • Outer medicine, lesser medicine: produced when spirit and breath first meet and the opening of the Mysterious Pass opens; it stirs outside the kidney channel and its breath is small; see Medicine.
  • Inner medicine, great medicine: produced by the union of Qian and Kun; it stirs within the root of the breath; also called the Golden Elixir.
  • Ordinary father and ordinary mother: the Posterior Heaven spirit and breath.
  • Spiritual father and holy mother: the Prior Heaven spirit and breath.
  • Fire entering the land of water: the spirit entering the breath, the spirit-light shining down on the elixir field.
  • True sign: a reliable confirmation.
  • Qian tripod, Kun belly: the head and the abdomen.
  • Heaven answering with stars, earth answering with tides, the six roots shaking: the phenomena when the great medicine is born; see the shaking of the six roots.
  • Holy Embryo: refined by taking the great medicine as the elixir-mother and turning the River Chariot again; also called the infant.
  • The child comes because it longs for the mother, the mother stays because she longs for the child: a metaphor for breath returning to essence, the child entering the mother’s womb.
  • The lead nearly spent, the mercury dried up, they transform into a block of purple-gold frost: a metaphor for forming the elixir.
  • The medicine is at root one breath: the three are only the one Prior Heaven breath, differing in quality.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Distinguish the stages: lesser medicine (warmth in the elixir field, the Mysterious Pass first opening) → great medicine (true yang stirring greatly) → Holy Embryo. Do not take the first warmth for the great medicine.
  2. The lesser medicine only strengthens the lower origin: Qiu Ling says it very clearly — the outer medicine can only strengthen the lower origin and cannot form the elixir. Once you have a little sensation of breath, go on nourishing quietly and do not hurry to circulate it.
  3. The great medicine and Holy Embryo spoken of in this section belong to a very advanced stage; the main thing is to understand them.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is one paragraph; the original book does not give the source. It is divided into four short passages.
  2. “产此为灵父圣母交媾而育者也”: so punctuated in the original book; “产此” belongs with the following clause, meaning that what produces this great medicine is the union of the spiritual father and holy mother, which nurtures it.
  3. “The medicine is at root one breath” (药本一炁也): the original text says only “the ancients said”.
