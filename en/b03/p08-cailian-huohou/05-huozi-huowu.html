Chapter Eight · Gathering, Refining and Fire Phasing · 活子活午火候

Fire Phasing at the Living Zi and Living Wu

Fire phasing at the living zi and living wu — when the living zi hour comes, true knowing is needed; when the living wu hour comes, spiritual knowing is needed; before obtaining, use martial fire; once obtained, nourish warmly with the fire of the spirit; when Qian meets Xun, look at the Cave of the Moon; where Earth meets Thunder, see the Root of Heaven

About This Chapter

This section speaks of the two key moments of fire phasing — the living zi hour and the living wu hour — and of Shao Kangjie’s poem on “the Cave of the Moon and the Root of Heaven”.

  1. Gathering in movement and nourishing in stillness must not be muddled together: if one only knows nourishing in stillness and not gathering in movement, how can it return to the palace? If one only knows gathering in movement and not nourishing in stillness, how can the elixir form?
  2. The living zi hour (yang arising): “dim and indistinct, there is something; obscure and dark, there is essence” — these phenomena are still only figurative images of Prior Heaven yin and yang, not the substance of the Supreme Ultimate. The substance of the Supreme Ultimate is that, as that is touched, this responds, stirring with a single moment of awareness — “when the time comes, the spirit knows”: this is Prior Heaven true knowing, and one must recognize it clearly.
  3. The living wu hour (yin arising): although within Li there is the omen of the essence of utmost yin appearing as an image — “perfectly round, brilliantly shining” — this is still not Prior Heaven true essence. What is real is this: at this moment one is wide awake and unclouded, a natural thought appears, able to be master over the ten thousand changes — the spiritual knowing in the mind. What is most to be feared at this time: beginners who see the omen are delighted, keep a mind of watching it, and want it to last — the moment one lingers, another layer of obstruction is added, and Prior Heaven is clouded by Posterior Heaven. Only neither clinging nor apart, neither forgetting nor helping.
  4. Fire phasing: before the medicine is born, one may count the breaths and regulate the breath (martial fire); once the medicine-breath has returned, one must never again use the water-clock martial fire, but leave it to nature — only then is the original spirit not scattered by the cognitive spirit. Seeking before obtaining, one cannot avoid using martial fire; once obtained, one must not use martial fire again, but nourish warmly with the natural fire of the spirit, and it is enough for the master to sit illuminating in the center. Huang Yuanji says this is “what the elixir classics since ancient times have been unwilling to divulge lightly”.
  5. The Cave of the Moon and the Root of Heaven: Shao Kangjie’s “When Qian meets Xun, look at the Cave of the Moon; where Earth meets Thunder, see the Root of Heaven” is the gist of advancing the yang fire and withdrawing the yin tally.
    • Earth meets Thunder (seeing the Root of Heaven): one yang born beneath pure yin — the living zi hour; from here one gathers and refines.
    • Qian meets Xun (looking at the Cave of the Moon): when yang has risen to its utmost, surging and filling the head and eyes, become still and settled for a moment, stop the fire and do not go on, and without one’s noticing one yin arises — the living wu hour; carry out the withdrawal of the tally, looking down with the eyes at the elixir gate (the lower elixir field).
  6. Ablution at mao and you: advance the fire without advancing too far, carrying out ablution at mao midway; withdraw the tally without withdrawing too far, carrying out ablution at you midway — yin and yang harmonized, nature and life both complete.

Qiu Ling’s note: at the living zi hour, advance the fire, gather and refine with martial fire; at the living wu hour, withdraw the tally, carry out ablution and nourish warmly with civil fire. He explains the Cave of the Moon and the Root of Heaven with the trigrams: Kun ☷ containing Zhen ☳ is “Earth meets Thunder”; Qian ☰ meeting Xun ☴ forms Gou ䷫, which is “Qian meets Xun”. Huang warns: at the living zi hour one must have true knowing and not be bewitched by obscure, dark phenomena; at the living wu hour one must have spiritual knowing and not be bewitched by the “perfectly round, brilliantly shining” omen.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

世人只知静养而不知动采，何以回宫？又或但知动采而不知静养，何以结丹？此处切不可胡混，尤要知活子时到。所谓恍恍惚惚，其中有物；杳杳冥冥，其中有精。有物有精等景象犹是先天阴阳比象，还不是太极之体。太极之体彼感此应，一觉即动，所谓时至神知，即先天之真知。学人须于此认得清，方得先天一气。

People in the world only know nourishing in stillness and do not know gathering in movement (gathering at the moment of movement) — how then can it return to the palace? Or they only know gathering in movement and do not know nourishing in stillness — how then can the elixir form? On this point one must never be muddled. Above all, one must know the arrival of the living zi hour. As it is said, “Dim and indistinct, within it there is something; obscure and dark, within it there is essence” — these phenomena of “something” and “essence” are still only figurative images of Prior Heaven yin and yang, not yet the substance of the Supreme Ultimate itself. The substance of the Supreme Ultimate is that, as that is touched, this responds, stirring with a single moment of awareness — what is called “when the time comes, the spirit knows”: this is Prior Heaven “true knowing”. The student must recognize this clearly here; only then can he obtain the one Prior Heaven breath.

§2
Original (Chinese)

活午时到，离中虽有至阴之精兆而为象，如圆陀陀、光灼灼，犹非先天真精，太极立基之本也。要知此时惺惺不昧，天然一念现前，能为万变主宰。此即古人所谓心中之灵知，先天至真之精发见也。斯时也，在无知之学人偶然朕兆当前，心神欢悦，即存一了照之心，或欲长存下去，如此先天虽本无物，因此一心了照他、留恋他，又添一重障蔽，先天顿为后天所蒙，天心顿为人心所汩。学者于此天然真宰现前，惟有不即不离，勿忘勿助得矣！

When the living wu hour arrives, although within Li there is the omen of the essence of utmost yin appearing as an image, like something “perfectly round, brilliantly shining” (a round, bright ball of light), this is still not Prior Heaven true essence, the root on which the Supreme Ultimate establishes its foundation. Know that at this time one is wide awake and unclouded (clear and lucid), a natural thought appears, able to be master over the ten thousand changes — this is what the ancients called the “spiritual knowing” in the mind, the manifestation of the most true Prior Heaven essence. At this time, a student who does not understand happens to see the omen appear, is delighted at heart, and so keeps a mind of watching it, or wants it to stay forever — and in this way, although in Prior Heaven there is originally nothing, because of this one mind watching it and lingering over it, another layer of obstruction is added; Prior Heaven is at once clouded by Posterior Heaven, and the heavenly mind is at once drowned by the human mind. When this natural true master appears, the student need only be neither clinging nor apart, neither forgetting nor helping, and he has it!

§3
Original (Chinese)

但初行持须要知肾中一阳生而有真知现象；心中一阴生而有灵知兆形。到得工深学久，肾中之真知亦化为灵，心中之灵知亦化为真，真灵合而为一，真灵化而无有。吾观诸子打坐，未尝不是，但未得药生之时，可数息以调息，至于药气已归，切不可再用刻漏武火，须任其天然自然，元神始不为识神打散。诸子行功虽久，不能大生阳气者由于此处少理会也。孔子称颜子得一善，拳拳服膺而弗失。盖未得而求得，不容不用武火，既求而已得，又不可再行武火，须以天然神火温养还丹，主人翁坐照当中足矣！此方合一动一静，一武一文修养之道。吾今日所传，自古丹经不肯轻泄者。

But when you begin to practice, know this: when the one yang arises in the kidneys, there is the phenomenon of “true knowing”; when the one yin arises in the heart, there is the omen of “spiritual knowing”. When the practice deepens and you have studied long, the true knowing in the kidneys also transforms into the spiritual, and the spiritual knowing in the heart also transforms into the true; the true and the spiritual join as one, and in the end even the true and the spiritual transform into nothing. I see you sitting, and it is not that you are doing it wrong, but know this: before the medicine has been born, you may use counting the breaths to regulate the breath; once the medicine-breath has returned, you must never again use the time-keeping martial fire, but must leave it to its own nature; only then is the original spirit not scattered by the cognitive spirit. Although you have practiced a long time, you cannot produce yang energy in abundance, precisely because you are lacking in understanding here. Confucius praised Yan Hui, who, having obtained one good, “held it fast to his breast and never lost it”. Seeking it before obtaining it, one cannot avoid using martial fire; once it has been obtained, one must not use martial fire again, but must nourish the returned elixir warmly with the natural fire of the spirit, and it is enough to let “the master” sit illuminating in the center! Only this accords with the way of cultivation — one movement, one stillness; one martial, one civil. What I transmit today is what the elixir classics since ancient times have been unwilling to divulge lightly.

§4
Original (Chinese)

邵子云：“乾遇巽时观月窟，地逢雷处见天根”二句，即进阳火退阴符之大要也。何谓地逢雷？即坤卦中含孕震卦，震下有一阳来复，即是纯阴之下忽然有一阳生，即阳生活子时也。谓之天根者，以其混沌世界黑暗无光，忽闻一画开天，而阴阳动静迭为升降，天地定位，日月运行，万物之生生不息，此即天之根也。学者须从地下雷动时采之炼之，方有踏实地步。

Master Shao (Shao Kangjie) said: “When Qian meets Xun, look at the Cave of the Moon; where Earth meets Thunder, see the Root of Heaven.” These two lines are the gist of advancing the yang fire and withdrawing the yin tally. What is “Earth meets Thunder”? It is the Kun trigram (☷, earth) holding within it the Zhen trigram (☳, thunder) — at the bottom of Zhen the one yang returns, that is, beneath pure yin a single yang suddenly arises: this is the living zi hour of yang arising. Why is it called “the Root of Heaven”? Because the world of chaos was dark and without light, and suddenly a single stroke opened heaven (Fu Xi drawing the trigrams, opening heaven and earth); from then on, yin and yang, movement and stillness, alternated in rising and falling, heaven and earth took their positions, sun and moon moved in their courses, and the ten thousand things were born without cease — this is the root of heaven. The student must gather and refine from the moment the thunder stirs beneath the earth (the one yang first arising); only then is there solid ground to stand on.

§5
Original (Chinese)

到阳气已极，重阳之下忽有一阴生，此即乾遇巽时也。乾纯阳也，巽为老阴，学人行功而至于阳升已极，蓬蓬勃勃充周于头目之上，其势有不可遏者，我即静定片刻，停火不行，不知不觉即有一阴生来。夫以上行之气机至此而转为下降，即阴生于巽也。到得阴生之时，即真正活午时，我即行退符之法，以目下观丹扃，不似进火之凝神于泥丸，即顺阴生之常矣！是谓之观月窟。

When yang energy has reached its utmost, beneath the doubled yang a single yin suddenly arises — this is the time of “Qian meets Xun”. Qian is pure yang, and Xun (☴) is yin; when in his practice the student reaches the point where yang has risen to its utmost, surging and filling the head and eyes with an irresistible force, I become still and settled for a moment, stop the fire and do not go on, and without my noticing a single yin arises. The breath-dynamic that was moving upward turns here to descend — this is yin arising at Xun. When yin arises, that is the true living wu hour, and I carry out the method of withdrawing the tally: using the eyes to look down and illuminate the elixir gate (the gateway of the lower elixir field), not concentrating the spirit in the Muddy Pellet as when advancing the fire — this is following the ordinary course of yin arising. This is called “looking at the Cave of the Moon”.

§6
Original (Chinese)

至若卯门沐浴，即阳气上进于中正之位，是阴中生阳其半也。故酉沐浴者，即阴气下退于中正之位，是阳中阴生其半也。苟阳气太升，则阴气必亏；阴气太降，则阳气必陷。惟进火而不过进，且于中行卯沐浴之法；退符而不过退，更于中行酉沐浴之方，自然阴阳燮理，性命双完矣！诸子每日行功，到阳气一生务要顺其上升之常，若稍有壮旺，即行卯沐浴法。到阴阳一起，即行下降之功，恐阴气太盛，即行酉沐浴法。定静片晌，不行火不退符。如此暂休，到得纯任自然，斯道得矣！若阴阳反覆，两两归于中黄宫内，当行温养之法。总之，学道之要惟以真意为主，所谓以真土擒真铅，以真铅制真汞，三家合一，两性交欢，斯道在是矣！

As for ablution at the mao gate, it is when yang energy has advanced upward to the central, balanced position, halfway in the birth of yang within yin; ablution at the you gate is when yin energy has withdrawn downward to the central, balanced position, halfway in the birth of yin within yang. If yang energy rises too far, yin energy is bound to be depleted; if yin energy descends too far, yang energy is bound to sink and drown. Only by advancing the fire without advancing too far, carrying out ablution at mao midway, and withdrawing the tally without withdrawing too far, carrying out ablution at you midway, are yin and yang naturally harmonized, and nature and life both made whole! In your daily practice: as soon as yang energy arises, be sure to follow its ordinary course of rising; if it becomes a little strong, carry out the method of ablution at mao; as soon as yin energy arises, carry out the work of descending; if you fear yin energy becoming too abundant, carry out the method of ablution at you — be settled and still for a moment, neither moving the fire nor withdrawing the tally. Resting for a while in this way, until you reach pure reliance on nature, the Way is obtained! If yin and yang go back and forth, and both return into the palace of the Central Yellow, one should carry out the method of warm nourishing. In short, the essentials of studying the Way rest only on true intention as the master — as it is said, “with true earth seize true lead, with true lead subdue true mercury; the three families join as one, the two natures delight in union” — the Way is right here!

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§6
Original note (Chinese)

邵子即汉代邵康节，著有月窟天根诗。

活子时到，即阳生之候，当行进火采炼，行武火；活午时到，即阴生之候，当行退符沐浴，行文火温养。

邵康节诗月窟天根二句乃此段功法及火候写照。坤卦（☷）象为地，震卦（☳）象为雷。地逢雷，即坤卦中含孕震卦，震卦为长男得乾卦（☰）初爻而成，即震下有一阳来复，纯阴之下有一阳生，活子时到，谓之见天根。乾卦（☰）象为纯阳，巽卦（☴）象为老阴，乾遇巽，即乾上巽下变为姤卦（䷫），一阴与五阳相遇，重阳之下忽生一阴，活午时到，谓之观月窟。此不过阴中含阳，阳中有阴，阴极阳动，阳极阴生之理。

黄氏于此警告学者，阴极阳动即活子时到来之际，须有真知，勿为杳杳冥冥景象所迷，一觉而醒，时至神知，把握采炼之机。阳极阴生即活午时到来之际，须有灵知，勿为眼前圆陀陀、光灼灼朕兆所迷，要不即不离，勿忘勿助，把握沐浴温养之候。此正是动采与静养不可胡混之良法。

Master Shao is Shao Kangjie, who wrote the poem on “the Cave of the Moon and the Root of Heaven”.

When the living zi hour arrives, it is the time of yang arising, and one should advance the fire, gather and refine, using martial fire; when the living wu hour arrives, it is the time of yin arising, and one should withdraw the tally and carry out ablution, nourishing warmly with civil fire.

The two lines on “the Cave of the Moon” and “the Root of Heaven” in Shao Kangjie’s poem are exactly a portrait of this practice and its fire phasing. The Kun trigram (☷) symbolizes earth, and the Zhen trigram (☳) symbolizes thunder. “Earth meets Thunder” is the Kun trigram holding within it the Zhen trigram — Zhen is the eldest son, formed when Kun receives the first line of the Qian trigram (☰); that is, at the bottom of Zhen one yang returns, beneath pure yin one yang arises, and the living zi hour arrives: this is called “seeing the Root of Heaven”. The Qian trigram (☰) symbolizes pure yang, and the Xun trigram (☴) symbolizes yin; “Qian meets Xun” is Qian above and Xun below, becoming the Gou hexagram (䷫), one yin meeting five yang, beneath the doubled yang a single yin suddenly arising, and the living wu hour arrives: this is called “looking at the Cave of the Moon”. This is no more than the principle that yin contains yang, yang holds yin, at the extreme of yin yang stirs, and at the extreme of yang yin arises.

Here Huang warns the student: at the extreme of yin, when yang stirs and the living zi hour arrives, one must have “true knowing”, not be bewitched by obscure, dark phenomena, but awaken at a single moment of awareness — when the time comes, the spirit knows — and seize the moment for gathering and refining; at the extreme of yang, when yin arises and the living wu hour arrives, one must have “spiritual knowing”, not be bewitched by the “perfectly round, brilliantly shining” omen before one’s eyes, but be neither clinging nor apart, neither forgetting nor helping, and seize the time for ablution and warm nourishing. This is exactly the good method by which gathering in movement and nourishing in stillness are not muddled together.

Key Terms

  • Gathering in movement, nourishing in stillness: gathering when yang arises, nourishing warmly when yin arises.
  • Living Zi Hour: the time of yang arising, stirring with a single moment of awareness; when the time comes, the spirit knows.
  • Living wu hour: the time of yin arising; wide awake and unclouded, a natural thought appears.
  • True knowing, spiritual knowing: the knowing when the one yang arises in the kidneys, and the knowing when the one yin arises in the heart.
  • Perfectly round, brilliantly shining: the round light seen in stillness; not true essence, not to be lingered over.
  • Water-clock martial fire: martial fire that keeps time by counting the breaths.
  • Held it fast to his breast and never lost it: the Doctrine of the Mean‘s words in praise of Yan Hui.
  • The master: the original spirit.
  • When Qian meets Xun, look at the Cave of the Moon; where Earth meets Thunder, see the Root of Heaven: a poem by Shao Kangjie.
  • Root of Heaven: one yang born beneath pure yin; the living zi hour.
  • Cave of the Moon: one yin born beneath doubled yang; the living wu hour.
  • Elixir gate: the gateway of the lower elixir field.
  • Ablution at mao, ablution at you: stopping the fire and nourishing warmly midway through advancing the fire and withdrawing the tally; see ablution.
  • Harmonizing (燮理): bringing into balance.

Putting It into Practice

  1. When you see light, do not cling to it: if a round light or brightness appears in stillness and you feel delight and want to keep it — that very thought is the obstruction. Neither clinging nor apart; let it come and go.
  2. Once it is obtained, stop the martial fire: before the medicine is born you may count the breaths; as soon as there is a warm, full stirring, drop the counting at once, leave it to nature, and simply sit illuminating with true intention.
  3. When yang rises to the head, stop: when the breath-dynamic fills the head and eyes with an irresistible force, become still and settled for a moment, stop the fire and do not go on, and wait for it to turn naturally into descending; as it descends, let the eyes’ gaze shine lightly down on the elixir field.
  4. Safety note: the time when “yang has risen to its utmost, filling the head and eyes” is exactly when deviations happen most easily. Huang Yuanji’s method is to become still and settled, stop the fire, and turn to shining down on the elixir field — never to keep pushing upward. If a swollen head, headache, flushed face or ringing in the ears appear, relax at once and rest the intention lightly on the lower belly or the soles of the feet.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is two paragraphs; the original book does not give the source. The first paragraph is divided into three short passages, and the second into three.
  2. “Qian is pure yang, Xun is old yin” (乾纯阳也，巽为老阴): so in the original book. Among the eight trigrams Xun is usually called “the eldest daughter”, a yin trigram; here “old yin” is used in contrast to Qian’s pure yang, and the translation renders it simply as “yin”.
  3. Qiu Ling’s note says “Master Shao is Shao Kangjie of the Han dynasty”: Shao Kangjie (Shao Yong) lived in the Northern Song; “Han” should be “Song”.
  4. The trigram symbols (☷☳☰☴䷫) are printed as trigram drawings in the original book; standard symbols are used here instead.
