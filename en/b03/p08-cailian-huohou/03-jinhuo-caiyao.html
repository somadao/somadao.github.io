Chapter Eight · Gathering, Refining and Fire Phasing · 进火采药之法

The Method of Advancing the Fire and Gathering the Medicine

The method of advancing the fire and gathering the medicine — advancing the fire is concentrating the spirit with a single will, gathering the medicine is the outer breathing naturally wrapping around it; like a blacksmith’s furnace and bellows; when restless breath surges during sitting, nourish warmly with the original fire of the true person

About This Chapter

This section treats “advancing the fire” and “gathering the medicine” separately, and gives the remedy for restless breath surging during sitting.

  1. Two things: in Posterior Heaven practice, advancing the fire and gathering the medicine are two items, not one.
    • Advancing the fire = concentrating the spirit with a single, undivided will: when yang stirs and the medicine is born, concentrate the inner spirit single-mindedly in the elixir tripod.
    • Gathering the medicine = the outer breathing rising and falling, going out and coming in, following its own nature, wrapping the medicine around.
  2. Neither can be lacking: if only the outer breathing comes and goes and the spirit is not concentrated in the elixir tripod, then although the true dynamic arises, it scatters through the whole body with nowhere to settle; if one only concentrates with the intention and does not wrap it with the breathing, the medicine stays where it is and can only strengthen the lower origin.
  3. The blacksmith metaphor: advancing the fire is like adding firewood and charcoal to the furnace; gathering the medicine is like working the bellows. Only working the bellows without adding charcoal, the fire is not strong and the metal does not melt; only adding charcoal without working the bellows, the furnace is merely warm.
  4. A higher level: “gathering the great medicine within non-action, carrying out fire phasing within non-movement” — fire and medicine as one, advancing and gathering undivided. But the disciples have not yet reached this step.
  5. Counting the breaths in ordinary times: when there is no yang arising, use counting the breaths to gather the mind and keep it from running about; when yang arises and the medicine is produced, gather it into the furnace and nourish warmly with the fire of the spirit — “using fire without fire, gathering the medicine without medicine”.
  6. What to do when restless breath surges: if during sitting you feel restless breath surging and unease, either the intention is too tight, or yin breath in your own body has congealed and stagnated. The remedy is to nourish it warmly with the ordinary fire of breathing and the original fire of the true person, letting it dissolve by itself. The original fire of the true person is “ears, eyes and mouth, the three treasures — close them and let nothing out; the true person sinks into the deep abyss, floating and roaming, keeping to the compass’s center” — using without using. There is also the surging of breath when yang energy is very strong and the River Chariot is about to be turned, but then spirit and breath are at ease and the mind has no other thought; if the mind is at peace and the breath harmonious, one may practice the Lesser Celestial Circuit. One must judge for oneself.

Qiu Ling’s note: fire phasing is of two kinds, “with phases and with action” and “without phases and without action” — advancing the fire and gathering the medicine belong to the former, used in the Lesser Celestial Circuit; gathering the great medicine within non-action belongs to the latter, nourishing warmly with the True Breath, used in the Greater Celestial Circuit. Awakening to Reality says that although non-action is wondrous, its foundation is action. When restless and agitated during sitting, overcome it with the original fire of the true person: that is, withdraw the sight and turn back the hearing, close the mouth and hold the saliva, and revert the light to shine on the elixir field.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

进火采药在后天原是两项，不是一事。夫进火者凝神一志不分也。采药是用外呼吸之气一升一降，一出一入，顺其自然是也。若阳动药生之时，即将内之精神一意凝于丹鼎，即是进火。将外之呼吸出入升降以包裹之，即是采药。进火是进火，采药是采药，不可混而为一也。若但用外呼吸升降往还，而神不凝于丹鼎，则虽真机勃发，必散漫一身而无归宿之处。若但见阳气勃发，以意凝注而不用后天呼吸以包裹之，则药止于其所，惟以壮旺下元冲举肾气而已。

Advancing the fire and gathering the medicine are, in Posterior Heaven practice, originally two items, not one thing. Advancing the fire is concentrating the spirit with a single will, without the slightest scattering; gathering the medicine is using the breath of the outer breathing, rising and falling, going out and coming in, following its own nature. When yang stirs and the medicine is born, concentrating the inner spirit single-mindedly in the elixir tripod (the lower elixir field) is advancing the fire; wrapping it with the going out and coming in, rising and falling, of the outer breathing is gathering the medicine. Advancing the fire is advancing the fire, gathering the medicine is gathering the medicine — they must not be lumped together. If one only uses the outer breathing rising and falling, coming and going, and the spirit is not concentrated in the elixir tripod, then although the true dynamic bursts forth, it is bound to scatter through the whole body with nowhere to settle; if one only sees yang energy bursting forth and concentrates on it with the intention, without wrapping it with the Posterior Heaven breathing, then the medicine stays where it is and can only strengthen the lower origin and lift the kidney breath.

§2
Original (Chinese)

生等若未了然，吾再喻之。夫进火犹铁匠之炉而加以柴炭也。采药犹铁匠之风箱而抽动之也。若但抽其风箱而炉中不加以炭火，则火不雄而金不化。若但加以炭火而手中不抽动其箱，纵有柴有炭亦只温温炉内而已，安望炼成有用之物哉！生等思之，火是火，药是药，进是进，采是采，后天功法原是如此。他如采大药于无为之内，行火候于不动之中，此是火药合一，进采无分。生等此时功夫尚未到此，以后阳生之时还要自家审得归真地步，方是有为无为、有作无作的实际。

If you still do not understand, let me give another comparison: advancing the fire is like a blacksmith adding firewood and charcoal to his furnace; gathering the medicine is like a blacksmith working the bellows. If he only works the bellows without adding charcoal fire to the furnace, the fire is not strong and the metal will not melt; if he only adds charcoal fire without working the bellows, then even with firewood and charcoal the furnace is merely warm — how could anything useful be forged! Think about it: fire is fire, medicine is medicine, advancing is advancing, gathering is gathering — Posterior Heaven practice is originally like this. As for “gathering the great medicine within non-action, carrying out fire phasing within non-movement”, that is fire and medicine as one, advancing and gathering undivided; your practice has not yet reached this step; later, when yang arises, you must yourselves examine your way to the stage of returning to the true — only then is it the reality of action and non-action, of doing and not-doing.

§3
Original (Chinese)

吾教生等用数息之法，以收敛其心志，平居无阳之时有此功法，可以把持自家的心不致乱走。一到阳生药产须采之归炉，神火温养，尤须要用火无火，采药无药，方合天地细缊，元气可以生生不已，化化无穷者焉。至于一阳初动用提掇之法，此是生等迩时之功，亦不外内之神思聚而不散，外之气息调其自然而已耳。

I teach you the method of counting the breaths to gather in the mind: in ordinary times, when there is no yang arising, this practice lets you hold your mind so that it does not run about wildly. As soon as yang arises and the medicine is produced, you must gather it into the furnace and nourish it warmly with the fire of the spirit, and above all achieve “using fire without fire, gathering the medicine without medicine” (using fire without feeling that you are using fire, gathering the medicine without feeling that you are gathering the medicine) — only then does it accord with the principle of the mingling of heaven and earth, and the original breath can be born without end and transform without limit. As for the method of “lifting” used when the one yang first stirs, this is your practice of late, and it is nothing other than: the spirit and thoughts within gathered and not scattered, the breath without regulated to its natural state.

§4
Original (Chinese)

生每打坐时觉得躁气冲动，有不安之意，此不是意思打紧，即是自己色身上阴气凝滞。法当用呼吸之凡火、真人之元火以温养之，使之自化而后可。何谓真人元火？古云：“耳目口三宝，闭塞勿发通，真人潜深渊，浮游守规中。”此即真人元火，用而不用者也。生等向自家身心上体认到恰好处，行持到极当时，自无此躁气焉。不然，或阳气太旺，将用河车之际，亦有此气息冲冲之状，然其神气自若而心无他也。若是心安气和，又当运用河车行小周天功法。生其自审度可也。

Each time you sit, you feel restless breath surging and a sense of unease — either the intention is held too tightly, or yin breath in your own body has congealed and stagnated. The method is to nourish it warmly with the ordinary fire of breathing and the original fire of the true person, letting it dissolve by itself. What is the original fire of the true person? The ancients said: “Ears, eyes and mouth, the three treasures — close them and let nothing out; the true person sinks into the deep abyss, floating and roaming, keeping to the compass’s center.” (Ears, eyes and mouth, these three treasures, are closed and not opened outward; the true person is hidden in the deep abyss, floating at ease, keeping to the center of the compass.) This is the original fire of the true person, which is “using without using”. When you recognize in your own body and mind just the right measure, and practice it until it is utterly fitting, naturally there will be no such restless breath. Otherwise, it may be that yang energy is very strong and the River Chariot is about to be turned, when there can also be this surging of breath; but then spirit and breath are at ease and there are no other thoughts in the mind. If the mind is at peace and the breath harmonious, one may turn the River Chariot and practice the Lesser Celestial Circuit. Judge it for yourselves.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

采药火候可分有候有为及无候无为两种。进火采药当属前一种，进火为凝神，采药用外呼吸，即有作之候，用于小周天功法。采大药于无为之内，行火候于不动之中，则属后一种，用真息温养，用于大周天功法。此两种火候视时机如何而分别采用之。《悟真篇》说：“始于有作无人见，及至无为家始知。但信无为为要妙，孰知有作是根基。”无为虽妙，根基却是有作。有作行于小周天，炼成大药必靠有为，如无大药又何以言成丹与结胎？

打坐往往觉有燥急之意，当用真人元火以克服之。真人元火即是“耳目口三宝，闭塞勿发通，真人潜深渊，浮游守规中”之意。此语出自《周易参同契》，即收视返听，闭口含津，将元神常栖于泥丸之地，而回光返照丹田。

The fire phasing of gathering the medicine can be divided into two kinds: “with phases and with action” and “without phases and without action”. Advancing the fire and gathering the medicine belong to the first kind — advancing the fire is concentrating the spirit, gathering the medicine uses the outer breathing; this is fire phasing with action, used in the practice of the Lesser Celestial Circuit. “Gathering the great medicine within non-action, carrying out fire phasing within non-movement” belongs to the second kind, nourishing warmly with the True Breath, used in the practice of the Greater Celestial Circuit. These two kinds of fire phasing are to be used separately according to the right time. Awakening to Reality says: “It begins with action, which no one sees; when it comes to non-action, the household first knows. People only believe that non-action is the wondrous key — who knows that action is the foundation?” (It begins with action, which no one can see; when it reaches non-action, everyone then knows. People only believe that non-action is the wondrous key; who knows that action is the foundation?) Although non-action is wondrous, its foundation is action. Action is carried out in the Lesser Celestial Circuit, and refining the great medicine must rely on action; without the great medicine, how can one speak of forming the elixir and forming the embryo?

During sitting one often feels restless and agitated, and should overcome it with the original fire of the true person. The original fire of the true person is the meaning of “ears, eyes and mouth, the three treasures — close them and let nothing out; the true person sinks into the deep abyss, floating and roaming, keeping to the compass’s center”. This line comes from the Zhouyi cantong qi, and it means withdrawing the sight and turning back the hearing (the eyes not looking outward, the ears not listening outward), closing the mouth and holding the saliva, letting the original spirit dwell in the region of the Muddy Pellet, and reverting the light to shine back on the elixir field.

Key Terms

  • Advancing the fire: concentrating the spirit in the elixir tripod when yang stirs and the medicine is born.
  • Gathering the medicine: wrapping the medicine with the natural going out and coming in of the outer breathing.
  • Elixir tripod: the lower elixir field.
  • The blacksmith’s furnace and bellows: metaphors for advancing the fire and gathering the medicine.
  • Gathering the great medicine within non-action, carrying out fire phasing within non-movement: fire and medicine as one, advancing and gathering undivided.
  • Counting the breaths: the method for gathering the mind in ordinary times; see regulating the breath.
  • Using fire without fire, gathering the medicine without medicine: advancing the fire and gathering the medicine without fixing the intention.
  • Lifting: lifting lightly when the one yang first stirs.
  • Restless breath surging: caused by holding the intention too tightly or by yin breath congealing and stagnating in the body.
  • Original fire of the true person: withdrawing the sight and turning back the hearing, closing the mouth and holding the saliva, keeping to the compass’s center; see Three Lights.
  • Ears, eyes and mouth, the three treasures — close them and let nothing out; the true person sinks into the deep abyss, floating and roaming, keeping to the compass’s center: a line from the Zhouyi cantong qi.
  • With phases and with action, without phases and without action: the fire phasing of the Lesser and Greater Celestial Circuits; see Celestial Circuit.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Concentrated spirit + natural breathing: when yang arises, let the mind rest lightly and concentrated in the elixir field (advancing the fire), while the breathing goes in and out naturally as usual (gathering the medicine), the two working together. Do not hold the breath, and do not let the spirit scatter.
  2. Count the breaths in ordinary times: when no yang is arising, counting the breaths to gather the mind is enough.
  3. The remedy for restless surging breath (the most practical point): when during sitting you feel agitated and the breath surges upward, first check whether you are holding the intention too tightly — if so, relax; then withdraw the sight and turn back the hearing, close the mouth and hold the saliva, revert the light to shine on the elixir field, and let it dissolve by itself.
  4. The Lesser Celestial Circuit is not a must: it only comes into question when the mind is at peace, the breath harmonious, and spirit and breath at ease; otherwise go on nourishing quietly.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is one paragraph; the original book does not give the source. It is divided into four short passages.
  2. “This is your practice of late” (此是生等迩时之功): ershi 迩时 means recently, of late.
  3. In Qiu Ling’s note, the quotation from Awakening to Reality reads “when it comes to non-action, the household first knows” (及至无为家始知); the original book prints 家 (“household”), reproduced as it stands; the line usually reads 众始知 (“everyone then knows”), probably a typesetting error.
  4. “Letting the original spirit dwell constantly in the region of the Muddy Pellet, and reverting the light to shine back on the elixir field” (将元神常栖于泥丸之地，而回光返照丹田): so in Qiu Ling’s note, reproduced as it stands; in light of Qiu Ling’s note in Chapter One, people with yin deficiency and yang excess, or with high blood pressure, should not fix the intention on the head, and should mainly revert the light to shine on the elixir field.
