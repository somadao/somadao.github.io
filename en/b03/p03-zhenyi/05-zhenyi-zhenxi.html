Chapter Three · The True Intention · 真意与真息、真气

The True Intention, the True Breath and True Breath-Energy

The true intention, the true breath and true breath-energy — the true intention is the spirit that unites, the true breath the means of uniting; gather the true breath-energy with the true intention, and when they merge into one, that is called the elixir

About This Chapter

The last section of this chapter places the true intention within the relationship of the three — “spirit, breathing, breath-energy”:

  1. Division of labor: the true intention is the “spirit” that makes spirit and breath unite in refining the elixir; the true breath is the “means” of making spirit and breath unite. Without the true breath, the true breath-energy cannot rise and fall, meet and form the elixir by itself; with the true breath but without the true intention to command and hold it, the true breath cannot come and go freely either.
  2. The true intention comes from the Original Spirit: only at the first opening of the Mysterious Pass does one recognize this point of true intention and then turn back to hold it. What flows forth from the Original Spirit is the true intention, the “One” of “obtain the One and all things are accomplished”.
  3. Two meanings of “subduing the breath”:
    • hiding the breath away, returning it to the central palace, at peace and unmoving;
    • strictly subduing the Posterior Heaven ordinary breath, not allowing the going in and out of inner and outer breathing to shake one’s original spirit and breath.
  4. Where the dual cultivation of nature and life lands: “all images empty, one numinous point shining alone” is the true intention (nature); “one awakening and it stirs, one yang arising” is the original breath (life). Without the true intention, nature has nothing to rely on; without the true breath-energy, life cannot be cultivated. Gather the true breath-energy with the true intention, and when they merge into one, that is the elixir.
  5. Action and non-action: within non-action there must be action, within not-knowing there must be awareness — only then does one neither fall into emptiness nor cling to being. When the work is deep, emptiness and form are both forgotten, and one is in accord with the Way.

Qiu Ling’s note: the relationship of the true intention to the true breath and true breath-energy is in fact the relationship of spirit and breath. Without the true breath, the breath of mouth and nose will surely disturb the Original Spirit; without the true intention, the true breath-energy cannot be gathered, and spirit and breath cannot unite.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

真意者炼丹交合之神，真息者炼丹交合之具，要皆以神气二者合而为一而已矣！第无真息，则真气不能自升自降，会合温养结成玄珠。既得真息，若无真意为之号令摄持严密，则真息亦不能往来进退，如如自如。故曰：真意者炼丹之要。

The true intention is the “spirit” that makes spirit and breath unite in refining the elixir; the True Breath is the “means” that makes spirit and breath unite in refining the elixir — in short, all for the sake of making spirit and breath unite into one! But without the true breath, the true breath-energy cannot rise and fall and meet by itself, and through warm nourishing form the mysterious pearl (the elixir). With the true breath, if there is no true intention to give commands and hold it firmly, the true breath cannot come and go, advance and withdraw, at ease and free either. So it is said: the true intention is the key to refining the elixir.

§2
Original (Chinese)

然真意不得真正元神，则真意从何而始？惟于玄关窍开之初，认取这点真意，于是返而持之，斯得之矣！况元神所流露即是真意，即是一善，亦即得一而万事毕之道。学人认得分明，大丹之本立矣！

Yet if the true intention does not issue from the genuine Original Spirit, where would the true intention begin? Only at the moment the opening of the Mysterious Pass first opens, recognize this point of true intention, and then turn back to hold it — and so you have it! Moreover, what flows forth from the Original Spirit is the true intention, the “one goodness” (that point of pure goodness), which is the principle of “obtain the One and all things are accomplished” (obtain this “One”, and everything is completed). When the student recognizes it clearly, the root of the great elixir is established!

§3
Original (Chinese)

昔邱祖云：“息有一毫之未定，命非己有”。吾示学人欲求长生，先须伏气，然伏有二义：一是伏藏此气归于中宫，如如不动；一是管摄严密降伏后天凡息，不许内外呼吸出入动摇吾固有之神气。

Formerly Patriarch Qiu (Qiu Chuji) said: “As long as the breath is not settled in the slightest, nature and life are not one’s own.” I tell students: if you seek long life, first “subdue the breath”. But “subduing” has two meanings: first, hiding this breath away, returning it to the central palace (the lower elixir field), at peace and unmoving; second, strictly governing and subduing the Posterior Heaven ordinary breath (breathing through mouth and nose), not allowing the going in and out of inner and outer breathing to shake my original spirit and breath.

§4
Original (Chinese)

吾前云：“万象咸空，一灵独照”，此为真意。又曰：一觉而动，一阳发生，是为元气。采药炼丹不过炼此性命二者。若无真意，性将何依？若无真气，命何由修？以真意采真气，两者浑化为一，即返于太极之初，斯谓之丹。故无为之中又要有作有为，无知之内又要有知有觉，方不堕空不著有，迨至功力弥深，空即是色，色即是空，久之空色两忘，浑然物化，斯与道大适矣！

I said earlier: “All images are empty, one numinous point shines alone” — this is the true intention. I also said: “One awakening and it stirs, one yang arises” — this is the original breath. Gathering the medicine and refining the elixir is nothing but refining these two, nature and life. Without the true intention, what does nature (the mind-spirit) rely on? Without the true breath-energy, from where is life (the original breath in the body) to be cultivated? Use the true intention to gather the true breath-energy, the two merge undividedly into one body and return to the beginning of the Supreme Ultimate (the source before yin and yang divided) — this is called “the elixir”. So, within non-action there must also be action, within not-knowing there must also be knowing and awareness; only then does one neither fall into emptiness nor cling to being. When the work grows ever deeper, “emptiness is form, form is emptiness” (emptiness and being are no longer opposed); in time, both emptiness and form are forgotten, and one merges undividedly with all things — this is complete accord with the Way!

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

细味之，真意与真息、真气之关系，实即神与气之关系而已。真意乃元神所化，若无真息，则口鼻之气必扰我元神；若无真意，则真气又何由采，神气又何由合？举一反三，可知三者关系之密切。

On careful reflection, the relationship of the true intention to the true breath and true breath-energy is in fact the relationship of spirit and breath. The true intention is transformed from the Original Spirit; without the true breath, the breath of mouth and nose will surely disturb my Original Spirit; without the true intention, from where could the true breath-energy be gathered, and how could spirit and breath unite? Reasoning from one case to the others, one can see how close the relationship of the three is.

Key Terms

  • The spirit that unites, the means of uniting: the true intention is the master that makes spirit and breath unite; the true breath is the means by which they unite.
  • True Breath: see Chapter Two.
  • Mysterious pearl: another name for the elixir; see Golden Flower.
  • Commanding and holding: giving orders, gathering and holding.
  • One goodness: that point of pure goodness flowing forth from the Original Spirit, that is, the true intention.
  • Obtain the One and all things are accomplished: obtain the fundamental “One”, and everything is achieved.
  • Subduing the breath: first, hiding the original breath away and returning it to the central palace; second, subduing the Posterior Heaven ordinary breath.
  • Central palace: in this book, the lower elixir field.
  • Nature, life: nature is the mind-spirit, life the original breath in the body; see inner nature / life.
  • One numinous point shining alone: amid the emptiness of all images, one point of numinous clarity shining alone — that is, the true intention.
  • Neither falling into emptiness nor clinging to being: not falling into dull emptiness, nor clinging to traces of form; see dull emptiness.
  • Emptiness is form, form is emptiness: from the Buddhist Heart Sutra; emptiness and being are not two.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The division of labor among the three: the true intention is the presiding spirit, the true breath is the means by which spirit and breath unite, the true breath-energy is the medicine gathered. None of the three can be lacking.
  2. Where to recognize the true intention: at the first opening of the Mysterious Pass, that instant of one awakening and it stirs — recognize it, then hold it.
  3. Subduing the breath is not holding the breath: “subduing the Posterior Heaven ordinary breath” means letting the breathing of mouth and nose no longer disturb spirit and breath; the method is still the natural drawing-in and listening to the breath taught in Chapter Two, not forcibly closing off the breath.
  4. Action and non-action: within non-action, that point of presiding; within not-knowing, that point of awakened illumination — neither falling into emptiness nor clinging.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is in two paragraphs; the original book does not give the source. The first paragraph is divided into three short passages.
  2. “But without the true breath” (第无真息): di 第 means “but, if only”.
  3. “Formerly Patriarch Qiu said”: Patriarch Qiu is Qiu Chuji, founding patriarch of the Longmen branch of Quanzhen. Under the Qing, to avoid the taboo on Confucius’s personal name (Qiu 丘), the character 丘 was often written 邱.
  4. “Emptiness is form, form is emptiness”: from the Buddhist Heart Sutra (Prajñāpāramitā Hṛdaya).
