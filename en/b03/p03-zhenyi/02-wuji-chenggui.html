Chapter Three · The True Intention · 戊己成圭

Wu and Ji Form the Gui

Wu and ji form the gui — the intention in stillness is ji earth, the intention in movement is wu earth; one intention a little early and the true spring has not yet appeared, one breath a little late and ordinary thoughts have already arisen

About This Chapter

This section explains the two uses of the true intention: in stillness and in movement. Alchemists represent them by “wu earth” and “ji earth” — the ancients compared the intention to “earth” (earth occupies the center and can harmonize the four directions), and the two “earth” characters together make gui 圭, hence “wu and ji form the gui”.

  1. Ji earth (yin earth) — the intention in stillness: when movement reaches its extreme and turns to stillness, the true intention presides. Then, dim and indistinct, yin and yang unite, and one goes deep into the obscure.
  2. The appearance of true lead: in concentrated stillness, suddenly a thread of warm breath rises continuously, the breath flowing freely and the spirit melting, meeting in the Yellow Court (in this book, the lower elixir field); the flower of the heart opens and a warm feeling spreads — this is the sign of the Mysterious Pass.
  3. Wu earth (yang earth) — the intention in movement: at this moment use a point of true mind to issue the true intention and gather it in. In fact it is only one intention, divided into wu and ji according to movement and stillness.
  4. Timing: use the intention a little early, and the true spring has not yet appeared, so gathering does no good; one breath late, and ordinary thoughts have already arisen, so what is gathered is mixed. One must recognize it clearly and hold it firmly within this single breath.
  5. When it first opens the breath is soft; in time it gains strength: at first there is only a faint, slight warmth rising from the lower origin; preside over it with the true intention without scattering, and in time the breath’s dynamism gains great strength; then let it move as it will, adding no more intention.

Qiu Ling’s note compares the true intention to a switch: if one can turn it on, one can reach utmost emptiness and steadfast stillness and the opening of the Mysterious Pass. He also explains the “Living Zi Hour” — there is no need to cling to the zi hour at midnight; whenever in the day one can turn on the switch of the true intention, that is the Living Zi Hour, and the practice is as effective as at the true zi hour; once practiced, one can do the work while walking, standing, sitting or lying down.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

动极而静之际，忽来真意以主持之，此意属阴，谓之己土。少焉恍恍惚惚，阴阳交媾，大入杳冥之境，似梦非梦，似醒非醒，于此定静之中，忽觉一缕热气混混续续，气畅神融，两两交会于黄庭之间，将判未判，未判忽判，此即真铅现象，心花发露，暖气融融，元神跃跃，不由感触，自然发生，斯乃玄关兆象，太极开基也。

When movement reaches its extreme and turns to stillness, suddenly the true intention comes to preside; this intention belongs to yin and is called “ji earth” (the true intention in stillness). Before long, dim and indistinct, yin and yang unite, and one goes deep into the realm of the obscure, as if dreaming yet not dreaming, as if awake yet not awake; and within this concentrated stillness, one suddenly feels a thread of warm breath arising continuously, the breath flowing freely and the spirit melting, the two meeting in the Yellow Court (in this book, the lower elixir field), about to divide yet not divided, not divided yet suddenly dividing — this is the scene of true lead (the Prior Heaven true yang breath) appearing: the flower of the heart opens, a warm feeling spreads, the Original Spirit is eager to stir; it is not brought about by the touch of outer things but arises naturally — this is the sign of the Mysterious Pass, that is, “the Supreme Ultimate laying its foundation” (the vital impulse beginning from here).

§2
Original (Chinese)

斯时惟用一点真心发真意以收摄之，此意属阳，为戊土。其实一意不过以动静之基分为戊己之土而已。盖玄牝未开，混沌之中有此真意为主，即无欲观妙之意，谓之阴土。及玄牝开而真机现，即有欲以观其窍，谓之阳土。一为无名天地之始，一为有名万物之母，生天生地，生人生物，皆此一点真意为之贯注。

At this moment simply use a point of true mind to issue the true intention and gather it in; this intention belongs to yang and is called “wu earth” (the true intention in movement). In fact it is only one intention, divided into wu earth and ji earth according to the two states of movement and stillness. For before the gate of the Mysterious Female opens, this true intention presides in the midst of chaos — it is the intention of “without desire, observe its wonder”, called “yin earth”; when the gate of the Mysterious Female opens and the true spring appears, it is “with desire, observe its opening”, called “yang earth”. The one is “nameless, the beginning of Heaven and Earth”; the other is “named, the mother of the ten thousand things”; giving birth to Heaven and Earth, to people and things, it is all this one point of true intention pouring through.

§3
Original (Chinese)

修行人能以真意主宰运行，庶不至感而有思，动而他驰，所谓天关由我，地轴由心，宇宙在乎身，万化生于心，皆此时之灵觉为之运用而主持也。故曰：略先一意则真机未现，采之无益；略后一息，则凡念已起，采之又多夹杂，不堪为我炼丹大药。此须有大智慧大力量，方能于此一息中，认得清，把得定。

If the practitioner can use the true intention to preside over the movement, perhaps he will not, at every contact, give rise to thoughts, nor at every stirring rush outward. What is called “the pass of Heaven is up to me, the axis of Earth is up to the mind; the universe lies in the body, and all transformations are born from the mind” (the opening and closing of Heaven and Earth is mine to command; the universe is within my body; all changes arise from the mind) — all of it is this point of numinous awareness at work and presiding at this moment. So it is said: use the intention a little early, and the true spring has not yet appeared, so gathering is useless; one breath a little late, and ordinary thoughts have already arisen, so what is gathered is much mixed, and cannot serve as the great medicine for refining my elixir. This takes great wisdom and great strength, to recognize it clearly and hold it firmly within this single breath.

§4
Original (Chinese)

虽然此个玄关，始而其气柔脆，只觉微有热意从下元起，久则踊跃周身似有不可遏抑之势。学人须于至微处辨得明白，以我真意主持，毫不分散，久之气机大有力量，一任兀兀腾腾，随其所至，不加一意，不参一见，斯得之耳。到得气机壮旺，一静即天机发动，迅速如雷，虽一切喧哗之乡不能禁止，总要有灵觉之心为之主持，乃无差也已。

Even so, when this Mysterious Pass first opens, its breath is very soft and tender; one feels only a faint, slight warmth rising from the lower origin (the lower elixir field); in time it will surge through the whole body, as if with an irresistible force. The student must discern it clearly at its subtlest, preside over it with my true intention, without the slightest scattering; in time the breath’s dynamism gains great strength, and then let it rise and surge, going wherever it will, adding not a bit more intention, mixing in not a bit of opinion — and so you obtain it. When the breath’s dynamism is strong and abundant, as soon as you become still the spring of Heaven stirs, swift as thunder, and even in the noisiest places it cannot be stopped; but there must always be a mind of numinous awareness presiding, so that there is no error.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

古人把练出真意叫做“练己”，己者指意中真土，又分为己土与戊土，己土管静态，戊土管动态，两土合而成圭，即药物，即先天真气。气功态中不外动静循环，真意既主持静态，又主持动态，则知真意实主持气功态的全过程。

真意像一个开关，练功者能打开它，就能达到虚极静笃，得到玄关窍开和阳生活子时。古人认为练功时间于半夜子时（即夜间十一时至一时）最有效应，因其时太阳正在北方，而人身之气到达尾闾关，与天地相应，乃天人合发盗取阴阳之机。但除了这个正子时，还有活子时，即古人说的“十二时中均有子”和“欲求大药为丹本，须认身中活子时”，亦即《入药镜》中所说：“一日内，十二时，意所到，皆可为。”也就是说，一日内二十四小时的任何时刻，练功者只要随时能打开真意这个开关，那就是活子时，练功可以获得和正子时一样的效果。所以，练功者要学会随时能打开真意开关，使之有序化、习惯化，这样就能把练功和日常生活结合起来，行、住、坐、卧之间均能练功。达到这样境界，即古人说的：“真土之生，虽舆中马上一切喧闹之地不能禁止”。

The ancients called training the true intention “refining the self” (lianji, “refining ji”). Ji refers to the true earth within the intention, which is further divided into ji earth and wu earth: ji earth governs the still state, wu earth the moving state; the two “earths” together form gui 圭 (the two characters 土 of wu earth and ji earth stacked one on the other make the character 圭), which is the medicine, that is, the Prior Heaven true breath-energy. The qigong state is nothing but a cycle of movement and stillness; since the true intention presides over both the still state and the moving state, we can see that the true intention in fact presides over the whole course of the qigong state.

The true intention is like a switch: if the practitioner can turn it on, he can reach utmost emptiness and steadfast stillness, and obtain the opening of the Mysterious Pass and the Living Zi Hour when yang is born. The ancients held that the most effective time for practice is the zi hour at midnight (eleven at night to one in the morning), because at that time the sun is due north (having turned beneath the earth), and the breath of the human body reaches the Caudal Funnel pass, in correspondence with Heaven and Earth — just the moment when Heaven and the human arise together and one can steal the spring of yin and yang. But besides this “true zi hour” there is the “Living Zi Hour”: what the ancients meant by “in all twelve double-hours there is a zi” (a zi hour can occur in any of the twelve double-hours of the day), and “if you want the great medicine as the root of the elixir, you must recognize the Living Zi Hour in the body”; and what the Mirror for Compounding the Medicine says: “Within one day, in the twelve double-hours, wherever the intention reaches, all can be done.” (Within one day, in all twelve double-hours, when the true intention arrives, one can do the work at any time.) That is, at any moment of the twenty-four hours of the day, if only the practitioner can turn on the switch of the true intention, that is the Living Zi Hour, and practice can obtain the same effect as at the true zi hour. So the practitioner should learn to be able to turn on the switch of the true intention at any time, making it orderly and habitual; then practice can be combined with daily life, and one can practice while walking, standing, sitting or lying down. Reaching this state is what the ancients meant by: “Once the true earth is born, even in a carriage or on horseback, in every noisy place, it cannot be stopped.”

Key Terms

  • Ji earth: the true intention in stillness, belonging to yin, also called yin earth — the intention of “without desire, observe its wonder”.
  • Wu earth: the true intention in movement, belonging to yang, also called yang earth — the intention of “with desire, observe its opening”.
  • True earth, the Yellow Dame: both code names for the true intention; see True Intention.
  • Gui: the two earths of wu and ji stacked form 圭, a metaphor for the medicine, the Prior Heaven true breath-energy; see knife-point.
  • Yellow Court: in this book, the lower elixir field.
  • True lead: the Prior Heaven true yang breath; see Lead and Mercury.
  • The flower of the heart opening: the scene of openness and joy in the heart.
  • The Supreme Ultimate laying its foundation: the vital impulse beginning, that is, the Mysterious Pass opening.
  • Nameless, the beginning of Heaven and Earth; named, the mother of the ten thousand things: chapter 1 of the Daode jing.
  • The pass of Heaven is up to me, the axis of Earth is up to the mind: the opening, closing and turning of Heaven and Earth are commanded by one’s own mind.
  • Refining the self: the ancients called training the true intention “refining ji”.
  • True zi hour: eleven at night to one in the morning.
  • Living Zi Hour: any time of day when stillness reaches its extreme, the true intention switches on and true yang stirs.
  • In a carriage or on horseback: a metaphor for busy, noisy places.

Putting It into Practice

  1. One intention, two uses: in stillness (ji earth), it presides in the midst of chaos, not letting the mind scatter; in movement (wu earth), when true yang stirs, it is used to gather it in.
  2. The timing lies within a single breath: too early and the true spring has not appeared; too late and ordinary thoughts have arisen. Observe closely in ordinary times, and you will be able to hold it when the moment comes.
  3. When it first opens it is only a faint warmth. Do not scorn it for being small; preside over it with the true intention without scattering; only in time does it gain strength.
  4. Once it has strength, let go: when the breath’s dynamism is strong, let it rise and circulate, adding no intention and mixing in no opinion, keeping only that point of numinous awareness presiding. If the surging of the breath’s dynamism becomes pronounced and makes you uneasy, relax and return to ordinary breathing; do not use intention to suppress it or chase it.
  5. The Living Zi Hour: there is no need to cling to midnight; whenever you turn on the switch of the true intention, that is the Living Zi Hour; once practiced, you can do the work while walking, standing, sitting or lying down.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is one paragraph; the original book does not give the source. It is divided into four short passages.
  2. “Wu and ji form the gui”: the ancients matched the five phases with the directions; wu and ji belong to earth and occupy the center, and alchemists use “earth” to represent the true intention that harmonizes from the center. The two “earth” characters 土 of wu earth and ji earth stacked together make 圭 (gui), hence “wu and ji form the gui”.
  3. “So that there is no error” (乃无差也已): so in the original book, recorded as is.
  4. Qiu Ling’s note, “because at that time the sun is due north”: this is the ancients’ earth-centered way of speaking, meaning that the sun has reached its lowest, farthest position (midnight), just the moment when one yang is first born.
