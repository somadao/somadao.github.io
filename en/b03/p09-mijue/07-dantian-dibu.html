Chapter Nine · Other Secretly Transmitted True Formulas · 丹田地步

Where the Elixir Field Is

Where the elixir field is — it has the name but not the substance, yet it cannot be said to be wholly without substance; it seems to circle in empty space, yet one must not go chasing off into empty space

About This Chapter

Where exactly is the elixir field? Is it a definite place? This section answers:

  1. First stop thinking and deliberating, and let one expanse of spiritual light shine brightly straight down to the elixir field; before long, like a small child, yang energy will steam through you and your spirits will be strong.
  2. Do not think that true yang is a thing with form and image, or that the elixir field is a definite place. The ancients called it the Sea of Breath, the Ancestral Opening, the root of heaven and earth, the gate of the Mysterious Female — it has the name but not the substance; yet it cannot be said to be wholly without substance: call it empty, and the ten thousand transformations are born from here; call it solid, and no thing can be found there.
  3. In time, one no longer knows even where this place, the “elixir field”, is; it seems to circle in empty space — yet one must not go chasing off into empty space. This measure is hard to put into words.

Qiu Ling’s note: although Huang places the lower elixir field one cun three fen below the navel, when illuminating it he advocates “as if there and as if not there, neither clinging nor apart”, and opposes rigid fixation; the book also has methods of illuminating “the Cave of Emptiness below the heart and above the kidneys” and illuminating “the space one cun three fen from the skin and flesh below the navel”, all of which aim at emptiness, spirit and liveliness.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

吾故教生先息思虑，庶一片灵光炯炯直达其所。不久之间仍如幼年稚子，阳气薰蒸，日充月壮，其精神健旺无已也。

So I teach you first to stop thinking and deliberating, so that one expanse of spiritual light may shine brightly straight down to that place (the elixir field). Before long you will again be like a young child, yang energy steaming through you, growing fuller day by day and stronger month by month, your spirits strong and inexhaustible.

§2
Original (Chinese)

又莫谓真阳如似一物，实有形象，而丹田实有地方。虽古人谓为气海，谓为祖窍，谓为天地之根，玄牝之门，有其名却无其实，然亦不可谓全无实也。以为虚也而万化生于此。以为实也究竟寻不着一个物事出来。久久如此，即吾所谓丹田地步，亦杳不知其所之，似在空中盘旋一般，然亦不可竟向空中驰逐也。此中分际一言难尽。

Again, do not think that true yang is like a thing, really having form and image; nor think that the elixir field really is a definite place. Although the ancients called it the Sea of Breath, the Ancestral Opening, the root of heaven and earth, the gate of the Mysterious Female, it has the name but not the substance; yet it cannot be said to be wholly without substance: call it empty, and all the ten thousand transformations are born from here; call it solid, and in the end no thing can be found there. If you go on like this for a long time, even what I call “the place of the elixir field” will be lost in obscurity — you will not know where it is; it will seem to circle in empty space; yet you must not go chasing single-mindedly off into empty space. The measure in this is hard to put fully into words.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§2
Original note (Chinese)

黄氏虽将下丹田定位于脐下一寸三分之处，但在观照此窍时主张在若有若无之间，不即不离，反对死死执着，故云丹田地步似在空中盘旋。行功中更有观照心之下肾之上虚无窟子、观照脐下丹田离皮肉一寸三分间之法，均是讲求虚灵活泼，学者宜善会之。

Although Huang places the lower elixir field one cun three fen below the navel, when illuminating this opening he advocates staying between “as if there and as if not there”, neither clinging nor apart, and opposes rigid fixation — that is why he says the place of the elixir field “seems to circle in empty space”. In practice there are also the methods of illuminating the Cave of Emptiness “below the heart and above the kidneys”, and of illuminating “the space one cun three fen from the skin and flesh in the elixir field below the navel” (see Chapter Two, Section 3), all of which aim at emptiness, spirit and liveliness; students should savor this well.

Key Terms

  • The place of the elixir field: where the elixir field is.
  • Sea of Breath, Ancestral Opening, root of heaven and earth, gate of the Mysterious Female: various names for the elixir field; “Ancestral Opening” here refers to the lower elixir field; see lower elixir field.
  • Having the name but not the substance: there is a name, but no actual thing that can be pointed to.
  • Measure (分际): the right degree.

Putting It into Practice

  1. First stop thinking, then illuminate the elixir field. Once the mind is quiet, that point of spiritual light naturally reaches it.
  2. Do not look for a “point”: the elixir field is roughly in the lower belly, but do not look for a precise point, still less imagine a ball of something.
  3. But do not drift off either: not fixing on a location does not mean paying no attention at all and letting the mind float off into the air — a touch of watchfulness is still there.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is one paragraph; the original book does not give the source. It is divided into two short passages.
