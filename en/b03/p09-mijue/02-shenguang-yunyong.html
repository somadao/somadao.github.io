Chapter Nine · Other Secretly Transmitted True Formulas · 两眼神光之运用

Using the Spirit-Light of the Two Eyes

Using the spirit-light of the two eyes — the whole body is yin, only the light of the eyes still belongs to yang; gather it in constantly and let it shine faintly downward

About This Chapter

This section is very short, and explains why the spirit-light of the two eyes matters:

  1. The whole body is yin, only the light of the eyes is yang: the spirit-light of the two eyes should be constantly gathered in and let shine faintly downward, and essence, breath and spirit will naturally come together as one family.
  2. The effect: the breath of the elixir field becomes strong, rises to the Muddy Pellet, spreads through the Nine Palaces and pours into the Yellow Court; yin breath is wholly dispelled and yang breath always remains — as when the sun shines, the clouds and fog disperse of themselves.

Qiu Ling’s note is very useful: - Many people know only “fixing the intention on the elixir field”, without knowing that “fixing the intention” itself includes the spirit-light of the two eyes turning back to look within; without the spirit-light, fixing the intention is all dark and murky, and it is hard to achieve anything. - Wu Chongxu says “turning back to look and shining within is the wondrous working of true intention”: “intention” is true intention, and “fixing” is using the spirit-light of the two eyes to illuminate the location. - The ancients said “when the eyes do not move, the mind stays” and “where the eyes go, the mind goes too; where the mind goes, the breath goes too”.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

夫人一身之中虽是神气为之运用，要不若两目之神光炯炯不昧，惺惺长存。故昔人谓一身皆是阴，惟有目光犹属阳，须常常收摄，微微下照，则精气神自会合一家。到得丹田气壮，直上泥丸，遍九宫，注黄庭，自然阴气消尽而阳气常存，犹之太空日照，云露自消归无有。

In the human body, although it is the spirit and breath that are at work, nothing equals the spirit-light of the two eyes, bright and unclouded, always awake. So earlier people said: “The whole body is yin; only the light of the eyes still belongs to yang.” One must constantly gather it in and let it shine faintly downward, and essence, breath and spirit will naturally come together as one family. When the breath of the elixir field becomes strong, it rises straight up to the Muddy Pellet (the crown of the head), spreads through the Nine Palaces (the nine palaces of the head), and pours into the Yellow Court; naturally the yin breath is wholly dispelled and the yang breath always remains — just as when the sun shines in the great void, clouds, fog and dew disperse of themselves and return to nothing.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1
Original note (Chinese)

不少学者练功但知“意守丹田”，却不知“意守”二字实即包括运用两眼神光返观内照，于是意守时昏暗莫辨，如落污暗地狱，功亦难有所成。

伍冲虚在《仙佛合宗》一书中说：“返观内照即真意之妙用也。”可知“意”者指真意，“守”者指运用两眼神光观照窍位。所以说，练功中觉照之心不可无，有如天上红日，光明洞照，一毫昏黑不得。

两眼神光的作用亦由两眼在人身中的生理机制所决定。《皇极经世书》云：“天之神栖于日，人之神发于目。”古语云：“机不动则弩住，目不动则心住”，故曰：“机在目”。从中医学观点来看，则五脏精华皆发于目。古来气功家在其著作中惯用“眼前”、“目前”，以隐喻两眼神光的作用及炼出眼前慧光的成效。

练功中无论采取、交媾、烹炼、沐浴、温养，结胎和脱胎，无不靠两眼神光观照之功。昔人云：“目之所至，心亦至焉，心之所至，气亦至焉”。两眼神光之用实有助于扫除周身阴邪，使成阴尽而阳纯之体。

Many students in their practice know only “fixing the intention on the elixir field”, without knowing that the two words “fixing the intention” in fact include using the spirit-light of the two eyes to turn back and look within; so when they fix the intention, all is dark and murky, nothing can be made out, as if falling into a filthy, dark hell, and the practice is hard to bring to anything.

Wu Chongxu says in The Common Origin of Immortals and Buddhas: “Turning back to look and shining within is the wondrous working of true intention.” From this we know that “intention” refers to true intention, and “fixing” refers to using the spirit-light of the two eyes to illuminate the location of the opening. That is why it is said that in practice the mind of illuminating awareness must never be absent: like the red sun in the sky, its light shining everywhere — not a speck of darkness is allowed.

The working of the spirit-light of the two eyes is also determined by the physiological role of the eyes in the human body. The Book of Supreme World-Ordering Principles says: “The spirit of heaven dwells in the sun; the spirit of the human issues from the eyes.” An old saying goes: “When the trigger does not move, the crossbow stays; when the eyes do not move, the mind stays.” (When the trigger is not moved, the crossbow bolt is not released; when the eyes do not move, the mind rests.) So it is said that “the trigger is in the eyes” (the key lies in the eyes). From the point of view of Chinese medicine, the essence of the five organs all shows in the eyes. Qigong masters since ancient times have habitually used words like “before the eyes” and “before one’s eyes” in their writings to hint at the working of the spirit-light of the two eyes and at the result of refining the light of wisdom before one’s eyes.

In practice, whether gathering, uniting, cooking and refining, ablution, warm nourishing, forming the embryo or shedding the embryo, there is not one that does not rely on the work of the spirit-light of the two eyes illuminating. Earlier people said: “Where the eyes go, the mind goes too; where the mind goes, the breath goes too.” The use of the spirit-light of the two eyes truly helps to sweep away the yin evils of the whole body, making the body one in which yin is exhausted and yang is pure.

Key Terms

  • Spirit-light: the light of the two eyes; see Light.
  • The whole body is yin; only the light of the eyes still belongs to yang: the spirit-light is the most usable yang in the human body.
  • Nine Palaces: nine palace-positions in the head, with the Muddy Pellet at the center.
  • Yellow Court: in this book, the lower elixir field.
  • Turning back to look and shining within: using the spirit-light of the two eyes to look and shine inward; see Reverting the Light.
  • The trigger is in the eyes: the key lies in the eyes.
  • Book of Supreme World-Ordering Principles: written by Shao Yong of the Northern Song.
  • The Common Origin of Immortals and Buddhas: an elixir book by Wu Chongxu of the Ming.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Fixing the intention = the spirit-light shining lightly. When fixing the intention on the elixir field, do not “think” about it; let the eyelids lower like curtains, and let the light of the eyes shine lightly down on the lower belly.
  2. Shine faintly downward: do not stare or fix your gaze; relax the eyelids and keep the gaze soft.
  3. Neither dim nor dark: if fixing the intention is all torpor, the spirit-light has not been raised; lift your spirits a little and let that point of illuminating awareness grow a little brighter.
  4. Safety note: the original text speaks of the breath “rising straight up to the Muddy Pellet” — this happens naturally once the breath of the elixir field is full; do not use the gaze or the intention to lead the breath up to the crown of the head.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is one paragraph; the original book does not give the source.
  2. “Clouds and dew disperse of themselves and return to nothing” (云露自消归无有): so in the original book; 露 (“dew”) here means much the same as 雾 (“fog”).
