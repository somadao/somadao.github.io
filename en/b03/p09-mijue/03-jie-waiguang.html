Chapter Nine · Other Secretly Transmitted True Formulas · 戒贪看外光

Beware of Craving to Look at Outer Light

Beware of craving to look at outer light — the Way of Heaven values gathering in, not sending out; the flowing pearl of the sun always wants to leave people; take it back against the current, and the Golden Flower is stored within

About This Chapter

When brightness appears before the eyes in quiet sitting, many people take it for a result, and the more they look, the more they love to look. This section deals specifically with this fault:

  1. The Way of Heaven values gathering in, not sending out. “The flowing pearl of the sun always wants to leave people” — the spirit-light always wants to run outward; only by taking it back against the current is the Golden Flower (the elixir) stored within.
  2. If one lets it flow outward, running after seeing, hearing, speaking and moving, after greed, anger, delusion and attachment, it is worn away day by day, and one cannot even keep this body, let alone have a body beyond the body.
  3. The remedy is very simple: lower the curtains and stop up the mouth, gathering the one point of spiritual light into the Cave of Emptiness, neither going out nor coming in, without thought or worry; all it takes is a slight intention to gather in the gaze.

Qiu Ling’s note: the more one craves to look at this light and admires it, the more it sends itself outward; the original spirit and original breath are worn away day by day, and after practice, far from the spirit-light shining forth, the eyes are dim and blurred and things look hazy. This light is seen in “a sudden glance of reverting the light”, not obtained by deliberately staring at it for a long time. There is only one method: revert the light to shine back, gathering it into the lower elixir field.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

吾常言下手兴功莫如人之眼目，盖目者神之光也。学者每每好贪外光显示于双眸之前，以为金光焕发即修真之效验。岂知天道贵收而不贵发，人道又何独不然。古人云：“太阳流珠，常欲去人，逆而纳之则金华内蕴矣！”苟不知逆而喜顺，常将神光发越在外，驰于视听言动之妄、贪嗔痴爱之非，日殒日销，即欲长有此身犹且不能，而况身外有身乎？

I often say that in beginning the practice nothing is more important than the eyes, because the eyes are the light of the spirit. Students often like to crave looking at the outer light that appears before their eyes, taking the shining forth of golden light as a result of cultivating the true. Little do they know that the Way of Heaven values gathering in, not sending out — how could the human way be any exception? The ancients said: “The flowing pearl of the sun always wants to leave people; take it back against the current, and the Golden Flower is stored within!” (The flowing pearl of the sun — a metaphor for the light of the mind and spirit — always wants to leave and go; take it back against the current, and the Golden Flower is stored within!) If one does not know how to gather it back against the current and only likes to let it flow outward, constantly sending the spirit-light out, chasing the illusions of seeing, hearing, speaking and moving and the faults of greed, anger, delusion and attachment, it is worn away and dispersed day by day; one cannot even keep this body for long, let alone have “a body beyond the body” (achieving the Dharma-body)!

§2
Original (Chinese)

惟有垂帘塞兑，常将我一点灵光收入虚无窟里，不出不入，无虑无思，久之金光养足，自可化为阳神而为我身主宰，且充满于虚空上下，岂特一灵炯炯洞见如来已哉！但恐太阳流珠有欲去人之意，而我即随其流而逐之，则元神日梏，元气无存，生机遂绝矣！此件功法浑无难事，只须稍有意思将目光收敛之足矣！

Only by lowering the curtains and stopping up the mouth (the eyes lowered like curtains, the mouth closed), constantly gathering this one point of my spiritual light into the Cave of Emptiness, neither going out nor coming in, without thought or worry, will the golden light, once fully nourished over time, naturally be able to transform into the yang spirit, become the master of my body, and fill the empty void above and below — and how much more than a single point of spiritual brightness, shining clearly, seeing the Tathāgata! The only fear is that as soon as the flowing pearl of the sun has the slightest intention of leaving, I follow its flow and chase after it; then the original spirit is bound tighter day by day, the original breath no longer remains, and the life-force is cut off! This practice is not difficult at all; all it takes is a slight intention to gather the gaze back in, and that is enough!

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§2
Original note (Chinese)

好贪看外光于双眸之前，此亦为学人之通病。殊不知越贪看此光，欣赏此光，则此光越向外发越，去人越远，所谓“太阳流珠常欲去人”，于是我之元神元气日益耗损，功后不是两眼神光焕发，视物清晰，而反感两眼昏花，视物模糊，弊端立见，受害非浅。

因此，行功时必须戒贪看外光。我所以发现此光，乃瞥地回光所见，决非有意久久注视。两眼神光之用大矣，但如不知经常收敛之内蕴之，则有用反变为有害。法惟回光返照，垂帘塞兑将此光收入虚无窟里。虚无窟指下丹田。此即“逆而纳之”养光之法。

Liking to crave looking at the outer light before the eyes is also a common failing among students. Little do they know that the more one craves to look at this light and admires it, the more it sends itself outward and the farther it goes from one — this is what is meant by “the flowing pearl of the sun always wants to leave people”. So my original spirit and original breath are worn away day by day, and after practice, far from the spirit-light of the eyes shining forth and things looking clear, one feels the eyes dim and blurred and things look hazy — the harm shows at once, and it is no slight harm.

Therefore, in practice one must give up craving to look at outer light. When I discover this light, it is seen in “a sudden glance of reverting the light” (reverting the light in an instant), never obtained by deliberately staring at it for a long time. The working of the spirit-light of the two eyes is great, but if one does not know how constantly to gather it in and store it within, what is useful turns harmful instead. The only method is to revert the light to shine back: the eyes lowered like curtains, the mouth closed, gathering this light into the Cave of Emptiness. The Cave of Emptiness refers to the lower elixir field. This is the method of nourishing the light by “taking it back against the current”.

Key Terms

  • Outer light: brightness appearing before the eyes in quiet sitting.
  • The flowing pearl of the sun: a metaphor for the light of the mind and spirit (mercury), flowing unsteadily and always wanting to run outward.
  • Taking it back against the current: gathering it back in reverse.
  • Golden Flower: another name for the elixir.
  • Greed, anger, delusion and attachment: craving, hatred, folly and clinging love.
  • A body beyond the body: achieving the Dharma-body; see spontaneous Dharma Body.
  • Lowering the curtains and stopping up the mouth: the eyes lowered like curtains, the mouth closed; see lowering the curtains.
  • Cave of Emptiness: according to Qiu Ling’s note, the lower elixir field.
  • Yang spirit: see Holy Embryo.
  • A sudden glance of reverting the light: reverting the light in an instant.

Putting It into Practice

  1. If there is light before your eyes, do not look at it or chase it. Treat it as nothing special; gently gather the gaze back and let it shine down into the elixir field.
  2. A check: if after sitting your eyes are clear and bright and your spirits good, you are doing it right; if after sitting your eyes are dim, blurred and dry, you have been craving to look at outer light and the spirit-light has run outward.
  3. Use only a slight intention: gathering in the gaze takes no force; a slight gathering is enough.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is one paragraph; the original book does not give the source. It is divided into two short passages.
  2. “The flowing pearl of the sun always wants to leave people” (太阳流珠，常欲去人): from the Zhouyi cantong qi.
