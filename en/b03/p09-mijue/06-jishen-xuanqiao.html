Chapter Nine · Other Secretly Transmitted True Formulas · 集神于玄窍

Gathering the Spirit in the Mysterious Opening

Gathering the spirit in the mysterious opening — the square inch is the hall, the mysterious opening the inner chamber; ordinary people guard the spirit in the square inch, cultivators gather the spirit in the mysterious opening

About This Chapter

Where in the human body does the original spirit dwell? Where should the spirit be placed when beginning the practice?

  1. Three views: the square inch (the heart) is the dwelling of the original spirit; within the Mysterious Pass is the house of the original spirit; the Heavenly Valley (the Muddy Pellet) is guarded by the original spirit. All three are right, but where should one begin?
  2. Hall and chamber: the ancients said that the square inch is the “hall” (the reception room) of one’s body, and within the mysterious opening is its “chamber” (the inner room). Ordinary people guard the spirit in the square inch, and the ears and eyes can come in and shake their essence; cultivators gather the spirit in the mysterious opening, and the ears and eyes have no door through which to peer.
  3. So when beginning: one should gather the spirit within the opening of the Mysterious Pass, inside the circle of emptiness, muddled and undivided, obscure and dark; only then can one nurture the undivided original spirit. If one is always clearly and keenly aware in the heart, one is bound to be dragged about day and night by worldly feelings and ordinary concerns.
  4. The Heavenly Valley: the head has nine palaces, and the middle one is called the Heavenly Valley, pure and without dust; if one can settle the original spirit there, attaining truth and realizing sagehood lies right here.

Qiu Ling’s note gives an order: the square inch is the heart, the Heavenly Valley and Kunlun are both the Muddy Pellet, and the mysterious opening here means “emptiness”. When beginning, first gather the spirit in the mysterious opening (emptiness), and the original spirit has no door to leak through; gathering the spirit in the heart, it is easily disturbed by the cognitive spirit; only after the practice gradually deepens is it fitting to gather the spirit in the Muddy Pellet.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

昨言元神斡运其间，究竟元神在人身中藏于何所？长于何地？有曰：方寸之地为元神之居；有曰：玄关之内为元神之宅；又曰：天谷元神守之。果真此三处皆元神之所栖，但不知下手之初何处为始？

Yesterday I said that the original spirit turns and presides within; where then is the original spirit actually stored in the human body? Where does it grow? Some say: the area of the square inch (the heart) is the dwelling of the original spirit; some say: within the Mysterious Pass is the house of the original spirit; others say: the Heavenly Valley (the Muddy Pellet) is guarded by the original spirit. These three places are indeed all where the original spirit rests, but I do not know, at the very beginning, which one to start from.

§2
Original (Chinese)

《易》云：“洗心退藏于密”。是又闻古云：方寸之地吾身之堂也，玄窍之内吾身之室也，众人则守神于方寸之地，耳目得入而摇其精，修士集神于玄窍之间，耳目无门而窥其隙。如此看来，下手之时即当集神于玄关窍中，虚无圈内，庶几混混沌沌，杳杳冥冥，无人无我，何地何天，方能养成不二元神。若不藏于隐幽之地，而常于方寸中了了灵灵，未有不驰于尘情俗虑而日夜无休息也。

The Book of Changes says: “Wash the mind and withdraw into secrecy.” (Wash clean the thoughts of the mind and withdraw into a hidden place.) I have also heard the ancients say: the area of the square inch is the “hall” of one’s body (the outer hall for receiving guests); within the mysterious opening is the “chamber” of one’s body (the inner room). Ordinary people guard the spirit in the area of the square inch, and the ears and eyes can come in and shake their essence; those who cultivate the Way gather the spirit within the mysterious opening, and the ears and eyes have no door through which to peer at a crack. Seen in this light, when beginning, one should gather the spirit within the opening of the Mysterious Pass, inside the circle of emptiness; only thus can one be muddled and undivided, obscure and dark, with neither others nor self, not knowing what place or what heaven it is, and only thus can one nurture the undivided (pure and unmixed) original spirit. If it is not stored in a hidden, secluded place, but is always clearly and keenly present in the heart (thinking things over distinctly), there is no one who is not dragged about by worldly feelings and ordinary concerns, without rest day or night.

§3
Original (Chinese)

何谓天谷？盖人头有九宫，中有一所名曰天谷，清净无尘，能将元神安置其中毫不外驰，则成真证圣即在此矣！所以《黄庭经》云：“子欲不死守昆仑”。是可见守此天谷有无限妙蕴也。

What is the Heavenly Valley? The human head has nine palaces, and in the middle there is one called the “Heavenly Valley”, pure and without dust; if one can settle the original spirit there, without the slightest running and scattering outward, attaining truth and realizing sagehood lies right here! That is why the Yellow Court Scripture says: “If you wish not to die, guard Kunlun.” (If you want not to die, guard Kunlun — that is, the Muddy Pellet at the crown of the head.) From this we can see that guarding this Heavenly Valley has boundless wonders.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§3
Original note (Chinese)

方寸喻心，天谷、昆仑均喻泥丸，玄窍在此意为虚无。

下手兴功垂帘塞兑，即《周易参同契》所云：“耳、目、口三宝，闭塞勿发扬”之意，我宜集神于玄窍，元神走泄无门；若集神于心府，易为识神所扰；至功行渐深，则又宜集神于泥丸，此中清净无尘，更觉隐幽。如此亦合练功程序。

“The square inch” is a metaphor for the heart; “the Heavenly Valley” and “Kunlun” are both metaphors for the Muddy Pellet; “the mysterious opening” here means “emptiness”.

Lowering the curtains and stopping up the mouth when beginning the practice is what the Zhouyi cantong qi means by “ears, eyes and mouth, the three treasures — close them and do not let them spread out” (ears, eyes and mouth, these three treasures, are closed and not let out). I should gather the spirit in the mysterious opening (within emptiness), and then the original spirit has no door to leak through; if I gather the spirit in the heart, it is easily disturbed by the cognitive spirit; when the practice gradually deepens, it is then fitting to gather the spirit in the Muddy Pellet, which is pure and without dust and feels even more hidden and secluded. This also accords with the sequence of the practice.

Key Terms

  • Square inch: the heart.
  • Mysterious opening, opening of the Mysterious Pass: here, the realm of emptiness; see Mysterious Opening / Mysterious Pass.
  • Heavenly Valley, Kunlun: the Muddy Pellet (the crown of the head); see Muddy Pellet.
  • Hall and chamber: the hall is the outer room, the chamber the inner room; metaphors for the heart and the mysterious opening.
  • Undivided original spirit: the original spirit pure and unmixed.
  • Nine Palaces: nine palace-positions in the head.
  • Ears, eyes and mouth, the three treasures — close them and do not let them spread out: a line from the Zhouyi cantong qi.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Do not “guard” by thinking in the heart: placing the spirit in the heart is like keeping watch in the reception room — anything can come in and disturb it.
  2. Gather the spirit in emptiness: lower the curtains, close the mouth, and let the spirit withdraw to an empty, hollow place, neither inside nor outside, without thinking of a specific location.
  3. The Muddy Pellet comes later: Qiu Ling says explicitly that only “when the practice gradually deepens” is it fitting to gather the spirit in the Muddy Pellet. Beginners, and people with yin deficiency and yang excess or high blood pressure, should not guard the crown of the head; if a swollen head, headache, flushed face or palpitations appear, stop and return to letting the spirit-light shine down on the elixir field.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is one paragraph; the original book does not give the source. It is divided into three short passages.
  2. “If you wish not to die, guard Kunlun” (子欲不死守昆仑): from the Scripture of the Inner Effulgences of the Yellow Court.
