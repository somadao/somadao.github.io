Introduction to Chapter Nine

What this chapter covers

The first eight chapters are arranged in the order of the practice: the Mysterious Pass, the Mysterious Female, true intention, mind and nature, essence, breath and spirit, Kan and Li and Qian and Kun, the River Chariot, and fire phasing. There are also many concrete key points that Huang Yuanji mentioned in the course of his teaching which do not fit under these headings; Qiu Ling has gathered them in this chapter, fifteen sections in all.

This chapter is the closest of the whole book to everyday practice: how to manage to sit still, how to use the eyes, what to do when you see strange visions, where the elixir field actually is, how the upper and lower elixir fields work together, how to cultivate in daily life, how to sleep and how to walk, how women should practice — all receive concrete answers.

What this chapter is about (in one sentence)

Before taking your seat, settle your affairs, and once seated, sit fully; let the spirit-light of the two eyes shine gently downward, do not crave looking at outer light, and pay no mind to strange visions; do not guard the elixir field rigidly, and when drowsy, shine a little on the upper field; when there are affairs, responding as they come and forgetting them as they go is refining nature; when there are none, the spirit-light shining on the elixir field is refining life; one point of the mind of illuminating awareness is always the master — when the mind is level the spirit concentrates, when the breath is harmonious the breathing is regulated.

Reading tips

  1. For beginners, the most practical are Sections 1, 2, 3, 9, 10 and 12.
  2. Sections 5, 6 and 8 involve the upper elixir field (the Root of the Mountain, the Muddy Pellet). Qiu Ling warns twice, in Chapter One and in Section 8 of this chapter: people with yin deficiency and yang excess, or with high blood pressure, should not shine much on the upper elixir field. When in doubt, let the spirit-light shine mainly down on the lower elixir field.
  3. Section 14 is a method from the stage of refining breath into spirit; Qiu Ling says “beginners will find it hard to practice”, so it is for understanding only.
  4. The “strange scenes” of Section 4: pay no mind to any of them. If unusual sensations keep recurring and affect your sleep or mood, pause your sitting practice and consult a doctor if necessary.
