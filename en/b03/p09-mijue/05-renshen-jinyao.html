Chapter Nine · Other Secretly Transmitted True Formulas · 人身紧要之处

The Crucial Places of the Human Body

The crucial places of the human body — the two openings of the Root of the Mountain and the Mysterious Breast are both vital passageways through which essence and breath come and go

About This Chapter

This section speaks of two “crucial” openings:

  1. The Root of the Mountain: on the bridge of the nose between the two eyes. By visualizing the Root of the Mountain, the true breath-energy naturally moves up and down and returns to its old place in the Yellow Court. The ancients said “the Root of the Mountain is the stem of life at a person’s birth”; opening the Governing vessel, closing the Conception vessel, letting the breath flow back and forth — all happen at this one opening.
  2. The Mysterious Breast: under the tongue, between the two points Golden Fluid and Jade Liquid. By illuminating the Mysterious Breast, the true saliva is naturally drawn up. “The Mysterious Breast and the windpipe receive the tally of essence”; “the one opening of the Mysterious Breast is the shore between life and death.”

Qiu Ling’s note explains where the two openings are, and quotes the Xingming guizhi: the Mysterious Breast “is the sea of fluids and saliva, the source of generation and transformation”.

Please take special note: Qiu Ling’s note to Chapter One, Section 1, already said that fixing the intention on the Ancestral Opening or visualizing the Root of the Mountain is not suitable for people with yin deficiency and yang excess, or with high blood pressure. In this section Huang Yuanji praises the Root of the Mountain; readers should read the two places together (see “Putting It into Practice”).

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

人身还有紧要之处，如山根、玄膺二窍，皆是通精气往来要道。人能存想山根，则真气自然上下复归黄庭旧处。人能观照玄膺，则真津自然摄提而上。尔等每行一次，此二穴不可忽也。古云：“玄膺气管受精符。”又曰：“玄膺一窍生死岸。”又古云：“山根是人初生命蒂。”吾人开督闭任，通气往来，即是此窍。苟能存神于兹，自可长生不老，却病延年。

There are also some crucial places in the human body, such as the two openings of the Root of the Mountain and the Mysterious Breast, which are both vital passageways through which essence and breath come and go and communicate. If one can visualize the Root of the Mountain, the true breath-energy will naturally flow up and down and return to its original place in the Yellow Court (the lower elixir field); if one can illuminate the Mysterious Breast, the true saliva (the fluid in the mouth) will naturally be drawn up. Each time you practice, these two points must not be neglected. The ancients said: “The Mysterious Breast and the windpipe receive the tally of essence.” (The Mysterious Breast connects with the windpipe and receives the tally of the essence-breath.) They also said: “The one opening of the Mysterious Breast is the shore between life and death.” (This one opening, the Mysterious Breast, is the dividing line between life and death.) And: “The Root of the Mountain is the stem of life at a person’s birth.” (The Root of the Mountain is the root of life at the moment a person is born.) Our opening the Governing vessel, closing the Conception vessel, and letting the breath come and go and communicate all happen at this one opening. If one can keep the spirit here, one can naturally live long without aging, dispel illness and extend one’s years.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1
Original note (Chinese)

山根一穴，在两眼之间鼻梁上，即祖窍或玄关窍的俗称，本书第一章第一节注释中曾论及，可以参阅，

玄膺一窍在舌下金津、玉液二穴之间。此窍可通气管，即《黄庭经》所谓“玄膺气管受精符。”《性命圭旨》云：“玄膺一窍乃是津液之海，生化之源，溉灌一身皆本于此。”

The point called the Root of the Mountain is on the bridge of the nose between the two eyes; it is a common name for the Ancestral Opening or the opening of the Mysterious Pass; it was discussed in the notes to Chapter One, Section 1, of this book, which may be consulted,

The one opening of the Mysterious Breast is under the tongue, between the two points Golden Fluid and Jade Liquid. This opening connects with the windpipe; it is what the Yellow Court Scripture means by “the Mysterious Breast and the windpipe receive the tally of essence”. The Xingming guizhi says: “The one opening of the Mysterious Breast is the sea of fluids and saliva, the source of generation and transformation; the irrigation of the whole body takes it as its basis.”

Key Terms

  • Root of the Mountain: on the bridge of the nose between the two eyes; that is, the Ancestral Opening.
  • Mysterious Breast: under the tongue, between the two points Golden Fluid and Jade Liquid.
  • Golden Fluid and Jade Liquid: two points on either side under the tongue, where the fluid of the mouth comes from.
  • Yellow Court: in this book, the lower elixir field.
  • True saliva: the clear, sweet fluid that arises naturally in the mouth.
  • Stem of life: the root of life.
  • Opening the Governing and closing the Conception vessel: letting the Governing vessel open through and the Conception vessel draw closed, so that the breath comes and goes and communicates; see Conception and Governing vessels.
  • Xingming guizhi: an elixir book of the Ming.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The Mysterious Breast can be used: rest the tip of the tongue lightly against the palate, and the fluid in the mouth naturally increases; when the mouth is full, simply swallow it slowly.
  2. Use the Root of the Mountain with care: visualizing the Root of the Mountain makes the breath move upward. It is not suitable for people with yin deficiency and yang excess, or with high blood pressure (Qiu Ling’s note to Chapter One, Section 1). Most people, too, should only shine on it lightly once and then withdraw, and not dwell for long between the eyes; the method of beginning in this book is still the spirit-light shining down on the elixir field.
  3. Safety note: if, while visualizing the Root of the Mountain, a swollen head, headache, flushed face, palpitations or swollen eyes appear, stop at once and switch to letting the spirit-light shine down on the elixir field, relaxing naturally.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is one paragraph; the original book does not give the source.
  2. “The Mysterious Breast and the windpipe receive the tally of essence” (玄膺气管受精符): from the Scripture of the Inner Effulgences of the Yellow Court.
  3. The first paragraph of Qiu Ling’s note ends with a comma in the original book; reproduced as it stands.
