Chapter One · The Single Opening of the Mysterious Pass · 玄关开后

After the Mysterious Pass Opens

After the Mysterious Pass opens — concentrating the spirit in the Cavity of Breath; the two methods of guarding the center and the River Chariot; Li Hanxu’s formula of moving past the lower Magpie Bridge

About This Chapter

The last section of Chapter One answers the most practical question of all: once the Mysterious Pass has opened, what do you do?

  1. First concentrate the spirit in the Cavity of Breath. One awakening and it stirs, one wakes with a start — like midnight at the junction of the end of the hai hour and the beginning of the zi hour. At this moment one must concentrate the spirit in the Cavity of Breath — and this Cavity of Breath is not a lump of flesh with a shape, but the place where spirit and breath unite. When one has sat until the ordinary breath stops and the breathing through mouth and nose seems present and absent, embryonic breathing starts up from the lower origin; then the medicine is gathered into the “golden tripod and jade furnace” to be forged and refined.
  2. Where the Cavity of Breath is, and where it is not. Roughly beside the Sea of Breath (where a man’s essence stirs, where a woman’s womb is attached), but one must not cling to it rigidly — “dim and indistinct, as if here, and as if not here”.
  3. Two kinds of “birth of yang”: when the mysterious opening first opens, one first sees the original nature in the palace of Li; this is called the birth of yang of the nature — only a chance movement of the spirit, to be warmly nourished slowly with the fire of the spirit; this is “guarding the center”. When the fire of the spirit shines downward, and the water, meeting the fire, transforms into a single breath, diffuse and mingling, this is the gold born at the bottom of the water, called the birth of yang of life; at this point one turns the “River Chariot”.
  4. There are only two methods for long life: guarding the center and the River Chariot. The fire of guarding the center only nourishes gently and warmly; only the reverse turning of the River Chariot has the distinctions of zi and wu, mao and you, civil and martial fire.

Qiu Ling’s note sums up this section in one sentence: when the Mysterious Pass opens, let the true intention preside, concentrate the spirit in the Cavity of Breath and warmly nourish it; wait until true yang arises in full, then run the zi-wu Celestial Circuit. He also quotes another route from Li Hanxu’s Secret Principles of the Three Carts, which we read in the second book: abruptly turn “the mind that has just woken”, move it past the lower Magpie Bridge to the Caudal Funnel, and guard it without leaving; the true breath will then rise up the spine of itself — this combines guarding the center and the River Chariot into a single method.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

忽焉一觉而动，一惊而醒，犹亥末子初交半夜。是学者于此须凝神入气穴。此个气穴非有形有象，肉团子上是神气合一之气穴也。神气聚则有形，神气散则机息。学人坐到凡息停时，口鼻之息似有似无，然后胎息始从下元发起，兀兀腾腾，氤氤氲氲，所谓一元兆象，大地回春，桃红柳绿，遍满山原，于此收回药物，采入金鼎玉炉，煅之炼之，大丹可成矣！

Suddenly one awakening and it stirs, one wakes with a start — like midnight at the junction of the end of the hai hour and the beginning of the zi hour (the moment in the day when yin reaches its extreme and yang is born). At this moment the student must concentrate the spirit in the Cavity of Breath (gather the spirit and bring it into the lower elixir field). This Cavity of Breath is not a place with shape and form; in this body of flesh, the place where spirit and breath unite is the Cavity of Breath. When spirit and breath gather, it has a visible form; once spirit and breath scatter, its vital spring comes to rest. When the student has sat until the ordinary breath stops and the breathing through mouth and nose seems present and absent, then embryonic breathing begins to start up from the lower origin (the lower elixir field), rising and surging, diffuse and mingling — this is what is called “the omen of the one origin appears; spring returns to the great earth; red peach blossoms and green willows fill the hills and plains” (one yang first appears, like spring returning to the earth with peach blossoms and green willows over the hills and fields) — at this moment gather back the medicine, take it into the golden tripod and jade furnace (the lower elixir field), forge it and refine it, and the great elixir can be accomplished!

§2
Original (Chinese)

虽然金鼎非真有鼎，玉炉非真有炉，亦无非神气合一，凝聚于人身气海之旁，即男子媾精之所，女子系胞之地是。然亦不可死死执着此处烹炼也，不过以人身元气，自一阳来复，神气交会于此，归根复命于此，烹炼神丹，采取归来，亦离不得此。除此而外，则无修炼之处，若执着此处，未可以成神胎也，须知神气团聚一区，恍惚若在此，又若不在此，方与虚无之丹相合。

Although we speak of a “golden tripod”, there is no real tripod; though we speak of a “jade furnace”, there is no real furnace either. It is nothing but spirit and breath uniting and gathering beside the Sea of Breath in the human body — that is, where a man’s essence stirs and where a woman’s womb nurtures the fetus (deep in the lower elixir field). But neither must one cling rigidly to this place for the cooking and refining. It is only that the original breath of the human body, from the moment the one yang returns, meets the spirit here and returns to the root and restores life here; cooking and refining the spirit elixir, gathering the medicine and bringing it back, also cannot do without this place. Apart from here there is no place for cultivation; yet if one clings to this place, one cannot accomplish the spirit embryo. You must know that when spirit and breath are gathered in one area, dim and indistinct, as if here, and as if not here — only then does it accord with the elixir of emptiness.

§3
Original (Chinese)

玄窍初开，只见离宫元性，所以谓之性阳生。然此是神之偶动，非气之真动，只可以神火慢慢温养，听其一上一下之气机往来内运，蕴藏于中黄正位，此为守中一法，水火济，坎离交之候；又谓前行短，二候采牟尼是。到得神火下照，那水见火自然化为一气，氤氤氲氲，兀兀腾腾，此方是水底金生，古人云阳生活子时是，又曰命阳生。

When the mysterious opening first opens, one sees only the original nature (the light of one’s original nature) in the palace of Li (the heart); this is therefore called “the birth of yang of the nature”. But this is only a chance movement of the spirit, not a true movement of the breath; it can only be nourished slowly and warmly with the fire of the spirit (the light of the spirit), letting the dynamism of the breath, going up and down, move back and forth within the body and be stored in the true central yellow position (the lower elixir field) — this is the method of “guarding the center”, the time when water and fire assist each other and Kan and Li meet; it is also called “the forward course is short” (this stretch of the road is short: one only nourishes in the elixir field, without running the Celestial Circuit), and is also “gathering the muni in two stages”. When the fire of the spirit shines downward, and that water (the essence-breath of the kidneys), meeting the fire, naturally transforms into a single breath, diffuse and mingling, rising and surging — only then is it “gold born at the bottom of the water” (metal born from water, i.e. the Prior Heaven true yang breath being produced). What the ancients called “the Living Zi Hour when yang is born” is this, also called “the birth of yang of life”.

§4
Original (Chinese)

果有此气机之动，不必蓬蓬勃勃充塞一身内外，即粗见气机，果从神火下入水乡，是为坎离交而产药，亦是微阳初动，亦要勤勤采取，运动河车，栖息泥丸，所谓补脑还精，长生之道在是矣！人欲长生，除此守中、河车二法，行持不辍，别无积精累气之法焉。虽然，守中之火，只有温温铅鼎，惟河车逆运，则有子午、卯酉，或文或武之别。诚能常常温养，令我元神常栖于心，元气常潜于身，虽欲死之，其将何以死之？以神气交媾，常常不失也。

If there truly is such a movement of the breath’s dynamism, there is no need to wait for it to surge and fill the whole body inside and out — even if one sees only a rough trace of the breath’s dynamism, as long as it truly comes from the fire of the spirit shining down into the land of water (the lower elixir field), this is Kan and Li meeting and producing the medicine; it is also the first stirring of faint yang, and it too must be diligently gathered, turning the River Chariot (letting the true breath rise along the governing vessel and descend along the conception vessel) and letting it rest in the Muddy Pellet (the crown of the head). This is what is called “replenishing the brain and returning the essence”, and the way of long life lies here! If people want long life, apart from persisting without pause in these two methods, guarding the center and the River Chariot, there is no other method of accumulating essence and breath. Nevertheless, the fire of guarding the center only nourishes the lead tripod (the lower elixir field) gently and warmly; only the reverse turning of the River Chariot has the distinctions of zi and wu (advancing and withdrawing), mao and you (ablution), civil or martial fire. If one can truly nourish warmly all the time, so that my Original Spirit always dwells in the heart and my original breath always lies hidden in the body, then even if one wanted him to die, how could one make him die? For spirit and breath are always united and never part.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

玄关窍开之时，我即以真意主宰，凝神气穴，温之养之，等待真阳大发，我再运行子午周天，此为守中、河车二法之运用，亦玄关窍开之后积精累气不易之法。

清代李涵虚著《三车秘旨》一书，对玄关窍开之后功法另有真诀。李氏云：“学人把初醒之心，陡地拨转，移过下鹊桥（按在肛门至尾椎骨之间），即天罡前一位，誓愿不传之真诀也。此心名曰天地之心，又名妙心，又名元神，又名真意，又名玄关发见。移至尾闾，守而不离，霎时间真气温温，从尾闾骨尖两孔中透过腰脊，升至玉枕，钻入泥丸。古仙云：‘夹脊双关透顶门，修行路径此为尊’，即指此也。”李氏之法，实合守中、河车二法为一法行之，另辟蹊径，简而捷。

When the Mysterious Pass opens, I let the true intention preside, concentrate the spirit in the Cavity of Breath, and warm it and nourish it; I wait until true yang arises in full, and then I run the zi-wu Celestial Circuit — this is the application of the two methods of guarding the center and the River Chariot, and also the unchanging method of accumulating essence and breath after the opening of the Mysterious Pass.

The Secret Principles of the Three Carts by Li Hanxu of the Qing dynasty has a separate true formula for the practice after the opening of the Mysterious Pass. Li says: “The student abruptly turns the mind that has just woken and moves it past the lower Magpie Bridge (note: between the anus and the coccyx), that is, ‘one position in front of the Celestial Dipper’ (Li Hanxu’s cryptic expression for the location of the lower Magpie Bridge); this is a true formula sworn never to be transmitted lightly. This mind is called the mind of Heaven and Earth, also the wondrous mind, also the Original Spirit, also the true intention, also the appearance of the Mysterious Pass. Move it to the Caudal Funnel and guard it without leaving, and in an instant the true breath becomes warm, passes from the two holes at the tip of the coccyx through the lumbar spine, rises to the Jade Pillow Pass and bores into the Muddy Pellet. An immortal of old said: 'The twin passes of the spine pierce through to the gate at the crown — of all paths of cultivation, this is the most honored’; this is what it refers to.” Li’s method in fact combines the two methods of guarding the center and the River Chariot into a single method; it opens a separate path, simple and quick.

Key Terms

  • Midnight at the junction of the end of hai and the beginning of zi: the moment in the day when yin reaches its extreme and yang is born; a metaphor for the awakening of the Mysterious Pass.
  • Concentrating the spirit in the Cavity of Breath: the first step after the Mysterious Pass opens.
  • Cavity of Breath: not a lump of flesh with a shape, but the place where spirit and breath unite; roughly beside the Sea of Breath; see lower Cinnabar Field.
  • Rising and surging, diffuse and mingling: the warm, pervasive state when embryonic breathing starts up from the lower origin.
  • Golden tripod and jade furnace: no real tripod or furnace, but the place where spirit and breath unite and gather.
  • Where a man’s essence stirs, where a woman’s womb is attached: a popular way of describing the depth of the lower elixir field.
  • Dim and indistinct, as if here, and as if not here: the right measure of keeping the mind on a place without clinging.
  • The birth of yang of the nature: when the mysterious opening first opens, one sees only the original nature in the palace of Li (the light of nature); a chance movement of the spirit, not a true movement of the breath.
  • The birth of yang of life: the fire of the spirit shines down, the water meeting the fire transforms into a single breath, gold is born at the bottom of the water — that is, the Living Zi Hour when yang is born.
  • Guarding the center: warmly nourishing with the fire of the spirit, letting the breath’s dynamism move up and down within and be stored in the lower elixir field; see guarding the center and embracing the One.
  • True central yellow position: in this book, the lower elixir field.
  • The forward course is short: the stage of guarding the center and warm nourishing, a short road, as opposed to the reverse turning of the River Chariot.
  • Gold born at the bottom of the water: see One Breath of Prior Heaven.
  • River Chariot: running the Celestial Circuit; see Celestial Circuit.
  • Replenishing the brain and returning the essence: the true breath rising along the governing vessel to the Muddy Pellet.
  • Lead tripod: the lower elixir field.
  • Zi and wu, mao and you: advancing and withdrawing, and ablution, in the reverse turning of the River Chariot; see fire phasing and ablution.
  • Lower Magpie Bridge: here Qiu Ling places it between the anus and the coccyx; see Magpie Bridge.
  • One position in front of the Celestial Dipper: Li Hanxu’s cryptic expression for the location of the lower Magpie Bridge.

Putting It into Practice

  1. When the Mysterious Pass opens, do not rush to “circulate” anything. The first step is to concentrate the spirit in the Cavity of Breath and nourish warmly — gently bring the spirit of that awakening back to the lower elixir field.
  2. Do not hold the Cavity of Breath rigidly at one point. It is roughly deep in the lower abdomen, “as if here, and as if not here”.
  3. Tell the two kinds of movement apart: a brightness before the eyes, a clearing of the mind, is “the birth of yang of the nature” — only nourish it warmly, do not move it around; only when a mass of warm breath truly spreads out in the lower abdomen is it “the birth of yang of life”, and only then is there any question of the River Chariot.
  4. The River Chariot must wait until true yang arises in full. Qiu Ling’s own words are: “wait until true yang arises in full, then run the zi-wu Celestial Circuit”. Forcibly pulling the breath around the back with the mind when there is no true breath is not the River Chariot, and can easily cause discomforts such as a swollen head or insomnia (in the second book, Qiu Ling’s note on the Secret Principles of the Three Carts quotes Talks on the Openings of the Dao: the River Chariot “is not a method of visualizing and moving things around”).
  5. Li Hanxu’s “moving past the lower Magpie Bridge” is another route; the section on the Secret Principles of the Three Carts in the second book, Essentials for Entering Alchemical Practice, has detailed notes and can be read alongside this one.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is in two paragraphs, both from the Sayings from the Hall of Joyful Nurture, each divided into two short passages.
  2. “In this body of flesh, the place where spirit and breath unite is the Cavity of Breath” (肉团子上是神气合一之气穴也): the sense is that this Cavity of Breath does not consist in some lump of flesh; in this body of flesh, it is the place where spirit and breath unite.
  3. “Its vital spring comes to rest” (神气散则机息): when spirit and breath scatter, that spring stops.
  4. “Gathering the muni in two stages”: in the first section, Huang Yuanji says “directing and gathering is one stage, returning to the furnace and warmly nourishing is one stage”; here he also calls guarding the center and warm nourishing “the forward course is short, gathering the muni in two stages”.
  5. The words of Li Hanxu quoted in Qiu Ling’s note come from “The First Cart: the River Chariot” in the Secret Principles of the Three Carts, which is translated in full in the second book.
