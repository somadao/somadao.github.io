Chapter Six · The Union of Kan and Li and of Qian and Kun · 凝神泥丸

Concentrating the Spirit in the Muddy Pellet

Concentrating the spirit in the Muddy Pellet — if below is not closed, the fire does not gather and the metal does not rise; if above is not closed, the fire does not concentrate and the elixir does not form; it all takes but a moment’s work, a cleansing, and then gathering back into the tripod and furnace

About This Chapter

The last section of this chapter adds a step in the union of Qian and Kun that is often overlooked: concentrating the spirit in the Muddy Pellet for a moment.

  1. Gathering the fire in two places: advancing the fire at zi and withdrawing by the tally at wu is Kan and Li meeting below the Winding River (the lower elixir field), gathering the fire to carry it up to Qian (the head, the tripod); Qian and Kun meet at the Muddy Pellet, gathering the fire to concentrate it and send it down to Kun (the abdomen, the furnace). The method of gathering the fire is the key point of cultivating the elixir.
  2. “Closing below” and “closing above”: the ancients said: if below is not closed, the fire does not gather and the metal (breath, the medicine) does not rise; if above is not closed, the fire does not concentrate and the elixir does not form. The fire is the spirit, and the spirit is I — the commander of cultivating the Way. Closing below is concentrating the spirit in the lower field; closing above is concentrating the spirit in the upper field.
  3. Why concentrate the spirit in the Muddy Pellet for a moment: most people know to concentrate the spirit in the lower field, but few know to concentrate the spirit in the Muddy Pellet for a moment. The metal breath that rises to the Muddy Pellet is forged out of utmost yin turbidity and filth; though called true yang, it is in fact much mixed with the fire of desire; it is pushed upward by the fierce cooking of the spirit-fire, and its dregs have not yet been washed away — without concentrating the spirit for a moment, the yang breath is not true; how could it be gathered back into the furnace to form the elixir?
  4. The image of cleansing: in a moment, what is light and clear rises to Heaven and what is heavy and turbid sinks to Earth; after a cleansing it is gathered back into the tripod and furnace and nourished warmly with the spirit-fire, and naturally becomes bright — like the sun washed in the sea, the morning sun first rising, ever more radiant.

Qiu Ling’s note: “concentrate the spirit in the navel of Kun and the medicine is produced; concentrate the spirit at the crown of Qian and the elixir is formed” — concentrating the spirit in the Muddy Pellet in the union of Qian and Kun is an essential method of gathering the fire. He quotes the Awakening to Reality and the Mirror for Compounding the Medicine, “produced in Kun, planted in Qian”, and “returning the essence to replenish the brain”, to show the importance of the Muddy Pellet; and he quotes Huang’s words: when the Original Spirit shines down on the watery storehouse and the original breath of the lower field surges, it is called “golden lotuses welling up from the earth”; when the original breath rises to the Muddy Pellet, it is called “a jeweled canopy hanging from the sky”.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

至于子进火、午退符者，是坎离交媾于曲江之下，聚火载之而上升于乾。乾即鼎，鼎即首也。乾坤交媾于泥丸之地，聚火凝之而下降于坤。坤即炉，炉即腹也。是聚火之法为修丹要旨。昔人云，下不闭则火不聚而金不升，金即气，气即药也；上不闭则火不凝而丹不结，丹即外之阳气以合人身之阴精。两相交合为丹，犹夫妇交会精血结为子也。总之，得药结丹，火为要矣！火即神，神即我，修道之主帅也。

As for advancing the fire at zi and withdrawing by the tally at wu, this is Kan and Li meeting below the Winding River (the lower elixir field), gathering the fire to carry it up to Qian — Qian is the tripod, and the tripod is the head; Qian and Kun meeting at the place of the Muddy Pellet, gathering the fire to concentrate it and send it down to Kun — Kun is the furnace, and the furnace is the abdomen. So the method of gathering the fire is the key point of cultivating the elixir. Earlier people said: “If below is not closed, the fire does not gather and the metal does not rise” — the metal is breath, and breath is the medicine; “if above is not closed, the fire does not concentrate and the elixir does not form” — the elixir is the yang breath coming from outside united with the yin essence of the human body. The two unite and form the elixir, just as husband and wife meet and essence and blood form an embryo. In short, in obtaining the medicine and forming the elixir, fire is what matters most! The fire is the spirit, and the spirit is I, the commander of cultivating the Way.

§2
Original (Chinese)

下闭即凝神下田，上闭即凝神上田。世之修士多有知下田凝神之法，而泥丸一所能知凝神片晌者少矣！盖此时金气虽升泥丸，要知此气从至阴浊秽之中煅出，虽名真阳，其实夹杂欲火者多。既上泥丸，无非神火猛烹，追逐之力为之上腾其中，渣滓尚未能淘汰得净，煅炼得清，如此不凝神一刻则阳气不真，安得收归炉内而成丹？故曰：都来片晌工夫，轻清者上升于天，重浊者下降于地。故经一番洗刷，然后收归鼎炉加以神火温养，自然缉熙光明，犹太阳之洗刷于海中，然后旭日瞳瞳，越见光华可爱、净清无尘耳。此理同也。

“Closing below” is concentrating the spirit in the lower elixir field; “closing above” is concentrating the spirit in the upper elixir field (the Muddy Pellet). Practitioners in the world mostly know the method of concentrating the spirit in the lower elixir field, but those who know to concentrate the spirit for a moment at this place, the Muddy Pellet, are very few! For at this time, although the metal breath has risen to the Muddy Pellet, you must know that this breath is forged out of utmost yin turbidity and filth; though called true yang, it in fact contains much of the fire of desire. That it has risen to the Muddy Pellet is nothing but the force of the spirit-fire cooking fiercely and driving it upward; its dregs have not yet been washed out and refined pure — if at this time one does not concentrate the spirit for a moment, the yang breath is not true; how could it be gathered back into the furnace to form the elixir? So it is said: “It all takes but a moment’s work”; what is light and clear rises to Heaven, what is heavy and turbid sinks to Earth. So it must pass through a cleansing, and then be gathered back into the tripod and furnace and nourished warmly with the spirit-fire, and naturally it gradually becomes bright — just as the sun, after being washed in the sea, rises as the morning sun, ever more radiant and lovely, pure and without dust. The principle is the same.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§2
Original note (Chinese)

语云：“凝神入坤脐而生药，凝神入乾顶而成丹。”可知乾坤交媾中凝神于泥丸，乃为紧要的聚火之法。《悟真篇》云：“依他坤位生成体，种在乾家交感宫”。《入药镜》说：“产在坤，种在乾。”均言凝神于泥丸，以促药之出炉升鼎，并促阳气与阴精在泥丸气化、净化为甘露神水的重要性。

古人云：“欲得不老，还精补脑”，抽铅添汞实是还精补脑。又云：“脑神精根字泥丸”，“识得本来真面目，始知生死在泥丸”。由此可见，学者岂能忽视泥丸的作用？

黄元吉氏称，以离宫之元神下照水府，则水府金生，下田元气蓬勃，氤氲鼓荡，即所谓“地涌金莲”；久之此元气上升泥丸，即所谓“天垂宝盖”。

A common saying goes: “Concentrate the spirit in the navel of Kun and the medicine is produced; concentrate the spirit at the crown of Qian and the elixir is formed.” From this we know that in the union of Qian and Kun, concentrating the spirit in the Muddy Pellet is an essential method of gathering the fire. The Awakening to Reality says: “Relying on the position of Kun, the substance is formed; it is planted in the palace of union of the house of Qian.” The Mirror for Compounding the Medicine says: “Produced in Kun, planted in Qian.” Both say that concentrating the spirit in the Muddy Pellet can help the medicine leave the furnace and rise to the tripod, and help the yang breath and yin essence transform and be purified in the Muddy Pellet into sweet dew, the spirit water — this step is very important.

The ancients said: “If you want not to grow old, return the essence to replenish the brain.” Drawing out lead and adding mercury is in fact returning the essence to replenish the brain. They also said: “The spirit of the brain is named Root of Essence, styled Muddy Pellet”, and “Recognize your original true face, and you will know that life and death lie in the Muddy Pellet.” From this we can see — how could students overlook the role of the Muddy Pellet?

Huang Yuanji says: use the Original Spirit of the palace of Li to shine down on the watery storehouse, and the watery storehouse gives birth to metal; the original breath of the lower elixir field surges, diffusing, mingling and stirring — this is what is called “golden lotuses welling up from the earth”; in time this original breath rises to the Muddy Pellet — this is what is called “a jeweled canopy hanging from the sky”.

Key Terms

  • Advancing the fire at zi, withdrawing by the tally at wu: the advance and withdrawal of the fire phasing; see fire phasing.
  • Winding River: a coded expression for the area of the lower elixir field.
  • Qian is the tripod, the tripod is the head: the head is the tripod.
  • Kun is the furnace, the furnace is the abdomen: the abdomen is the furnace.
  • The method of gathering the fire: concentrating with the spirit so that the medicine rises and the elixir forms.
  • Closing below, closing above: concentrating the spirit in the lower field and in the upper field (the Muddy Pellet).
  • It all takes but a moment’s work: only a moment of work in all.
  • Growing bright: gradually becoming bright.
  • Concentrate the spirit in the navel of Kun and the medicine is produced, concentrate the spirit at the crown of Qian and the elixir is formed: the medicine is produced below, the elixir formed above.
  • Produced in Kun, planted in Qian: words of the Mirror for Compounding the Medicine.
  • Returning the essence to replenish the brain: another way of saying drawing out lead and adding mercury.
  • The spirit of the brain is named Root of Essence, styled Muddy Pellet: words of the Scripture of the Inner Effulgences of the Yellow Court.
  • Golden lotuses welling up from the earth, a jeweled canopy hanging from the sky: the scenes of the original breath surging in the lower field and rising to the Muddy Pellet.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Two “closings”: concentrating the spirit in the lower field is the foundation (gathering the fire to produce the medicine); concentrating the spirit in the Muddy Pellet is only “a moment” — just a moment.
  2. After the moment, gather it back: concentrate the spirit briefly in the Muddy Pellet, wait for the light and clear to rise and the heavy and turbid to sink, then gather it back into the elixir field for warm nourishing; do not stay at the head for long.
  3. Safety note: this section speaks of what happens at the stage of the union of Qian and Kun, after the true breath-energy has already risen to the Muddy Pellet. If you have not reached this stage, do not deliberately fix the mind on the Muddy Pellet or the crown. Qiu Ling has already warned in his note to Chapter One that people with yin deficiency and yang excess or high blood pressure should not fix the mind on the head; if a swollen feeling in the head, headache, a flushed face, ringing in the ears or insomnia appear, stop at once and return to the spirit-light shining down on the elixir field.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is one paragraph; the original book does not give the source. It is divided into two short passages.
  2. “It all takes but a moment’s work”: based on Zhang Ziyang’s Awakening to Reality, “It all takes but a moment’s work, to preserve forever endless ease and joy”.
  3. “The spirit of the brain is named Root of Essence, styled Muddy Pellet”: from the Scripture of the Inner Effulgences of the Yellow Court, “The Utmost Way” chapter.
