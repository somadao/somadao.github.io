Chapter Six · The Union of Kan and Li and of Qian and Kun · 醍醐灌顶

Ghee Poured on the Crown

Ghee poured on the crown — mercury descends, lead rises, meeting in the central palace, and the medicine is obtained; true lead rises to the Muddy Pellet and becomes one with the yin essence, transforming into sweet dew, the spirit water, which is swallowed and sent into the Yellow Court

About This Chapter

This section uses the image of “ghee poured on the crown” (the Buddhist image of anointing with the finest essence of milk) to go through the process of the meeting of Kan and Li and the union of Qian and Kun once more in full, giving the scenes of each stage.

  1. First understand lead and mercury: the ancients said, “Mercury is what my own house has always had; lead is the deathless recipe from another house.” Mercury is the numinous fluid in the heart (true water), lead is true yang (true breath-energy). But taken simply as mercury being essence and lead being breath, both are still Posterior Heaven things with form; used in the forward direction they can only produce children, and cannot serve as the great medicine of long life.
  2. Kan and Li meet and the medicine is obtained: mercury descends, lead rises, meeting in the central palace; concentrate the spirit and regulate the breath, and in a moment it rises and surges, like mist and smoke, like tide and sea — only this counts as true lead, fit to be the root of refining the elixir.
  3. Qian and Kun unite and the elixir forms: set the yang fire and yin tally going, up in reverse from the Caudal Funnel to the Muddy Pellet, and the yin essence long accumulated in the Muddy Pellet becomes one with this point of true lead breath.
  4. The scene: head and eyes clear, unlike the usual dullness, like wind scattering the clouds and the sky clear and bright; lead and mercury merge and transform in the Muddy Pellet, and one feels only a cool, tranquil flavor transforming into sweet dew, the spirit water, fragrant and sweet — this is “ghee poured on the crown”. It falls from the palate, is swallowed and sent into the Yellow Court for warm nourishing — this is sealing.
  5. After this: once the true essence is born, the whole body is soft as cotton, as if about to sleep yet not asleep, about to wake yet not awake, and the usually restless body and mind are undividedly still and unmoving — this state cannot be reached without great stillness and great concentration.
  6. The knack of gathering and union: gathering is a single intention concentrated without the slightest scattering — “gathering without gathering is better than gathering”; union is the spirit entering the breath and the breath wrapping the spirit, the two inseparable.

Qiu Ling’s note: the yang breath rises to the Muddy Pellet and joins with the yin essence, and the breath transforms into sweet dew, the spirit water; at this time one should concentrate the spirit in the Muddy Pellet for a moment, sweep away stray thoughts and nourish it warmly; in an instant the mouth is full of sweet dew, a cold spring dripping drop by drop down to the Crimson Palace, all cool and tranquil; then lead it into the elixir field, cook it warmly with the spirit-fire, and the numinous fluid transforms again into true breath-energy.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

学人欲修性命，先明铅汞。古云：“汞是我家固有之物，铅乃他家不死之方。”若但言心性，无从捉摸，古人借名为汞。此个汞非他，乃心中之灵液。

If the student wants to cultivate nature and life, he must first understand lead and mercury. The ancients said: “Mercury is what my own house has always had; lead is the deathless recipe from another house.” (Mercury is what I have of my own; lead is the deathless recipe obtained from “another house” — within Kan.) If one spoke only of “mind and nature”, people would have nothing to grasp, so the ancients borrowed the name “mercury”. This mercury is nothing else but the numinous fluid in the heart.

§2
Original (Chinese)

灵液即真水，真水即汞也。真阳即真气，真气即铅也。汞为精，铅为气，二者皆后天有形有象之物，只可顺而生男育女，不可为长生大药。必从此汞之下降，铅之上升，会合中宫，凝神调息，片刻间兀兀腾腾如雾如烟如潮如海，才算是真铅可为炼丹之本，所谓坎离交而得药是也。于是运起阳火阴符，逆从尾闾直上泥丸，泥丸久积阴精与我这点真铅之气配合为一，即所谓乾坤交而结丹是也。

The numinous fluid is true water, and true water is mercury; true yang is true breath-energy, and true breath-energy is lead. But if mercury is merely essence and lead merely breath, both are still Posterior Heaven things with form and image, which can only be used in the forward direction to produce sons and daughters, and cannot serve as the great medicine of long life. One must let this mercury descend and the lead rise, meeting in the central palace (the lower elixir field), concentrate the spirit and regulate the breath, and in a moment it rises and surges, like mist and smoke, like tide and sea — only this counts as true lead, fit to be the root of refining the elixir; this is what is called “Kan and Li meet and the medicine is obtained”. Then set the yang fire and yin tally going (advancing the fire and withdrawing the tally), running in reverse from the Caudal Funnel straight up to the Muddy Pellet; the yin essence long accumulated in the Muddy Pellet is matched with my point of true lead breath and becomes one — this is what is called “Qian and Kun unite and the elixir forms”.

§3
Original (Chinese)

阳气上升泥丸有何景象？觉得头目爽利非等平日之昏晕，有如风吹云散而天朗气清，另有一番气象，才算是真汞，以前之汞还是凡汞，不可以养成仙胎。铅汞会于泥丸，斯时之凡精凡气合同而化，不见有铅亦不见有汞，只是一清凉恬淡之味化为甘露神水，香甜可口，不似平日粗精浊气，即古人谓醍醐灌顶是。从上腭落下吞而服之，送入黄庭温养，即封固矣！

What is the scene when the yang breath rises to the Muddy Pellet? One feels head and eyes clear, unlike the usual dullness and heaviness, like wind scattering the clouds, the sky clear and the air fresh, with an atmosphere all its own — only this counts as true mercury; the mercury of before was still ordinary mercury, which cannot nourish the immortal embryo. When lead and mercury meet in the Muddy Pellet, the ordinary essence and ordinary breath of this time merge and transform; no lead is seen, nor any mercury, only a cool, tranquil flavor transforming into Sweet Dew, the spirit water, fragrant and sweet, unlike the coarse essence and turbid breath of ordinary times — this is what the ancients called “ghee poured on the crown”. Let it fall from the palate, swallow it, send it into the Yellow Court (the lower elixir field) and nourish it warmly — that is sealing!

§4
Original (Chinese)

此个真精一生，浑身苏软如绵，欲睡不睡，欲醒不醒，而平日动荡之身心至此浑然湛然不动不摇，自安所止，又何殆之有哉！此境非大静大定不能。若夫采取之法，即一意凝注毫不分散，古人谓不采之采甚于采是。所谓交媾者，即神入气中，气包神外，两两不分是。学人行一步自有一步之效验。

Once this true essence is born, the whole body is soft as cotton, as if about to sleep yet not asleep, about to wake yet not awake; the body and mind that were restless in ordinary times are now undividedly still, unmoving and unshaken, naturally resting where they should — what danger is there! This state cannot be reached without great stillness and great concentration. As for the method of gathering, it is a single intention concentrated, without the slightest scattering — the ancients said “gathering without gathering is better than gathering”, and this is what they meant. What is called union is the spirit entering into the breath and the breath wrapping around the spirit, the two inseparable. For every step the student takes, there is naturally a step of result.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

阳气上升泥丸与阴精结合，起气化作用化为甘露神水，此即以灵父配圣母，以阳气制阴精，以阳铅配阴汞而化为灵液。我于此时须凝神泥丸片刻，务要奋起精神，扫除杂念，温养一番，霎时间甘露满口，寒泉滴滴，落我绛宫，一片清凉恬淡。我将之引入丹田，以神火温烹，灵液又化为真气，久久运转周天煅炼，愈炼愈净，久之外丹成内丹亦就。

The yang breath rises to the Muddy Pellet and joins with the yin essence, and through the action of transformation by breath it becomes sweet dew, the spirit water — this is “matching the numinous father with the holy mother”, subduing yin essence with yang breath, matching yin mercury with yang lead, transforming into numinous fluid. At this time I must concentrate the spirit in the Muddy Pellet for a moment, rousing my spirit, sweeping away stray thoughts and nourishing it warmly; in an instant the mouth is full of sweet dew, a cold spring dripping drop by drop, falling to my Crimson Palace (between heart and chest), all cool and tranquil. I lead it into the elixir field and cook it warmly with the spirit-fire, and the numinous fluid transforms again into true breath-energy; turning the Celestial Circuit and refining it for a long time, the more it is refined the purer it becomes, and in time, when the outer elixir is formed, the inner elixir is formed too.

Key Terms

  • Mercury is what my own house has always had, lead is the deathless recipe from another house: mercury is one’s own fluid of the heart, lead the true yang in Kan.
  • Numinous fluid, true water: the mercury in the heart.
  • True yang, true breath-energy: the lead in Kan.
  • Central palace: in this book, the lower elixir field.
  • Yang fire and yin tally: advancing the fire and withdrawing the tally; see fire phasing.
  • Ghee poured on the crown: lead and mercury meeting in the Muddy Pellet and transforming into cool, fragrant, sweet dew, the spirit water; see Sweet Dew.
  • Palate: the roof of the mouth, from which the sweet dew falls.
  • Sealing: sending into the Yellow Court for warm nourishing, stored without leaking.
  • Gathering without gathering is better than gathering: a single concentrated intention is itself gathering, without deliberate effort.
  • Numinous father, holy mother: metaphors for the yang breath and the yin essence.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The sign of the true medicine: in sitting, mercury descends and lead rises, meeting in the elixir field, and in a moment it is like mist and smoke, like tide and sea — only this is true lead fit to be the root.
  2. What to do with the sweet dew: the cool, sweet saliva in the mouth should not be spat out; let it go down naturally from the palate, gently sending it to the elixir field with the intention.
  3. Gathering without force: “gathering without gathering is better than gathering” — a single intention concentrated without scattering is gathering.
  4. Safety note: “running in reverse from the Caudal Funnel straight up to the Muddy Pellet” and “concentrating the spirit in the Muddy Pellet for a moment” belong to a fairly advanced stage, and must come after the true breath-energy of the lower elixir field is ample and stirs naturally. Beginners should not lead the breath up the spine with the mind, nor keep the mind on the crown for long. People with high blood pressure, yin deficiency and yang excess, headaches or insomnia should especially avoid it. If a swollen feeling in the head, headache, ringing in the ears or a flushed face appear, stop at once and return to natural relaxation.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is in two paragraphs; the original book does not give the source. The second paragraph is divided into three short passages.
  2. “What danger is there” (又何殆之有哉): dai means danger.
  3. “Mercury is what my own house has always had; lead is the deathless recipe from another house”: the original only says “the ancients said”, without a source.
