Chapter Six · The Union of Kan and Li and of Qian and Kun · 坎离交而产药

Kan and Li Unite and the Medicine Is Produced

Kan and Li unite and the medicine is produced — the one yang in Kan rises, the one yin in Li descends, meeting in the Xu-Wei cavity; true lead is not born from Kan nor had from Li, but forged out of the Cave of Emptiness

About This Chapter

This section speaks of the first result of the meeting of Kan and Li: the production of the medicine.

  1. First regulate the Posterior Heaven water and fire: if one wants to trace back to that point of empty yang at the beginning of being born and receiving breath, one must first harmonize the water and fire of Kan and Li in the physical body. Once the Posterior Heaven water and fire are regulated, the one yang in Kan rises from below and the one yin in Li descends from above, meeting in the Xu-Wei cavity (the perineum); after cooking and refining, the Prior Heaven one breath returns and the gate of the Mysterious Female shows its signs.
  2. “Method, wealth, companions and place”: cultivating the Way requires four things. Huang Yuanji speaks of the four “inner” ones — the one yang in Kan and the one yin in Li are the inner wealth; finding a wondrous opening in one’s body and mind is the inner method; the original person is the inner companion; the Xu-Wei cavity is the inner place. The outer method, wealth, companions and place the students already know, so he does not speak of them.
  3. Where true lead comes from: when the true yin and true yang of Kan and Li come forth at one stroke, and water becomes firm and fire yielding, the tripod empty and the medicine full, a point of true yang breath of Heaven and Earth naturally comes, neither from within nor from without — only this is true lead, the Prior Heaven Qian metal. It is not born from Kan, nor had from Li, but forged by the water and fire of Kan and Li out of the Cave of Emptiness, neither inside nor outside. Huang Yuanji points this out specially, so that students will not mistake the yang breath in Kan for the root of refining the elixir.

Qiu Ling’s note: the method of the meeting of Kan and Li is first to regulate the outer breathing so as to bring forth the true breath (“as soon as the breath is drawn in, that is the true union of Kan and Li”), with the true intention presiding. Turn the light back on the lower field, let the true breath descend into the yinqiao (the perineum), lift the moving breath of the perineum up into the Yellow Court; and use the true breath to lead the yin essence of the Crimson Palace down to meet in the elixir field — in time the yin essence transforms into true yang and the ordinary breath into true yin, vigorous and surging. He also explains the outer method, wealth, companions and place: the method of practice, the money for living and practicing, companions on the Way, and the place for practice.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

修行人知生死之关，明真假之故，欲穷生身受气之初那一点虚无之阳，必先向色身中调和坎离水火。迨后天水火既调，然后坎中一阳自下而上，离中一阴自上而下，上下相会于虚危穴中，烹之炼之而先天一气来归，玄牝之门兆象矣！

When the practitioner knows the pass of life and death and understands the reasons for true and false, and wants to trace back to that point of empty yang at the beginning of being born and receiving breath, he must first harmonize the water and fire of Kan and Li (heart-fire and kidney-water) in the physical body. When the Posterior Heaven water and fire are harmonized, then the one yang in Kan rises from below and the one yin in Li descends from above, meeting above and below in the Xu-Wei cavity (the perineum); cook it and refine it, and the Prior Heaven one breath returns, and the gate of the Mysterious Female shows its signs!

§2
Original (Chinese)

此坎中一阳、离中一阴即内财也。日夜神火温养，不许一丝渗漏，即积内财也。能向自家身心寻出一个妙窍，即内法也。前言本来人，即内伴侣也。虚危一穴即内地也。欲炼神丹，四者岂可不备乎？内之法、财、侣、地吾已道破，外之法、财、侣、地诸子谅已知之，吾不再赘。

This one yang in Kan and one yin in Li are the “inner wealth”; nourishing them warmly with the spirit-fire day and night, not letting a drop seep away, is accumulating the inner wealth. Being able to find a wondrous opening within one’s own body and mind is the “inner method”. The “original person” spoken of earlier is the “inner companion”. This Xu-Wei cavity is the “inner place”. If one wants to refine the spirit elixir, how could these four not be in place? The inner method, wealth, companions and place I have now laid bare; the outer method, wealth, companions and place you probably already know, so I will not say more.

§3
Original (Chinese)

有此坎离真阴真阳一鼓而出，及至水刚火柔，鼎虚药实，自然天地一点真阳之气不自内、不自外出来。此即所谓真铅也，又即所谓先天乾金也。夫以凡铅而言，则坎中一阳、离中一阴皆真铅。以先天真铅而论，则坎中一阳、离中一阴皆属后天有气有质之物。从此想来，此个真铅真阳，不自坎生，不自离有，原从不内不外虚无窟里由坎离水火二物煅锻而来者也。吾今道破，以免学人误认坎中阳气为吾人炼丹之本。

When the true yin and true yang of Kan and Li come forth at one stroke, and water becomes firm and fire yielding (water and fire assisting each other), the tripod empty and the medicine full, naturally a point of the true yang breath of Heaven and Earth comes forth of itself, neither from within nor from without — this is what is called “true lead”, also what is called “the Prior Heaven Qian metal”. Speaking in terms of ordinary lead, the one yang in Kan and the one yin in Li may both be called true lead; speaking in terms of Prior Heaven true lead, the one yang in Kan and the one yin in Li still both belong to Posterior Heaven things with breath and substance. Thinking of it this way, this true lead, this true yang, is not born from Kan, nor had from Li; it is originally forged out of the Cave of Emptiness, neither inside nor outside, by the two things water and fire of Kan and Li. I lay this bare today so that students will not mistake the yang breath in Kan for the root of our refining the elixir.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§3
Original note (Chinese)

坎离交然后真阳现，即坎离交而产药。其法为必先调外呼吸以引起真人之息。古人云，一自敛息，即是坎离真交。而调外呼吸则靠真意主宰之。我此时须回光返照下田，并以真息下入阴跻，阴跻即是虚危穴，即是会阴穴，提起会阴之动气，上入黄庭中，又以真息引起绛宫之阴精，下会丹田，久之阴精与阳气两相交融，凝于丹田之中，自然阴精化为真阳，凡气化为真阴，蓬蓬勃勃，真气冲冲，此即坎离交媾之景。

由此可见，坎中一阳、离中一阴交合后可生真阳，称为内财；向自家身中寻出一个妙窍，即指玄关窍，为内法；看到本来面目（本来人）即内伴侣；虚危一穴即内地。四者总称为“内法、财、侣、地”。至于“外法、财、侣、地”，法指功法；财为维持练功者生活及练功之费用；侣指道侣；地指练功之所。

After Kan and Li meet, true yang appears — this is “Kan and Li unite and the medicine is produced”. The method is: one must first regulate the outer breathing, so as to bring forth the breath of the true person (the true breath). The ancients said: “As soon as the breath is drawn in, that is the true union of Kan and Li.” And regulating the outer breathing depends on the true intention presiding. At this time I must revert the light to shine within the lower elixir field, and let the true breath descend into the yinqiao — the yinqiao is the Xu-Wei cavity, that is, the perineum point — lifting the moving breath of the perineum up into the Yellow Court (in this book, the lower elixir field); and with the true breath I lead the yin essence of the Crimson Palace (the heart) down to meet in the elixir field. In time, the yin essence and the yang breath blend and gather in the elixir field; naturally the yin essence transforms into true yang and the ordinary breath into true yin, vigorous and surging, the true breath-energy rushing forth — this is the scene of the union of Kan and Li.

From this we see: the one yang in Kan and the one yin in Li, once united, can give birth to true yang, and are called “inner wealth”; finding a wondrous opening in one’s own body — that is, the opening of the Mysterious Pass — is the “inner method”; seeing the original face (the original person) is the “inner companion”; the Xu-Wei cavity is the “inner place”. The four together are called “the inner method, wealth, companions and place”. As for “the outer method, wealth, companions and place”: method refers to the method of practice; wealth is the money needed for the practitioner’s living and practice; companions refers to companions on the Way; place refers to the place for practice.

Key Terms

  • Xu-Wei cavity: the perineum point, the yinqiao; see perineum.
  • Method, wealth, companions and place: the four conditions necessary for cultivating the Way; the inner ones are the wondrous opening, true yin and true yang, the original person and the Xu-Wei cavity; the outer ones are the method, money, companions on the Way and the place.
  • Inner wealth: the one yang in Kan and the one yin in Li.
  • Inner method: the wondrous opening in body and mind, that is, the opening of the Mysterious Pass.
  • Inner companion: the original person (the original face).
  • Inner place: the Xu-Wei cavity.
  • Water firm and fire yielding: water and fire assisting each other, firmness and yielding exchanged.
  • The tripod empty and the medicine full: the elixir field empty and the medicine ample.
  • True lead: the Prior Heaven Qian metal, forged out of the Cave of Emptiness; see Lead and Mercury.
  • As soon as the breath is drawn in, that is the true union of Kan and Li: when breathing is drawn in, that is the meeting of Kan and Li.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The meeting of Kan and Li begins with regulating the breathing. Qiu Ling: “one must first regulate the outer breathing so as to bring forth the breath of the true person”, with the true intention presiding.
  2. Do not take the yang breath of the lower body for the medicine. Huang Yuanji points this out specially: true lead is not born from Kan nor had from Li, but forged out of the emptiness neither inside nor outside — become still until empty, and it comes of itself.
  3. The four outer things must also be in place: method, money for living, companions, a quiet place — cultivation is not something cut off from life.
  4. Safety note: “lifting the moving breath of the perineum up into the Yellow Court” in Qiu Ling’s note refers to a slight drawing up after the true breath has naturally sunk to the perineum, not forcefully contracting the anus or holding the breath. Excessive force easily causes stagnation of breath, abdominal bloating, constipation and other discomforts.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is in two paragraphs; the original book does not give the source. The first paragraph is divided into two short passages.
  2. “As soon as the breath is drawn in, that is the true union of Kan and Li”: quoted in Qiu Ling’s note as “the ancients said”, without a source.
