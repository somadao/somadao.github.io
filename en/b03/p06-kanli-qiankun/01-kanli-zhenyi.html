Chapter Six · The Union of Kan and Li and of Qian and Kun · 坎离真义

The True Meaning of Kan and Li

The true meaning of Kan and Li — from the Bubbling Spring to the Sea of Breath is Kan, from the Muddy Pellet to the Mysterious Pass is Li; it is the breaths of water and fire that are Kan and Li, not the heart and kidneys

About This Chapter

Chapter Six speaks of “the union of Kan and Li” and “the union of Qian and Kun” — the two most common and also most obscure sets of expressions in the alchemical way. The first section begins by making clear what Kan and Li actually refer to.

  1. Kan and Li come from Qian and Kun: once Qian and Kun (pure yang, pure yin) are broken, they become Kan and Li, no longer the original things. Li (☲) is yang outside and yin inside, Kan (☵) yin outside and yang inside — what is outside is false, only what is inside is true.
  2. Their position in the human body: from the Bubbling Spring (the sole of the foot) to the Sea of Breath (the lower elixir field) belongs to yang and is Kan; from the Muddy Pellet (the crown) to the Mysterious Pass (between the two eyes) belongs to yin and is Li. It is the breaths of water and fire that are Kan and Li, not the heart and kidneys.
  3. Divided by function: whatever gathers hearing, holds the light of the eyes and accomplishes the perceptions of smell, taste, touch and mind is the spirit-fire in charge, and so belongs to Li; the flesh and blood of the whole body are nothing but essence, and where essence is, there is nothing but breath — where essence and breath are belongs to Kan.

Qiu Ling’s note explains with the trigram diagrams: in Prior Heaven (the trigrams of Fuxi), Qian is above and Kun below; fallen into Posterior Heaven (the trigrams of King Wen), the middle line of Qian enters Kun, and Kun becomes Kan; the middle line of Kun enters Qian, and Qian becomes Li. So to return to the root and restore the origin, one must “take from Kan to fill Li” — gather the true yang within Kan to replenish the true yin within Li; this is the union of Kan and Li.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

自乾坤破为坎离，已非旧物矣！离外阳而内阴，坎外阴而内阳。外者为假，内者为真。且离中所有者精神，坎宫所有者气血，坎虚而成实，离有而成无。

Ever since Qian and Kun were broken and became Kan and Li, they have no longer been the original things! Li (☲) is yang outside and yin inside; Kan (☵) is yin outside and yang inside. What is outside is false; only what is inside is true. Moreover, what Li holds is essence and spirit, what the palace of Kan holds is breath and blood; Kan, originally empty, has become full; Li, originally having, has become empty.

§2
Original (Chinese)

自涌泉以至气海皆属阳，阳则为坎。自泥丸以至玄关皆属阴，阴则为离。是水火之气为坎离，非以心肾为坎离也明矣！

From the Bubbling Spring (the sole of the foot) to the Sea of Breath (the lower elixir field) all belongs to yang, and yang is Kan; from the Muddy Pellet (the crown) to the Mysterious Pass (between the two eyes) all belongs to yin, and yin is Li. So it is the breaths of water and fire that are taken as Kan and Li, not the heart and kidneys — this is quite clear!

§3
Original (Chinese)

要知离非属心也，凡凝耳韵、含眼光、成香味触法皆是神火主事，故曰属离。坎非为肾也，一身血肉团子无非是精，凡精所有无非是气，精气所在即是属坎。

You must know that Li does not refer solely to the heart: whatever gathers the sounds in the ear, holds the light in the eye and accomplishes smell, taste, touch and mind is the spirit-fire in charge, and so is said to belong to Li. Nor does Kan refer solely to the kidneys: the flesh and blood of the whole body are nothing but essence; wherever essence is, there is nothing but breath — wherever essence and breath are belongs to Kan.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§3
Original note (Chinese)

此古人运用《周易》八卦以解释人身先后天之分别及阴阳、水火、神气交合之理。坎离交媾代表气功的一次重要进程。

先天卦即伏羲八卦，乾为三个阳爻☰，坤为三个阴爻☷。乾在上（南），为天为阳。坤在下（北），为地为阴。一旦先天落于后天，其卦即为文王八卦，离坎代替了乾坤的方位，乾之中爻入于坤，纯阳之乾遂变为外阴而内阳之坎，即☵，属水；坤之中爻入于乾，纯阴之坤遂变为外阳而内阴之离☲，属火。这样，学者要复还乾坤本来面目，即返本还原，就要采坎中之真阳以补离中真阴，就得实现坎离交媾。

黄元吉氏认为，坎离在人身上的位置是：自涌泉至气海为坎。涌泉在足心陷中，气海即指下丹田。

自泥丸至玄关为离。泥丸在头顶百会穴内，玄关则在两眼之间。他并以神火主事之处和精气所在之地而分离坎。

This is the ancients’ use of the eight trigrams of the Zhouyi to explain the distinction between the Prior and Posterior Heaven in the human body, and the principle of the union of yin and yang, water and fire, spirit and breath. The union of Kan and Li represents an important stage of qigong.

The Prior Heaven trigrams are the trigrams of Fuxi: Qian is three yang lines (☰), Kun three yin lines (☷); Qian is above (south), Heaven and yang; Kun is below (north), Earth and yin. Once the Prior Heaven falls into the Posterior Heaven, the trigrams become those of King Wen, and Li and Kan take the places of Qian and Kun: the middle line of Qian enters Kun, and pure yang Qian becomes Kan (☵), yin outside and yang inside, belonging to water; the middle line of Kun enters Qian, and pure yin Kun becomes Li (☲), yang outside and yin inside, belonging to fire. Thus, if the student wants to restore the original face of Qian and Kun, that is, to return to the root and restore the origin, he must gather the true yang within Kan to replenish the true yin within Li — and for this the union of Kan and Li must be achieved.

Huang Yuanji holds that the position of Kan and Li in the human body is: from the Bubbling Spring to the Sea of Breath is Kan. The Bubbling Spring is in the hollow of the sole of the foot; the Sea of Breath refers to the lower elixir field.

From the Muddy Pellet to the Mysterious Pass is Li. The Muddy Pellet is inside the Baihui point at the crown, and the Mysterious Pass is between the two eyes. He also distinguishes Li and Kan by the place where the spirit-fire is in charge and the place where essence and breath are.

Key Terms

  • Qian and Kun: pure yang and pure yin, the original Prior Heaven face.
  • Conjunction of Kan and Li: taking the true yang within Kan to replenish the true yin within Li, returning to Qian and Kun.
  • Li yang outside and yin inside, Kan yin outside and yang inside: the middle line of Li ☲ is yin, the middle line of Kan ☵ is yang.
  • Bubbling Spring: a point on the sole of the foot.
  • Sea of Breath: here, the lower elixir field.
  • Muddy Pellet: inside the Baihui point at the crown; see Muddy Pellet.
  • Mysterious Pass: here, between the two eyes.
  • The spirit-fire in charge: all functions of perception, belonging to Li.
  • Where essence and breath are: the flesh, blood, essence and breath of the whole body, belonging to Kan.
  • Trigrams of Fuxi, trigrams of King Wen: the Prior Heaven and Posterior Heaven arrangements, with Qian and Kun at south and north, and Li and Kan at south and north respectively.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Kan and Li are not two organs. When reading alchemical books, “Li” refers to spirit (perception, the mind-spirit side), “Kan” to essence and breath (the body, the original breath side); do not map them rigidly onto the heart and kidneys.
  2. What union means: spirit (Li) meeting essence and breath (Kan) below — this is “the spirit-light shining down on the elixir field” repeated throughout the earlier chapters.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is in three paragraphs; the original book does not give the source.
  2. “Accomplishes smell, taste, touch and mind” (成香味触法): so in the original book; listed alongside “gathers the sounds in the ear, holds the light in the eye”, it refers to the perceptions of smell, taste, touch and consciousness.
  3. The trigram signs (☰☷☵☲) are printed as trigram diagrams in the original book; standard symbols are used here instead.
