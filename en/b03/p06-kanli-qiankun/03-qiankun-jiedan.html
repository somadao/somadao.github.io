Chapter Six · The Union of Kan and Li and of Qian and Kun · 乾坤交而结丹

Qian and Kun Unite and the Elixir Forms

Qian and Kun unite and the elixir forms — fire enters the land of water, spirit enters breath, and a moving breath arises in the yinqiao vessel; only when the yang breath rises to the Muddy Pellet and becomes one with the yin essence can the elixir return; move the spirit, not the breath

About This Chapter

The previous section spoke of Kan and Li uniting and the medicine being produced; this section speaks of the second step: Qian and Kun uniting and the elixir forming.

  1. What lead and mercury are: nothing but the two poles of yin and yang. Matching mercury with lead is like matching a woman with a man; after their union the original breath is born, and only when the original breath, together with the yin breath, is brought into the central palace is the elixir formed.
  2. Why there must be “union”: in Prior Heaven, Li was pure yang Qian and Kan pure yin Kun; as soon as the breath’s dynamism stirred, the middle line of Qian entered Kun and the middle line of Kun entered Qian, and they became Kan and Li. Over time, spirit gives birth to essence and breath transforms into blood, and the Posterior Heaven “nature of temperament” and “life of allotted span” arise.
  3. The meeting of Kan and Li (producing the medicine): the accomplished person uses a method to draw in the point of ji mercury (the fluid of the heart) in Li; carrying the nature of fire, it descends into the palace of Kan and steams the point of yin blood there — fire enters the land of water, spirit enters breath, like ice meeting fire or a charcoal fire heating a cauldron; it naturally grows warm, and a moving breath arises in the yinqiao vessel; the hot breath steams and rises — this is true lead being born.
  4. Rising to the Muddy Pellet (transforming into fluid): with the spirit presiding, it rises to the Muddy Pellet — like the hot vapor in distilling liquor rising to the cooling lid and condensing into drops that fall into the jar — this is true mercury, breath transforming into fluid; returned to the furnace and nourished warmly, the fluid transforms again into breath, rising and falling, until the yin breath of the body of breath and blood is stripped away entirely and the ordinary body is transformed into a golden body.
  5. The union of Qian and Kun (forming the elixir): obtaining the birth of lead is only one side of the work. This yang breath must be led upward to unite as one with the yin essence of the whole body and the spirit-marrow and numinous fluid of the Muddy Pellet and Crimson Palace — only this is Qian and Kun uniting and the elixir forming. The earlier meeting of Kan and Li only produced the medicine; only here are there true lead and true mercury. The key is a single sentence: move the spirit, not the breath.

Qiu Ling’s note: from the meeting of Kan and Li to the union of Qian and Kun one must pass through “taking from Kan to fill Li” (“drawing out lead and adding mercury”). The methods of accumulating essence and breath come down to two: guarding the center (at the front of the body, called “the forward course is short”, with the two stages of gathering and sealing) and the River Chariot (at the back of the body, called “the backward course is long”). Obtaining the medicine by guarding the center is only half of the learning; one must go on to turn the River Chariot before the elixir can form.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

丹道言铅言汞，究是何物？不妨明辨之。要知此个物事，不外阴阳两端。以汞配铅即如以女配男，交媾之后化生元气出来，又将元气合阴气入中宫，然后成丹。在先天离是纯阳之乾，坎是纯阴之坤。因气机一动，乾之中爻走入坤宫，坤之中爻走入乾窍，乾遂虚而为离，坤遂实而成坎。故乾虽阳而有阴，坤虽阴而有阳，即非先天纯阴纯阳太极浑沦之旧，然犹不失其正也。

What, after all, are the lead and mercury spoken of in the alchemical way? Let me make it clear. You must know that this thing is nothing but the two poles of yin and yang. Matching mercury with lead is like matching a woman with a man: after their union, the original breath is born; then the original breath, together with the yin breath, is sent into the central palace, and the elixir is formed. In Prior Heaven, Li was originally pure yang Qian, and Kan originally pure yin Kun. Because the breath’s dynamism stirred, the middle line of Qian went into the palace of Kun, and the middle line of Kun went into the opening of Qian; so Qian became empty in the middle and became Li (☲), and Kun became full in the middle and became Kan (☵). Thus Qian, though yang, contains yin, and Kun, though yin, contains yang — although no longer the old appearance of pure yin and pure yang of Prior Heaven, the undivided Supreme Ultimate, they have not yet lost their uprightness.

§2
Original (Chinese)

久之神则生精，气则化血，而质气之性、气数之命从事出矣！盖以有思虑知觉之心，气血形体之身，不似乾坤原物。至人以法追摄离中一点己汞，汞为心液，液虽属阴，却从离火中出，带有火性，下入坎宫，熏坎宫一点阴血。血为坎水，水虽属阳，却从坎水中生，实为寒体。

Over time, spirit gives birth to essence and breath transforms into blood, and from this arise the Posterior Heaven “nature of temperament” (disposition) and “life of allotted span” (a lifespan with a fixed term)! For once a person has a mind that thinks and perceives and a body of breath, blood and form, he is no longer like the original Qian and Kun. The accomplished person uses a method to draw in the point of ji mercury in Li: mercury is the fluid of the heart; the fluid, though yin, comes out of the fire of Li and carries the nature of fire; it descends into the palace of Kan and steams the point of yin blood in the palace of Kan. The blood is the water of Kan; the water, though (containing within it) yang, is born of the water of Kan and is in fact cold by nature.

§3
Original (Chinese)

古人谓火入水乡，神入气里，犹冰凝之遇火，如炭火之热釜，自然温暖生出阴跻一脉动气来。虽然火入水中犹釜底加炭，热气薰蒸上腾，即真铅生也。自此以神运之而上升泥丸（主宰之而已），犹烤酒瓶中热气被火而升于天锅，则成露珠滴入瓮中，此即吾教曰真汞，又曰：“忙将北海初潮水，灌济东山老树根。”其实气化为液而已。复行归炉温养，液又化气，循环不已，一升一降直将气血之躯阴气剥尽，凡身化为金身，浊体变成纯体，俨然成太极虚无之法身焉。

What the ancients called “fire enters the land of water, spirit enters breath” is like frozen ice meeting fire, or a charcoal fire heating a cauldron: it naturally grows warm, and a moving breath arises in the yinqiao (perineum) vessel. Fire entering water is like adding charcoal under a cauldron: the hot breath steams and rises — this is true lead being born. From then on, use the spirit to move it, rising to the Muddy Pellet (only presiding over it, not pushing with force); just as in distilling liquor, the hot vapor in the vessel, heated by fire, rises to the cooling lid and condenses into drops that fall into the jar — this is what we Daoists call “true mercury”, and it is also said: “Quickly take the first tide-water of the northern sea to water the roots of the old tree on the eastern mountain.” In fact it is simply breath transforming into fluid. Returned to the furnace and nourished warmly, the fluid transforms again into breath, cycling without end, rising and falling, until the yin breath of the body of breath and blood is stripped away entirely, the ordinary body transformed into a golden body, the turbid body into a pure body, solemnly becoming the empty Dharma-body of the Supreme Ultimate.

§4
Original (Chinese)

学人得铅生，只算一边功夫，安望结胎成圣？惟将此阳气引之上升，复合周身之阴精，更与泥丸、绛宫之神髓、灵液交合为一，此正谓“东家女（木汞也），西舍郎（金铅也），夫妻配合入洞房，黄婆劝饮醍醐酒，每日薰薰醉一场。”此乾坤交而结丹，前只是坎离交而产药。有此真铅真汞一合，才可还丹。铅即水中所生之金，汞即火中所生之木。前只算凡铅凡汞，到此才算真铅真汞。学人照此用功，运神不运气，庶不至误事焉。

If the student obtains the birth of lead, that counts only as one side of the work; how could he hope from it to form the embryo and become a sage? Only by leading this yang breath upward, uniting it with the yin essence of the whole body, and further uniting it as one with the spirit-marrow and numinous fluid of the Muddy Pellet and the Crimson Palace — this is precisely what is meant by: “The girl of the eastern house (wood mercury), the youth of the western house (metal lead), husband and wife matched enter the bridal chamber; the Yellow Dame urges them to drink the wine of ghee, and every day they get mellow and drunk.” (The Yellow Dame is the true intention.) Only this is Qian and Kun uniting and the elixir forming; what came before was only Kan and Li uniting and the medicine being produced. Only with true lead and true mercury joined can the elixir return. Lead is the metal born in water; mercury is the wood born in fire. What came before counted only as ordinary lead and ordinary mercury; only here are there true lead and true mercury. If students work in this way, moving the spirit, not the breath (using only the spirit to preside, not using force to move the breath), they will not go wrong.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

从坎离交媾而至乾坤交媾，须经取坎填离，即取坎中真阳补离中真阴，复还乾坤本来面目。法为抽铅添汞，即以汞投铅，以铅制汞，使真铅真汞会合而结丹。

铅汞不过是代号，犹如人身中乾坤、坎离，总之是神气、水火、性命、阴阳而已。我须常存夫妇相配之念，以神入气，以火入水乡，即是夫妇相配，则真阳出，亦即真铅产。人身上半为天为阳，下半为地为阴，非有神火烹煎，氤氲之气何由发生？坎中所生阳气上至泥丸，即是阳气制阴精，以阳铅配阴汞，亦夫妻相配而产药。药即气化所生灵液，又名甘露神水，再引之入丹田温烹，乾坤覆合，又化为气，即是先天乾元一气。

积精累气之法不外守中（中即虚）和河车（周天）二法。守中一法即水火济、坎离交之候，在身前进行又谓“前行短”，有采取、封固二候功夫。河车一法即阳生后乾坤交媾之候，在身后进行又谓“后行长”，运动河车，栖神泥丸，□□□□是，有起小周天、进退颠倒、沐浴、火足止火等四□□□。

总之，守中得药只算半边学问，即使阳生亦只□□，如无阴汞以配之，犹不能结丹，故须继行河车，久久运转，□□万炼，才能成丹。

From the union of Kan and Li to the union of Qian and Kun one must pass through “taking from Kan to fill Li” — taking the true yang within Kan to replenish the true yin within Li, restoring the original face of Qian and Kun. The method is “drawing out lead and adding mercury”: casting mercury into lead and subduing mercury with lead, so that true lead and true mercury meet and form the elixir.

Lead and mercury are only code names, like Qian and Kun, Kan and Li in the human body; in short, they are nothing but spirit and breath, water and fire, nature and life, yin and yang. I must constantly keep the thought of husband and wife matched: spirit entering breath, fire entering the land of water — this is husband and wife matched, and true yang comes forth, that is, true lead is produced. The upper half of the human body is Heaven and yang, the lower half Earth and yin; without the cooking of the spirit-fire, from where would the diffuse, mingling breath arise? The yang breath born in Kan rising to the Muddy Pellet is subduing yin essence with yang breath, matching yin mercury with yang lead — also husband and wife matched to produce the medicine. The medicine is the numinous fluid born of the transformation of breath, also called Sweet Dew, the spirit water; then lead it into the elixir field and cook it warmly, Qian and Kun turning over to cover each other, and it transforms again into breath — this is the Prior Heaven one breath of Qian the Origin.

The methods of accumulating essence and breath come down to two: guarding the center (the center is emptiness) and the River Chariot (the Celestial Circuit). The method of guarding the center is used when water and fire assist each other and Kan and Li meet; it is done at the front of the body, also called “the forward course is short”, and has the work of two stages, gathering and sealing. The method of the River Chariot is used after yang is born, when Qian and Kun unite; it is done at the back of the body, also called “the backward course is long”: turning the River Chariot, lodging the spirit in the Muddy Pellet, … there are starting the Lesser Celestial Circuit, advancing and withdrawing and reversing, ablution, stopping the fire when it is sufficient, and so on.

In short, obtaining the medicine by guarding the center counts only as half of the learning; even when yang is born, it only …; if there is no yin mercury to match it, it still cannot form the elixir, so one must go on to turn the River Chariot, turning it for a long, long time, … refined ten thousand times, before the elixir can be formed.

Key Terms

  • Lead and Mercury: code names for the two poles of yin and yang; lead is the metal in Kan (breath), mercury the wood in Li (spirit, the fluid of the heart).
  • Middle line: the middle line of a trigram.
  • Nature of temperament, life of allotted span: the Posterior Heaven disposition and a lifespan with a fixed term.
  • Ji mercury: the point of true mercury in Li, that is, the fluid of the heart.
  • Fire enters the land of water, spirit enters breath: the spirit-light shining down on the elixir field.
  • Yinqiao: the perineum; see perineum.
  • Cooling lid (tianguo): in the old liquor still, the pot on top cooled with cold water so that the vapor condenses into liquor.
  • True mercury: the fluid formed by the transformation of breath after true lead rises to the Muddy Pellet.
  • The girl of the eastern house, the youth of the western house: mercury and lead; the Yellow Dame is the true intention; see Yellow Dame.
  • Wine of ghee: a metaphor for the sweet dew formed by the transformation of breath in the Muddy Pellet.
  • Taking from Kan to fill Li, drawing out lead and adding mercury: taking the true yang in Kan to replenish the true yin in Li; see conjunction of Kan and Li.
  • Guarding the center: warm nourishing at the front of the body, “the forward course is short”; see guarding the center and embracing the One.
  • River Chariot: turning at the back of the body, “the backward course is long”; see Celestial Circuit.
  • Move the spirit, not the breath: let the spirit only preside, without using force to move the breath.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Two steps: first the meeting of Kan and Li — the spirit-light shines down, fire enters the land of water, the elixir field grows warm and a moving breath arises (producing the medicine); then the union of Qian and Kun — this yang breath rises and unites with the yin essence of the Muddy Pellet, transforms into sweet dew, and descends back to the elixir field (forming the elixir).
  2. One key formula: move the spirit, not the breath. The spirit presides, the breath moves by itself; do not forcibly push the breath up to the head with the mind.
  3. Guarding the center first, the River Chariot after. Guarding the center (warm nourishing in the elixir field) is the foundation; without ample true yang, there is no question of the River Chariot.
  4. Safety note: “rising to the Muddy Pellet” is the natural upward movement of the yang breath once it is ample, not pulling the breath up to the crown with the mind. People with high blood pressure or yin deficiency and yang excess should especially not place the mind on the head; if a swollen feeling in the head, headache, a flushed face or ringing in the ears appear, stop at once and return to natural relaxation.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is in two paragraphs; the original book does not give the source. The first paragraph is divided into three short passages.
  2. “Steams the point of yin blood in the palace of Kan. The blood is the water of Kan; the water, though yang”: the trigram Kan is yin outside and yang inside, so the water of Kan is said to “belong to yang” (holding one yang within).
  3. The poem “The girl of the eastern house, the youth of the western house…” is introduced in the original only as “this is precisely what is meant by”, without a source.
