Chapter Seven · The River Chariot (the Celestial Circuit) · 河车之路

The Road of the River Chariot

The road of the River Chariot — kindle the fire at the Xu-Wei cavity, lead it to the Caudal Funnel, raise it to the Muddy Pellet, then down the Storied Tower into the Crimson Palace; take firmness in advancing the fire, softness in withdrawing by the tally; can you concentrate the breath to attain softness, like an infant?

About This Chapter

This section speaks of the route and the fire phasing of the River Chariot.

  1. The route: after true yang is greatly born and the true breath-energy greatly stirs, kindle the fire at the Xu-Wei cavity (the perineum), lead it to the Caudal Funnel, “beat the ninefold iron drum, drive the three-legged golden toad”, and raise it to the top of the head — all with a single, undivided, uninterrupted mind. Once it rises to the Muddy Pellet, the muni pearl is obtained; only by nourishing it warmly in the Muddy Pellet for a while can the yin essence there transform, so that lead and mercury melt together into sweet dew, the spirit water, moistening all the vessels of the body. Then lead it down the Storied Tower (the windpipe) into the Crimson Palace — this is withdrawing by the yin tally in the afternoon.
  2. Fire phasing: advancing the fire must be firm — without the strong force of Qian, the true metal cannot rise; withdrawing by the tally must be soft — without the virtue of yielding, the yang lead remains scattered and cannot subdue mercury to form the elixir.
  3. “Can you concentrate the breath to attain softness, like an infant?”: in the afternoon when yin is born, illuminate brightly with a single mind and let the breath’s dynamism descend, adding no intention and using no force — this is the way of the hexagram Kun, “yielding, favorable, steadfast”.
  4. Sealing: nourish it warmly in the Crimson Palace, send it back into the earthen cauldron (the lower elixir field) and seal it firmly, treating it only with tranquility and harmony; in an instant the breath seems gone and the spirit’s mechanism seems cut off. Nourish it warmly for a moment, and spirit and breath return to the root, like a banked fire in the stove. The four limbs are soft, like the body of an infant, like cotton wool, like thread — this is enough to prove that the elixir has formed.
  5. After this, the mind must be cleansed still more: as soon as the mind wanders off, the elixir runs off. “Can you cleanse the mysterious mirror until it is without flaw?” — though the outer elixir is obtained, if the inner illumination is not strict and human desire not purified, how can the pearl like a grain of millet be formed?
  6. Preserving essence and enriching breath must follow nature: “preserving without preserving is better than preserving, enriching without enriching is better than enriching” — clutching it as a treasure seldom ends without danger. The elixir is the Prior Heaven original breath, without form; seeking it by clinging to traces only stirs the Posterior Heaven fire and loses the great Prior Heaven way.

Qiu Ling’s note: this section is taken from chapter 10 of the Commentary on the Daode jing. The route of the River Chariot: kindling the fire at the perineum, leading it to the Caudal Funnel, through the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow up to the Muddy Pellet, is called advancing the fire, and should use martial fire, firm and strong; from the Muddy Pellet down the Storied Tower into the Crimson Palace for warm nourishing, sending it into the lower field, is called withdrawing by the tally, and should use civil fire, soft and yielding. The Storied Tower is the windpipe.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

真阳大生，真气大动，由是运行河车自虚危穴起火，引至尾闾，敲九重铁鼓，运三足金蟾，上升于顶。俱要一心专注，不二不息。及至升上泥丸，牟尼宝珠已得。若不于此温养片时，则泥丸阴精不化，怎得铅汞融和化成甘露神水，以润一身百脉？既温养泥丸矣，复引之下重楼入绛宫，即午退阴符也。

After true yang is greatly born and the true breath-energy greatly stirs, one turns the River Chariot: kindle the fire at the Xu-Wei cavity (the perineum), lead it to the Caudal Funnel, “beat the ninefold iron drum, drive the three-legged golden toad” (a metaphor for the true breath-energy breaking through the layered passes of the spine and rising all the way), and raise it to the top of the head. Throughout one must be single-minded and attentive, without a second thought and without interruption. When it has risen to the Muddy Pellet, the muni pearl is obtained. If one does not nourish it warmly in the Muddy Pellet for a moment, the yin essence of the Muddy Pellet cannot transform; how then could lead and mercury melt together and become Sweet Dew, the spirit water, to moisten all the vessels of the body? After warm nourishing in the Muddy Pellet, lead it down the Storied Tower (the windpipe) into the Crimson Palace — this is withdrawing by the yin tally in the afternoon.

§2
Original (Chinese)

但进火之时法取其刚，非用乾健之力，真金不能自升；退符之候法用其柔，非以柔顺之德，阳铅依然散漫不能伏汞成丹。故曰：“专气致柔，能如婴儿乎？”其意教人于阴生午后一心朗照，任其气机下降，如如自如，了了自了，却不加一意用一力，此即坤卦“柔顺利贞，君子攸行”之道也。

However, when advancing the fire, the method must take firmness — without the strong force of the hexagram Qian, the true metal cannot rise of itself; when withdrawing by the tally, the method must use softness — without the virtue of yielding, the yang lead remains scattered and cannot subdue mercury to form the elixir. So Laozi says: “Can you concentrate the breath to attain softness, like an infant?” The meaning is to teach people that in the afternoon, when yin is born, they should illuminate brightly with a single mind, letting the breath’s dynamism descend by itself, as it is, complete in itself, yet adding not the slightest intention and using not the slightest force — this is the principle of the hexagram Kun, “yielding, favorable, steadfast; the noble person has somewhere to go” (yielding and keeping to what is right, this is how the noble person acts).

§3
Original (Chinese)

至绛宫温养，送归土釜牢牢封固，惟以恬淡处之，冲和安之。一霎时间，气息如无，神机似绝，此致柔也。温养片晌，神气归根自如炉中火种，久久凝注不令纷驰，自然真气流行运转周身。一心安和，四肢苏软，不啻婴儿之体，如絮如缕，有柔弱不堪任物之状，此足徵丹凝之象。

Nourish it warmly in the Crimson Palace, then send it back to the earthen cauldron (the lower elixir field) and seal it firmly, treating it only with tranquility and settling it with harmony. In an instant the breath seems gone and the spirit’s mechanism seems cut off — this is “attaining softness”. Nourish it warmly for a moment, and spirit and breath return to the root, naturally like a banked fire in the stove; concentrate on it for a long time without letting it race off, and naturally the true breath-energy flows and circulates through the whole body. The mind is at peace, the four limbs soft, just like the body of an infant, like cotton wool, like thread, so soft and weak it seems unable to hold anything — this is enough to prove that the elixir has formed.

§4
Original (Chinese)

从此铅汞相投，水火既济，又当洗心涤虑，独修一味真铅。苟心一走作，丹即奔驰，不惟丹无由就，即前取水乡之铅亦不为我有。《清静经》云，心无其心，物无其物，空无所空，无无亦无，湛然常寂。又何瑕疵之有？故曰：“涤除玄览，能无疵乎？”倘外丹虽得，内照不严，则人欲未净，天理未纯，安得一粒黍珠虚而成象？

From then on lead and mercury join each other and water and fire are in balance; one must further cleanse the mind and wash away thoughts, cultivating this single ingredient, true lead, with a single mind. As soon as the mind runs off, the elixir runs off — not only can the elixir not be accomplished, but even the lead gathered earlier from the land of water (the lower elixir field) will no longer be mine. The Scripture of Clarity and Stillness says: “The mind has no mind, things have no things; emptiness has nothing to be emptied, even non-being is not, deep and ever still.” What flaw is there then? So Laozi says: “Can you cleanse the mysterious mirror until it is without flaw?” (Can you wash clean the bright mirror in the depths of the heart until there is not a single flaw?) If, though the outer elixir has been obtained, the inner illumination is not strict, human desire not yet purified and the principle of Heaven not yet pure, how could one obtain a pearl like a grain of millet taking form out of emptiness?

§5
Original (Chinese)

到得丹有于身，犹须保精裕气以成圣胎。虽然，其保精也要顺自然，其裕气也须随自在。此不保之保胜于保，不裕之裕胜于裕。否则，矜持宝贵，鲜不危焉！夫以丹为先天元气，无有形状，何须作为若执迹象以求？未免火动后天而先天大道亡矣！

When the elixir is already in the body, one must still preserve the essence and enrich the breath to form the holy embryo. Even so, preserving the essence should follow its nature, and enriching the breath should follow its ease — this is “preserving without preserving is better than preserving, enriching without enriching is better than enriching” (preserving without deliberate preserving is better than deliberate preserving; nourishing without deliberate nourishing is better than deliberate nourishing). Otherwise, clutching it tight as a treasure seldom ends without danger! The elixir is the Prior Heaven original breath, without form — what need is there for action? If one seeks it by clinging to traces, one can hardly avoid stirring the Posterior Heaven fire, and the great Prior Heaven way is lost!

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§5
Original note (Chinese)

此节引自黄元吉《道德经注释》第十章部分注文。老子原文有“载营魄抱一，能无离乎？专气致柔，能如婴儿乎？涤除玄鉴，能无疵乎？”玄鉴，喻心灵深处明澈如镜。

《周易》坤卦象曰：“牝马地类，行地无疆，柔顺利贞。君子攸行，先迷失道，后顺得常”。此处取母马性情柔顺、祥和、纯正之义。

重楼即气管。此节言河车运行之路及火候。自会阴穴起火，引至尾闾，经夹脊、玉枕而升上泥丸，名为进火，法取武火宜刚健；从泥丸复下重楼入绛宫温养送入下田，名为退符，法取文火宜柔顺，即沐浴之候。

运行河车属乾坤交媾，在此复述铅汞融和化成甘露神水，犹如醍醐灌顶，以润一身百脉之义。

This section is taken from part of Huang Yuanji’s commentary on chapter 10 of his Commentary on the Daode jing. Laozi’s original text includes: “Carrying the souls and embracing the One, can you keep them from separating? Concentrating the breath to attain softness, can you be like an infant? Cleansing the mysterious mirror, can you be without flaw?” The “mysterious mirror” is a metaphor for the depths of the heart, clear and bright as a mirror.

The tuan commentary on the hexagram Kun in the Zhouyi says: “The mare is of the earth’s kind, roaming the earth without bound; yielding, favorable, steadfast. The noble person has somewhere to go; first he goes astray and loses the way, later he follows and finds the constant.” Here it takes the meaning of the mare’s nature: yielding, gentle and pure.

The Storied Tower is the windpipe. This section speaks of the route and the fire phasing of the River Chariot: kindling the fire at the perineum point, leading it to the Caudal Funnel, through the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow up to the Muddy Pellet, is called advancing the fire, and its method uses martial fire, which should be firm and strong; from the Muddy Pellet down the Storied Tower into the Crimson Palace for warm nourishing and then into the lower elixir field is called withdrawing by the tally, and its method uses civil fire, which should be soft and yielding — this is also the time of ablution.

Turning the River Chariot belongs to the union of Qian and Kun, and here the meaning of lead and mercury melting together into sweet dew, the spirit water, like ghee poured on the crown, moistening all the vessels of the body, is restated.

Key Terms

  • Xu-Wei cavity: the perineum; see perineum.
  • Beat the ninefold iron drum, drive the three-legged golden toad: alchemical code, a metaphor for the true breath-energy breaking through the passes and rising.
  • Muni pearl: a metaphor for the elixir; see Golden Flower.
  • Storied Tower: the windpipe, the section of the throat.
  • Withdrawing by the yin tally at wu: in the afternoon yin is born and the fire is withdrawn downward; see fire phasing.
  • Firmness in advancing the fire, softness in withdrawing by the tally: martial fire for rising, civil fire for descending.
  • Can you concentrate the breath to attain softness, like an infant: chapter 10 of the Daode jing.
  • Yielding, favorable, steadfast; the noble person has somewhere to go: the hexagram Kun in the Zhouyi.
  • Earthen cauldron: the lower elixir field.
  • Attaining softness: the soft state in which the breath seems gone and the spirit’s mechanism cut off.
  • Can you cleanse the mysterious mirror until it is without flaw: chapter 10 of the Daode jing; the received text reads xuanlan 玄览, Qiu Ling’s note xuanjian 玄鉴.
  • Millet pearl: the elixir the size of a grain of millet; see Golden Flower.
  • Preserving without preserving is better than preserving: preserving and nourishing that follows nature.
  • Holy Embryo: what is formed by further warm nourishing after the elixir is formed.
  • Three passes: the Caudal Funnel, the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow; see Three Passes.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The route is a description, not something “for you to turn”: perineum → Caudal Funnel → Spine-Straddling Pass → Jade Pillow → Muddy Pellet → Storied Tower → Crimson Palace → lower elixir field is the natural course of the true breath-energy once it is ample; the precondition is “the openings unfolding” of the previous section.
  2. Firmness and softness: do not block it when it rises (firmness); when it descends, neither push nor press, letting it descend by itself (softness) — adding no intention, using no force.
  3. Sealing: once back in the elixir field, keep tranquil and harmonious; in a moment the breath seems gone and the four limbs are soft — this is the sign of the elixir forming.
  4. After this, cleanse the mind all the more: as soon as the mind wanders off, all previous effort is lost.
  5. Safety note: a reminder once again: before the true breath-energy is naturally full and the openings have unfolded, do not travel this route with the mind. In particular, do not linger long or exert force at the head (the Muddy Pellet); if a swollen feeling in the head, headache, a flushed face, palpitations or insomnia appear, stop at once and return to natural relaxation.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is one paragraph, taken from chapter 10 of Huang Yuanji’s Commentary on the Daode jing (see Qiu Ling’s note); it is divided into five short passages.
  2. “This is enough to prove that the elixir has formed” (此足徵丹凝之象): 徵 is the same as 征, to prove.
  3. “Beat the ninefold iron drum, drive the three-legged golden toad”: alchemical code, not explained in the original; the translation gives the general sense.
