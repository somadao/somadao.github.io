Chapter Seven · The River Chariot (the Celestial Circuit) · 以柔以默以静

With Softness, with Silence, with Stillness

With softness, with silence, with stillness — yang needs yin as its match, using hardness and movement is male matched with male; when true yang first arises, only light and gentle; the road of the River Chariot is like the workings of heaven and earth over a year; if true yang does not stir greatly, nourish quietly for a long time

About This Chapter

This section is Huang Yuanji’s supplement on the River Chariot, and the most important section of the whole chapter: the River Chariot must go with softness, with silence, with stillness.

  1. Why softness: the one yang first stirring and the medicine first born, body and mind tranquil and the elixir first congealing — none of these can do without softness, silence and stillness. Yang needs yin as its match — using hardness and movement is “male matched with male”, so how could there be any transformation? Moreover, if the mind and spirit do not return to simple wholeness and clarity, they scatter and roam outside and do not join with the breath. Laozi says: “Blunt the sharpness, untangle the knots, soften the glare, join the dust.”
  2. The measure of light and heavy: when true yang first arises, it is only fitting to gather and raise it lightly and gently — “two parts of fresh, tender water matched with two parts of fire”. When it rises to the lumbar spine and the breath-dynamic is vigorous, with a slight sense of jostling, it does no harm to make the intention a little firmer.
  3. Like a year of heaven and earth: at the winter solstice all the yin congeals and closes, and the one yang first rises — what is the yang like at that time? And at “three yang bring prosperity”, and at six yang in the great heat? After yang is at its height, one yin suddenly arises, gradually reaching autumn cool and deep winter. Advancing, withdrawing and returning to the furnace must all follow the Way of Heaven.
  4. The River Chariot has dangers: the elixir classics all say the River Chariot is very dangerous; practicing without following the Way of Heaven brings much misfortune and little good fortune.
  5. The most practical sentence: if true yang does not stir greatly, it does no harm to nourish quietly for a long time, without interruption through the twelve double-hours; naturally the breath becomes full and the medicine is born, in less than two or three months.

Qiu Ling’s note: Huang holds that the River Chariot goes with softness, silence and stillness, that even when advancing the fire one should not use too much force, treating it in the spirit of “softening the glare and joining the dust” — the Way follows nature, and one must wait quietly for the dynamic to stir before acting: when the dynamic of movement appears, one may go; when the dynamic of stillness appears, one may stop.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

吾示河车一法，其中还有未仔细处。夫天人冥合，一阳初动，药之初生有如此状。身心恬静，专气致柔，丹之初凝亦如此状。俱离不得以柔以默以静。何也？阳须阴配，若是用刚、用动，是男配男也，焉有变化？且心神不归浑璞，不归于清清朗朗，光明洞达，神即散游于外不与气交，此所以必用柔也。老子云：“挫其锐，解其纷，和其光，同其尘，”可以默会矣！

In the method of the River Chariot I have taught you, there are still places I have not explained in detail. Heaven and the human merging in the dark, the one yang first stirring — that is what it is like when the medicine is first born; body and mind tranquil, concentrating the breath to reach softness — that is also what it is like when the elixir first congeals. Neither can do without “softness, silence, stillness”. Why? Yang must have yin as its match; if you use hardness and movement, that is male matched with male — how could there be any transformation? Moreover, if the mind and spirit do not return to simple wholeness, to clear brightness and luminous openness, the spirit scatters and roams outside and does not join with the breath — this is why softness is necessary. Laozi says: “Blunt the sharpness, untangle the knots, soften the glare, join the dust.” (Wear away the sharp edges, loosen the tangles, soften the brightness, mingle with the dust of the world.) This can be quietly savored!

§2
Original (Chinese)

真阳始生之初，只宜轻轻微微采取提升。古云：二分新嫩之水以二分火配之。到得升而至于腰脊，斯时气机蓬勃，略有冲突之状，又不妨意思著紧。

When true yang has just arisen, it is only fitting to gather and raise it lightly and gently. The ancients said: “Two parts of fresh, tender water are matched with two parts of fire.” (When the newly born, tender water is only two parts, match it with only two parts of fire.) When it has risen to the lumbar spine, the breath-dynamic is then vigorous, with a slight sense of jostling, and it does no harm to firm up the intention a little.

§3
Original (Chinese)

总之，河车一路象天地一年造化，从冬至群阴凝闭，一阳初起火，试思此时之阳为何如哉？到得三阳开泰又是何状？至于六阳已到，天气大暑，又是如何？从此阳盛之时，忽生一阴，渐渐秋凉至于隆冬严寒。进退归炉俱要观天道，以执天行，庶合法度。否则，河车一法，丹经俱言大有危险。不顺天道行功，势必多凶少吉。至于真阳不见大动，不妨久久静养，十二时中无有间断，自然气满药生，不须二、三月为也。

In short, the road of the River Chariot is like the workings of heaven and earth over a year: from the winter solstice, when all the yin congeals and closes and the one yang first rises with its fire — just think, what is the yang like at that time (faint)? What is it like at “three yang bring prosperity” (the first month)? And when six yang have arrived (the fourth month) and the weather is in its great heat, what then? From this time of flourishing yang, one yin suddenly arises, gradually turning to autumn cool, until the severe cold of deep winter. Advancing the fire, withdrawing the tally and returning to the furnace must all observe the Way of Heaven and act according to the movement of heaven — only then do they accord with the rule. Otherwise, of the method of the River Chariot the elixir classics all say it is very dangerous — practicing without following the Way of Heaven is bound to bring much misfortune and little good fortune. As for when true yang has not stirred greatly, it does no harm to nourish quietly for a long time, without interruption through the twelve double-hours; naturally the breath becomes full and the medicine is born, in less than two or three months.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§3
Original note (Chinese)

河车一法，黄氏实主张以柔以默以静，虽进火之候亦不宜过于用强，以“和光同尘”之态度来对待为宜，所谓道法自然，必须静俟机动而后行，动机出现可以行，静机出现可以住，方不背乎自然，合乎法度。

As for the method of the River Chariot, Huang in fact holds to softness, silence and stillness; even when advancing the fire, one should not use too much force, and it is best treated in the spirit of “softening the glare and joining the dust”. As the saying goes, the Way follows nature: one must wait quietly for the dynamic to stir and only then act — when the dynamic of movement appears, one may go; when the dynamic of stillness appears, one may stop — only thus does one not go against nature, and accord with the rule.

Key Terms

  • With softness, with silence, with stillness: the fundamental attitude of the River Chariot.
  • Yang needs yin as its match: yang energy needs yin softness to accompany it before it can transform.
  • Male matched with male: hardness matched with hardness, with no transformation.
  • Blunt the sharpness, untangle the knots, soften the glare, join the dust: Daode jing, chapters 4 and 56.
  • Two parts of fresh, tender water matched with two parts of fire: when the medicine is tender, the fire is light.
  • Three yang bring prosperity, six yang: stages in the gradual growth of yang through the year; see fire phasing.
  • Acting according to heaven: doing it according to the movement of the Way of Heaven.
  • When the dynamic moves, go; when it is still, stop: when the breath-dynamic stirs, do the work; when it is still, stop.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The guiding principle of this chapter is here: softness, silence, stillness. The River Chariot is not something you “train out” — it comes out of stillness and waiting.
  2. Light or heavy according to the medicine: when the medicine is tender, the fire is light; when the breath-dynamic jostles, make the intention slightly firmer; the rest of the time, simply look after it lightly.
  3. If true yang has not stirred greatly, nourish quietly: do not run the River Chariot; simply stay still and nourish constantly through the twelve double-hours — this is the safest way.
  4. Safety note: Huang Yuanji himself says “the elixir classics all say it is very dangerous” and “without following the Way of Heaven, much misfortune and little good fortune”. Our site’s advice: make quiet nourishment the main thing, and do not chase the circulation of the Celestial Circuit. If any discomfort appears (a swollen head, headache, flushed face, palpitations, insomnia, persistent heat or tightness somewhere in the body), stop at once and return to natural relaxation, and consult a doctor if necessary.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is one paragraph; the original book does not give the source. It is divided into three short passages.
  2. “If the mind and spirit do not return to the uncarved jade” (心神不归浑璞): pu 璞 is jade not yet carved, a metaphor for simple, original wholeness.
  3. “It does no harm to firm up the intention” (又不妨意思著紧): zhuojin 著紧 means to tighten, to make firmer.
  4. “Not needing two or three months” (不须二、三月为也): so in the original book; it means in less than two or three months.
