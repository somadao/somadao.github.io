Chapter Seven · The River Chariot (the Celestial Circuit) · 起火之法

The Method of Kindling the Fire

The method of kindling the fire — the medicine is the breath of the true One, the fire is the spirit-breath of the elixir field; Wu Chongxu’s four-word formula, inhale, lick, contract, close, half nature and half human effort; setting up the furnace and tripod, body and mind upright and unshaken

About This Chapter

This section speaks of “kindling the fire” in the River Chariot, and introduces Wu Chongxu’s four-word formula, “inhale, lick, contract, close”.

  1. Medicine and fire: at this stage both the true fire and the true medicine are present. The true medicine is the Prior Heaven breath of the true One, which in Posterior Heaven is the original essence and original breath. Signs: the ordinary breath stops and embryonic breathing stirs; true saliva fills the mouth — the sign of the original essence being produced; the whole body leaps and surges — the sign of the original breath stirring; pure, still and natural, delightful beyond compare.
  2. Why the fire must be kindled: though the breath of the true One has stirred, without knowing how to kindle the fire it cannot rise to the Muddy Pellet, transform into jade fluid and nectar, and be swallowed into the belly to form the elixir — when the medicine is born but the fire is not advanced, it can only “stir the lower origin and warm and strengthen the kidney breath”. The medicine is the breath of the true One, the fire is the spirit-breath of the elixir field; only by moving the true breath-energy with the spirit-breath can it penetrate the whole body.
  3. The four-word formula (Wu Chongxu):
    • Inhale: during the work, gather the breath and concentrate the spirit in the elixir field, storing it carefully and tightly, not letting a thread leak out.
    • Lick: the tongue against the palate, “the red dragon stirring the sea”, so that true saliva arises and transforms into sweet dew, the spirit water, to subdue the fire in Li.
    • Contract: upper and lower teeth together, upper and lower lips closed, thoughts within not going out and thoughts without not coming in, spirit and breath relying on each other, merged into one piece.
    • Close: close the grain road below and the mouth and nose above, the six gates shut tight, preserving the spirit within — “let the true master sit in the Yellow Court”.
  4. The most important sentence: the work of these four is half nature and half human effort. When the medicine first arises, use a little effort; in time it is wholly natural, without borrowing the slightest human effort.
  5. Setting up the furnace and tripod: the outer furnace and tripod is the whole body standing upright from top to bottom; the inner furnace and tripod is first the spirit as tripod and the breath as furnace, then the breath as tripod and the spirit as furnace — in short, body and mind upright and unshaken. Then heart-fire descends and kidney-water rises, and in time true yin and true yang are refined out of ordinary yin and yang — this is the birth of the medicine, and it should then be gathered.

Qiu Ling’s note: the seven kinds of yin dregs are mucus, spittle, saliva, essence, breath, blood and fluid. Although inhale, lick, contract and close are half human effort, they are still a workable method, and so have been used through the ages; but only when, after long practice, it is wholly natural without the slightest human effort is real attainment seen — that is Huang’s true intent.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

生等行功至此，真火真药两般俱有。夫真药即先天真一之气，其在后天即元精元气。所谓真阴真阳形而为真一之气也。是即凡息停而胎息动，真津满口，即验元精之产也。周身踊跃，即见元气之动也。此时清净自然，美快无比，即真一之气藏于个中矣！

When your work has reached this point, both the true fire and the true medicine are present. The true medicine is the Prior Heaven breath of the true One; in Posterior Heaven it is the original essence and original breath — what is called true yin and true yang transforming and taking form is the breath of the true One. That is: the ordinary breath stops and embryonic breathing stirs; true saliva filling the mouth is the sign of the original essence being produced; the whole body leaping and surging is the sign of the original breath stirring. At this time all is pure, still and natural, delightful beyond compare, and the breath of the true One is hidden within it!

§2
Original (Chinese)

然真一之气虽动，不明起火之法尚不能升于泥丸，化为玉液琼浆，吞入于腹而结为长生之丹。夫以药生不进火，止于冲举下元壮暖肾气而已。药即真一之气，火即丹田神息，以神息运真气，方能透彻一身上下中外。古云：“抽铅添汞。”又曰：“还精补脑。”又曰：“以虎嫁龙。”要之此功自下而上由逆而修。始而玄关初开，必须猛火急烹，既而药苗新生，不自倒行逆施则金丹不就。

However, though the breath of the true One has stirred, without knowing the method of kindling the fire it still cannot rise to the Muddy Pellet, transform into jade fluid and nectar, and be swallowed into the belly to form the elixir of long life. When the medicine is born but the fire is not advanced, it can only stir the lower origin and warm and strengthen the kidney breath. The medicine is the breath of the true One; the fire is the spirit-breath in the elixir field (spirit and breath united as one); only by moving the true breath-energy with the spirit-breath can it penetrate the whole body above and below, inside and out. The ancients said “draw out lead and add mercury”, and also “return the essence to replenish the brain”, and also “marry the tiger to the dragon” — in short, this work goes from below upward, cultivated in reverse. At the start, when the Mysterious Pass first opens, one must cook it with a fierce fire; then, when the shoots of the medicine are newly born, without reversing it (turning it upward), the Golden Elixir cannot be formed.

§3
Original (Chinese)

伍冲虚氏示河车功法所以有吸、舐、撮、闭之说也。吸者，行功时聚气凝神于丹田，蕴蓄谨密不许一丝外漏。舐者，舌抵上腭使赤龙绞海，而真津始生，化为甘露神水，以伏离中之火，即古云：“铅龙升，汞虎降，驱二物，勿纵放。”是又即以铅制汞结成砂是。若非舌之上舐，安得七般阴渣之物化为神水，而成一粒黍珠哉！撮者，牙齿上下紧紧相粘，口唇上下紧紧相抱，务使内想不出，外想不入，神依于息，息依于神，神气打成一片，两两不分也。闭者，下闭谷道，上闭口鼻，六门紧闭存神，即教真主坐黄庭。俗云丹田有宝是矣！古云：“上不闭则火不凝而丹不结，下不闭则火不聚而金不生。”是以金丹之要，凝神要矣，而聚气添火尤不可少焉。

So when Wu Chongxu transmitted the method of the River Chariot, he spoke of “inhale, lick, contract, close”.

  • Inhale: during the work, gather the breath and concentrate the spirit in the elixir field, storing it carefully and tightly, not letting a thread leak out.
  • Lick: the tongue against the palate, making “the red dragon stir the sea” (the tongue moving in the mouth), so that true saliva arises and transforms into Sweet Dew, the spirit water, to subdue the fire in Li — this is what the ancients called “the lead dragon rises, the mercury tiger descends; drive the two things, do not let them loose”, that is, subduing mercury with lead and forming cinnabar. If the tongue did not lick upward, how could the seven kinds of yin dregs be transformed into spirit water and become a pearl like a grain of millet!
  • Contract: the upper and lower teeth lightly together, the upper and lower lips firmly closed, so that thoughts within do not go out and thoughts without do not come in, the spirit relying on the breath and the breath relying on the spirit, spirit and breath merged into one piece, inseparable.
  • Close: close the grain road (the anus) below and the mouth and nose above, the six gates shut tight, preserving the spirit within — this is “let the true master sit in the Yellow Court”. The common saying “there is a treasure in the elixir field” refers to this!

The ancients said: “If above is not closed, the fire does not concentrate and the elixir does not form; if below is not closed, the fire does not gather and the metal is not born.” So in the essentials of the Golden Elixir, concentrating the spirit is what matters most, and gathering the breath and adding fire cannot be lacking either.

§4
Original (Chinese)

总之，四者之功，一半天然一半人力。学者于药生之初微微用一点力，久久而纯乎天而不假一毫人力为之矣！再者，下手之初必要安炉立鼎，方可采取运用。夫炉鼎有几般？一身上下亭亭直立，即安炉立鼎，天尊地卑，上下分明矣！此外炉鼎也。若内炉鼎，始以神为内鼎，以气为外炉，继以气为内鼎，以神为外炉，总是身心挺立不摇而已。炉鼎安立，然后心火下降，肾水上升，久之则离火中有真水下降，肾水中有真火上升，从凡阴凡阳中炼出真阴真阳之物来，即是药生，便当采取。

In short, these four kinds of work are half nature and half human effort. When the medicine first arises, the student uses a little effort; in time it becomes wholly natural, without borrowing the slightest human effort! Moreover, at the very start one must set up the furnace and tripod before one can gather and use. How many kinds of furnace and tripod are there? The whole body standing upright from top to bottom is setting up the furnace and tripod — Heaven is high and Earth low, above and below clearly distinct! This is the outer furnace and tripod. As for the inner furnace and tripod: first the spirit is the inner tripod and the breath the outer furnace, then the breath is the inner tripod and the spirit the outer furnace — in short, it is simply body and mind upright and unshaken. Once the furnace and tripod are set up, heart-fire descends and kidney-water rises; in time, true water descends within the fire of Li and true fire rises within the kidney-water, and true yin and true yang are refined out of ordinary yin and yang — this is the birth of the medicine, and it should then be gathered.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

伍冲虚河车功法，见其著作《天仙正理》、《仙佛合宗》二书。

七般阴渣之物为涕、唾、津、精、气、血、液。

运转河车进火之法用吸、舐、撮、闭，不过为了促进聚气凝神、真津化为神水、神气打成一片以及增添火力，虽半属天然半属人力，亦不失为可行之法，故历代气功家沿用之。黄氏于此作详细介绍，并附以己意解释之，使其义越明，足见黄氏亦赞同此法。而行之既久，达到纯乎自然而不用一丝人力，才见功力，方是黄氏本意。

Wu Chongxu’s method of the River Chariot is found in his two works, the Correct Principles of Celestial Immortality and The Common Origin of Immortals and Buddhas.

The seven kinds of yin dregs are: mucus, spittle, saliva, essence, breath, blood and fluid.

Using “inhale, lick, contract, close” when advancing the fire in turning the River Chariot is only to help gather the breath and concentrate the spirit, transform true saliva into spirit water, merge spirit and breath into one piece, and add to the strength of the fire; although it is half nature and half human effort, it is still a workable method, and so qigong masters through the ages have used it. Huang introduces it here in detail and adds his own explanation, making the meaning clearer, which shows that Huang also approves of this method. But only when, after long practice, it reaches the point of being wholly natural without the slightest human effort is real attainment seen — that is Huang’s true intent.

Key Terms

  • True medicine: the Prior Heaven breath of the true One, which in Posterior Heaven is the original essence and original breath; see Medicine.
  • True saliva filling the mouth: the sign of the original essence being produced.
  • The whole body leaping and surging: the sign of the original breath stirring.
  • Stirring the lower origin and warming and strengthening the kidney breath: with only the medicine and no fire advanced, it can only warm the lower burner.
  • Spirit-breath: spirit and breath united as one, that is, the fire of the elixir field.
  • Drawing out lead and adding mercury, returning the essence to replenish the brain, marrying the tiger to the dragon: all expressions for reverse cultivation.
  • Four-word formula: inhale, lick, contract, close — Wu Chongxu’s method of the River Chariot.
  • The red dragon stirring the sea: the tongue moving in the mouth to produce saliva.
  • The lead dragon rises, the mercury tiger descends: lead (breath) rises, mercury (fluid) descends.
  • The seven kinds of yin dregs: mucus, spittle, saliva, essence, breath, blood and fluid.
  • Grain road: the anus.
  • The six gates: the openings such as eyes, ears, nose and mouth.
  • The true master sitting in the Yellow Court: the spirit dwelling in the lower elixir field.
  • Setting up the furnace and tripod: outwardly the body upright, inwardly spirit and breath serving each other as furnace and tripod.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The weight of the four-word formula: half human effort, half nature; use “a little effort” only when the medicine first arises, and let it go completely once practiced.
  2. The safest and most commonly used is “lick”: the tip of the tongue lightly against the palate; saliva arises in the mouth, and when the mouth is full, swallow it slowly in several parts, sending it to the elixir field with the intention.
  3. “Contract” and “close” must both be light: lips lightly together and teeth lightly together are enough — do not clench; “closing the grain road” is only a light drawing in, not forcefully contracting the anus or holding the breath. Excessive force easily causes tightness in the chest, a swollen head, abdominal bloating, constipation and worsening hemorrhoids.
  4. Setting up the furnace and tripod begins with posture: the upper body naturally upright and unshaken — this is the outer furnace and tripod.
  5. Safety note: the four-word formula is to be used when the true breath-energy has already stirred, not as actions to perform with force as soon as you sit down; people with high blood pressure, heart disease, glaucoma or who are pregnant should not do breath-holding, anal contraction, forceful gathering of breath and similar actions.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text of this section is one paragraph; the original book does not give the source. It is divided into four short passages.
  2. “Contract means the upper and lower teeth stuck tightly together” (撮者，牙齿上下紧紧相粘): so in the original book. In the commonly transmitted “inhale, lick, contract, close”, “contract” usually refers to lightly drawing up the grain road; Huang Yuanji here explains it as the teeth and lips coming together, and treats the grain road under “close”; we translate according to the original book.
  3. “The lead dragon rises, the mercury tiger descends; drive the two things, do not let them loose”: from Cui Xifan’s Mirror for Compounding the Medicine.
