Chapter Seven · The River Chariot (the Celestial Circuit) · 运转河车之时

When to Turn the River Chariot

When to turn the River Chariot — when the true breath-energy surges up to the heart and the seven openings and the pores all open, this is “the openings unfolding”; before this scene, never turn the River Chariot hastily

About This Chapter

Chapter Seven speaks of the “River Chariot”, that is, the Celestial Circuit: the movement of the true breath-energy rising along the Governing vessel at the back and descending along the Conception vessel at the front. The first section begins by answering the most crucial question — when may the River Chariot be turned?

  1. Why some people practice the River Chariot for years without result: at the very beginning they did not obtain the truly pure medicine, and it was mixed with desire and delusion — like tiles fired in a kiln with dregs mixed in, which can never be fired into good tiles however long they burn.
  2. The right procedure: first set a period of time, extinguish the spirit of knowledge and discrimination, dissolve thoughts and worries, the body sitting like a mountain, the mind still as water. Once body and mind are at ease and the breath calm, close the eyes slightly, concentrate the spirit and regulate the breath, letting it be natural — breathing out without letting it be coarse, breathing in without letting it be agitated — until over time there is no going out or coming in, and it dwells in the central palace; this is the ordinary breath stopping; as soon as the ordinary breath stops, embryonic breathing appears of itself.
  3. “The openings unfolding”: nourishing it slowly like this, the true breath-energy surges up to the heart (the Crimson Palace), the seven openings open wide and every pore of the body opens — this is called “the openings unfolding”. Only at this stage may one start from the Xu-Wei cavity, go back to the Caudal Funnel and rise straight to the Muddy Pellet.
  4. Never turn it hastily: if the breath’s dynamism only stirs faintly, or surges only to the heart, without the seven openings opening wide and the pores all opening, it is not the true unfolding of the openings; never turn the River Chariot hastily — running fire without water will surely scorch the whole body.
  5. The scene of true yang appearing in full: the five senses and hundred bones are diffuse and surging, as if walking in clouds and mist or sitting in clear emptiness; above, between the eyebrows and eyes, it is bright like specks of starlight; below, in the elixir field, it is vast like a spreading tide (both are metaphors, not to be sought in traces); like the full moon on the fifteenth filling the great thousand worlds — only this is the great medicine first being born, when the River Chariot can be turned.
  6. Turning it before this scene, there is no true seed in the tripod; running water and fire rashly, one instead chases yang with yin breath, and day by day sees yang waning and yin growing.

Qiu Ling’s note: students must read this section carefully and test and verify it for themselves — Huang has let out a secret not transmitted in the world. The best time to turn the River Chariot is “the openings unfolding”: the true breath-energy surges up to the Crimson Palace, the seven openings and pores all open, the whole body breathes, wave upon wave of freshness, and even the Muddy Pellet feels unobstructed. Without this condition, one must not run the River Chariot rashly; even if a little true breath-energy surges upward without the openings unfolding, one must not turn the River Chariot. Otherwise it is what the Awakening to Reality says: “If there is no true seed in the tripod, it is like boiling an empty pot with water and fire.”

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

某生行功多年，河车运转已非朝夕，何以不见其成者？良由下手之初不得真清药物，是以夹杂欲妄，一任日积月累，不啻窑头之瓦夹有渣滓在内，终劳而无成也。今为生示，日夜行功须要先定一时，灭却知识之神，泯乎思虑之念，身坐如山，心静如水。如此澄净一番，果然身心安泰，气息平和，于是双目微闭，凝其心神，调其气息，任其自自然然，一往一来，一开一阖，呼而出不令之粗，吸而入不使之躁，久久自无出无入，安然自在，住于中宫，此真凡息停也。凡息一停，胎息自见。

A certain student has worked for many years and has been turning the River Chariot for more than a day or two; why has he seen no result? It is because at the very beginning he did not obtain the truly pure medicine, so it is mixed with desire and delusion; however much he accumulates day by day and month by month, it is like tiles fired in a kiln with dregs mixed in — in the end, labor without result. Now I tell you: in working day and night, you must first set a period of time, extinguish the spirit of knowledge and discrimination, dissolve thoughts and worries, the body sitting like a mountain, the mind still as water. After clarifying in this way, when body and mind are truly at ease and the breath calm, then close the eyes slightly, gather the mind-spirit, harmonize the breath, letting it be quite natural, going and coming, opening and closing — breathing out without letting it be coarse, breathing in without letting it be agitated; over time, naturally there is no going out or coming in, at ease and natural, dwelling in the central palace (the lower elixir field) — only this is the true stopping of the ordinary breath. As soon as the ordinary breath stops, embryonic breathing naturally appears.

§2
Original (Chinese)

如此慢慢涵养，自然真气冲冲上达心府，此“展窍”也。盖以真气有力直上冲乎绛宫，庶几一身毛窍亦有自开之时。所谓一窍相通，窍窍光明，是又谓一根既返本，六根成解脱。学者行功到此，始可自虚危穴起往后而达尾闾，直上泥丸之宫。若但气机微动，或仅冲心府，不见七窍大开，又不见一身毛眼皆开，此非真“展窍”时，切不可骤运河车。况无水行火，必烧灼一身。

Nourishing it slowly like this, naturally the true breath-energy surges up to the heart — this is called “the openings unfolding”. Because the true breath-energy is strong and rushes straight up to the Crimson Palace (between heart and chest), the pores of the whole body also have their time of opening of themselves. This is what is called “one opening penetrated, every opening bright”, also “once one faculty returns to the root, the six faculties are liberated”. Only when the student’s work reaches this step may he start from the Xu-Wei cavity (the perineum), go back to the Caudal Funnel and rise straight to the palace of the Muddy Pellet. If the breath’s dynamism only stirs faintly, or surges only to the heart, without the seven openings opening wide or the pores of the whole body all opening, this is not the true time of “the openings unfolding”, and one must never turn the River Chariot rashly. Moreover, running fire without water will surely scorch the whole body.

§3
Original (Chinese)

务要有此境况，方得内真外应，外感内灵，吾身之气与太虚元气合为一体，所谓真药者此也，又谓人盗天地之气以成丹者此也。诸子果有真药发生流通一身内外，则多年凝滞阴气自化为汗，从毛眼而出，一切浊垢之污消融净尽。

Only with such a condition can the inner be true and the outer respond, the outer stir and the inner be numinous, the breath of my body becoming one with the original breath of the Great Void — what is called “the true medicine” is this, and what is called “a person steals the breath of Heaven and Earth to form the elixir” is also this. If you truly have the true medicine arising and flowing through the whole body inside and out, then the yin breath stagnant for many years will naturally turn into sweat and leave through the pores, and all turbidity and filth will melt away completely.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§3
Original note (Chinese)

此节学者须仔细阅读，自考自证，黄氏于此泄出世间不传之秘。开始运转河车（即周天）的最好时机是真气上冲绛宫而有“展窍”现象，即我身七窍及毛眼皆开，感到浑身通气，有一阵阵清爽之感，甚至泥丸亦感通畅。如无此境况，不得妄行河车；即使略有真气上冲而无“展窍”现象发生，亦不得运转河车。否则，即是《悟真篇》所云：“鼎内若无真种子，犹将水火煮空铛。”

此节还指出，所谓真清药物，是我身之真气与太虚元气合为一体。一旦“展窍”，可感到天地清空之气不断入我体内与我身之气相结合。

Students must read this section carefully and test and verify it for themselves; Huang has let out here a secret not transmitted in the world. The best time to begin turning the River Chariot (the Celestial Circuit) is when the true breath-energy surges up to the Crimson Palace and the phenomenon of “the openings unfolding” appears: the seven openings and the pores of my body all open, I feel the whole body breathing, wave upon wave of freshness, and even the Muddy Pellet feels unobstructed. Without this condition, one must not run the River Chariot rashly; even if a little true breath-energy surges upward without the phenomenon of “the openings unfolding”, one must not turn the River Chariot. Otherwise it is what the Awakening to Reality says: “If there is no true seed in the tripod, it is like boiling an empty pot with water and fire.”

This section also points out that the so-called “truly pure medicine” is the true breath-energy of my body becoming one with the original breath of the Great Void. Once “the openings unfold”, one can feel the clear, empty breath of Heaven and Earth continually entering my body and joining with the breath of my body.

§4
Original (Chinese)

真阳一动大有气机可凭，漫说天地人物不知谁何，就是我五官百骸到此细缊蓬勃，真气运行于一身内外，恍如云雾中行，清虚中坐，所谓忘忘是。然忘忘又不能尽其状也。不知此气此神从何而有？从何而生？但觉天地之大、日月之明，皆不足拟其分量。我自有一重天地、两轮日月，不与凡人同。此天地日月也，是杳杳冥冥真景，亦即自家玄窍生气，特地现出其状。生等打坐，若得这个窍开，又见这个妙相，即是真阳大现，可以运行河车。未到此景犹恐鼎无真种，妄行水火反将阴气追逐阳气，而日见阳消阴长，到得后来全是一派阴邪之私用事。

Once true yang stirs, there is great dynamism of breath to rely on: not to speak of not knowing who Heaven, Earth, people and things are — even my five senses and hundred bones are now diffuse and surging, the true breath-energy moving through the whole body inside and out, as if walking in clouds and mist or sitting quietly in clear emptiness — what is called “forgetting and forgetting”; yet even “forgetting and forgetting” cannot fully describe it. One does not know where this breath and this spirit come from or are born from, only feeling that the vastness of Heaven and Earth and the brightness of sun and moon are not enough to compare with its weight. I have a Heaven and Earth of my own, a sun and moon of my own, unlike ordinary people. This Heaven and Earth, sun and moon, are the true scene in the deep and obscure, the vital breath in one’s own mysterious opening specially showing its form. When you sit, if you obtain this opening and see this wondrous form, it is true yang appearing in full, and the River Chariot can be run. Before this scene appears, one fears that there is no true seed in the tripod; running water and fire rashly is instead chasing yang with yin breath, seeing yang waning and yin growing day by day, until in the end it is entirely the selfishness of yin and evil in charge.

§5
Original (Chinese)

若要搬运升降，往来无穷，必待药气充盈，勃然滃然，上至眉目之间朗朗然如星光点点，即其气机开朗无比，非谓果有星光点点纷飞而可见也。下而丹田中浩浩然如潮水漫漫，其真气流动充盈有如此，非果有潮水泛流也。此是比喻之法，一切不可着迹以求。有此景到，始如十五一团明月遍满大千，普照恒河，即是大药初生，可以兴功采取，搬运河车，升之降之，进之退之。

If one wants to move it up and down, coming and going without end, one must wait until the breath of the medicine is full, surging and billowing: above, between the eyebrows and eyes, it is bright like specks of starlight — this means that its dynamism is open and clear beyond compare, not that specks of starlight are really flying about to be seen; below, in the elixir field, it is vast like a spreading tide — this means that the true breath-energy flows and fills to this degree, not that a tide is really overflowing. These are metaphors, and none of it may be sought by clinging to traces. Only when this scene arrives is it like the full moon on the fifteenth, filling the great thousand worlds and shining over the Ganges — this is the great medicine first being born, when one can begin the work of gathering, turn the River Chariot, raise it and lower it, advance it and withdraw it.

Qiu Ling's noteon §4–§5
Original note (Chinese)

读此两段真阳大现景象，则运行河车之时机更明。“展窍”之时，亦当有上述真阳大现景象，可二者合看。

Having read these two passages on the scene of true yang appearing in full, the timing of running the River Chariot is even clearer. When “the openings unfold”, there should also be the scene of true yang appearing in full described above; the two can be read together.

Key Terms

  • River Chariot: the movement of the true breath-energy rising along the Governing vessel and descending along the Conception vessel, that is, the Celestial Circuit; see Celestial Circuit.
  • Truly pure medicine: the pure medicine of the true breath-energy united with the original breath of the Great Void; see Medicine.
  • Tiles of the kiln: tiles fired in a kiln, which cannot be fired properly if dregs are mixed in.
  • Central palace: in this book, the lower elixir field.
  • The openings unfolding: the true breath-energy surging up to the heart, the seven openings and pores all opening — the time to turn the River Chariot.
  • Heart, Crimson Palace: between heart and chest; see Crimson Palace.
  • One opening penetrated, every opening bright: when one place opens, every place opens.
  • Once one faculty returns to the root, the six faculties are liberated: after the meaning of the Śūraṅgama Sūtra.
  • Xu-Wei cavity: the perineum; see perineum.
  • Running fire without water: running the fire when the true breath-energy (water, the medicine) is insufficient, which is sure to harm the body.
  • Forgetting and forgetting: the state of forgetting and forgetting again.
  • The great medicine first being born: true yang appearing in full, when the River Chariot can be turned.
  • If there is no true seed in the tripod, it is like boiling an empty pot with water and fire: words of the Awakening to Reality.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The River Chariot comes from “waiting”, not from “turning”. First sit until the ordinary breath stops and embryonic breathing appears, then nourish until the true breath-energy surges up to the heart by itself and all the pores of the body open — only that is the time.
  2. The criterion (Qiu Ling): without “the openings unfolding”, even if a little breath surges upward, do not turn the River Chariot.
  3. The scenes are all metaphors: specks of starlight, a spreading tide — do not seek or imagine them by clinging to traces.
  4. Safety note (important): Huang Yuanji says plainly, “running fire without water will surely scorch the whole body”. Beginners should not follow a diagram and use the mind to make the breath go round along the spine — this is exactly the forcing of the River Chariot “without obtaining the true medicine at the beginning” that this section criticizes. Common deviations include a swollen feeling in the head, headache, a red face and ears, palpitations, insomnia, and heat or tightness in the back or at the crown. If these appear, stop all moving of the breath at once, return to natural breathing and relaxed attention, and consult a doctor if necessary.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original text comes in two places (each followed by a note by Qiu Ling in the original book); the original book does not give the source. The first is one paragraph, divided into three short passages; the second is two paragraphs.
  2. “Breathing out without letting it be coarse, breathing in without letting it be agitated” (呼而出不令之粗，吸而入不使之躁): so in the original book.
  3. “Surging and billowing” (boran wengran 勃然滃然): 滃 is read wěng, describing clouds welling up.
  4. “Once one faculty returns to the root, the six faculties are liberated”: based on the Śūraṅgama Sūtra, “once one faculty returns to the source, the six faculties are liberated”.
