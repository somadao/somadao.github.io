Appendix 3 · Song Formulas of the Sixteen-Step Health Method

Appendix 3 · Song formulas of the sixteen-step health method

About This Chapter

These are the song formulas into which the Old Man of Qianfeng put the sixteen-step method of the dual cultivation of nature and life, so that his disciples could learn and recite it easily; the last two lines sign it themselves: “If you ask who made this song — the Old Man of Qianfeng composed it with his own mouth.”

Before the song there is a short remark by Niu Jinbao, a disciple of Qianfeng, explaining where the song came from and why it is being made public.

Compared with the “short formula of the sixteen steps” in Appendix 1, the song is somewhat more detailed, and can be read as a verse summary of the whole book.

Safety note (translator): The song condenses the entire content of the sixteen steps; from Step Ten onward (guarding against night leakage, taking and lifting the fire, stopping the fire and gathering the medicine, the wooden seat and plugging the nose, leaving the womb and facing the wall, etc.) please read for understanding only; the safety notes in the main text of each step apply here as well.

Original Text and Translation

Niu Jinbao's Remark
Original (Chinese)

牛金宝按：余师千峰老人为了使入门弟子便于学习和背诵不忘，将十六步性命双修养生功法编成歌诀。今将此歌公诸于世，供养生爱好者习诵。

Niu Jinbao remarks: my teacher, the Old Man of Qianfeng, so that disciples who had just entered the school could learn and recite it easily without forgetting, put the sixteen-step health method of the dual cultivation of nature and life into song formulas. I now make this song public, for friends who love nurturing life to study and recite.

Steps One to Four
Original (Chinese)

初步　初打坐、心要定，脚分阴阳气下降。

垂帘冥心守祖窍，舌闭天池玄膺开。

掐子午、杂念排，祖炁修足玄关开。

下座拂面舒筋气，静久而动子时来。

二步　子时到、采小药，提根转轮收小药。

火龙难降两头吹，一提一降龙炁回。

收龙炁、是火候，火候不足莫伤丹；

单等二候真火足，开通八脉再采药。

三步　八脉开、气血舒，以意领气下功夫。

一吸督脉升泥丸，二呼任脉降海根；

三吸带脉至肩窝，四呼阳维到手心；

五吸阴维胸前定，六呼至带归一根；

七吸冲脉至绛宫，八呼阳跻到涌泉；

九吸阴跻升炁穴，十呼归根入窍中；

八脉开通身属阳，二候一到采大药。

四步　阴跻开、身属阳，二候一到采大药；

下手点住生死窍，急用巽风六候收。

橐籥动时阖辟转，升住起止沐浴功；

收真宝、再封固，封固呼吸用巽风。

此是道教顺逆诀，释教涅槃三更传；

儒教回仁传子思，三教一诀不用争。

真口诀、皆单传，回仁养我诀浩然；

涅槃断淫不生灭，顺逆颠倒精炁还。

三教源流是一理，传授千峰归一根。

Step One: When you first sit, the heart must be settled; the two feet divide yin and yang (sitting cross-legged), and the breath descends. Lower the eyelids, quiet the heart and guard the Ancestral Opening; the tongue closes the Heavenly Pool (the tongue against the palate), and the Mysterious Breast opens. The two hands pinch the zi-wu formula (the thumb pressing a finger joint, see Step One), clearing away stray thoughts; when the ancestral breath is cultivated to the full, the Mysterious Pass opens. When getting off the seat, stroke the face with the hands and stretch the sinews and breath; after long stillness movement arises naturally, and the zi hour (the Living Zi Hour) comes.

Step Two: When the zi hour comes, gather the lesser medicine: lift the root (a lift of the perineum), turn the Dharma Wheel, and draw the lesser medicine back. When the yang member (the fire dragon) is hard to subdue, use “blowing at both ends” (the flute without holes); one lift, one descent, and the dragon breath returns. Drawing back the dragon breath depends on fire phasing; if the fire phasing is insufficient, do not harm the elixir. Only wait until the true fire of the two periods is sufficient and the eight vessels are opened, then gather the medicine.

Step Three: When the eight vessels are open, qi and blood flow freely; work by leading the breath with intention: first inhalation, the Governing Vessel rises to the Muddy Pellet; second exhalation, the Conception Vessel descends to the root of the sea (the perineum); third inhalation, the Belt Vessel to the hollows of the shoulders; fourth exhalation, the Yang Linking Vessel to the palms; fifth inhalation, the Yin Linking Vessel settles in front of the chest; sixth exhalation, to the Belt Vessel, returning to the one root (the Opening of Life and Death); seventh inhalation, the Penetrating Vessel to the Crimson Palace; eighth exhalation, the Yang Heel Vessel to the Bubbling Spring; ninth inhalation, the Yin Heel Vessel rises to the Cavity of Breath; tenth exhalation, returning to the root, entering the opening. When the eight vessels are opened, the whole body belongs to yang; as soon as the two periods arrive, gather the Great Medicine.

Step Four: When the Yin Heel Vessel is open, the body belongs to yang, and as soon as the two periods arrive, gather the medicine; set to work by pressing the Opening of Life and Death, and quickly draw it back with the Wind of Xun and the six periods. When the bellows moves, closing and opening turn; rising, staying, starting and stopping are the work of ablution. After drawing back the true treasure, seal it; the breathing of the sealing uses the Wind of Xun. This is the Daoist formula of “going with and against the current,” the “nirvāṇa” that Buddhism transmits at the third watch, the “humaneness” transmitted by Yan Hui and Zisi of the Confucian teaching; the three teachings are all one oral formula, and there is no need to argue. True oral formulas are all transmitted singly; “Hui’s humaneness” nourishes my flood-like breath; “nirvāṇa” is cutting off lust, neither born nor perishing; “going with and against, turning upside down” is essence and breath returning to the origin. The source and flow of the three teachings are one principle, transmitted down to Qianfeng and returning to one root.

Steps Five to Eleven
Original (Chinese)

五步　金木合，回光照，文武二火提出窍；

三成武火渣滓出，七成文火回光照。

回光照，接命根，心存生处能接炁；

身内如云炁朝上，六根无漏津液生。

六步　进阳火，退阴符，闭目四九转神轮；

开关四六二十四，乾九坤六度数真，

此是卯酉周天数，差之毫厘炁不生。

七步　龙虎炁、翕聚光，三花聚顶见月亮，

离己坎戊玄关动，子卯午酉予收藏，

收回我有成津液，送归土釜炼纯阳，

牵得龙虎回家养，炁归元海寿无疆。

八步　牢封固　是蛰藏，回光返照呼吸存；

对斗明星炁自动，上不过心下至肾。

久动而静入窍中，大定得之蛰藏功；

水火既济戊己门，知止定静凝神入。

九步　凝神入、蒂踵引，消息同动相兼连；

真炁上升后天降，真炁下降后天升。

四个呼吸不用鼻，若用口鼻道不真，

会得蒂踵呼吸法，不羡尘世有黄金。

十步　真炁动、防夜漏，闭住龙虎二穴门，

舌接督脉鼓巽风，左右盘旋转神轮，

四九提缩闭精炁，固精关门养元神，

子午封固要口诀，舍利将成莫思淫。

十一步　虚室白，蟾光现，取火提火一九转，

内有暗光丹不足，照前用功莫怠慢，

谨戒十损勿伤炁，六欲七情要斩断，

神炁满足无食睡，舍利养足六景现。

Step Five: Metal and wood join (the two eyes joined), turning the light around to shine back; with the two fires, civil and martial, draw (the root of illness) out of the opening: three parts martial fire, and the dregs are expelled; seven parts civil fire, turning the light around to shine back. Turning the light around to shine back can join the root of life; when the heart rests on “the place of birth” (the elixir field), it can join the breath; within the body it is like clouds, the breath rising up; the six roots do not leak, and saliva is produced by itself.

Step Six: Advance the yang fire, withdraw the yin tally: eyes closed, four times nine circles, turning the wheel of the spirit; eyes open (opening the gate), four times six circles, twenty-four in all; Qian uses nine and Kun six, and the degrees must be true — these are the numbers of the mao-you circuit; off by a hair, and the breath is not produced.

Step Seven: The breath of dragon and tiger, the light gathered in; the three flowers gather at the crown, and you see the moon (the light of the moonbeams); the ji earth within Li and the wu earth within Kan join, and the Mysterious Pass stirs; turn the eyes by zi, mao, wu and you to store it away; drawn back, it is mine, transformed into saliva, sent back to the earthen pot (the lower elixir field) and refined into pure yang; lead the dragon and tiger home to be nourished, the breath returns to the original sea, and life is without bound.

Step Eight: Sealing firmly is hibernation; turning the light around to shine back, the breathing is kept within. Facing the Dipper with the bright star (looking back at the elixir field), the breath stirs by itself; above it does not pass the heart, below it stops at the kidneys. Long movement turns to stillness and enters the opening; obtaining the great absorption is the work of hibernation. The mutual completion of water and fire is at the gate of wu and ji (the central palace); knowing stopping, settledness and stillness, condense the spirit and enter.

Step Nine: Condense the spirit and enter (the Cavity of Breath), led by the stem and the heel; waning and waxing move together, joined and linked: when the true breath rises, the Posterior Heaven breath descends; when the true breath descends, the Posterior Heaven breath rises. The four breaths do not use the nose; if mouth and nose are used, the Way is not true. Having learned the method of stem-and-heel breathing, you will not envy the gold of the dusty world.

Step Ten: As soon as the true breath stirs, guard against night leakage: close the gates of the two cavities of dragon and tiger (the middle fingers of both hands pressing the palms), the tongue joins the Governing Vessel, stir up the Wind of Xun, circling left and right, turning the wheel of the spirit; four times nine circles, lifting, contracting, closing in the essence and breath; securing the essence and shutting the gate, nurture the Original Spirit. Sealing at zi and wu requires an oral formula; when the śarīra is about to form, do not think of lust.

Step Eleven: The empty room produces whiteness, the toad light appears; take the fire, lift the fire, turn one round of nine circles; if there is dim light within, the elixir is insufficient — work as before and do not slacken. Carefully guard against the ten harms and do not injure the breath; the six desires and seven emotions must be cut off. When spirit and breath are full, there is no desire for food or sleep; when the śarīra is nourished to the full, the six scenes appear.

Steps Twelve to Sixteen
Original (Chinese)

十二步　养舍利，淫根断，六景齐来蟾光现；

三花五炁归祖窍，金机飞电在眼前。

龙又吟，虎又啸，仙乐齐鸣在耳窍；

自知舍利通身足，景到止火采大药。

十三步　性命合，金光现，法财侣地备足全，

二现止火不用风，三现采药六根震。

下有木座五龙捧，羊鹿牛车吸托升。

舍利动时而后引，吸舐撮闭侣搓行。

过三关，升泥丸，塞住鼻窍木来年，

阳火阴符数要足，脱落玄膺送丹田。

十四步　三千法，炁合胎，对斗明星炼胎形；

千升绛宫调养补，识死性活出胎神。

十五步　天花坠，出胎时，念动而向太空出；

庙门一开二光合，急速收回七再出。

十六步　阳神足，面壁功，炼虚化炁空不空，

阳神退藏祖窍中，阳神百炼而百灵，

大道已成功完毕，性命双修臻上乘。

性命功，溯根源，丘祖龙门教外传，

了空恩师亲口授，传于千峰把道衍，

要问此歌何人作，千峰老人亲口编。

Step Twelve: Nourish the śarīra, the root of lust is cut off (no longer rising), the six scenes all arrive, the toad light appears; the three flowers and five breaths return to the Ancestral Opening, and “the golden mechanism, flying lightning” is right before the eyes. The dragon chants again, the tiger roars again, immortal music sounds together in the openings of the ears; you know yourself that the śarīra already fills the whole body, and as soon as the scene arrives, stop the fire and gather the Great Medicine.

Step Thirteen: Inner nature and life join, the golden light appears, and Dharma, means, companions and place are all prepared; at the golden light’s second appearance stop the fire and do not use the Wind of Xun; at the third appearance gather the medicine, and the six roots tremble. Below there is a wooden seat, five dragons bearing up the holy one; the goat cart, the deer cart and the ox cart, inhaling to lift and raise it. Only after the śarīra has moved, go on to guide it; inhale, lick, contract, close, with the companion of the Way beside you rubbing and pinching in coordination. Pass the three passes, rise to the Muddy Pellet, plug the openings of the nose with the mulainian; the numbers of the yang fire and yin tally must be full, and (the true seed) drops down through the Mysterious Breast and is sent to the elixir field.

Step Fourteen: Use the method of the three moves; the breath unites and forms the embryo; facing the Dipper with the bright star, refine it into the form of the embryo; move it up to the Crimson Palace, adjust, nourish and replenish it; the cognitive spirit dies and the true nature comes alive, and the spirit leaves the womb.

Step Fifteen: Heavenly flowers raining down in profusion is the time to leave the womb: as soon as a thought stirs, it goes out toward the great void; when the temple gate opens, the two lights join; draw it back quickly, and go out again after seven days.

Step Sixteen: When the yang spirit is full, do the work of facing the wall; refine emptiness and transform the breath, empty yet not empty; the yang spirit withdraws and hides in the Ancestral Opening, and the yang spirit, refined a hundred times, is a hundred times numinous. The great Way is complete, the work is finished, nature and life cultivated together, reaching the highest vehicle. The work of nature and life, traced back to its source, is a separate transmission outside the teaching of Patriarch Qiu’s (Qiu Chuji’s) Dragon Gate school; my gracious teacher Liaokong transmitted it with his own mouth, passed it to Qianfeng, to hand the Way on. If you ask who made this song — the Old Man of Qianfeng composed it with his own mouth.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Recite it as an outline: a few lines for each step, matching the main text exactly — very convenient for review.
  2. The first five steps are the foundation: guarding the Ancestral Opening, gathering the heart, opening the eight vessels, turning the light around to shine back, warming and nourishing with civil fire — this is the part everyone can benefit from.
  3. “The three teachings are one formula, no need to argue”: Zhao’s position of fusing Confucianism, Buddhism and Daoism is stated most plainly in the song.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original writes 阴跻 and 阳跻; 跻 should be 跷 (see the Translator’s Notes to Step Three); reproduced as is, and the translation reads “Heel Vessel.”
  2. “Pinching zi-wu”: refers to the two hands pinching the zi-wu formula; see Step One for details.
  3. “Rising, staying, starting, stopping — the work of ablution” (升住起止沐浴功): Step Four has “moving, staying, starting, stopping” (行住起止); so in the original, reproduced as is.
  4. “Store it away by zi, mao, wu, you” (子卯午酉予收藏): 予 is probably a mistake for 于 or 子; reproduced as is.
  5. “Step Eight: sealing firmly — is hibernation” (八步　牢封固　是蛰藏): in the original there is a space between “sealing firmly” and “is hibernation”; reproduced as is.
  6. “千 up to the Crimson Palace, adjust, nourish, replenish” (千升绛宫调养补): 千 (“thousand”) should be a mistake for 迁 (“move”); reproduced as is; “the method of the three thousands” (三千法) should be “the method of the three moves” (三迁法); reproduced as is.
  7. “Refine emptiness and transform the breath, empty yet not empty” (炼虚化炁空不空): so in the original; reproduced as is.
  8. “The companion rubbing” (侣搓行): refers to the companion of the Way rubbing and pinching; the original writes 搓; reproduced as is.
