Appendix 1 · The Old Man of Qianfeng Answers His Disciples

Appendix 1 · The Old Man of Qianfeng answers his disciples’ questions

About This Chapter

Appendix 1 contains Zhao Bichen’s answers to five questions from his disciples. For each question, he first lists the sayings of patriarchs through the ages, then concludes with “The Old Man of Qianfeng answered”:

  1. What is “the Way is before your eyes”? — After the dual cultivation of nature and life, with the three treasures united into one, the Prior Heaven true breath arises before the eyes; it is also called the Mysterious Pass.
  2. Cultivating the Way is “most simple and most easy”; is “beheading the red dragon” a real practice or a matter of principle? — It is the formula of “the man sets to work seizing the white tiger, the woman beheads the red dragon,” transmitted in person from patriarch to patriarch, most simple and most easy for one who meets a true teacher; rubbing the belly, rubbing the breasts and sucking in air are not the true method.
  3. Why do the elixir classics say “laughable” when they reach the true formula? — What is laughed at is “regulating the outer medicine.”
  4. Is the dual cultivation of nature and life two people cultivating together, or the union of heart and kidneys? — Neither; it is simply the spirit and breath within one person’s body.
  5. Is there a simple formula for the sixteen steps? — Zhao condenses each of the sixteen steps into two lines, easy to memorize.

This last part, “the short formula of the sixteen steps,” is the best review outline of the whole book.

Original Text and Translation

Question 1 · The Way Is Before Your Eyes
Original (Chinese)

问曰：弟子看丹经道书，每言道在眼前人不知，眼前有道是什么？

马丹阳祖师曰：玄微妙诀无多言，只在眼前人不顾。

又曰：道在眼前甚容易，得服之人妙难比。

陈泥丸曰：眼前有路不知处，造空伏死徒冥冥。

又曰：大道分明在眼前；终日相随在目前。

张三丰祖曰：今日方知道在眼前。

薛道光祖师曰：思量只是眼睛前，自是时人不见。

刘海蟾祖师曰：眼前觑着不识真。

萧紫虚曰：金液还丹在眼前，迷者多而悟者少。

上阳子曰：此窍分明在目前。

吕祖曰：目前咫尺长生路，多少愚人不细悟。

吕祖又曰：真阴真阳是真道，只在眼前何远讨。

千峰老人答曰：各位祖师言的是，性命双修，三品合一，先天真炁发生目前，又名玄关。修道修的是此炁，参禅打坐炼的也是此炁，成道也是此炁，故此炁无的可说，强名曰道。下手采药，性命双修，炼出此炁为玄关，又为真性。故尔非是枯坐盲修瞎炼，单炼阴性，是自己一人下手，性命双修，炼出阳性之炁光在眼前。

Question: When your disciple reads the elixir classics and Daoist books, they often say “the Way is before your eyes, yet people do not know it.” What is the Way before the eyes?

(The following are the words of patriarchs through the ages.) Patriarch Ma Danyang said: “The wondrous formula of the mysterious and subtle has few words; it is right before the eyes, but people pay no heed.” He also said: “The Way is before the eyes, very easy; for those who obtain it and ingest it, the wonder is beyond compare.” Chen Niwan (Chen Nan) said: “There is a road before your eyes, yet you do not know where it is; fabricating emptiness and lying still until death, all in vain, in darkness.” He also said: “The great Way is clearly before your eyes; all day long it follows you, before your eyes.” Patriarch Zhang Sanfeng said: “Only today do I know the Way is right before the eyes.” Patriarch Xue Daoguang said: “Thinking it over and over, it is just in front of the eyes, but people of the world cannot see it.” Patriarch Liu Haichan said: “Looking before the eyes, yet not recognizing the true.” Xiao Zixu said: “The golden liquor reverted elixir is right before the eyes; those who are deluded are many, those who awaken are few.” Shangyangzi (Chen Zhixu) said: “This opening is clearly right before the eyes.” Patriarch Lü said: “The road of long life is but a foot before your eyes; how many fools fail to realize it carefully.” Patriarch Lü also said: “True yin and true yang are the true Way; it is right before your eyes — why seek it far away?”

The Old Man of Qianfeng answered: what the patriarchs are saying is this: with the dual cultivation of nature and life, the three treasures of essence, breath and spirit united into one, the Prior Heaven true breath arises before the eyes, and it is also called the “Mysterious Pass.” What one cultivates in cultivating the Way is this breath; what one refines in Chan meditation and sitting is also this breath; what attains the Way is also this breath; so of this breath there is nothing that can be said, and it is reluctantly named “the Way.” Setting to work gathering the medicine, cultivating nature and life together, refining this breath — that is the Mysterious Pass, and it is also true inner nature. So you must not sit dry, cultivating blindly and refining at random, refining only the yin nature; rather, set to work yourself, alone, cultivating nature and life together, and refine the light of the breath of the yang nature before your eyes.

Question 2 · Most Simple and Most Easy
Original (Chinese)

问曰：丹经每言修道，最简最易，无多文字，如何简易？斩赤龙是实事？是讲理？

《六祖坛经》曰：五祖传六祖三更时，片时暗点，无多文字，最简易。

《黄庭经》曰：至道不烦诀存真。又曰：治生之道了不烦。

《参同契》曰：事省而不烦。

钟离祖曰：此道分明事不多，奈缘福薄执迷何。

萧紫虚曰：从来至道无多事，自是愚人识不全。

白紫清曰：只缘简易妙天机，散在丹经不肯泄。

石杏林曰：简易之语，不过半句；证验之效，只在片时。

张紫阳曰：知者惟简易，昧者愈烦愈难。又曰：虽愚昧小人，行之立跻圣地。

薛紫贤曰：其道至简，其事匪遥，但非丰功伟行，不能遭遇真师。

千峰老人答曰：此是乾生下手，擒白虎之法诀。坤生斩赤龙之诀也。由古至今，祖祖亲传，不作文字，若遇真师，最简最易。若是以吸空气为下手采药，揉小肚子，揉两乳，吸空气，为斩赤龙，妄引丹经，猜度之见，误人诀已。

Question: The elixir classics often say that cultivating the Way is most simple and most easy, without many words; in what way is it simple and easy? Is “beheading the red dragon” a real practice, or is it a matter of principle?

The Platform Sutra of the Sixth Patriarch says: the Fifth Patriarch transmitted the Dharma to the Sixth Patriarch at the third watch of the night, pointing it out secretly in a moment, without many words — most simple and easy. The Scripture of the Yellow Court says: “The utmost Way is not troublesome; the formula lies in preserving the true.” It also says: “The way of nourishing life is not the least bit troublesome.” The Cantong qi says: “The matter is simple and not troublesome.” Patriarch Zhongli said: “This Way is clear and the matter is small, but alas, those of thin blessings cling to their delusion and do not awaken.” Xiao Zixu said: “From the beginning the utmost Way has involved few matters; it is only that fools do not fully recognize it.” Bai Ziqing (Bai Yuchan) said: “Only because it is simple and easy is it the wondrous mechanism of heaven, scattered through the elixir classics and not willingly revealed.” Shi Xinglin said: “The words of simplicity and ease are no more than half a sentence; the proof of its effect lies in a moment.” Zhang Ziyang said: “Those who understand find it simple and easy; those who do not understand find it ever more troublesome and ever harder.” He also said: “Even an ignorant small person, doing it, can at once ascend to the holy ground.” Xue Zixian (Xue Daoguang) said: “This Way is extremely simple, and this matter is not far away; but without great merit and great deeds, one cannot meet a true teacher.”

The Old Man of Qianfeng answered: this is the formula by which a man (a Qian-born) sets to work “seizing the white tiger,” and the formula by which a woman (a Kun-born) “beheads the red dragon.” From ancient times to the present the patriarchs have all transmitted it in person by word of mouth, not putting it into writing; if one meets a true teacher, it is most simple and most easy. If one takes sucking in air as setting to work gathering the medicine, takes rubbing the lower belly, rubbing the two breasts and sucking in air as “beheading the red dragon,” quoting the elixir classics at random on the strength of guesswork — this only misleads people (the original has 误人诀已, see the Translator’s Notes).

Question 3 · It Sounds Ugly When Said
Original (Chinese)

问曰：弟子看丹经道书，每到真口诀地方，言可笑，有何可笑？

《悟真篇》曰：功夫容易药非遥，说破世人须失笑。

薛道光曰：神仙不肯分明说，说与分明笑杀人。

吕祖曰：性命根，生死窍，说着丑，行着妙，人人憎，个个笑。

《葫芦歌》曰：行着妙，说着丑，惹得遇人笑破口。

上阳子曰：偶获一人两人知之，即来千人万人之谤。

石杏林曰：此道易生毁谤。

老子曰：下士闻道大笑之。

千峰老人答曰：所笑者，调外药也。药不调不生，不知调药之功，无药可采。调药者，正在二候正子时，不老不嫩，调的药产神知，可采药也。谓此调法可笑，不笑不是真道。

余胞兄赵魁一曰：调外药，真可笑，尔不笑，不知道。

了然、了空禅师曰：到二候正子时，可调药采取先天真炁。

敲蹻道师《道源精微歌》曰：调药一着，乃上天之秘宝，勿敢妄泄矣。

张懋德号润亭师叔曰：调外药，即勒阳关，所以精炁生也。

柳华阳师祖云：勒阳关，调外药，调到药产神知。

冲虚子师祖云：调动其机，精生炁动，采取之谓也。

李虚庵曰：忙里偷闲调外药。

Question: When your disciple reads the elixir classics and Daoist books, whenever they reach the true formula, they say “laughable.” What is there to laugh at?

Awakening to Reality says: “The work is easy, and the medicine is not far; once it is told plainly, the people of the world are sure to burst out laughing.” Xue Daoguang said: “The immortals will not say it plainly; said plainly, it would make people die laughing.” Patriarch Lü said: “The root of inner nature and life is in the Opening of Life and Death; it sounds ugly when said, it is wondrous when done; everyone loathes it, everyone laughs.” The Song of the Gourd says: “Wondrous when done, ugly when said; it makes those who meet it laugh till their mouths split.” Shangyangzi said: “If by chance one or two people come to know it, it draws the slander of thousands and tens of thousands.” Shi Xinglin said: “This Way easily draws slander.” Laozi said: “When the inferior person hears of the Way, he laughs loudly at it.”

The Old Man of Qianfeng answered: what is laughed at is “regulating the outer medicine” (see Step Four). If the medicine is not regulated, it is not produced; without knowing the work of regulating the medicine, there is no medicine to gather. Regulating the medicine takes place precisely at “the true zi hour of the second period,” neither old nor tender; when regulation reaches “when the medicine is produced, the spirit knows,” one can gather the medicine. They say this method of regulation is laughable — if it were not laughed at, it would not be the true Way.

My elder brother Zhao Kuiyi said: “Regulating the outer medicine is truly laughable; if you do not laugh, you do not know it.” The Chan masters Liaoran and Liaokong said: “When the true zi hour of the second period arrives, one can regulate the medicine and gather the Prior Heaven true breath.” The Song of the Subtle Essentials of the Source of the Way by the Daoist teacher Qiaoqiao (Liu Mingrui) says: “This move of regulating the medicine is heaven’s secret treasure, not to be revealed rashly.” My teacher’s fellow disciple Zhang Maode (styled Runting) said: “Regulating the outer medicine is reining in the yang gate, and from it essence and breath are produced.” Master Liu Huayang said: “Rein in the yang gate, regulate the outer medicine, regulate until, when the medicine is produced, the spirit knows.” Master Chongxuzi (Wu Chongxu) said: “Set its mechanism in motion, the essence is produced and the breath stirs — this is called gathering.” Li Xu’an said: “Snatch leisure in the midst of busyness to regulate the outer medicine.”

Question 4 · The Dual Cultivation of Nature and Life
Original (Chinese)

问曰：丹经、世人皆言性命双修，请问老师，是否两人同修，或是心肾合一处为双修？

千峰老人答曰：非是两人同修，心肾合一双修也非法诀。这性命双修，是一人身上神炁而已。神与炁交，能生舍利子，是初关之法诀；中关炁与炁交，能生先天真一之炁，能结胎；上关神与神交，能出阳神。此为性命双修真功，非是闭目枯坐瞎修，无所成也。

Question: The elixir classics and people of the world all speak of “the dual cultivation of nature and life.” May I ask, teacher, is it two people cultivating together, or is the union of heart and kidneys in one place called dual cultivation?

The Old Man of Qianfeng answered: it is not two people cultivating together, and calling the union of heart and kidneys dual cultivation is not the true formula either. This dual cultivation of nature and life is simply the spirit and breath within one person’s body: when spirit and breath meet, they can produce the śarīra — this is the formula of the first pass; at the middle pass breath meets breath, which can produce the breath of the Prior Heaven true One and form the embryo; at the upper pass spirit meets spirit, which can send out the Yang Spirit. Only this is the true practice of the dual cultivation of nature and life, not sitting dry with eyes closed cultivating blindly — that brings no achievement.

Question 5 · The Short Formula of the Sixteen Steps
Original (Chinese)

问曰：前十六步大功，细而又细，可有简易口诀传出？

千峰老人答曰：初步，垂簾冥心守祖窍，手脚和合扣连环；二步，气安炉鼎前后转，无孔双吹收元精；三步，开通八脉炁血走，手脚麻木气通行；四步，下手采药六候转，巽风橐籥沐浴中；五步，日月合并渣滓出，文火温养要七成；六步，进阳三六由左转，退符二四性光生；七步，翕聚祖炁收光法，送归土釜神炁凝；八步，心肾相交真炁动，坎离交媾丹田温；九步，四个吸呼法轮转，若用口鼻道不真；十步，龙虎二穴收精炁，舌接任督转法轮；十一步，灵丹入鼎吞入腹，虚室生白百脉停；十二步，龙吟虎啸温养丹，片时黄芽白雪生；十三步，止火采药过大关，五龙捧圣吸撮闭；十四步，提到中宫养圣胎，真空炼形慧光生；十五步，胎足念动朝元法，二光合一中显形；十六步，身外有身不为奇，虚空粉碎归这个。此十六步口诀记在心内，用功时而不忘也。

Question: The great work of the sixteen steps above is detailed upon detailed; is there a simple formula that can be passed on?

The Old Man of Qianfeng answered:

  • Step One: Lower the curtains, still the heart, guard the Ancestral Opening (lower the eyelids, quiet the heart, guard the Ancestral Opening); hands and feet united, clasped in linked rings (the two hands clasped, the two feet crossed, forming linked rings).
  • Step Two: The breath settles in furnace and tripod, turning behind and before (the breath settles in furnace and tripod and turns along the back and front of the body); blowing the holeless flute at both ends draws in the original essence.
  • Step Three: Open the eight vessels, qi and blood flow; numb hands and feet open as the breath goes through (the eight vessels open, qi and blood flow, and the numbness of hands and feet opens with the breath).
  • Step Four: Setting to work gathering the medicine, turn by the six periods, with the Wind of Xun and the bellows, in ablution (in gathering the medicine, turn by the six periods, using the Wind of Xun and the bellows, with ablution at mao and you).
  • Step Five: Sun and moon joined, the dregs come out (the two eyes joined, gazing until tears flow, the dregs expelled); warming and nourishing with civil fire must take seventy percent.
  • Step Six: Advance yang thirty-six, turning from the left (advance the yang fire thirty-six circles, turning from the left); withdraw the tally twenty-four, and the light of inner nature is born (withdraw the yin tally twenty-four circles, and the light of inner nature is born).
  • Step Seven: Gather in the ancestral breath by the method of drawing in the light; send it back to the earthen pot, spirit and breath condense (with the light-drawing method of gathering in the ancestral breath, send the light back to the lower elixir field, and spirit and breath condense).
  • Step Eight: Heart and kidneys meet, the true breath stirs; Kan and Li unite, the elixir field is warm.
  • Step Nine: With the four breathings the Dharma Wheel turns; if one uses mouth and nose, the Way is not true.
  • Step Ten: The two points of dragon and tiger draw in the essence and breath (pressing the dragon and tiger points in the palms, draw in the essence and breath); the tongue joins Conception and Governing, turning the Dharma Wheel.
  • Step Eleven: The numinous elixir enters the tripod, swallowed into the belly; the empty room produces whiteness, the hundred vessels stop.
  • Step Twelve: The dragon chants and the tiger roars, warming and nourishing the elixir; in a moment the Yellow Sprouts and white snow are born.
  • Step Thirteen: Stop the fire, gather the medicine, pass the great pass; five dragons bear up the holy one — inhale, contract, close (inhale, lick, contract, close).
  • Step Fourteen: Lift it to the central palace to nourish the holy embryo; refining form in true emptiness, the light of wisdom is born.
  • Step Fifteen: The embryo full, a thought stirs — the method of returning to the origin; the two lights unite into one, and form appears in their midst.
  • Step Sixteen: A body outside the body is nothing remarkable; empty space shattered, all returns to this (returns to this one “〇”).

Keep these sixteen steps of formula in your heart, and when practicing you will not forget them.

Key Terms

  • The Way is before your eyes: a common saying of the patriarchs through the ages; Zhao explains it as the Prior Heaven true breath (the Mysterious Pass) that arises before the eyes after the dual cultivation of nature and life.
  • Seizing the white tiger, beheading the red dragon: the formulas for setting to work of a man (Qian-born) and a woman (Kun-born) respectively; beheading the red dragon refers to a woman refining away her menstruation (see Appendix 4).
  • Regulating the outer medicine, reining in the yang gate: see Step Four; Zhao says that what the elixir classics call “laughable” refers to this move.
  • First pass, middle pass, upper pass: spirit and breath meeting produce the śarīra, breath and breath meeting form the embryo, spirit and spirit meeting send out the Yang Spirit.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The Way is before your eyes: the “before your eyes” that the patriarchs repeat can be understood as no need to seek far away — it is right in present awareness.
  2. Easy, not simplistic: “the utmost Way is not troublesome,” but “without great merit and great deeds, one cannot meet a true teacher” — simplicity and ease presuppose the right method and long practice.
  3. The dual cultivation of nature and life is one person’s affair: Zhao explicitly rejects the notion of “two people cultivating together,” which is very important for recognizing deviant teachings.
  4. The short formula of the sixteen steps: it can be memorized as an outline of the whole book, but the content from Step Thirteen on is for understanding only.

Translator's Notes

  1. In the original, Appendix 1 consists of five questions and answers, each first listing the sayings of patriarchs and then answering; we divide them into five sections by question.
  2. “误人诀已”: so in the original; the gist is “this only misleads people”; reproduced as is.
  3. “Lower the 簾”: 簾 is the same as 帘 (“curtain”); reproduced as is.
  4. Xiao Zixu, Xue Zixian (that is, Xue Daoguang), Shi Xinglin (Shi Tai), Bai Ziqing (Bai Yuchan), Zhang Ziyang (Zhang Boduan) and Chen Niwan (Chen Nan) belong to the line of the Five Patriarchs of the Southern School; Zhang Maode (styled Runting) was a fellow disciple of Zhao’s teacher, and his life is unknown.
  5. Sayings of the patriarchs such as “for those who obtain it and ingest it, the wonder is beyond compare” are mostly given in the original without sources; reproduced as is.
