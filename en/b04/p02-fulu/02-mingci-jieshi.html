Appendix 2 · Explanation of Terms

Appendix 2 · Explanation of terms — a small dictionary of elixir terms compiled by Zhao Bichen himself

About This Chapter

This is the small dictionary of terms that Zhao Bichen compiled himself for Clear Directions on the Methods and Formulas of Nature and Life, about a hundred and forty entries in all, mostly four-character phrases, each with an explanation of a sentence or two. The original arranges them roughly by the number of strokes of the first character (one, two, ten, seven, eight …); this site follows the original order and presents them in six groups.

These explanations have two features: first, they are very colloquial, often going straight to the point with “is simply” or “that is”; second, they carry the Qianfeng school’s own usages — for example “outer medicine,” “inner medicine,” “śarīra,” “toad light,” “stopping the fire” and so on do not mean quite the same as in the elixir classics generally; while reading the sixteen steps above, you can come back here at any time to look them up.

The translation gives each entry a brief explanation, noting where needed which step of the main text explains it in detail. Each entry gives the Chinese term in parentheses.

Original Text and Translation

Entries (1)
Original (Chinese)

一心学道　学道人心坚实，广看丹经道书，遇明师知真。

一心禅定　入山面壁，撒手老比丘之法。

一阳之力　周天时候，上升的事情。

二候之时　阳生、药产为二候。活子时、炁动为二候。

十字街中　两眼中心，内有一管，为祖窍，发生先天真炁。

七返还丹　七是金的成数，使铅汞返入炁穴，还升泥丸。照着这样做可以成丹，所以有此名称。

八脉之路　以生死窍为枢轴，生死窍后是督脉，前是任脉，中是冲脉，横是带脉，上通心，下通阳关，上前通脐，上后通肾，此是通精八脉。

二十四退阴符　用六爻策数，周天酉时沐浴度数。

二禅之工　初禅觉观，二禅炁绝。

人道　即是生人之道，和仙佛之道相反。

人火之法　使神火入于炁穴内。

三十六进阳火　由子至巳为进阳，督脉上升之路，周天卯时沐浴度数。

三事调和　前三三，后三三，合之炼。

下手法诀　下手采药，在生死窍，使精逆回补脑之诀法。

下降之路　由午至亥为退阴符，任脉下降之路，周天酉时沐浴度数。

子炁发生　精炁发生时候，为子炁。

子后午前　是进阳火、退阴符，任督二脉之路。

子箱之路　送精炁走路，为子箱。用鼻气输送精炁，同路而不同体，采药的内里消息。

  • With a single heart, learn the Way (一心学道): the heart of one who learns the Way must be firm; read widely in the elixir classics and Daoist books, and only on meeting an enlightened teacher can one know what is true.
  • Single-hearted dhyāna absorption (一心禅定): going into the mountains to face the wall — the method of the old bhikṣus at the final stage of “letting go” (see Step Sixteen).
  • The force of the one yang (一阳之力): the force that rises upward when moving the circuit.
  • The time of the two periods (二候之时): “yang being born” and “the medicine being produced” are the two periods; one may also say “the Living Zi Hour” and “the breath stirring” are the two periods (see Step Eight).
  • In the middle of the crossroads (十字街中): the center between the two eyes; within it is a duct, the Ancestral Opening, the place where the Prior Heaven true breath arises (see Step Two).
  • The reverted elixir of seven returns (七返还丹): “seven” is the completing number of metal (in the River Chart metal’s generating number is four and its completing number nine; so in the original, see the Translator’s Notes); making lead and mercury return to the Cavity of Breath and then rise to the Muddy Pellet — doing this one can form the elixir, hence “the reverted elixir of seven returns.”
  • The road of the eight vessels (八脉之路): with the Opening of Life and Death as the pivot: behind is the Governing Vessel, in front the Conception Vessel, in the middle the Penetrating Vessel, across the Belt Vessel; above it reaches the heart, below the yang gate, forward and up the navel, back and up the kidneys — these are “the eight vessels through which essence flows” (see Step Three).
  • Twenty-four, withdrawing the yin tally (二十四退阴符): reckoned by the number of stalks of the six lines of the Kun hexagram (four times six, twenty-four), the degrees of the circuit with the ablution at the you hour (see Step Four).
  • The work of the second dhyāna (二禅之工): the first dhyāna is “awareness and contemplation” (there is still awareness and observation); the second dhyāna is “the breath cut off” (breathing stops).
  • The human way (人道): that is, the way of producing people (going with the current to give birth), the opposite of the way of immortals and buddhas (going against the current to form the elixir).
  • The method of “human fire” (人火之法): making the fire of the spirit enter the Cavity of Breath (“human fire” 人火 should be “entering fire” 入火; see Step Five).
  • Thirty-six, advancing the yang fire (三十六进阳火): from zi to si is advancing yang, the rising road of the Governing Vessel, the degrees of the circuit with the ablution at the mao hour.
  • Harmonizing the three matters (三事调和): “three threes in front, three threes behind” (three positions rising in front, three descending behind), refined together (see Step Four).
  • The formula for setting to work (下手法诀): the formula for setting to work gathering the medicine at the Opening of Life and Death, making the essence turn back to replenish the brain.
  • The road of descent (下降之路): from wu to hai is withdrawing the yin tally, the descending road of the Conception Vessel, the degrees of the circuit with the ablution at the you hour.
  • The zi breath arising (子炁发生): when essence and breath arise, it is called the “zi breath” (the breath of the zi hour).
  • After zi, before wu (子后午前): refers to advancing the yang fire and withdrawing the yin tally, that is, the road of the Conception and Governing vessels.
  • The road of the zi box (子箱之路): the road along which essence and breath are conveyed is called the “zi box” (the bellows issuing from the zi position); conveying essence and breath with the breath of the nose, on the same road as the essence and breath but not the same substance — the waxing and waning in the body when gathering the medicine.
Entries (2)
Original (Chinese)

小周天法　后升前降转轮之法，将精炁收归炉内。

小药发生　精炁不足为小药，淫根动时为发生。

大周天法　筑基之工终了之后，采大药，无时无候无间断。

大力白牛　精炁发生活动之力，佛比作大力白牛。

凡铅凡汞　元炁为真铅，元神为真汞，铅汞在内为真，外见为凡。

火候功法　火是火，候是候，是两样功法。

火逼金行　用火催促，用心息运炁，后升前降。

月华荣光　精炁足满，发现炁光放目前，为玄关。

太阳太阴　心内元炁为太阳，肾中元炁为太阴。

元关炁海　即是丹田，正中有炁穴，精炁由此而生。

元精元炁　发动外见为元精，能生育；采回逆升为元炁，能作丹。

幻丹幻药　有念采的药，为幻丹幻药，有夜内走泄之患。

五通之鬼　炼得天眼、天耳、他心、宿命、神境，为五通之鬼。

止火之景　炼丹成的时候，由目至丹田有白光。

止火之功　采药时，不行吸呼之气，为止火功法。

止观均等　是禅定面壁之事，一切全空，撒手之法也。

心中元神　是心中本体。精神系精粹，为元神。

心中神火　心者性也，发于二目，入火、降火、凝火、以火、移火、离火、心火，皆是心目转动。不动之火，能化元精，而助元炁。

水火交媾　二目之神火，与心意入于炁穴内，久之，水上火下，真炁自生。

水中火发　即是精炁，火是运火、取火、提火、坎火、坤火、水中火、炉中火，皆是先天真炁，能化吸呼而助元神。

丹田内丹　炁穴内能作丹，丹是精化成的。

中正中宫　万事合于中正。中宫者，心下一寸二分，即绛宫。女子生血黄之地。生炁之总根，即肝的根。

天罡天机　北辰，譬喻神在炁穴内主宰天机，即药产之时。

天地氤氲　是神炁融合的事情。神为天，炁为地，万物由此而生。

天然交合　神与炁交，自然而然，全身皆空，为自然交媾。

  • The method of the Lesser Celestial Circuit (小周天法): the method of rising behind and descending in front, turning the Dharma Wheel, drawing the essence and breath back into the furnace (see Step Four).
  • The lesser medicine arising (小药发生): when essence and breath are insufficient, it is called the “lesser medicine”; when the root of desire stirs, that is “arising.”
  • The method of the Greater Celestial Circuit (大周天法): after the work of laying the foundation is complete, gathering the Great Medicine, turning at no fixed time or period, without interruption (see Steps Nine and Thirteen).
  • The great white ox of great strength (大力白牛): the force of essence and breath arising and moving, which the Buddhists liken to a “great white ox of great strength.”
  • Ordinary lead and ordinary mercury (凡铅凡汞): the original breath is true lead, the Original Spirit true mercury; lead and mercury within are true, and when they show outwardly they are ordinary.
  • The method of fire phasing (火候功法): “fire” is fire, “phasing” is phasing — two methods (see Step Five).
  • The fire pressing the metal onward (火逼金行): urging with fire, moving the breath with heart and breath, rising behind and descending in front.
  • The glorious light of moonbeams (月华荣光): essence and breath full, the light of the breath appears before the eyes — that is the Mysterious Pass (see Steps Six and Seven).
  • Great yang, great yin (太阳太阴): the original breath within the heart is great yang, the original breath within the kidneys great yin.
  • The 元关, the sea of breath (元关炁海): that is, the elixir field; at its exact center is the Cavity of Breath, from which essence and breath are produced (元关 is 玄关, the Mysterious Pass).
  • Original essence, original breath (元精元炁): what stirs and shows outwardly is the original essence, which can produce offspring; what is gathered back and rises in reverse is the original breath, which can refine the elixir.
  • Illusory elixir, illusory medicine (幻丹幻药): medicine gathered with thought is illusory elixir and illusory medicine, with the danger of leaking at night (see Step Five).
  • The ghost of five powers (五通之鬼): one who has refined only the five powers of the heavenly eye, heavenly ear, knowing others’ minds, knowing past lives and spiritual realms (without the power of the exhaustion of leaks) is called “a ghost of five powers.”
  • The scene of stopping the fire (止火之景): when the elixir is refined, there is white light all the way from the eyes to the elixir field (see Steps Twelve and Thirteen).
  • The work of stopping the fire (止火之功): not moving the breath of breathing when gathering the medicine is the work of stopping the fire.
  • Calming and contemplation in balance (止观均等): the matter of dhyāna absorption facing the wall, everything wholly empty; the method of letting go.
  • The Original Spirit in the heart (心中元神): the original substance in the heart; the quintessence within essence and spirit is the Original Spirit.
  • The spirit-fire in the heart (心中神火): the heart is inner nature, issuing from the two eyes; entering fire, descending fire, condensing fire, resting fire, moving fire, Li fire and heart fire are all the turning of the heart and eyes; the unmoving fire can transform the original essence and help the original breath (see Step Five).
  • The union of water and fire (水火交媾): the spirit-fire of the two eyes enters the Cavity of Breath together with the heart and intention; in time, water above and fire below, true breath is naturally produced.
  • Fire arising in the water (水中火发): that is, essence and breath; the “fire” here refers to circulating fire, taking fire, lifting fire, Kan fire, Kun fire, fire within water and fire within the furnace, all the Prior Heaven true breath, which can transform the breathing and help the Original Spirit (see Step Five).
  • The inner elixir in the elixir field (丹田内丹): the elixir can be refined within the Cavity of Breath, and the elixir is transformed from essence.
  • The upright center, the central palace (中正中宫): all affairs accord with the upright center. The central palace is one cun two fen below the heart, that is, the Crimson Palace; it is the place where women produce blood (the original has “produce blood-yellow”), the general root for producing breath, that is, “the root of the liver.”
  • The Dipper’s handle, the mechanism of heaven (天罡天机): the North Star, a metaphor for the spirit presiding over the mechanism of heaven within the Cavity of Breath, that is, the time when the medicine is produced.
  • Heaven and earth intermingling (天地氤氲): the matter of spirit and breath merging: spirit is heaven, breath is earth, and the ten thousand things are produced from them.
  • Natural union (天然交合): spirit and breath meeting, naturally, the whole body empty, called “natural union.”
Entries (3)
Original (Chinese)

斗柄回寅　譬喻真炁后升前降，行周天之事。

内外吸呼　内吸呼于踵，非是用口鼻；外是巽风吸呼口鼻之气。

内景自生　自身用功于阳生时候，自觉脐下温暖，是内景自生。

内药外药　内药是精炁合一之光，外药是采精化炁，能成舍利子。

火之吸呼　有起火、引火、火逼行火、止火，用口鼻吸呼气，能化五谷百味之精，以助元精。

火候实用　吸呼之火能化饮食之谷精以助元精；心目神火能化元精以助元炁；元炁之火能化吸呼以助元神；元神之火能化形而还虚以助道成。

文薰武炼　用文火薰蒸，武火锻炼，坐定心不动，后升前降。

六度真功　布施、持斋、忍辱、精进、禅定、智慧这六种，是度人之丹。

生死窍处　从丹田到精道的道路，下通阳关，采药时将要走泄的精，使他逆回之处。

未化之精　采药时巽风小，精不化炁，从炁穴内精自流出来。

外药外丹　采取的有形药。将要走到外边的精，不令走出，下手采回为丹。

外念淫精　采取时有念，为淫精，成为幻丹，准走泄。

四揲成章　是易经之卦数，阳火、阴符定位为四揲。

北斗北海　斗是炁行周天，北是炁穴地位，海是纳水之处。

用九乾数　六爻全动，称为用九，四九三十六，周天度数。

用六坤数　六爻全动，称为用六，四六二十四周天度数。

有情下种　无念阳生，生到二候正子时，可下真种，即是采药。

因地果生　生果之地，即是丹田。生真种结果之基地。

回光返照　二目合之，下照丹田，久之真炁自生。

玄关发现　丹田精炁神足，发现目前之光，为玄关发现。

行住起止　周天运行之数，为四正，卯酉不在内。

先天真精　无念采的真精化成为炁；有念采的不是先天真精。

先天真炁　就是无念元炁，由无念元精所生。

  • The Dipper’s handle turns back to yin (斗柄回寅): the handle of the Northern Dipper turns back to the yin position (spring returns), a metaphor for the true breath rising behind and descending in front, moving the circuit.
  • Inner and outer breathing (内外吸呼): inner breathing is “inhaling at the heel” (see Step Nine), not using mouth and nose; outer breathing is the Wind of Xun, that is, the breath of breathing through mouth and nose.
  • The inner scene arising by itself (内景自生): when through one’s own practice yang is born and one feels warmth below the navel, that is “the inner scene arising by itself.”
  • Inner medicine, outer medicine (内药外药): the inner medicine is the light of essence and breath united; the outer medicine is what is gathered from essence and transformed into breath, and can become the śarīra (see Steps Four and Six).
  • The breathing of fire (火之吸呼): raising fire, drawing fire, fire pressing fire onward and stopping fire use the breathing of mouth and nose, and can transform the essence of the five grains and hundred flavors to help the original essence.
  • The practical use of fire phasing (火候实用): the fire of breathing can transform the essence of food and grain to help the original essence; the spirit-fire of heart and eyes can transform the original essence to help the original breath; the fire of the original breath can transform the breathing to help the Original Spirit; the fire of the Original Spirit can transform form to return to emptiness and help complete the Way (consistent with the Scripture of Wind and Fire quoted in Step Five).
  • Steaming with civil, refining with martial (文薰武炼): steaming with civil fire, forging with martial fire, sitting settled, the heart unmoving, rising behind and descending in front.
  • The true practice of the six perfections (六度真功): giving, keeping the fast (keeping the precepts), patience, vigor, dhyāna and wisdom — the Buddhist “six perfections,” the elixir that ferries people across.
  • The place of the Opening of Life and Death (生死窍处): the passage from the elixir field to the seminal duct, connecting down to the yang gate; the place where, when gathering the medicine, the essence about to leak is turned back.
  • Untransformed essence (未化之精): when gathering the medicine, if the Wind of Xun (the breath) is too small, the essence is not transformed into breath and flows out of the Cavity of Breath by itself.
  • Outer medicine, outer elixir (外药外丹): the medicine with form that is gathered: the essence about to go outside, not allowed to go out, gathered back by setting to work to form the elixir.
  • Lustful essence from outer thought (外念淫精): if there is thought when gathering, it becomes lustful essence and forms an illusory elixir, which is sure to leak.
  • Counted off by fours, the pattern complete (四揲成章): the numbers of the hexagrams of the Book of Changes; the yang fire and yin tally are fixed as “counted off by fours” (see Step Four).
  • The Northern Dipper, the Northern Sea (北斗北海): “Dipper” is the breath moving the circuit; “north” is the position of the Cavity of Breath; “sea” is where water is received.
  • Using nine, the number of Qian (用九乾数): when all six lines of the Qian hexagram move, it is called “using nine”; four times nine, thirty-six, the degrees of the circuit.
  • Using six, the number of Kun (用六坤数): when all six lines of the Kun hexagram move, it is called “using six”; four times six, twenty-four, the degrees of the circuit.
  • Sowing the seed with feeling (有情下种): yang born without thought, reaching the true zi hour of the second period, one can “sow the true seed,” that is, gather the medicine.
  • From the ground, the fruit is born (因地果生): the ground where the fruit is born is the elixir field, the base where the true seed is born and bears fruit.
  • Turning the light around to shine back (回光返照): the two eyes joined, shining down on the elixir field; in time true breath is naturally produced.
  • The Mysterious Pass appearing (玄关发现): when essence, breath and spirit in the elixir field are full and light appears before the eyes, that is the Mysterious Pass appearing.
  • Moving, staying, rising, stopping (行住起止): the degrees of the circuit’s movement, the “four cardinal points,” of which mao and you are not part (see Step Four).
  • Prior Heaven true essence (先天真精): the true essence gathered without thought transforms into breath; what is gathered with thought is not the Prior Heaven true essence.
  • Prior Heaven true breath (先天真炁): that is, the original breath without thought, produced from the original essence without thought.
Entries (4)
Original (Chinese)

先天真神　元炁所助之无念身中元神，非是烧香磕头之神。

西南荣华　坤位（即丹田）至二目一路虚白，为西南路上月华荣。

沐浴温养　无他无我，一切全空，为沐浴温养。

坎离真炁　坎位是丹田，内生真炁，顺出是精；离位是心，内生真炁，由中发动炁血，人身脉动即此也。

坎水变炁　采药时，顺出是精逆回是炁。

吸机之阖　吸是鼻内往下一吸气，内里先天真炁上升，是吸机之阖。外气下降，内炁上升。

丹药走失　丹要结成的时候，因为有念在内，夜内走失元精。

吹嘘功法　身内有幻丹，用不加神的吸呼吹嘘，幻丹化真丹。

收丹功法　身内吹嘘，不用口鼻吸呼气。丹成采取，不用吸呼气，为止火。

身中璇玑　即是转法轮路脉。

牟尼成珠　神炁合一，炼成真种，法轮数足，炼成舍利子，运过后三关，提升中宫为牟尼珠。炁与意合一，名曰道胎。

金丹金鼎　神炁交足为金丹。炁发动为炉鼎。

呼机之辟　呼是鼻内往外上升出气，内里先天真炁下降，为呼机之辟。阖辟总得二目随之转动，为吸升呼降之阖辟。

空运转气　鼎内若无真种子，不可水火煮空铛。

周天筑基　是下手补精之法。幼年、中年、老人，传法不是一样。

性中之真　性者心也，发于二目，心静为真性，命足发现灵光。

河车之路　转运周天时炁的运行道路。

命门之处　脐下一寸三分有一管，血至此处，化为白色阴精下通阳关。

盲修瞎炼　不知下手炼精、转手炼炁、了手炼神、撒手面壁，为瞎炼，是无明师之过。

活子时到　阳生物举，为活子时，不可急采药，正子时再采。

炁亏补工　世人将炁耗亏，当用采法补之。

炁穴子母　炁穴是丹田，母是精，炼成化炁而神安，是子。

  • Prior Heaven true spirit (先天真神): the Original Spirit within the body, without thought, helped by the original breath — not the spirits worshipped by burning incense and kowtowing.
  • The glory of the southwest (西南荣华): an empty whiteness all the way from the Kun position (the elixir field) to the two eyes — that is, “on the southwest road the moonlight is glorious” (see “fire within the furnace” in Step Five).
  • Ablution, warming and nourishing (沐浴温养): without other and without self, everything wholly empty — that is ablution, warming and nourishing.
  • The true breath of Kan and Li (坎离真炁): the Kan position is the elixir field, within which true breath is produced; going out with the current, it is essence; the Li position is the heart, within which true breath is produced, from which qi and blood stir — the beating of the pulse in the human body is this.
  • Kan water turning into breath (坎水变炁): when gathering the medicine, going out with the current it is essence; turned back, it is breath.
  • The closing of the inhaling mechanism (吸机之阖): inhaling is drawing breath downward in the nose while the Prior Heaven true breath within rises; this is “the closing of the inhaling mechanism”: the outer breath descends, the inner breath rises (see Steps Four and Nine).
  • The elixir medicine lost (丹药走失): when the elixir is about to form, because there is thought in the heart, the original essence is lost at night.
  • The method of blowing and puffing (吹嘘功法): when there is an illusory elixir in the body, blowing and puffing with breathing that adds no spirit or intention can transform the illusory elixir into a true elixir.
  • The method of drawing in the elixir (收丹功法): blowing and puffing within the body, not using the breathing of mouth and nose; when the elixir is formed and gathered, not using the breathing — that is stopping the fire.
  • The armillary within the body (身中璇玑): that is, the road and vessels traveled in turning the Dharma Wheel.
  • The mani becoming a pearl (牟尼成珠): spirit and breath united, refined into the true seed; the count of the Dharma Wheel complete, refined into the śarīra, moved past the three back passes and lifted to the central palace — that is the “mani pearl”; breath and intention united into one is called the “Embryo of the Way.”
  • Golden elixir, golden tripod (金丹金鼎): when spirit and breath have met fully, that is the Golden Elixir; when the breath stirs, that is the furnace and tripod.
  • The opening of the exhaling mechanism (呼机之辟): exhaling is breath going outward and up in the nose while the Prior Heaven true breath within descends; this is “the opening of the exhaling mechanism.” In both closing and opening the two eyes must turn with it — that is the closing and opening of “rising on the inhalation, descending on the exhalation.”
  • Turning the breath empty (空运转气): if there is no true seed in the tripod, one must not boil an empty pot with water and fire.
  • The circuit laying the foundation (周天筑基): the method of setting to work to replenish essence; for the young, the middle-aged and the old, the method transmitted differs.
  • The true within inner nature (性中之真): inner nature is the heart, issuing from the two eyes; when the heart is still, that is true inner nature; when life is full, numinous light appears.
  • The road of the River Chariot (河车之路): the road the breath travels when moving the circuit.
  • The place of the Gate of Life (命门之处): one cun three fen below the navel there is a duct; the blood arriving here turns into white yin essence and passes down to the yang gate (see Step Two).
  • Cultivating blindly, refining at random (盲修瞎炼): not knowing to set to work refining essence, turn the hand refining breath, complete by refining spirit, and let go facing the wall, is refining blindly — the fault of not having an enlightened teacher.
  • The Living Zi Hour arriving (活子时到): yang born and the thing rising is the Living Zi Hour; do not rush to gather the medicine — gather it at the true zi hour.
  • Work to replenish depleted breath (炁亏补工): people of the world have depleted their breath, and should replenish it by the method of gathering.
  • Mother and child of the Cavity of Breath (炁穴子母): the Cavity of Breath is the elixir field; the mother is essence; refined, transformed into breath, the spirit at peace — that is the child.

Original (Chinese)

后天之精　生育者，元精也。逆回者，元炁也。

后天之气　口鼻吸呼之气，用巽风采药即此也。

后天之神　散动之心，情欲之神气，耗散无神则虚。

法吞津液　吞津液而养身，补助阴精多生，吞法前已言明。

法轮常转　本是佛语，实即行周天任督二脉转法轮之事。

封固真宝　采完药后，先用武封，后用文封。

阳关实闭　阳关是走精炁管，闭住真炁不走。

阴缩不举　幼年亏欠，阳萎不举，非是采药炼得不举。

救护命宝　中年、老年人将精耗散，先得采药，为救护命宝。

黄芽复生　时来内里生精，药嫩不可采取，转法轮可复生。

采取药物　下手之法诀，补以前亏欠之精，补足，可马阴藏相。

真铅真汞　真铅是元炁，真汞是元神。

真性真命　性是心中灵气，发于二目。命是肾中元炁，发于淫根。

真人吸呼　真人吸呼于踵，于蒂，非是用口鼻吸呼气。

雪山白牛　聚雪成山，如同念经枯坐。白牛是先天真炁。

造化二机　男女合生子女之机，神炁合生丹药之机。

阳生炁旺　阳生是内景暖气，炁旺是阳举之急，痒生毫窍，是正子时到，速下手采取。

虚危穴地　即丹田，是北方的星名，北斗也是它。

虚无之窟　也是丹田，待炁发生之时，有这种名称。

混采混炼　活子时初到，就采药，嫩为混采。无药行火炼药，为混炼。

巽风升降　鼻内吸呼气，伴着神炁，后升前降，为巽风升降。

无漏真炁　阳关一闭，个个长生，由一闭再得一闭，返成童身。

温养之功　以文火养之，身心意助之，非是沐浴温养。

圣胎显形　神的性光，炁的命光，二光合一，由中生出圣胎。

微风吹动　用吸呼微微吹嘘，就是温养的文火。

顿法渐法　顿法是童身用功，渐法是破身用功。

乱提生火　药炁未动，乱行周天之功，生邪火。

  • Posterior Heaven essence (后天之精): what is used for producing offspring is the original essence; what is turned back is the original breath.
  • Posterior Heaven breath (后天之气): the breath of breathing through mouth and nose; gathering the medicine with the Wind of Xun uses it.
  • Posterior Heaven spirit (后天之神): the scattered, wildly moving heart, the spirit-breath of feelings and desires; when it is dissipated and there is no spirit, a person is depleted.
  • The method of swallowing saliva (法吞津液): swallowing saliva to nourish the body and help produce more yin essence; the method of swallowing has been explained above (see Step Two).
  • The Dharma Wheel turning constantly (法轮常转): originally a Buddhist term; in fact it is the matter of moving the circuit, turning the Dharma Wheel in the Conception and Governing vessels.
  • Sealing the true treasure (封固真宝): after gathering the medicine, first use “martial sealing,” then “civil sealing.”
  • The yang gate truly closed (阳关实闭): the yang gate is the duct through which essence and breath leave; when it is closed, the true breath does not leave.
  • Withdrawn and not rising (阴缩不举): depletion in youth, impotence and not rising — this is not the “not rising” achieved by gathering the medicine (it is not the horse-like retraction).
  • Rescuing and protecting the treasure of life (救护命宝): the middle-aged and the old who have dissipated their essence must first gather the medicine; this is called “rescuing and protecting the treasure of life.”
  • The Yellow Sprouts born again (黄芽复生): when the time comes, essence is produced in the body; when the medicine is tender it must not be gathered; turning the Dharma Wheel can make it be born again.
  • Gathering the medicine (采取药物): the formula for setting to work, replenishing the essence one owed before; when it is replenished to the full, one can have the horse-like retraction.
  • True lead, true mercury (真铅真汞): true lead is the original breath, true mercury the Original Spirit.
  • True inner nature, true life (真性真命): inner nature is the numinous breath in the heart, issuing from the two eyes; life is the original breath in the kidneys, issuing from the root of desire.
  • The breathing of the true person (真人吸呼): the true person “inhales at the heel and exhales at the stem,” not breathing through mouth and nose (see Step Nine).
  • The Snowy Mountains, the white ox (雪山白牛): the “Snowy Mountains” are snow piled into a mountain, like chanting scriptures and sitting dry (cultivating only inner nature and not life); the “white ox” is the Prior Heaven true breath.
  • The two mechanisms of creation (造化二机): man and woman uniting to produce sons and daughters is one mechanism; spirit and breath uniting to produce the elixir medicine is the other.
  • Yang born, breath flourishing (阳生炁旺): yang born is the scene of warm breath in the body; breath flourishing is the member rising urgently and the pores itching — the true zi hour has come, and one should quickly set to work gathering.
  • The ground of the Xu and Wei points (虚危穴地): that is, the elixir field; “Xu” and “Wei” are names of northern lunar lodges, and the Northern Dipper also refers to it.
  • The cave of emptiness and non-being (虚无之窟): also the elixir field, called by this name while waiting for the breath to arise.
  • Gathering and refining at random (混采混炼): gathering the medicine as soon as the Living Zi Hour arrives, when the medicine is tender, is called “gathering at random”; moving the fire to refine the medicine when there is no medicine is called “refining at random.”
  • The Wind of Xun rising and descending (巽风升降): the breath of breathing in the nose, accompanying spirit and breath, rising behind and descending in front, is called “the Wind of Xun rising and descending.”
  • The true breath without leaks (无漏真炁): once the yang gate is closed, every one lives long; from one closing obtaining another closing, one returns to a virgin body.
  • The work of warming and nourishing (温养之功): nourishing it with civil fire, helping it with body, heart and intention — this is not the warming and nourishing of “ablution.”
  • The holy embryo taking visible form (圣胎显形): the light of inner nature of the spirit and the light of life of the breath, the two lights united into one, and the holy embryo is born from their midst (see Step Fourteen).
  • A gentle wind blowing (微风吹动): blowing and puffing gently with the breath — that is the civil fire of warming and nourishing.
  • The sudden method and the gradual method (顿法渐法): the sudden method is practice for a virgin body, the gradual method practice for a body whose virginity is broken (see Step Two).
  • Lifting at random breeds fire (乱提生火): moving the work of the circuit at random before the breath of the medicine has stirred breeds deviant fire.

Original (Chinese)

炼精化炁　下手采药的功法，还精补脑之诀。

经传口诀　由古至今，祖师口口亲传，不可轻传于人。

对斗明星　对是返观，斗是丹田，明星是丹田炁发生。

鼎炉位置　是烹药的器具。丹田炁发为炉，炁升上为鼎。

漏尽通成　精炁永不下耗，阳关自闭，内里有精化为炁，名通成。

调外药法　勒阳关，调到药产神知，精炁动时为调。

谷精火道　谷精是阴精，不生精虫，最搅乱人心，生邪念，得风吹化，为阳精，炼足采回成丹。

慧命之根　元精足生慧，慧光即是精炁补神足，发现目前为根。

潮来之生　阳炁满足，阳物自举，用回返照，绵绵若存之功，自回。

辨时之时　炼丹阳生采药，药产产周天止火，全要按相应时候。

静中炁动　是精囊不足之故。用周天转动之功，存养元精。

筑基之功　是采药运周天之功。

运虚不空　心身意空，而入忘我之境，目前慧光为不空。

龙宫一声　龙宫是炁穴，一声是炁动向外，有诀逆回。

暖炁内景　丹田发暖，是内里生精之景。

窍中之窍　就是炁穴。下通生死窍，八脉辏成一窍，为窍中窍。

药产神知　精炁出来之时，自知下手采取。

药之老嫩　活子时将到是嫩，正子时已过是老，在二候采之。

炉中火种　微小的阳生，要温养，微微吹动，能生元精。

摄精吸呼　即神气，同用，用转小周天之法，收回将要走泄之精。

观慧不空　精炁神足，观空有慧而不空，是真。

  • Refining essence into breath (炼精化炁): the practice of setting to work gathering the medicine, the formula for returning the essence to replenish the brain.
  • The oral formula transmitted (经传口诀): from ancient times to the present, the patriarchs transmitted it in person by word of mouth; it must not be passed on lightly.
  • Facing the Dipper with the bright star (对斗明星): “facing” is turning the gaze back, the “Dipper” is the elixir field, the “bright star” is the breath of the elixir field arising (see Qiu Ling’s note to Step Eight).
  • The position of tripod and furnace (鼎炉位置): the vessels for cooking the medicine: the breath of the elixir field stirring is the “furnace,” the breath rising is the “tripod” (see Step Two).
  • The power of the exhaustion of leaks attained (漏尽通成): the essence and breath never again wasting downward, the yang gate closing by itself, all the essence in the body transformed into breath — this is called “the power of the exhaustion of leaks attained.”
  • The method of regulating the outer medicine (调外药法): reining in the yang gate, regulating until “when the medicine is produced, the spirit knows”; regulate when the essence and breath stir (see Step Four).
  • The essence of grain and the road of fire (谷精火道): the essence of grain (essence transformed from food) is yin essence, does not produce sperm, and most disturbs the heart and breeds deviant thoughts; blown and transformed by the Wind of Xun, it becomes yang essence, and when refined to sufficiency and gathered back, it can form the elixir.
  • The root of wisdom-life (慧命之根): when the original essence is full, wisdom is born; the light of wisdom is essence and breath replenishing the spirit to the full and appearing before the eyes — this is the “root.”
  • Born with the tide (潮来之生): when the yang breath is full, the member rises by itself; with the work of turning the light around to shine back, soft and continuous as if barely there, it goes back by itself.
  • Discerning the time of times (辨时之时): in refining the elixir, yang being born, gathering the medicine, the medicine being produced, the circuit and stopping the fire must all keep to their proper times.
  • Breath stirring in stillness (静中炁动): (the breath stirring and the member rising during quiet sitting) is because the seminal vesicles are insufficient; with the work of turning the circuit, preserve and nourish the original essence.
  • The work of laying the foundation (筑基之功): that is, the work of gathering the medicine and moving the circuit.
  • Moving emptiness that is not empty (运虚不空): heart, body and intention all empty, entering the state of forgetting oneself; the light of wisdom before the eyes is “not empty.”
  • One sound in the Dragon Palace (龙宫一声): the Dragon Palace is the Cavity of Breath; “one sound” is the breath stirring and going outward; there is a formula for turning it back.
  • The inner scene of warm breath (暖炁内景): warmth in the elixir field is the scene of essence being produced in the body.
  • The opening within the opening (窍中之窍): that is, the Cavity of Breath: connecting down to the Opening of Life and Death, the eight vessels gathered into one opening, called “the opening within the opening” (see Step Eight).
  • When the medicine is produced, the spirit knows (药产神知): when the essence and breath come out, one knows oneself to set to work gathering.
  • Old and tender medicine (药之老嫩): when the Living Zi Hour is about to arrive it is tender, when the true zi hour has passed it is old; gather it in the time of the two periods.
  • The ember in the furnace (炉中火种): the faint birth of yang, to be warmed and nourished, gently blown, can produce original essence.
  • Breathing that draws in the essence (摄精吸呼): that is, spirit and breath used together, using the method of turning the Lesser Celestial Circuit to draw back the essence about to leak.
  • Contemplating wisdom that is not empty (观慧不空): when essence, breath and spirit are full, one contemplates emptiness and there is the light of wisdom — not empty; only this is true.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Use it as a reference book: when you meet a term you do not understand in the sixteen steps above, you can look up Zhao’s own explanation here.
  2. Note the Qianfeng school’s special usages: “outer medicine,” “inner medicine,” “śarīra,” “stopping the fire” and so on do not mean quite the same as in the elixir classics generally.
  3. A few that anyone can use: “turning the light around to shine back,” “ablution, warming and nourishing,” “a gentle wind blowing,” “the inner scene of warm breath” — shining lightly on the elixir field, breathing faintly, the heart empty and without self, are all safe heart methods for quiet sitting.

Translator's Notes

  1. Most entry names in the original are four characters, a few three or five (such as “twenty-four, withdrawing the yin tally” and “thirty-six, advancing the yang fire”); reproduced as is. In the original a space separates each entry from its explanation; in the Chinese text on this site they are separated by a full-width space.
  2. “The reverted elixir of seven returns”: “seven is the completing number of metal” — by the numbers of the River Chart, metal’s generating number is four and its completing number nine, while “seven” is the completing number of fire; in the alchemists’ saying “seven returns, nine reversions,” seven is usually fire and nine metal. So in the original, reproduced as is.
  3. “The method of human fire”: “human fire” (人火) should be a mistake for “entering fire” (入火); reproduced as is.
  4. “The upright center, the central palace”: “the place where women produce blood-yellow” is as in the original; reproduced as is.
  5. The entry name “discerning the time of times” (辨时之时) and the doubled character in “medicine produced produced, circuit, stopping the fire” (药产产周天止火) are so in the original; reproduced as is.
  6. “The essence of grain and the road of fire”: so in the original; the correspondence between the entry name and its explanation is not very clear; reproduced as is.
  7. “Breathing that draws in the essence” (摄精吸呼　即神气，同用): the punctuation of the original (“that is, spirit-breath, used together”) is as given; reproduced as is.
  8. In the original, the entries of this appendix are arranged roughly by the stroke count of the first character, but not strictly; this site follows the original order and divides them into six groups by the pages of the original.
