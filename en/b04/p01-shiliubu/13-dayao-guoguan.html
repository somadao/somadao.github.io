The Sixteen Steps of Method Formulas · 第十三步 大药过关

Step Thirteen · The Great Medicine Passes the Gates

The formula of Step Thirteen, the Great Medicine passes the gates — seated firm on the wooden base, the breath rises; five dragons bear up the holy one through the three passes

About This Chapter

This is the main practice of the Greater Celestial Circuit: the refined śarīra (the Great Medicine) is raised from the lower elixir field through the three passes — the Caudal Funnel, the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow — to the Muddy Pellet, where it unites with “true inner nature” and becomes the “golden liquor true seed,” then descends by the upper Magpie Bridge, the Mysterious Breast and the Twelve-Storied Tower, through the Crimson Palace, and is planted back in the lower elixir field — what the ancients called “the Great Medicine passing the gates and being ingested,” “the golden liquor reverted elixir.”

  1. Conditions: the six scenes appearing before you, the yang light appearing three times, the horse-like retraction; and one must also have “the method, the means, the companion and the place” — without these, “it cannot be gathered.” What is called “gathering the Great Medicine in seven days” is in fact obtained on the sixth day.
  2. The six roots quaking: the roots of nose and tongue draw air back in with the intention, the roots of ear and eye resound, the root of desire stirs by itself — the śarīra flows lively in the seminal vesicles and surges toward the position of the heart; since the Penetrating Vessel is not open, it turns downward and surges back at the Caudal Funnel of the Governing Vessel.
  3. Two dangers: the “lower Magpie Bridge” — the Great Medicine rushing down from the Caudal Funnel to the grain path (the anus) and leaking away; the “upper Magpie Bridge” — the true seed leaking out through the nostrils as it descends from the Mysterious Breast. That is why a “wooden base seat” is pressed against the grain path and a “mulainian” plugs the nostrils.
  4. Five dragons bearing up the holy one, the three carts: when the true treasure is blocked and does not move, one condenses the spirit with a single intention and does not move, while the Daoist companion gently rubs the Caudal Funnel, the Spine-Straddling Pass, the Jade Pillow and Fengfu — “wait for it to move by itself and then lead it; do not lead it to make it move”; when it surges at a pass by itself, the hand pressing the Opening of Life and Death gives an upward lift, together with inhaling, the tongue licking the palate and straightening the eyes of the waist; passing the Caudal Funnel like a goat cart (light and slow), the Spine-Straddling Pass like a deer cart (light and fast), the Jade Pillow like an ox cart (with force).
  5. Inhale, lick, contract, close: inhale — drawing in through the nose to support the rise; lick — the tongue pressed back against the palate, the eyes turning from left to right; contract — the Daoist companion pinching the Governing Vessel; close — the wooden base seat, the mulainian and the finger closing three places.
  6. Advancing yang and withdrawing yin: when the true treasure reaches the Muddy Pellet and unites with inner nature, close the eyes and turn four sets of nine from left to right (Figure 30), then open the eyes and turn four sets of six from right to left (Figure 31); the true seed condenses, turns into golden liquor and descends, is planted in the Cavity of Breath, and “flowers and bears fruit.”

Qiu Ling’s note sums up this stage of work in three parts — “the fire sufficient and the fire stopped,” “the Great Medicine produced” and “passing the gates and ingesting” — quotes Wu Chongxu’s four accounts of “the production of the Great Medicine,” and points out that the wooden seat and nose-clamping methods of the Wu-Liu school are considered overly cumbersome; Wu Chongxu stresses again and again that passing the gates must “follow its own impulse, with the subtle intention of mutual awareness,” and “the essential is only to go along with what is natural.”

Important safety note (translator): This is the step in the whole book least suitable for trying on your own. The original requires pressing a foreign object against the anus, plugging the nostrils with a wooden stopper, having another person rub the spine, and forcefully “lifting” the perineum while inhaling and straightening the waist — plugging the nostrils carries a risk of suffocation, pressing objects against the perineum and anus may cause injury, and straining with held breath while straightening the waist may trigger cardiovascular or cerebrovascular accidents. Both Zhao and Qiu Ling say this step is “not at all easy to meet,” and that it must be done only with a true transmission and with a companion watching over one. This site only edits and translates the old texts and does not recommend that anyone follow them.

Original Text and Translation

Figure 29 · Passing the Great Pass

In the figure a monk with a shaven head sits upright cross-legged, holding a ruyi scepter; to the upper left of the head is a circle inscribed “light of wisdom, Mysterious Pass.” On the right stand two sword-shaped objects marked “mulainian” (in the original the horizontal text reads right to left as 年来木); below is a semicircle marked “wooden base seat” (read in the original as 座底木). These are the two implements used in passing the great pass: the mulainian for plugging the nostrils, the wooden base seat for pressing against the grain path.

Formula
Original (Chinese)

座定木底炁上升，

五龙捧圣过三关。

三车拉上昆仑顶，

塞鼻牵牛木来年。

Seated firm on the wooden base seat, the breath rises; “five dragons bear up the holy one” (the True Intention and the Original Spirit bear up the true treasure) through the three passes (the Caudal Funnel, the Spine-Straddling Pass, the Jade Pillow). With the three carts (the goat cart, the deer cart, the ox cart) it is drawn up to the summit of Kunlun (the Muddy Pellet); what plugs the nose and “leads the ox” (keeps the true seed from leaking out of the nose) is the mulainian.

§1
Original (Chinese)

采大药过大关功法，为静坐参禅，神炁归丹田，以木底座抵住生死窍，使下窍不漏，上窍则用木来年将鼻塞住，以防舍利子走失。以静而照，以柔而用，待动而托升，柔护而行，以双忘而定。静则大药升，动者真意托，不可用意引，用五龙捧圣，三车牵上，用吸、舐、撮、闭真口诀，大药得矣。采大药者，名曰七日，实在得之六日，非得景现，不能采也。无法、财、侣、地，不能采也。

The practice of gathering the Great Medicine and passing the great pass: sit quietly in Chan meditation, spirit and breath returning to the elixir field; press the wooden base seat against the Opening of Life and Death so that the lower opening does not leak; for the upper opening, plug the nose with the mulainian to keep the śarīra from being lost. Shine on it with stillness, use it with softness; wait until it moves and then support it upward, protecting it gently as it goes; settle it with “double forgetting” (forgetting both things and self). In stillness, the Great Medicine rises; when it moves, support it with the True Intention — do not lead it with the intention. Use “five dragons bearing up the holy one,” draw it up with the “three carts,” use the true formula of inhale, lick, contract, close, and the Great Medicine is obtained. Gathering the Great Medicine is nominally called “seven days,” but in fact it is obtained on the sixth day; without the scenes appearing, it cannot be gathered; without the method, the means, the companion and the place, it cannot be gathered either.

§2
Original (Chinese)

前十二步功大药炼足，有六景现前。非是一日六景现出，先有丹田发热，两肾汤煎，眼生金机，左耳虎啸，右耳龙吟，脑后鹫鸣，身涌鼻搐之类，皆是得药之景。此时精尽化炁，将炁养足，摄此动炁，凝成为舍利子，方得淫根缩如童子，为马阴藏相，龟缩不举，阳关自闭，真炁不漏，真长生得矣。前闭是保生命真炁不走，故李虚庵祖师曰，由一闭，再得一闭，如此便得真长生；不得闭，便不得真长生。求长生者当于此勉之求之。后闭阳关，是功夫炼成，自然自闭。阳关自闭，淫根绝无举动，无精可炼，则火当止，精囊内所积聚元精化为舍利子，舍利子之金光，由内发现于目前，为金光一现，当预备法、财、侣、地。舍利子在精囊内，总得阳炁助养，阳炁尽归于炁根，方得金光二现。金光二现，则阳炁足满，纵有动机，当可采足满之药，补还我幼年初破身之精。采这一回，可还到童身之体。采时不用吸呼之气，为止火。随着吸呼之气不用，用心神意后升前降，以培养舍利子实足。忽有眉间又掣电光，虚室生白，由炁内生出金光三现，是采大药之景到。阳光二现，是止火之景到，当止火采之；若阳光三现，是采大药之景到，正是时候，采取过大关；若等阳光四现，是止火时，不止火，用吸呼气之过也，大药必随四现之火溢出于外，化为后天有形之元精，前功枉废。学者细心悟之，不可大意。

When the twelve steps above have refined the Great Medicine to sufficiency, the six scenes appear before you. The six scenes do not all appear in one day; first there is heat in the elixir field, the two kidneys simmering as in hot water, golden light born in the eyes, the tiger’s roar in the left ear, the dragon’s chant in the right ear, the cry of a vulture behind the head, surging in the body and twitching of the nose and so on — all scenes of obtaining the medicine. At this time all the essence is transformed into breath; nourish the breath to the full, hold this stirring breath and condense it into the śarīra, and only then can the root of desire retract like a boy’s, which is the horse-like retraction: withdrawn like a tortoise and not rising, the yang gate closes by itself, the true breath does not leak, and true long life is obtained.

The first “closing” keeps the true breath of life from leaving; that is why Patriarch Li Xu’an said: “From one closing, obtain another closing, and thus one obtains true long life; without closing, one does not obtain true long life.” Those who seek long life should strive for it here. The second closing of the yang gate is the practice having been accomplished, so that it closes naturally by itself. When the yang gate has closed by itself and the root of desire no longer rises, there is no essence to refine, and the fire should stop; the original essence accumulated in the seminal vesicles transforms into the śarīra, and the golden light of the śarīra appears from within before the eyes — that is “the golden light appearing once,” and one should prepare the method, the means, the companion and the place. The śarīra in the seminal vesicles must always have the help and nourishment of the yang breath; only when the yang breath has all returned to the root of breath can one obtain “the golden light appearing twice.” When the golden light appears twice, the yang breath is full; even if there is still a stirring, one can gather this full medicine to repay the essence of one’s first loss of virginity in youth — gathering it this once, one can return to the body of a virgin. When gathering, the breath of breathing is not used; this is called “stopping the fire”: the breath of breathing being no longer used, one uses heart, spirit and intention, rising behind and descending in front, to cultivate the śarīra so that it is truly sufficient. Suddenly lightning flashes again between the eyebrows, the empty room produces whiteness, and golden light is born from within the breath — that is “the third appearance,” the scene of gathering the Great Medicine having come.

When the yang light appears twice, the scene of stopping the fire has come, and one should stop the fire and gather; when the yang light appears three times, the scene of gathering the Great Medicine has come, it is exactly the time, and one gathers it and passes the great pass; if one waits until the yang light appears four times, it is the fault of not stopping the fire when it should have been stopped, still using the breath of breathing, and the Great Medicine is sure to overflow out of the body with the fire of the fourth appearance and turn into Posterior Heaven original essence with form, wasting all previous work. Students must realize this carefully and not be careless.

§3
Original (Chinese)

采大药过关服食七日口诀，为先入静室，每日二目下视丹田，久之，六景现前，须用六根震动口诀，将舍利子震动，好过后三关。六根者，鼻根、淫根、眼根、耳根、舌根、意根是也。此六根震动，是鼻根、舌根用意根往回吸空气，抽而震动，耳根、眼根响动，淫根自发动。由于六根紧震，舍利子在精囊内流动活泼，下触阳关，阳关已闭，自能转上冲心位，而心位冲脉不开，炁向下降，自然向下后冲督脉尾闾关。若不用五龙捧圣吸、舐、撮、闭以神提羊车之法诀，便过不去，自转动由尾闾而下奔走谷道，谷道易开，大药泄去，前功废也！此是下鹊桥之危险也。昔曹、邱二真人预用木座，状如馒首，覆绵取软，座抵谷道，其势上耸，不使大药下奔，为外固之法器也。

The “seven-day formula” for gathering the Great Medicine, passing the gates and ingesting: first enter a quiet room, and every day let the two eyes look down at the elixir field; in time the six scenes appear before you, and one must use the formula of “the six roots quaking” to shake the śarīra into motion so that it can pass the three back passes. The six roots are the roots of nose, desire, eye, ear, tongue and intention. In the quaking of these six roots, the roots of nose and tongue use the root of intention to draw air back in, shaking with one pull; the roots of ear and eye resound; the root of desire stirs by itself. Because the six roots quake tightly, the śarīra flows lively in the seminal vesicles and touches the yang gate below; the yang gate is already closed, so it naturally turns upward and surges toward the position of the heart; but the Penetrating Vessel at the position of the heart is not open, so the breath descends, and naturally surges downward and backward at the Caudal Funnel pass of the Governing Vessel. Without the formula of “five dragons bearing up the holy one,” inhale, lick, contract, close, and raising the “goat cart” with the spirit, it cannot get through; turning by itself, it will rush down from the Caudal Funnel to the grain path (the anus); the grain path opens easily, the Great Medicine leaks away, and the previous work is wasted! This is the danger of the “lower Magpie Bridge.” In the past the two Perfected Cao (Cao Huanyang) and Qiu (Qiu Chuji) prepared a wooden seat beforehand, shaped like a steamed bun, wrapped in cotton wadding to make it soft, sitting on it pressed against the grain path, pushing upward, not letting the Great Medicine rush downward — this is the implement of “outer securing.”

§4
Original (Chinese)

大药内升督脉时，由道侣轻撮尾闾，尾闾迂阻而真宝不动，若用意望上引导，不能过关。心内一用真意关自闭，故有五龙捧圣之法，乃善引之正功。当真宝迂阻不动之时，一意凝神不动，由道侣轻撮尾闾，热而随解，自己用手抵生死窍，真宝之炁自撞尾闾关，候自动而后引，不可引而使动。当其忽自动冲关之时，随其动机，手抵生死窍向上一捧，两眼轻轻慢慢一转，如同羊拉车，力大筋小，轻微上引，舌舐上腭，随其真宝自动之机，鼻内往回一吸气，腰眼一直，咕嘟一声自然进入尾闾关，道侣向上急撮，真宝一度一度上升夹脊关，迂阻而真宝不动，道侣又轻撮夹脊，我凝神不动，热而随解，忽有自动冲开关窍之机，我手抵生死窍，往上一捧，两眼快快一转，如同鹿拉车轻而快，将真宝快提，舌舐上腭，鼻内向回一吸气，腰眼一挺，咕嘟一声自然撞过夹脊关。道侣又向上急撮，真宝又一度一度上升，升至玉枕关，迂阻而不动，道侣又急撮玉枕关，我凝神不动，热而随解，真宝之炁自撞玉枕关，随其动机，手抵生死窍朝上一托，此为五龙捧圣上升，而眼用力一转，如同牛拉车，眼向上一转，舌抵上颚，随其炁自动之机，鼻往回一吸气，腰眼一挺，咕噜一声，自然冲过玉枕关。然后道侣往上急撮风府，将风府髓窍撮热，髓解而真宝一度一度进入风府，与延髓结合，内通小脑，至大脑中心，此真命炁之宝，迂真性神之宝，此二宝神炁合一，为性命同宫。这时道侣用木来年塞住练功者鼻窍，使鼻根不漏，速将二目先闭合，由左向右转，九回一定，看正中慧光，再由左向右转九回一定，如此转四个九回一定。转法见图30，此为进阳。再睁开眼，由右向左转六回，闭眼看慧光，再睁开眼，由右向左转六回，再闭眼看慧光，如此转四个六回，转法见图31，此为退阴。脑中神炁合一，用眼转动，内里神炁合一之真宝凝聚一处，名曰先天真种。二目下照，真种脱落下降，由祖窍之根玄膺穴下降，向前下至印堂内，炁阻不通，向前行于鼻根下，若无木来年塞封，真种可能由鼻窍泄出，则前功废也！此是上鹊桥大危险也。故木来年、木底座之用，不可不预为防也。真种不致下驰于鼻窍，在印堂内遇阻而不动。我惟一意不生，凝神下照，以待真种自动，随其动机而有两相知之微意，轻轻引下玄膺穴，过十二重楼接合，若迂明师亲传，便吞进十二重楼，过绛宫，又名中宫。真种至此下落脐下一寸三分之下丹田不动。前是过三关，此是服食正功。

As the Great Medicine rises inward along the Governing Vessel, the Daoist companion gently rubs and pinches the Caudal Funnel. When the Caudal Funnel meets an obstruction (the original has 迂阻, see the Translator’s Notes), the true treasure does not move; if at this time one uses the intention to lead it upward, it cannot pass the pass — as soon as the heart uses intention, the pass closes by itself. That is why there is the method of “five dragons bearing up the holy one,” which is the right practice of skillful leading. When the true treasure is blocked and does not move, condense the spirit with a single intention and do not move, while the Daoist companion gently rubs and pinches the Caudal Funnel; when it grows warm, it opens accordingly; press the Opening of Life and Death with one’s own hand, and the breath of the true treasure strikes the Caudal Funnel pass by itself — wait for it to move by itself and then lead it; do not lead it to make it move.

When it suddenly moves by itself and surges at the pass, following its impulse, the hand pressing the Opening of Life and Death gives an upward lift, the two eyes turn once, lightly and slowly, like a goat pulling a cart (great strength, small sinews, a slight upward lead); the tongue licks the palate, and following the moment when the true treasure moves by itself, draw breath back in through the nose, straighten the eyes of the waist, and with a “gurgle” it naturally enters the Caudal Funnel pass. The Daoist companion rubs quickly upward, and the true treasure rises degree by degree to the Spine-Straddling Pass; meeting an obstruction, the true treasure does not move; the companion again gently rubs the Spine-Straddling Pass, I condense the spirit and do not move, and when it grows warm it opens accordingly; suddenly there is the moment of its moving by itself to break through the pass; my hand pressing the Opening of Life and Death gives an upward lift, the two eyes turn once quickly, like a deer pulling a cart (light and fast), lifting the true treasure quickly; the tongue licks the palate, draw breath back in through the nose, straighten the eyes of the waist, and with a “gurgle” it naturally breaks through the Spine-Straddling Pass. The companion again rubs quickly upward, the true treasure again rises degree by degree to the Jade Pillow pass, meets an obstruction and does not move; the companion again rubs the Jade Pillow pass quickly, I condense the spirit and do not move, and when it grows warm it opens accordingly, and the breath of the true treasure strikes the Jade Pillow pass by itself; following its impulse, the hand pressing the Opening of Life and Death gives an upward push — this is “five dragons bearing up the holy one in its ascent” — the eyes turn once with force, like an ox pulling a cart, turning upward; the tongue pressed against the palate, following the moment when the breath moves by itself, draw breath back in through the nose, straighten the eyes of the waist, and with a “gurgle” it naturally breaks through the Jade Pillow pass.

Then the Daoist companion rubs quickly upward at Fengfu (at the center of the back of the neck, above the hairline), rubbing the marrow opening at Fengfu until warm; once the marrow opening is loosened, the true treasure enters Fengfu degree by degree, joins with the medulla oblongata, connects inward with the cerebellum and reaches the center of the cerebrum. This is the treasure of the breath of “true life” meeting the treasure of the spirit of “true inner nature”; when these two treasures of spirit and breath unite into one, it is “inner nature and life in the same palace.” At this time the Daoist companion plugs the practitioner’s nostrils with the mulainian so that the root of the nose does not leak; quickly close the two eyes first and turn nine circles from left to right, settle a moment and look at the light of wisdom in the center; then turn nine circles from left to right again and settle a moment; turn four sets of nine like this (for the way of turning see Figure 30); this is “advancing yang.” Then open the eyes and turn six circles from right to left, close the eyes and look at the light of wisdom; open the eyes again and turn six circles from right to left, and close the eyes again to look at the light of wisdom; turn four sets of six like this (for the way of turning see Figure 31); this is “withdrawing yin.” When spirit and breath in the brain unite into one and are turned with the eyes, the true treasure of spirit and breath united within condenses in one place; this is called the “Prior Heaven true seed.”

When the two eyes shine downward, the true seed drops off and descends, coming down from the Mysterious Breast point at the root of the Ancestral Opening, forward and down into the Hall of Impression; when the breath is blocked and cannot pass, it moves forward to below the root of the nose — if there were no mulainian plugging it, the true seed might leak out through the nostrils, and the previous work would be wasted! This is the great danger of the “upper Magpie Bridge.” So the function of the mulainian and the wooden base seat must be prepared for in advance. The true seed, not going off down through the nostrils, meets an obstruction in the Hall of Impression and does not move; I simply let not a single intention arise, condense the spirit and shine downward, waiting for the true seed to move by itself; following its impulse, with a slight intention of “mutual awareness,” lead it gently down the Mysterious Breast point and over the Twelve-Storied Tower to connect it; if one has met an enlightened teacher’s personal transmission, one swallows it into the Twelve-Storied Tower and past the Crimson Palace (also called the central palace). Arriving here, the true seed drops into the lower elixir field one cun three fen below the navel and does not move. What came before is “passing the three passes”; this is the right practice of “ingesting.”

§5
Original (Chinese)

问曰：淫根不动，缩如童子，为马阴藏相。因何淫根不动？

答曰：由下手采药修起，修到淫根不动，如同十四五岁小孩外阳一样，再加工细炼，炼得如同四、五岁小孩外阳一样，迂妇女同眠而不动心，还得细炼，炼得返回父母未生前，外阳不出来，为马阴藏相，阳关自闭矣。此是真长生得矣！

Question: When the root of desire does not stir and is retracted like a boy’s, it is called the horse-like retraction. Why does the root of desire not stir?

Answer: Cultivating from the first gathering of the medicine, one cultivates until the root of desire does not stir, like the outer yang of a child of fourteen or fifteen; then, refining further with care, one refines it until it is like the outer yang of a child of four or five, so that even meeting (the original has 迂) a woman and sleeping beside her, the heart does not stir; one must refine still further, until returning to “before father and mother were born,” the outer yang retracted and not coming out — that is the horse-like retraction, and the yang gate has closed by itself. This is truly obtaining long life!

§6
Original (Chinese)

问曰：何为五龙捧圣之秘机？

答曰：前派仙师欲明过关秘旨，故借玄帝舍身得道比喻言之。五者土数，真意属土；龙者乃元神，故云五龙捧圣，乃大药过关之秘机。采大药过关，六根震动，将舍利子发动之时，谷道有木底座抵住，生死窍自闭，还得用手指点住。真意属土为五数，元神乃龙也。用手捧住生死窍，真宝一升，真意同手向上一捧并吸，此为捧也。圣者，是真宝上升。此乃前派祖师喻言之词，但不言法诀。

Question: What is the secret mechanism of “five dragons bearing up the holy one”?

Answer: The immortal teachers of earlier generations, wishing to explain the secret purport of passing the gates, borrowed as a metaphor the story of the Dark Emperor Zhenwu (the Supreme Emperor of the Dark Heaven) attaining the Way by giving up his body. “Five” is the number of earth, and the True Intention belongs to earth; the “dragons” are the Original Spirit; hence “five dragons bearing up the holy one” — this is the secret mechanism of the Great Medicine passing the gates. When gathering the Great Medicine and passing the gates, as the six roots quake and the śarīra stirs, the grain path is pressed by the wooden base seat and the Opening of Life and Death is naturally closed, but it must still be pressed with the fingers. The True Intention belongs to earth, its number five; the Original Spirit is the dragon. Hold the Opening of Life and Death with the hand, and as the true treasure rises, the True Intention and the hand give an upward lift together, while inhaling — this is “bearing up.” “The holy one” is the true treasure rising. This is the metaphorical language of the patriarchs of earlier generations, but they did not state the formula.

§7
Original (Chinese)

问曰：七日采大药过关，内有吸、舐、撮、闭，何解？

答曰：这吸、舐、撮、闭，是七日采大药过关全功。吸者，是后天鼻内吸气，真宝向上一升，吸气与真意在后向上一吸托。舐者，舌尖倒顶上腭，真宝一升，鼻气向上一吸托，眼要由左向右一转，眼转时要分羊车、鹿车、牛车力量转动，学者细心悟之。撮者，道侣撮督脉也，真宝发动时，谷道有木底座抵住，生死窍有手点住，真宝不能泄出，道侣速撮尾闾关，髓热而解，真宝咕噜一声进入尾闾关，道侣速向上撮，过夹脊、玉枕至泥丸宫后不撮。闭者，是木底座抵住谷道，木来年塞住鼻窍，手指闭住生死窍，此三处闭法通是怕真宝泄漏。

Question: In gathering the Great Medicine in seven days and passing the gates there are “inhale, lick, contract, close”; how are they explained?

Answer: This inhale, lick, contract, close is the whole practice of gathering the Great Medicine in seven days and passing the gates. Inhale: the Posterior Heaven breath drawn in through the nose; as the true treasure rises, the inhalation and the True Intention give an upward draw and support from behind. Lick: the tip of the tongue pressed back against the palate; as the true treasure rises, the breath of the nose gives an upward draw and support, and the eyes turn once from left to right; when turning the eyes, one must apportion the force of the goat cart, deer cart and ox cart — students should realize this carefully. Contract: the Daoist companion pinches the Governing Vessel — when the true treasure stirs, the grain path is pressed by the wooden base seat and the Opening of Life and Death is pressed by the hand, so the true treasure cannot leak out; the companion quickly rubs and pinches the Caudal Funnel pass, the marrow grows warm and loosens, and with a “gurgle” the true treasure enters the Caudal Funnel pass; the companion quickly rubs and pinches upward, past the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow, and after it reaches the Muddy Pellet palace stops pinching. Close: the wooden base seat pressed against the grain path, the mulainian plugging the nostrils, the fingers closing the Opening of Life and Death — these three closings are all for fear of the true treasure leaking.

§8
Original (Chinese)

问曰：采大药过三关，真宝上升至泥丸宫，因何又用进阳火、退阴符？

答曰：采大药过三关，真宝上升泥丸宫，这真宝是何物？即是舍利子的命炁。采此命炁后过三关，上升泥丸宫，迂性炁合为一炁。二炁合一，为先天真种。用阳火、阴符转动，将属阴之性中阳炁和属阳之命中阴炁转动，混合为一，名曰真种。此真种化为金液降下，非是口中津液，若无木来年塞住鼻窍，金液失矣。金液由祖窍下降玄膺穴、十二重楼。金液真种一落十二重楼，若无真师口传心授，便将真种喷出，前功枉废，岂不惜哉！法曰：金液真种未落之前，先用津液将重楼润之，容金液一落气管、一润吞，咕噜一声，落下重楼，至绛宫和合，壮我金液真种之力，下炁穴。金液真种内有阳炁，如同五谷种子一样，种在炁穴，好开花结果。

Question: In gathering the Great Medicine and passing the three passes, the true treasure rises to the Muddy Pellet palace; why then must one again use advancing the yang fire and withdrawing the yin tally?

Answer: In gathering the Great Medicine and passing the three passes, the true treasure rises to the Muddy Pellet palace — what is this true treasure? It is the breath of life of the śarīra. Gathering this breath of life and passing the three passes, it rises to the Muddy Pellet palace, meets (the original has 迂) the breath of inner nature, and the two unite into one breath. The two breaths united into one are the Prior Heaven true seed. By turning with the yang fire and the yin tally, the yang breath within inner nature, which belongs to yin, and the yin breath within life, which belongs to yang, are set turning and merge into one, called the “true seed.” This true seed transforms into golden liquor and descends; it is not the saliva of the mouth; if there were no mulainian plugging the nostrils, the golden liquor would be lost. The golden liquor descends from the Ancestral Opening to the Mysterious Breast point and the Twelve-Storied Tower. As soon as the golden liquor true seed drops into the Twelve-Storied Tower, without a true teacher’s oral and heart-to-heart transmission, one would spit the true seed out, and all previous work would be wasted — what a pity! The method: before the golden liquor true seed drops, first moisten the Storied Tower with saliva; as soon as the golden liquor drops into the windpipe, moistened, swallow it at once, and with a “gurgle” it drops down the Storied Tower to the Crimson Palace, unites with it there, strengthening the power of my golden liquor true seed, and goes down to the Cavity of Breath. Within the golden liquor true seed there is yang breath; like the seed of the five grains, it is planted in the Cavity of Breath so that it may flower and bear fruit.

§9
Original (Chinese)

问曰：师言这金液真种，种在炁穴内好开花结果，实在不明白是何道理？

答曰：这金液真种不易得之，种在真炁穴内发生黄芽，能开花结果。这开花，即是目前见的慧光。花中有雌蕊雄蕊，雌雄蕊一合，即能结果。若不雌雄和合，结不了果。雌蕊者，即是性炁发生的慧光；雄蕊者，即是命炁发生的金光。二光合一，由中生出有形之胎，即为结果。

Question: Teacher, you say this golden liquor true seed is planted in the Cavity of Breath so that it may flower and bear fruit; I really do not understand the principle.

Answer: This golden liquor true seed is not easy to obtain; planted in the true Cavity of Breath, it produces the Yellow Sprouts and can flower and bear fruit. This “flowering” is the light of wisdom seen before the eyes. In a flower there are the female and male stamens; when the female and male stamens unite, it can bear fruit; if female and male do not unite, it cannot bear fruit. The female stamen is the light of wisdom produced by the breath of inner nature; the male stamen is the golden light produced by the breath of life. When the two lights unite, an embryo with form is born from them — that is “bearing fruit.”

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§9
Original note (Chinese)

【注释】经前十二步功，纯阳真炁凝聚下丹田，已炼成舍利子，所谓丹熟珠灵，马阴藏相，六景相继呈现，欲采金丹大药，需要七日大定之功。七日之期，亦大概而言之。本步功为大周天之正功。

这段功程，一般分为火足止火，大药产生和过关服食。六根震动，六景次第呈现，即为火足之候；阳光二现是止火之景，阳光三现大药可采；至阳光四现，大药溢出于外，化为后天有形之精，前功枉废。大药之生，伍冲虚《仙佛合宗》共有四说：（一）交媾而后生，即通过眸光专视，使心中之神与肾中元炁，实行交媾，亦即水火交媾，自然团成大药，如火珠之形。（二）勾引而后生，即用双眸之光勾引真意于上，则大药自相随出现于下。（三）静定而后生，因眸光之专视，使神炁俱得定机，由是元炁成形，因静而生动，生于内矣。（四）息定而后生，先天元神元炁因眸光专视而得定机，后天自运之火亦得定机而有所归依，真息因之而定，大药自生。以上四法皆以目光为招摄，故曰正法眼藏，为采大药之秘法天机也。至于大药过关服食，则有吸、舐、撮、闭之功，有五龙捧圣之秘机，还有挟鼻牵牛防漏之法等，本步功法均言之甚详。但伍柳派传承之使用木座及挟鼻诸法被认为过于繁琐。

伍冲虚在《仙佛合宗》中一再强调，大药过关要“随其动机而有两相知之微意”，“大药凭真意之转旋而升降，犹天轮藉天心之斡运而循环”，“其行住之机，惟在顺其自然为要也”。此方是过关正功。

大周天过关服食的口诀中，较重要的是进阳火和退阴符。据牛金宝氏所传，进阳火是闭目由下往左上转睛，走子、卯、午、酉、子位。子、卯、午、酉转九个圈一停，闭目看光，共转三十六周。退阴符是二目睁开，从上午位向左下右上转睛，走午、卯、子、午、酉位，午、卯、子、酉转六圈一停，闭目看光，共转二十四周。如此将真宝化成甘露，吞下绛宫送入丹田。

古人称大药过关服食为金液还丹。金液是经过大周天乾坤交媾，进阳火、退阴符所化成的甘露，是由真性与真命二者合一化成的真种脱落而来，非练功初阶和小周天时产生的津液或玉液可比，是更高级的精炁神的结晶体，是能够成为圣胎、婴儿的东西。《性命圭旨》有诗云：“金满三车夺圣机，冲开九窍过曹溪，迢迢运入昆仑顶，万道霞光射紫微”，得其形象。

After the twelve steps of practice before, the true breath of pure yang condenses in the lower elixir field and the śarīra has been refined — what is called “the elixir ripe and the pearl numinous,” the horse-like retraction, the six scenes appearing one after another; to gather the Great Medicine of the Golden Elixir, one needs the work of seven days of great absorption. The period of seven days is also only approximate. This step is the main practice of the Greater Celestial Circuit.

This stage of work is generally divided into three parts: “the fire sufficient and the fire stopped,” “the Great Medicine produced” and “passing the gates and ingesting.” When the six roots quake and the six scenes appear in turn, that is when the fire is sufficient; the yang light appearing twice is the scene of stopping the fire; when the yang light appears three times, the Great Medicine can be gathered; when the yang light appears four times, the Great Medicine overflows out of the body and turns into Posterior Heaven essence with form, and the previous work is wasted. On the production of the Great Medicine, Wu Chongxu’s The Common Origin of Immortals and Buddhas gives four accounts in all: (1) produced after union: by gazing intently with the light of the eyes, the spirit in the heart and the original breath in the kidneys are brought into union, that is, the union of water and fire, and naturally ball together into the Great Medicine, shaped like a pearl of fire. (2) produced after coaxing: with the light of the two eyes coaxing the True Intention above, the Great Medicine naturally follows and appears below. (3) produced after stillness and absorption: because the light of the eyes gazes intently, spirit and breath both obtain the mechanism of settling, and so the original breath takes form; from stillness movement is born, and it is born within the body. (4) produced after the breath settles: the Prior Heaven Original Spirit and original breath obtain the mechanism of settling because the light of the eyes gazes intently, the naturally moving fire of Posterior Heaven also obtains the mechanism of settling and has a place to return to; the True Breath therefore settles, and the Great Medicine is naturally produced. These four methods all use the light of the eyes to draw it in, which is why it is called “the treasury of the true Dharma eye,” the secret mechanism of heaven for gathering the Great Medicine. As for the Great Medicine passing the gates and being ingested, there is the work of inhale, lick, contract, close, the secret mechanism of five dragons bearing up the holy one, and also the method of “clamping the nose and leading the ox” to prevent leakage and so on, all explained in detail in this step. But the use of the wooden seat, nose clamping and similar methods in the Wu-Liu lineage is considered overly cumbersome.

In The Common Origin of Immortals and Buddhas, Wu Chongxu stresses again and again that the Great Medicine passing the gates must “follow its own impulse, with the subtle intention of mutual awareness” (following its own impulse, with a slight intention of each being aware of the other), “the Great Medicine rises and descends by the turning of the True Intention, just as the wheel of heaven circles by the turning of the heart of heaven” (the Great Medicine relies on the rotation of the True Intention to rise and fall, as the heavenly bodies rely on the turning of the heart of heaven to circle), “the essential in its moments of moving and staying is only to go along with what is natural” (in its timing of going and resting, the only essential is to follow what is natural). This is the right practice of passing the gates.

In the formula of passing the gates and ingesting in the Greater Celestial Circuit, the more important elements are advancing the yang fire and withdrawing the yin tally. According to what Niu Jinbao transmitted: advancing the yang fire is done with the eyes closed, turning the eyeballs from below up to the left, going through the positions zi, mao, wu, you, zi; after nine circles of zi, mao, wu, you, pause, close the eyes and look at the light; thirty-six circles in all. Withdrawing the yin tally is done with the two eyes open, turning the eyeballs from the wu position at the top down to the left and up to the right, going through the positions wu, mao, zi, wu, you (so in the original); after six circles of wu, mao, zi, you, pause, close the eyes and look at the light; twenty-four circles in all. In this way the true treasure is turned into sweet dew, swallowed down to the Crimson Palace and sent into the elixir field.

The ancients called the Great Medicine passing the gates and being ingested “the golden liquor reverted elixir.” The golden liquor is the sweet dew transformed through the union of Qian and Kun in the Greater Celestial Circuit, advancing the yang fire and withdrawing the yin tally; it comes from the true seed that drops off after true inner nature and true life have united into one, and cannot be compared with the saliva or jade liquor produced at the early stage of practice and in the Lesser Celestial Circuit; it is a higher crystallization of essence, breath and spirit, something that can become the holy embryo, the infant. The Jade Tablet of Inner Nature and Life has a poem: “Gold fills the three carts, seizing the holy mechanism; breaking open the nine openings, it passes Caoxi; carried far up to the summit of Kunlun, ten thousand rays of rosy light shoot at Ziwei.” (The Golden Elixir fills the three carts and seizes the holy mechanism, breaks open the nine openings and passes Caoxi — the Governing Vessel; carried far up to the summit of Kunlun, ten thousand rays of rosy light shoot straight at the Ziwei star.) This poem paints its image.

Key Terms

  • The Great Medicine passes the gates: the śarīra (the Great Medicine) rises through the three passes of the Caudal Funnel, the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow to the Muddy Pellet, unites with inner nature, turns into golden liquor and descends into the lower elixir field; the main practice of the Greater Celestial Circuit.
  • Ingesting: the golden liquor true seed swallowed through the Mysterious Breast, the Twelve-Storied Tower and the Crimson Palace into the lower elixir field.
  • Seven days of gathering work: gathering the Great Medicine is called seven days but is in fact obtained on the sixth (Qiu Ling’s note: seven days is also approximate).
  • The six roots quaking: the roots of nose, tongue and intention draw back in, the roots of ear and eye resound, the root of desire stirs by itself, so that the śarīra is shaken into lively flow.
  • Lower Magpie Bridge, upper Magpie Bridge: the danger of the Great Medicine going down from the Caudal Funnel to the grain path; the danger of the true seed leaking out by the Hall of Impression and the root of the nose.
  • Wooden base seat: a soft seat shaped like a steamed bun and wrapped in cotton, pressed against the grain path.
  • Mulainian: a wooden implement for plugging the nostrils.
  • Five dragons bearing up the holy one: the True Intention (earth, number five) and the Original Spirit (the dragon) bear up the true treasure as it rises; a metaphor drawn from the story of the Dark Emperor Zhenwu attaining the Way by giving up his body.
  • Three carts: the goat cart (light and slow), the deer cart (light and fast), the ox cart (with force), metaphors for the force of turning the eyes in passing the Caudal Funnel, the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow.
  • Inhale, lick, contract, close: inhale — drawing in through the nose to support the rise; lick — the tongue against the palate, the eyes turning; contract — the Daoist companion pinching the Governing Vessel; close — closing the three places of grain path, nostrils and Opening of Life and Death.
  • Fengfu: at the center of the back of the neck, one cun within the hairline.
  • Inner nature and life in the same palace: the treasure of the breath of life reaches the Muddy Pellet and unites with the treasure of the spirit of inner nature.
  • Prior Heaven true seed: the true treasure of spirit and breath united in the brain, condensed through advancing yang and withdrawing yin.
  • Golden liquor reverted elixir: the golden liquor true seed dropping into the lower elixir field, that is, the ingesting of the Great Medicine.
  • Female stamen, male stamen: the light of wisdom produced by the breath of inner nature and the golden light produced by the breath of life; the two lights uniting is bearing fruit.
  • The yang light appearing four times: the fire not stopped when it should have been, the Great Medicine overflowing out of the body, all previous work wasted.

Putting It into Practice

  1. This is a record, not a practice: the techniques of this step (pressing with foreign objects, plugging the nose, being rubbed by another person, lifting the perineum, straightening the waist while inhaling) all carry clear risks; this site presents them only as a documentary record.
  2. Qiu Ling’s attitude: the wooden seat and nose-clamping methods “are considered overly cumbersome”; the right practice of passing the gates lies in “following its own impulse” and “going along with what is natural.”
  3. A principle that runs through the whole book: “wait for it to move by itself and then lead it; do not lead it to make it move” — wait for it to move by itself, do not force it to move. This applies to any practice of quiet sitting.
  4. Twelve steps are enough: Step Twelve has already made clear that practicing up to that point lengthens one’s years and increases one’s life; from Step Thirteen on, special conditions are required, and ordinary people need not pursue it.

Translator's Notes

  1. In the original, this step has one formula, three figures (Figures 29 to 31), four passages of explanation and five questions and answers; we number them §1–§9 (§4 contains the last two passages of the original’s explanation; Figures 30 and 31 are inserted within §4 in the original text).
  2. 迂阻, “迂 the treasure of the spirit of true inner nature,” 若迂明师, 迂妇女同眠, “迂 the breath of inner nature and unite into one breath”: 迂 should be a mistake for 遇 (“to meet”); reproduced as is, and translated as “meet.”
  3. Mulainian (木来年): the name of an implement in the original; its exact form is unknown; the original figure draws it as two sword-shaped objects. The “plugging the nostrils tightly with a mulainian” of Step Twelve refers to this object.
  4. “The tongue pressed against the palate” (舌抵上颚) is used alongside “the tongue licks the palate” (舌舐上腭) elsewhere; 颚 and 腭 are each as in the original.
  5. “Five dragons bearing up the holy one”: the legend of the Great Emperor Zhenwu (the Supreme Emperor of the Dark Heaven), who cultivated the Way and attained perfection and was borne aloft by five dragons; Zhao uses it as a metaphor.
  6. The route of withdrawing the yin tally from Niu Jinbao given in Qiu Ling’s note, “going through the positions wu, mao, zi, wu, you,” does not entirely agree with the “wu, mao, zi, you” given just before; so in the original, reproduced as is.
  7. “Clamping the nose and leading the ox”: that is, plugging the nose with the mulainian; “leading the ox” borrows the image of a rope threaded through an ox’s nose.
  8. “Li Xu’an” was Wu Chongxu’s teacher; the two Perfected Cao and Qiu are Cao Huanyang and Qiu Chuji.
