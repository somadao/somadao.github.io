The Sixteen Steps of Method Formulas · 第七步 翕聚祖炁

Step Seven · Gathering In the Ancestral Breath

The formula of Step Seven, gathering in the ancestral breath — guarding the center and embracing the One is the whole practice; when the ancestral breath gathers, inner nature and life are one

About This Chapter

After the training of the first six steps, essence, breath and spirit are full and unite, returning to the Ancestral Opening, and a ball of light appears before the eyes — Zhao Bichen draws it as “〇” and calls it the Mysterious Pass, which is also the light of wisdom, the light of inner nature, the breath of the Prior Heaven true One. This step tells how to hold this light together, refine it and draw it back in, which is called “gathering in the ancestral breath” (xi: to draw together, to gather in).

  1. What the Mysterious Pass is: the light of the essence, breath and spirit refined in the first six steps, rising and appearing in front of the Ancestral Opening; it is the Supreme Ultimate within the body, the original face. “When the mechanism stirs it becomes an opening; when the mechanism rests it is vague and boundless again” — it appears only at the utmost of stillness, and vanishes as soon as a thought arises.
  2. Do not draw it in as soon as you see it: wait until the Mysterious Pass “suddenly moves and goes far off” before gathering it in and drawing it into the Crimson Palace; drawing it in the moment you see it causes pain in the head.
  3. The method of drawing it in: when the light is about to go up or down, do not let the eyes follow the light, but turn one circle through zi (below), mao, wu (above), you and back to zi (Figure 21); after turning, close the eyes; the mouth is full of golden liquor, and the heart, spirit and intention send the breath of the true One, together with the golden liquor, down to the true Cavity of Breath — this is called “sending it back to the earthen pot and sealing it tight.”
  4. Telling true from false: the white light seen as soon as the eyes are closed by someone just beginning to sit is the “yin spirit” and cannot be used; only the light refined through the first six steps — gathering and replenishing the outer and inner medicines and returning the essence to replenish the brain — is “the light of wisdom of the Yang Spirit.” Red light is called the “blood Mysterious Pass”; only white light is the true Mysterious Pass.
  5. The three teachings return to one: the Daoists call it “guarding the One,” the Confucians “the one thread,” the Buddhists “returning to the One” — all speak of this one “〇.”

Qiu Ling says this chapter’s account of the light of wisdom and the method of gathering it in “is rarely found in Daoist books and elixir classics, and only one who has been through it can understand it”; he adds the gradations of color of the light of wisdom (pale red — white — gold), warns that one must “strictly avoid pursuing or admiring it,” that “the eyes see the Mysterious Pass but the heart has no Mysterious Pass — only then is one not attached to form,” and presents the five types into which modern somatic science classifies the inner light seen in stillness.

Safety note (translator): Seeing light before the eyes is something that appears naturally in stillness; do not deliberately pursue it or “stare” at it with the eyes, or you may easily become tense, get headaches or even hallucinate. Zhao too says “one must not draw it in the moment one sees it, which makes the head feel pain.” Turn the eyes gently when drawing in the light. If you experience persistent visual hallucinations of light, headaches, insomnia or emotional disturbance, stop practicing and see a doctor.

Figure 20 · Gathering In the Ancestral Breath

In the figure a long-bearded Daoist in a cross-collared robe sits upright cross-legged, holding a flowering stem, with a sash hanging at his side. To the upper left of the head, a circle contains the jade hare (the moon); to the upper right, a circle contains the golden crow (the sun) — sun and moon are the two eyes, and also yin and yang, dragon and tiger; when sun and moon join, the ancestral breath gathers into that ball of light in front of the two eyes.

Formula
Original (Chinese)

守中抱一是全功，

祖炁聚会性命同。

坎戊离己日月合，

龙虎二体光中升。

Guarding the center and embracing the One is the whole practice; when the ancestral breath gathers, inner nature and life return to one body. The wu earth within Kan and the ji earth within Li (see wu and ji) — that is, sun and moon (the two eyes) — unite; the two bodies of dragon and tiger (the two breaths of yin and yang) rise within this light.

§1
Original (Chinese)

本书第一步口诀至第四步口诀，为下手法，至第八步口诀，为转手法。因要坚固命蒂，先须还精补脑。功夫逐节详明，了然于心，方可从事。纵有真正天机，不可举办，总要日期延长，始能夺得天地造化之机，而真种可得矣。上阳子曰：“求师固由我，而求药不由我”。若平素演习不纯熟，则临机茫然，无所措手，此强不知以为知之过也。采取药物当用周天火候锻炼，务使尽化为炁，精炁满足，而我之性命才能和合归一。盖因每次添加周天真火，则先天精、炁、神归一，顿觉发生这个〇，仿佛白日当空，在我目前。此是通过前六步功法锻炼而得，皆自身之精气，名曰玄关。玄关者至玄至妙之机关也，即人身中太极是也。经云：“此窍正在乾之上，坤之下，震之西，兑之东，八脉九窍空悬一穴，即我身中天地之正中也”。邱祖云：“但著在形体上摸索皆不是，亦不可离形体而向外寻求，因其机发始成窍，机息复渺茫”。《性命圭旨》云：“空洞无涯是玄窍，知而不守是功夫”。静极玄关出现，若有一念，便落后天，倏然不见，一无所有，如再苦求，了不可得，因著色相故也。我师了然、了空传我时曰：“此玄关是前六步功所采精、炁、神和合归祖窍一处，现出这个〇，乃是先天真一之炁”。魁一子曰：“精、炁、神足则上升，而发现真性光，即真一之慧也”。常见明星或电光，皆为得玄关之预兆。玄关在虚空之中，发现于祖窍之前，为真一之炁，即我本来面目，一片虚空是也。故曰：“先天真一之炁，自虚无中来”。但见玄关忽动离远，而后我方可翕聚此祖炁，收入我身绛宫，真炁往来跳动，口中清香无比为证。不可一见立收，致使脑部感受痛苦，自寻苦恼。虚空乃鸿濛已判，《易》之乾一也，儒曰一贯，道曰守一，释曰归一，皆本和合之理。一贯者以其一而贯乎其中，守一者以其一而守乎其中，归一者以其一而归乎其中。故祖炁吞入腹内，丹经谓之得一而万事毕者此也。

The formulas of Steps One to Four of this book are the “methods for setting to work” (methods for entering the gate); up to the formula of Step Eight are the “methods for turning the hand” (methods for moving on to a higher stage). For to make the stem of life firm, one must first return the essence to replenish the brain. The practice must be understood clearly section by section, grasped thoroughly in the heart, before it can be done. Even with the true mechanism of heaven, one must not act rashly; one must extend the period of days, and only then can one seize the mechanism of creation of heaven and earth and obtain the true seed. Shangyangzi (Chen Zhixu) said: “Seeking a teacher is certainly up to me; seeking the medicine is not up to me.” (Finding a teacher depends on me; when the medicine is produced does not.) If one has not practiced until fluent in ordinary times, then when the moment comes one is at a loss and does not know how to set to work — this is the fault of pretending to know what one does not know. The medicine gathered should be forged with the fire phasing of the circuit until it is wholly transformed into breath; only when the essence and breath are full can my inner nature and life unite and return to one. For each time the true fire of the circuit is added, the Prior Heaven essence, breath and spirit return to one, and suddenly one feels this one “〇” appear, like the sun in the sky, before my eyes. This is obtained through the training of the first six steps; it is all one’s own essence and breath, and is called the “Mysterious Pass.” The Mysterious Pass is the most mysterious and wondrous mechanism, the Supreme Ultimate within the human body. The scriptures say: “This opening is right above Qian, below Kun, west of Zhen and east of Dui (the exact center of above, below, left and right); among the eight vessels and nine openings a cavity hangs in emptiness — it is the exact center of the heaven and earth within my body.” Patriarch Qiu (Qiu Chuji) said: “Groping about in the bodily form is all wrong; nor may one leave the bodily form to seek it outside. For ‘when the mechanism stirs it becomes an opening; when the mechanism rests it is vague and boundless again.'” The Jade Tablet of Inner Nature and Life says: “Empty, hollow and boundless is the mysterious opening; knowing without holding on is the practice.” (What is empty, hollow and without limit is the mysterious opening; to know it without clinging to it is the practice.)

When stillness reaches its utmost, the Mysterious Pass appears; if a single thought arises, one falls into Posterior Heaven, and the Mysterious Pass vanishes in an instant, leaving nothing at all; if one then strives painfully for it, one gets it even less — because one is attached to form. When my teachers Liaoran and Liaokong transmitted it to me, they said: “This Mysterious Pass is the essence, breath and spirit gathered in the first six steps, united and returned to the one place of the Ancestral Opening; this one ‘〇’ that appears is the breath of the Prior Heaven true One.” Kuiyizi said: “When essence, breath and spirit are full, they rise, revealing the light of true inner nature, which is the wisdom of the true One.” Often seeing bright stars or flashes of lightning are signs of obtaining the Mysterious Pass. The Mysterious Pass is in empty space and appears in front of the Ancestral Opening; it is the breath of the true One, my original face, one vast emptiness. Hence it is said: “The breath of the Prior Heaven true One comes from emptiness and non-being.”

Only when one sees the Mysterious Pass suddenly move and go far off may I gather in this ancestral breath and draw it into the Crimson Palace of my body; the true breath leaps back and forth, and the mouth has an incomparable clear fragrance — that is the proof. One must not draw it in the moment one sees it, which makes the head feel pain — seeking trouble for oneself. Empty space is when the primordial chaos has already divided; it is the “Qian one” of the Book of Changes. The Confucians call it “the one thread,” the Daoists “guarding the One,” the Buddhists “returning to the One,” all based on the principle of union: “the one thread” is threading through with this one; “guarding the One” is guarding within with this one; “returning to the One” is returning within with this one. So swallowing the ancestral breath into the belly is what the elixir classics mean by “obtain the One and the ten thousand affairs are done.”

§2
Original (Chinese)

问曰：师言玄关即是这个〇，而万法三教归一亦是这个〇，敢问二者理由安在？既炼之后，有何功效及对我身中有何益处？

答曰：此〇是前六步大功炼成，即先天祖炁，是精、炁、神上升之光也，现于祖窍之前，无的可说，强名曰道。不知祖窍之功，不生此祖炁，则不知鼎炉道路；不生此炁，不知下手采药，精虫不化真祖炁，则不能升于脑部；不知开通八脉，则真炁不能运行；不知四相和合，则真祖炁不聚；不知进阳火退阴符，则真祖炁不能发光。第七步祖炁翕聚之法，即守中抱一功夫。从前炼就的精炁神之炁，上升于祖窍之前，即是性光。久之性光发散，不知所之，复次又见，仍与前同，洁白无滓，若不得翕聚之法，任其飞散，当面错过，坐失造化真机，而使前功尽弃，未免可惜。若得金公木母和合，日月归并一处，两眼珠并合行回光返照之法，照见真炁之光盘旋于祖窍之前，自然翕聚此炁不散，愈聚光聚圆。此光圆圆陀陀时，是戊己二土、龙虎二气合并，而刀圭成矣。坎离交而天地泰，龙虎交而戊己合，戊己合为一体，则四象会合中宫，大药生矣。《易》曰：“天地氤氲，万物化醇，男女媾精，万物化生”。天地以阴阳交媾而生万物，丹道以阴阳交媾，而产大药。大药生出，药嫩准飞走，无法制止。得师真诀者，收归我有，化成金液大丹，送归土釜。

Question: Teacher, you say the Mysterious Pass is this one “〇,” and that the ten thousand methods and the three teachings returning to one are also this one “〇.” May I ask what the principle of the two is? Once it is refined, what effect does it have, and what benefit to my body?

Answer: This “〇” is refined by the great work of the first six steps; it is the Prior Heaven ancestral breath, the light of essence, breath and spirit rising, appearing in front of the Ancestral Opening; there is nothing that can be said about it, and it is reluctantly given the name “the Way.” Without knowing the work of the Ancestral Opening (Step One), this ancestral breath cannot be produced, and one does not know the road of the tripod and furnace (Step Two); without producing this breath, one does not know how to set to work gathering the medicine, the seminal essence cannot be transformed into true ancestral breath and cannot rise to the brain (Step Four); without knowing how to open the eight vessels, the true breath cannot circulate (Step Three); without knowing the union of the four forms, the true ancestral breath does not gather (Step Five); without knowing how to advance the yang fire and withdraw the yin tally, the true ancestral breath cannot emit light (Step Six). The method of gathering in the ancestral breath in Step Seven is the work of guarding the center and embracing the One. The breath of essence, breath and spirit refined before rises to the front of the Ancestral Opening — that is the light of inner nature. After a while the light of inner nature disperses and one does not know where it has gone; later it appears again, just as before, white and flawless. If one does not know the method of gathering in, and lets it fly off and scatter, missing it face to face, one forfeits the true mechanism of creation and wastes all previous work — a great pity. If one obtains “the union of Metal Father and Wood Mother” (metal and wood, feeling and inner nature uniting), sun and moon (the two eyes) return to one place, the two eyeballs are brought together, and one practices the method of turning the light around to shine back, seeing the light of the true breath circling in front of the Ancestral Opening, then naturally one gathers this breath in and keeps it from scattering, and the more one gathers it, the rounder the light. When this light is perfectly round, it is the two earths wu and ji and the two breaths of dragon and tiger joined, and the “knife-point” is formed. When Kan and Li meet, heaven and earth are in Tai (free flow); when dragon and tiger meet, wu and ji unite; when wu and ji unite into one body, the four images meet in the central palace, and the Great Medicine is produced. The Book of Changes says: “Heaven and earth intermingle, and the ten thousand things are refined; man and woman join their essences, and the ten thousand things are born.” (The two breaths of heaven and earth blend, and the ten thousand things are refined; man and woman join their essence and breath, and the ten thousand things come to life.) Heaven and earth produce the ten thousand things through the union of yin and yang; the elixir path produces the Great Medicine through the union of yin and yang. When the Great Medicine is first produced, the medicine is tender and is sure to fly off, and nothing can stop it; one who has obtained a teacher’s true formula can take it into his possession, transform it into the great elixir of golden liquor, and send it back to the earthen pot (the lower elixir field).

§3
Original (Chinese)

问曰：何为大药？如何化为金液？如何归土釜？

答曰：大药者是内外二药而生真一之光也。如得四象和合，日月合并，久之而甘露下降，会采药者得金液，不会采药者得玉液。送归土釜者，即翕聚之法诀也。炼前六步功合而为一，才得先天真一之性光出现。真炁发于外为光，光嫩则恍恍惚惚不定于中宫。静极则光现，由小渐大，复归于无，或自大化小，又变为三光，及见眉月者，均可认为精炁不甚充足之验。忽光要向上或向下走，眼不可随光去看，赶紧翕聚收回，归我存蓄，将心意定住，按眼之部位转，下为子，左为卯，上为午，右为酉，复落于子（图21），转完将眼一闭，此时口内金液满足，心、神、意将转的先天真一之炁向下一送，金液随真炁，送在真炁穴，此谓之送归土釜牢封固。如将金液送下丹田，腹响如雷，即将两眼合并，久视真炁穴，而前日已失之光复来，此为翕聚祖炁之法。张紫阳云：“既驱二物归黄道，争得金丹不解生”，即此意也。吕祖云：“二物会时为道本，五行全处得丹名”，亦此意也。我师了空曰：“性命非一日炼得，速用翕聚收内藏”。魁一子曰：“翕聚真炁祖窍前，旋转收归入丹田”。人有两个身，一为有形有象之色身；一为无形无象之法身。色身者即本身也，乃是父母遗体。法身者即前六步功炼出之先天真一之炁，由无极而太极，生出此〇，是我自己真性。学者由此真性入手，养成乾元之体，则灵台露出一点真灵，形依神而形不坏，神依性而神不灭。故知此真性，必能尽性，以至于命。各教曰教外别传之旨，所秘密者，即此先天一点真阳炁也。要知这一点真阳之炁，生于天地之前，长于万物之先，圆圆陀陀，光灼灼，净倮倮，赤洒洒，丝毫不挂，一尘不染，就是目前这个〇，是由我性命双修炼出来的真性，非是枯坐空修性也。

Question: What is the Great Medicine? How is it transformed into golden liquor? How does it return to the earthen pot?

Answer: The Great Medicine is the light of the true One produced by the union of the outer and inner medicines. If one obtains the union of the four images and the joining of sun and moon, after a while sweet dew descends: one who knows how to gather the medicine obtains “golden liquor,” one who does not obtains “jade liquor.” “Sending it back to the earthen pot” is the formula of gathering in. Only by refining the work of the first six steps until they unite into one can one see the light of inner nature of the Prior Heaven true One appear. When the true breath issues outward, it is light; when the light is tender, it is dim and indistinct and cannot settle in the central palace. When stillness reaches its utmost, the light appears: growing from small to large and then returning to nothing; or changing from large to small and then becoming three balls of light; or appearing as a crescent moon like an eyebrow — all of these can be taken as signs that the essence and breath are not yet quite full.

If the light suddenly starts to go up or down, the eyes must not follow the light, but quickly gather it in and draw it back to be stored as mine: settle the heart and intention, and turn by the positions of the eyes — below is zi, left is mao, above is wu, right is you, then come back down to zi (Figure 21); after turning, close the eyes; now the mouth is full of golden liquor, and the heart, spirit and intention send the turning breath of the Prior Heaven true One downward, the golden liquor following the true breath to the true Cavity of Breath — this is called “sending it back to the earthen pot and sealing it tight.” When the golden liquor has been sent down to the elixir field, the belly rumbles like thunder; then bring the two eyes together and look at the true Cavity of Breath for a long time, and the light lost in previous days returns again — this is the method of gathering in the ancestral breath. Zhang Ziyang said: “Once the two things are driven back to the yellow path, how could the Golden Elixir fail to be born?” (Once the two things — inner nature and life, lead and mercury — are driven back to the yellow path — the central palace — how could the Golden Elixir fail to form?) That is what this means. Patriarch Lü said: “When the two things meet, that is the root of the Way; where the five phases are complete, the elixir gets its name.” That is also what this means. My teacher Liaokong said: “Inner nature and life are not refined in a day; quickly gather them in and store them within the body.” Kuiyizi said: “Gather in the true breath in front of the Ancestral Opening, and turning, draw it back into the elixir field.”

A person has two bodies: one is the “form body,” with shape and appearance; the other is the “Dharma-body,” without shape or appearance. The form body is this body, the remains left by father and mother. The Dharma-body is the breath of the Prior Heaven true One refined by the work of the first six steps, which from the Ultimateless becomes the Supreme Ultimate and gives birth to this one “〇”; it is my own true inner nature. When the student enters from this true inner nature and nourishes it into “the body of the Qian origin” (a body of pure yang), a point of true numinosity shows in the Spirit Tower (the heart); when form depends on spirit, form does not decay; when spirit depends on inner nature, spirit does not perish. So whoever knows this true inner nature will surely fulfill inner nature and so arrive at life. The aim of what the various teachings call “a separate transmission outside the teaching,” what they keep secret and do not proclaim, is this one point of the Prior Heaven breath of true yang. Know that this one point of the breath of true yang was born before heaven and earth and grew before the ten thousand things; perfectly round, blazing with light, clean and pure, bare and naked, not a thread on it, not a speck of dust — it is this one “〇” before the eyes. It is true inner nature refined through my dual cultivation of nature and life, not obtained by sitting dry and cultivating inner nature in emptiness.

Figure 21 · Gathering In the Ancestral Breath (Turning the Eyes to Draw In the Light)

A dotted circle with “eyes” written in the middle: at the bottom “zi” (marked one), on the right “mao” (marked two), at the top “wu” (marked three), on the left “you” (marked four); an arrow on the circle points from mao toward wu. This is the turning of the eyes to draw in the light described in §3: starting from zi, through mao, wu and you, coming back down to zi, then closing the eyes and sending it down. (The text says “left is mao, right is you,” from the practitioner’s own point of view; the figure is drawn face to face, so mao is on the right.)

§4
Original (Chinese)

问曰：此〇是父母未生前真面目？其形象若何？

Question: Is this “〇” the true face before father and mother were born? What is its appearance?

Answer: Everyone who cultivates the true practice of the dual cultivation of nature and life faithfully, diligently and tirelessly, pressing on with practice, obtains this light of wisdom. Red light is called the “blood Mysterious Pass”; only white light is the true Mysterious Pass. For it has no head and no tail, neither changes nor alters; it is neither nothing nor something, neither round nor square; it has no excess and no lack, neither decreases nor increases; it neither comes nor goes, neither perishes nor is born; it has no inside and no outside, is neither yellow nor red; it neither sends off nor welcomes, is neither white nor blue; it has no sound and no smell, seems present and seems absent, as if lost and as if kept; used, it moves; rolled up, it hides; it goes in and out at no fixed time, and its whereabouts are unknown. From ancient times to the present it stands alone and endures between heaven and earth; it is the center of the ten thousand things, the root of ceaseless generation, the source of ceaseless transformation; there is nothing in heaven, earth, humankind or things that does not depend on it to come into being. To recognize this circle is to recognize one’s original true face before father and mother gave birth to me.

This circle is one’s own essence, breath and spirit; when they are full, they rise, and the eyes see “three stars,” which is called “the three flowers gather at the summit”; going one step further, the light of true inner nature of wisdom and life appears at once, which is called “the five breaths return to the origin.” This light is my true inner nature; rising from this rung of the ladder, one has great creative power; then, proceeding step by step, one refines true life, and the two things, inner nature and life, are not hard to bring together; only then is the aim of the dual cultivation of nature and life reached — this is the most crucial first formula. From ancient times to the present, no matter how gifted and quick, getting everything right at one stroke, no one can work it out by himself; only through a true teacher’s oral transmission and heart-to-heart instruction can one grasp its mysteries. I have never heard of anyone who left his teacher (the original has 迁师, see the Translator’s Notes) and did without the formulas of the first six steps, yet became a sage, an immortal or a buddha. Those who cultivate the elixir must, in the period of “refining the self and laying the foundation,” verify for themselves that Steps One to Six are effective. Take the work of stillness and absorption: if one does not struggle desperately every day with the cognitive spirit (fighting stray thoughts), the breath does not sink, the monkey of the heart and the horse of the intention never pause, and it is hard to enter absorption. If the four images do not unite, the Mysterious Pass does not appear before the eyes, the Original Spirit does not find its place, and it cannot be called “the human heart dies and the Original Spirit lives.” To obtain the formula of a step without being able to verify its effect is the same as never having heard it.

The Old Man of Pure Yang (Lü Dongbin) said: “The road of long life is but a foot before your eyes; how many fools fail to wake up to it!” Liu Haichan said: “Those who look before their eyes do not recognize the true; they fabricate emptiness and lie still until death, all in vain, in darkness.” Chen Niwan said: “All day long it follows you, before your eyes.” Ma Danyang said: “It is right before the eyes, but people pay no heed.” The Buddhists say: “All morning face to face, yet you do not know who it is.” The Daoist teaching says: “The Way is near, yet one seeks it far off; the matter is easy, yet one seeks it in the difficult; one actually abandons the light and throws oneself into darkness — is that not too foolish!” The Confucians say: “The Way is not far from people; if people pursue a way that takes them far from people, it cannot be considered the Way.” All these speak of this one thing, the Mysterious Pass. Because it has no form and no image, it can be present in heaven, earth and humankind at once. This breath is the great treasure of heaven, earth and humankind; nourishing life and preserving life all depend on the presence of this breath. So heaven, obtaining this breath, endures long; earth, obtaining this breath, lasts long; sun, moon and stars, obtaining this breath, shine; grass and trees, obtaining this breath, return to the root and restore their life; the crane, obtaining this breath, can carry its embryo; the clam, obtaining this breath, can produce pearls; humans, obtaining this breath, can live long.

Question: Teacher, you say that when the Yang Spirit refined in the first six steps is full, the light of wisdom appears, and it is called the Mysterious Pass, also the Yang Spirit, also the original face — many names, all for this circle of light. When I first refined the practice of inner nature in Step One, there was also this circle of white light; is that the true inner nature of the Mysterious Pass?

Answer: What appears when practicing the first stage is the “yin spirit”: every time you close the eyes, white light appears before them — this is the yin spirit and cannot be used. If, from Step One to Step Six, you gather and replenish the outer and inner medicines and return the essence to replenish the brain, and refine the light of wisdom of the Yang Spirit, it is called the Mysterious Pass — only this is the true inner nature of the dual cultivation of nature and life. If what one refines is the yin spirit, then on closing the eyes “one contemplates emptiness and it is empty” (one looks at emptiness and it really is empty), and in the end one is governed by the cognitive spirit and achieves nothing up to old age and death. If one gathers the medicine through the work of the first six steps, cultivating nature and life together and refining the Yang Spirit, then “one contemplates emptiness and it is not empty” (it looks empty but in fact is not), and it is “the spirit that is not spirit-like.” This light of inner nature comes from the Ultimateless: when one’s original essence is full, it is there; when the original essence is insufficient, it is not. The dual cultivation of nature and life cultivates precisely this light of the breath of the Prior Heaven true One. Students must understand: the breath of the Prior Heaven true One must go from outside to inside, be sent back to the elixir field and sealed tight. Human existence depends wholly on the presiding of the Prior Heaven spirit-breath of emptiness and non-being: when the breath gathers and the spirit comes, one lives; when the breath scatters and the spirit departs, one dies. Hence it is said: spirit without breath cannot live; breath without spirit cannot die. If one does not know how to gather the medicine and goes so far as to refine the yin spirit, it is only the breath of turbid essence. When students have refined up to the work of Step Seven and can gather in the light of inner nature of the Prior Heaven true One, sending it with the saliva back to the earthen pot to unite and return to one with my true life — this is the method of “hibernating storage” of Step Eight, and only thus can one lengthen one’s years and increase one’s life.

Original note (Chinese)

【注释】此章论述慧光（性光）及翕聚之法甚详，为道书丹经所罕见，非过来人莫能懂得。

经前六步功法之锻炼，学者祖窍之前当有慧光出现，悬于虚空之中，古人以〇代之，赵氏称之为玄关。既称为玄关，只有虚极静笃时才出现，所谓“机发始成窍，机息复渺茫”是也。此〇乃先天真炁凝成，亦即精、炁、神充足及和合一处，才能出现在祖窍之前。

若精、炁、神充足，慧光便见圆明和稳定，若精、炁、神不足，则此光发散，或如眉月，或变小隐去。若能静定，慧光越显；若有杂念，或识神干扰，则此光便倏然不见。

初得慧光，一般为淡红色，即赵氏所谓血玄关是也；及现白光，方为正玄关；及见金光，称之为蟾光，是练功进入更高层次炼成舍利子所见之光。

常见明星或电光，固为得玄关之预兆，但已得慧光者，闭目已非黑暗一片，眼前常有恍惚、透明、幽深之景，用物掩住双目，亦可模糊略见事物轮廓。慧光千变万化，但切戒追求或欣尝。所谓眼见玄关而心无玄关，方为不着色相。

慧光悬于眼前，故古圣多说能知“眼前”二字，便得练功的诀窍。慧光悬于虚空，为真一之炁，是我本来面目，一片虚空，故古人说：“先天真一之炁，自虚无中来”。人有两个身：一为色身；一为法身。色身就是肉身，法身就是慧光。三教归一亦是这个〇，故道谓之丹，儒谓之仁，释谓之珠。

在世界现代人体科学研究中，人静中眼前看到的人体内光即性光被约略分为五大模式：一、光点型。这是好像小光点散在空间的忽明忽灭的模式，人们最初看到的内光，往往就是这种范围的形象。二、银河状型。看起来有很像朦胧的银河一般的光辉扩散的感觉。三、圆形型。稍呈圆形，没有散漫感。四、镜状型。边缘部分很清楚，圆形光辉好像一面圆镜浮在空间，是静止的模式。五、生物状型。有显明的圆明感，其周围部分像细胞的动作一般在蠕动着。除以上五种模式外，也有人看到比较特殊的模式，但大致上不出上述五种模式的范围。这五种模式中，以生物状型为最高级，密宗、瑜伽术和我国道家丹功，都以炼出这种型的内光，才算上乘。这对以千峰老人为代表的关于慧光形成及其形状的理论，提供了考证。

赵氏此第七步口诀，主要介绍聚光、炼光、收光之法。眼前性光既是先天真一之炁所凝成，即是最宝贵的生体能量，就不能让它在眼前飞散，应当聚之、炼之、收之归我存蓄，最后炼成金丹和阳神。当性光盘旋于祖窍之前向上或向下走，渐渐远去时，眼不可随光去看，赶紧翕聚收回，急用二目鼻根同时吸气，眼转意随看四正，意念由下子位左上到卯位至午位，再右下到酉位落于子位，转完将眼一闭，将转的炁光收回，此时津液满口，将津液随真炁送归丹田，谓之送归土釜牢封固。此即是翕聚祖炁收归腹中之法。

This chapter’s discussion of the light of wisdom (the light of inner nature) and of the method of gathering it in is very detailed; it is rarely found in Daoist books and elixir classics, and only one who has been through it can understand it.

After the training of the first six steps of practice, the student should find the light of wisdom appearing in front of the Ancestral Opening, hanging in empty space; the ancients represented it by “〇,” and Zhao calls it the Mysterious Pass. Since it is called the Mysterious Pass, it appears only at the utmost of emptiness and deep stillness — what is meant by “when the mechanism stirs it becomes an opening; when the mechanism rests it is vague and boundless again.” This “〇” is condensed from the Prior Heaven true breath; that is, only when essence, breath and spirit are full and united in one place can it appear in front of the Ancestral Opening.

If essence, breath and spirit are full, the light of wisdom appears round, bright and stable; if essence, breath and spirit are insufficient, the light disperses, or looks like a crescent moon, or shrinks and disappears. The more settled one is in stillness, the clearer the light of wisdom; if there are stray thoughts, or the cognitive spirit interferes, the light vanishes in an instant.

When the light of wisdom is first obtained, it is generally pale red, which is what Zhao calls the “blood Mysterious Pass”; only when white light appears is it the true Mysterious Pass; when golden light is seen, it is called “toad light,” the light seen when practice enters a higher level and the śarīra is refined.

Often seeing bright stars or flashes of lightning is certainly a sign of obtaining the Mysterious Pass; but for one who has already obtained the light of wisdom, closing the eyes is no longer total darkness: before the eyes there is often a dim, transparent, deep scene, and even with the eyes covered one can vaguely see the outlines of things. The light of wisdom changes in a thousand ways, but one must strictly avoid pursuing or admiring it. As it is said, “the eyes see the Mysterious Pass but the heart has no Mysterious Pass” — only then is one not attached to form.

The light of wisdom hangs before the eyes, which is why the ancient sages often said: whoever understands the two words “before the eyes” has obtained the key to the practice. The light of wisdom hangs in empty space; it is the breath of the true One, my original face, one vast emptiness; hence the ancients said: “The breath of the Prior Heaven true One comes from emptiness and non-being.” A person has two bodies: one is the form body, the other the Dharma-body. The form body is the flesh body; the Dharma-body is the light of wisdom. The three teachings returning to one is also this one “〇”: the Daoists call it the “elixir,” the Confucians “humaneness,” the Buddhists the “pearl.”

In modern research on somatic science around the world, the inner light of the body (the light of inner nature) that people see before their eyes in stillness has been broadly divided into five major patterns: first, the point-of-light type: like small points of light scattered in space, flickering on and off; the inner light people first see is often of this kind. Second, the Milky Way type: looking very much like a hazy Milky Way, with a feeling of radiance spreading out. Third, the round type: somewhat round, without a sense of diffuseness. Fourth, the mirror type: with very clear edges, a round radiance like a round mirror floating in the air; a still pattern. Fifth, the organic type: with a distinct sense of round brightness, its edges wriggling like the activity of cells. Besides these five patterns, some people see rather special patterns, but they generally do not go beyond the range of the five above. Of these five patterns the organic type is the highest; Tantric Buddhism, yoga and our country’s Daoist elixir practice all consider it superior to refine this type of inner light. This provides corroboration for the theory, represented by the Old Man of Qianfeng, of how the light of wisdom forms and what shape it takes.

Zhao’s formula of Step Seven mainly presents the methods of gathering the light, refining the light and drawing in the light. Since the light of inner nature before the eyes is condensed from the breath of the Prior Heaven true One, it is the most precious life energy, and must not be allowed to fly off and scatter before the eyes; one should gather it, refine it and draw it in, storing it as one’s own, and finally refine it into the Golden Elixir and the Yang Spirit. When the light of inner nature circles in front of the Ancestral Opening, going up or down and gradually moving away, the eyes must not follow the light but quickly gather it in and draw it back: quickly breathe in with the two eyes and the root of the nose together, the eyes turning and the intention following, watching the four cardinal points — the intention goes from the zi position below, up to the left to the mao position, to the wu position, then down to the right to the you position, and back down to the zi position; after turning, close the eyes and draw back the turning breath-light. Now the mouth is full of saliva; send the saliva with the true breath back to the elixir field — this is called “sending it back to the earthen pot and sealing it tight.” This is the method of gathering in the ancestral breath and drawing it back into the belly.

Key Terms

  • Gathering in (xiju): xi means to draw together, to gather in; holding together and drawing back the light of wisdom before the eyes.
  • Mysterious Pass: for Zhao, the light (〇) refined by the first six steps and appearing in front of the Ancestral Opening — the breath of the Prior Heaven true One, the Supreme Ultimate within the human body.
  • Blood Mysterious Pass, true Mysterious Pass, toad light: the light of wisdom first appearing pale red is the blood Mysterious Pass; white light is the true Mysterious Pass; golden light is called toad light (Qiu Ling’s note).
  • Yin spirit, Yang Spirit: the white light a beginner sees on closing the eyes is the yin spirit — “one contemplates emptiness and it is empty” — and cannot be used; what is refined by gathering the medicine through the first six steps is the light of wisdom of the Yang Spirit — “one contemplates emptiness and it is not empty.”
  • Methods for setting to work, methods for turning the hand: Steps One to Four are the entry-level methods for setting to work; up to Step Eight are the methods for turning the hand toward a higher stage.
  • Metal Father and Wood Mother: metal and wood, feeling and inner nature; the joining of the two eyes is also called metal and wood joined.
  • Knife-point: the character 圭 formed by the two earths wu and ji (土 + 土), referring to the first formation of the elixir.
  • Earthen pot: the lower elixir field; “sending it back to the earthen pot and sealing it tight” means sending the light and saliva into the lower elixir field to be sealed and stored.
  • Golden liquor, jade liquor: when sweet dew descends, one who knows how to gather the medicine obtains golden liquor, one who does not obtains jade liquor.
  • Form body, Dharma-body: the flesh body born of father and mother is the form body; the light of wisdom, the true inner nature refined through cultivation, is the Dharma-body.
  • The three teachings return to one: the Daoist “guarding the One,” the Confucian “one thread” and the Buddhist “returning to the One” all refer to this one “〇.”
  • Hibernating storage: the method of Step Eight, sending the gathered light of inner nature with the saliva back to the earthen pot to unite with true life.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Don’t seek the light: the light appears naturally when essence, breath and spirit are full; “if a single thought arises, one falls into Posterior Heaven and it vanishes in an instant” — pursuing it only makes it harder to obtain.
  2. See without attaching: Qiu Ling’s reminder is the most important — “the eyes see the Mysterious Pass but the heart has no Mysterious Pass”: even when you see it, do not admire it or cling to it.
  3. Don’t rush to draw it in: Zhao says “one must not draw it in the moment one sees it”; wait until it moves and is about to leave.
  4. Draw it in gently: the eyes do not follow the light; the intention turns one light circle from zi (below) through mao, wu and you back to zi, the eyes close, and it is sent down along with the saliva, the intention resting on the elixir field.
  5. Recognize the yin spirit: the white light seen when just beginning to sit is nothing to bother about; it is not the Mysterious Pass this step speaks of.
  6. The foundation is the first six steps: Zhao says again and again that without personal verification of the first six steps, there is nothing to say about this step.

Translator's Notes

  1. In the original, this step has one formula, two figures, one passage of explanation and four questions and answers, with a passage on the method of replenishing the spirit in between; we number them §1–§6. Figures 20 and 21 have the same title in the original, “Gathering In the Ancestral Breath”; this site adds “(Turning the Eyes to Draw In the Light)” to Figure 21 to distinguish them.
  2. “Never heard of anyone who 迁师”: the character 迁 (“to move away from”) is as in the original; the gist is leaving the teacher, doing without a teacher’s transmission; reproduced as is.
  3. “Those who cultivate the elixir 炼已筑基”: 炼已 should be a mistake for 炼己 (“refining the self”); reproduced as is, and translated as “refining the self.”
  4. “The more one gathers the light, the more it gathers round” (愈聚光聚圆): so in the original; the gist is “the more one gathers it, the rounder the light.”
  5. “The Book of Changes says”: in the original there is a semicolon before the quotation mark; reproduced as is. The quotation “Heaven and earth intermingle …” comes from the “Appended Statements, Part Two” of the Book of Changes.
  6. In Qiu Ling’s note, “strictly avoid pursuing or 欣尝 it”: 欣尝 should be a mistake for 欣赏 (“admiring”); reproduced as is.
  7. Figure 21: the text’s “left is mao, right is you” is from the practitioner’s own orientation; the original figure is drawn face to face, with mao on the right and you on the left; this site follows the original figure.
  8. The five patterns of inner light from “somatic science” mentioned in Qiu Ling’s note are an account from Chinese qigong research of the 1980s–1990s; they are translated as in the original and do not represent this site’s assessment.
  9. Shangyangzi is Chen Zhixu of the Yuan; Kuiyizi is unidentified; the Scripture of the Mind Seal is the Wondrous Scripture of the Mind Seal of the Exalted Jade Emperor.
