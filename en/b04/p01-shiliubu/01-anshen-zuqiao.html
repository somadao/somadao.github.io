The Sixteen Steps of Method Formulas · 第一步 安神祖窍

Step One · Settling the Spirit in the Ancestral Opening

The formula of Step One, settling the spirit in the Ancestral Opening — lower the curtains, make the mind bright and guard the Ancestral Opening; seeing the light of inner nature is the real work

About This Chapter

The first of the sixteen steps begins with the “work on inner nature” (refining the heart-mind and spirit): you settle the spirit in the Ancestral Opening between the two eyes and guard it until a ball of light appears of itself before your eyes — Zhao Bichen calls it the light of wisdom and draws it as “〇”.

  1. Preparing before and while sitting: first sweep away stray thoughts and loosen your clothes and belt; once seated, let the body be like withered wood and the mind like cold ashes, lower the curtains of the eyes to look at the tip of the nose, and rest the intention at the point level with and midway between the two eyes. When heart and breath are calm, draw in the light of the eyes, still the hearing, put the tongue against the palate and regulate the breathing through the nose. Sit cross-legged and form the hand seal called the “zi-wu eight-trigram linked-ring seal”.
  2. Where the Ancestral Opening is: straight in from the midpoint between the eyes. In front (in the empty space before the eyes) is the Mysterious Pass; behind (further in) is the Valley Spirit; in the very center is the Ancestral Opening — “in the very center there is an emptiness that is not empty”.
  3. Why guard it: the Ancestral Opening is “the place where inner nature is refined, the root where life is established”; guarding it is guarding the center and embracing the One; the breathing too must return here, so that every breath returns to the root.
  4. How to guard it: you must not guard it rigidly with deliberate mind (clinging to form), nor search for it at random with no mind (falling into emptiness). Eyes, ears, tongue and thought, walking, standing, sitting and lying, never leave this opening; in time “the empty chamber produces whiteness”, light appears before the eyes, and a point of true yang issues from within — this is the phenomenon of the Mysterious Pass.
  5. Tongue against the palate: the palate is the Celestial Pool point; with the tongue pressed against it, the true breath-energy can descend through the Mysterious Breast to the elixir field and produce Sweet Dew.
  6. Telling true from false: the true Way is nothing but the spirit and breath of the Prior Heaven true One. Real “gathering of the medicine” is not breathing in air, but making the original essence turn back and returning the essence to replenish the body.

Qiu Ling’s note is important: when the Ancestral Opening has been guarded for a long time, the light of wisdom (the light of inner nature) appears before the eyes, and the rest of the book treats it as a marker of progress in the practice; but people with yin deficiency and yang excess, or with high blood pressure, should not guard the Ancestral Opening. People of this constitution should first skip Step One and begin with Step Two, “The Jade Tripod and Golden Furnace”, guarding the lower elixir field; or else only shine the spirit-light of the two eyes back on the Ancestral Opening for a moment and then turn at once to the lower elixir field.

Original Text and Translation

Figure 1 · Settling the Spirit in the Ancestral Opening

The figure shows a person sitting upright with legs crossed, hands clasped in front of the belly. Above the head, to the left and right, are two Taiji diagrams: the one on the left is the “Jade Hare”, standing for the moon; the one on the right is the “Golden Crow”, standing for the sun — the “sun in the west, moon in the east” of the formula refers to the two eyes: the Ancestral Opening is at the midpoint between them. The three phrases in the circle in the middle, “the gate of the Mysterious Female”, “the Mysterious Pass, the Ancestral Opening” and “the root of breathing”, explain that this opening is the gate of the Mysterious Female spoken of by Laozi, the Ancestral Opening where the Mysterious Pass is found, and also the root of breathing.

Formula
Original (Chinese)

（一）

天下地上安祖窍，日西月东聚先天。

玄关之后谷神前，正中有个空不空。

（二）

乾坤合成灵祖窍，包罗天地空不空。

杳杳冥冥圆光献，这个正位神归中。

(1) Below “heaven” and above “earth” (the head above is heaven, the body below is earth; here it means the very center of the head), settle the spirit in the Ancestral Opening; west of the “sun” and east of the “moon” (the two eyes are a sun and a moon, so this means between the eyes), gather the Prior Heaven breath. It lies behind the Mysterious Pass and in front of the Valley Spirit; in the very center there is a place that is “empty and yet not empty”.

(2) Qian and Kun (yang and yin, spirit and breath) join to form this numinous Ancestral Opening; it can encompass heaven and earth — it looks empty, but in truth it is not. When you guard it until all is dim and deep (remote, quiet, as if there and not there), a round ball of light will appear; this central position is where the spirit returns to the center and abides.

§1
Original (Chinese)

我师了然、了空禅师曰：初炼性命之功，先得炼性。每於静坐之前，务要扫除一切杂念，宽放衣带，身体不受束缚，自然血脉流通无阻。及入坐时，身如槁木，心似寒灰，两目下观鼻准，不可太闭，太闭则神气昏暗，亦不可过开，过开则神光外驰，当以垂帘看鼻准，意念在两目中间齐平处为最佳。久之，慧光自然现出。此乃修丹起初收拾念头之法。俟心气适和后，含眼光，凝耳韵，舌顶上颚，调鼻息。如息不调，恐有闭塞喘急之患；息调身心全忘，塞兑终日如愚。盘膝稳坐，左腿向外，右腿向内，为阳抱阴。左手大指捏定中指，右手大指进入左手内捏子诀，右手在外，为阴抱阳。此名子午八卦连环诀。经云“手脚和合扣连环，四门紧闭守正中”是也。

My teachers, the two Chan masters Liaoran and Liaokong, said: when you begin to cultivate the work of inner nature and life, you must first refine “inner nature” (the heart-mind and spirit). Before each session of quiet sitting, you must sweep away all stray thoughts completely and loosen your clothes and belt, so that the body is not constrained and the blood flows freely of itself. Once seated, let the body be motionless like withered wood and the mind raise no thoughts, like cold ashes (withered wood and dead ashes), and let the two eyes look down at the tip of the nose — not closed too tightly, for if they are closed too tightly the spirit and breath grow dim (you easily become torpid); nor opened too wide, for if they are opened too wide the spirit-light runs outward (you easily become scattered). Best of all is to lower the curtains (let the eyelids droop naturally, leaving a thread of light) and look at the tip of the nose, with the intention resting at the point level with and midway between the two eyes. In time, the light of wisdom will appear of itself. This is the method for first gathering in one’s thoughts in cultivating the elixir. When heart and breath have become harmonious, hold in the light of the eyes (do not look outward), still the hearing of the ears (do not listen outward), press the tongue against the palate and regulate the breathing through the nose. If the breathing is not regulated, you may suffer from tightness in the chest and short, rapid breath; once the breathing is regulated, body and mind are both forgotten, and “with the Dui closed” (the mouth shut and silent — Dui stands for the mouth) you are like a simpleton all day long (thinking of nothing). Sit steadily cross-legged, the left leg outside and the right leg inside — this is called “yang embracing yin”. The left thumb presses the left middle finger; the right thumb goes into the web of the left hand and presses the “zi point” at the base of the left ring finger, the right hand wrapping the left hand from outside — this is called “yin embracing yang”. This hand seal is called the “zi-wu eight-trigram linked-ring seal”. It is what the scripture means by “hands and feet joined and linked in a ring, the four gates tightly shut, guarding the very center” (hands and feet clasped into a ring, the four gates of eyes, ears, mouth and nose shut tight, guarding the very center).

§2
Original (Chinese)

吾人脑中之仁，上接天谷，下达涌泉，中通於心。丹经云：“性者心也，发於二目；命者肾也，发於淫根”。真性乃心中灵气，发於脑仁，是以眼视正中，性光现出。炼之日久，即与命接合而为一。性命和合，为观空不空。不知和合之理，无所成也。

The “kernel” in our brain (the brain’s kernel, the center of the brain) connects above with the Celestial Valley (the Muddy Pellet at the crown of the head), reaches below to the Bubbling Spring (the sole of the foot), and communicates in the middle with the heart. An alchemical scripture says: “Inner nature is the heart, and issues from the two eyes; life is the kidneys, and issues from the root of lust.” True inner nature is the numinous breath in the heart, issuing from the brain’s kernel; so when the two eyes look toward the very center, the light of inner nature appears. Refined for a long time, inner nature joins with life and becomes one. When inner nature and life are united, that is “contemplating the emptiness that is not empty” (it looks empty, but in truth is not). Without understanding the principle of their union, nothing can be achieved.

§3
Original (Chinese)

观空而不空，为真道；观空而空，为伪道。因不知归中，故慧光不能发现。心下肾上中间虚空之处，内有灵炁，机发则成窍，神炁归中，灵炁上腾，而成这个〇，为不空。无光之虚空，为顽空；灵光之虚空，为真空。真空不空，顽空乃空。不空即是灵光，实为神炁，由於黄庭所发者。昔我师了空曰：“金机飞电，虚室生白，圆圆陀陀玄关生。”有玄关者为不空，而人之生死，全赖此虚无神炁以为之主宰。炁聚神来则人活，炁散神去则人死。故曰：“有神无炁不能生，有炁无神不能死。”心中元神为性，肾中元炁为命，神炁和合方为正功。

What looks empty and in truth is not empty is the true Way; what looks empty and really has nothing is a false way. Because people do not know how to return the spirit to the very center, the light of wisdom cannot appear. Below the heart and above the kidneys, in the empty space between, there is a numinous breath; when the time comes and it stirs, it becomes the “opening”; when spirit and breath return to the center, the numinous breath rises and becomes this one “〇” (a ball of light) — this is “not empty”. Emptiness without light is dull emptiness (dead emptiness); emptiness with numinous light is true emptiness. True emptiness is in truth not empty; dull emptiness is truly empty. What is meant by “not empty” is this numinous light; it is in fact spirit and breath, issuing from the Yellow Court. Formerly my teacher Liaokong said: “The golden spark flies like lightning (light comes flying like a flash of lightning), the empty chamber produces whiteness (white light arises in an empty room), round and full (a perfectly round ball), and the Mysterious Pass appears.” Only where there is a Mysterious Pass is there “not empty”; and a person’s life and death are wholly governed by the spirit and breath within this emptiness: when breath gathers and spirit comes, the person lives; when breath scatters and spirit leaves, the person dies. So it is said: “With spirit but no breath, one cannot live; with breath but no spirit, one cannot die either (meaning only that one cannot live long and see far — see the Translator’s Notes).” The Original Spirit in the heart is inner nature, the original breath in the kidneys is life; only when spirit and breath unite is it the right practice.

§4
Original (Chinese)

祖窍真处，举世罕知。正在天之下，地之上，日之西，月之东。正中是祖窍，前是玄关，后是谷神。中是真性，内藏真息，虽与口鼻之息相通，而常人之息以喉，由口鼻进出，不能入於祖窍以归根。真人之息行内呼吸，四个往来，不用口鼻吸呼，则息息归根矣。欲寻真人之息，须调后天呼吸之息，以寻真息归根，其气藏於祖窍，故息调则气和，息住气不散。我师了然所谓“炁归元海寿无穷”是也。

Few people in the world know where the Ancestral Opening truly is. It is precisely below “heaven” and above “earth”, west of the “sun” and east of the “moon” (the very center of the head, between the two eyes). In the very center is the Ancestral Opening; in front is the Mysterious Pass; behind is the Valley Spirit. In the very center is hidden true inner nature, and within it is stored the True Breath. Although the True Breath communicates with the breathing of mouth and nose, the breathing of ordinary people reaches only the throat (the breathing of ordinary people comes from the throat), going in and out through mouth and nose, and cannot enter the Ancestral Opening and return to the root. The breathing of the true person is inner breathing, “four comings and goings” (see the Translator’s Notes), not using mouth and nose, so every breath returns to the root (every breath returning to the root). To find the breathing of the true person, you must first regulate the Posterior Heaven breathing of mouth and nose, and use it to seek the True Breath and make it return to the root; the true breath-energy is stored in the Ancestral Opening, so when the breathing is regulated the breath is calm, and when the breathing stops (as if there and not there) the breath does not scatter. This is what my teacher Liaoran meant by “when the breath returns to the original sea, life is without end”.

§5
Original (Chinese)

二目之中心内，即是祖窍，老子曰玄牝之门，是为守中抱一。内里有颗黍米珠，为人身天地之正中，藏元始祖炁之窍也。是知窍而不知妙，妙者性光也，就是这个〇而已，儒谓之仁，《易》曰无极，释谓之珠，亦曰圆明，道谓之丹，亦曰灵光，皆指先天真一之炁。知此一窍，则金丹大道之能事尽矣。所谓得其一，万事毕此也。是以用功之时，两眼归中守一，养於祖窍之内，勿勤勿怠，谓之安神祖窍，为炼性之所，立命之根。昔我师了然曰：“不炼祖窍，则真息不住，而神化无基，药物不全，而金丹亦不结。”盖此窍为总持之门，为万法之都，无内无外，不可以有心守，不可以无心求。以有心守之，则着相。以无心求之，则落空。然则如何炼之而后可？曰：“垂帘明心守祖窍，见着性光是功夫。闭口藏舌顶上颚，五行之中神炁凝。”吾之念正，则天地之心亦正。吾之神清，则太极之理自明。倘能一念不起，久久澄清，虚极静笃之时，则虚室生白，金机飞电，复见天地之心而自明矣！

Straight in from the midpoint of the two eyes is the Ancestral Opening, which is what Laozi called “the gate of the Mysterious Female”; guarding it is guarding the center and embracing the One. Within it is a pearl the size of a grain of millet; it is the very center of the small heaven and earth of the human body, the opening where the original ancestral breath is stored. But knowing this opening is not enough; you must also know its “wonder”, and the “wonder” is simply the light of inner nature, that is, this one “〇”. The Confucians call it “humaneness”, the Book of Changes calls it the “Limitless”, the Buddhists call it the “pearl” and also “perfect brightness”, the Daoists call it the “elixir” and also the “numinous light” — all refer to the breath of the Prior Heaven true One. Once you know this one opening, the whole matter of the great Way of the Golden Elixir is complete. “Obtain the One, and the ten thousand affairs are done” (obtain this “One”, and everything is accomplished) — that is what it means. So when practicing, bring the two eyes back to the center, guard this “One”, and nourish it in the Ancestral Opening, neither forcing too hard nor slackening — this is called “settling the spirit in the Ancestral Opening”; it is the place where inner nature is refined and the root where life is established. Formerly my teacher Liaoran said: “If you do not refine the Ancestral Opening, the True Breath cannot settle, and the transformations of the spirit have no foundation; the medicine will be incomplete, and the Golden Elixir cannot form.” For this one opening is the gate that governs everything (the gate of total retention), the place where all methods converge, with neither inside nor outside; it must not be guarded with deliberate mind, nor sought with no mind — guarded with deliberate mind, you cling to form; sought with no mind, you fall into emptiness. How then should it be refined? Answer: “Lower the curtains, make the mind bright and guard the Ancestral Opening; seeing the light of inner nature is the real work. Close the mouth, hide the tongue against the palate, and within the five phases spirit and breath congeal.” (With the eyes’ curtains lowered and the mind clearly guarding the Ancestral Opening, only seeing the light of inner nature counts as having real attainment; close the mouth and hide the tongue pressed against the palate, and within the five phases — the breaths of the five organs — spirit and breath will congeal.) When my thought is upright, the heart of heaven and earth is upright too; when my spirit is clear, the principle of the Great Ultimate becomes clear of itself. If you can let not one thought arise, staying clear and settled for a long time, then at the extreme of emptiness and the depth of stillness the empty chamber produces whiteness and the golden spark flies like lightning (white light appears before the eyes, like a flash of lightning), and you see again “the heart of heaven and earth” (that point of vitality when the one yang returns) — then you will naturally understand!

§6
Original (Chinese)

昔吾师了空曰，上颚是天池穴，因其上通脑髓，恐其往下泄炁，须用舌顶住，引真炁由玄膺穴下降丹田，生出甘露，顺归气管过十二重楼。故舌尖倒顶上颚，则真炁聚於祖窍之前。眼常观此窍，耳常逆听此窍，舌常顶向此窍，念念不离此窍，行立坐卧心心常在此窍，一转瞬间亦不可忘却此窍，悠然心清气爽，浩然炁畅身壮，寂然无思无虑，豁然知空不空，了然悟性。真性工夫至此，自然精神朗发，智慧日生，心性灵通，忽有一点真阳，从中而发出，此为玄关现矣。故我师南无派敲蹻道人曰：“玄关无定位，黄庭一路为玄关，若不舌顶天池穴，而黄庭先天真一之气泄矣！”如不顶住上颚，真炁不能下降丹田。此是真人行吸呼炁之法也。

Formerly my teacher Liaokong said: the palate is the Celestial Pool point; because it connects upward with the brain marrow, there is a danger that the true breath-energy will leak downward from here, so it must be blocked with the tongue; in this way the true breath-energy is led down through the Mysterious Breast point to the elixir field and produces Sweet Dew (the clear, sweet saliva in the mouth), which goes down the windpipe through the Twelve-Storied Tower (the throat). So with the tip of the tongue turned up against the palate, the true breath-energy gathers in front of the Ancestral Opening. Let the eyes always look at this opening, let the ears always listen inward to this opening, let the tongue always press toward this opening, let every thought never leave this opening; walking, standing, sitting or lying, let every thought always be in this opening, and do not forget it for the blink of an eye. Then, serenely, the mind is clear and the breath fresh; vastly, the breath flows and the body is strong; silently, there is no thought and no worry; suddenly you understand “empty and yet not empty”, and clearly awaken to your original nature. When the work of true inner nature reaches this point, the spirit naturally shines, wisdom grows day by day, and the heart-mind becomes numinous and penetrating; suddenly a point of true yang issues from within — this is the appearance of the Mysterious Pass. That is why my teacher, the Daoist Qiaoqiao (Liu Mingrui) of the Nanwu school, said: “The Mysterious Pass has no fixed position; the whole way from the Yellow Court to the Ancestral Opening is the Mysterious Pass. If you do not press the tongue against the Celestial Pool point, the breath of the Prior Heaven true One in the Yellow Court will leak away!” Without pressing the palate, the true breath-energy cannot descend to the elixir field. This is the method by which the true person breathes the true breath-energy.

§7
Original (Chinese)

真伪不可不辨。真道无他，只是先天真一神炁耳。神者性也，炁者命也，而此炁亦在元精之内。柳华阳祖师云：“守窍灵慧自然生，生出真慧，下与炁穴真命相接，即为金丹。”祖师恐慧命发生之时，学者不知下手之诀，慧命顺出阳关能生人；如在元精正行半路之际，得真师口诀，一下手便使元精逆回，便能作丹。此元精将出之时，若不下手点住生死窍，一出阳关，化为后天有形之精而泄矣！顺出是精，逆回即元炁。学者若得真师，先问有无下手诀法，如有此诀，则问是否采空气，若言非采空气，而是还精补我身，体如童体，马阴藏相，阳物不举，方为真道。至于采药，无念采之，为先天小药；有念采之，乃是幻丹，无所成功。

True and false must be distinguished. The true Way is nothing else: it is only the spirit and breath of the Prior Heaven true One. Spirit is inner nature, breath is life, and this breath is also stored within the original essence. Patriarch Liu Huayang said: “Guard the opening, and numinous wisdom arises of itself; when true wisdom arises, it joins below with the true life in the Cavity of Breath and becomes the Golden Elixir.” The patriarch feared that when the wisdom-life (the original essence and original breath) stirs, the student would not know the formula for taking it in hand: if the wisdom-life goes forward and out through the yang gate, it can produce a human being (it turns into reproductive essence); if, when the original essence is just halfway along, one has obtained a true teacher’s formula, one can take it in hand and turn the original essence back, and so make the elixir. When this original essence is about to go out, if you do not take it in hand and press the “gate of life and death” (see the Translator’s Notes), once it passes the yang gate it turns into Posterior Heaven essence with form and is lost! Going forward and out, it is essence; turned back, it is the original breath. If a student meets a teacher, let him first ask whether the teacher has a formula for taking it in hand; if so, ask whether he “gathers empty air” (breathes in outside air); if he says it is not gathering air but “returning the essence to replenish my body”, training until the body is like a boy’s, with the horse-like retraction (the external genitals drawn in) and the member no longer rising (no lustful thoughts arising) — that is the true Way. As for gathering the medicine: gathered without thought, it is the Prior Heaven lesser medicine; gathered with thought, it is an illusory elixir, and nothing will come of it.

§8
Original (Chinese)

张紫阳真人曰：“此窍非凡窍，乾坤共合成，名为神炁穴，内有坎离精。”刘海蟾曰：“中央神室本虚闲，自有先天真炁到。”吕纯阳曰：“守中绝学方知奥，抱一无言始见佳。”正阳翁曰：“要识金丹端的处，未生身处下工夫。”邱祖长春曰：“当时一句师边得，默默垂帘仔细看。”马祖丹阳曰：“若能常守弯弯窍，神自灵明炁自充。”柳华阳曰：“本性灵光，不得慧命，不能了道。”伍冲虚曰：“无相光中常自在。”李虚庵曰：“性者空空，发於正中。”了然禅师曰：“一念不生全体献。”了空禅师曰：“心中元神能放光，肾中元精能化炁。”

(What follows are the words of the patriarchs of successive ages about this opening.) The Perfected Zhang Ziyang said: “This opening is no ordinary opening; it is formed jointly by Qian and Kun (yin and yang); it is called the cavity of spirit and breath, and within it is the essence of Kan and Li (heart and kidneys, spirit and breath).” Liu Haichan said: “The central Chamber of the Spirit is by nature empty and at leisure; of itself the Prior Heaven true breath arrives there.” Lü Chunyang said: “Guarding the center and cutting off all miscellaneous learning, one comes to know its mystery; embracing the One and speaking no more, one sees its good.” Old Master Zhong Zhengyang (Zhongli Quan) said: “If you would know the exact place of the Golden Elixir, work at the place from before you were born (the original state of Prior Heaven).” Patriarch Qiu Changchun said: “In those days I received a single formula from my teacher’s side, and silently lowered the curtains and looked carefully.” Patriarch Ma Danyang said: “If you can always guard this winding opening, the spirit becomes numinous and bright of itself, and the breath full of itself.” Liu Huayang said: “The numinous light of original nature, if it does not obtain the wisdom-life (the work on life), cannot complete the Way.” Wu Chongxu said: “Within the light that has no form, always at ease.” Li Xu’an said: “Inner nature is empty, issuing from the very center.” The Chan master Liaoran said: “When not one thought arises, the original substance is fully revealed.” The Chan master Liaokong said: “The Original Spirit in the heart can emit light; the original essence in the kidneys can transform into breath.”

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§8
Original note (Chinese)

【注释】本步口诀为从性功入手，至于命功。其法在意守两眼中间鼻梁上之祖窍（又名山根）。此窍为道门奥秘，千峰老人赵避尘之祖师伍冲虚、柳华阳在其著作《伍柳仙宗》中从未明言。尹真人《性命圭旨》一书中虽有安神祖窍一节，亦未言此窍位置。赵氏却直指此窍为“炼性之所，立命之根”，意守此窍即是“守中抱一”，即是“不空”，即能“息息归根”。

赵氏还指出，守祖窍久之，自然在眼前现出慧光（即性光），所谓“垂帘明心守祖窍，见着性光是功夫”。古人用〇来表示慧光，亦称为珠（圆明），为丹（灵光）。柳华阳在《慧命经》开首说：“成佛作祖，是本性灵光”，即示此。再形容之则为金机飞电，虚室生白，圆圆陀陀者是也。这不过是先天真一之炁所凝成。自此本书用很大篇幅来谈此慧光，作为练功进程之标志。

赵氏指出，两眼之间正中是祖窍，祖窍之前（即眼前）是玄关，祖窍之后（内）是谷神。机发则成窍，机息复渺茫，慧光现才有玄关。谷神为真性，内藏真息。这里包含了欲见玄关则必须炼出聚于祖窍之前的光而成慧光，欲寻真息，则必须呼吸入于祖窍之内以归根之意。一般认为，祖窍即是玄关，与赵氏之说不同。赵氏认为，玄关在祖窍之前虚空中，亦即慧光所形成的这个〇；祖窍是玄关出入之门户。

祖窍即今所称之上丹田，入手炼功得法，在一两月内即有慧光自然出现，其出现过程，一般由小到大，由暗到明，由游移到静定，由散漫到凝聚。行功越静笃，此光越显现。若从意守下丹田入手而不守祖窍，则慧光不易出现。但若守下丹田时善于运用两眼神光，用回光返照之法，即用二眼珠合并，下照丹田，久之亦见慧光。赵氏认为，此光实为神气，由黄庭所发。此处黄庭指脐上。赵氏又认为，炼功久之仍不见慧光，说明是功夫不精进。此论乃从习练上乘功夫出发而言，若从健身治病而言，练功虽不见慧光，对身体仍有好处。

历来对意守祖窍颇有争议。盖各人素质不同，并非人人都宜于意守此窍。古人有认为意守祖窍非上根利器即上等素质善于练功之人不可。吕祖《太乙金华宗旨》一书引《黄庭经》所说“寸田尺宅可治生”，尺宅指面也，面上寸田即是祖窍，有“郁罗萧台之胜，玉京丹阙之奇，乃至虚至灵之神所注”，但又说：“此中妙诀，虽不差毫末，然而甚活，全要聪明，又须沉静。非极聪明人行不得，非极沉静人守不得”。

就各人体质而言，阴虚阳亢之体及高血压症患者实不宜守祖窍。若勉强行之，气血易于上冲，虚火上升，轻则头昏目眩，重则病情恶化。故今人传功，对意守上窍均小心翼翼，宁从意守下窍着手以求其稳妥，此所以意守下丹田功法得以大行之故。一味鼓吹祖窍之奇奥，甚至认为从意守下丹田入手为不得法，不从学者体质出发，不从晚年修持先论救护命宝出发，不识量体裁衣，势必招致不良后果。

学者若欲练本功，下手即须有此知识，方不自误。具有不适宜意守祖窍之体质者欲练本功，仅宜之法为暂放弃习练第一步口诀安神祖窍，可参照第二步口诀玉鼎金炉，从意守下丹田入手，即从命功开始至于性功。但须注意意守时用回光返照方法，微微运用两眼神光下照下丹田，以达神气和合，方是正功；或只回光返照祖窍一下，切勿过久，迅即转向下丹田，亦可无碍。

This step’s formula begins with the work on inner nature and then moves to the work on life. The method is to rest the intention on the Ancestral Opening (also called the Root of the Mountain) on the bridge of the nose between the two eyes. This opening is a secret of the Daoist school; the patriarchs of the Old Man of Qianfeng, Zhao Bichen — Wu Chongxu and Liu Huayang — never stated it openly in their work The Immortal Lineage of Wu and Liu. Although the Perfected Yin’s book The Jade Tablet of Inner Nature and Life (Xingming guizhi) has a section on “settling the spirit in the Ancestral Opening”, it too does not say where this opening is. Zhao, however, points straight to it as “the place where inner nature is refined, the root where life is established”: resting the intention on this opening is “guarding the center and embracing the One”, is “not empty”, and makes possible “every breath returning to the root”.

Zhao also points out that when the Ancestral Opening has been guarded for a long time, the light of wisdom (that is, the light of inner nature) naturally appears before the eyes — this is what is meant by “lower the curtains, make the mind bright and guard the Ancestral Opening; seeing the light of inner nature is the real work”. The ancients represented the light of wisdom by “〇”, and also called it the “pearl” (perfect brightness) and the “elixir” (numinous light). Liu Huayang says at the beginning of the Book of Wisdom and Life: “Becoming a Buddha or a patriarch is the numinous light of original nature.” That is what he means. To describe it further: “the golden spark flies like lightning”, “the empty chamber produces whiteness”, “round and full”. In fact it is nothing but the breath of the Prior Heaven true One congealed. From here on, this book devotes a great deal of space to this light of wisdom, taking it as a marker of progress in the practice.

Zhao points out: the very center between the two eyes is the Ancestral Opening; in front of the Ancestral Opening (that is, before the eyes) is the Mysterious Pass; behind the Ancestral Opening (within) is the Valley Spirit. When the time stirs, it becomes the opening; when the time subsides, it vanishes without trace; only when the light of wisdom appears is there a Mysterious Pass. The Valley Spirit is true inner nature, and within it is stored the True Breath. This contains two layers of meaning: to see the Mysterious Pass, you must refine the light gathered in front of the Ancestral Opening into the light of wisdom; to find the True Breath, you must let the breathing enter the Ancestral Opening so that it can return to the root. People generally consider the Ancestral Opening to be the Mysterious Pass, which differs from Zhao’s view. Zhao holds that the Mysterious Pass is in the empty space in front of the Ancestral Opening — that is, this one “〇” formed by the light of wisdom — and that the Ancestral Opening is the gateway through which the Mysterious Pass goes in and out.

The Ancestral Opening is what is today called the upper elixir field. If you begin the practice in the right way, the light of wisdom will usually appear of itself within one or two months. As it appears, it generally goes from small to large, from dim to bright, from wandering to calm and steady, from diffuse to concentrated. The more still and deep the practice, the clearer this light. If you begin by resting the intention on the lower elixir field and do not guard the Ancestral Opening, the light of wisdom does not appear so easily; but if, while guarding the lower elixir field, you make good use of the spirit-light of the two eyes, using the method of reverting the light to shine within — that is, bringing the two eyeballs together (drawing the gaze inward) and shining down on the elixir field — in time you can also see the light of wisdom. Zhao holds that this light is in fact spirit and breath, issuing from the Yellow Court; the Yellow Court here means the area above the navel. Zhao also holds that if after long practice the light of wisdom still does not appear, the practice is not advancing. This is said from the standpoint of practicing the higher work; if the aim is to strengthen the body and cure illness, practice benefits the body even if the light of wisdom is never seen.

Resting the intention on the Ancestral Opening has long been controversial. Because people’s constitutions differ, not everyone is suited to it. Some of the ancients held that resting the intention on the Ancestral Opening requires “people of superior roots and sharp faculties” (that is, people of superior constitution who are good at practice). Lü Dongbin’s The Secret of the Golden Flower quotes the line of the Yellow Court Scripture, “the inch-field in the foot-house can govern life”: the “foot-house” is the face, and the “inch-field” in the face is the Ancestral Opening, where there are “the splendors of the Yuluo Terrace and the marvels of the Jade Capital and the Cinnabar Palace” (as wondrous as the palaces and towers of the heavenly immortals), the place into which the utterly empty, utterly numinous spirit pours; but the book also says: “Although the wondrous formula within admits not the slightest error, it is very lively, depending wholly on intelligence and also on calm. Without the greatest intelligence one cannot practice it; without the greatest calm one cannot keep it.”

In terms of individual constitution, people with yin deficiency and yang excess, and people with high blood pressure, are really not suited to guarding the Ancestral Opening. If they force themselves to do it, the breath and blood easily rush upward and deficiency-fire rises — at best causing dizziness and blurred vision, at worst making their illness worse. That is why people who teach the practice today are very cautious about resting the intention on the upper opening, preferring to begin with the lower opening to be safe — which is why methods of resting the intention on the lower elixir field have become so widespread. To extol blindly the wonders of the Ancestral Opening, and even to consider beginning with the lower elixir field a wrong method, without starting from the student’s constitution, without starting from the need in later life first to rescue and protect the “treasure of life” (the original breath, the breath of essence), and without knowing how to cut the cloth to fit the body, is bound to bring bad results.

A student who wants to practice this method must have this knowledge from the very start, so as not to harm himself. For those whose constitution is not suited to resting the intention on the Ancestral Opening and who want to practice this method, the only suitable way is to set aside Step One, “Settling the Spirit in the Ancestral Opening”, for the time being, and, following the formula of Step Two, “The Jade Tripod and Golden Furnace”, begin by resting the intention on the lower elixir field — that is, begin with the work on life and then move to the work on inner nature. But note that while resting the intention you should use the method of reverting the light to shine within, gently using the spirit-light of the two eyes to shine down on the lower elixir field, so that spirit and breath unite — this is the right practice; or else only revert the light onto the Ancestral Opening for a moment, never for long, and then turn at once to the lower elixir field; done this way, there is no harm.

Key Terms

  • Ancestral Opening: the opening straight in from the midpoint between the two eyes (today called the upper elixir field, commonly the area of the Root of the Mountain). Zhao Bichen holds that in front is the Mysterious Pass, behind is the Valley Spirit, and the Ancestral Opening is the gateway through which the Mysterious Pass goes in and out.
  • Mysterious Pass: for Zhao Bichen specifically the “〇” formed by the light of wisdom in the empty space in front of the Ancestral Opening — a different angle from the Mysterious Pass that “stirs at a moment of awareness” in Huang Yuanji (book 3).
  • Valley Spirit: the seat of true inner nature behind (within) the Ancestral Opening, where the True Breath is stored.
  • Light of wisdom: the light that appears of itself before the eyes after long guarding of the opening; also called the light of inner nature, perfect brightness or numinous light, drawn as “〇”; this book treats it as a marker of progress in the practice.
  • Tip of the nose (鼻准): the end of the nose.
  • Point level with and midway between the two eyes: the midpoint of the line joining the two eyes, where the intention rests lightly.
  • Holding in the light of the eyes, stilling the hearing: drawing back the gaze and the hearing, not letting them run outward.
  • Closing the Dui (塞兑): closing the mouth; the Dui trigram stands for the mouth.
  • Zi-wu eight-trigram linked-ring seal: a hand seal in which the left thumb presses the middle finger, the right thumb goes into the web of the left hand to press the “zi point”, and the right hand wraps the left from outside.
  • Celestial Pool: the palate. Pressing the tongue against it links the Conception and Governing vessels and leads the true breath-energy downward.
  • Mysterious Breast: an opening under the tongue (some say above the throat) through which true breath-energy and saliva descend.
  • Twelve-Storied Tower: the throat (windpipe).
  • Four comings and goings: the going and coming of the True Breath in and out of, up and down within, the Ancestral Opening (not explained in the original; see the Translator’s Notes).
  • Every breath returning to the root: every breath returns to the root (here, the Ancestral Opening).
  • The empty chamber produces whiteness, the golden spark flying like lightning: in very deep stillness, white light arises in the emptiness before the eyes, flashing like lightning.
  • Dull emptiness: a dead stillness that is only empty, without numinous light.
  • The horse-like retraction: the external genitals drawn in like a boy’s, showing that essence no longer leaks out.
  • Gate of life and death: the gateway where essence is about to go out (the area of the perineum and the yang gate); pressing it lets the original essence turn back.
  • Illusory elixir: an “elixir” gathered with thought, obtained by imagination — not real.

Putting It into Practice

  1. First consider your constitution. People with yin deficiency and yang excess, high blood pressure, a tendency to a hot face or to headaches and insomnia, should not guard the Ancestral Opening; begin directly with Step Two, “The Jade Tripod and Golden Furnace”, guarding the lower elixir field; at most shine the spirit-light lightly once between the eyes, then turn at once to the lower elixir field.
  2. What you guard is “the point level with and midway between the two eyes” — do not stare hard. Lower the curtains and look at the tip of the nose, resting the intention lightly between the eyes — “do not guard it with deliberate mind, do not seek it with no mind”. Guarding rigidly with force is “clinging to form”, and easily makes the head feel swollen.
  3. Tongue against the palate, closing the mouth, regulating the breath are the basic actions; if the breathing is not even you will feel stifled and short of breath, so first let the breathing become even before talking of guarding the opening.
  4. The light of wisdom appears of itself; do not “look at” it or chase it. Huang Yuanji in book 3, and this book from Step Three onward, will say it again: when you see the light, do not cling to it.
  5. One criterion for telling true from false: the true Way is not “gathering air”, but returning the essence to replenish the body, so that the body grows stronger day by day. Be wary of anyone who teaches forceful inhaling, holding the breath, or imagining a ball of light.

Translator's Notes

  1. In the original book this step has two formula poems and eight passages of explanation, the last of which collects sayings of the patriarchs of successive ages; we follow the original’s division and number them §1–§8. Throughout the original book, “breath” (qi) is mostly written with the old character 炁; the Chinese original on this site keeps it.
  2. “The left thumb presses the middle finger; the right thumb goes into the left hand and presses the zi point” (左手大指捏定中指，右手大指进入左手内捏子诀): the zi point is the base of the ring finger (where zi falls when the twelve Earthly Branches are laid out on the fingers). The translation follows the usual explanation of the “zi-wu seal”: the right thumb enters through the web of the left hand and presses the base of the left ring finger.
  3. “With breath but no spirit, one cannot die” (有炁无神不能死): literally, “with breath but no spirit, one cannot die”. Daoists usually say “with spirit but no breath one cannot live, with breath but no spirit one cannot live long”, meaning that spirit and breath must unite; the original prints it this way, and the translation renders it literally with an explanation in parentheses.
  4. “Four comings and goings” (四个往来): not explained in the original. From the context it should refer to the going and coming of the True Breath in and out of, up and down within, the Ancestral Opening (the Wu-Liu school also speaks of “the four images of breathing”); the translation does not force an interpretation.
  5. “Neither diligent nor slack” (勿勤勿怠): literally, “do not be diligent, do not be slack”; the usual expression is “neither forget nor help”, meaning neither forcing too hard nor slackening; the translation follows this.
  6. “The Daoist Qiaoqiao” (敲蹻道人): the Daoist name of Liu Mingrui of the Nanwu school. Qiu Ling’s preface prints it 敲跻, while here the original prints 敲蹻; the Chinese original on this site follows the book in both places.
  7. “The Yellow Court here means the area above the navel”: the position of the Yellow Court in Zhao Bichen’s book differs from book 3; the next step, “The Jade Tripod and Golden Furnace”, discusses it.
  8. The “Jade Hare” and “Golden Crow” in the figure: the ancients represented the sun by the golden crow (a three-legged crow) and the moon by the jade hare; here they stand for the two eyes, with the same meaning as “sun in the west, moon in the east” in the formula.
