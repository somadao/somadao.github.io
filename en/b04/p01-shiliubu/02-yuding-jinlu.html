The Sixteen Steps of Method Formulas · 第二步 玉鼎金炉

Step Two · The Jade Tripod and Golden Furnace

The formula of Step Two, the jade tripod and golden furnace — facing the navel in front and the kidneys behind, in the middle there is a true golden furnace

About This Chapter

Step One settled the spirit in the head (the Ancestral Opening); this step speaks of the two “vessels” of cultivation — the jade tripod above (the center of the head, the Muddy Pellet) and the golden furnace below (the lower elixir field, the true Cavity of Breath) — and of the method of “harmonizing, uniting, congealing and gathering”, in which the heart-spirit is lowered to the lower elixir field so that spirit and breath unite.

  1. The jade tripod: in the center of the brain, the dwelling of Prior Heaven true inner nature (the Original Spirit); it is connected with the two eyes and communicates below with the heart.
  2. The golden furnace: one cun three fen below the navel (Qiu Ling’s note: actually inside the navel), at the exact center “seven in front, three behind”, “facing the navel in front and the kidneys behind”; also called the Cavity of Breath, the place where spirit is stored and breath nourished. Neither tripod nor furnace is a real object; only when the true breath-energy stirs do they “become” tripod and furnace.
  3. Harmonizing, uniting, congealing and gathering: true inner nature (spirit) in the heart descends to the place of true life (the lower elixir field); the two eyes come together and shine down on the area of the navel, and spirit and breath congeal in one place. In time, the light of wisdom rises from the Cavity of Breath — a sign that essence, breath and spirit are full; those who practice diligently and accumulate virtue can see it in about a hundred days.
  4. The Living Zi Hour: when, in quiet sitting, the outer kidney (the male member) rises without any thought, that is the Living Zi Hour; it must be “gathered without thought”, using “turning the Dharma Wheel” (one inhalation rising from the Caudal Funnel to the crown, one exhalation descending back to the Cavity of Breath) to draw it back and transform it into original breath. Depending on age there are the sudden method, the gradual method, and for the elderly the method of “adding oil to prolong life”, and the book gives in detail the counts of thirty-six inhalations for advancing the yang fire and twenty-four descents for withdrawing the yin tally.
  5. Swallowing the saliva: with the tongue against the palate, wait until the mouth is full of saliva, then stretch the neck and swallow, sending it into the Conception vessel and down to the Cavity of Breath, where it turns into yin essence.
  6. True and false: an erection that comes with thought is Posterior Heaven deviant essence; one may only draw the yang back up by breathing in through mouth and nose, and must not gather it as medicine.

Two points in Qiu Ling’s note are especially important: first, only after entering the state of the dim and obscure, with a real “yang arising and the opening unfolding”, can there be any question of turning the Dharma Wheel; otherwise it is merely “transporting by imagination, coming and going in emptiness, like boiling an empty pot with water and fire”; second, he quotes Liu Huayang’s famous description in the Book of Wisdom and Life of the scene when the “true seed is produced”, for students to compare.

Safety note (translator): This step describes forcefully lifting the perineum, holding the breath to draw upward, rolling the eyes upward and rapid breathing (martial fire); these are not suitable for people with high blood pressure, heart disease, glaucoma, hemorrhoids or prostate conditions, and no one should apply excessive force or repeat them many times in a row. If you feel a swelling or pain in the head, palpitations, or swelling or pain in the lower belly or perineum, stop at once.

Original Text and Translation

Figure 2 · The Jade Tripod and Golden Furnace

The figure is a person seen in profile. A double-lined channel runs around the outline of the body: on the back side it is marked “mao” and “Governing vessel”, rising to the crown of the head, marked “wu” and “tripod” — the small circle there is the jade tripod; on the front side it is marked “you” and “Conception vessel”, descending to the bottom, marked “zi” — the small circle there is the perineum. From “throat” and “heart” in the middle of the body a dotted line runs down to a dotted circle in the belly, in which a furnace is drawn — this is the golden furnace (the lower elixir field). The meaning of the figure: above is the jade tripod, below the golden furnace; the Governing vessel rises from zi (below) to wu (above), the Conception vessel descends from wu back to zi, forming one Celestial Circuit.

Formula
Original (Chinese)

前对脐轮后对肾，

中间有个真金炉。

十字街前安玉鼎，

神炁之宅呼吸根。

Facing the navel in front and the two kidneys behind, in the middle there is a true “golden furnace” (the lower elixir field). In front of the “crossroads” (where the line joining the two eyes crosses the bridge of the nose, that is, the area of the Ancestral Opening, and further in to the center of the head) set up the “jade tripod” — this is the dwelling of spirit and breath, the root of breathing.

§1
Original (Chinese)

玉鼎，正在大脑中心，内藏一胞，为先天真性所居之处，即元神室也。联於眼珠，复下通心，故曰性者心也，发於二目，又曰鼎内存性。鼎原无鼎，真炁发时与性合一，方得玉鼎之名。又脐下一寸三分，前七后三正中心处，曰真炁穴。即前对脐轮后对肾，上有黄庭下关元，中空之一穴又名金炉。炉内存命，命者精也，发於淫根。炉原无炉，精生炁发则为炉。血液至此变化为阴精，亦即生精之所也。此窍是存神养炁之妙处，将祖炁藏於窍内，若藏非藏，若无非无，心意不可胡思乱想，呼吸之气随意出入，是为正功。

The jade tripod is right in the center of the brain; within it is hidden a “sac” (a hollow), the dwelling of Prior Heaven true inner nature, that is, “the chamber of the Original Spirit”. It is connected with the two eyeballs and communicates downward with the heart; hence it is said “inner nature is the heart, and issues from the two eyes”, and also “inner nature is stored in the tripod”. The tripod is by nature no tripod (there is no actual object); only when the true breath-energy stirs and unites with inner nature does it earn the name “jade tripod”. Further, one cun three fen below the navel, at the exact center “seven in front, three behind” (seven parts in from the front, three in from the back), is what is called the “true Cavity of Breath”. It is “facing the navel in front and the kidneys behind”: above it is the Yellow Court, below it the Origin Pass (Guanyuan), and this empty cavity in between is also called the “golden furnace”. What is stored in the furnace is “life” — life is essence, issuing from the reproductive organ. The furnace too is by nature no furnace; when essence is produced and breath issues, it becomes the furnace. Here the blood transforms into yin essence, so this is also the place where essence is produced. This opening is the wondrous place for storing spirit and nourishing breath: store the ancestral breath in the opening, as if stored and as if not stored, as if absent yet not truly absent; let the mind not wander in fancies, and let the breath go in and out naturally — this is the right practice.

§2
Original (Chinese)

将心中真性下降于真命之处，谓之和合凝集之法。和者，心中阴气能和肾中阳气，阴气得阳气，则有安心立命之所。合者，肾中阳气承受心中阴气，则自敛收坚固其体。凝者，是凝神之法，二目和合归并一处。集者，下照坤脐，肾中真命，自然集合一处。此即性命双修养神养气之法。和合凝集已久，忽然下身融合，真阳升起，用气意自尾闾关一吸，升到头顶，一呼降到真炁穴，谓之法轮一转，为采取烹炼。永是意同呼吸转，外肾自缩回，复观炁穴，久则由炁穴升出慧光是这个〇，乃身之精气神充满而自然发生者也，原此〇即太极也。真阳之炁与真性和而为一，才生出此慧光〇，亦即鼎中真性、炉中真命之光也。学者当细悟之。若一二年之久不见慧光，可知此人平日用工毫无精勤之处，而又不肯积德。工勤德厚者百日内外，丹苗足可发现。

Lowering true inner nature (spirit) from the heart to the place of true life (the lower elixir field) is called the method of “harmonizing, uniting, congealing and gathering”. “Harmonizing”: the yin breath in the heart can be attuned with the yang breath in the kidneys; yin breath, obtaining yang breath, has a place to settle the mind and establish life. “Uniting”: the yang breath in the kidneys receives the yin breath of the heart and naturally gathers in, making its substance firm. “Congealing”: the method of concentrating the spirit — the two eyes draw together and return to one place. “Gathering”: shining down on the Kun navel (the area of the navel; Kun stands for the belly), the true life in the kidneys naturally gathers in one place. This is the method of the dual cultivation of inner nature and life, nourishing spirit and nourishing breath. When harmonizing, uniting, congealing and gathering have gone on long enough, suddenly the lower body becomes warm and harmonious and true yang rises; then, with breath and intention, one inhalation from the Caudal Funnel pass rises to the crown of the head, one exhalation descends back to the true Cavity of Breath — this is called “the Dharma Wheel has turned once” (one turn of the Celestial Circuit), and it is gathering and cooking. Throughout, the intention follows the breath as it turns, and the outer kidney naturally draws back in; then turn back to contemplate the Cavity of Breath, and in time the light of wisdom will rise from the Cavity of Breath — this one “〇”. It arises naturally once essence, breath and spirit in the body are full; in fact this “〇” is the Great Ultimate. Only when the breath of true yang unites with true inner nature does this light of wisdom arise — it is the light given off jointly by true inner nature in the tripod and true life in the furnace. The student should savor this carefully. If after a year or two the light of wisdom has not appeared, one knows that this person has not practiced diligently at all, and is moreover unwilling to accumulate virtue. For one who practices diligently and has accumulated much virtue, about a hundred days is enough to see the “elixir sprout” (the first shoot of the elixir).

§3
Original (Chinese)

外肾一举，名为活子时到，须要无念采取。而采取法复有老年、少年、童真之别。童真本元体，毫无亏损，如将其圆陀陀光灼灼之慧命，收归中宫，时时惺悟，刻刻觉照，护持十月，即可大药过关。再得出胎口诀，将道胎引出，亲为佛子，谓之顿法。余受师传后，若遇童真，非得留后再传，无使人绝其后嗣也。中年以下手法，使其补足从前亏耗之精，精气神充足，慧光自现，马阴藏相，即可大药过关，谓之渐法。如遇年迈之人，已过六十四岁者，身中真阳不生，当以添油接命法。得此真诀，淫根一萌，外肾必举，立即凝心以宰之，吸呼而吹之，顷刻之间，淫根缩回矣。

As soon as the outer kidney (the male member) rises, the “Living Zi Hour” has arrived, and it must be gathered without thought. And the method of gathering differs for the old, the young and the virginal. The virginal (those who have never lost their virginity) have an original body that is whole, without the slightest loss; if they gather their round, shining wisdom-life (original essence and original breath) into the central palace, awake at every hour and illuminating at every moment, and protect it for ten months, then the great medicine passes the gates; obtaining further the formula for “leaving the womb”, they can lead out the Embryo of the Way and become a child of the Buddha in person — this is called the “sudden method” (reaching the goal in one step). After receiving my teacher’s transmission, whenever I met a virginal person I always waited until he had left descendants before transmitting it, so as not to make anyone break his family line. The method for the middle-aged to begin is to make good the essence they have depleted in the past; when essence, breath and spirit are full, the light of wisdom appears of itself, the horse-like retraction takes place, and the great medicine can pass the gates — this is called the “gradual method” (advancing step by step). If one meets an old person who is past sixty-four, in whom true yang is no longer produced, one should use the method of “adding oil to prolong life”. The true formula for it: as soon as the root of lust stirs, the outer kidney is bound to rise; at once settle the heart-spirit to take charge of it, and “blow” on it with an inhalation and an exhalation (stirring it with the wind of the breath), and in a moment the root of lust draws back in.

§4
Original (Chinese)

即行前法而阳不缩回，另用文武火，心意注定生死窍，淫根一缩，然后吸气从尾闾关一提，升到头顶心，呼气一降落於丹田。急促谓之武火，微微谓之文火，务要息息归根，片时真阳必缩回。张三丰祖师云，年迈之人，真情不动，可用敲竹唤龟法唤之，将真阳唤起，速接真炁，非口鼻吸呼气，乃人之元炁。而人之生死，实赖此祖炁存亡以为断。身内有此炁，即有精则生；失此炁，精源竭则必死。参看炼接先天真一之炁图（图3），尤觉明了。

If, having done the above, the member still does not draw back, use civil and martial fire in another way: fix the mind on the “gate of life and death” (the area of the perineum); as soon as the root of lust draws in, then inhale, lift once from the Caudal Funnel pass, and rise to the center of the crown; exhale, and descend once back to the elixir field. Rapid breathing is called martial fire, gentle breathing civil fire; you must make every breath return to the root, and in a moment true yang will surely draw back. Patriarch Zhang Sanfeng said: in old people the true feeling (vitality) does not stir; one can use the method of “tapping the bamboo to call the tortoise” (see the Translator’s Notes) to rouse true yang, and quickly connect it with the true breath-energy — this true breath-energy is not the breath of mouth and nose but the original breath of the person. A person’s life and death truly depend on whether this ancestral breath is present or lost: with this breath in the body there is essence, and the person lives; losing this breath, the source of essence dries up, and the person must die. Look at the “Figure of Refining and Joining the Breath of the Prior Heaven True One” (Figure 3), and it will become still clearer.

Figure 3 · Refining and Joining the Breath of the Prior Heaven True One

The twelve Earthly Branches are arranged in a circle, which is one Celestial Circuit: at the very bottom is “zi” (the perineum); going up the right side through “chou, yin, mao, chen, si”, the right side is marked “Governing vessel” and “inhale and rise: advancing the yang fire”; at the very top is “wu” (the crown of the head); going down the left side through “wei, shen, you, xu, hai”, the left side is marked “Conception vessel” and “exhale and descend: withdrawing the yin tally”, returning to “zi”. In the middle is a leaf shape marked “heart”; below the heart a dotted line leads to a small circle, below which are a furnace stand and “fire” — meaning that the heart-spirit shines downward and fire (true yang) arises in the furnace. The “nine inhalations from zi to chou” and “one exhalation descending to wei” in the following passage are counted by position on this figure.

§5
Original (Chinese)

既唤起后，用意向上吸气送到炉下，九吸自子至丑。阳不缩回，再九吸自子至寅。不缩如前，一点到卯。少停，下降归炉，又九吸自子至卯。少停，复升至辰。不缩如前，又九吸自子至卯。少停，复升至巳，下降归炉。计吸四九，合成三十六，为进阳火。若吸至此，外肾仍不缩回，急速采药接炁，以补其亏损，然后复一吸自子至卯。少停，再升至午。先升不用意，后降须用意。再少停，一呼用意下降到未，六降归炉。如前一吸自子至午，一呼用意，下降到申，六降归炉。复如前法，一吸自子至午，一呼下降至酉。少停为沐浴，再降到戌，六降归炉。仍如前一吸自子至午，一呼至酉再至子，六降为止。如此二十四降，为退阴符。淫根催动炉内真火，盖此火善化五谷百味之精，变为阴精，身体恃此以强壮，精神因之而倍增，延寿保命意中之事。

Once (true yang) is roused, inhale upward with the intention and send the breath to below the furnace: nine inhalations, rising from “zi” to “chou”. If the member still does not draw back, nine more inhalations, rising from “zi” to “yin”. If it still does not draw back as before, rise in one stroke to “mao”. Pause a little, descend back into the furnace; again nine inhalations from “zi” to “mao”. Pause a little, then rise to “chen”. If it still does not draw back, again nine inhalations from “zi” to “mao”; pause a little, then rise to “si”, and descend back into the furnace. In all, four “nines” of inhalations, making thirty-six — this is called “advancing the yang fire”. If after inhaling this far the outer kidney still does not draw back, you must quickly gather the medicine and connect the breath to make good its loss; then inhale once more from “zi” to “mao”, pause a little, and rise to “wu”. In rising, do not use the intention; in descending, you must use the intention. Pause a little again, then with one exhalation descend with the intention to “wei” — (in this way) six descents back to the furnace. As before, one inhalation from “zi” to “wu”, one exhalation descending with the intention to “shen”, six descents back to the furnace. Again as before, one inhalation from “zi” to “wu”, one exhalation descending to “you”; pause a little here — this is called “ablution”; then descend to “xu”, six descents back to the furnace. Still as before, one inhalation from “zi” to “wu”, one exhalation to “you” and then to “zi”, six descents and stop. In all, twenty-four descents — this is called “withdrawing the yin tally”. (The stirring of) the root of lust drives the true fire in the furnace; this fire is good at transforming the essence of the five grains and the hundred flavors into yin essence, by which the body grows strong and the spirits are doubled; lengthening one’s years and preserving one’s life follow as a matter of course.

§6
Original (Chinese)

口中若生津液，吞入任脉，落於丹田，立化成阴精。此吞津液法，求师口传，否则必入食道，经大小肠，出二便，无所用矣。此吞法乃是造精捷径路，以舌顶上颚，舌根两穴较他时生出津液，速而且多，待至口不能容，气管正要喷出，斯时引颈而吞，必入任脉，下至真炁穴，渐化为阴精阳精，精足则气足，气足则神旺，身体焉有不强健之理。

If saliva arises in the mouth, swallow it into the Conception vessel and let it fall to the elixir field, and it at once turns into yin essence. This method of swallowing saliva must be taught by a teacher by word of mouth; otherwise the saliva will surely go into the gullet, pass through the large and small intestines, and be excreted — of no use. This way of swallowing is a shortcut to making essence: press the tongue against the palate, and the saliva produced by the two points beside the root of the tongue (Golden Fluid and Jade Liquid) comes faster and more abundantly than usual; when the mouth is so full it cannot hold any more and (the saliva) is about to spurt from the windpipe, then stretch the neck and swallow — it will surely enter the Conception vessel and go down to the true Cavity of Breath, gradually transforming into yin essence and yang essence. When essence is sufficient, breath is sufficient; when breath is sufficient, spirit flourishes — how could the body fail to be strong?

§7
Original (Chinese)

首步功夫，用真意守窍，静极慧光自然发现，久之真阳自举，乃是无念自举，当心空无我，洗心涤虑，凝神入炁穴，是为火入水中。肾水得神火之炁，化为真炁，宜急速下手采取真精。若不知静中无念，阳生采取，起邪淫之念，必变为后天邪精，不可采取，速起口鼻吸气，将阳提回，此是采自己空气，不可下手采取真阳之精。学者於此，当自留意。凡同志修道者及我门诸弟子，若用有念采，绝不能结得金丹，非师之过也，尔其慎之。

The work of Step One is to guard the opening with True Intention; when stillness reaches its extreme, the light of wisdom appears of itself; in time, true yang rises by itself — it rises of itself without any thought. At this time one should empty the mind and be without self, wash the heart and cleanse the thoughts, and concentrate the spirit and enter the Cavity of Breath — this is called “fire entering water” (the fire of the heart-spirit entering the land of kidney water). Kidney water, receiving the breath of the spirit-fire, transforms into true breath-energy, and one should quickly take it in hand and gather the true essence. If one does not know to gather it when yang arises in stillness and without thought, but instead lustful thoughts have arisen (and only then the member rises), it is bound to turn into Posterior Heaven deviant essence and cannot be gathered; one must quickly breathe in through mouth and nose and draw the yang back up — this is only gathering one’s own empty air (the breath of mouth and nose), and one must not take in hand the essence of true yang to gather it. Students should take note of this for themselves. Any fellow cultivators of the Way, and all the disciples of my school, who gather “with thought” will never form the Golden Elixir — and then it is not the teacher’s fault. Be careful!

§8
Original (Chinese)

白玉蟾真人云：“性之根，命之蒂，同出异名分两类，合归一处结成丹，还为元始先天炁。”了空禅师曰：“督升任降成鼎炉，锻炼五谷化精炁。”了然禅师曰：“火逼金行颠倒转，化谷结丹养性命。”柳华阳祖师曰：“和合凝集转法轮，吸呼薰炼性命存。”伍冲虚祖师云：“烹炼铅汞於鼎炉，炼精炼神根本地。”曹还阳祖师云：“返观凝神入炁穴，炼精百日黄芽生。”

(What follows are the words of the patriarchs of successive ages.) The Perfected Bai Yuchan said: “The root of inner nature and the stem of life come from one source but differ in name, divided into two kinds; joined and returned to one place, they form the Golden Elixir and revert to the primordial Prior Heaven breath.” The Chan master Liaokong said: “The Governing vessel rising and the Conception vessel descending form the tripod and furnace; forging the essence of the five grains, it is transformed into essence and breath.” The Chan master Liaoran said: “Fire presses the metal (the breath of true yang) to move, turning upside down (running the circuit in reverse), transforming the grain and forming the elixir, nourishing inner nature and life.” Patriarch Liu Huayang said: “Harmonizing, uniting, congealing and gathering, turning the Dharma Wheel; refined by the breath, inner nature and life are preserved.” Patriarch Wu Chongxu said: “Cooking lead and mercury (breath and spirit) in the tripod and furnace — this is the root ground for refining essence and refining spirit.” Patriarch Cao Huanyang said: “Turning the gaze back to shine within, concentrating the spirit and entering the Cavity of Breath, refining essence for a hundred days, the Yellow Sprouts (the shoots of the elixir) come forth.”

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§8
Original note (Chinese)

【注释】本口诀借玉鼎金炉之喻，指出泥丸和下丹田位置。玉鼎在泥丸，正在两耳尖上联线之中心内，即在大脑中心，又名元神室，乃神炁之宅呼吸之根。金炉即下丹田，在脐内一寸三分处，又名真炁穴，此所谓“前对脐轮后对肾，中间有个真金炉”是也。丹经常谓下丹田在脐下一寸三分处，学者须要参悟，盖古人将铜人仰卧而测之，脐下实为脐内。因此，下丹田实在脐内一寸三分处，才合“前对脐轮后对肾”之说。下丹田是存神养炁之妙处。赵氏认为真炁发动才成炉鼎，亦即机发则成窍之意。本节主要论述神炁和合凝集及阳生活子时转法轮收炁之法。学者闭目内观，两眼神光合性光下照丹田，不可拘执，在此处停神安息，以自然为主，静之又静，清之又清，一切放下，全体皆忘，心神默默，气息绵绵，进而入恍惚杳冥之境，则神与气合，阴与阳和，性命双修，即所谓“和合凝集，决定成就”，一阳初动，无中生有，外肾（即阳物）无念兴起，活子时到。行动中进入杳冥之境至为重要，若无此境，妄念未净，心亦未净，便言运河车转法轮，焉望成就？其次，必须阳生展窍，有此物质基础，才可运转河车，否则岂非存想搬运，空空往来，犹将水火煮空铛，有何益哉？赵氏在此节中未详言及此，但他在下文谈及阳生物举为活子时，阳举之急，痒生毫窍是正子时。由此则知阳物举急，正是小药不老不嫩之时，已具物质基础，并非空空搬运，须要无念采取。此即所谓“气安炉鼎前后转，无孔双吹收元精”。

古人以“六合同春，物物得所”来形容丹田之内忽然无中生有，融暖和畅，一派春景，其乐无穷。柳华阳在《慧命经》中曾有一段有名的文字描写神炁相交产出真种时的景象。他说：“六合者，周身也。春者喻周身之暖信也。物者乃释教之别名，即儒所谓元炁也。功到时至，此物当产之时，不知不觉忽然丹田融融洽洽，周身苏绵快乐，痒生毫窍，身心无主，丹田暖融，渐渐而开，阳物勃然而举，忽然一吼，呼吸顿断，心物如磁石之相翕，意息如蛰虫之相含，不觉入于恍惚，天地人我，莫知所之，浑浑沦沦，又非今之禅家枯寂无为，恍惚之中，心自不肯舍其物，物自不肯离其心，相亲相恋，纽结一团，其中景象，似施似翕，而实未见其施翕，似泄似漏，而实未至于泄漏，其妙不可以言语形容，故心经解云：‘一阳初动，有无穷之消息’。少焉，恍恍惚惚，心以复灵，呼吸复起，丹田之炁自下往后而行，肾管之根，毛际之间，痒生快乐，实不能禁止，所谓炁满任督自开，此之谓也。迅时速采归源，转大法轮，不然，此物满而又溢，则前功废却矣！”此段描写，具体而生动，学者可于行功中到时加以证验。柳华阳又云：“毋忽却其中景象，但得二、三，即是真种所产矣！固不必规规如此，而又在禀受形体有同异之别也。”

静坐行功中，一阳初动，无念而阳物勃兴起，是活子时到来，如本节所述，不等念起，急用转法轮生精接炁法。千峰门人牛金宝在其所著《性命双修养生延寿法》一书中，比较通俗地介绍了这一方法。即用力一提会阴（又名生死窍），鼻根吸气，眼往上翻看，以神领气，心意随之，再由子位（会阴）向后走尾闾、夹脊、玉枕，至午位（百会）一停。一吸由督脉上来稍停。再一用鼻根呼气，眼往下看，以神领气，心意随之，再由午位（百会）下来走祖窍、绛宫、炁穴（即下丹田），直达子位（会阴）。如此后升前降一周，一吸一呼转一周为一次。三至五个呼吸，外阳即可缩回。如经七次呼吸，外阳仍然不倒，应改用无孔笛颠倒两头吹降龙法以制止。此法是凝神会阴，向上一提，鼻根吸气，意想由会阴提到下丹田，再用绛宫之气，下沉丹田，鼻根呼气，心神意随之一沉。一提一沉为一次，如此一至三次，外阳立缩。行此转法轮法，即是采小药，不使元精顺流而出，逆行而为我所用，以补亏损。晚间睡醒无念而阳举，亦是活子时到，可起坐行上法。

This step’s formula uses the metaphor of the “jade tripod and golden furnace” to point out the positions of the Muddy Pellet and the lower elixir field. The jade tripod is in the Muddy Pellet, straight in from the midpoint of the line joining the tips of the two ears, that is, the center of the brain; it is also called the “chamber of the Original Spirit”, the dwelling of spirit and breath, the root of breathing. The golden furnace is the lower elixir field, one cun three fen inside the navel, also called the “true Cavity of Breath” — this is what is meant by “facing the navel in front and the kidneys behind, in the middle there is a true golden furnace”. The alchemical scriptures often say the lower elixir field is “one cun three fen below the navel”; students should consider this carefully: the ancients measured on a bronze acupuncture figure laid flat on its back, so “below the navel” really means “inside the navel” (from the navel into the body). Therefore the lower elixir field is really one cun three fen inside the navel, which alone fits the saying “facing the navel in front and the kidneys behind”. The lower elixir field is the wondrous place for storing spirit and nourishing breath. Zhao holds that only when the true breath-energy stirs does it become furnace and tripod, which is the meaning of “when the moment stirs, it becomes the opening”.

This section mainly speaks of the harmonizing, uniting, congealing and gathering of spirit and breath, and of the method of “turning the Dharma Wheel to gather in the breath” when yang arises and the Living Zi Hour arrives. The student closes the eyes and looks within, letting the spirit-light of the two eyes, joined with the light of inner nature, shine down on the elixir field — not rigidly or with fixation; here let the spirit stop and the breath settle, taking naturalness as the main thing; still and stiller, clear and clearer, putting everything down, forgetting the whole body, the heart-spirit silent, the breath soft and continuous, one then enters the state of the dim and obscure; then spirit and breath unite, yin and yang harmonize, inner nature and life are cultivated together — this is what is meant by “harmonize, unite, congeal and gather, and success is certain”. At this time the one yang first stirs, being is born from non-being, the outer kidney (that is, the male member) rises without thought, and the Living Zi Hour has arrived. Entering the obscure state during practice is what matters most: without this state, with deluded thoughts not yet cleared and the mind not yet pure, how can one hope for success by talking of running the River Chariot and turning the Dharma Wheel? Next, there must be “yang arising and the opening unfolding”; only with this material basis can one turn the River Chariot; otherwise is it not transporting by imagination, coming and going in emptiness, like boiling an empty pot with water and fire — what good would it do? Zhao does not speak of this in detail in this section, but later he says that “yang arising and the member rising” is the Living Zi Hour, and that “when the yang rises urgently and itching arises in the pores” it is the True Zi Hour. From this we know that when the member rises urgently is exactly when the lesser medicine is neither too old nor too tender; the material basis is already there, it is not empty transporting, and it must be gathered without thought. This is what is meant by “the breath settles in furnace and tripod and turns behind and in front; blowing the holeless flute at both ends, one gathers back the original essence”.

The ancients used “the six directions sharing one spring, every thing finding its place” to describe the scene in the elixir field when suddenly being is born from non-being, melting warm and harmonious, a whole expanse of spring, with joy beyond end. Liu Huayang has a famous passage in the Book of Wisdom and Life describing the scene when spirit and breath join and the true seed is produced. He says: “‘The six directions’ means the whole body. ‘Spring’ is a metaphor for the warm message throughout the body. ‘Thing’ is another Buddhist name, what the Confucians call the original breath. When the work is done and the time has come and this ‘thing’ is about to be produced, without one’s knowing, the elixir field suddenly melts in harmony, the whole body is soft and blissful, the pores itch, and neither body nor mind can be its own master; the elixir field is warm and harmonious and gradually opens, and the member rises vigorously; suddenly, all at once, the breath stops dead, the mind and the thing attract each other like lodestones, intention and breath hold each other like hibernating insects, and without knowing one enters the dim state; heaven and earth, others and self, all vanish who knows where, all undivided and murky — yet it is not the withered, inactive stillness of today’s Chan practitioners. In the dimness the mind naturally will not let go of its ‘thing’, and the thing naturally will not leave its mind; they cling lovingly to each other and knot into one mass. The scene within seems to be releasing and seems to be closing, yet there is no real releasing or closing; it seems about to leak and about to drip, yet there is no leaking. Its wonders cannot be put into words, which is why the Explanation of the Heart Sutra says: ‘When the one yang first stirs, there is endless news.' After a while, in the dimness the mind becomes numinous and bright again and the breathing resumes; the breath of the elixir field moves from below toward the back, and at the root of the spermatic duct, among the pubic hair, there is itching and bliss that truly cannot be held back — what is meant by ‘when the breath is full, the Conception and Governing vessels open of themselves’ is this. At this time one must gather it swiftly and return it to its source, turning the great Dharma Wheel; otherwise this ‘thing’, once full, will overflow again, and the earlier work will be wasted!” This description is concrete and vivid, and students can verify it when their practice reaches this point. Liu Huayang also says: “Do not neglect the scene within; if you have two or three of its features, the true seed has been produced! It need not be like this in every respect; this depends on whether the forms people are endowed with are the same or different.”

In quiet-sitting practice, when the one yang first stirs and the member rises vigorously without thought, the Living Zi Hour has arrived; as this section says, before any thought arises, quickly use the method of “turning the Dharma Wheel, producing essence and connecting the breath”. Niu Jinbao, a disciple of the Qianfeng school, introduces this method fairly plainly in his book Methods of Nourishing Life and Prolonging Years through the Dual Cultivation of Inner Nature and Life: lift the perineum (also called the gate of life and death) firmly once, inhale through the root of the nose, roll the eyes upward, lead the breath with the spirit, the intention following, from the zi position (the perineum) back through the Caudal Funnel, the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow, to the wu position (Baihui, the crown), and stop there — this inhalation comes up the Governing vessel; pause briefly. Then exhale through the root of the nose, look downward, lead the breath with the spirit, the intention following, from the wu position (Baihui) down through the Ancestral Opening, the Crimson Palace and the Cavity of Breath (that is, the lower elixir field), all the way to the zi position (the perineum). In this way, rising behind and descending in front makes one round; one inhalation and one exhalation turning one round counts as one time. After three to five breaths, the outer yang can draw back. If after seven breaths the outer yang still does not go down, one should switch to the “method of subduing the dragon by blowing the holeless flute at both ends upside down” to stop it: concentrate the spirit at the perineum, lift upward once, inhale through the root of the nose, imagining it drawn from the perineum up to the lower elixir field; then let the breath of the Crimson Palace (below the heart) sink down to the elixir field, exhale through the root of the nose, and let the heart-spirit and intention sink along with it. One lift and one sinking make one time; done one to three times, the outer yang draws back at once. Doing this method of turning the Dharma Wheel is gathering the lesser medicine: not letting the original essence flow forward and out, but making it run back for one’s own use to make good the loss. When on waking at night the member rises without thought, that too is the arrival of the Living Zi Hour, and one can get up, sit, and do the method above.

Key Terms

  • Jade tripod: the center of the head (the Muddy Pellet), where the Original Spirit dwells; see Muddy Pellet.
  • Golden furnace, true Cavity of Breath: the lower elixir field, one cun three fen inside the navel, “facing the navel in front and the kidneys behind”; see Cavity of Breath, lower elixir field.
  • Crossroads: where the line joining the two eyes crosses the midline of the nose (the area of the Ancestral Opening).
  • Seven in front, three behind: the front-to-back thickness of the body divided into ten parts, the point seven parts in from the front and three in from the back.
  • Harmonizing, uniting, congealing and gathering: the heart-spirit descends and unites with the kidney breath (harmonizing, uniting), the two eyes return to one (congealing), shining down on the navel and belly so that true life gathers (gathering).
  • Kun navel: the area of the navel.
  • Dharma Wheel: the turning of the circuit; one inhalation rising from the Caudal Funnel to the crown and one exhalation descending back to the Cavity of Breath is “one turn of the Dharma Wheel”; see Celestial Circuit.
  • Living Zi Hour: when, in stillness, the outer kidney rises of itself without thought.
  • Sudden method, gradual method: for the virginal, protecting it in one step to form the elixir is the sudden method; for the middle-aged, first making good the losses and then advancing is the gradual method.
  • Adding oil to prolong life: for those past sixty-four in whom true yang is no longer produced, a method of rousing and connecting the original breath with breath and intention.
  • Gate of life and death: the area of the perineum, the gateway through which essence goes out.
  • Tapping the bamboo to call the tortoise: a method of rousing the vitality of the elderly (not explained in the original; see the Translator’s Notes).
  • Advancing the yang fire, withdrawing the yin tally: inhaling up the Governing vessel is advancing the yang fire (thirty-six inhalations in all); exhaling down the Conception vessel is withdrawing the yin tally (twenty-four descents in all).
  • Ablution: pausing briefly at the mao and you positions during the circuit, neither advancing nor withdrawing.
  • Swallowing the saliva: when the mouth is full of saliva, stretching the neck and swallowing it into the Conception vessel, sending it to the Cavity of Breath to turn into essence.
  • Fire entering water: the heart-spirit (fire) shining down on the kidneys and lower elixir field (water).
  • Yellow Sprouts: the shoots of the elixir.
  • Yang arising and the opening unfolding: true yang stirring and the opening opening; see “unfolding the opening” in book 3.
  • Method of subduing the dragon by blowing the holeless flute at both ends upside down: a method of drawing back the yang by lifting the perineum and sinking the Crimson Palace.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Where the lower elixir field is: one cun three fen “inside” the navel, midway between the navel in front and the kidneys behind — not the surface of the belly. When shining on it, “do not be rigid or fixated”; take naturalness as the main thing.
  2. The order must not be reversed: first “harmonize, unite, congeal and gather” — the spirit shines down on the elixir field, still until dim and obscure; only when there is a real arising of yang can there be any question of turning the Dharma Wheel. Turning the circuit by intention without yang arising is “boiling water in an empty pot”, which Qiu Ling clearly opposes.
  3. Distinguish with thought from without thought: only what arises without thought in stillness is “medicine”; what arises from thought or desire is only to be drawn back up by breathing in through mouth and nose, not gathered as medicine. This is the most important distinction in this step.
  4. Breathing and counts: the original’s thirty-six inhalations and twenty-four descents are rules of fire phasing; beginners need not count rigidly. Martial fire (rapid breathing) may only be used briefly; ordinarily take civil fire (gentle breathing) as the main thing.
  5. Safety: forcefully lifting the perineum, rolling the eyes upward and rapid breathing are unsuitable for people with high blood pressure, heart disease, glaucoma, hemorrhoids or prostate problems; anyone who feels a swollen head, palpitations, or swelling or pain in the lower belly or perineum should stop, and replace these actions with the spirit-light gently shining downward.
  6. Swallowing saliva: with the tongue against the palate, swallow calmly when the mouth is full of saliva, sending it with the intention to the elixir field — simple and safe, something anyone can do.

Translator's Notes

  1. In the original book this step has one formula, two figures and seven passages of explanation, the last collecting sayings of the patriarchs; we follow the original’s division and number them §1–§8, placing Figure 3 where the original mentions it (after §4).
  2. “Seven in front, three behind” (前七后三): not explained in the original. The usual understanding divides the front-to-back thickness of the belly into ten parts, the lower elixir field lying at the boundary seven parts from the front and three from the back.
  3. “Root of lust”, “outer kidney”, “male member” (淫根、外肾、阳物): in the original all refer to the male reproductive organ; the translation renders them as meant, without euphemism. This step describes men’s practice; women’s practice is in Appendix Four.
  4. “Blow on it with inhalation and exhalation” (吸呼而吹之): stirring it with the wind of the breath; alchemists often liken breathing to the “Wind of Xun” or to a “bellows”.
  5. “Tapping the bamboo to call the tortoise” (敲竹唤龟): a coded term from Zhang Sanfeng’s alchemical formulas; the original does not elaborate, and explanations differ. This site does not speculate and translates it as it stands.
  6. “Nine inhalations from zi to chou … in one stroke to mao”: the counts and positions are translated as in the original; the two occurrences of “again nine inhalations from zi to mao” are so in the original, possibly a repetition, and have not been changed.
  7. “Then stretch the neck and swallow, and it will surely enter the Conception vessel” (斯时引颈而吞，必入任脉): in modern physiology saliva can only enter the gullet; this is the alchemists’ way of describing sending the saliva downward with the intention, and the translation renders it as meant.
  8. Qiu Ling’s remark that “below the navel” really means “inside the navel” can be compared with Huang Yuanji’s “one cun three fen from the skin and flesh below the navel” in book 3: both stress that it is inside the belly, not on the surface.
  9. Qiu Ling’s note retells the method in Niu Jinbao’s Methods of Nourishing Life and Prolonging Years through the Dual Cultivation of Inner Nature and Life; this passage is Qiu Ling’s own writing and is reproduced as it stands.
