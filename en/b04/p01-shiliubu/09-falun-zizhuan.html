The Sixteen Steps of Method Formulas · 第九步 法轮自转

Step Nine · The Dharma Wheel Turns by Itself

The formula of Step Nine, the Dharma Wheel turns by itself — the closing and opening of Qian and Kun are the count of the circuit; closing is inhaling, opening is exhaling, and the bellows rise

About This Chapter

In the earlier steps the circuit had to be “turned” with the intention and the breath; at this step the circuit turns by itself — this is called “the Dharma Wheel turns by itself.” Zhao Bichen says that at this point one no longer breathes through mouth and nose; instead there are the “four breathings going back and forth” of “inhaling at the heel, exhaling at the stem.”

  1. Heel and stem: “heel” (zhong) originally means the back of the foot, but Zhao says “the head of the heel is in the sea of the back of the brain, that is, the medulla oblongata, and it connects down to the back of the foot”; the “stem” (di) is at the Ancestral Opening (the Mountain Root) and connects down to the Opening of Life and Death. On the inhalation, the heart and intention go from the Ancestral Opening to the medulla; this is called “waning”; on the exhalation, from the medulla to the Ancestral Opening; this is called “waxing” — these are the two Posterior Heaven breathings.
  2. The four breathings: the Posterior Heaven breath, rising and descending, draws the Prior Heaven true breath to descend and rise — “this one up, that one down; that one down, this one up”; two Posterior Heaven and two Prior Heaven together are called the four breathings going back and forth. This happens of itself; it is not turned with the heart and intention.
  3. Entering absorption: after the Dharma Wheel turns by itself, one enters embryonic breathing and great absorption — “lesser stillness one day, middle stillness three days, great stillness seven days”; someone must watch over you, and you must not be startled.
  4. Guarding against deviations: a cold body giving off cool breath is a “yin scene,” treated with the samādhi true fire (the light of the spirit shining on the Cavity of Breath); “deviant fire” arising in sitting (blurred vision, double vision, dilated pupils, reddened whites of the eyes, restlessness and thirst) is treated with Step Five, or by visualizing a black ball and breathing in cool breath.
  5. Guarding against losing the original essence at night: against the demons of lust, food and sleep, he presents the sleeping method of “five dragons coiling the body” and the “method of restful repose.”
  6. Zhao’s own account: at the mao hour on a day in the second month of 1922, he experienced in practice the scene of the Dharma Wheel turning in a great way.

Qiu Ling’s note distinguishes the “four breathings” one by one (which two belong to Posterior Heaven and which two to Prior Heaven), quotes Liu Huayang on how the True Intention presides (the intention is the hub, the spirit the spokes, the breathing the axle), and says that at this step the Lesser Celestial Circuit is about to be completed and the Greater Celestial Circuit is about to begin.

Safety note (translator): The account in this step of absorption “in a chaotic unknowing, as if the breath had stopped and the body died,” lasting up to seven days, is a record of ancient practitioners; do not imitate it; deep absorption requires someone to watch over you. The signs of “deviant fire” listed here — double vision, dilated pupils, reddened whites of the eyes, a hot heart and restlessness — may, in the view of modern medicine, be signs of eye, neurological or cardiovascular disease; if they occur, see a doctor promptly and do not rely on practice methods alone. Visualizations such as “fierce flames leaping” or a “black ball” should be done gently, without strain.

Original Text and Translation

Figure 23 · Breathing at the Heel and the Stem

In the figure a person kneels, facing left, both fists joined and raised before the chest. Above the head a circle is marked “wu,” below the body a circle is marked “zi.” A double-lined channel runs from the crown down the back, turns forward at the seat, and runs down the legs to the feet. In front of the face is marked “connects to the stem,” behind the head “connects to the heel,” in front of the belly (at the seat) “the stem connects,” and beside the feet “connects to the heel” — meaning: the stem (the Ancestral Opening) connects down to the Opening of Life and Death, and the heel (the medulla) connects down to the back of the foot.

Formula
Original (Chinese)

乾坤阖辟周天数，

阖吸辟呼橐籥升。

乾九坤六河车转，

四揲成章真意空。

The one closing and one opening of Qian and Kun are the count of the circuit; closing is inhaling, opening is exhaling, and the bellows (the inner bellows) rise and fall. Qian uses nine and Kun uses six, and the River Chariot turns by itself; counted off by fours, the pattern is complete (making up the counts of thirty-six and twenty-four, see Step Four), yet the True Intention is empty (one does not push with the intention).

§1
Original (Chinese)

阖吸虽是下坤，坤腹元炁踵升上乾。辟呼虽是上乾，乾顶元炁蒂降下坤。此吸呼之气，非是口鼻吸呼气，是真人吸于踵呼于蒂之消息也。若用口鼻吸呼气，非是真道。若无真师传授，法轮不自转，非是四个呼吸往来。

Although closing and inhaling go down to Kun (the belly), the original breath of the Kun belly rises from the “heel” up to Qian (the crown); although opening and exhaling go up to Qian, the original breath of the Qian summit descends from the “stem” down to Kun. This breath of inhaling and exhaling is not the breath of breathing through mouth and nose, but the waxing and waning of “the true person inhales at the heel and exhales at the stem” (the Zhuangzi: “the true person breathes from the heels”). If one breathes through mouth and nose, it is not the true Way. Without a true teacher’s transmission, the Dharma Wheel will not turn by itself, and it is not “the four breathings going back and forth.”

§2
Original (Chinese)

前第八步功是蛰藏之法诀，将祖炁收入安神祖窍内，送入下丹田，此时二目下照丹田，心意无他无我，混沌不分，出息微微，入息绵绵，渐渐入而渐渐柔，渐渐和而渐渐定，久则窍中发动息，上不过心，下不过肾，久动而定，自然内气不出，外气返进，此是胎息还元之初，众妙归根之始也。入我窍中之窍，大定得矣，正在胎息妙凝，入无积聚，出无分散，恍恍惚惚，无他无我之时。入定久久，内外合一，动静俱无，璇玑停轮，日月合璧，万里阴沉春气合，九宵清彻露华凝，妙矣哉！其阴阳交感之真景象得矣！元精吐华，而乾金出矿矣。

The practice of Step Eight above is the formula of hibernating storage: drawing the ancestral breath into the “settling the spirit in the Ancestral Opening” and sending it into the lower elixir field. Now the two eyes shine down on the elixir field, the heart and intention without other and without self, an undivided chaos, the out-breath faint, the in-breath soft and continuous, gradually going deeper, gradually softer, gradually more harmonious, gradually more settled; after a long time “breath” stirs within the opening, going up no further than the heart and down no further than the kidneys; after long movement it settles, and naturally the inner breath does not go out and the outer breath turns back in — this is the beginning of embryonic breathing returning to the origin, the start of all wonders returning to the root. Entering my “opening within the opening,” one attains great absorption: just when the embryonic breathing is wondrously condensed, going in without accumulating, coming out without scattering, dim and indistinct, without other and without self. When absorption has lasted long, inner and outer become one, movement and stillness both vanish, “the armillary stops turning” (the motion of the heavens comes to a halt), “sun and moon join like a jade disc” (the two eyes become one), “across ten thousand li of gloom the breath of spring merges; the nine heavens are clear and the dew condenses” — wonderful! The true scene of the mutual stirring of yin and yang is obtained! The original essence gives off its quintessence, and “the metal of Qian comes out of the ore” (the metal of pure yang is refined out of the ore).

§3
Original (Chinese)

此系重开混沌，再入胞胎，开我后天胎息之气，非是着于口鼻，亦非闭气于丹田。若用口鼻吸呼气，非是性命双修金丹大道。后天吸呼气者，吸于踵，呼于蒂，以为吹嘘，逼运真炁动也。后天吸于踵，呼于蒂，是消息也。一吸踵，真炁由后督脉上升至巳。一呼蒂，真炁由任脉下降至亥，久久升降。此是后天呼吸，一上一下，引动先天炁穴内真炁。后天一呼蒂气，先天真炁由泥丸宫经任脉一度一度下降生死窍。后天一吸踵气，先天真炁由督脉上升至泥丸。此是内外先天后天炁气四个往来，不用口鼻吸呼气。若用口鼻，非是真道。不由口鼻呼吸，究由何处出入？吸于踵，是由祖窍内至延髓，下通于踵（即脚后跟）。祖窍内一吸气，心意由祖窍至延髓为“消”。后天真气由踵上升至尾闾，过夹脊、玉枕至泥丸宫，此为根生于踵。又呼于蒂，是由延髓往外至祖窍，下通任脉之根（即生死窍）。一呼气，心意由延髓至祖窍为“息”。后天真气由蒂下降至祖窍，过十二重楼、绛宫、炁穴至生死窍，此为发于蒂。此是后天两个呼吸，踵蒂消息，不用口鼻呼气。此踵蒂消息气久炼，自然先天真炁被后天气引动，先天炁升，后天气降；后天气升，先天炁降。是此上彼下，彼下此上之炁气，此是四个呼吸，是自然而然之功。真炁转动，是炼舍利子之法，是借后天之息以为吹嘘，逼运炁穴，俟生机发动，因此而调息。既调动炁穴内真息，而后天之息也自然至于炁穴，相兼相连同动，是此升彼降之息息，故先天后天原有兼连之消息。凡调后天之息时，二目专视先天炁穴内，好融化原炁养舍利子。行住起止，是转法轮之四正。借行住起止，融化真炁，养我真命。此真炁，与后天之息转动，是无时无候无间断，自然而然之真息转动，此为法轮自转，大周天也。

This is reopening the primordial chaos and re-entering the womb, opening my Posterior Heaven embryonic breath — not fixing on mouth and nose, nor shutting the breath in the elixir field. If one breathes through mouth and nose, it is not the great Way of the Golden Elixir of the dual cultivation of nature and life. What is called Posterior Heaven breathing is “inhaling at the heel and exhaling at the stem,” used to blow and puff (to stir), driving the true breath to move. Posterior Heaven inhaling at the heel and exhaling at the stem is “waning and waxing” (the change of one waning and one growing). With one inhalation at the heel, the true breath rises from behind up the Governing Vessel to “si”; with one exhalation at the stem, the true breath descends by the Conception Vessel to “hai”; rising and descending for a long time. This is Posterior Heaven breathing, one up and one down, drawing the true breath in the Prior Heaven Cavity of Breath: with one Posterior Heaven exhalation of stem breath, the Prior Heaven true breath descends degree by degree from the Muddy Pellet palace by the Conception Vessel to the Opening of Life and Death; with one Posterior Heaven inhalation of heel breath, the Prior Heaven true breath rises by the Governing Vessel to the Muddy Pellet. These are the “four goings and comings” of the breath inner and outer, Prior Heaven and Posterior Heaven, without breathing through mouth and nose. If one uses mouth and nose, it is not the true Way.

If not through mouth and nose, then where exactly does it go in and out? “Inhaling at the heel” is from inside the Ancestral Opening to the medulla oblongata (the lower back part of the brain), connecting down to the “heel” (that is, the back of the foot): with one inhalation within the Ancestral Opening, the heart and intention go from the Ancestral Opening to the medulla, which is called “waning”; the Posterior Heaven true breath rises from the heel to the Caudal Funnel, passes the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow, and reaches the Muddy Pellet palace — this is called “rooted at the heel.” “Exhaling at the stem” is from the medulla outward to the Ancestral Opening, connecting down to the root of the Conception Vessel (that is, the Opening of Life and Death): with one exhalation, the heart and intention go from the medulla to the Ancestral Opening, which is called “waxing”; the Posterior Heaven true breath descends from the “stem” to the Ancestral Opening, passes the Twelve-Storied Tower, the Crimson Palace and the Cavity of Breath, and reaches the Opening of Life and Death — this is called “issuing from the stem.” These are the two Posterior Heaven breathings, that is, “the waning and waxing of heel and stem,” without breathing through mouth and nose.

When this breath of the waning and waxing of heel and stem has been refined for a long time, the Prior Heaven true breath is naturally drawn by the Posterior Heaven breath: when the Prior Heaven breath rises, the Posterior Heaven breath descends; when the Posterior Heaven breath rises, the Prior Heaven breath descends — this breath that is “this one up, that one down; that one down, this one up” is the “four breathings,” a practice that happens of itself. The turning of the true breath is the method of refining the śarīra: borrowing the Posterior Heaven breath to blow and puff, driving the Cavity of Breath to move, waiting for the mechanism of life to stir, and on that basis regulating the breath. Once the True Breath within the Cavity of Breath has been set in motion, the Posterior Heaven breath also naturally reaches the Cavity of Breath, and they move together, joined and linked — this rising and that descending, breath linked with breath, so that Prior and Posterior Heaven have from the start a joined and linked waxing and waning. Whenever regulating the Posterior Heaven breath, the two eyes gaze intently into the Prior Heaven Cavity of Breath, so as to melt the original breath (the original writes 原炁) and nourish the śarīra. Moving, staying, rising and stopping are the “four cardinal points” of turning the Dharma Wheel (see Step Four); by means of moving, staying, rising and stopping, the true breath is melted and my true life nourished. This true breath turns together with the Posterior Heaven breath, at no fixed time or period, without interruption; it is the turning of the True Breath that happens of itself — this is “the Dharma Wheel turns by itself,” this is the Greater Celestial Circuit.

§4
Original (Chinese)

行功至此，须防夜内走失元阳，一不留神，元精失去，前功罔费。学者至此，细心炼之。若有一日不用功，夜里睡魔生梦，失去元精；功勤者无梦。将元精炼足，成为舍利子，全凭四个吸呼往来。不用口鼻吸呼气，此是真息。伍祖冲虚云：“火候谁云不可传，随机默运入玄玄，达观往昔千千圣，呼吸分明了却仙”。吸呼气无者，须得静定之极，不省人事，气息全无，六脉皆住，此是蛰藏之功。前八步功法，是小定静。这无口鼻吸呼气，是大定静。初入定静时，小静一日，混沌无知，如气绝身死一般；中静三日；大静七日。不可认为这是坐化，这是神炁归根复命之时，是炁养舍利子成珠之景象。静定时须要侣伴护持，千万不可惊动入定之阳神。修士亦不可因机而动，妄自出静，应当由炁住而自然入于大定，此时将见先天一炁，自虚无中来。无有口鼻呼吸气，便为入定，由息住而胎稳如山，用功久之，小定大定得矣。万象全凭虚无感化，昼夜凝神入于其中，而舍利子全凭虚无静定养成。若不能入定，皈于虚无，永无结舍利子之理。入定者如同命将绝，然绝后复生是正功。如何绝后又复生？此是炼舍利子之正功。当性命入于混沌窍内，一不小心谨守，神离窍中，夜内真宝遗失，此正是接命在此，伤命亦在此。要死心入定，凝神于窍中，此为紧要之法诀。古仙云：“当初一念转动，坠入苦海；我今一念主静，渡过彼岸”。

When practice reaches this point, one must guard against losing the original yang at night: at the slightest lapse the original essence is lost and all previous work is wasted. Students who have come this far must refine carefully. If one day one does not practice, at night the demon of sleep produces dreams and the original essence is lost; those who practice diligently have no dreams. Refining the original essence to sufficiency so that it becomes the śarīra depends wholly on the four breathings going back and forth. Not breathing through mouth and nose — that alone is the True Breath. Patriarch Wu Chongxu said: “Who says fire phasing cannot be transmitted? Moving it silently with the moment, one enters the mystery of mysteries; look back over the thousands upon thousands of sages of the past: they made the breathing clear and so completed immortality.”

What is called having no breathing must be stillness and absorption at their utmost, unconscious of everything, the breath entirely gone, the six pulses all stopped — this is the work of hibernating storage. The eight steps of practice before are “lesser stillness and absorption”; this, without breathing through mouth and nose, is “great stillness and absorption.” When one first enters stillness and absorption, lesser stillness lasts one day, in a chaotic unknowing, as if the breath had stopped and the body died; middle stillness three days; great stillness seven days. One must not take this for “dying while seated”; it is the time when spirit and breath return to the root and restore life, the scene of the breath nourishing the śarīra and forming the precious pearl. In stillness and absorption there must be a companion to watch over one, and the Yang Spirit in absorption must on no account be startled. Nor should practitioners rashly come out of stillness because some circumstance stirs; they should let the breath come to rest and so enter great absorption naturally; at this time they will see the one breath of Prior Heaven come from emptiness and non-being. Having no breathing through mouth and nose is entering absorption; from the breath coming to rest, the embryo becomes as steady as a mountain; with long practice, both lesser and great absorption are obtained. The ten thousand phenomena depend wholly on emptiness and non-being to transform them; condense the spirit day and night and enter into it; the śarīra depends wholly on the stillness and absorption of emptiness and non-being to be nourished. If one cannot enter absorption and return to emptiness and non-being, there is never any way to form the śarīra. The one in absorption seems about to lose his life, but after it is cut off it revives again — only this is the right practice. How does it revive after being cut off? This is the right practice of refining the śarīra. When inner nature and life enter the opening of chaos, at the slightest lapse in careful guarding, the spirit leaves the opening, and at night the true treasure is lost — prolonging life lies here, and harming life lies here too. Set your heart wholly on entering absorption and condense the spirit within the opening — this is a crucial formula. The ancient immortals said: “Once, a single thought stirred, and I fell into the sea of suffering; now a single thought presides over stillness, and I cross to the other shore.”

§5
Original (Chinese)

如有身冷出凉气之时，此皆因自然之吹嘘间断，元神不守窍内，内养不到，而丹田火冷，丹光不现，故有此阴景。然而阴景变化多端，皆是丹田炁冷之故。若何为之？法诀曰，聚我三昧真火而锻炼之，凝神入于中宫，注意于胎息处，以二目合并，用眸光射定炁穴内，武光吹嘘，存心结想，一团真火，烈焰腾腾，满鼎神光，照耀周身，薰蒸四大，则阴魔坏景自然消灭，而中宫之丹光复明矣。这是修性炼命之真功，要细心锻炼，若有一点粗心，丹失光无，岂不徒劳哉！亦有静坐生邪火者，是由肝肾两处生出，先是两眼云朦昏花，视物或成二体，久视则光不收，瞳仁散大，白眼珠发红，而不能使之消灭。此患因多嗜饮食，内有生邪火之物，或因心想邪火之事，或因热水浴身引动丹火，到处流光，火焰焚身者有之。其幻景亦有多端，皆是自己不戒于火象，或觉心热、烦燥发渴、饮食不休，倘不知制伏，夜内亦会走失真宝。功夫至此，学者应注意以何法灭之。若是肝肾之火，可用第五步功，可将此火灭之；若是心邪、饮食等等之火，可于静坐时，存心结想面前一团黑球，其大如拳，存想黑云亦可，即以真意凝聚，用第七步翕聚法，将黑光收归我有，用口意吸清凉气，送入丹田内，再一呼气，微微喷出，邪火慢慢被引出，自然消灭，心地自然清凉。其想面前黑云、黑球，吸入丹田，呼出毒火，可不拘次数，总以热气邪火退尽为止，从此安乐太平，神清气爽，方保无失。

If there are times when the body feels cold and gives off cool breath, it is always because the natural blowing and puffing (the stirring of the breath) has been interrupted, the Original Spirit does not stay within the opening, inner nourishment does not reach, the fire of the elixir field has gone cold and the light of the elixir does not appear — hence this “yin scene.” Yin scenes change in many ways, all because the breath of the elixir field is cold. What is to be done? The formula says: gather my “samādhi true fire” (the true fire of heart, kidneys and bladder united into one) to forge it: condense the spirit and enter the central palace, attend to the place of embryonic breathing, join the two eyes and fix the light of the eyes within the Cavity of Breath, blow and puff with the “martial light,” and visualize a ball of true fire, its fierce flames leaping, the whole tripod filled with spiritual light, shining through the whole body and steaming the four great elements — the yin demons and bad scenes naturally vanish, and the light of the elixir in the central palace is bright again. This is the true practice of cultivating inner nature and refining life, to be forged with care; at the slightest carelessness the elixir is lost and the light gone — would that not be all that toil for nothing!

There are also those in whom quiet sitting produces “deviant fire,” which arises from the liver and kidneys: first the two eyes seem covered by cloud and mist, blurred, sometimes seeing things double; after long looking the light cannot be drawn back, the pupils dilate, the whites of the eyes redden, and one cannot make it go away. This trouble comes from indulging too much in food and drink, with things in the body that produce deviant fire; or from the heart dwelling on matters of deviant fire; or from bathing in hot water, which stirs the elixir fire so that light flows everywhere, some even feeling as if flames were burning the body. Its illusory scenes are also of many kinds, all because one has not guarded oneself against the signs of fire — or one feels the heart hot, restless and thirsty, and eats without stopping; if one does not know how to subdue it, the true treasure will also be lost at night. When practice reaches this point, students should take care to know by what method to extinguish it. If it is the fire of the liver and kidneys, one can extinguish it with the practice of Step Five (the outer civil fire); if it is the fire of a deviant heart, of food and so on, one can, while sitting quietly, visualize a black ball in front of one, the size of a fist (visualizing black clouds also works), condense it with the True Intention, and with the gathering-in method of Step Seven take the black light into one’s possession; breathe in cool breath with the mouth and intention and send it to the elixir field; then with one exhalation, puff it out gently, and the deviant fire is slowly drawn out and naturally extinguished, and the ground of the heart naturally becomes cool. Visualizing black clouds or a black ball in front of one, breathing it into the elixir field and breathing out the poisonous fire may be done any number of times, always until the heat and deviant fire have entirely receded. From then on there is peace and ease, the spirit clear and the breath fresh, and only then can one be sure of making no mistake.

§6

问曰：师言色魔、食魔好炼，惟有睡魔难消，又言用五龙盘体之法诀，其梦全消，其精自固不失，乞示知。

Question: Teacher, you say the demons of lust and food are easy to refine, only the demon of sleep is hard to dispel; you also say that with the formula of “five dragons coiling the body,” dreams disappear entirely and the essence naturally stays firm and is not lost. Please instruct me.

Answer: Against the demon of lust, one can use the Wind of Xun, the bellows and the six periods (see Step Four), transforming until the horse-like retraction, and the demon of lust naturally vanishes. Against the demon of food, if the body is strong enough, one can eat plain vegetarian rice and vegetables and give up all spices and the “five pungents” (scallion, garlic, chives, shallot, asafoetida and other pungent things). For fragrance scatters the breath, and the five pungents can produce lustful essence; carefully giving up the five strong-smelling foods is dispelling the demon of food. Only the demon of sleep is hard to refine, which is why the Chan school has the method of “long sitting without lying down.” Everything people dream of in sleep is false; only the loss of the original essence is real. Yet why is the original essence lost at night even without dreams? A person’s cognitive spirit lodges in the eyes by day and is stored in the kidneys at night during sleep; in deep sleep one snores, shaking the six roots, and the original essence comes out by itself and so is lost.

The method of preventing the loss: when we sleep, press the tongue against the palate, gather in the spirit and store it below in the elixir field, the heart empty and without thought, the mouth closed, the breath entering through the nostrils and descending to the elixir field to unite with the breath, water and fire united into one, the breath soft and continuous, spirit and breath embracing; as if fast asleep yet not fast asleep, a natural sleep — how could the original essence be lost? There is also the method of “five dragons coiling the body,” whose formula is: sleep with the head to the east, lying on the side, coiled like a dragon, curled like a dog; one hand with the elbow bent pillowing the head, the other hand stretched out touching the navel and belly; one leg straight, the other drawn up; “before the heart sleeps, let the eyes sleep” (close the eyes first, and the heart grows quiet after them); reach the utmost of emptiness and guard deep stillness; spirit and breath naturally return to the root, breathing is naturally contained and nurtured; without regulating the breath, the breath regulates itself; without subduing the breath, the breath subdues itself. Sleeping like this, one naturally has no dreams and keeps the original essence without loss. But to practice this sleeping method, one must also know the “method of restful repose”; if one sleeps in a muddle, without cutting off turbid breath and thoughts, then once in deep sleep the yang breath is entirely pressed down by the yin breath.

Question: Teacher, you said earlier that the Dharma Wheel turns by itself, but cannot turn by itself without a teacher’s transmission. What does that mean?

Answer: The Dharma Wheel turning by itself borrows the Posterior Heaven breath to drive the Prior Heaven breath. “Closing the door” is the mechanism of inhaling, which goes downward, and so it is called Kun; “opening the door” is the mechanism of exhaling, which goes upward, and so it is called Qian. This is Posterior Heaven breathing, two goings and comings, drawing out the breath of the Prior Heaven true yang: stirring from the Cavity of Breath, it connects down to the Caudal Funnel and the Governing Vessel, and the yang breath rises little by little up the “yue pipe” to Qian, while the Posterior Heaven mechanism of inhaling descends to Kun; the yang breath descends little by little down the “tuo pipe,” the Conception Vessel, to Kun, while the Posterior Heaven mechanism of exhaling rises to Qian — that one up, this one down; this one up, that one down. If one calls breathing in and out through mouth and nose “going and coming without end,” one is far from the Prior Heaven great Way. Dīpaṃkara Buddha said: “Constantly turn the Dharma Wheel.” The World-Honored One (Śākyamuni) said: “Constantly turn such a wondrous Dharma Wheel.”

In the second month of 1922, when I was practicing at the age of sixty, at the mao hour (five to seven in the morning) on that day my whole body felt as if ants were crawling over it, and without my noticing, suddenly the elixir field grew hot, the whole body tingled and went limp, with a joy hard to bear; suddenly my heart did not know where it had gone, and the member rose vigorously; in the dim and indistinct, the Prior Heaven true breath of the tuo pipe stirred; my root of desire drew back, my heart was unwilling to let go of that breath, and the breath, reined in by the root of desire, merged into one mass; the scene within seemed like losing and leaking, but in fact nothing was lost or leaked. The breath of the true yang rose little by little from below toward the back, and at this moment the Conception and Governing vessels opened by themselves; I quickly used the Posterior Heaven “closing,” the breath of inhaling at the heel, to drive it continuously, pressing the true breath to rise; and as the Prior Heaven yang breath descended little by little in front, I used the Posterior Heaven “opening,” the breath of exhaling at the stem, to drive the true breath continuously downward — this is “the great turning of the Dharma Wheel.” The master within Qian and Kun is my True Intention. Making the Prior Heaven breath and the Posterior Heaven breath turn, this one up and that one down, that one up and this one down — its mystery cannot be described in words. Even so, if I do not say it plainly, how can later students enter into understanding? These four breathings, without mouth and nose — the key lies simply in whether or not one has met a teacher (the original has 迂师不迂师, see the Translator’s Notes). The great Way is most secret; who would dare to reveal it all? I have sought the Way up to the age of seventy-three, and now I reveal the whole formula, hoping that those with aspiration will understand it all within a few days. Apart from these four breathings, the śarīra has no way to be refined.

Know that the seminal essence in the body, without the help and nourishment of the Prior Heaven true breath, cannot become the śarīra. When the śarīra has been nourished to the full, it emits “the Prior Heaven breath within Prior Heaven,” which is the light of wisdom. This breath is the ancestral breath of the primal beginning, the most essential, most numinous, most holy breath of Prior Heaven, the medicine of long life. This medicine is not on the sea (the immortals’ mountains) nor in the pharmacy; it must be sought within the body. Master Cui’s (Cui Xifan’s) Mirror for Compounding the Medicine says: “Prior Heaven breath, Posterior Heaven breath — whoever obtains them is always as if drunk.” The student who knows how to use this breath obtains true long life.

Long ago my teachers Liaoran and Liaokong, transmitting it to me, said: “The four breathings, inhaling and exhaling, are divided into before and after; if one uses mouth and nose, the Way is not true; inhaling at the heel and exhaling at the stem link with the true breath; this waxing and waning draws the wheel to turn.” They also said: “Posterior Heaven inhaling and exhaling are at the Mountain Root; the Prior Heaven true breath rises from the elixir field; when the two breaths unite, above and below are linked; off by a hair’s breadth, it does not turn.” (Posterior Heaven breathing is at the Mountain Root — between the eyes — and the Prior Heaven true breath rises from the elixir field; when the two breaths unite, above and below are linked; off by the slightest amount, it will not turn.) The head of this “heel” is in the sea of the back of the brain, that is, the medulla oblongata, and it connects down to the back of the foot. With one inhalation, from the heel to the Mountain Root (that is, the stem), from the back of the foot up to the Caudal Funnel, past the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow, to the Muddy Pellet. With one exhalation, from the stem to the Opening of Life and Death, that is, from the Muddy Pellet down to the upper Magpie Bridge, the Mysterious Breast, the Twelve-Storied Tower, the Crimson Palace and the true Cavity of Breath, to the Opening of Life and Death. Whenever the Posterior Heaven breath is borrowed to blow and puff, driving the inside of the true Cavity of Breath, the true breath stirs, and the Posterior Heaven breath, meeting (the original has 迂) the Prior Heaven True Breath, moves together with it, joined and linked. This one up and that one down, that one up and this one down — it all lies in the mechanism of waxing and waning; the Prior Heaven and Posterior Heaven breaths will become clear by themselves without needing to be told apart. If one has mastered the eight steps before, that is the method of refining essence to replenish the body, which can lengthen one’s years and increase one’s life; this step is the method of refining breath, replenishing the seminal essence and forming the śarīra, in preparation for gathering the Great Medicine and passing the great passes.

【注释】本章论述经过踵蒂后天吸呼，实现法轮自转，以炼养舍利子。功法又深入一层，达到胎息和入定阶段。前八步功是炼精补身之法，此步功是炼炁补精养成舍利子之法。

法轮自转，并非像世俗所谓仅是一股暖流在任督二脉自行转动那样简单，而是四个吸呼往来，即吸于踵（脚后跟），呼于蒂（泥丸），用后天吸呼以引动先天真炁，形成先天炁升，后天气降；后天气升，先天炁降，此上彼下，彼下此上的交叉境局。这种法轮自转，并非用心意转动，而是自然而然的转动，是练功到此，自然到来的一种境界。这种境界是以前八步功为基础的。没有前八步功是不能达到这种境界的，就会像烈火煮空铛，劳而无功。作为功法而言，法轮自转的发动，是由后天一吸一呼的消息来引动先天的真气。亦即由祖窍吸气，心意由祖窍往里至玉枕，接着由玉枕再往祖窍呼气，吸呼往来于祖窍与玉枕之间。从祖窍到玉枕一吸为“消”，从玉枕到祖窍一呼为“息”。这里后天两个吸呼，即蒂踵消息，不用口鼻吸呼气。消息即机关之意。练功者可以感到，吸于踵时有一股暖流从督脉上升泥丸，与此同时又有一股暖流经任脉下降到生死窍（会阴）；呼于蒂时有一股暖流从泥丸经任脉下降到生死窍，与此同时又有一股暖流经督脉上升到泥丸。这是四个吸呼往来真实之内景。这种景况自然因各人体质之差别而有感觉轻重之异。此时口鼻吸呼几无，胎息已凝，进入静定之境。《性命圭旨》一书描绘法轮自转对人体的感受时说：“滃然如云雾之四塞，飒然如风雨之暴至，恍然如昼梦之初觉，涣然如沉疴之脱体，精神冥合，如夫妇之交接，骨肉融和，如澡浴之方起。《易》曰：‘黄中通理，正位居体，美在其中，而畅于四肢’。斯言信矣”！本章亦以“万里阴沉春气合，九宵清彻露华凝”形容之。可知法轮自转所引起的人体感受是如何美妙！

然则四个吸呼往来，哪两个吸呼属于后天？哪两个吸呼属于先天呢？这里须再度分清楚。柳华阳《慧命经》说：“修道何也？答曰：道用先天借后天之爪板转法轮也。阖户即是吸机。吸机者往下也，故曰坤矣。辟户即是呼机。呼机者往上也，故曰乾矣。此乃后天一边之理也”。这就是说，吸于踵时，气从泥丸经任脉下降会阴者；呼于蒂时，气从会阴经督脉上升到泥丸者，均属后天一边。柳华阳又说：“阖吸虽是下坤，而坤腹之元炁过我升之，升之者升于乾。辟呼虽是上乾，而乾首之元炁过我降之，降之者降于坤。总是先后两个升降面、背、中三条道路，共乾坤之毂轴，通元关之消息，而主宰在乎意，运行总在乎神，一吸一升，一呼一降，不可差之毫发，循规行途数之限步，不可不及而太过”。这就是说，吸于踵时，元炁从会阴经督脉上升泥丸者；呼于蒂时，元炁从泥丸经任脉下降会阴者，均属先天一边。

既明先后天之二炁，那么，法轮转动时真意又将如何主宰呢？柳华阳说：“凡转法轮之际，意主丹田而为轮心，神运炁而为轮爪，呼吸催迫而为轮毂，亦出乎自然而然之消息”。又说：“虽然如是，而先后又不可并主重用。升降之际，意虽主斗杓，其神重在先天同行，不过借后天阖辟之机以运先天耳”。这即是说，当转运法轮之际，真意必须主宰丹田为轮心，亦即斗杓；同时元神则偏重于先天之炁的运行。

除此以外，学者还须明沐浴之机。沐浴者即是在卯、酉二位不行呼吸，神炁相抱相守，定而再转也。呼吸退为沐浴，呼吸进亦为沐浴，但有前后之分而已。柳华阳曰：“行法轮之时，呼吸之气有回转之机，在此回转处，而有沐浴也”。学者能善用之，古称为“转妙法轮”。

法轮自转可以融化真炁，补我元精，养我真命。元精炼足，元炁充盈，就结成舍利子。法轮若泛然于任督二脉道外，渺渺茫茫，不由道而循行，不能成舍利子。

The practice has gone one level deeper, reaching the stage of embryonic breathing and entering absorption. The first eight steps are methods of refining essence to replenish the body; this step is the method of refining breath to replenish essence and nourishing the śarīra.

The Dharma Wheel turning by itself is not, as popularly said, simply a warm current turning by itself in the Conception and Governing vessels; it is “the four breathings going back and forth”: inhaling at the heel (the back of the foot) and exhaling at the stem (the Muddy Pellet), using Posterior Heaven breathing to draw the Prior Heaven true breath, forming a crossing pattern of “when the Prior Heaven breath rises, the Posterior Heaven breath descends; when the Posterior Heaven breath rises, the Prior Heaven breath descends,” this one up and that one down, that one down and this one up. This Dharma Wheel turning by itself is not turned with the heart and intention, but turns of itself; it is a state that comes naturally when practice reaches this point. This state is founded on the first eight steps; without them it cannot be reached, and it would be like boiling an empty pot over a fierce fire — labor without result.

As a method of practice, the Dharma Wheel turning by itself is set going by the waxing and waning of one Posterior Heaven inhalation and exhalation, drawing the Prior Heaven true breath. That is: inhale from the Ancestral Opening, the heart and intention going inward from the Ancestral Opening to the Jade Pillow; then exhale from the Jade Pillow back to the Ancestral Opening; the breathing goes back and forth between the Ancestral Opening and the Jade Pillow. One inhalation from the Ancestral Opening to the Jade Pillow is called “waning”; one exhalation from the Jade Pillow to the Ancestral Opening is called “waxing.” These two Posterior Heaven breathings are “the waning and waxing of stem and heel,” without breathing through mouth and nose. “Waning and waxing” (xiaoxi) means a mechanism. The practitioner can feel that when inhaling at the heel, a warm current rises by the Governing Vessel to the Muddy Pellet, while at the same time another warm current descends by the Conception Vessel to the Opening of Life and Death (the perineum); when exhaling at the stem, a warm current descends from the Muddy Pellet by the Conception Vessel to the Opening of Life and Death, while at the same time another warm current rises by the Governing Vessel to the Muddy Pellet. This is the real inner scenery of the four breathings going back and forth. Naturally, owing to differences in constitution, the sensations are stronger or weaker. At this time breathing through mouth and nose has almost ceased, embryonic breathing has condensed, and one enters the realm of stillness and absorption. The Jade Tablet of Inner Nature and Life, describing how the Dharma Wheel turning by itself feels in the body, says: “Billowing, as if clouds and mist filled all four sides; rushing, as if wind and rain suddenly arrived; startled, as if just waking from a daydream; dispersed, as if a long illness had suddenly left the body; spirit and essence merge darkly, like husband and wife in union; bones and flesh melt in harmony, as if just rising from a bath. The Book of Changes says: ‘Yellow in the center, penetrating the pattern; in the right position, dwelling in the body; beauty is within it and flows out into the four limbs.' How true these words are!” This chapter also describes it with “across ten thousand li of gloom the breath of spring merges; the nine heavens are clear and the dew condenses.” We can see how wonderful the bodily sensations caused by the Dharma Wheel turning by itself are!

Then, of the four breathings going back and forth, which two belong to Posterior Heaven and which two to Prior Heaven? This needs to be distinguished more clearly. Liu Huayang’s Book of Wisdom and Life says: “What is cultivating the Way? Answer: the Way uses Prior Heaven and borrows the ‘handle’ (lever) of Posterior Heaven to turn the Dharma Wheel. Closing the door is the mechanism of inhaling, and the mechanism of inhaling goes downward, so it is called Kun; opening the door is the mechanism of exhaling, and the mechanism of exhaling goes upward, so it is called Qian. This is the principle on the side of Posterior Heaven.” That is to say, when inhaling at the heel, the breath that descends from the Muddy Pellet by the Conception Vessel to the perineum, and when exhaling at the stem, the breath that rises from the perineum by the Governing Vessel to the Muddy Pellet — both belong to the side of Posterior Heaven. Liu Huayang also says: “Although closing and inhaling go down to Kun, the original breath of the Kun belly passes through me and rises, rising to Qian; although opening and exhaling go up to Qian, the original breath of the Qian head passes through me and descends, descending to Kun. In short, there are two risings and descendings, of Prior Heaven and Posterior Heaven, traveling three roads — front, back and center — sharing the hub and axle of Qian and Kun, reaching the waxing and waning of the Mysterious Pass; the master is the intention, the movement always lies in the spirit; one inhalation, one rising; one exhalation, one descending — not off by a hair, following the rule, keeping to the limits of road and count, neither falling short nor going too far.” That is to say, when inhaling at the heel, the original breath that rises from the perineum by the Governing Vessel to the Muddy Pellet, and when exhaling at the stem, the original breath that descends from the Muddy Pellet by the Conception Vessel to the perineum — both belong to the side of Prior Heaven.

Having understood the two breaths of Prior and Posterior Heaven, how then does the True Intention preside when the Dharma Wheel turns? Liu Huayang says: “Whenever the Dharma Wheel is turned, the intention presides over the elixir field as the hub of the wheel; the spirit moves the breath as the spokes; the breathing drives it as the axle; this too comes from what happens of itself.” He also says: “Even so, Prior Heaven and Posterior Heaven must not be weighted equally at the same time. When rising and descending, although the intention presides over the Dipper’s handle, the spirit is weighted toward moving together with Prior Heaven, merely borrowing the mechanism of Posterior Heaven closing and opening to move Prior Heaven.” That is to say, when turning the Dharma Wheel, the True Intention must preside over the elixir field as the hub of the wheel, that is, the Dipper’s handle; at the same time the Original Spirit is weighted toward the movement of the Prior Heaven breath.

Besides this, students must also understand the mechanism of “ablution.” Ablution means not moving the breathing at the two positions mao and you, spirit and breath embracing and guarding each other, turning again only after settling. When the breathing withdraws, that is ablution; when the breathing advances, that too is ablution; there is only a difference of before and after. Liu Huayang says: “When moving the Dharma Wheel, the breath of breathing has a mechanism of turning back, and at this turning-back point there is ablution.” Students who can use it well — the ancients called this “turning the wondrous Dharma Wheel.”

The Dharma Wheel turning by itself can melt the true breath, replenish my original essence and nourish my true life. When the original essence is refined to sufficiency and the original breath is full, the śarīra is formed. If the Dharma Wheel drifts vaguely outside the road of the Conception and Governing vessels, boundless and unfocused, not following the road, it cannot become the śarīra.

But this chapter points out that in practicing this work, whether or not one dreams at night, there is the danger of losing the original essence, and it offers methods for preventing the loss of the original essence at night. Students must pay special attention to this and guard against trouble before it arises. The next step has a chapter devoted to it. As for deviations in practice such as yin scenes and deviant fire, this chapter has already offered methods of correction, which are very important for practicing this step well.

Reaching the Dharma Wheel turning by itself in the formula of this step means that the work of the Lesser Celestial Circuit is about to be completed and the Greater Celestial Circuit is about to begin.

Key Terms

  • The Dharma Wheel turns by itself: the circuit runs naturally, not pushed by the intention, the Posterior Heaven breathing drawing the Prior Heaven true breath; Zhao calls it the Greater Celestial Circuit.
  • Heel, stem: Zhao says “the head of the heel is in the sea of the back of the brain, that is, the medulla oblongata, and it connects down to the back of the foot”; the stem is at the Ancestral Opening (the Mountain Root) and connects down to the Opening of Life and Death. Qiu Ling’s note has “inhaling at the heel (the back of the foot), exhaling at the stem (the Muddy Pellet).”
  • Waning, waxing: on the inhalation the heart and intention go from the Ancestral Opening to the medulla (the Jade Pillow), which is waning; on the exhalation from the medulla to the Ancestral Opening, which is waxing; together called “waning and waxing” (xiaoxi), that is, the mechanism.
  • Four breathings: the Posterior Heaven breath rising and descending, the Prior Heaven breath descending and rising — “this one up, that one down; that one down, this one up.”
  • Tuo pipe, yue pipe: Zhao’s terms; the Prior Heaven yang breath rises by the yue pipe (the Governing Vessel) and descends by the tuo pipe (the Conception Vessel).
  • Lesser stillness and absorption, great stillness and absorption: the first eight steps are lesser absorption; without breathing through mouth and nose it is great absorption; “lesser stillness one day, middle stillness three days, great stillness seven days.”
  • Yin scene: in practice, the body feeling cold, giving off cool breath and the like, caused by the breath of the elixir field being cold.
  • Samādhi true fire: the true fire gathered from the light of the spirit, used to treat yin scenes.
  • Deviant fire: signs of fire arising from the liver and kidneys in quiet sitting, such as blurred or double vision, dilated pupils, reddened whites of the eyes, a hot heart, restlessness and thirst.
  • Demons of lust, food and sleep: three obstacles in cultivation.
  • Five pungents: scallion, garlic, chives, shallot, asafoetida and similar pungent things.
  • Five dragons coiling the body: a way of sleeping with the head to the east, lying on the side, one hand pillowing the head and one on the belly, one leg straight and one drawn up.
  • Method of restful repose: before sleeping or sitting, loosen the clothes, breathe out the turbid and breathe in the clear, the heart calm and the breath harmonious, condensing one point of the Original Spirit into the Cavity of Breath.
  • Hub, spokes, axle: Liu Huayang’s metaphor — the intention presiding over the elixir field is the hub, the spirit moving the breath is the spokes, the breathing is the axle.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Of itself: the key word of this step is “by itself” — Zhao, Kuiyizi and Qiu Ling all stress that it is not turned with the heart and intention. If the foundation before is not there, there is no need to pursue it.
  2. Breathe lightly: “not breathing through mouth and nose” means not placing intention on mouth and nose, the breathing extremely light and fine — not holding the breath.
  3. Restful repose before sleep, lying on the side: loosen the clothes, breathe out the turbid and breathe in the clear, tongue against the palate, gather the spirit into the elixir field, lie curled on the side — a good way of sleeping that anyone can use.
  4. Eat plainly: less spice and pungent food helps the heart to be still and reduces dreams.
  5. See a doctor for deviations: with cold, heat, blurred or double vision, changes in the pupils, restlessness or thirst, first rule out physical illness; the practice methods can only be a supplement.
  6. Don’t force absorption: “let the breath come to rest and so enter great absorption naturally” — still less should one hold the breath or stop eating for the sake of “entering absorption.”

Translator's Notes

  1. In the original, this step has one formula and one figure; in the middle of the explanation is one very long passage, followed by two questions and answers and the words of several teachers; we number them §1–§9 by content (§2–§4 are a single natural paragraph in the original).
  2. “The true person inhales at the heel”: from the chapter “The Great Ancestral Teacher” of the Zhuangzi: “The true person breathes from the heels; ordinary people breathe from the throat.” Zhao explains the “heel” as the medulla oblongata, connecting down to the back of the foot, while Qiu Ling’s note says “exhaling at the stem (the Muddy Pellet),” not quite the same as Zhao’s “the stem is at the Ancestral Opening”; each is translated as in the original.
  3. “The nine 宵 are clear” (九宵清彻): 九宵 is usually written 九霄 (“the nine heavens”); reproduced as is.
  4. “Melt the 原炁” (融化原炁): 原炁 is 元炁 (the original breath); reproduced as is.
  5. “It lies simply in 迂师不迂师” and “迂 the Prior Heaven True Breath”: 迂 should be a mistake for 遇 (“to meet”) (the 迁师 of Step Seven may be a mistake of the same kind); reproduced as is, and translated as “meet.”
  6. “Restless” (烦燥): should be 烦躁; reproduced as is.
  7. Zhao’s own account, “in the second month of 1922, at the age of sixty”: Zhao Bichen was born in 1860, so in 1922 he would have been sixty-three by Chinese reckoning; so in the original, reproduced as is.
  8. The layering of quotation marks in Qiu Ling’s quotation from The Jade Tablet of Inner Nature and Life is as in the original; the quotation from the Book of Changes is from the “Commentary on the Words of the Text” for the Kun hexagram.
  9. Li Xu’an was the teacher of Wu Chongxu in the Ming; the “Lovely Maiden” refers to mercury, the Original Spirit.
