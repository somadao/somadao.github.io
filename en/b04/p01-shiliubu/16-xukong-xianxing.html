The Sixteen Steps of Method Formulas · 第十六步 虚空显形

Step Sixteen · Taking Form in Empty Space

The formula of Step Sixteen, taking form in empty space — the śarīra turns to light and comes out of the Ancestral Opening; in one surge come forth ten thousand times ten thousand spirits

About This Chapter

The last of the sixteen steps speaks of “returning to emptiness and facing the wall” and “empty space shattered” — refining spirit to return to emptiness, refining emptiness to unite with the Way. Zhao Bichen says frankly: “My own practice has not reached this point, but I received the oral formula from my teacher, and now I pass it on to later generations.” Qiu Ling also says that among the ancients, those who could practice up to this state were “hard to find one in ten million,” so for this step “the editor does not intend to annotate further in detail.”

  1. The method of the hibernating dragon: draw the Yang Spirit, refined a hundred times, into the Ancestral Opening and return it to the sea of inner nature; “close and refine” the form body, merging it into the Dharma-body; like a dragon nourishing its pearl, like a hen brooding its egg, relying on the absorption of extinction to be quiescent and extinguished.
  2. Release from the corpse and letting go: the Chan master Liaokong speaks of the “formula of release from the corpse,” pressing the tip of the tongue against the Mysterious Breast point so that the body cannot move by itself, “like a dead person.”
  3. Empty space shattered: after nine years of facing the wall, the spiritual light is all-sufficient and the Dharma form complete, transforming into a great red light that “shines everywhere upon the three thousand great thousand worlds.”
  4. The four stages: refining essence into breath is setting to work, refining breath into spirit is turning the hand, refining spirit to return to emptiness is completing, refining emptiness to unite with the Way is letting go — from the gradual method into the sudden method, from deliberate action into non-action, from the not-empty into true emptiness.
  5. The final 〇: the golden body, the Dharma-body, the original face, empty space — “all are this 〇”; “heaven and earth may perish, but this 〇 does not perish.”

Qiu Ling’s note explains that the so-called “nine years of facing the wall” does not mean completing the great elixir in nine years, but uses the story of Bodhidharma facing the wall as a metaphor for this stage of “constant stillness and constant absorption,” “one body with the universe” — which is why it is also called “refining emptiness to unite with the Way.”

Safety note (translator): The “release from the corpse” described in this step — pressing the tip of the tongue against the Mysterious Breast point so that a person “cannot move by himself, like a dead person” — is extremely dangerous and must never be attempted; it may cause suffocation or accidents. “Nine years of facing the wall” and “empty space shattered” are metaphors and descriptions of a state. Zhao Bichen himself says he did not reach this step.

Original Text and Translation

Figure 36 · Taking Form in Empty Space

Below, a Daoist sits upright cross-legged, with radiating light behind his head. From his crown many branches spread upward, bearing up clouds one after another, and on each cloud stands a row of small figures, rank upon rank — this is what the formula calls “in one surge come forth ten thousand times ten thousand spirits”: the Dharma-body divides its form and scatters its image, transforming into hundreds of billions of bodies. (In the original figure the figures wear various costumes; this site has simplified, drawing only outlines.)

Formula
Original (Chinese)

舍利化光出祖窍，

一涌而出万万神。

这个才是真实体，

九天之上任吾行。

打破虚空消息路，

我登彼岸不用舟。

炼神还虚千变化，

撒手虚空是金身。

The śarīra turns into light and comes out of the Ancestral Opening; in one surge come forth thousands upon thousands, ten thousand times ten thousand spirits. Only this is the real body; above the nine heavens I go wherever I will.

Breaking through empty space, one also breaks through all the roads of waxing and waning; I reach the other shore without a boat. Refining spirit to return to emptiness, with a thousand changes and ten thousand transformations; letting go in empty space — that is the golden body.

§1
Original (Chinese)

还虚面壁之功，古云总得九年。我功未至此，但我得师传口诀，今传于后。我师了空禅师曰：还虚一着，是将从前十魔百炼之阳神，分形散影，显化于世，救急消灾，与人治病；复将阳神收入祖窍，归于性海之内，勿另其出色身；复将色身闭住炼化，浑入法身之中，此是先天之中先天性命；复将阳神退藏法身祖窍之内，要将色身炼得不有不无，非色非空，无内无外，不出不入，无始无终，如龙养珠，蛰藏而不动，如鸡抱卵，安眠而不起，沉之又沉，静之又静，依灭尽定而寂灭之。此是蛰龙之法，谨谨护持，毋容阳神再出。盖阳神百炼而百灵，千炼而愈精，炼炼不已，则阳神之慧光内神火，隐而收之愈密，放之愈普，隐之无可隐，显之无可显。将阳神收藏祖窍之内，定极灭尽之余，久而久之，将阳神真火养足，而一炉神光，兀兀腾腾；满鼎真火，炎炎烈烈，自内窍透出外窍，由大窍贯入小窍，无内无外，无大无小，透顶彻底，光光相烛，窍窍相映，而天地万物莫不照耀于神光之中矣。

The work of returning to emptiness and facing the wall, the ancients said, always takes nine years. My own practice has not reached this step, but I received the oral formula transmitted by my teacher, and now I pass it on to later generations. My teacher the Chan master Liaokong said: the move of “returning to emptiness” is to take the Yang Spirit that has been through the ten demons and a hundred refinings, divide its form and scatter its image, manifest it in the world to rescue those in urgent need, dispel calamities and cure people’s illnesses; then to draw the Yang Spirit back into the Ancestral Opening, returning it into the sea of inner nature, not letting it leave the form body again; then to close and refine the form body, merging it into the Dharma-body — this is “the Prior Heaven inner nature and life within Prior Heaven”; then to withdraw and hide the Yang Spirit within the Ancestral Opening of the Dharma-body, and to refine the form body until it is neither being nor non-being, neither form nor emptiness, without inside or outside, neither going out nor coming in, without beginning or end: like a dragon nourishing its pearl, hibernating and unmoving; like a hen brooding its egg, resting and not rising; sinking and sinking again, still and still again, relying on the “absorption of extinction” to be quiescent and extinguished. This is “the method of the hibernating dragon,” to be guarded with the utmost care, not allowing the Yang Spirit to go out again. For the Yang Spirit, refined a hundred times, becomes a hundred times more numinous; refined a thousand times, it becomes ever more refined; refined and refined again, the light of wisdom of the Yang Spirit, the inner spirit-fire, the more it is hidden and gathered in, the finer and closer it becomes, the more it is released, the more pervasive it becomes, hidden until there is nothing more to hide, manifest until there is nothing more to manifest. Store the Yang Spirit within the Ancestral Opening; after absorption has reached its utmost and everything is extinguished, in time the true fire of the Yang Spirit is nourished to the full, and then a furnace of spiritual light towers and surges, a tripod full of true fire blazes and flames, passing from the inner openings through to the outer openings, from the great openings into the small openings, without inside or outside, without great or small, through the crown and to the very bottom, light shining on light, opening reflecting opening, and of heaven, earth and the ten thousand things there is nothing not illumined within the spiritual light.

§2
Original (Chinese)

顺一子问曰：师传后将色身闭住炼化，性命复归法身祖窍之内，此为蛰龙之法。后有阳神真火养足，贯入窍中，直待四大崩散，虚空粉碎，是如何闭住炼化色身？何是蛰龙之法？如何将四大一身崩散？乞老师示知。

了空禅师答曰：丹经万卷俱藏头露尾，非得口口相传，心心相印，句句可考。丹经云：阅进丹经万万篇，末后一着无人言；阳关一闭准多生，千佛万祖皆单传。尔修道修的是先天祖炁，炼的也是祖炁，成道的也是祖炁，成为这个〇，聚者能成元身，散者能成空气。又有尸解之诀，为撒手之法也。撒手者，诀云：口内上腭是天池穴，通脑髓中炁胞，吾人说话，出先天真炁，故云：日出千言不损自伤。尔不知上腭后有个玄膺穴，上通真炁胞，下通十二重楼，即是通肺气管。用舌尖顶玄膺穴，人不能自主矣。因舌尖闭住真炁不通，故身不能自动，如死人一样。昔年老比丘用的就是此法。若有人将老比丘舌放开，则老比丘即能复生说话。因肉身能通真炁，故而复活。此玄膺穴两旁有薄肉如簾，有前簾后簾，中有核大如杏仁，生脂以润喉咙，为口之界。由古至今，各位祖师秘而不传，单传至今，尔要细心悟之。

Shunyizi asked: Teacher, you have taught that later one must close and refine the form body, inner nature and life returning again into the Ancestral Opening of the Dharma-body — this is “the method of the hibernating dragon”; later the true fire of the Yang Spirit is nourished to the full and penetrates into the openings, until the four great elements collapse and empty space is shattered. Then how is the form body closed and refined? What is the method of the hibernating dragon? How does one make the single body of the four great elements collapse and scatter? Please instruct me, teacher.

The Chan master Liaokong answered: the elixir classics, ten thousand scrolls of them, all hide the head and show only the tail; only by transmission from mouth to mouth and the seal of heart to heart can every sentence have a basis that can be checked. The elixir classics say: “I have read through ten thousand times ten thousand elixir scriptures; the last move no one speaks of; once the yang gate is closed, one surely lives many lives; the thousand buddhas and ten thousand patriarchs all transmitted it to one alone.” (Having read through tens of millions of elixir scriptures, no one speaks of the last move; once the yang gate is closed, one is sure to live long; the thousand buddhas and ten thousand patriarchs all transmitted it to a single disciple.) What you cultivate in cultivating the Way is the Prior Heaven ancestral breath; what you refine is also the ancestral breath; what attains the Way is also the ancestral breath, becoming this one “〇”: gathered, it can become the “original body”; dispersed, it can become air. There is also the formula of “release from the corpse,” which is the method of “letting go.” As for what is called letting go, the formula says: the palate in the mouth is the Celestial Pool point, connected to the “breath sac” in the brain marrow; when we people speak, Prior Heaven true breath goes out, hence the saying “speaking a thousand words a day, one does not see the loss but harms oneself” (speaking a thousand sentences a day, no loss can be seen, but one is in fact harming oneself). You do not know that behind the palate there is a Mysterious Breast point, connected above to the sac of true breath and below to the Twelve-Storied Tower, that is, the windpipe leading to the lungs. When the tip of the tongue presses against the Mysterious Breast point, a person can no longer act of himself: because the tip of the tongue closes it off and the true breath cannot pass, the body cannot move by itself, like a dead person (see the safety note). This was the method used by the old bhikṣus of the past; if someone released the old bhikṣu‘s tongue, the old bhikṣu could revive and speak — because the flesh body could again pass the true breath, and so came back to life. On both sides of this Mysterious Breast point there is thin flesh like a curtain, with a front curtain and a back curtain, and in the middle a kernel the size of an apricot stone that produces a fatty fluid to moisten the throat; it is the boundary of the mouth (referring to the uvula and soft palate). From ancient times to the present, all the patriarchs kept this secret and did not transmit it, passing it to one alone down to today; you must realize it carefully.

§3
Original (Chinese)

了然禅师曰：九年面壁，虚空粉碎之法诀，韬光依灭尽定而寂灭之，寂灭日久，直至三年九载，空定衡极，灭尽无余之际，神光周足，法相圆满，色空俱泯，形神俱妙。其敛也，至精至彻，纳入芥子而无间；其放也，至大至刚，包罗须弥而无外，将见无极神光，化为大红光恰似赫赫日轮，从太虚玄关窍内，一涌而出，崩开分散，灿烂弥满，无边无量，为万道毫光透彻于九天之上，贯通于九地之下，若千万杲日，放大光明，普照三千大千世界，森罗万象莫不齐现于玄关之中。余胞兄赵魁一曰：古仙云身外有身未为奇，虚空粉碎方露全真。所以出胎之后，正要脚踏实地坐功，炼得虚空粉碎，与道合真，才见无量之宝光，直充塞于四大，彼此交光，合并一体，成为虚无一个圈子，我身犹如摩尼珠，光耀无比。

The Chan master Liaoran said: the formula of nine years of facing the wall and empty space shattered is to hide one’s light, relying on the absorption of extinction to be quiescent and extinguished; when quiescent extinction has lasted long, up to three years, nine years, when empty absorption has reached its utmost and everything is extinguished without remainder, the spiritual light is all-sufficient and the Dharma form complete, form and emptiness both vanish, body and spirit are wondrously united. When it gathers in, it is utterly fine and penetrating, entering a mustard seed without any gap; when it releases, it is utterly great and strong, enveloping Mount Sumeru without any outside. One will see the spiritual light of the Ultimateless transform into a great red light, like the blazing sun, surging out in one burst from within the opening of the Mysterious Pass of the great void, bursting and scattering, brilliant and filling everything, boundless and immeasurable, transforming into ten thousand rays of fine light, penetrating above the nine heavens, passing through below the nine earths, like thousands upon thousands of bright suns giving forth great light, shining everywhere upon the three thousand great thousand worlds, and all the myriad phenomena without exception appear together within the Mysterious Pass.

My elder brother Zhao Kuiyi said: the ancient immortals said, “A body outside the body is nothing remarkable yet; only when empty space is shattered is the complete truth revealed.” So after leaving the womb, one must do the sitting practice with feet firmly on the ground, refining until empty space is shattered and one unites with the Way in truth, and only then can one see the immeasurable precious light, filling the four great elements, the lights meeting each other and merging into one body, becoming a single circle of emptiness and non-being; my body is like a maṇi jewel, incomparably radiant.

§4
Original (Chinese)

由七真祖师后，炼虚空粉碎者鲜矣！余功未至此，师受口诀在焉，非是不传，亦非怕受天谴，实在不易得之粉碎，故达摩祖有9年雪山之功。古仙云：饶经八万劫，终是落空亡。殊不知寿命有限，而不能修，虽得此法，却炼不到粉碎。余前云炼精化炁，是下手之法；炼炁化神，是转手之法；炼神还虚，是了手之法；而炼虚合道为是撒手之法也。是由渐法而入顿法，由有为而入无为，由不空而入真空。无上师云：炼得金丹似月圆，未免有圆还有缺。若不得诀者，速访明师，问撒手之法，炼至神光满穴，自内窍达于外窍，九窍之中，窍窍皆有神，小窍八万四千之中，窍窍皆有灵光，彻内彻外，透底透顶，在在皆有神光，如百千灯光，照耀一室，散之则无数，收之而为一。炼到此时，又于三千大千世界之中，复放无量宝光，直充塞于极乐世界，我身犹如摩尼宝珠。荷泽禅师云：本来面目是真如，舍利光中认得渠；万劫迷头今始悟，方知自性是文殊；自性清静便是无垢佛，自性如如便是自在佛，自性不昧便是光明佛，自性坚固便是不坏佛，位位诸佛，自身具有，说亦不尽，惟一性尔。性即是心，心即是佛。新佛旧成无二体，金身也是这个〇，法身也是这个〇，本来面目也是这个〇，虚空也是这个〇，上乎天下乎地全是这个〇，天地有坏，这个〇不坏。请问这个〇是甚么？是天地人身万物，真阴阳之炁。炼至纯阳真炁成为这个〇，才是真我，才是真性命，才是金仙不坏真体，才是不生不灭之元神，才是一切无有如来佛。

Since the patriarchs of the Seven Perfected of Complete Perfection, few have refined until empty space was shattered! My own practice has not reached this step, but the oral formula my teacher transmitted remains; it is not that I do not transmit it, nor that I fear heaven’s punishment; it is truly that “shattering” is not easily attained — which is why Patriarch Bodhidharma had the work of nine years (the original has “9”) in the Snowy Mountains. The ancient immortals said: “Even passing through eighty thousand kalpas, in the end one falls into empty nothingness.” Little do they know that life is limited and there is no time to cultivate; even if one obtains this method, one cannot refine it to shattering.

I said above: refining essence into breath is the method of “setting to work”; refining breath into spirit is the method of “turning the hand”; refining spirit to return to emptiness is the method of “completing”; and “refining emptiness to unite with the Way” is the method of “letting go.” This is going from the gradual method into the sudden method, from deliberate action into non-action, from the not-empty into true emptiness. The Unsurpassed Teacher said: “Even if you refine the Golden Elixir round as the full moon, you cannot avoid that what is round will also wane.” (Even if the Golden Elixir is refined as round as the full moon, it cannot escape waning after being full.) If you have not yet obtained the oral formula, quickly seek out an enlightened teacher and ask for the method of letting go: refine until the spiritual light fills the cavity and reaches from the inner openings to the outer openings, so that among the nine openings every opening has its spirit, and among the eighty-four thousand small openings every opening has numinous light; through and through, inside and out, to the very bottom and through the crown, everywhere there is spiritual light, like the light of hundreds and thousands of lamps shining in one room, dispersed it is countless, gathered it is one. When refined to this point, one again releases immeasurable precious light within the three thousand great thousand worlds, filling right up to the Land of Ultimate Bliss, and my body is like a maṇi jewel.

The Chan master Heze said: “The original face is true suchness; in the light of the śarīra I recognize it; lost for ten thousand kalpas, I now awaken, and only now know that my own nature is Mañjuśrī.” (The original face is true suchness; in the light of the śarīra one recognizes it; having lost one’s own head — one’s original nature — for ten thousand kalpas, one only now awakens, and knows that one’s own nature is Mañjuśrī.) “Self-nature pure is the stainless buddha; self-nature suchlike is the free buddha; self-nature unobscured is the buddha of light; self-nature firm is the indestructible buddha.” All the buddhas, one after another, are complete in oneself; they cannot be told in full, and they are only one “nature.” Nature is the heart, the heart is the buddha. New buddhas and old buddhas have no two bodies: the golden body is this one “〇,” the Dharma-body is this one “〇,” the original face is this one “〇,” empty space is this one “〇,” up to heaven and down to earth, all is this one “〇”; heaven and earth may perish, but this “〇” does not perish. May I ask what this “〇” is? It is the breath of true yin and yang of heaven, earth, the human body and the ten thousand things. Refined into the true breath of pure yang, becoming this one “〇” — only that is the true self, only that is true inner nature and life, only that is the indestructible true body of the golden immortal, only that is the Original Spirit that is neither born nor perishes, only that is the Tathāgata Buddha of “nothing whatever.”

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

【注释】此步功法为道家气功的最高理想境界，所谓九年面壁之功，不是说九年完成大丹，而是用达摩九年面壁的故事，来隐喻本阶段的常寂常定，依灭尽定而寂灭之，大地山河尽归于虚，我与天地合一，与宇宙同体，色空俱泯，形神俱妙，神光周足，法相圆满，一切归元，所以也叫炼虚合道。

据说古人能练功到此境界者，千万人中亦难得一人。本书作者千峰老人亦承认功未到此，只得乃师口传。此节所述，在使学者阅之可窥见道家功法全程，编者不拟再详加注释。

This step is the highest ideal state of Daoist qigong. The so-called work of “nine years of facing the wall” does not mean completing the great elixir in nine years; it uses the story of Bodhidharma facing the wall for nine years as a metaphor for the constant stillness and constant absorption of this stage: relying on the absorption of extinction to be quiescent and extinguished, the great earth, mountains and rivers all return to emptiness, I am one with heaven and earth, one body with the universe, form and emptiness both vanish, body and spirit both wondrous, the spiritual light all-sufficient, the Dharma form complete, everything returned to the origin — which is why it is also called “refining emptiness to unite with the Way.”

It is said that among the ancients, those who could practice up to this state were hard to find one in ten million. The author of this book, the Old Man of Qianfeng, also admits that his own practice did not reach this step, and that he only received his teacher’s oral transmission. What is set out in this section is so that students, reading it, may glimpse the whole course of the Daoist methods; the editor does not intend to annotate further in detail.

Key Terms

  • Returning to emptiness and facing the wall: the stage of refining spirit to return to emptiness and refining emptiness to unite with the Way; “nine years of facing the wall” uses the story of Bodhidharma as a metaphor.
  • Method of the hibernating dragon: the Yang Spirit drawn into the Ancestral Opening, the form body closed and refined, like a dragon nourishing its pearl, like a hen brooding its egg, relying on the absorption of extinction to be quiescent and extinguished.
  • Absorption of extinction: a deep absorption in which mind and mental factors are all extinguished (a Buddhist term).
  • Release from the corpse: the Daoist method of leaving the form behind and departing; what Zhao describes is pressing the tongue against the Mysterious Breast point so that the body is like a dead person (dangerous, must never be attempted).
  • Empty space shattered: the ultimate state of refining emptiness to unite with the Way, uniting with the Way in truth.
  • Setting to work, turning the hand, completing, letting go: the four stages of refining essence into breath, refining breath into spirit, refining spirit to return to emptiness, and refining emptiness to unite with the Way.
  • Maṇi jewel: the wish-fulfilling jewel, a metaphor for the incomparable radiance of a body of pure yang.
  • Seven Perfected: the seven disciples of Wang Chongyang of the Complete Perfection school (Ma Danyang, Qiu Chuji and others).
  • 〇: the symbol that runs through Zhao’s whole book, standing for the breath of the Prior Heaven true One, the original face, the golden body, the Dharma-body, empty space.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The end point of the whole book is the “〇”: all names and forms finally return to “this one 〇” — the original face, which everyone has.
  2. The author himself says he did not reach it: Zhao Bichen’s candor is worth remembering; this step can simply be read as a “map” for understanding the whole course of the elixir path.
  3. Never attempt “release from the corpse”: pressing the tongue against the Mysterious Breast point so that the body cannot move, like a dead person, carries serious dangers such as suffocation.
  4. Return to daily life: the Chan master Heze says “self-nature pure is the stainless buddha” — the foothold of cultivation is a pure heart and nature and ordinary daily life.

Translator's Notes

  1. In the original, this step has two formulas, one figure, one passage of explanation, one question and answer, and the words of several teachers; we number them §1–§4.
  2. “勿另其出色身”: 另 (“other”) is probably a mistake for 令 (“let”); reproduced as is.
  3. “阅进 ten thousand times ten thousand elixir scriptures”: 进 is probably a mistake for 尽 (“through”); reproduced as is.
  4. “Empty absorption 衡极”: 衡 is probably a mistake for 臻 (“reach”); reproduced as is.
  5. “Which is why Patriarch Bodhidharma had the work of 9 years in the Snowy Mountains”: the original uses the Arabic numeral “9” here; reproduced as is. Bodhidharma is usually said to have faced the wall for nine years at the Shaolin Monastery on Mount Song, while the “Snowy Mountains” were where Śākyamuni practiced austerities; so in the original.
  6. “Self-nature 清静 is the stainless buddha”: 清静 is usually written 清净; reproduced as is.
  7. “With a front 簾 and a back 簾”: 簾 is the same as 帘 (“curtain”); reproduced as is.
  8. The Chan master Heze is Shenhui of the Tang (a disciple of Huineng, the Sixth Patriarch of Chan); Shunyizi is unidentified.
