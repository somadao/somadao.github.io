The Sixteen Steps of Method Formulas · 第十步 收炁法诀

Step Ten · The Formula for Drawing In the Breath

The formula of Step Ten, the formula for drawing in the breath — close the key points of the dragon and tiger passes, the eyes guard the Muddy Pellet, the tongue joins the Governing Vessel

About This Chapter

Step Nine pointed out that when the śarīra is about to be formed but is not yet complete, the greatest hidden danger is emission at night without dreams or thoughts (losing the original yang). This step is a protective method specifically for that, called “the formula for drawing in the breath.”

  1. The warning sign: in the absence of thought, the root of desire “throbs, throbs” (it does not rise) — a sign that the true treasure is about to be lost that night.
  2. The method: before sleep, sit quietly with a calm heart, the right hand above and the left hand below, palms facing each other in front of the belly; the middle finger of the left hand hooks back to press the center of the right palm (the dragon point, which connects to the heart), and the middle finger of the right hand presses the center of the left palm (the tiger point, which connects to the kidneys) (Figure 25). With the intention draw the root of desire back once, close the mouth, tongue against the palate; inhaling through the nose, the eyes look up from the lower left; exhaling through the nose, the eyes look down from the upper right — one inhalation and one exhalation make one circle; turn nine circles, pause, four sets of nine in all (thirty-six circles), and then go to sleep.
  3. A story: in 1930 a disciple of the same age as Zhao (seventy), busy with household affairs during the day, forgot to do this practice before sleep and lost his original yang; Zhao told him he could only make it good by refining again from the first step.
  4. Breaking wind and the two calls of nature: Zhao also explains how to hold in breaking wind (xugong) when the true breath is full, and how to hold back when the bowels or bladder are urgent.
  5. Two kinds of loss must be distinguished: when the ancient patriarchs lost the elixir after it had ripened because they did not stop the fire in time, it was “the elixir full and the breath complete”; the throbbing of the root of desire now is “the elixir not full, the breath not complete, deviant fire throbbing” — the two are different.

Qiu Ling’s note points out that the dragon and tiger points are the Laogong point in the center of the palm (on the Hand Jueyin Pericardium channel), which clears the heart, drains heat and steadies the heart and kidneys; he adds that on closing the practice one rubs the eyes of the waist with the palms until warm — “close the door, lock it and seal it too.”

Safety note (translator): The original says that when the bowels or bladder are urgent one can use this method to “contract them and let it out only after half a day” — please do not hold back urine or stool for long periods; this harms the bladder and intestines, and is especially unsuitable for people with prostate, urinary, hemorrhoid or constipation problems. Breaking wind is a normal physiological phenomenon and need not be deliberately held in. Turn the eyes gently; people with eye conditions should leave this out. Emission within a certain frequency is a normal physiological phenomenon and no cause for anxiety; if it is frequent or accompanied by discomfort, see a doctor.

Original Text and Translation

Figure 24 · The Formula for Drawing In the Breath

In the figure an old man with a long beard and a shaven head, in a wide-sleeved robe, sits upright cross-legged, his two hands joined in front of the lower belly; below the hands, at the waist, a gourd-shaped knot is tied, with two cords hanging down to the knees — a metaphor for the seminal ducts drawn in and closed tight, like a tied-up bag.

Formula
Original (Chinese)

闭住龙虎关诀穴，

目守泥丸舌接督。

吸提呼降气归窍，

阳升气发急回中。

Close the two key points of the dragon and tiger (press them with the middle fingers of both hands), the eyes guard the Muddy Pellet, the tongue joins the Governing Vessel (the tongue against the palate). Draw up on the inhalation, lower on the exhalation, and the breath returns into the opening; as soon as yang rises and the breath stirs, quickly draw it back into the central palace.

§1
Original (Chinese)

前第九步功是精炁成舍利子之法，其舍利子要成未成之时，夜内作怪，无梦无念自己泄出，修至此要慎重习练。然而以何诀法戒之？必得用第十步口诀，可以保护夜内不失真宝。当真精足时，或是采取有念的幻精，或是眼视色欲而心动，或是耳闻淫声而念动，不论有何动，如淫根自动，即是夜里要遗失真宝之兆。淫根自动者，是无念之中，淫根由根一动一动，非是自举也。如不用第十步法诀，夜内遗失真宝，甚可惜也！

The practice of Step Nine above is the method of refining essence and breath into the śarīra. When the śarīra is about to be formed but is not yet complete, at night it “plays tricks,” leaking out by itself without dreams or thoughts; one must practice with great care at this point. With what formula can it be prevented? One must use the formula of Step Ten, which can protect the true treasure from being lost at night. When the true essence is full — whether one has gathered “illusory essence” with thought, or the heart has stirred at the sight of sexual desire, or a thought has stirred at the sound of lewd voices — whatever the kind of stirring, as long as the root of desire stirs by itself, it is a sign that the true treasure is about to be lost at night. What is meant by the root of desire stirring by itself is that, in the absence of thought, the root of desire throbs, throbs from its base, without rising up by itself. If one does not use the formula of Step Ten and the true treasure is lost at night, it is a great pity!

§2
Original (Chinese)

回想公元一九三〇年，有一弟子年已七十，与我同岁，来佛堂泣曰：弟子千辛万苦修至此，今夜遗失元阳，请问老师可有法挽回否？答曰：由初步功再炼补至此，可以挽回，无有别的法诀。昔日传你时曾曰，如有淫根一动一动之景，即是夜内遗失元阳景到，睡觉前急速用第十步收炁法诀，若不炼，夜内准漏失元阳，尔不细心用功，有遗失之景到，睡觉时忘了用功，致有此患，你怨何人？回答曰：弟子白天时自觉淫根发动，心想今夜睡时再用第十步功夫，但这天净办家务之事，睡觉时忘却用功，致有遗失之患。余曰：从今往后，每日早晚功勤，要平心静坐，将二目合并归一，下视丹田，鼻内吸呼气亦由内下降丹田，百日内外丹田发热，眼发电光，耳内风声，至此可将失去元阳炼回，可用小周天采补之法，尔当细心锻炼，不可粗心，由此炼舍利子成也。

I recall that in 1930 a disciple, already seventy and the same age as I, came to the Buddha hall weeping and said: “With a thousand hardships I have cultivated up to this point, and tonight I lost my original yang. Teacher, is there any way to retrieve it?” I answered: “Refine and replenish it again from the first step up to this point, and it can be retrieved; there is no other formula. When I transmitted it to you before, I told you: if there is the scene of the root of desire throbbing, throbbing, the scene of losing the original yang at night has come, and before sleep you must quickly use the formula for drawing in the breath of Step Ten; if you do not refine it, you are sure to lose your original yang at night. You did not practice carefully; when the scene of loss came, you forgot to practice at bedtime, and so this misfortune befell you — whom can you blame?” He answered: “During the day I felt the root of desire stirring, and thought I would do the work of Step Ten tonight when I went to sleep; but this whole day I was busy with household affairs, and at bedtime I forgot to practice, and so I suffered this loss.” I said: “From now on, practice diligently every morning and evening; sit quietly with a calm heart, bring the two eyes together into one and look down at the elixir field, and let the breathing in the nose also descend from within to the elixir field; in a hundred days or so, the elixir field will grow hot, lightning will flash in the eyes, and there will be the sound of wind in the ears; at that point the lost original yang can be refined back; then you can use the method of gathering and replenishing of the Lesser Celestial Circuit. You must forge it carefully, not carelessly, and from this you can refine the śarīra.”

§3
Original (Chinese)

盖炼养舍利子在要足不足之时，往往内里作怪，淫根涨动，临睡时要平心静坐，右手在上，左手在下，掌心相对，置于腹前，将左手中指一回，点住右手手心之处，即是龙穴，此穴是活的，内炁通心；再用右手中指点住左手心虎穴处，此穴内炁通肾（图25）。用意将淫根往回一缩，闭口舌顶督脉，鼻向内一吸气，眼要由下左边朝上一看；又鼻往外一呼气，眼要由上右边向下一看，此为神气一吸一呼一转。如此转九回一停，稍定一刻，照前将淫根往回一缩，神、气、目照前做法再转九回，又一定。如此共转四个九回。神气转时，舌尖倒顶，接我任督二脉。转完三十六回再睡觉，精炁管闭住，如何有夜失之患？

When refining and nourishing the śarīra reaches the point where it is about to be full but not yet full, the body often “plays tricks,” and the root of desire throbs and swells. At bedtime, sit quietly with a calm heart: the right hand above, the left hand below, palms facing each other, in front of the belly; hook the middle finger of the left hand back to press the center of the right palm — this is the “dragon point”; this point is alive, and the breath within it connects to the heart; then press the “tiger point” in the center of the left palm with the middle finger of the right hand — the breath within this point connects to the kidneys (Figure 25). With the intention draw the root of desire back once, close the mouth, the tongue pressing the Governing Vessel (the palate); as the nose breathes in, the eyes look upward from the lower left; as the nose breathes out, the eyes look downward from the upper right — this is the spirit and breath turning one circle with one inhalation and one exhalation. Turn nine circles like this and pause, settling for a moment; draw the root of desire back once as before, and let spirit, breath and eyes turn another nine circles in the same way, then settle again. Turn four sets of nine like this in all. While the spirit and breath are turning, the tip of the tongue presses back (curled up and back against the palate), connecting my Conception and Governing vessels. Go to sleep after turning thirty-six circles; with the ducts of essence and breath closed, how could there be any worry of loss at night?

Figure 25 · Pressing the Dragon and Tiger Points

Two hands with the palms facing each other: the right hand above (marked “upper hand, right”), coming in from the right, palm down; the left hand below (marked “lower hand, left”), coming in from the left, palm up. The middle finger of the left hand hooks back to press the center of the right palm (the dragon point), and the middle finger of the right hand presses the center of the left palm (the tiger point).

§4
Original (Chinese)

精炁由前不能泄漏，还得用小周天采补之法采补之，越补越足，自有真炁由肛门泄出，即是爱出虚恭。此虚恭若是饮食虚气，或许着了寒凉，肛门内不好受，可以随便出，不必禁止；若是真炁足出虚恭，要禁止，不可令它出来。法诀曰，照前点住龙虎二穴，舌接督脉，肛门一缩，用神气一吸一呼，如此上下七回，虚恭内真炁散于周身，助我身体强壮，自然虚恭停止。若大小二便闭不住，又离厕所远，速用手点住龙虎二穴，舌接督脉，用意将肛门一缩，用神气吸提呼降，将大小二便活动筋缩住，过半天后再出。以上所说，全是怕走漏失真炁。因无真炁，不能成舍利子。舍利子不足，止火之景不到，止不了火，过不了大关，成不了漏尽通，马阴不能藏相，如何养成牟尼珠？牟尼珠不成，道胎由何而生？古时人修功至此，往往有走丹者，皆因诀法不明不全，而心不专。昔日白玉蟾、邱长春、曹还阳等祖师精足丹熟，已到止火之候，未及采病，夜里走失真阳，是丹足炁满，不止火之过；今淫根涨动，是丹不足炁不满，邪火涨动，若不收炁闭精管，准失元阳。学者宜细心悟之，对此二者要分别清楚。

Once the essence and breath can no longer leak out in front, one must still gather and replenish them with the method of the Lesser Celestial Circuit, and the more one replenishes, the fuller they become; (at this time) true breath will naturally leak out through the anus — that is, one tends to break wind (“xugong”). If this wind is empty breath produced by food and drink, or comes from catching cold and discomfort in the anus, it can be let out freely without holding it in; but if one breaks wind because the true breath is full, it must be held in and not allowed out. The formula: press the dragon and tiger points as before, the tongue joining the Governing Vessel, contract the anus once, and with the spirit and breath make one inhalation and one exhalation, up and down seven times like this; the true breath in the wind disperses through the whole body, helping my body to be strong, and naturally one no longer breaks wind. If one cannot hold the bowels or bladder and is far from a toilet, quickly press the dragon and tiger points with the hands, the tongue joining the Governing Vessel, contract the anus once with the intention, and with the spirit and breath drawing up on the inhalation and lowering on the exhalation, contract the sinews that control the bowels and bladder, and let it out only after half a day (see the safety note).

All the above is for fear of leaking the true breath. For without the true breath the śarīra cannot be formed. If the śarīra is not full, the scene of stopping the fire does not come; without stopping the fire, one cannot pass the great pass or attain “the power of the exhaustion of leaks,” the horse-like retraction cannot occur — how then could one nourish the “mani pearl” (the precious pearl, meaning the elixir)? If the mani pearl is not formed, from where would the Embryo of the Way be born? When people of old practiced up to this point, there were often those who “lost the elixir,” always because the formula was unclear or incomplete and the heart was not single-minded. In the past, patriarchs such as Bai Yuchan, Qiu Changchun (Qiu Chuji) and Cao Huanyang, their essence full and their elixir ripe, having already reached the time to stop the fire, did not manage to gather it in time (the original has 采病, see the Translator’s Notes) and lost their true yang at night — that was the fault of the elixir being full and the breath complete without stopping the fire; the throbbing of the root of desire now is the elixir not full, the breath not complete, deviant fire throbbing, and if one does not draw in the breath and close the seminal ducts, one is sure to lose the original yang. Students should realize this carefully and distinguish these two situations clearly.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

【注释】行功到第九步法轮自转，炼养舍利子，此中大患，为夜间泄精走失元阳真宝，故千峰老人特设专章论述，介绍收炁法诀，预早指出遗失元阳之兆，保护真宝以免走漏。

收炁法诀中两手中指须点住手心龙虎两穴，此两穴即是手厥阴心包经之劳宫穴，此穴连于心包，功用清心泄热，可镇定心肾功能。心者性也，发于二目；肾者命也，发于淫根（阳物、男生殖器）。故行此功时，淫根一缩，心意一提，两眼转动，心通两目，即有闭紧精管不使外漏之效。况舌舐上颚天池穴，因其上通脑髓，接通任督两脉，引炁归根，防止精炁下泄。

此功呼吸转眼之法，同第二步口诀转法轮收炁法一样，提肛吸气，眼随头转，由会阴子位向左上泥丸午位，随之鼻子一呼气，再从午位由上向右下复到子位，为一圆周。转完三十六周，再静坐一、二十分钟，然后用两手掌心搓腰眼，发热为止。这是关门上锁再加封。

赵氏称：“此法是仙佛所留，丹经道书不敢泄漏，余今全泄之”。可见此法素为古人所习用。

When practice reaches the Dharma Wheel turning by itself in Step Nine and the refining and nourishing of the śarīra, the great danger is emission at night, losing the true treasure of the original yang; so the Old Man of Qianfeng devotes a special chapter to it, presenting the formula for drawing in the breath, pointing out in advance the signs of losing the original yang, and protecting the true treasure from leaking away.

In the formula for drawing in the breath, the middle fingers of both hands must press the dragon and tiger points in the centers of the palms. These two points are the Laogong point of the Hand Jueyin Pericardium channel (the place in the palm that the tip of the middle finger touches when making a fist); this point is connected to the pericardium, and its function is to clear the heart and drain heat; it can steady the functions of the heart and kidneys. The heart is inner nature, issuing from the two eyes; the kidneys are life, issuing from the root of desire (the member, the male genital organ). So in doing this practice, the root of desire draws back, the heart and intention lift, the two eyes turn, and the heart connects with the two eyes, which has the effect of closing the seminal ducts tight and keeping them from leaking. Moreover, the tongue licking the Celestial Pool point on the palate, because it connects upward with the brain marrow, can join the Conception and Governing vessels, lead the breath back to the root and keep the essence and breath from leaking downward.

The method of breathing and turning the eyes in this practice is the same as the method of turning the Dharma Wheel to draw in the breath in the formula of Step Two: draw up the anus and inhale, the eyes following the head, from the zi position at the perineum, up to the left to the wu position at the Muddy Pellet; then with one exhalation through the nose, from the wu position down to the right back to the zi position — that is one circle. After turning thirty-six circles, sit quietly for another ten or twenty minutes, then rub the eyes of the waist with both palms until warm — this is “close the door, lock it and seal it too.”

Zhao says: “This method was left by the immortals and buddhas, and the elixir classics and Daoist books have never dared to reveal it; now I reveal all of it.” We can see that this method has long been in habitual use among the ancients.

Key Terms

  • Formula for drawing in the breath: a special method for preventing emission at night when the śarīra is about to be formed but is not yet complete.
  • The root of desire stirring by itself: in the absence of thought, the base of the member throbs, throbs (without rising) — a sign that the true treasure is about to be lost that night.
  • Dragon point, tiger point: the center of the right palm is the dragon point (connected to the heart), the center of the left palm the tiger point (connected to the kidneys); Qiu Ling’s note says they are the Laogong point.
  • Laogong point: the center of the palm, where the tip of the middle finger touches when making a fist, on the Hand Jueyin Pericardium channel.
  • Tongue tip pressed back: the tip of the tongue curled up and back against the palate, joining the Conception and Governing vessels.
  • Xugong: a polite term for breaking wind.
  • Losing the elixir: the essence and breath being lost, either because the fire was not stopped in time after the elixir ripened, or because the breath was not full and deviant fire throbbed.
  • Mani pearl: the precious pearl, meaning the elixir, the śarīra.
  • Power of the exhaustion of leaks: the state in which essence no longer leaks out; see Step Four.

Putting It into Practice

  1. A small practice before sleep: palms facing each other, the middle fingers pressing the centers of the palms, tongue against the palate, a light lift of the perineum, and the eyes turning slowly in circles with the breath — in itself a fine way to calm the spirit before sleep.
  2. Light, not forceful: “draw back” and “lift” are a light gathering with the intention, not clenching hard; turning the eyes is also just looking gently.
  3. Rub the eyes of the waist on closing: rub the eyes of the waist with both palms until warm — simple and easy.
  4. Don’t hold back the bowels or bladder: the original’s “let it out only after half a day” must not be followed; go when you need to.
  5. No need for anxiety: occasional emission is a normal physiological phenomenon; keep up quiet sitting and a regular daily routine; if it is frequent or uncomfortable, see a doctor.

Translator's Notes

  1. In the original, this step has one formula, two figures and three passages of explanation; we number them §1–§4 (the second half of §3 and §4 are one passage in the original, divided here at Figure 25).
  2. “The tongue presses the Governing Vessel,” “the tongue joins the Governing Vessel”: the tongue pressed against the palate, so that the Conception and Governing vessels are joined.
  3. “Did not manage to 采病” (未及采病): 病 (“illness”) is probably a mistake for 取 (“take”); reproduced as is, and translated as “gather.”
  4. Zhao says that in 1930 the disciple was “already seventy, the same age as I,” which agrees with Zhao Bichen having been born in 1860.
  5. The words Qiu Ling’s note quotes, “This method was left by the immortals and buddhas … now I reveal all of it,” do not appear in the text of this step; they may come from elsewhere in Zhao’s book; reproduced as is.
  6. Qiu Ling’s note says the dragon and tiger points are the Laogong point; Laogong lies in the center of the palm between the second and third metacarpal bones, on the Hand Jueyin Pericardium channel.
