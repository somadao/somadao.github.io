The Sixteen Steps of Method Formulas · 第十四步 婴儿显形

Step Fourteen · The Infant Takes Visible Form

The formula of Step Fourteen, the infant takes visible form — leaving the sea of suffering and reaching the other shore, letting go of everything, a carefree man at leisure

About This Chapter

After the Great Medicine has passed the gates and been ingested, one enters “refining breath into spirit” — what tradition calls “ten months of nourishing the embryo.” This step describes how the Embryo of the Way (also called the infant, the holy embryo, the Yang Spirit) is nourished in the central palace, how it moves up to the Muddy Pellet, and the scenes before it leaves the womb.

  1. The two breaths help the spirit form the embryo: the passes and openings are all open, and the two breaths (the breath of inner nature and the breath of life) naturally rise and descend in a cycle along the road of the three passes; in time one even forgets that they are turning by themselves — this is “refining the embryo-spirit.” After ten months the turning of the two breaths is very faint; after a year the true breath at the navel wheel no longer stirs, and “there remains only the Original Spirit, quietly illuminating” — this is true embryonic breathing. From passing the great pass to this point takes about two years in all.
  2. The three moves: refining essence into breath in the lower elixir field → moving to the central palace (the Crimson Palace) to refine breath into spirit and nourish the Embryo of the Way → moving to the upper elixir field (the Muddy Pellet) to refine the spirit for flight and leave the womb.
  3. The four dhyānas: in the first dhyāna thought stops, in the second the breath stops, in the third the pulse stops, in the fourth everything is extinguished.
  4. The cognitive spirit and the six powers: when the Embryo of the Way is fully nourished there are the “six powers” (the exhaustion of leaks, the heavenly eye, the heavenly ear, knowledge of past lives, knowledge of others’ minds, and the power of spiritual realms). Of these, the power of spiritual realms calls for the greatest care — the fortunes and misfortunes of people often appear within the light of wisdom, and if one likes to tell people of their fortunes and misfortunes and to show off the powers of one’s cultivation, one is bound by the cognitive spirit, and deviant demons arise from it — “having cultivated to this point, one becomes a useless person; it would have been better not to cultivate at all.”
  5. The scene of leaving the womb: snowflakes drifting in the air and heavenly flowers falling in profusion are the scene that the Embryo of the Way is complete and should leave the womb; to have the scene and not leave the womb is called being a “corpse-guarding ghost.”

Qiu Ling’s note quotes Wu Chongxu and Liu Huayang on the method of nourishing the embryo — “although it is in the middle field, it must be joined with the lower field and transformed together into one realm of emptiness” — and on how in the ten months of nourishing the embryo “each month has its own proofs”; and he points out that the “five eyes and six powers” spoken of by the ancients correspond to what are now called “extraordinary powers” such as seeing through objects and remote viewing.

Safety note (translator): What this step says about “great absorption,” “no breath coming out of the nose, no pulse in the hands,” “food must not contain salt,” “both food and pulse cut off,” as well as the “six powers” and foreknowing fortune and misfortune, are all accounts of ancient practitioners; do not imitate fasting or salt restriction, and do not pursue “supernatural powers.” Zhao Bichen himself warns clearly in this step that liking to tell people their fortunes and showing off powers is being bound by the cognitive spirit and going astray. If in quiet sitting you have experiences such as “being able to see other people’s fortunes” or “being able to hear special sounds,” and come to believe in them more and more, stop practicing and talk with your family or a professional.

Original Text and Translation

Figure 32 · Nourishing the Holy Embryo

In the figure a long-bearded Daoist in a wide-sleeved robe sits upright cross-legged, holding in both hands before his chest and belly a small figure sitting cross-legged — behind the small figure is a flame-shaped halo. This small figure is the “holy embryo” (the Embryo of the Way, the infant), nourished in the central palace.

Formula
Original (Chinese)

脱离苦海登彼岸，

撒手逍遥是闲汉。

作近三千大功德，

度尽世界成罗汉。

Leave the sea of suffering and reach the other shore; let go of everything and be free and at ease, a man at leisure. Complete (the original has 作近, see the Translator’s Notes) three thousand great meritorious deeds, ferry across all the beings of the world, and become an arhat.

§1
Original (Chinese)

采大药，过三关，服食之后，关窍俱已开通，此后二炁勤生，自能运转三关已通之路，一升一降，循环不已，自然而然转动，是为二炁助神结胎。久久炼之，将二炁运行，忘却自转，此为炼胎神。久久忘形转之，用双目内照丹田，十月之后，二炁转机甚微，但微动脐轮之内，虚境而已。炼之一年后，脐轮真炁不动，饮食不能吃盐，独存一寂照之元神，此为真胎息。至此鼻无出气，手无六脉，则大定得矣。真胎息在内，久静而心神意又动，胎息内有传情之意，即是心神意内，有一灵胎之意，停于真炁穴内。由过大关至此，总得二年之久，才有真胎息。将真胎息用三迁之法，迁升到中宫，中宫即是绛宫。胎息升到绛宫，才能得养灵胎。下丹田是炼精化炁之所，中丹田是炼炁化神之所，上丹田是炼神飞升之所。灵胎现形，当用迁行中宫之法诀。法诀者，初禅念住，二禅息住。胎息住者，是对下丹田胎炁，用两眼交并，合一内照，自然胎息一点一点上升中宫，此为神入炁中，待升中宫，然后炁包乎其神，即绛宫之炁包乎胎神，昏昏默默，浑浑沦沦，如在母胎一般，此为息住。中宫真炁包住的名曰道胎，道胎一结，胎炁发于目前，此是真慧光发现。学者见慧不用实在难，要尔心死性活，不被家务束缚，不教世尘牵连，一切之事灭尽，只知有慧光，别无他事，此为灭尽。若身内有一分阴气，自有慧魔发现，当用退魔之法，将魔磨去。此以上为初禅念住，二禅息住，三禅脉住，四禅灭尽。

After gathering the Great Medicine, passing the three passes and ingesting it, the passes and openings are all open; from then on the two breaths (the breath of inner nature and the breath of life) are diligently produced and can naturally turn along the road already opened through the three passes, one rising and one descending, cycling without end, turning of themselves — this is “the two breaths helping the spirit form the embryo.” Refining for a long time and letting the two breaths move, one forgets even that they “turn by themselves” — this is “refining the embryo-spirit.” Turning for a long time in forgetfulness of form, shine inward on the elixir field with the two eyes; after ten months the turning of the two breaths is very faint, only a slight stirring within the navel wheel, just a realm of emptiness. After a year of refining, the true breath at the navel wheel no longer stirs, food must not contain salt (see the safety note), and there remains only the Original Spirit, quietly illuminating — this is “true embryonic breathing.” At this point there is no breath coming out of the nose and no six pulses in the hands (the pulse can hardly be felt), and one attains great absorption.

With true embryonic breathing within, after long stillness the heart, spirit and intention stir again; within the embryonic breathing there is an intention of passing feeling, that is, within the heart, spirit and intention there is an intention of a “numinous embryo,” resting in the true Cavity of Breath. From passing the great pass to this point takes about two years in all before there is true embryonic breathing. With the method of the “three moves,” move the true embryonic breathing up to the central palace — the central palace is the Crimson Palace. Only when the embryonic breathing has risen to the Crimson Palace can the numinous embryo be nourished. The lower elixir field is the place for refining essence into breath, the middle elixir field the place for refining breath into spirit, the upper elixir field the place for refining the spirit to fly up. When the numinous embryo takes form, one should use the formula of “moving it to the central palace.” The formula: in the first dhyāna thought stops, in the second dhyāna the breath stops. What is called “the embryonic breathing stopping” is this: facing the embryonic breath in the lower elixir field, join the two eyes and shine inward as one, and the embryonic breathing naturally rises little by little to the central palace — this is “the spirit entering the breath”; when it has risen to the central palace, then “the breath envelops the spirit,” that is, the breath of the Crimson Palace envelops the embryo-spirit, dim and silent, undifferentiated and whole, as if in the mother’s womb — this is “the breath stopping.” What the true breath of the central palace envelops is called the “Embryo of the Way.” Once the Embryo of the Way is formed, the embryonic breath issues before the eyes, which is the true light of wisdom appearing. For the student to see the light of wisdom and not use it is truly hard: you must have the heart die and inner nature live, not be bound by household affairs, not be entangled in worldly dust, all affairs extinguished, knowing only the light of wisdom and nothing else — this is “extinction.” If there is still a part of yin breath in the body, “demons of wisdom” will naturally appear, and one should grind away the demons with the method of driving off demons. The above is: in the first dhyāna thought stops, in the second dhyāna the breath stops, in the third dhyāna the pulse stops, in the fourth dhyāna everything is extinguished.

§2
Original (Chinese)

问曰：师言胎息上升中宫，用中宫先天炁将胎息包住，名为道胎。胎炁发于目前，即是慧光。这慧光内，如何发出世界之事，又生邪魔，通在慧光发现，是何理由？

答曰：道胎养于中宫，心似莲叶不着水，光光净净，空空如如，一个无事无为、自在逍遥之散汉也。随方就圆，温养于中，将道胎养足，炁发于目前，此为纯阳之神能生慧，自有六通之验矣。六通者，漏尽通、天眼通、天耳通、宿命通、他心通、神境通。前下手炼精时，已有漏尽一通，至此才有五通之验也。惟神境通有识神用事，若不能保护心君，为识神所缚，魔已入于心矣。识神用事，慧光内常现世人过福，你见着人爱说人的过福，显我修道之功能，殊不知尔被识神所缚，由此邪魔生出，种种之事发现。修功至此，成一废人，不如不修好。学者若见识神用事，速求明师指点退魔之法诀，用子、卯、午、酉、子天机，其魔当时不见，内里阴气，化为阳炁，此为识死性活。古佛云：三迁刻中无间断，行行坐坐转分明。此发明退魔口诀，要尔尽夜黑白转运，此是真火，将魔磨化为元神，谓之神境通。

Question: Teacher, you say that when the embryonic breathing has risen to the central palace and is enveloped by the Prior Heaven breath of the central palace, it is called the Embryo of the Way; when the embryonic breath issues before the eyes, it is the light of wisdom. How is it that within this light of wisdom the affairs of the world appear, and deviant demons arise too, all appearing within the light of wisdom? What is the principle?

Answer: When the Embryo of the Way is nourished in the central palace, the heart is like a lotus leaf that water does not cling to, bright and clean, empty and suchlike, simply a person at leisure with nothing to do and no action, free and at ease. Adapting to square and round, one warms and nourishes within it; when the Embryo of the Way is fully nourished and the breath issues before the eyes, this is the spirit of pure yang able to give rise to wisdom, and naturally there are the proofs of the “six powers.” The six powers are: the power of the exhaustion of leaks, the power of the heavenly eye, the power of the heavenly ear, the power of knowing past lives, the power of knowing others’ minds, and the power of spiritual realms. When one first set to work refining essence, one already had the power of “the exhaustion of leaks”; only here are there proofs of the other five powers. Only in the power of spiritual realms is the cognitive spirit at work; if one cannot protect the “sovereign of the heart” (the original heart) and is bound by the cognitive spirit, the demon has already entered the heart. When the cognitive spirit is at work, the fortunes and misfortunes of people (the original has 过福, see the Translator’s Notes) often appear within the light of wisdom; seeing them, you like to tell people their fortunes and misfortunes and show off the powers of my cultivation — not knowing that you are already bound by the cognitive spirit, and from this deviant demons arise and all sorts of things appear. Having cultivated to this point, one becomes a useless person; it would have been better not to cultivate at all. If students find the cognitive spirit at work, they should quickly seek an enlightened teacher to point out the formula for driving off demons: using the mechanism of heaven of zi, mao, wu, you, zi (turning the eyes), the demon vanishes at once, and the yin breath in the body turns into yang breath — this is “the cognitive dies and inner nature lives” (the cognitive spirit dies and true inner nature comes alive). An ancient buddha said: “The work of the three moves, moment by moment, without interruption; walking and sitting, turn it clearly.” This explains the formula for driving off demons: you must keep turning without stopping day and night (the original has 尽夜), black and white; this is the true fire, grinding the demon away and transforming it into the Original Spirit — this is called “the power of spiritual realms.”

§3
Original (Chinese)

问曰：这道胎在中宫，用先天炁养足，迁升上丹田，有景无景？若无景，何时上迁？

答曰：有景。若无景到，何时知上迁泥丸宫？其景者，即目前之白月光，内有金光，此是上迁之景到，速当移念，将此金光入于性内，合璧阴阳神养之。阳神未壮，比如婴儿幼小，必用乳哺之法哺之。其法曰：空而不空。内实有金光，为不空。又由祖窍内生出月华之光，二光合一，惟阳神寂照于上丹田，相与浑融，化成一虚空之大境，斯为存养之全体，乃为乳哺之首务也。存养功久，自有出神之景到。以上为上迁之法也。而三迁者，是精炁神三宝合一，化成这个月华之光，非得见雪花纷飞，天花乱坠，才能出胎，亲为佛子。

Question: When this Embryo of the Way in the central palace has been fully nourished with the Prior Heaven breath and moves up to the upper elixir field, is there a scene? If there is no scene, when does it move up?

Answer: There is a scene. If there were no scene, how would one know when to move it up to the Muddy Pellet palace? The scene is: golden light within the white moonlight before the eyes — this is the scene for moving up having come; one should quickly “shift the thought,” draw this golden light into inner nature, join it with the spirit of yin and yang like a jade disc, and nourish it. While the Yang Spirit is not yet strong, it is like a small infant and must be fed by the method of “nursing.” The method: “empty yet not empty” — within there really is golden light, so it is “not empty.” From within the Ancestral Opening the light of moonbeams is also born; the two lights unite into one, and only the Yang Spirit quietly illuminates in the upper elixir field, merging with it, transforming into one great realm of empty space — this is the whole of preserving and nourishing, the first task of nursing. When the work of preserving and nourishing has gone on long, the scene of “sending out the spirit” will naturally come. The above is the method of moving up. What are called the “three moves” are the three treasures of essence, breath and spirit uniting into one and transforming into this light of moonbeams; only when one sees snowflakes whirling and heavenly flowers falling in profusion can one leave the womb and personally become a child of the Buddha.

§4
Original (Chinese)

问曰：师言三迁之法是炼精成舍利子，又为真种，迁升到中宫，是炼炁成道胎之所，又迁升到上丹田，为泥丸宫，是炼神出胎之所。实在不明白精能成舍利子，又能成道胎，又能出泥丸宫，此三迁之法乞师剖解。

答曰：人与人交能生人，一人身内神与炁交，能生舍利子，是精虫化生，因神炁同宫合一，将精虫团聚归一，成为舍利子。舍利子养足，光华射目，神炁犹如磁石吸铁，两不相离，一得永得，无所妄驰，安稳自在，识性渐渐消磨，真性渐渐灵觉，妄事不想，正念自存，久久观照，神炁合一，此为识性死，真性活，心意无内无外，浑然一团禅定。久静而动，动者舍利子真种自动也，由下丹田，一点一点上升中宫定住。中宫者即是绛宫，此处是炁与气交合之处，能生先天真一之炁，即是道胎。二炁者，一是性内先天真一性炁，其光是月华之光；一是命内先天真一命炁，其光是金黄之光，二光交合，能化生道胎。到此又不可用无为，佛炁有生活之理，吸呼有滋养之机，自当将内呼吸气归于真炁内，为结胎之本，又为养胎之源，自当知道以心主宰而定息；息未定时，以心调之，即以心炁团之为调。息不调，道胎不足。只知有神，不知有胎，全体归于神内，谓之万法归一。此真一之炁，即是性内真阳之炁，与命内真阴之炁结合，成为真一之祖炁，为道胎足满，上迁泥丸宫，又谓之一迁。此是上丹田，即是脑髓，此处是神与神交合之府，能生先天真一之阳神。神与神交合归一者，即是左眼为阳神，右眼为阴神，二眼和合归一，息无出入之息，遂得禅定三昧之乐；六根灭尽，二性圆明，慧光朗彻。如来云：“分明不受燃灯记，自有灵光照古今”。此慧光一出，无昼无夜，光明朗彻，得大自在，俱足六通，实谓之无上正等正觉也。久之自有一切诸事发见，见慧不用，守定中心，不被识神魔障所害。燃灯佛曰：“寂灭为乐”。寂灭非是死亡，乃是道胎圆彻之实证也。六祖云：“禅心无想，禅性无生”。六脉全无，鼻息灭尽，故曰寂灭。道胎佛性融融，如杲日当空，故曰为乐，又为真空而不空。若是到此时，雪花飘空，天花乱坠，此是出胎景到。有景到，而不出胎，谓之守尸鬼。

Question: Teacher, you speak of the method of the three moves: refining essence into the śarīra, also called the true seed; moving up to the central palace, the place for refining breath into the Embryo of the Way; moving up again to the Muddy Pellet palace of the upper elixir field, the place for refining the spirit and leaving the womb. I really do not understand: essence can become the śarīra, can also become the Embryo of the Way, and can also leave by the Muddy Pellet palace — please, teacher, analyze this method of the three moves.

Answer: When person meets person, a person can be born; when spirit and breath meet within one person’s body, the śarīra can be born — it is transformed from the seminal essence: because spirit and breath unite in the same palace, the seminal essence is gathered and returned to one, becoming the śarīra. When the śarīra is fully nourished, its radiance shines into the eyes, and spirit and breath are like a magnet drawing iron, never apart; once obtained, it is obtained forever, no longer racing off wildly, settled and at ease; the cognitive nature gradually wears away, true inner nature gradually awakens, one does not think of deluded matters, right thought is naturally present, one contemplates and illuminates for a long time, and spirit and breath unite into one — this is “the cognitive nature dies and true inner nature lives”; heart and intention have no inside and no outside, one undifferentiated mass of dhyāna absorption. After long stillness there is movement, and what moves is the śarīra true seed itself, rising little by little from the lower elixir field to the central palace and settling there. The central palace is the Crimson Palace; this is the place where “breath (炁) and breath (气) unite,” which can produce the breath of the Prior Heaven true One, that is, the Embryo of the Way. What are called the “two breaths”: one is the Prior Heaven true One breath of inner nature within inner nature, whose light is the light of moonbeams; the other is the Prior Heaven true One breath of life within life, whose light is golden yellow; when the two lights unite, they can transform into and give birth to the Embryo of the Way.

Here again one must not use non-action: the Buddha breath has the principle of living, breathing has the mechanism of nourishing, and naturally one should return the breath of inner breathing into the true breath, as the root for forming the embryo and as the source for nourishing it; naturally one should know to let the heart preside and settle the breath; when the breath is not yet settled, regulate it with the heart — that is, use the heart to hold the breath together, which is called “regulating.” If the breath is not regulated, the Embryo of the Way is not sufficient. Knowing only the spirit and not knowing the embryo, the whole returns into the spirit — this is called “the ten thousand methods returning to one.” This true One breath is the true yang breath within inner nature uniting with the true yin breath within life to become the true One ancestral breath; when the Embryo of the Way is full, it moves up to the Muddy Pellet palace, which is also called “one move.” This is the upper elixir field, that is, the brain marrow; this is the mansion where “spirit and spirit unite,” which can produce the Prior Heaven true One Yang Spirit. What is called “spirit and spirit uniting and returning to one” is this: the left eye is the yang spirit, the right eye the yin spirit; the two eyes unite and return to one, the breath has no going out or coming in, and so one obtains the joy of dhyāna and samādhi: the six roots extinguished, the two natures perfectly bright, the light of wisdom clear and penetrating. The Tathāgata said: “Clearly not receiving Dīpaṃkara’s prediction, there is a numinous light of its own that shines through past and present.” (Clearly there is no need for Dīpaṃkara Buddha to give the prediction; naturally there is a numinous light shining through past and present.) Once this light of wisdom comes forth, there is no day or night, the light clear and penetrating, one attains great freedom, the six powers all complete — truly it can be called “unsurpassed perfect enlightenment.” In time all sorts of affairs will naturally appear; seeing the light of wisdom, one does not use it, and guarding the center firmly, one is not harmed by the cognitive spirit and demonic obstructions. Dīpaṃkara Buddha said: “Quiescent extinction is joy.” Quiescent extinction is not death but the real proof of the Embryo of the Way being perfectly complete. The Sixth Patriarch said: “The dhyāna heart has no thought; the dhyāna nature has no birth.” The six pulses all gone, the breath of the nose wholly extinguished — hence “quiescent extinction”; the Buddha nature of the Embryo of the Way is warm and harmonious, like a bright sun in the sky — hence “is joy,” also called “true emptiness that is not empty.” If at this time snowflakes drift in the air and heavenly flowers fall in profusion, this is the scene of leaving the womb having come. To have the scene and not leave the womb is called being a “corpse-guarding ghost” (a ghost that guards its corpse).

§5
Original (Chinese)

问曰：师言雪花飘空，天花乱坠，是由何处发生？因何天花乱坠？

答曰：神与神交合，能生神。此神是性之真神，与命之真神二光合一（图33）。此胎圆炁养足，才有天花乱坠。此是出胎景到，速当出胎。天花乱坠者，是尔眼内所见。雪花飘飞，是胎足满之故。寂无禅师曰：“胎圆节至雪花飞，念动飘空上顶机，莫谓如来枯寂道，法身出寂又归依”。华阳祖著《慧命经》集说云：“此乃出定之时，当出而不出，则滞于法身，为定之所缚，不能神通，千百亿化身”。胎圆节至者，道胎圆之极也。见雪花，离凡体，而念动向太空。不知此机，是未得师也。

Question: Teacher, from where do the snowflakes drifting in the air and the heavenly flowers falling in profusion arise? Why do heavenly flowers fall in profusion?

Answer: When spirit unites with spirit, it can give birth to spirit. This spirit is the true spirit of inner nature and the true spirit of life, the two lights uniting into one (Figure 33). Only when this embryo is complete and the breath fully nourished do heavenly flowers fall in profusion. This is the scene of leaving the womb having come, and one should quickly leave the womb. The heavenly flowers falling in profusion are what you see in your eyes; the snowflakes whirling are because the embryo is full. The Chan master Jiwu said: “The embryo complete, the season come, snowflakes fly; a thought stirs, drifting into the air, up at the mechanism of the crown; do not call the Tathāgata’s way a withered stillness — the Dharma-body comes out of stillness and returns again to take refuge.” (When the embryo is complete and the season has come, snowflakes whirl; a thought stirs, and it drifts into the air, out through the crown; do not say the Tathāgata’s way is withered stillness — the Dharma-body comes out of quiet absorption and returns again to take refuge.) The Book of Wisdom and Life by Patriarch Huayang (Liu Huayang), in its “Collected Sayings,” says: “This is the time to come out of absorption; if one should come out and does not, one stays stuck in the Dharma-body, bound by absorption, unable to have spiritual powers, unable to transform into hundreds of billions of transformation bodies.” “The embryo complete, the season come” is the Embryo of the Way complete to the utmost. Seeing the snowflakes, one leaves the ordinary body; a thought stirs, and it goes toward the great void. Not knowing this moment is because one has not obtained a teacher(‘s true transmission).

Figure 33 · Inner Nature and Life United

Two intersecting circles, one above the other: the upper circle is inscribed “inner nature,” the lower circle “life,” and the part where the two circles overlap “embryo” — when the true spirit of inner nature and the true spirit of life unite their two lights into one, the Embryo of the Way is born.

§6
Original (Chinese)

问曰：弟子看华阳祖《慧命经》云：念动向太空，不知此机，是未得师传也。《慧命经》未传出胎口诀，乞师传出。

答曰：尔将灵光上迁泥丸宫，由泥丸宫炼出慧光，慧光内发现雪花飘飞，此是真空炼形，虽曰有作，其实无为。虽曰炼形，其实炼神。是修外而兼修内也。古仙云：“形以道全，命以术延”。此术是窃无涯之元炁，续有限之形躯。无涯之元炁，是天地阴阳所生真精，炼成有形之道胎，是神与神交所生，是一人身内灵父灵母之炁，即是真阴真阳和合而成。后天凡父凡母生有形之胎，得十个月胎足，降生有形之小孩，也是凡父凡母之炁所成。道胎是一人身内阴阳炁合一而成，也得弥历十个月胎足，天花乱坠，速用念动向太空口诀，可出胎。性命二光合一，胎可现有形之我。散则是气，聚则成形。此是先天中先天炁，实则是纯阳之正炁，即是阳神，是五眼六通所成，众人可见。阴神者，闭眼五通所成，是一股阴灵气，众人看不见。若庙门不开，阳神出不来。出胎念动庙门开向太空法诀，乃是真诀。

Question: Your disciple has read in Patriarch Huayang’s Book of Wisdom and Life: “A thought stirs, toward the great void; not knowing this mechanism is not having obtained a teacher’s transmission.” The Book of Wisdom and Life did not transmit the formula for leaving the womb; please, teacher, transmit it.

Answer: You move the numinous light up to the Muddy Pellet palace, and from the Muddy Pellet palace refine the light of wisdom, and within the light of wisdom snowflakes appear whirling — this is “refining form in true emptiness”: though said to be action, it is in fact non-action; though said to be refining form, it is in fact refining spirit; it is cultivating the outer while also cultivating the inner. The ancient immortals said: “Form is kept whole by the Way; life is prolonged by art.” This “art” is stealing the boundless original breath to continue the limited bodily frame. The boundless original breath is the true essence produced by the yin and yang of heaven and earth, refined into the Embryo of the Way with form — born of the meeting of spirit and spirit, the breath of the “numinous father and numinous mother” within one person’s body, that is, formed by the union of true yin and true yang. The ordinary father and ordinary mother of Posterior Heaven produce an embryo with form, which takes ten months to be complete before a child with form is born, also formed from the breath of the ordinary father and ordinary mother. The Embryo of the Way is formed by the yin and yang breaths within one person’s body uniting into one, and also takes ten months to be complete; when heavenly flowers fall in profusion, quickly use the formula of “a thought stirs, toward the great void,” and one can leave the womb. When the two lights of inner nature and life unite into one, the embryo can manifest a “me” with form: dispersed, it is breath; gathered, it takes form. This is the Prior Heaven breath within Prior Heaven, in fact the correct breath of pure yang, that is, the Yang Spirit, formed by the “five eyes and six powers,” and everyone can see it. The yin spirit is formed by the “five powers” with closed eyes; it is a breath of yin numinosity, and people cannot see it. If the “temple gate” (the Gate of Heaven at the crown) does not open, the Yang Spirit cannot come out. Only the formula for leaving the womb — “a thought stirs, the temple gate opens, toward the great void” — is the true formula.

§7
Original (Chinese)

问曰：师言五眼六通，这六通前已言明，何为五眼？

答曰：这五眼是天、地、神、人、鬼。天者是天眼；地者是灵眼；神者是慧眼；人者是明眼；鬼者是透眼。这六通是天眼通、天耳通、宿命通、他心通、漏尽通、神境通。这神境通最要留心，恐其邪魔生出，要会磨魔的口诀，胎可出也。

Question: Teacher, you speak of the “five eyes and six powers”; the six powers have been explained above; what are the five eyes?

Answer: These five eyes are heaven, earth, spirit, human and ghost: heaven is the heavenly eye; earth is the numinous eye; spirit is the wisdom eye; human is the bright eye; ghost is the penetrating eye. The six powers are: the power of the heavenly eye, the power of the heavenly ear, the power of knowing past lives, the power of knowing others’ minds, the power of the exhaustion of leaks, and the power of spiritual realms. This power of spiritual realms calls for the most care, for fear it gives rise to deviant demons; one must know the formula for grinding away demons, and only then can the embryo come out.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§7
Original note (Chinese)

【注释】大药过关服食之后，法轮自转助神结胎，我惟用双目寂照丹田，久之二炁转机渐微，得真胎息，再将胎息由下丹田迁入中丹田（即中宫绛宫），遂成道胎。道胎者又名婴儿、圣胎、阳神等，乃先天真炁所凝成。柳华阳说：“牟尼珠归于中宫，与意两相合一，意在珠中，犹如磁石吸铁一般，故名曰道胎矣”。又说：“神入乎其炁，炁包乎其神，昏昏默默，浑浑沦沦，如母胎一般景象，故曰道胎”。

道胎养在中宫，识死性活，法喜禅悦，真安妙乐，无内无外，浑然一团，自有一番景象。古人描写为：“朗朗兮性如秋月，融融兮命似醉熏。心自内观，舍利熏蒸其骨肉如沐浴，而心性似太空。了达无为兮，安寂六根。静照八识兮，空其五蕴。虽有循环之机，而真性安然无余”。《慧命经》亦曰：“忽然溶溶如谷云，霏霏似春雨，盘旋敛聚于中宫，斯谓之结道胎。盖舍利登中宫之时，周身如云之腾，似雨之施，百脉冲和，畅于四肢”。当此时，按子、卯、午、酉四正位，急将心目左旋右转三十六次而定住，再右旋左转二十四次而定住，则性命盘聚于中宫，结成道胎。

养胎之法虽在中田，必兼下田合化成一虚境，方无滞碍。关于此点，伍冲虚在《仙佛合宗》中解释道：“初行大周天之火，元神虽居中田，却连合下田二炁，以为妙用。必元神寂照于中、下二田，相与浑融，化为一虚空之大境，使二炁助神结胎，故二田皆是落处。若拘守于一田，则神有滞碍，而失大圆镜之智用矣”。又说：“守中之理，中也者非中间之谓，中乃虚空之谓中。守也者非拘守之谓，乃致虚之谓守。守中也者，不着意二田，亦不纵意于二田，即所谓元神寂照二田，成一虚空是也”。可见寂照同用，乃养胎之旨。

十月养胎中，历月自有证验，伍冲虚曰：“初入定时，守定三月，则二炁之动机甚微，但微动于脐轮之虚境而已。若守至四、五月间，则二炁因元神之寂照，以至服食已尽，而皆归定灭；元神因元炁之培育，以致阳明不昧，而得证真空，二炁俱定，食性已绝，独存一寂照之元神，以为胎仙之主矣。更守到六、七月间，不但心不生灭，且昏睡全无。守至八、九月间，寂照已久，百脉俱住。更守至十月，候足纯阳，神归大定，于是定能生慧，自有六通之验矣”。养胎十月之期，亦概而言之，实非止十月，以上历月证验，可视为分几个阶段之证验可也。

古人认为，行功到此，发白再黑，齿落重生，并有五眼六通之验。所谓五眼六通者，即今之透视、遥视、搬运、传感等种种特异功能。

十月养胎乃炼炁化神之喻，只在绵绵密密寂照之功。千峰老人认为，此时只知有慧光，别无他事，谓之灭尽。直到二炁俱无，食脉两绝，才证胎圆。但无论在十月内外，若有一毫昏沉之意，则余阴尚在；若有一毫散乱之念，则神未纯阳。必须守到昏沉尽绝，散乱俱无之时，方得纯阳果满之胎神。神全胎满，再用迁法迁于上田，进于出神之功。

柳华阳《慧命经》云，当近胎圆之际，静定中忽有一轮皓月从丹田升于目前，以真意留而待之，忽又有一轮红日升起与月并合，收于中藏之。后静定习于寂灭，静极生动，又有一物上合于道胎，即有一点纯阳之物从涌泉升于中宫，与道胎和合为一，又自往下行，由尾闾上头顶，降于中宫，而法轮又重转。柳华阳称之为助胎圆之至宝。此种内景，古人名之为“日月合璧”。赵氏于此步功口诀中未提及，在此引以待征。

After the Great Medicine has passed the gates and been ingested, the Dharma Wheel turns by itself and helps the spirit form the embryo; I simply shine quietly on the elixir field with the two eyes; in time the turning of the two breaths grows gradually fainter, and one obtains true embryonic breathing; then the embryonic breathing is moved from the lower elixir field into the middle elixir field (that is, the central palace, the Crimson Palace), and so the Embryo of the Way is formed. The Embryo of the Way is also called the infant, the holy embryo, the Yang Spirit and so on; it is condensed from the Prior Heaven true breath. Liu Huayang says: “The mani pearl returns to the central palace and unites with the intention, the intention within the pearl, like a magnet drawing iron; so it is called the Embryo of the Way.” He also says: “The spirit enters the breath, the breath envelops the spirit, dim and silent, undifferentiated and whole, like the scene of a mother’s womb; so it is called the Embryo of the Way.”

When the Embryo of the Way is nourished in the central palace, the cognitive dies and inner nature lives; there is Dharma-joy and dhyāna-delight, true peace and wondrous bliss, without inside or outside, one undifferentiated mass — a scene of its own. The ancients described it thus: “Clear and bright, inner nature like the autumn moon; warm and harmonious, life like a slight intoxication. The heart turns its gaze inward of itself, the śarīra steams the bones and flesh as if bathing, and heart and nature are like the great void. Understanding non-action, one quiets the six roots; quietly illuminating the eight consciousnesses, one empties the five aggregates. Though there is still the mechanism of cycling, true inner nature is at peace, with nothing left over.” The Book of Wisdom and Life also says: “Suddenly it is soft and flowing like clouds in a mountain valley, fine and drizzling like spring rain, circling, gathering and collecting in the central palace — this is called forming the Embryo of the Way. For when the śarīra ascends to the central palace, the whole body is like clouds rising, like rain falling, the hundred vessels in harmony, flowing through the four limbs.” At this time, by the four cardinal positions zi, mao, wu and you, quickly turn the heart and eyes left and right thirty-six times and settle; then right and left twenty-four times and settle; inner nature and life coil and gather in the central palace and form the Embryo of the Way.

Although the method of nourishing the embryo is in the middle elixir field, it must be joined with the lower elixir field and transformed together into one realm of emptiness, and only then is there no obstruction. On this point Wu Chongxu explains in The Common Origin of Immortals and Buddhas: “When the fire of the Greater Celestial Circuit is first moved, although the Original Spirit dwells in the middle field, it joins with the two breaths of the lower field for its wondrous function. The Original Spirit must quietly illuminate the middle and lower fields, merging them, transforming them into one great realm of empty space, so that the two breaths help the spirit form the embryo; so both fields are footholds. If one clings to one field, the spirit is obstructed and loses the function of the ‘great perfect mirror wisdom.'” He also says: “The principle of guarding the center: ‘center’ does not mean the middle; empty space is called the center; ‘guarding’ does not mean clinging; reaching emptiness is called guarding. What is called guarding the center is neither fixing the intention on the two fields nor letting the intention wander away from the two fields — that is what is meant by the Original Spirit quietly illuminating the two fields and becoming one vast emptiness.” We can see that using stillness and illumination together at the same time is the very aim of nourishing the embryo.

In the ten months of nourishing the embryo, each month has its own proofs. Wu Chongxu says: “When one first enters absorption, after guarding for three months the stirring of the two breaths is very faint, only a slight stirring in the realm of emptiness of the navel wheel. If one guards for four or five months, the two breaths, because of the quiet illumination of the Original Spirit, and with eating completely ended, all return to absorption and extinction; the Original Spirit, because of the nurturing of the original breath, becomes yang, bright and unobscured, and realizes true emptiness; the two breaths are both settled, the nature of eating is cut off, and there remains only one quietly illuminating Original Spirit as the master of the embryo-immortal. Guarding further for six or seven months, not only does the heart neither arise nor cease, but drowsiness and sleep are entirely gone. Guarding for eight or nine months, quiet illumination having lasted long, the hundred vessels all stop. Guarding further to ten months, awaiting the fullness of pure yang, the spirit returns to great absorption, and so absorption gives rise to wisdom, and naturally there are the proofs of the six powers.” The period of ten months of nourishing the embryo is also approximate; in fact it is more than ten months; the month-by-month proofs above may be regarded as proofs of several stages.

The ancients held that when practice reaches this point, white hair turns black again, fallen teeth grow again, and there are the proofs of the “five eyes and six powers.” The so-called five eyes and six powers are what are now called all kinds of “extraordinary powers” such as seeing through objects, remote viewing, moving objects and telepathy.

“Ten months of nourishing the embryo” is a metaphor for refining breath into spirit, and lies solely in the soft, continuous, close work of quiet illumination. The Old Man of Qianfeng holds that at this time one knows only the light of wisdom and nothing else, which is called “extinction”; only when the two breaths are both gone and both food and pulse are cut off is the completion of the embryo realized. But whether within the ten months or beyond, if there is the slightest drowsiness, it shows that remaining yin is still present; if there is the slightest scattered thought, it shows that the spirit is not yet pure yang. One must guard until drowsiness is entirely gone and scattering completely absent, and only then can one obtain the embryo-spirit of pure yang with its fruit complete. When the spirit is whole and the embryo full, one again uses the method of moving to move it to the upper elixir field, and enters the work of sending out the spirit.

Liu Huayang’s Book of Wisdom and Life says that when the embryo is nearing completion, in the midst of stillness and absorption a bright moon suddenly rises from the elixir field to before the eyes; one keeps it and waits for it with the True Intention; then suddenly a red sun rises, joins with the moon, and is drawn into the central palace and stored. Later, in stillness and absorption, accustomed to quiescent extinction, when stillness reaches its utmost and gives rise to movement, something else rises to unite with the Embryo of the Way — that is, a point of something of pure yang rises from the Bubbling Spring to the central palace and unites with the Embryo of the Way into one, then goes downward by itself, up by the Caudal Funnel to the crown, down to the central palace, and the Dharma Wheel turns again. Liu Huayang calls it “the supreme treasure that helps the embryo to completion.” This inner scene the ancients called “sun and moon joined like a jade disc.” Zhao does not mention it in the formula of this step; it is cited here to await verification.

Key Terms

  • The two breaths help the spirit form the embryo: after passing the gates and ingesting, the breath of inner nature and the breath of life naturally rise and descend along the road of the three passes, helping the Original Spirit form the Embryo of the Way.
  • True embryonic breathing: the true breath at the navel wheel no longer stirs, no breath comes out of the nose, and there remains only the quietly illuminating Original Spirit.
  • Three moves: refining essence into breath in the lower elixir field, moving to the middle elixir field (the Crimson Palace) to refine breath into spirit and nourish the Embryo of the Way, then moving to the upper elixir field (the Muddy Pellet) to refine the spirit and leave the womb.
  • Embryo of the Way: the embryo formed when the true breath of the central palace envelops the embryo-spirit; also called the infant, the holy embryo, the Yang Spirit.
  • Four dhyānas: in the first thought stops, in the second the breath stops, in the third the pulse stops, in the fourth everything is extinguished.
  • Extinction: all affairs extinguished, knowing only the light of wisdom and nothing else.
  • Six powers: the power of the exhaustion of leaks, of the heavenly eye, of the heavenly ear, of knowing past lives, of knowing others’ minds, of spiritual realms.
  • Five eyes: the heavenly eye, the numinous eye (earth), the wisdom eye (spirit), the bright eye (human), the penetrating eye (ghost).
  • Power of spiritual realms: the one of the six powers that calls for the most care; Zhao says the cognitive spirit is at work in it, and liking to tell people their fortunes is being bound by the cognitive spirit.
  • The cognitive dies and inner nature lives: the cognitive spirit dies and true inner nature comes alive.
  • Nursing: the Yang Spirit, newly born like an infant, preserved and nourished by quietly illuminating the upper elixir field, “empty yet not empty.”
  • Snowflakes drifting in the air, heavenly flowers falling in profusion: the scene of leaving the womb.
  • Corpse-guarding ghost: having the scene of leaving the womb but not leaving it.
  • Numinous father and numinous mother: the true yin and true yang within the body.
  • Temple gate: the Gate of Heaven at the crown, where the Yang Spirit goes out and comes in.
  • Sun and moon joined like a jade disc: the inner scene described by Liu Huayang of a bright moon and a red sun joining before the embryo is complete (Qiu Ling’s note).

Putting It into Practice

  1. This is a description of states: the ten months of nourishing the embryo, the three moves, the six powers and leaving the womb are records of the highest stage of the elixir path; this site presents them only as a documentary record.
  2. Don’t pursue supernatural powers: Zhao himself says that seeing people’s fortunes within the light of wisdom and going to tell them is being bound by the cognitive spirit — “one becomes a useless person; it would have been better not to cultivate at all.”
  3. See wisdom and do not use it: the sentence most worth remembering in this step — “seeing the light of wisdom, one does not use it, and guards the center firmly.” Whatever you see, do not use it, do not speak of it, do not cling to it.
  4. Stillness and illumination together: Qiu Ling’s note quotes Wu Chongxu: “guarding” is not clinging but reaching emptiness; “center” is not the middle but empty space.
  5. Don’t fast or restrict salt: “food must not contain salt” and “both food and pulse cut off” are ancient accounts and must not be imitated.
