The Sixteen Steps of Method Formulas · 第三步 开通八脉

Step Three · Opening the Eight Vessels

The formula of Step Three, opening the eight vessels — in front it opens the Conception Vessel, behind it reaches the kidneys; across it opens the Belt Vessel, in the middle the Penetrating Vessel

About This Chapter

The first two steps settled the spirit in the Ancestral Opening and identified the tripod and furnace; this step is a supporting practice: using the mind together with the breath to open, one by one, the body’s “eight vessels” (the Eight Extraordinary Vessels), clearing the road for gathering the medicine and forming the elixir later on.

  1. Two sets of eight vessels: Zhao Bichen divides the eight vessels into “the eight vessels through which essence flows” and “the eight vessels through which breath flows.” The first describes the pathways along which essence is produced, transformed, lost and refined (the Governing Vessel rising, the Conception Vessel descending, plus the Belt Vessel, the Penetrating Vessel and others); the second describes the pathways of the true breath.
  2. The common root is the Opening of Life and Death: both sets have their common root in the “Opening of Life and Death” (the perineum), which connects upward to the Muddy Pellet (the crown) and downward to the Bubbling Spring (the sole of the foot).
  3. The ten-breath method of moving the breath: every day at dawn, starting from the Opening of Life and Death, with each inhalation and exhalation you travel in turn the Governing Vessel, Conception Vessel, Belt Vessel, Yang Linking, Yin Linking, Penetrating Vessel, Yang Heel and Yin Heel vessels — ten breaths for one full round.
  4. Relief for numb legs: when the legs and feet go numb during sitting, raise the toes once and push out the heel once, and let the mind travel along the inside and outside of the legs with the breath; the numbness goes away by itself.
  5. Prior Heaven and Posterior Heaven breath: in the womb the Conception and Governing vessels are connected; after birth, when the umbilical cord is cut, the Conception Vessel is “severed” with it — cultivating the Way means joining it up again.

Qiu Ling’s note first quotes Li Shizhen’s Study of the Eight Extraordinary Vessels to make clear where the eight vessels are, and then points out that the method of opening the eight vessels was once a secret never passed on outside the Wu-Liu school; though it is a method of deliberate action, a technique of guiding the breath, it is simple and easy, can be done walking, standing, sitting or lying down, and is a very important supporting practice in elixir work; the use of the Opening of Life and Death (the perineum, the yinqiao) is its key.

Safety note (translator): The original says “close the mouth” (闭口鼻气), but what follows clearly counts one inhalation and one exhalation at a time, so this is not holding the breath; it means closing the mouth, breathing gently through the nose, and placing no intention on mouth or nose. Please do not hold your breath or “push” the breath with force; for the Penetrating Vessel the original specifically says “it must not go beyond the heart,” so the intention goes only as far as below the pit of the heart. People with high blood pressure or heart disease should be especially gentle. If you feel a swelling in the head, tightness in the chest or palpitations, stop at once.

Original Text and Translation

Figure 4 · Opening the Eight Vessels

The figure is the silhouette of a person sitting with knees bent, facing right, hands resting on the knees. A double-lined channel starts below the buttocks (the Opening of Life and Death), rises along the back, curves over the crown, then descends down the front of the body back into the belly — marked beside it “Cavity of Breath”: this is the Governing Vessel rising and the Conception Vessel descending. Several vertical channels in the chest, marked “heart” at the top, are the Penetrating Vessel rising to just below the heart and stopping there. A channel along the arm runs to the hand (the Yang Linking and Yin Linking vessels), a channel on the back (the Belt Vessel branching to the eyes of the waist and the shoulder hollows), and a channel along the leg runs from the Opening of Life and Death through the thigh, knee and calf to the foot (the Yang Heel and Yin Heel vessels).

Formula
Original (Chinese)

前通任脉后通肾，

横通带脉中通冲。

下通阳关上通心，

上前通脐后通肾。

In front it opens the Conception Vessel, behind it reaches the kidneys; across it opens the Belt Vessel, in the middle the Penetrating Vessel. Below it reaches the yang gate (the gate of essence), above it reaches the heart; upward and forward it reaches the navel, backward it reaches the kidneys.

§1
Original (Chinese)

初学之人，乍一参禅打坐，腰腿不能运动，四肢百脉易於壅塞，气血不通，忽感麻木痛苦，此诀可使血液流通，而手脚麻木立即消失。然八脉有通精八脉，有通气八脉之别。先论通精八脉，吾人身中八脉之总根为生死窍，后通尾闾，上夹脊到玉枕，进脑髓入泥丸宫为督脉。自大脑中心之䯕髓，下行一管名曰玄膺穴，穴内有一小岔，通上颚骨，真炁易由此漏，下对气管，名曰十二重楼。过肺脏下通右心血房，至膈膜下名为绛宫，在心下一寸二分处，下至炁穴，再下至睾丸宫，回至生死窍为任脉。此外，横通带脉上通心，下通阳关前通脐，上后通肾中通冲，此是生精化精走精炼精之八脉，此脉一结不能化精，人由此而老矣，比如灯内无油准灭。

When beginners first take up Chan meditation and sitting, the waist and legs cannot move, the hundred vessels of the four limbs easily become congested, the qi and blood do not flow, and suddenly they feel numbness and pain; this formula can make the blood circulate, and the numbness of hands and feet disappears at once. But among the eight vessels there is a distinction between “the eight vessels through which essence flows” and “the eight vessels through which breath flows.” First, the eight vessels of essence: the common root of the eight vessels in our body is the “Opening of Life and Death” (the perineum); from here it runs back to the Caudal Funnel (the tip of the tailbone), up through the Spine-Straddling Pass (the middle of the back, roughly level with the elbows) to the Jade Pillow (the occipital bone at the back of the head), enters the brain marrow, and reaches the Muddy Pellet palace (the center of the crown) — this is the Governing Vessel. From the marrow at the center of the brain (the original uses a rare character here; the sense is simply the brain marrow), a duct runs downward called the “Mysterious Breast point” (the area above the root of the tongue, at the top of the throat); in this point there is a small fork that leads up to the palate, where the true breath easily leaks away; downward it faces the windpipe, called the “Twelve-Storied Tower” (the windpipe and throat, which the ancients said had twelve rings). Passing further through the lungs, it leads down to the right blood chamber of the heart and below the diaphragm to what is called the “Crimson Palace,” one cun two fen below the heart; further down to the Cavity of Breath (the lower elixir field), further down to the “testicle palace,” and back to the Opening of Life and Death — this is the Conception Vessel. In addition, across it opens the Belt Vessel, upward it reaches the heart, downward the yang gate, forward the navel, upward and backward the kidneys, and in the middle the Penetrating Vessel. These are the eight vessels for producing, transforming, losing and refining essence. Once these vessels become knotted and blocked, essence cannot be transformed, and a person grows old because of it — like a lamp that has run out of oil and must go out.

§2
Original (Chinese)

年迈之人，已过六十四岁者通身属阴，不能生精，可按第二步功口诀生精接气之法炼之。化精者，由尾闾往上升，过夹脊，经玉枕，到泥丸，再降下，由玄膺过重楼，到绛宫、真炁穴、名为一周天。如此数次可矣。倘是走精，按照第十步口诀，则夜里不致遗泄。炼精在第六步口诀，有进阳火退阴符法，使阳精皆化为元炁。以上四法，须当格外注意，方能融会贯通，而性命之学，思过半矣！

Old people who have passed sixty-four, whose whole body belongs to yin and can no longer produce essence, may refine themselves according to the method of “producing essence and joining the breath” in the formula of Step Two (see “adding oil to prolong life” in the previous step). “Transforming essence”: (the mind and breath) rise from the Caudal Funnel, pass the Spine-Straddling Pass, go through the Jade Pillow to the Muddy Pellet; then descend again, from the Mysterious Breast through the Storied Tower to the Crimson Palace and the true Cavity of Breath — this is called one Celestial Circuit. Doing this a few times is enough. If it is “losing essence” (leakage), follow the formula of Step Ten, and you will not have nocturnal emissions. “Refining essence” is in the formula of Step Six, with the methods of advancing the yang fire and withdrawing the yin tally, so that all the yang essence is transformed into original breath. The four methods above (producing essence, transforming essence, stopping the loss of essence, refining essence) must be given particular attention before you can understand them thoroughly; then more than half of the learning of inner nature and life is understood!

§3
Original (Chinese)

人身通气，八脉总根在生死窍，上通泥丸，下通涌泉，真气聚散，皆以此窍为转移。此窍可使血脉周流全身贯通，和气上朝，阳长阴消，水中火发，雪里花开，天根月窟闲来往，三十六宫都是春，乃是生炁之根，百姓日用而不知者此也。故每日清晨早起先行八脉之气，闭口鼻气，心意先由生死窍起，一吸由尾闾关升到头中为督脉，二呼由前任脉降至生死窍；三吸由生死窍上升至炁穴为带脉；双分开至背后双腰眼，双上双膀窝定住；四呼由两膀窝双走两肘，外为阳维脉走中指至手心定住；五吸由两手心走阴维脉，双回至胸前定住；六呼由两胸前双降至带脉合归一处回生死窍；七吸由生死窍直升至绛宫定住为冲脉，不可过心，八呼由心下降至生死窍分开，双走两腿外，为阳蹻脉，过脚指到足心为涌泉穴定住；九吸由涌泉穴双回两腿内为阴蹻脉，过生死窍上至真炁穴定住，十呼由真炁穴降至生死窍定住。此八部脉须以气通之，能驱逐一身百窍之阴邪。阴气不除，为结丹之障碍。以上法诀逐日行持，果觉脉中有气蠕动，乃是畅通预兆。若不通此八脉，邪气不驱除，则无法采药，纵采之亦无结丹希望。阅者其勿忽焉。

Next, the body’s “eight vessels through which breath flows”: their common root is the Opening of Life and Death, connecting upward to the Muddy Pellet and downward to the Bubbling Spring (the sole of the foot); the gathering and dispersing of the true breath all turn on this one opening (it is the hinge). This opening makes the blood vessels circulate and the whole body connect throughout; harmonious breath rises to pay court above, yang grows and yin wanes, “fire arises in the water, flowers bloom in the snow” (yang breath is born in the midst of yin cold), “the Root of Heaven and the Moon Cave come and go at leisure, and all thirty-six palaces are spring” (lines by Shao Yong: the yang breath moves unhurried between the lower elixir field and the crown, and all the organs and joints of the body are full of spring; see the last chapter of Book Three) — it is the root of the life breath, the very thing ordinary people use every day without knowing it. So every morning, rise early and first move the breath through the eight vessels. Close the mouth (place no intention on mouth or nose), and let the mind begin from the Opening of Life and Death:

  • First, inhale: from the Caudal Funnel pass up into the head — this is the Governing Vessel;
  • Second, exhale: down the Conception Vessel in front to the Opening of Life and Death;
  • Third, inhale: from the Opening of Life and Death up to the Cavity of Breath — this is the Belt Vessel; then split into left and right, to the two eyes of the waist on the back (the hollows on either side of the lower back), and on up to the two “shoulder hollows” (around the armpits and the hollows of the shoulders), and stop;
  • Fourth, exhale: from the two shoulder hollows go in two paths to the two elbows, along the outside of the arms — this is the Yang Linking Vessel — through the middle fingers, to the centers of the palms (the Laogong point), and stop;
  • Fifth, inhale: from the two palm centers travel the Yin Linking Vessel (the inside of the arms), in two paths back to the front of the chest, and stop;
  • Sixth, exhale: from the front of the chest, the two paths descend to the Belt Vessel, join into one, and return to the Opening of Life and Death;
  • Seventh, inhale: from the Opening of Life and Death rise straight to the Crimson Palace (below the heart) and stop — this is the Penetrating Vessel, it must not go beyond the heart;
  • Eighth, exhale: from below the heart descend to the Opening of Life and Death, divide into two paths along the outside of the legs — this is the Yang Heel Vessel — past the toes, to the Bubbling Spring point in the sole of the foot, and stop;
  • Ninth, inhale: from the Bubbling Spring points, in two paths along the inside of the legs back up — this is the Yin Heel Vessel — past the Opening of Life and Death, up to the true Cavity of Breath, and stop;
  • Tenth, exhale: from the true Cavity of Breath descend to the Opening of Life and Death and stop.

These eight vessels must be opened with the breath; this can drive out the yin pathogens from all the hundred openings of the body. If the yin breath is not removed, it is an obstacle to forming the elixir. Practice the formula above every day; if you feel breath wriggling in the vessels, that is a sign they are opening. If these eight vessels are not opened and the pathogenic breath is not driven out, the medicine cannot be gathered; and even if it is gathered, there is no hope of forming the elixir. Readers must on no account neglect this.

§4
Original (Chinese)

初打坐时，腿脚麻木，乃因平日不惯於久坐。静坐时间每欲延长，辄痛楚难忍，是腿之内外气血管，因受压力而闭塞，气血见阻不得畅流，故麻木立生。斯时可闭口鼻，脚尖向上一扬，脚跟向下一蹬，并吸气，心意由涌泉双上阴蹻脉至炁穴，又呼气，心意由炁穴双走阳蹻脉至涌泉为止，如此数回，麻木自无矣。

The numbness of legs and feet when you first begin sitting comes from not being used to sitting for long. Each time you try to lengthen your sitting, the pain becomes unbearable — this is because the qi and blood vessels inside and outside the legs are compressed and blocked, so the qi and blood are obstructed and cannot flow freely, and numbness sets in at once. At such a time you can close the mouth (breathe through the nose), raise the toes once and push the heel down and out once, while inhaling, with the mind going from the Bubbling Spring up the Yin Heel vessels along the inside of the legs to the Cavity of Breath; then exhale, with the mind going from the Cavity of Breath down the Yang Heel vessels along the outside of the legs to the Bubbling Spring, and stop. Do this a few times, and the numbness naturally disappears.

§5
Original (Chinese)

气由口鼻出入者，均是后天吸呼气也。有之则生，无之则死。小孩在衣胞时，不能直接吸呼天地之气，母吸随母吸，母呼随母呼，先天祖炁在任督二脉后升前降，轮转周流，一身气血全通。及降生后，脐带剪断，祖气不能自转，任脉从此亦断，性命从此分开。性归心发於二日，命归肾发於淫根。《回光集》云：“千年铁树开花易，一失人身再复难”趁此身在世，速访明师，求指性命双修真功，将剪断任脉接上，性命和合归一，返到父母未生前，圆圆陀陀金光献，方保性命长久住。

The breath that goes in and out through mouth and nose is all Posterior Heaven breathing. With it we live; without it we die. While a child is in the placenta (in the mother’s womb), it cannot breathe the breath of heaven and earth directly: when the mother inhales, it inhales with her; when the mother exhales, it exhales with her. The Prior Heaven ancestral breath rises behind and descends in front in the Conception and Governing vessels, turning and circulating, and the qi and blood of the whole body are all connected. Once it is born and the umbilical cord is cut, the ancestral breath can no longer turn by itself, the Conception Vessel is broken from then on, and “inner nature” and “life” separate: inner nature returns to the heart and issues from the two eyes (the original has “two suns,” evidently a mistake for “two eyes”; see the Translator’s Notes); life returns to the kidneys and issues from the reproductive organ. The Collection on Turning the Light Around says: “For a thousand-year iron tree to bloom is easy; once a human body is lost, it is hard to obtain one again.” While this body is still in the world, hurry to seek out an enlightened teacher and ask him to point out the true practice of the dual cultivation of nature and life, to join up the severed Conception Vessel and bring nature and life together into one, returning to the state “before father and mother were born” (Prior Heaven); the perfectly round golden light appears, and only then can life be preserved long and unharmed.

§6
Original (Chinese)

刘云普老师曰：“保命立身是任督，却病延年通维蹻”。刘名瑞老师曰：“尻督一开通属阳，法轮长转身体康”。了空老师曰：“走精走气八脉分，阴阳维蹻仔细行”。了然老师曰：“师授之后亲自用，八脉一开阳气升”。柳华阳祖师云；“亲自在脉中进行过，数百回方得成就。”伍冲虚祖师云：“用后天之真吸呼，寻找真人吸呼处。”

(The following are words of the teachers.) Teacher Liu Yunpu said: “Preserving life and establishing the body rest on the Conception and Governing vessels; to drive out illness and lengthen your years, open the linking and heel vessels.” Teacher Liu Mingrui said: “Once the sacrum and the Governing Vessel open, the whole body is open and all is yang; when the Dharma Wheel turns constantly, the body is healthy.” Teacher Liaokong said: “Distinguish the eight vessels of essence from the eight vessels of breath; travel the Yin Linking, Yang Linking, Yin Heel and Yang Heel carefully.” Teacher Liaoran said: “After the teacher transmits it, use it yourself; once the eight vessels open, the yang breath rises.” Patriarch Liu Huayang said: “I traveled the vessels myself, and only after several hundred times did I succeed.” Patriarch Wu Chongxu said: “Use the true Posterior Heaven breathing to find ‘the place where the true person breathes’ (the seat of Prior Heaven embryonic breathing).”

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§6
Original note (Chinese)

【注释】奇经八脉即阳维脉、阴维脉、阳跻脉、阴跻脉、冲脉、任脉、督脉、带脉。此八脉内温脏腑，外濡腠理，对人体健康有十分重要的作用。李时珍《奇经八脉考》云：“阳维起于诸阳之会，由外踝而上行于卫分，所以为一身之纲维也。阳跻起于跟中，循外踝上行于身之左右，所以使机关之跻捷也。督脉起于会阴，循背而行于身之后，为阳脉之总督，故曰阳脉之海。任脉起于会阴，循腹而行于身之前，为阴脉之承任，故曰阴脉之海。冲脉起于会阴，夹脐而行且冲于上，为诸脉之冲要，故曰十二经脉之海。带脉则横围于腰，状如束带，所以总约诸脉者也。”

开通八脉法过去是道家伍柳派不传之秘。此法虽是有为法，导引之术，但对疏通周身气血有很大作用，是丹功中重要的补助法。盖气血不通畅，不但静坐时手脚麻木，不耐久坐，而且妨碍丹功按程序正常进行。练好此法，当能使功程突飞猛进，事半功倍。

本法简单易行，练得纯熟，无论行、住、坐、卧均可行之。功后往往感到全身异常舒适轻快。八脉总根在生死窍，故运行路线多以此窍为转移。生死窍指阴跻，即会阴穴，在尾闾前阴囊下，两阴之间。张紫阳《八脉经》云：“阴跻一脉，散在丹经其名颇多，曰天根，曰死户，曰复命关，曰酆都鬼，曰生死根，有神主之名曰桃康，上通泥丸，下透涌泉，倘能知此，使真气聚散皆从此关窍，则天门常开，地户永闭，尻脉周流于一身，贯通上下，和炁自然上朝，阳长阴消，水中火发，雪里花开，所谓天根月窟闲来往，三十六宫都是春。得之者，身体轻健，容衰返壮，昏昏默默，如醉如痴，此其验也。”可见善用此窍之重要。防止漏精走精，炼精化炁使已举之阳物缩回，均用到此窍。柳华阳《慧命经》云：“当呼吸之机，我则从阴跻迎归炉，即达摩所谓采取也。或十迎，或数十迎，外形倒则止矣。”这是说，从会阴将炁迎归下田，既是采取，又可使阳物缩回。清代道门西派祖师李涵虚功法亦以运用此窍寻气及与下丹田联合动作为其特点。

此功行气路线与起点终点，应用了会阴、下丹田、双劳宫、双涌泉、双腰眼、两肩井、泥丸等重要穴位，足以疏通八脉及周身气血，更配合呼吸，其效益彰。

The Eight Extraordinary Vessels are the Yang Linking, Yin Linking, Yang Heel, Yin Heel, Penetrating, Conception, Governing and Belt vessels. Within, these eight vessels warm and nourish the organs; without, they moisten the skin and the grain of the flesh; they are very important for human health. Li Shizhen’s Study of the Eight Extraordinary Vessels says: “The Yang Linking Vessel begins where all the yang channels meet, rises from the outer ankle and runs in the defensive layer (the surface of the body), and so it is the ‘net and binding’ (the main rope that holds things together) of the whole body. The Yang Heel Vessel begins in the heel, rises along the outer ankle and runs along the left and right sides of the body, and so makes the joints move nimbly. The Governing Vessel begins at the perineum and runs up the back of the body; it governs all the yang vessels and so is called ‘the sea of the yang vessels.' The Conception Vessel begins at the perineum and runs up the front of the body along the belly; it bears all the yin vessels and so is called ‘the sea of the yin vessels.' The Penetrating Vessel begins at the perineum, rises on both sides of the navel and surges upward; it is the thoroughfare of all the vessels and so is called ‘the sea of the twelve channels.' The Belt Vessel circles the waist horizontally, shaped like a sash, and so it can bind all the vessels together.”

The method of opening the eight vessels was once a secret never passed on outside the Daoist Wu-Liu school. Although this method is a “method of deliberate action,” a technique of guiding the breath, it does a great deal to clear the qi and blood of the whole body and is an important supporting method in elixir work. For when qi and blood do not flow freely, not only do the hands and feet go numb in sitting so that you cannot sit long, but it also hinders elixir work from proceeding normally step by step. Mastering this method makes progress leap forward, achieving twice the result with half the effort.

The method is simple and easy; once it is practiced until fluent, it can be done walking, standing, sitting or lying down. After practicing, one often feels unusually comfortable and light throughout the body. The common root of the eight vessels is the Opening of Life and Death, so the route mostly turns at this opening. The Opening of Life and Death refers to the yinqiao, that is, the Huiyin point (the perineum), in front of the Caudal Funnel and below the scrotum, between the two lower orifices. Zhang Ziyang’s Classic of the Eight Vessels says: “This one vessel, the yinqiao, appears scattered through the elixir classics under many names: it is called the ‘Root of Heaven,' the ‘Door of Death,' the ‘Pass of Returning to Life,' the ‘Ghost of Fengdu’ (Fengdu being the legendary underworld), the ‘Root of Life and Death’; it has the name of a ‘presiding spirit,' called Taokang. It connects upward to the Muddy Pellet and downward through to the Bubbling Spring. If you can understand this, and let the gathering and dispersing of the true breath all go in and out through this pass, then the ‘Gate of Heaven’ (the crown) is always open and the ‘Door of Earth’ (the lower opening at the perineum) is forever closed; the sacral vessel circulates through the whole body, connecting above and below, harmonious breath naturally rises to pay court, yang grows and yin wanes, fire arises in the water, flowers bloom in the snow — what is called ‘the Root of Heaven and the Moon Cave come and go at leisure, and all thirty-six palaces are spring.' One who obtains it has a light and healthy body, the face turns from old age back to vigor, dim and silent, as if drunk, as if entranced — this is its proof.” This shows how important it is to make good use of this opening. Preventing leakage and loss of essence, as well as refining essence into breath and making an erect member retract, all require this opening. Liu Huayang’s Book of Wisdom and Life says: “At the moment of an exhalation and an inhalation, I welcome it (the original essence and original breath) back into the furnace from the yinqiao — this is what Bodhidharma called ‘gathering.' Welcome it ten times, or several tens of times, and stop when the outer form (the member) goes down.” This means that welcoming the breath back from the perineum to the lower elixir field is both gathering and a way of making the member retract. Li Hanxu, the patriarch of the Western School of the Daoist tradition in the Qing dynasty, also had a method characterized by using this opening to seek the breath and working it together with the lower elixir field.

The route of this practice, with its starting and ending points, uses important points such as the perineum, the lower elixir field, the two Laogong (palm centers), the two Bubbling Springs (soles), the two eyes of the waist, the two Jianjing (on the shoulders) and the Muddy Pellet, and is enough to clear the eight vessels and the qi and blood of the whole body; combined with the breath, the effect is even clearer.

Key Terms

  • Eight Extraordinary Vessels: the eight vessels Governing, Conception, Penetrating, Belt, Yang Linking, Yin Linking, Yang Heel and Yin Heel, which do not belong to the twelve regular channels and serve to “regulate, store and overflow.”
  • The eight vessels of essence, the eight vessels of breath: Zhao Bichen’s division; the first describes the pathways along which essence is produced, transformed, lost and refined, the second the pathways of the true breath.
  • Opening of Life and Death: the perineum, between the two lower orifices; Qiu Ling’s note says it is the yinqiao, the common root of the eight vessels.
  • Mysterious Breast: the area above the root of the tongue, at the top of the throat.
  • Twelve-Storied Tower: the windpipe and throat.
  • Crimson Palace: one cun two fen below the heart (around the middle elixir field).
  • Testicle palace: Zhao Bichen’s term for the scrotal area through which the Conception Vessel passes on its way down.
  • Eyes of the waist: the hollows on either side of the lower back.
  • Shoulder hollows: around the armpits and the hollows of the shoulders; Qiu Ling’s note matches them to Jianjing.
  • Laogong: the center of the palm; Bubbling Spring: the sole of the foot.
  • Method of deliberate action: a practice done actively with the mind and breath, as opposed to the “non-action” of pure stillness practice.
  • Wu-Liu school: the elixir lineage of Wu Chongxu and Liu Huayang, late Ming and early Qing.
  • Western School: the elixir lineage transmitted by Li Hanxu in the Qing dynasty.

Putting It into Practice

  1. This is a supporting practice: its purpose is to clear qi and blood and relieve the numbness of long sitting, laying a foundation for still practice; it is not the elixir work itself.
  2. Ten breaths for one round: Governing → Conception → Belt (to the eyes of the waist and shoulder hollows) → Yang Linking (to the palms) → Yin Linking (back to the chest) → down to the Opening of Life and Death → Penetrating (to below the heart) → Yang Heel (to the soles) → Yin Heel (back to the Cavity of Breath) → back to the Opening of Life and Death. Beginners can start with only the first two steps (Governing rising, Conception descending) and add the rest once they are familiar.
  3. Gentleness first: breathe through the nose, naturally and gently, without holding the breath; wherever the mind “travels,” just lightly attend to that place, without pushing.
  4. The Penetrating Vessel does not go past the heart: the seventh inhalation stops just below the pit of the heart (the Crimson Palace); this is a clear prohibition in the original.
  5. A small method for numb legs: raise the toes, push out the heel; on the inhalation the mind goes from the sole up the inside of the leg to the lower belly, on the exhalation from the lower belly down the outside of the leg to the sole — simple and safe, usable at any time during sitting.
  6. Don’t rush for results: the original says “if you feel breath wriggling in the vessels, that is a sign they are opening” — if it comes, it comes; if not, don’t chase the sensation.

Translator's Notes

  1. In the original, this step has one formula, one figure and five passages of explanation, with a final passage collecting the words of teachers; we follow the original’s divisions and number them §1–§6.
  2. The last two lines of the formula, “below it reaches the yang gate, above it reaches the heart; upward and forward it reaches the navel, backward it reaches the kidneys,” are worded differently from the text of §1, “across it opens the Belt Vessel, upward it reaches the heart, downward the yang gate, forward the navel, upward and backward the kidneys, in the middle the Penetrating Vessel”; and “behind it reaches the kidneys” in the first line repeats the fourth line. This is as in the original and has not been changed.
  3. “Brain marrow” (䯕髓): the original uses a rare character (䯕, the bone radical with 廷), kept as is; the sense is simply the brain marrow.
  4. “Inner nature returns to the heart and issues from the two suns” (性归心发於二日): “two suns” (二日) should be a mistake for “two eyes” (二目) (the text of Step Two has “inner nature is the heart, and issues from the two eyes”); the translation renders it as “two eyes.”
  5. “Close the mouth” (闭口鼻气): judging from the count of inhalations and exhalations that follows, this does not mean holding the breath, but closing the mouth and placing no intention on mouth or nose; this is this site’s understanding, offered for reference.
  6. 蹻, 跻 and 跷 (qiao): Zhao Bichen’s text writes 蹻; Qiu Ling’s note (including his quotations from Li Shizhen, Zhang Ziyang and Liu Huayang) is printed with 跻, evidently a mistake for 跷 (the simplified form of 蹻). The original text is kept as printed; the translation reads them all as qiao.
  7. “Patriarch Liu Huayang said;” (柳华阳祖师云；): the original has a semicolon before the quotation; kept as is.
  8. Zhao Bichen’s statement that the Conception Vessel “passes through the lungs and leads down to the right blood chamber of the heart” is his own account, drawing on the Western anatomy of his day, and differs from traditional channel theory; it is translated according to his meaning.
  9. “The Root of Heaven and the Moon Cave come and go at leisure, and all thirty-six palaces are spring” comes from a poem by Shao Yong of the Northern Song; for a detailed explanation see the last chapter of Book Three, “Explaining Master Shao’s Poem on the Cave of the Moon and the Root of Heaven.”
  10. In the “Ghost of Fengdu” (酆都鬼) from Zhang Ziyang’s Classic of the Eight Vessels quoted in Qiu Ling’s note, the character in the original looks like 鄷; we write it in the usual form 酆.
