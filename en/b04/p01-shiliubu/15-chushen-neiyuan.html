The Sixteen Steps of Method Formulas · 第十五步 出神内院

Step Fifteen · Sending Out the Spirit from the Inner Court

The formula of Step Fifteen, sending out the spirit from the inner court — wondrous beyond fathoming, the inner eye opens; the light of wisdom shines through the universe

About This Chapter

Qiu Ling says that the work of leaving the womb (sending out the spirit) “is the highest ideal of Daoist practice.” This step describes how, once the Embryo of the Way is complete, the Yang Spirit comes out from the crown, and how after it comes out it is “nursed” and guarded against demons.

  1. The formula for leaving the womb: when the embryo is complete and full and snowflakes whirl, use “a thought stirs, toward the great void; sun and moon open, the temple gate opens; pushing feeling to join inner nature, it turns; the two lights meet and appear.” Zhao says the immortal teachers of earlier generations never dared to write it in a book, while he, “receiving the command of my teacher and of heaven, reveals it plainly in a book.”
  2. The five breaths returning to the origin and the six syllables: the five breaths of heart, spleen, lungs, liver and kidneys turn and unite into one (gathering the five breaths with the five syllables om ma ni pad me), rising from the Caudal Funnel through the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow to the Muddy Pellet, coming out of the Ancestral Opening and striking Baihui; one thinks the syllable “hum,” opens the “temple gate” (the crown) and looks upward (Figure 35).
  3. The scene of sending out the spirit: a mass of golden light as large as a cartwheel appears at the crown, with the Yang Spirit within the golden light; the yin breath of one’s own body turns into “heavenly demons and heterodox ways,” a hundred scenes luring the Yang Spirit — if the heart is moved even slightly by a sound or sight, the Yang Spirit “goes and does not return,” and people think one has died while seated.
  4. The method of nursing: one must guard the golden light with a single intention and grind the demons away with the gathering-in method of Step Seven; when the golden light shrinks, draw it back to its home palace with one turn and one inhalation. When first sending out the Yang Spirit, choose a clear, bright day, “protecting it like a small child,” sending it out once every seven days, gradually more often after three months; when three years of nursing are complete, go on to the work of nine years of returning to emptiness, facing the wall.
  5. The words of the teachers: Liaokong, Liaoran, Wuchan, Liu Mingrui, Peng Maochang, Zhao Kuiyi and others all say the same thing: “do not be alarmed,” “do not cling,” “turn back quickly.”

Qiu Ling’s note stresses that in nursing, “returning to emptiness” is the urgent task, and that going out and drawing back must be done with great care; “for the method of leaving the womb, the Old Man of Qianfeng spares no effort in repeatedly quoting the true transmission of his teachers and patriarchs; if students further check it against The Immortal Lineage of Wu and Liu, especially the Book of Wisdom and Life within it, they may gain some insight.”

Safety note (translator): “The Yang Spirit leaving the body,” “leaving the form body,” “dying while seated” and the like are traditional descriptions of the highest state in the elixir path; they cannot and should not be taken as goals of practice. If in quiet sitting you have experiences such as “the soul leaving the body,” “seeing another self” or “hearing a voice calling,” or you feel fear or cannot tell what is real, stop practicing at once, tell your family, and seek help from a doctor or mental health professional. Zhao himself says again and again that at this point “if the heart is moved even slightly by a sound or sight,” things go wrong — “truly pitiful.”

Original Text and Translation

Figure 34 · Sending Out the Spirit

In the figure a Daoist sits upright cross-legged; a wisp of breath rises from the crown, bearing up a cloud; on the cloud sits a small cross-legged figure — the Yang Spirit. Both ends of the cloud curl up into flame-shaped cloud heads.

Formula
Original (Chinese)

神妙莫测内眼开，

慧光照彻宇宙间。

万法归一躯不坏，

作个法身不死人。

Wondrous beyond fathoming, the inner eye opens; the light of wisdom shines through the universe. The ten thousand methods return to one, the body does not decay; one becomes a person whose Dharma-body does not die.

§1
Original (Chinese)

前者十四步功是三迁之法诀，精炁神养胎之法，即是空而又空，虚而又虚，性如虚空，不著虚空相，故曰真虚空；若著虚空相，即有个虚空在，而为虚空所碍，则不为真虚空。真虚空者，乃自然而然，非有然而然，此即是顿法内性命合一之法也。非是破身顿法，闭目枯坐为禅机。夫胎中定力，在乎一念之诚，弥历十月之胎，必要念念在胎，非是口心之念，乃念住息定之定念，而后胎圆足满，自有雪花飘飞之景到，速当出胎，急用“念动向太空，日月庙门开，推情合性转，二光相迁献”出胎口诀。此诀古人隐而不露，或是怕泄漏天机，也许未得此口诀有之，故而不传。

The practice of Step Fourteen above is the formula of the three moves, the method of nourishing the embryo with essence, breath and spirit, that is, empty and again empty, void and again void, inner nature like empty space yet not clinging to the appearance of empty space, and so called “true empty space”; if one clings to the appearance of empty space, there is an “empty space” there, and one is obstructed by empty space, and it is not true empty space. True empty space is what is so of itself, not “made so by intention” — this is the method of uniting inner nature and life within the “sudden method”; it is not those whose virginity is broken using the sudden method, sitting dry with eyes closed and taking that for the mechanism of Chan. The power of absorption within the embryo lies in the sincerity of a single thought: through the ten months of the embryo, every thought must be on the embryo — not thought in the mouth or heart, but the settled thought of “thought stopped, breath settled”; then the embryo is complete and full, and naturally the scene of snowflakes whirling comes; one should quickly leave the womb, using at once the formula for leaving the womb: “a thought stirs, toward the great void; sun and moon open, the temple gate opens; pushing feeling to join inner nature, it turns; the two lights meet and appear.” (As a thought stirs, it goes toward the great void; as sun and moon — the two eyes — open, the temple gate — the crown — opens too; feeling is pushed to join inner nature and turn; the two lights meet — the original has 迁 — and appear.) The ancients kept this formula hidden and did not reveal it, perhaps for fear of leaking the mechanism of heaven, and perhaps also because some had not obtained the formula, and so it was not transmitted.

§2
Original (Chinese)

问曰：此出胎口诀，乞师明白详剖传出。

答曰：前派仙师秘密口传心授，不敢明泄于书，余奉师天命，明泄于书。当初实有出胎化身真口诀，此口诀不易得之。昔日有兰养素，胎神足满，天花乱坠，无师传授真诀，胎不能出。后有刘海蟾祖师假李玉溪以十韵寄之曰：“功成须是出神景，内院繁华勿累身，会取五仙超脱法，养成胎质离凡尘”。兰养素得诀之后，抚掌大笑而胎出。按《慧命经》柳华阳祖师云：“见雪花离凡体，而念动向太空”。不知此机，是未得师也。余师了然、了空授我时云：“攒簇五形炁归元，二炁合一胎身显。”余胞兄魁一子曰：“三花聚顶月华荣，五炁朝元金光显，二炁和合归元体，正中现出我法身。”以上口诀，通是出胎口诀，言语不一样，理是一也。法诀曰：胎圆雪花纷飞，是眼见也，以心、肝、脾、肺、肾五炁气聚于顶上，冲出祖窍，上顶门而向太空。其理是，攒五簇四，会三合二，而归一也。

Question: Please, teacher, analyze this formula for leaving the womb clearly and in detail and transmit it.

Answer: The immortal teachers of earlier generations all transmitted it secretly, by mouth and heart, and did not dare to write it plainly in a book; receiving the command of my teacher and of heaven, I write it plainly in a book. There was indeed from the beginning a true formula for leaving the womb and transforming the body, and this formula is not easy to obtain. Long ago there was a Lan Yangsu whose embryo-spirit was full and complete, with heavenly flowers falling in profusion, but without a teacher to transmit the true formula, the embryo could not come out. Later Patriarch Liu Haichan, through the hand of Li Yuxi, sent him a poem of ten rhymes, which said: “When the work is done, there must be the scene of sending out the spirit; let not the splendors of the inner court weigh down the body; grasp the five immortals’ method of transcendence, nourish the substance of the embryo and leave the dust of the world.” (When the work is done, there must be the scene of sending out the spirit; do not let the splendors of the inner court — within the body — weigh the body down; grasp the method of transcendence of the five immortals, nourish the substance of the embryo and leave the ordinary world.) When Lan Yangsu obtained the formula, he clapped his hands and laughed aloud, and the embryo came out. According to Patriarch Liu Huayang in the Book of Wisdom and Life: “Seeing the snowflakes, leave the ordinary body, and a thought stirs toward the great void.” Not knowing this moment is not having obtained a teacher. When my teachers Liaoran and Liaokong transmitted it to me, they said: “Gather the five forms, the breath returns to the origin; the two breaths unite into one, and the embryo-body appears.” My elder brother Kuiyizi said: “The three flowers gather at the summit, the moonbeams flourish; the five breaths return to the origin, the golden light appears; the two breaths unite and return to the original body; at the very center appears my Dharma-body.” The formulas above are all formulas for leaving the womb; the words differ, the principle is one. The formula is: the embryo complete and snowflakes whirling is what the eyes see; gather the five breaths of heart, liver, spleen, lungs and kidneys to the top of the head, break out of the Ancestral Opening, rise to the gate of the crown and go toward the great void. Its principle is: “gather the five and cluster the four, bring the three together and unite the two, and return to one” (see Step Four).

§3
Original (Chinese)

真理者，身不动则精固，而水朝元；心不动则气固，而火朝元；真性寂则魂藏，而木朝元；妄情忘则魄伏，而金朝元；四大安和则意定，而土朝元。此谓五气朝元，皆聚于顶也。其诀法曰；唵嘛呢叭咪吽。是以念动向太空，即性命圭旨大明，五炁聚于顶，现出金光，与三花聚顶之慧光合一。真阴真阳一合，胎形出现。男子身中本无胎，今而欲结一胎，乃因伏气于丹田炁穴中而结胎也。用念攒簇五炁，冲开祖窍，上出天门，庙门一开，二光聚于祖窍，冲出天谷，出顶门，现出一团金光，大如车轮，而阳神在金光之中。其本身丹光阴气，化为天魔外道，百般景象引诱阳神。若是稍有一点声色动心，阳神即一去不返，世人以为坐化，小成之果，而前功废矣，真可悲哉！此皆因炼己未纯，心无真死之过也。

The true principle is: when the body does not move, essence is firm and water returns to the origin; when the heart does not move, breath is firm and fire returns to the origin; when true nature is quiet, the ethereal soul is hidden away and wood returns to the origin; when deluded feelings are forgotten, the corporeal soul lies down and metal returns to the origin; when the four great elements are at peace, the intention is settled and earth returns to the origin. This is called the five breaths returning to the origin, the five breaths all gathering at the crown. Its formula is “om ma ni pad me hum” (the six-syllable mantra). So a thought stirs and goes toward the great void — that is, the great aim of inner nature and life is made plain, the five breaths gather at the crown, golden light appears, and it unites with the light of wisdom of the three flowers gathering at the summit. When true yin and true yang unite, the form of the embryo appears. In a man’s body there is originally no embryo; now an embryo is to be formed, because the breath lying hidden in the Cavity of Breath of the elixir field forms the embryo. With a thought, gather the five breaths, break open the Ancestral Opening and go up out of the Gate of Heaven; as soon as the temple gate opens, the two lights gather at the Ancestral Opening, break out of the “Valley of Heaven” (the Muddy Pellet at the crown), come out by the gate of the crown, and a mass of golden light appears, as large as a cartwheel, with the Yang Spirit within the golden light. The yin breath within the light of one’s own elixir turns into heavenly demons and heterodox ways, a hundred scenes luring the Yang Spirit; if the heart is moved even slightly by a sound or sight, the Yang Spirit goes and does not return — people think it is “dying while seated,” but it is only the fruit of a small attainment, and the previous work is wasted — truly pitiful! All this is the fault of refining the self not having been thorough and the heart not having truly died.

§4
Original (Chinese)

问曰：这魔障以何法消化我真身？出神如何炼？

答曰：必须一意守定金光，死心不动，阴魔一生，即用第七步功翕聚法，将魔磨化为阳气，助我阳神。将一切魔境化尽。金光缩小，用法将金光一转一吸，收归性海本宫，混而为一，静定久之。七日以后而复出之，此阴皆化为真神，现我面前，与我色身一样。然而初出阳神之日，总得看天朗气清，乃可调神出壳，如护小儿一般。大雾、大雨、大风、大雷电光之下，万不可出胎。凡调阳神出壳，急速旋回。七日一出壳，炼至三月后，知觉稍开，宜防惊恐，或出或入，俱按常期，调出旋入，不可任意。演习纯熟，睛天可出阳神，夜内不可出胎。半载以后，三日可出一次。一年之后，一日可出一次。只在色身边运动，不可离开色身。若有惊动，急速收旋回壳，不可大意。二年以后，不拘日夜次数，可以离开色身，还得乳哺。三年以后，可以调神出门，若见人见物，速可旋回。调养功勤，由此半里一里可出，可速收旋而回。总而言之，炼阳神出胎之法，调出旋入，演习纯熟，圣体老炼，总以在内者多，在外者少为事。炼至三年乳哺功成，后当行一定九年还虚之功，面壁大成。

Question: By what method are these demonic obstructions dissolved, so as to preserve my true body? How is sending out the spirit refined?

Answer: One must guard the golden light with a single intention, the heart dead and unmoving; as soon as a yin demon arises, use the gathering-in method of Step Seven to grind the demon away into yang breath, helping my Yang Spirit, and dissolve all demonic realms entirely. When the golden light shrinks, use the method to turn the golden light once and draw it in once, drawing it back to “the home palace of the sea of inner nature,” merged into one, in long stillness and absorption. After seven days send it out again; by then the yin breath has all turned into true spirit, appearing before me, the same as my form body. But on the day the Yang Spirit is first sent out, one must make sure the sky is clear and the air bright, and only then may one “regulate the spirit out of the shell,” protecting it like a small child; under heavy fog, heavy rain, strong wind, or great thunder and lightning, one must on no account leave the womb. Whenever the Yang Spirit is regulated out of the shell, it must be turned back quickly. Send it out of the shell once every seven days; after three months of refining, when its awareness has opened a little, one should guard against alarm and fright; whether going out or coming in, keep to the regular intervals, regulating it out and turning it back in, not at will. When the practice has become fluent, the Yang Spirit may be sent out on clear days, but the embryo must not come out at night. After half a year it may go out once every three days; after a year, once a day — moving only beside the form body, never leaving it; if there is a disturbance, quickly draw it back and turn it into the shell, not carelessly. After two years, regardless of day or night or number of times, it may leave the form body, but it must still be nursed. After three years, the spirit may be regulated to go out of the door; if it sees people or things, turn it back quickly. When the work of regulating and nourishing is diligent, from then on it may go out half a li or a li, and one must be able to draw it back quickly. In short, the method of refining the Yang Spirit to leave the womb: regulate it out and turn it in, practice until fluent, the holy body seasoned and mature, always on the principle of more time within and less time without. After three years of nursing have been completed, one should carry out a stage of the work of “nine years of returning to emptiness,” and the great achievement of facing the wall.

§5
Original (Chinese)

了空禅师云：神既迁到顶门之上，由祖窍出天门，且勿惊怖，只管放心大胆，一志凝神，存想法身，一念思出天门之外，随闭目往下轻轻一跳，如梦初醒，而身外有身矣。阳神初出之时，居于色身之旁，三四尺许，凡身外所有一切，万不可起视听之心，无论三亲六故、祖父妻子，仙佛来参，或真或幻，一切境界，皆当置之度外，一切莫认，一切莫染，切不可着他，只死心不动，绝虑忘情，一味入定，不睹不闻，静以待之，顷刻之间，而自己身中即透出一道金光，大如车轮，由下而上，现于面前，急用真意，将法身性光移到光前，合一凝聚。真意一定，存想金光渐渐收敛，金光即缩小如寸许，状似金钱，即将此光用意一转一收一吸，收入法身之中，而法身即入于凡躯祖窍之内，收入本宫，仍依灭尽定而寂灭之，深入大定。古云：金光以为化形之妙药，千万不可错过。此时金光散去，再无有矣。

了然禅师曰：阳神初出凡身，得静极之后，用五炁朝元口诀，念动而向太空，化为一团金光，大如车轮，与性光合一。二光之中心，阳神在其内，其光之阴气，化为天魔外道百般景象，引诱阳神，若稍着色于闻见，阳神即一去而不返。皆因炼己未纯，心无真死之过也。法曰：必须真意守定金光，死心不动，将魔光磨去，一切魔境不着自退。待魔境退尽，将金光缩小，照定金光一吸，连法身收回祖窍本宫，混合为一，静定久之，以后而复出之。阴魔皆化为阳神，现我面前，与色身一样，方保无失矣。

悟禅老师曰：但阳神由祖窍出来，念出旋回纯熟，须择良辰之日，乃可调阳出壳，如护小儿一般。大雾莫出门，大雨莫行路。时刻调理，不可一时有懈怠，恐阳神一出，而不回也。

敲蹻道师刘名瑞曰：胎从祖窍且出之初，防备外魔侵扰，只提正念，遂出遂入，不可远游。离凡躯二三尺，见一轮金光，本我所有之灵物，取而归之，亦为化形之妙药，且出之初，万物不可著也，只候自身中一轮金光现于空中，将法身近于光前，以法聚光，聚于法身之内，遂急纳法身入于凡身，久久乳哺。

彭茂昌老师曰：阳神初出时，圣体尚嫩，欲其慧光凝结不散，必须调养，养的坚固老成，金光发现，二光归并，即真阴光、真阳光交合，由正中发现圣胎，速急旋回乳哺。盖乳哺者，炼神出入之谓也。初出定之圣胎，易于摇动，调养收回入定久之，方能镇静，而不妄动，故曰定而又定，合乎自然之理。

胞兄赵魁一兄师曰：将阳神迁于上丹田，即是顶门内，此时静中内观，炼顶中三昧真火。顶上有太阳神火，须用真意寂照凝聚，使上火下射，下火上炎，内外夹攻，五炁转撞乾顶，由祖窍撞出，升至百会而出现。此时头顶内如一池银浪，满顶金汁，五炁攻的头顶雷声震震，轰开紫府内院，一霎时间觉得红光遍界，紫焰弥空，迅雷霹雳，响震一声，祖窍开顶门上献出我身，即是阳神，速而旋转收归于内，一日一出，炼至三年，阳神足矣，再炼还虚面壁等功。此我一生四十余年之功也。

The Chan master Liaokong said: when the spirit has moved up above the gate of the crown and comes out from the Ancestral Opening through the Gate of Heaven, do not be alarmed; just be at ease and bold, condense the spirit with a single will, visualize the Dharma-body, think single-mindedly of going out beyond the Gate of Heaven, and then, closing the eyes, give a light jump downward, as if just waking from a dream, and there is a body outside the body. When the Yang Spirit first comes out, it stays beside the form body, three or four feet away; toward everything outside the body, one must on no account give rise to a heart that looks and listens — whether relatives and old friends, grandparents, wife and children, or immortals and buddhas come to pay their respects, true or illusory, all realms should be set aside; recognize none of them, be stained by none, on no account cling to them, but simply keep the heart dead and unmoving, cut off worries and forget feelings, enter absorption single-mindedly, neither looking nor listening, and wait quietly. In a moment, a ray of golden light shines out of one’s own body, as large as a cartwheel, from below upward, and appears in front; quickly, with the True Intention, move the light of inner nature of the Dharma-body to in front of the golden light, and unite and condense them into one. When the True Intention is settled, visualize the golden light gradually drawing in, and the golden light shrinks to about one cun, shaped like a gold coin; then with the intention turn this light once, gather it once and draw it in once, drawing it into the Dharma-body, and the Dharma-body enters the Ancestral Opening of the ordinary body and returns to its home palace, again relying on the “absorption of extinction” to be quiescent and extinguished, deep in great absorption. The ancients said: the golden light is the wondrous medicine for transforming form, and must on no account be missed; if the golden light disperses at this time, it will never come again.

The Chan master Liaoran said: when the Yang Spirit first comes out of the ordinary body, after attaining the utmost of stillness, use the formula of the five breaths returning to the origin; a thought stirs and goes toward the great void, transforming into a mass of golden light as large as a cartwheel, which unites with the light of inner nature; at the center of the two lights is the Yang Spirit. The yin breath within that light turns into heavenly demons and heterodox ways, a hundred scenes luring the Yang Spirit; if the heart is moved even slightly by what is seen or heard, the Yang Spirit goes and does not return — all because refining the self was not thorough and the heart had not truly died. The method: one must guard the golden light with the True Intention, the heart dead and unmoving, grinding away the demonic light; pay no heed to any demonic realm, and it will withdraw by itself. When the demonic realms have entirely withdrawn, shrink the golden light, shine on it firmly and draw it in with one inhalation, drawing it back together with the Dharma-body to the home palace of the Ancestral Opening, merged into one, in long stillness and absorption, and send it out again later. Only when the yin demons have all turned into the Yang Spirit, appearing before me the same as the form body, can one be sure of making no mistake.

Teacher Wuchan said: when the Yang Spirit comes out from the Ancestral Opening and thinking it out and turning it back have both become fluent, one must still choose an auspicious hour and day before regulating the Yang Spirit out of the shell, protecting it like a small child: in heavy fog, do not go out the door; in heavy rain, do not travel; regulate it at every moment, never slackening for an instant, for fear the Yang Spirit, once gone out, will not come back.

The Daoist teacher Liu Mingrui, the Daoist Qiaoqiao, said: when the embryo first comes out from the Ancestral Opening, one must guard against the intrusion of outer demons, keep only right thought, go out and come in at once, and not wander far. Two or three feet away from the ordinary body, one sees a wheel of golden light; this is originally a numinous thing belonging to me, and should be taken back — it too is the wondrous medicine for transforming form. When one first comes out, one must cling to none of the ten thousand things, but simply wait for a wheel of golden light from one’s own body to appear in the air, bring the Dharma-body close in front of the light, gather the light by the method into the Dharma-body, then quickly bring the Dharma-body into the ordinary body and nurse it for a long time.

Teacher Peng Maochang said: when the Yang Spirit first comes out, the holy body is still tender; to make its light of wisdom condense and not scatter, it must be regulated and nourished until it is firm and mature; when the golden light appears and the two lights join — that is, the true yin light and the true yang light unite, and the holy embryo appears from the very center — quickly turn it back and nurse it. What is called nursing is refining the spirit going out and coming in. The holy embryo just out of absorption is easily shaken; regulating and nourishing it, drawing it back and entering absorption for a long time, it becomes steady and does not move rashly — hence “absorbed and again absorbed,” in accord with the principle of nature.

My elder brother Zhao Kuiyi said: move the Yang Spirit to the upper elixir field, that is, within the gate of the crown; at this time contemplate inwardly in stillness and refine the samādhi true fire within the crown. Above the crown there is the spirit-fire of the sun; one must quietly illuminate and condense it with the True Intention, so that the upper fire shoots down and the lower fire flares up, attacking from within and without; the five breaths turn and strike the summit of Qian, break out from the Ancestral Opening and rise to Baihui to appear. At this time the inside of the crown is like a pool of silver waves, the crown full of golden juice; the five breaths attack so that thunder rumbles in the crown, bursting open “the inner court of the Purple Mansion”; in an instant one feels red light filling the world, purple flames filling the sky, sudden thunder and lightning, a crash, and the Ancestral Opening opens, and from the gate of the crown my body is offered up — that is the Yang Spirit; quickly turn it and draw it back within. Sending it out once a day, refining for three years, the Yang Spirit is full; then refine the work of returning to emptiness, facing the wall and so on. This is my work of more than forty years of my life.

§6
Original (Chinese)

问曰：后学得此出胎法诀，一看就明白；若是不迂师者，就不明白。乞师再用白话传出，使后学未迂师能够明白。

答曰：闭目参禅打坐，以念将性光提到目前，二目和合归一，正中有光如月光，久而久之，见月光内有雪花纷飞，速用“念动向太空”口诀，即是五炁朝元之法；五炁归一法是唵嘛呢叭咪，即是心、脾、肺、肝、肾内里五炁转动合一，由后尾闾关过夹脊、玉枕至泥丸宫，出祖窍，上撞百会穴，是一吽字，急把庙门一开，望上一视。庙门开，即是将二目一睁，向上一看。念吽字撞出顶，是心意念动，不可用口念出。五炁若足，自有一金光由下而升上，迂性光一合，即是天上真阳之光炁，迂地下真阴之光炁合一，由中生出道胎，二目慢慢望下一闭，心意轻轻望下一跳，如梦初醒，而身外有身矣。此为出阳神诀法，若是无“念动向太空”五炁朝元之法，其胎出不来，五炁念动上朝元者见图35。

唵字是聚心中正炁，嘛字是聚脾中养炁，呢字是聚肺中金炁，叭字是聚肝中青炁，咪字是聚肾生炁。此五炁聚合一处，由尾闾上升，出祖窍，开百会，念吽字出真阴之金光，迂真阳之月光合一，由二光之中心生出神胎。目向下一视，我的色身坐于傍，我的法身与我色身一样。然而我的法身内里有一分阴炁，必有一分魔障，百般景象引诱阳神，心不能动，速以真意用眼按子、卯、午、酉一转，照定魔光一吸，收入色身内。久久炼之，阴魔之光化为阳神之光，助我胎足，再炼面壁还虚等功。此出胎五炁向空口诀，由古至今，各丹书经卷通不敢泄，余今用白话配图明泄之，后学有缘迂之，自能明白全诀法矣。

Question: When later students obtain this formula for leaving the womb, those who have met a teacher understand it at a glance; those who have not met (the original has 迂) a teacher do not understand it. Please, teacher, transmit it again in plain language, so that later students who have not met a teacher can understand it too.

Answer: Close the eyes and sit in Chan meditation, bring the light of inner nature before the eyes with a thought, the two eyes united and returned to one, and in the very center there is light like moonlight; after a long time, seeing snowflakes whirling within the moonlight, quickly use the formula “a thought stirs, toward the great void” — that is, the method of the five breaths returning to the origin. The method of returning the five breaths to one is “om ma ni pad me”: the five breaths within heart, spleen, lungs, liver and kidneys turn and unite into one, go from the Caudal Funnel pass at the back past the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow to the Muddy Pellet palace, come out of the Ancestral Opening and strike upward at the Baihui point — this is one syllable “hum”; quickly open the temple gate and look upward — “the temple gate opens” means opening the two eyes and looking upward. Thinking the syllable “hum” and striking out through the crown is done with the heart and intention; it must not be spoken aloud with the mouth. If the five breaths are sufficient, a ray of golden light naturally rises from below and unites with the light of inner nature — that is, the light-breath of true yang in heaven and the light-breath of true yin on earth unite into one, and from their midst the Embryo of the Way is born; slowly close the two eyes downward, and with the heart and intention give a light jump downward, as if just waking from a dream, and there is a body outside the body. This is the formula for sending out the Yang Spirit. Without the method of the five breaths returning to the origin, “a thought stirs, toward the great void,” the embryo cannot come out. For how the five breaths, as the thought stirs, rise and return to the origin, see Figure 35.

The syllable “om” gathers the upright breath in the heart, “ma” gathers the nourishing breath in the spleen, “ni” gathers the metal breath in the lungs, “pad” gathers the green breath in the liver, “me” gathers the life breath of the kidneys. These five breaths gather in one place, rise from the Caudal Funnel, come out of the Ancestral Opening, open Baihui; thinking the syllable “hum,” the golden light of true yin comes forth and unites with the moonlight of true yang, and from the center of the two lights the spirit-embryo is born. Looking down with the eyes, my form body sits beside me, and my Dharma-body is just like my form body. But if there is one part of yin breath within my Dharma-body, there is sure to be one part of demonic obstruction, a hundred scenes luring the Yang Spirit; the heart must not move; quickly, with the True Intention, turn the eyes one circle through zi, mao, wu and you, shine firmly on the demonic light and draw it in with one inhalation, drawing it into the form body. Refining for a long time, the light of the yin demons turns into the light of the Yang Spirit and helps my embryo to fullness; then refine the work of facing the wall, returning to emptiness and so on. This formula of “the five breaths toward the void” for leaving the womb has never, from ancient times to the present, been dared revealed in any of the elixir books and scriptures; now I reveal it plainly in vernacular language with a figure, and later students who have the karmic connection to meet it will naturally understand the whole formula.

Figure 35 · The Formula for Leaving the Womb

In the figure a person sits upright cross-legged, seen from the front. At the center of the chest is marked “om one,” on the right of the chest “ma two,” below the throat “ni three,” on the left of the chest “pad four,” below the belly “me five,” and further down along a dotted line “to the Caudal Funnel, then up the back to the crown”; from the crown a dotted line runs up to the syllable “hum,” above which three small circles are inscribed from bottom to top “yin,” “embryo,” “yang” (read from top to bottom in the original as “yang, embryo, yin”). This is what §6 describes: with the five syllables om ma ni pad me, gather in turn the five breaths of heart, spleen, lungs, liver and kidneys, down to the Caudal Funnel, then up the back to the crown; thinking the syllable “hum,” it comes out of the crown, and the two lights of yin and yang unite and give birth to the embryo.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§6
Original note (Chinese)

【注释】本书之次第功夫，下手时有和合真种之功，转手时有修炼舍利之功，了手时有温养道胎之功，撒手时有出胎面壁之功等等，而出胎（即出神）之功，乃是道家炼功的最高理想。

经前第十四步功，温养道胎于中宫，已达胎圆地步，再用迁法将道胎由中田迁于上田，即泥丸宫，但作为阳神而言，婴儿尚幼小，须加以乳哺，遂有三年乳哺之法。伍冲虚在《仙佛合宗》中说：“倘拘神于上丹田之小境，则失还丹之义旨，大悖乳哺之法矣。其法兼存养之全体、出收之大用而言者也。盖存养之功，不着意于上丹田，亦不纵意于上田；惟一阳神寂照于上丹田，相与混融，化成一虚空大境，斯为存养之全体，乃为乳哺之首务也。存养功纯，自有出神之景焉”。乳哺者炼神之喻，化成虚空大境者炼神还虚之义，通过虚空大境，炼之又炼，性合虚空，阳神久定亦还虚与虚空同体，遂能超出顶门。虚空大境，只有练功者可以体会得到。伍冲虚云：“故修士当以最初还虚为急务，若乳哺谨慎，能还虚于三年，则阳神始得老成，自可达地通天，入金石而无碍。”

雪花飘飞，为出神之景。此景发生在眼前慧光内。念动向太空，为出神之口诀，有此口诀阳神才能冲出顶门而向太空。但婴儿幼小，容易迷失难归，且又有外扰，故出入要十分谨慎。古人为此定下了严格的准则，即出收之时少，而始则出一步或多步而旋收，久之出一里而旋收等。至于收光之法，《慧命经》有禅机论曰：“寂灭之定久，纷纷白雪满空，又曰机矣！斯时出定之辨机，不令其迟阻，速以出之。若夫滞于胎中，则缺少神通之变化。即当而出之，从顶而出，斯谓之超出三界宇而待之，即离凡身一二尺候之，又曰禅矣！一片金光来悬于当空，又曰机矣！收而入之，定而又定，又曰禅矣！久久长定，形神俱化，而禅机之说，从此毕矣”！三界者指下丹田、中宫、顶门。古人认为，金光本是我所有之灵物和妙药，自当取而用之。

出神之法，千峰老人不惜反复引用其师及祖师真传，学者再印证《伍柳仙宗》，特别是其中《慧命经》，或可有所启悟。

The sequence of work in this book: in setting to work there is the work of “uniting the true seed,” in turning the hand there is the work of “cultivating and refining the śarīra,” in completing there is the work of “warming and nourishing the Embryo of the Way,” in letting go there is the work of “leaving the womb and facing the wall,” and so on; and the work of leaving the womb (that is, sending out the spirit) is the highest ideal of Daoist practice.

Through the work of Step Fourteen above, warming and nourishing the Embryo of the Way in the central palace, one has already reached the point of the embryo’s completion; then, by the method of moving, the Embryo of the Way is moved from the middle elixir field to the upper elixir field, that is, the Muddy Pellet palace. But as the Yang Spirit, the infant is still small and must be nursed, and so there is the method of “three years of nursing.” Wu Chongxu says in The Common Origin of Immortals and Buddhas: “If one confines the spirit to the small realm of the upper elixir field, one loses the aim of the reverted elixir and goes greatly against the method of nursing. This method refers both to the whole of preserving and nourishing and to the great function of going out and drawing back. The work of preserving and nourishing neither fixes the intention on the upper elixir field nor lets the intention wander away from it; the Yang Spirit simply illuminates quietly in the upper elixir field, merging with it, transforming into one great realm of empty space — this is the whole of preserving and nourishing, the first task of nursing. When the work of preserving and nourishing is mature, the scene of sending out the spirit naturally comes.” Nursing is a metaphor for refining the spirit; transforming into a great realm of empty space is the meaning of “refining the spirit to return to emptiness”: through the great realm of empty space, refining and refining again, inner nature unites with empty space, the Yang Spirit, long absorbed, also returns to emptiness and becomes one body with empty space, and so can pass out beyond the gate of the crown. The great realm of empty space can only be experienced by those who practice. Wu Chongxu says: “So the practitioner should make the first returning to emptiness the urgent task; if nursing is done with care and one can return to emptiness within three years, the Yang Spirit becomes mature, and naturally can reach the earth and penetrate heaven, entering metal and stone without obstruction.”

Snowflakes whirling is the scene of sending out the spirit, and this scene occurs within the light of wisdom before the eyes. “A thought stirs, toward the great void” is the formula for sending out the spirit; with this formula the Yang Spirit can break out of the gate of the crown and go toward the great void. But the infant is small, easily lost and hard to bring back, and there are outer disturbances, so going out and coming in must be done with great care. For this the ancients set strict rules: times of going out and drawing back should be few; at first, go out a step or a few steps and turn back at once; in time, go out a li and turn back at once, and so on. As for the method of drawing in the light, the Book of Wisdom and Life has a passage, “On the Mechanism of Chan,” that says: “When the absorption of quiescent extinction has lasted long, white snow whirls filling the sky — this is called the ‘mechanism’! This is the discerning mechanism for coming out of absorption; do not let it be delayed or obstructed, but go out quickly; if one stays in the embryo, one lacks the transformations of spiritual power. Going out at that moment, out through the crown, is called going beyond the realm of the three worlds and waiting for it, that is, leaving the ordinary body by one or two feet and waiting for it — this is called ‘Chan’! A sheet of golden light hangs in the sky — this again is called the ‘mechanism’! Drawing it in, absorbed and again absorbed — this again is called ‘Chan’! Absorbed for long, form and spirit both transformed — the teaching of the mechanism of Chan ends here!” The three worlds refer to the lower elixir field, the central palace and the gate of the crown. The ancients held that the golden light is originally a numinous thing and wondrous medicine belonging to me, and naturally should be taken and used.

For the method of sending out the spirit, the Old Man of Qianfeng spares no effort in repeatedly quoting the true transmission of his teachers and patriarchs; if students further check it against The Immortal Lineage of Wu and Liu, especially the Book of Wisdom and Life within it, they may gain some insight.

Key Terms

  • Sending out the spirit: the Yang Spirit leaving the form body through the gate of the crown; Qiu Ling’s note calls it “the highest ideal of Daoist practice.”
  • Formula for leaving the womb: “a thought stirs, toward the great void; sun and moon open, the temple gate opens; pushing feeling to join inner nature, it turns; the two lights meet and appear.”
  • Temple gate, Gate of Heaven, Valley of Heaven: the crown; “the temple gate opens” means opening the two eyes and looking upward.
  • Om ma ni pad me hum: Zhao uses these six syllables to gather the five breaths and go out through the crown: om gathers the upright breath in the heart, ma the nourishing breath in the spleen, ni the metal breath in the lungs, pad the green breath in the liver, me the life breath of the kidneys, and with hum it goes out through the crown. They must be thought with the heart, not spoken aloud.
  • Form body, Dharma-body: the flesh body and the body of the Yang Spirit.
  • Nursing: the Yang Spirit, newly come out like an infant, regulated and nourished in going out and coming in; “nursing means refining the spirit going out and coming in.”
  • Three years of nursing, nine years of returning to emptiness: three years of regulating and nourishing after sending out the spirit, then nine years of facing the wall and returning to emptiness.
  • Dying while seated: death in the sitting posture; Zhao says that when the Yang Spirit is lured away by demons and does not return, people mistakenly think one has died while seated.
  • Heavenly demons and heterodox ways: the various luring scenes transformed from the yin breath within the light of one’s own elixir.
  • Wondrous medicine for transforming form: the golden light that appears in the air when sending out the spirit.
  • Inner court of the Purple Mansion: the inside of the crown (Zhao Kuiyi’s term).
  • Setting to work, turning the hand, completing, letting go: the four stages of the work in this book listed in Qiu Ling’s note.
  • Three worlds: Qiu Ling’s note says the lower elixir field, the central palace and the gate of the crown.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Read, don’t practice: this step describes the highest stage of the elixir path; this site keeps it as a documentary record and does not recommend that anyone try it.
  2. The heart method that runs throughout: all the teachers stress the same thing — “recognize none of them, be stained by none,” “the heart dead and unmoving,” “keep only right thought.”
  3. Out little, in much: “always on the principle of more time within and less time without” — even in a metaphorical sense, this is about gathering the heart and guarding within.
  4. Seek help for unusual experiences: if in quiet sitting you have a feeling of leaving the body, or visual or auditory hallucinations, together with fear or confusion about reality, stop practicing and see a doctor promptly.

  7. “Om ma ni pad me hum” (唵嘛呢叭咪吽): the Buddhist six-syllable mantra (also written 嗡嘛呢叭咪吽); in the original figure 咪 is written 弥, and this site has made it uniformly 咪 in the redrawing. Zhao’s matching of the six syllables to the five organs is his own particular usage.
