The Sixteen Steps of Method Formulas · 第八步 蛰藏炁穴

Step Eight · Hibernating in the Cavity of Breath

The formula of Step Eight, hibernating in the Cavity of Breath — gathered in, the ancestral breath hibernates in the center; the breathing of the true person is kept within

About This Chapter

Step Seven held together and drew back the light of wisdom in front of the Ancestral Opening; this step tells how to draw it into the Cavity of Breath (the lower elixir field) and store it away, “hibernating” like an animal in its winter sleep, so that spirit and breath unite within and one enters absorption.

  1. The method of hibernating storage: draw the gathered point of the breath of the Yang Spirit into the belly (you will hear a “gulp”), then gaze at the elixir field for a long time; when a surge of heat stirs in the elixir field, move it with the intention to the position of the heart, then to the position of the kidneys, back and forth without stopping, until suddenly it drops into the Cavity of Breath and stays still — this is called “entering the opening within the opening,” and it is the true practice of the meeting of heart and kidneys. The key point is “above, not past the heart; below, not past the kidneys.”
  2. The four methods of harmonizing, uniting, condensing and gathering: the yin breath in the heart obtaining the yang breath in the kidneys is “harmonizing” (mercury coming to lead); the yang breath in the kidneys receiving the yin breath in the heart is “uniting” (lead coming to mercury); guarding the Ancestral Opening so the spirit condenses, and the condensing fire entering the Cavity of Breath, is “condensing”; distinguishing the two periods when yang is born and applying the method of gathering the medicine is “gathering.”
  3. Metal Father and Wood Mother, the Lesser Celestial Circuit stage by stage, the true seed: in questions and answers Zhao explains these concepts once more, and describes in detail the “three periods”: in the first period the medicine is obtained (tender); in the second the mani is gathered (just right); in the third the medicine is old.
  4. The words of the teachers: Kuiyizi (Zhao’s elder brother), Liaoran, Liaokong, Peng Maochang, Tan Zhiming and Liu Yunpu, from the angles of Kan and Li, wu and ji, the Yellow Dame, and the meeting of heart and kidneys, all speak of the same thing.

Qiu Ling points out that by this step the student’s constitution is already very different from when he first set to work, the Lesser Celestial Circuit is open, and he is entering the realm of the Greater Celestial Circuit; he speaks in particular about “absorption” — “of all the hundred works of the Daoists, cultivating absorption is the first essential”; what the first eight steps train is all “lesser absorption,” and only when there is no breathing through mouth and nose is it “great absorption.”

Safety note (translator): The original says that after entering absorption “one can go seven days without food” and “refine until going forty-nine days without food,” and Qiu Ling’s note also says the ancients in great absorption “abstained from grain” — these are accounts of ancient practitioners; on no account fast or go without food for long periods on your own, which would seriously harm your health. Qiu Ling also says “when practice enters great absorption, there must be a companion to watch over one.” The techniques in this step such as “pressing the Opening of Life and Death with all the force of the middle finger” and “closing the Conception and opening the Governing” are used only when the true yang stirs; do not use excessive force; they are not suitable for people with cardiovascular or cerebrovascular disease, or prostate or perineal conditions. If palpitations, tightness in the chest or dizziness occur during sitting, stop at once.

Original Text and Translation

Figure 22 · Hibernating in the Cavity of Breath

In the figure a long-bearded Daoist in a wide-sleeved robe sits upright cross-legged, his two hands joined in front of the lower belly. A large character “heart” is written on the chest, and the character “kidneys” on the lower belly beneath the hands — meaning: the spirit of the heart descends, the breath of the kidneys rises, heart and kidneys meet, and spirit and breath hibernate in the Cavity of Breath between the two.

Formula
Original (Chinese)

翕聚蛰藏祖炁中，

真人呼吸里边存。

窍内有窍名橐籥，

息往息来神入中。

Let the ancestral breath that has been gathered in hibernate in the center (the Cavity of Breath); the “breathing of the true person” (Prior Heaven embryonic breathing) is kept within. Within the opening there is another opening, called the “bellows” (see Step Four); between the breath going and the breath coming, the spirit enters it.

§1
Original (Chinese)

蛰藏之法，是将祖窍前翕聚那一点阳神炁，收归于气穴之内，名为凝神入炁穴。此炁穴有内外两窍，外窍为阴阳之源、神气之宅、胎息之根、呼吸之祖；内窍者长胎住息之所、入大定之室。内呼息上不过心，下不过肾，久而久之，真息入窍，忽然大定得矣。

The method of hibernating storage is to draw the point of the breath of the Yang Spirit gathered in front of the Ancestral Opening back into the Cavity of Breath; this is called concentrating the spirit and entering the Cavity of Breath. This Cavity of Breath has two openings, outer and inner: the outer opening is the source of yin and yang, the dwelling of spirit and breath, the root of embryonic breathing, the ancestor of breathing; the inner opening is the place where the holy embryo is nourished and the breathing stops, the chamber for entering great absorption. The breathing within the body goes up no further than the heart and down no further than the kidneys; after a long time the True Breath enters the opening, and suddenly one attains great absorption.

§2
Original (Chinese)

前第七步功法，系用我之涕、唾、津、精、气、血、液七宝，换来真种。实由于平素惩忿遣欲、默坐澄心功纯，达到人心死而道心活境界。盖心死则性月朗明，心生则欲尘遮蔽。修士修心归于一处，注意集中，清静而无所牵挂，自然识神溶化，五蕴皆空，不为物诱，神炁聚于内，方无涣散而不统一之虞。身为气之宅，心是神之舍，故炁一断而我之元神因之失护；身体强健者，静定良久，真炁由内而动，倏见本来面目，即我性光也。彼时舒适异常，以神聚之，而顾命之旨，尽在于斯矣。候性光色红或移动时，然后急用翕聚先天炁法诀，收归我有。然不得蛰藏炁穴之法，终属得必复失。其口诀无他，将祖窍中炼出先天阳神之炁光，收入腹中，立觉咕噜之响，即系已到海底之证，再以目光久视丹田，有股热力，激激动荡，用意运之于心位，及至心位，复用意运之于肾位，如此往来不已，忽落炁穴内，名曰入窍中窍，是为重入胞胎，再造乾坤之起始，实乃心肾相交真功。常人每日熟睡时，得神火下降，肾水感受热力薰蒸，化气而上升，则得心肾相交；待其觉醒开目时，而神奔入于二目，心肾遂不相交矣。若七日不食，则亦不交。何也？以其先天真炁耗尽无余，无有来源接济，而人即失生命存在能力，如同火车没有煤水，无从燃烧蒸汽。蛰藏炁穴为深根固蒂法，乃由收视返听，行持积累功纯则心虚，心虚则神凝，神凝则炁聚。欲其炁之常聚而不散，独赖每日抱元守一持久，方得静中无时不心肾交，断非寻常人每在睡眠后而得心肾之交也。

The practice of Step Seven above uses my “seven treasures” — nasal mucus, spittle, saliva, essence, breath, blood and fluids — in exchange for the true seed. This really comes from ordinarily “restraining anger and dismissing desire,” sitting in silence and clarifying the heart, until the practice is thoroughly mature and one reaches the state where “the human heart dies and the heart of the Way lives.” For when the human heart dies, the moon of inner nature (the light of one’s original nature) is bright; as soon as the human heart arises, the dust of desire covers it. When those who cultivate the Way cultivate the heart until it returns to one place, the attention focused, pure, still and without attachments, the cognitive spirit naturally dissolves, “the five aggregates are all empty” (form, sensation, perception, mental formations and consciousness are all empty), one is not lured by outer things, spirit and breath gather within the body, and only then is there no worry of their scattering or being disunited. The body is the dwelling of the breath, the heart the house of the spirit, so once the breath is cut off, my Original Spirit loses its protection as a result; for one whose body is strong, after long stillness and absorption the true breath stirs from within, and suddenly one sees one’s original face — that is my light of inner nature. At that time one feels unusually comfortable, and should hold it together with the spirit; the whole point of “caring for life” lies here.

When the light of inner nature turns red or moves, quickly use the formula for gathering in the Prior Heaven breath (Step Seven) to take it into my possession. But without the method of hibernating in the Cavity of Breath, what is gained will be lost again in the end. Its formula is nothing else: draw the light of the breath of the Prior Heaven Yang Spirit refined in the Ancestral Opening into the belly, and at once you will feel a “gulp” — that is the proof that it has reached the bottom of the sea (the lower elixir field, the area of the perineum); then gaze at the elixir field with the eyes for a long time, and there will be a surge of heat, stirring and shaking; move it with the intention to the position of the heart; when it reaches the position of the heart, move it with the intention to the position of the kidneys; back and forth like this without stopping, until suddenly it drops into the Cavity of Breath — this is called “entering the opening within the opening”; it is re-entering the womb and the beginning of re-creating Qian and Kun, and in fact it is the true practice of the meeting of heart and kidneys.

When ordinary people are fast asleep each day, the fire of the spirit descends, the kidney water is steamed by the heat, transforms into breath and rises, and so heart and kidneys meet; when they wake up and open their eyes, the spirit rushes into the two eyes, and heart and kidneys no longer meet. If one goes without food for seven days, they also no longer meet. Why? Because the Prior Heaven true breath is used up without remainder, and with no source to replenish it, the person loses the capacity to stay alive — just as a train without coal and water has no means of burning and producing steam. Hibernating in the Cavity of Breath is the method of “deepening the root and making the stem firm”; it begins with “withdrawing the sight and turning the hearing inward” (drawing back sight and hearing so they do not race outward), and with persistence and accumulation, when the practice is mature, the heart becomes empty; when the heart is empty, the spirit condenses; when the spirit condenses, the breath gathers. To keep the breath always gathered and never scattered, one can only rely on “embracing the origin and guarding the One” every day, persisting for a long time; only then do heart and kidneys meet at every moment in stillness — not at all the kind of meeting of heart and kidneys that ordinary people obtain only in sleep.

§3
Original (Chinese)

如何修性立命，尽性了命？《悟真篇》曰：“有为之功以了命，无为之道以了性”。性命俱了，道还其虚，即佛门虚空粉碎之理也。试就人身而论，上边口内天池穴漏真气，幸舌抵上腭，使其不泄；下边阳关泄炁，多于天池数倍，没有办理，虽童体仍有死之一大原因也。故破体人所亏欠元炁，必用有为之法，炼到有为之中无为，自然归根复命，命长在而载命之躯亦长在也。若不守真炁，复泄真精，则与未修者生死无异。盖神驰则炁散，炁散而为精，精竭则人亡矣。初用翕聚以神入乎炁穴，后则炁包乎其神，忽然真意寂静，即是神凝炁聚，合而为一，落于炁穴内，则入定矣。名之曰胎，非实有胎，亦非有形有相之物，乃是气质变化，即我之神炁合一耳。

How does one cultivate inner nature and establish life, fulfill inner nature and complete life? Awakening to Reality says: “The work of deliberate action is used to complete life; the great Way of non-action is used to complete inner nature.” When inner nature and life are both completed, the Way returns to emptiness — the principle of the Buddhist “empty space shattered.” Take the human body: above, the Celestial Pool point in the mouth (the palate) leaks true breath, but fortunately the tongue pressed against the palate keeps it from escaping; below, the yang gate leaks breath, many times more than the Celestial Pool, yet there is no way to deal with it — this is one great reason why even a virgin body still dies. So the original breath owed by those who have lost their virginity must be replenished by the method of deliberate action, refining until non-action is reached within deliberate action; naturally one returns to the root and restores life; when life endures, the body that carries life endures too. If one does not guard the true breath and also leaks the true essence, then one’s life and death are no different from those who have never cultivated. For as soon as the spirit races off, the breath scatters; when the breath scatters, it turns into essence; when the essence is exhausted, the person dies. At first one uses gathering-in, letting the spirit enter the Cavity of Breath; later the breath envelops the spirit; suddenly the True Intention is still, which is the spirit condensed and the breath gathered, united into one, settled within the Cavity of Breath — and one enters absorption. This is called the “embryo” — it is not really an embryo, nor anything with form or appearance, but a change in the quality of one’s breath; it is simply my spirit and breath united into one.

§4
Original (Chinese)

问曰：何谓和合凝聚之法？

答曰：凡炼气者，须先炼心。心者识神主事，乃属一团阴气，阴极阳生，方克期于养性续命，阳交于阴，阴得阳，性转为命，故曰修性立命，名为和法，即汞来投铅也。心虚身静，肾中阳炁承受心中阴气，阳交于阴，阳得阴，命转为性，故曰命立性存，名为合法，即铅来投汞也。以守祖窍功法，将心猿意马牢拴在双林树下，能得心虚，常凝我之神，则此凝火便入炁穴，名为凝法。而阳气来自虚无，元关立有顿开之机，外阳勃举，速转法轮七次，如其自萎，仍守祖窍，复觉其举，则目守脐轮，吸气使意在心位，呼气使意在肾位，曾用转法轮、无孔笛两种真意取之不得，外形不倒，再辨二候，施用采药法诀，实为集命之方，此名为集法。

Question: What is the method of “harmonizing, uniting, condensing and gathering”?

Answer: Everyone who refines the breath must first refine the heart. The heart is where the cognitive spirit is in charge, and belongs to a mass of yin breath; when yin reaches its extreme, yang is born, and only then can one hope to nourish inner nature and prolong life: yang meets yin, yin obtains yang, inner nature turns into life — that is why it is called “cultivating inner nature and establishing life,” and this is called the method of “harmonizing,” which is “mercury coming to lead.” When the heart is empty and the body still, the yang breath in the kidneys receives the yin breath in the heart; yang meets yin, yang obtains yin, life turns into inner nature — that is why it is called “life established, inner nature preserved,” and this is called the method of “uniting,” which is “lead coming to mercury.” Using the practice of guarding the Ancestral Opening (Step One), tie the monkey of the heart and the horse of the intention firmly “beneath the twin sāla trees” (where the Buddha entered nirvana, a metaphor for a place of quiet); one can attain emptiness of heart and constantly condense my spirit, and this condensing fire enters the Cavity of Breath — this is called the method of “condensing.” The yang breath comes from emptiness and non-being, and the Mysterious Pass (the original has 元关) at once has a mechanism of sudden opening; the outer yang rises vigorously; quickly turn the Dharma Wheel seven times; if it goes down by itself, keep guarding the Ancestral Opening; if you again feel it rise, guard the navel wheel with the eyes, letting the intention be at the position of the heart on the inhalation and at the position of the kidneys on the exhalation; if neither of the two True Intention methods — turning the Dharma Wheel and the holeless flute (see Step Two) — can bring it in, and the outer form still does not go down, then distinguish the “two periods” and apply the formula for gathering the medicine (see Step Four) — this is truly the method of gathering together the treasure of life, and it is called the method of “gathering.”

§5
Original (Chinese)

问曰：何为金公木母交并？

答曰：金公者日也，木母者月也。人在后天，若无真意主宰，则日月无以合其明。日月和合，加以回光返照，二圭合一，则真炁自然而生矣。肾属水，内藏元炁。心属火，内藏元神。初则真意下降坤脐，而神火下照北海，肾水因被薰蒸，化炁上腾，以火入水中，为水火交媾，取象既济。若外日月不交光，而内水火必不交合，则先天真炁莫由而生。若顺其自然，火就燥，水流湿，则火常在上，水常就下，任其交背驰驱，水不得热力，难望变炁腾空，取象未济。许旌阳云：“大丹若不以日月交光，乾坤合体，更假何物为之乎”？此金木交并关系，其功用甚大。

Question: What is “the joining of Metal Father and Wood Mother”?

Answer: Metal Father is the sun, Wood Mother is the moon (the two eyes). In the Posterior Heaven state, without the True Intention presiding, sun and moon cannot unite their light. When sun and moon are united, and the light is turned around to shine back, “the two gui unite into one” (the two earths wu and ji make the character 圭), and the true breath is naturally born. The kidneys belong to water and hide the original breath within; the heart belongs to fire and hides the Original Spirit within. At first the True Intention descends to the navel of Kun, the fire of the spirit shines down on the “Northern Sea” (the lower elixir field, the kidneys), and the kidney water, steamed, transforms into breath and rises — this is fire entering water, called “the union of water and fire,” taking its image from the hexagram Jiji, “After Completion” (water above, fire below). If outwardly sun and moon do not join their light, inwardly water and fire will certainly not unite, and the Prior Heaven true breath has no way to be born. If one goes along with nature, “fire goes to the dry, water flows to the wet,” fire always stays above and water always flows down; if they are left to go their opposite ways, the water gets no heat and can hardly hope to transform into breath and rise into the air — taking its image from the hexagram Weiji, “Before Completion” (fire above, water below). Xu Jingyang (Xu Xun) said: “If the great elixir did not use the joining of the light of sun and moon and the union of Qian and Kun into one body, what else could it rely on to be refined?” The relation of metal and wood joined has a very great function.

§6
Original (Chinese)

问曰：敢问小周天逐节功法。

答曰：小周天又名法轮，即施用风火之功，渐使真炁徘徊于任督二脉也。初行呼吸之际，须臾不间断，则外缘顿息，而神炁自不相离，时觉丹田融暖，甚至终日和暖，复觉阳炁逆上尾闾，经日久锻炼，而阳炁始逆上夹脊，心志有恒，不难过玉枕而直捣乾宫矣。若得三车口诀，立时即过三关。若阳炁生而下行，神虽宰炁，只可使阳炁暂伏，无息之招摄，则炁不随任督道路而行，必出阳关。以其炁之行，无呼吸迫促故也。如欲习炼，闭口卷舌，每吸气时，后天气自鼻入喉渐至丹田，同时真意从生死窍起，循脊髓之路，立达顶心。每呼气时，后天气自喉经鼻而出，同时真意从顶心起向眉间入舌后，而下行于绛宫炁穴，至生死窍而止。周而复始，往来不穷，性命两窍皆得跳动之景，此为炁方生预兆。回视前日之功，似乎空转法轮。若不如法勾引阳炁，则真炁无由而生；纵能生之，亦必散于周身别络。有时法轮自停，忽然静定，任其自然，不可强转，则真炁自觉向上而升，美在其中。或得外阳举时，此小周天之功当止，另用无孔笛吹之，以辨其炁来真假，假即外阳萎矣，真则丝毫不座，再施用其他功法可也。柳华阳祖师谓，道之妙用，莫如法轮；运行之蹊，莫如道路。法轮者真炁也，道路者任督脉也。修士遵余所言，万举万当。

Question: Please explain the practice of the Lesser Celestial Circuit stage by stage.

Answer: The Lesser Celestial Circuit is also called the “Dharma Wheel”; it is the work of applying wind (the breath) and fire (spirit and intention), gradually letting the true breath come and go in the Conception and Governing vessels. When one first begins the breathing, without a moment’s interruption, outer entanglements (outside disturbances) cease at once, spirit and breath naturally do not separate, and one constantly feels the elixir field warm and melting, even warm all day; one also feels the yang breath going back up the Caudal Funnel; after long training, the yang breath begins to go up the Spine-Straddling Pass; with a constant will it is not hard to pass the Jade Pillow and drive straight into the palace of Qian (the crown). If one obtains the formula of the “Three Carts,” one can pass the three passes (the Caudal Funnel, the Spine-Straddling Pass, the Jade Pillow) at once. If the yang breath, once born, goes downward, although the spirit can preside over the breath, it can only make the yang breath lie low for a while; without breathing to draw it in, the breath does not follow the road of the Conception and Governing vessels, and is sure to go out through the yang gate — because the movement of the breath has no breathing to drive it.

If you want to practice: close the mouth and curl the tongue (the tip of the tongue curled up against the palate); with each inhalation, the Posterior Heaven breath goes from the nose into the throat and gradually to the elixir field; at the same time the True Intention sets out from the Opening of Life and Death and follows the road of the spinal marrow straight to the center of the crown. With each exhalation, the Posterior Heaven breath goes out from the throat through the nose; at the same time the True Intention sets out from the center of the crown, goes toward the point between the eyebrows, enters behind the tongue, and goes down to the Crimson Palace and the Cavity of Breath, stopping at the Opening of Life and Death. Round and round, back and forth without end, both openings, of inner nature and of life (the head and the lower belly), show a scene of throbbing — the sign that the breath is just about to be born. Looking back at one’s earlier practice, it seems one was merely turning the Dharma Wheel empty. If one does not coax the yang breath according to the method, the true breath has no way to be born; even if it is born, it is sure to scatter into the side branches and collaterals throughout the body. Sometimes the Dharma Wheel stops by itself and one is suddenly still and absorbed; then let it be natural, do not force it to turn; the true breath will be felt rising by itself, and the wonder lies in this. If the outer yang rises, the work of this Lesser Celestial Circuit should stop, and one should instead blow the “holeless flute” (see Step Two) to tell whether the breath that has come is true or false: if false, the outer yang goes down; if true, it does not go down in the least (the original has 不座, see the Translator’s Notes), and then one may apply other methods. Patriarch Liu Huayang said: “Among the wondrous functions of the Way, none compares with the Dharma Wheel; among the paths of movement, none compares with the road.” The Dharma Wheel is the true breath; the road is the Conception and Governing vessels. Those who cultivate the Way and do as I say will not fail once in ten thousand times.

§7
Original (Chinese)

问曰：何者是真种子？何者是真药物？

答曰：但见祖炁盘旋于祖窍之前，以翕聚法诀聚之，而真种子得矣。

Question: What is the true seed? What is the true medicine?

Answer: As long as you see the ancestral breath circling in front of the Ancestral Opening and hold it together with the formula of gathering in, the true seed is obtained.

§8
Original (Chinese)

When heart and kidneys meet during sleep at night, never mind how long, at the yin hour (three to five in the morning) the outer thing (the member) is sure to rise; that is why it is said “man is born at yin.” If one has practiced stillness for a long time, as soon as heart and kidneys meet, the outer yang is sure to rise — this is exactly the moment when “the thing rises and the tide comes in,” and it is taken as the evidence of whether there is true breath. Only if the outer yang does not shrink can one be sure that the breath has truly come — the elixir books call this “when the time comes, the spirit knows.” This is the moment when the original breath is about to transform into original essence: the Living Zi Hour has come.

When the outer thing rises again, though the breath has quietly come, the spirit has not noticed; this is called “obtaining the medicine in the first period”; the medicine is still tender, and there is properly no form or substance to speak of; one should rein in the yang gate to respond to it — this is the secret mechanism of regulating the outer medicine. When regulation brings spirit and breath to stir at the same time, and the outer thing rises, it is exactly the moment of “gathering the fire to carry the metal, the fire pressing the metal onward,” neither old nor tender — the elixir books call it “when the medicine is produced, the spirit knows,” and this is called “gathering the mani in the second period” (gathering the precious pearl). Like a cat catching a mouse (focused and swift): below, press the Opening of Life and Death with all the force of the middle finger; above, close the Conception and open the Governing, applying the practice of “drawing in the breath with spirit and breathing,” like rivers returning to the sea that even mountains cannot stop, and the true medicine is obtained. When regulating the medicine, there should be nothing but right thought; if there are stray thoughts or deviant ideas, it is sure to turn into lustful essence, and the medicine becomes a medicine of dregs — useless! If one does not know the secret of “riding the breath with the spirit,” one is sure to suffer the trouble of the breath leaking and the mechanism ceasing (the moment passing); even if one knows the method of gathering, if at the furnace one does not do it thoroughly, though the essence has not leaked, the breath has long since leaked, and though the outer thing still stands, it becomes “metal past the full moon, no longer fit to taste” (metal past the time of the full moon, no longer usable) — this is called “the medicine is old in the third period” (see old and tender).

To go a step further: constantly turn the light around to shine back, constantly gather up body and heart, and the true seed and medicine naturally transform into the Prior Heaven true breath, rising little by little to the Crimson Palace; settle it with the True Intention, then lead this breath back down, descending little by little to the Cavity of Breath. At this time the hands and feet are completely empty, as if the body were in the clouds; there is neither other nor self, the four great elements are all empty, and suddenly the true fire enters the water. But the heart must not crave that delightful state of melting bliss; if one falls into deviant views, there is sure to be the danger of losing one’s way. The bodies within and without unite and their functions interconnect; this is truly the supreme and most true wondrous Way.

Original note (Chinese)

【注释】翕聚祖炁蛰藏炁穴功法，是修性立命，尽性了命，达到性命双修之法，但比第一步功法到第二步功法的修性立命，进入了更高的层次，因为行功到此，学者体质及炼功成就已同刚下手时大大不同。学者经过前七步功法的锻炼，神炁已经结合，慧光已经凝成，身体气血通畅，小周天已通，存年旧病大致已愈，现在是进入大周天的境界。

本步口诀主要介绍蛰藏功法。即把祖窍前先天真炁之光用意念使之随着吞咽津液收入腹内，蛰藏于炁穴（下丹田）之内封固，再用回光返照对斗明星法常照丹田，出息微微，入息绵绵，进入胎息之境，久视静极，而真炁必动，丹田有一股热流动荡其中，一点点上升到心下绛宫，用真一定，真炁又一点点下降于肾位丹田，此即上不过心，下不过肾，久动而后静，忽然之间，真炁入真炁穴内不动，遂进入大定。其法至简，但效应非常，即达到心肾相交、神凝炁聚、日月交光、水火既济、坎离交媾和阴阳一体的地步，是进行炼大丹大药，进入大定之时。这个大定，古人是一坐接连七天，甚至不止七天，并且辟谷。

把祖窍前之慧光翕聚而收归炁穴，久而久之，慧光印于下丹田，有如皓月印澄潭，则下丹田再不是黑暗一片，而是隐隐发出亮光，越虚极静笃此光越见清亮。语云：“对斗明星”，斗即北斗，喻丹田是也。明星者即丹田之内发光是也。此上下二光即所谓日月之光。这个外日月之光一旦交合，就表示体内心肾、水火、坎离、神炁得到进一步的和合，此时口中津液咽纳不尽，似周身处于一片祥和光幕之中，感到浑身通畅，真非言语所能形容，回顾自身似已空洞消失，或通身变白，此即“虚室生白”之验。

道家百般工程，以修“定”为第一要义。何谓“定”？即内外两忘，忘人忘我，忘天忘地，入于空寂之境。入定得深，往往过了一日一夜，只似刹那之间。能定则静自在其中。修静尚可，修定最难，有练功多年尚未能入定者。练丹功须能定方能得力，不能气静神闲，念止心定，百般作为皆无益也。道家炼丹，无火不成，而火生于定，故定为入道初基；收心炼性，亦是修炼定力。定力成，开关展窍以至其他工程，只要懂得其法，便可炼成。但无论凡圣，收心最难，故静定最难得。道门有四字诀，即“心息相依”，或者“神气相抱”，其法均通过凝神入炁穴进行。盖凝神寂照炁穴，可使元气自足而元阳自生，阳生一分，则阴消一分，阳全阴尽，即可益寿延年。欲阳之生，则又非定无以为功。此为丹道中的彻始彻终功夫。

前此八步功法练的均属于小定。练到无口鼻呼吸气，才是大定。有说小定一日，中定三日，大定七日；亦有说小定七日，大定数十日的。千峰老人说：大定是“静定之极，不省人事，气息全无，六脉皆住”。练功进入大定，须要侣伴护持。

The practice of gathering in the ancestral breath and hibernating in the Cavity of Breath is a method of cultivating inner nature and establishing life, fulfilling inner nature and completing life, achieving the dual cultivation of nature and life; but compared with cultivating inner nature and establishing life in the practices of Steps One and Two, it has entered a higher level — because by the time practice reaches this point, the student’s constitution and attainment are already very different from when he first set to work. Having gone through the training of the first seven steps, the student’s spirit and breath have already joined, the light of wisdom has condensed, the qi and blood of the body flow freely, the Lesser Celestial Circuit is open, old illnesses of many years have mostly healed, and he is now entering the realm of the Greater Celestial Circuit.

The formula of this step mainly presents the practice of hibernating storage: using the mind, let the light of the Prior Heaven true breath in front of the Ancestral Opening follow the swallowed saliva into the belly, to hibernate and be sealed in the Cavity of Breath (the lower elixir field); then constantly shine on the elixir field with turning the light around to shine back and the method of “facing the Dipper with the bright star,” the out-breath faint, the in-breath soft and continuous, entering the state of embryonic breathing; after long gazing, at the utmost of stillness, the true breath is sure to stir; a warm current surges in the elixir field and rises little by little to the Crimson Palace below the heart; settle it with the True Intention (the original has 用真一定, see the Translator’s Notes), and the true breath descends again little by little to the elixir field at the position of the kidneys — this is “above, not past the heart; below, not past the kidneys”; after long movement it becomes still again, and all of a sudden the true breath enters the true Cavity of Breath and stays still, and one enters great absorption. This method is extremely simple, but its effect is extraordinary: one reaches the meeting of heart and kidneys, the spirit condensed and the breath gathered, the joining of the light of sun and moon, the mutual completion of water and fire, the conjunction of Kan and Li and the oneness of yin and yang; it is the time for refining the great elixir and the Great Medicine and entering great absorption. In this great absorption, the ancients would sit for seven days at a stretch, or even longer, and abstain from grain.

When the light of wisdom in front of the Ancestral Opening is gathered in and drawn back to the Cavity of Breath, in time the light of wisdom is imprinted on the lower elixir field, like a bright moon reflected in a clear pool; the lower elixir field is no longer pitch dark but faintly gives off light; the more one reaches utmost emptiness and deep stillness, the clearer and brighter the light. There is a popular saying, “facing the Dipper with the bright star”: the Dipper is the Northern Dipper, a metaphor for the elixir field; the bright star is the light emitted within the elixir field. These two lights, above and below, are the so-called “light of sun and moon.” Once these outer lights of sun and moon join, it shows that heart and kidneys, water and fire, Kan and Li, spirit and breath within the body have achieved a further union. At this time the saliva in the mouth is more than one can swallow, one seems to be in a curtain of auspicious, harmonious light, and the whole body feels free-flowing, truly beyond what words can describe; looking back at oneself, it seems one has become hollow and disappeared, or that the whole body has turned white — this is the proof of “the empty room produces whiteness” (from the Zhuangzi: the empty, bright chamber of the heart gives rise to light).

Of all the hundred works of the Daoists, cultivating “absorption” is the first essential. What is “absorption”? It is forgetting both inner and outer, forgetting others and self, forgetting heaven and earth, and entering a state of empty quiet. When absorption goes deep, a whole day and night often seems only an instant. If one can be absorbed, stillness is naturally contained in it. Cultivating stillness is manageable; cultivating absorption is hardest, and there are those who have practiced for many years and still cannot enter absorption. Elixir practice must have absorption to take effect; if one cannot reach a still breath and an idle spirit, thoughts stopped and the heart settled, all efforts are of no benefit. Daoist elixir refining cannot succeed without fire, and fire is born of absorption, so absorption is the first foundation for entering the Way; gathering the heart and refining inner nature is also cultivating the power of absorption. Once the power of absorption is achieved, opening the passes and unfolding the openings and the other works can all be accomplished if one knows the methods. But for ordinary people and sages alike, gathering the heart is hardest, and so stillness and absorption are hardest to attain. The Daoist school has a four-word formula, “heart and breath depend on each other,” or “spirit and breath embrace each other,” and the method in either case is carried out through concentrating the spirit and entering the Cavity of Breath. For by concentrating the spirit and quietly illuminating the Cavity of Breath, the original breath becomes sufficient of itself and the original yang is born of itself; as each part of yang is born, a part of yin wanes; when yang is complete and yin exhausted, one can increase one’s life and lengthen one’s years. And to have yang born, nothing will do but absorption. This is the work that runs from the very beginning to the very end of the elixir path.

What the eight steps of practice so far have trained all belongs to “lesser absorption.” Only when one has trained to the point where there is no breathing through mouth and nose is it “great absorption.” Some say lesser absorption is one day, middle absorption three days, great absorption seven days; others say lesser absorption is seven days and great absorption several tens of days. The Old Man of Qianfeng said: great absorption is “stillness and absorption at their utmost, unconscious of everything, the breath entirely gone, the six pulses all stopped.” When practice enters great absorption, there must be a companion to watch over one.

Key Terms

  • Hibernating storage: like an animal’s winter sleep, drawing the gathered breath of the Yang Spirit into the Cavity of Breath and storing it deep.
  • Entering the opening within the opening: after the true breath has gone back and forth between the positions of the heart and the kidneys, it suddenly drops into the Cavity of Breath and stays still; “re-entering the womb and re-creating Qian and Kun.”
  • Above, not past the heart; below, not past the kidneys: the range within which the inner breathing and the intention move back and forth during hibernating storage.
  • Outer opening, inner opening: the two layers of the Cavity of Breath; the outer opening is the source of yin and yang and the ancestor of breathing, the inner opening the chamber for nourishing the embryo, stopping the breath and entering great absorption.
  • Seven treasures: nasal mucus, spittle, saliva, essence, breath, blood and fluids.
  • Harmonizing, uniting, condensing, gathering: the yin of the heart obtaining the yang of the kidneys is harmonizing (mercury coming to lead); the yang of the kidneys receiving the yin of the heart is uniting (lead coming to mercury); guarding the Ancestral Opening and concentrating the spirit in the Cavity of Breath is condensing; distinguishing the periods and gathering the medicine when yang is born is gathering.
  • Metal Father, Wood Mother: sun and moon, the two eyes; also lead as Metal Father and mercury as Lovely Maiden.
  • Yellow Dame: the True Intention, the “go-between” who brings Kan and Li, wu and ji together.
  • First, second, third period: in the first period the medicine is obtained (tender); in the second, “when the medicine is produced, the spirit knows,” the right moment to gather (gathering the mani); in the third the breath has already leaked and the medicine is old.
  • Three Carts: the formula for turning the River Chariot, enabling the true breath to pass the three passes of the Caudal Funnel, the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Jade Pillow in an instant.
  • Lesser absorption, great absorption: the first eight steps are lesser absorption; with no breathing through mouth and nose and the breath entirely gone, it is great absorption (Qiu Ling’s note).
  • The empty room produces whiteness: the empty, bright chamber of the heart gives rise to light; Qiu Ling uses it to describe the proof of the elixir field shining and the whole body turning white.
  • Facing the Dipper with the bright star: the eyes shine on the elixir field (the Dipper), and light is emitted within the elixir field (the bright star).

Putting It into Practice

  1. Just “store” it: the heart of this step is to settle the spirit and breath one has drawn back in the lower elixir field, the gaze and the intention shining on it lightly, “above, not past the heart; below, not past the kidneys,” and let it become still by itself.
  2. Don’t crave pleasant sensations: Zhao warns clearly that the state of melting bliss in stillness must not be craved — “if one falls into deviant views, there is sure to be the danger of losing one’s way.”
  3. Right thought matters most: when regulating the medicine “there should be nothing but right thought”; with stray or deviant thoughts, the medicine turns into dregs.
  4. Reject deviant teachings: Zhao takes particular care to make clear that “in every home” means everyone has it within his own body, not the deviant teaching of gathering and replenishing from another person.
  5. Absorption is the root: Qiu Ling says “of all the hundred works of the Daoists, cultivating absorption is the first essential”; heart and breath depending on each other, spirit and breath embracing each other, all start from concentrating the spirit and entering the Cavity of Breath.
  6. Fasting and great absorption: “seven days without food” and “forty-nine days without food” are ancient accounts that people today must on no account imitate; deep absorption requires someone to watch over you.

Translator's Notes

  1. In the original, this step has one formula, one figure, three passages of explanation and four questions and answers, followed by the words of Kuiyizi and several teachers; we number them §1–§12. §12 is a single natural paragraph in the original, containing the words of three teachers: Peng Maochang, Tan Zhiming and Liu Yunpu.
  2. “Kuiyizi” is Zhao Bichen’s elder brother (his words are quoted several times in Step Seven).
  3. “The 元关 at once has a mechanism of sudden opening”: 元关 is 玄关 (the Mysterious Pass); in the Qing dynasty 玄 was replaced by 元 to avoid the taboo on the Kangxi emperor’s personal name (Xuanye); reproduced as is.
  4. “If true, it does not 座 in the least” (真则丝毫不座): the character 座 is as in the original; from the sense it should be something like “does not give way” or “does not wilt”; reproduced as is, and translated as “does not go down in the least.”
  5. “The two gui unite into one” (二圭合一): the character 圭 is made of two 土 (“earth”) characters, referring to the two earths wu and ji.
  6. In Qiu Ling’s note, “settle it with the true One” (用真一定) should be “settle it with the True Intention” (用真意一定), with the character 意 dropped; reproduced as is.
  7. “Old illnesses of 存年” (存年旧病): so in the original; the gist is old illnesses of many years.
  8. Xu Jingyang is Xu Xun of the Eastern Jin; “the empty room produces whiteness” comes from the chapter “In the World of Men” of the Zhuangzi.
  9. “Man is born at yin”: there is an ancient saying, “Heaven opened at zi, earth unfolded at chou, man was born at yin”; Zhao borrows it to explain the rising of yang at the yin hour.
