The Alchemical Formulas of the Old Man of Qianfeng, Annotated

千峰老人丹功法诀注释 · Edited and annotated by Qiu Ling · Higher Education Press · 1992

The original book is Clear Directions on the Methods and Formulas of Nature and Life, written by the Old Man of Qianfeng, Zhao Bichen (1860–1942, Daoist name Shunyizi, from Changping near Beijing), when he was over seventy, and published in 1933. Zhao Bichen studied under more than thirty teachers, received the true transmission of the Wu-Liu school (the orthodox Dragon Gate line), also learned from the Nanwu school, and with his elder brother Zhao Kuiyi founded the Qianfeng branch. The book spells out, step by step, the formulas for setting to work that the Wu-Liu classics never state openly — sixteen steps in all, from settling the spirit in the Ancestral Opening to the form appearing in empty space, each with an illustration, a formula and an explanation — and among the alchemical books of all ages it is known for "speaking plainly without concealment." In Hong Kong in 1988 Mr. Qiu Ling removed the passages that forced old-fashioned anatomical terms onto the text, corrected the punctuation, added notes to each step, and renamed it The Alchemical Formulas of the Old Man of Qianfeng, Annotated.

How to read

  1. Every step follows the same order: About This Chapter → Original Text and Translation → Key Terms → Putting It into Practice → Translator's Notes. The classical Chinese original is folded away; you can read the translation alone and follow everything.
  2. Each step opens with Zhao Bichen's formula, followed by his explanation (often quoting his teachers Liaoran, Liaokong and others), and finally Qiu Ling's note.
  3. In the original book each step opens with an illustration; this site has redrawn them after the originals.
  4. Zhao Bichen's methods lean toward concrete, physical ways of setting to work, and Qiu Ling's notes repeatedly warn about constitution and safety — please read them too. Passages marked "Safety note" are for understanding only; do not imitate them on your own.
