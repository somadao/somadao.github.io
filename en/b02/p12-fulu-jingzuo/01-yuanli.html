Appendix: Quiet Sitting for Health · 原理

Principles

Appendix, Quiet Sitting for Health, section 1 · Principles — the root of human life, the relation between quiet sitting and the mind, the signs of a settled body and mind

About This Chapter

Xu Zhenyi first explains “why”, in three layers:

  1. The root of human life is at the navel. The fetus is joined to the mother by the umbilical cord, so we know that human life begins at the navel; cultivating this root depends on the working of the mind and intention. Quiet sitting is “gathering my mind and intention and focusing it on a point below the navel, so that it becomes settled”.
  2. The mind can govern the body. Blushing with shame, hair turning white from worry, joy and anger affecting appetite and the blood — all are examples. When the human mind is at war within and lured by things without, the mind and spirit scatter, and the body follows all kinds of cravings, and so a hundred illnesses arise; quiet sitting can gather the spirit of the whole body into one — “when the heavenly sovereign is at peace, the hundred members obey its command”.
  3. What a settled body and mind is like. The body and breath are calm and harmonious, without thought or worry; the mind and intention are silent, focused on one point, like a bright moon hanging in the sky.

Original Text and Translation

§1The root of human life
Original (Chinese)

人之初生，由女子之卵细胞，与男子之精细胞，结合而成胎。胎在母体中，其初生也，一端为胎儿，一端为胞衣，而中间联以脐带。孕育十月，至脱胎以后，而脐带方落。以此推之，可知人类胎生之始，必始于脐。脐即为其根本，培养此根本，当以心意之作用为之。静坐者，即使吾心意凝聚之灵力，以影响生理也。

人生之根本在脐。往古有道之士，盖早知之，故有修养丹田之法。丹田者，亦名气海，在脐下腹部也。悲哉世俗之人，不知反求其根本，而安定其身心，终日营营，神明憧扰，致心性失其和平，官骸不能从令，疾病灾厄，于焉乘之。殊可悯已！静坐之法，浅言之，乃凝集吾之心意，注于脐下之一点，使之安定。行持既久，由勉强几于自然，于是全身细胞，悉皆听命，烦恼不生，悦怿无量。儒家之主静，老氏之抱一，佛家之禅定，命名各异，究其实，无非求身心之安定而已。

When a human being is first formed, the female egg cell and the male sperm cell unite to make an embryo. In the mother’s body, when the embryo first grows, at one end is the fetus and at the other the afterbirth (the placenta), joined in the middle by the umbilical cord. After ten months of gestation, only after birth does the umbilical cord fall off. From this we can infer that the beginning of human life in the womb must begin at the navel. The navel is a person’s root; cultivating this root should be done through the working of the mind and intention. Quiet sitting means using the numinous power gathered by our mind and intention to influence our physiology.

The root of human life is at the navel. People of the Way in ancient times knew this long ago, which is why there are methods of cultivating the elixir field. The elixir field, also called the Sea of Breath, is in the belly below the navel. How sad that ordinary people do not know to turn back and seek their own root and settle their body and mind; busy all day long, their minds agitated and restless, their mind and nature lose their peace, the parts of the body no longer obey, and illness and misfortune take advantage of this. It is truly pitiable! The method of quiet sitting, put simply, is to gather our mind and intention and focus it on a point below the navel, so that it becomes settled. When one has practiced for a long time, going from effort to something close to naturalness, all the cells of the whole body obey, vexations do not arise, and joy is boundless. The Confucian “making stillness the master”, Laozi’s “embracing the One”, the Buddhist “meditative concentration” — the names differ, but in substance they are nothing but seeking the settling of body and mind.

§2The relation between quiet sitting and the mind
Original (Chinese)

人身有肉体与精神两方面，而其不可思议处，多在精神方面。概而论之，其例实多。愧耻内蕴，则颜为之赤。忧愁终夜，则发为之白。此精神之影响于形体一也。愉快时，则五官之所见所闻皆美；悲哀时则否。此精神之影响于形体二也。快感起时，则食欲增进；不快之时，则食欲减少。此精神之影响于肠胃也。忿怒嫉妒等不正感情起时，能使血液及各部组织中，发生毒素。此精神之影响于血液也。至若催眠术之利用暗示，使被术者执炽热之火箸，而告之曰：“不热”，执者即不觉其苦，并肌肤不变者，其例又不胜枚举也。精神之能左右肉体，从可知矣。

世人不知此义，心战于内，物诱于外，全体精神，既妄想颠倒，涣散而不统一，不能宰制肉体，于是肉体则徇种种嗜好，戕贼其生机。心与形日离，遂生百病，甚且夭折，比比然也。静坐者，能萃全身精神而统于一，天君泰然，百体从令，自然体气和平，却病延年。一者何，即神气混融，身心安定也。

The human being has two sides, body and mind, and what is inconceivable lies mostly on the side of the mind. Speaking generally, the examples are very many. Shame within, and the face blushes; worry all night, and the hair turns white — this is the first way the mind affects the body. When one is happy, everything the senses see and hear is beautiful; when one is sad, it is not — this is the second way the mind affects the body. When pleasant feelings arise, appetite increases; when one is displeased, appetite decreases — this is the mind affecting the stomach and intestines. When improper emotions such as anger and jealousy arise, they can produce toxins in the blood and the tissues of the body — this is the mind affecting the blood. As for hypnotism, which uses suggestion to have the subject hold red-hot fire tongs and tells him “they are not hot”, so that he feels no pain and even his skin is unchanged — such examples are too many to list. That the mind can govern the body is clear from this.

People do not understand this; the mind is at war within and lured by things without, and the whole spirit, deluded and topsy-turvy, scattered and not unified, cannot master the body; and so the body follows all kinds of cravings and harms its own vitality. As mind and form drift further apart day by day, a hundred illnesses arise, even early death, and this is everywhere to be seen. Quiet sitting can gather the spirit of the whole body and unify it in one — “when the heavenly sovereign is at peace, the hundred members obey its command” — and naturally body and breath become calm and harmonious, illness is warded off and life is lengthened. What is the “one”? It is spirit and breath merging, body and mind settled.

§3The signs of a settled body and mind in quiet sitting
Original (Chinese)

身心之安定，前既言之。然静坐时如何现象，不可不一述。身心安定之内界，体气和平，无思无虑，心意寂然，注于一点，如皓月悬空，洁净无滓。吕新吾曰：“静定中境界，与六合一般大，里面空空寂寂，无一个事物。”是为身心安定之内象，惟静坐可以得之，其妙有不可言喻者。

The settling of body and mind has been spoken of above. But what appears during quiet sitting must also be described. The inner world of a settled body and mind is this: body and breath calm and harmonious, without thought or worry, the mind and intention silent, focused on one point, like a bright moon hanging in the sky, pure and without a speck of sediment. Lü Xinwu says: “The state within stillness and concentration is as large as the six directions; inside it is empty and silent, without a single thing.” This is the inner sign of a settled body and mind, which only quiet sitting can bring, and its wonder is beyond words.

Key Terms

  • Elixir field, Sea of Breath: in the belly below the navel; see lower Cinnabar Field.
  • Making stillness the master, embracing the One, meditative concentration: the Confucian, Daoist and Buddhist names for the same thing; see guarding the center and embracing the One.
  • “When the heavenly sovereign is at peace, the hundred members obey its command”: when the mind is settled, the whole body obeys it. The “heavenly sovereign” is the mind.
  • Spirit and breath merging: the “one” that quiet sitting seeks to unify.
  • Six directions: above and below, and east, south, west and north.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The core of quiet sitting is a single sentence: gather the mind and intention, focus it on a point below the navel, and let it settle.
  2. From effort to naturalness. At first it takes a little effort; with time it becomes natural.
  3. Emotions directly affect the body. Quiet sitting is first of all about the mind no longer being “at war within”.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original was written in modern times; “egg cell”, “sperm cell”, “toxins”, “hypnotism” and so on are the scientific expressions of its day, translated according to the original meaning, and do not represent today’s medical conclusions.
  2. “Lü Xinwu”: the Ming scholar Lü Kun, whose sobriquet was Xinwu, author of Groaning Words (Shenyin yu).
  3. “When the heavenly sovereign is at peace, the hundred members obey its command”: from the “Admonition on the Mind” by Fan Jun of the Song.
  4. The sections of the original have subheadings, which we follow.
