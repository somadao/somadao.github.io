Xixinzi’s Answers to Students’ Questions · 周天名词问答

Questions on the Terms of the Celestial Circuit

Xixinzi’s Answers to Students’ Questions, section 3 · Questions on the terms of the Celestial Circuit — the self well refined, preserving and illuminating, muni, the handle of the Dipper, four counts per hour, the essentials of running the Lesser Circuit

About This Chapter

This section has forty-three exchanges, and almost all of them ask about the original words of the ninth part of this book, the Sayings of Alchemists through the Ages: “the self well refined”, “preserving and illuminating”, “blowing in reverse into the Cavity of Breath”, “the twelve methods of the Celestial Circuit”, “gathered at movement”, “the moment”, “setting up the handle of the Dipper”, “fixing the lines and the hours”, “four counts per hour”, “single intention on the compass center”, “merging, extracting and adding at the four cardinal points”, “the intercalary remainder”, “then to whom is the breathing entrusted?” … So this section can be read as a “running glossary” to the ninth part.

The most important is §27, “the inner and outer working of the Celestial Circuit”, in which Xixinzi explains the whole circulation of the Lesser Celestial Circuit in a single passage:

PositionZi position (Cavity of Breath)Wu position (Muddy Pellet)
HexagramKun, FuQian, Gou
BodyBellyHead
DirectionYang rises, advancing the yang fire, along the Governing vesselYin descends, withdrawing the yin tally, along the Conception vessel
RouteCaudal Funnel → Spine-Straddling Pass → Jade Pillow → Muddy PelletMuddy Pellet → windpipe → Crimson Palace → Cavity of Breath
DivisionsSix compasses (six hours)Six compasses (six hours)

There are only two essentials to the circulation: first, inner and outer breathing — when the outer breath descends, the inner breath rises; when the outer breath rises, the inner breath descends; second, the true intention sits guarding the Cavity of Breath, and the spirit leads the breath, rising and descending, moving and resting with it.

Original Text and Translation

§1Greater and Lesser Celestial Circuits
Original (Chinese)

问：火候次序论中，何为既了手行周天，周天何为大小？

答：周天者，天之运转也，人身亦如天之运转，到阳气旺时，当周转一次，初转为小，丹熟时一次为大。

Question: In “On the Order of Fire Phasing”, what is “finally one completes the work by carrying out the Celestial Circuit”? What are the greater and lesser Celestial Circuits?

Answer: The Celestial Circuit is the turning of Heaven; the human body too turns like Heaven. When the yang breath is strong, one should turn it round once; the first turnings are “lesser”, and the one turning when the elixir is ripe is “greater”.

§2The self well refined
Original (Chinese)

问：已熟何义？

答：已属土，即意也。熟是纯熟。将心意炼到常清静无妄想时，即已熟也。

Question: What does “the self well refined” mean?

Answer: The self (ji) belongs to earth; it is the intention. “Well refined” means thoroughly practiced. When the mind and intention are refined to the point of being constantly clear and still, without deluded thoughts, that is “the self well refined”.

§3Preserving and illuminating
Original (Chinese)

问：存照何义？

答：存心观照也，又非着相。

Question: What does “preserving and illuminating” mean?

Answer: Preserving the mind and observing and illuminating — but without clinging to forms.

§4The medicine in "only then is the medicine produced"
Original (Chinese)

问：何者为方得药产之药？

答：元精也。

Question: What is the medicine in “only then is the medicine produced”?

Answer: The original essence.

§5Gathering, sealing, circulating
Original (Chinese)

问：当采封运行之采封运，如何解？

答：采，即采药。封，即封固。运行，即运转周天也。

Question: How should “gathering, sealing and circulating” in “one should gather, seal and circulate” be understood?

Answer: Gathering is gathering the medicine; sealing is sealing and securing; circulating is running the Celestial Circuit.

§6Blowing in reverse into the Cavity of Breath
Original (Chinese)

问：何为逆吹气穴？

答：采药运行之时，要内外呼吸之气，反转去向气穴鼓吹也。

Question: What is “blowing in reverse into the Cavity of Breath”?

Answer: When gathering the medicine and circulating it, the breath of inner and outer breathing must be turned back to blow toward the Cavity of Breath.

§7Sealing, ablution, returning to the root
Original (Chinese)

问：封固，沐浴，归根何义？

答：封固者，用文火照顾温养之义。沐浴者，休息温养之义。归根者，归还气穴也。

Question: What do sealing and securing, ablution and returning to the root mean?

Answer: Sealing and securing means looking after it and nourishing it warmly with the civil fire. Ablution means resting and nourishing warmly. Returning to the root is returning to the Cavity of Breath.

§8*Muni*
Original (Chinese)

问：什么叫牟尼？

答：牟尼为至贵宝珠之名，即金丹之别名也。

Question: What is called muni?

Answer: Muni is the name of a most precious jewel-pearl; it is another name for the Golden Elixir.

§9Gathering from outside back to the furnace
Original (Chinese)

问：往外采归炉，外者何义？

答：阳气到阳关，则从阳关外将阳气收回气穴也。

Question: In “gathering from outside back to the furnace”, what does “outside” mean?

Answer: When the yang breath reaches the Yang Pass, one brings the yang breath back from outside the Yang Pass to the Cavity of Breath.

§10The twelve methods of the Celestial Circuit
Original (Chinese)

问：什么叫周天十二法？

答：看法轮六候图上升六规，降下六规，即十二法也，又在小周天始终论中谈到。

Question: What are called the twelve methods of the Celestial Circuit?

Answer: Look at the six rising compasses and the six descending compasses on the diagram of the six stages of the Dharma Wheel — these are the twelve methods; they are also discussed in “General Discussion of the Lesser Celestial Circuit from Beginning to End”.

§11Gathered at movement
Original (Chinese)

问：至动而取，何解？

答：至阳气发动时，取此阳气也。

Question: How should “gathered at movement” be understood?

Answer: When the yang breath stirs, take this yang breath.

§12The moment
Original (Chinese)

问：何谓机缄？

答：神气交媾也。

Question: What is called jijian, the moment?

Answer: The union of spirit and breath.

§13Nature and life smelted together
Original (Chinese)

问：性命双熔何义？

答：即神与气合一也。

Question: What does “nature and life smelted together” mean?

Answer: It is the spirit and the breath becoming one.

§14Old and young in the production of the medicine
Original (Chinese)

问：什么叫药产老嫩？

答：静候阳气发动到旺时采取，为得宜。

Question: What is called old and young in the production of the medicine?

Answer: To wait in stillness until the yang breath stirs and becomes strong, and then gather it, is right.

§15The breath of the medicine rushing outward
Original (Chinese)

问：何谓药气外驰？

答：阳气有向外走之势也。

Question: What is “the breath of the medicine rushing outward”?

Answer: The yang breath has a tendency to go outward.

§16The scenes of the medicine being produced arriving
Original (Chinese)

问：何谓药产景到？

答：阳气生时，阳兴即景也。

Question: What is “the scenes of the medicine being produced arriving”?

Answer: When the yang breath is born and the yang rises — that is the scene.

§17Setting up the handle of the Dipper
Original (Chinese)

问：立斗柄，何意？

答：斗柄者，北斗之柄也。身中斗柄，即真意也。

Question: What does “setting up the handle of the Dipper” mean?

Answer: The handle of the Dipper is the handle of the Northern Dipper. The handle of the Dipper in the body is the true intention.

§18River Chariot
Original (Chinese)

问：河车何意？

答：周天转运，如河车旋转流动之意。

Question: What does River Chariot mean?

Answer: The turning of the Celestial Circuit is like a water-wheel turning and the water flowing.

§19Blowing in reverse, faint and slow
Original (Chinese)

问：逆吹微缓何意？

答：用内呼吸在内，微微吹嘘也。

Question: What does “blowing in reverse, faint and slow” mean?

Answer: Using the inner breathing within, blowing very faintly.

§20Fixing the lines, the hours, the degrees and the positions
Original (Chinese)

问：为爻为时，为度为位，何意？

答：运周天时，以息数为爻为时为度为位也。

Question: What does “fixing the lines, the hours, the degrees and the positions” mean?

Answer: When running the Celestial Circuit, the count of breaths is used to fix the lines, the hours, the degrees and the positions.

§21Four counts per hour
Original (Chinese)

问：每时四揲多少时？

答：阳六时，阴六时，共十二时也。

Question: In “four counts per hour”, how many hours are there?

Answer: Six yang hours and six yin hours, twelve hours in all.

§22The number of breaths fixes the number of hours
Original (Chinese)

问：何谓以息数定时数？

答：以呼吸之数，定时刻之数。

Question: What is “the number of breaths fixes the number of hours”?

Answer: The number of breaths is used to fix the number of hours and quarters.

§23The tripod and furnace, the path; the medicinal ingredients, the fire phasing
Original (Chinese)

问：鼎炉道路、药物火候，何解？

答：运周天由任督两脉经过，即鼎炉道路。阳气由督脉上升，任脉下降，即药物火候。

Question: How should “the tripod and furnace, the path; the medicinal ingredients, the fire phasing” be understood?

Answer: Running the Celestial Circuit passes through the Conception and Governing vessels — that is the tripod and furnace and the path. The yang breath rising along the Governing vessel and descending along the Conception vessel — that is the medicinal ingredients and the fire phasing.

§24Gathering in the morning and refining in the evening
Original (Chinese)

问：朝采暮炼，何解？

答：日日行功不间也。

Question: How should “gather in the morning and refine in the evening” be understood?

Answer: Practicing every day without interruption.

§25Coming out of concentration in thousands of millions of transformation bodies
Original (Chinese)

问：出定千百亿化身何义？

答：此炼气化神功圆，由大静定中色身内，现出法身，从顶门出去，变化多端也。

Question: What does “coming out of concentration in thousands of millions of transformation bodies” mean?

Answer: When the work of refining breath into spirit is complete, out of great stillness and concentration, within the physical body, the Dharma body appears, goes out through the gate at the top of the head, and transforms in many ways.

§26The five phases turned upside down
Original (Chinese)

问：五行颠倒运于其中，何意？

答：人身五行之气，习惯顺行，运转周天为逆行，即是颠倒。

Question: What does “making the five phases turn upside down and circulate within” mean?

Answer: The breaths of the five phases in the human body habitually go along the ordinary course; running the Celestial Circuit goes in reverse — that is “upside down”.

§27The inner and outer working of the Celestial Circuit — the essentials of running the Lesser Celestial Circuit
Original (Chinese)

问：周天内外机动，以下难明所以，何意？

答：此言运转小周天之法，从中辩论精详，初学难知，兹举其要领。如行功到精化气足时，则活子发动，气穴即为子位，为坤卦，为复卦，为腹，为阳升，为进阳火，为督脉，中有六规，即六时，爻度位也。运转时，以神引气，由督脉上升，经过之处，自尾闾，到夹脊，上玉枕，入泥丸。泥丸宫为午位，为乾卦，为姤卦，为首，为阴降，为退阴符，为任脉，中亦有六规，即六时，爻度位也，阳气自泥丸入任督脉下降，经过气管绛宫，仍落气穴，复还本位。运行小周天之大概如此。运行之要法，一为内外呼吸，即柳华阳所谓外面气息降，则里面气息升，外面气息升，则里面气息降也；一为运行时，须真意坐守气穴，又须以神引气，随之升降行住也。任督二脉图，那是运小周天之道路，法轮六候图，是将运行道路上之规矩，详细表明。二图皆指运小周天一事，可以说是一图。

Question: “The inner and outer working of the Celestial Circuit” — from here on I cannot understand why. What does it mean?

Answer: This speaks of the method of running the Lesser Celestial Circuit; the discussion of it is detailed and precise, and hard for beginners to understand, so I give its essentials here. When the work reaches the point where the essence is transformed and the breath is full, the Living Zi stirs; the Cavity of Breath is then the zi position, the Kun hexagram, the Fu hexagram, the belly, the rising of yang, advancing the yang fire, the Governing vessel, with six compasses in it, which are six hours — the degrees and positions of the lines. When turning it, the spirit leads the breath up along the Governing vessel, passing from the Caudal Funnel to the Spine-Straddling Pass, up to the Jade Pillow and into the Muddy Pellet. The Muddy Pellet palace is the wu position, the Qian hexagram, the Gou hexagram, the head, the descent of yin, withdrawing the yin tally, the Conception vessel, which also has six compasses in it, which are six hours — the degrees and positions of the lines. The yang breath enters from the Muddy Pellet into the Conception (and Governing) vessel and descends, passing the windpipe and the Crimson Palace, and settles again in the Cavity of Breath, returning to its original position. This is the general outline of running the Lesser Celestial Circuit. The essential methods of the circulation are: first, inner and outer breathing — what Liu Huayang calls “when the outer breath descends, the inner breath rises; when the outer breath rises, the inner breath descends”; second, during the circulation, the true intention must sit guarding the Cavity of Breath, and the spirit must lead the breath, rising and descending, moving and resting with it. The diagram of the Conception and Governing vessels is the path of running the Lesser Celestial Circuit; the diagram of the six stages of the Dharma Wheel shows in detail the rules along this path. Both diagrams refer to the single matter of running the Lesser Celestial Circuit; one could say they are one diagram.

§28Obtaining the medicine
Original (Chinese)

问：得药何义？

答：道书多反复详言，凡见有名词字者，即指此也，书中凡言药，皆指元精元气也。得者，收到也。

Question: What does “obtaining the medicine” mean?

Answer: The books of the Way discuss it repeatedly and in detail; whenever you see this term, it refers to this; wherever the books speak of the medicine, they all mean the original essence and original breath. “Obtaining” means receiving.

§29Returning to the original position
Original (Chinese)

问：还复本位何意？

答：小周天转毕，仍旧复还气穴也。

Question: What does “returning to the original position” mean?

Answer: When the Lesser Celestial Circuit has been completed, it returns again to the Cavity of Breath.

§30The Penetrating Vessel and the Belt Vessel
Original (Chinese)

问：冲脉，带脉在何处？

答：冲脉在下部中间，带脉在腰。

Question: Where are the Penetrating Vessel and the Belt Vessel?

Answer: The Penetrating Vessel is in the middle of the lower part; the Belt Vessel is at the waist.

§31The Yang Pass
Original (Chinese)

问：阳关在何处？

答：泄精处也。

Question: Where is the Yang Pass?

Answer: Where the essence is emitted.

§32The Mysterious Pass
Original (Chinese)

问：玄关何意？

答：玄关者，求玄之关窍，玄妙之机关也。气发则成窍，机息则渺茫，此窍在身中去求。

Question: What does the Mysterious Pass mean?

Answer: The Mysterious Pass is the gateway for seeking the mystery, the mechanism of the mysterious and wondrous. When the breath stirs, it becomes an opening; when the mechanism rests, it is vague and boundless. This opening must be sought within the body.

§33Single intention on the compass center
Original (Chinese)

问：一意规中何解？

答：规中者，气穴也。一意者，真意也。

Question: How should “single intention on the compass center” be understood?

Answer: The compass center is the Cavity of Breath. The single intention is the true intention.

§34The beginning of the *zi* hour of running the Celestial Circuit
Original (Chinese)

问：何谓运周天子时之头？

答：气穴即是运周天子时之起头处。

Question: What is “the beginning of the zi hour of running the Celestial Circuit”?

Answer: The Cavity of Breath is the starting place of the zi hour of running the Celestial Circuit.

§35The yang fire and the yin tally
Original (Chinese)

问：何谓阳火阴符？

答：从督脉升上去为阳火，从任脉降下来为阴符。

Question: What are the yang fire and the yin tally?

Answer: Rising up along the Governing vessel is the yang fire; descending along the Conception vessel is the yin tally.

§36Four counts per step
Original (Chinese)

问：何谓每步四揲？

答：步，即法轮图上规也。十二规，即十二步，每步以四揲为限。

Question: What is “four counts per step”?

Answer: A step is a compass on the diagram of the Dharma Wheel. The twelve compasses are the twelve steps, and each step is limited to four counts.

§37Merging, extracting and adding at the four cardinal points
Original (Chinese)

问：要将四正融抽补，何义？

答：四正者，子午卯酉也，此四时皆沐浴处，须融会抽去后天武火，以行自然之熏蒸也。

Question: What does “one must merge, extract and add at the four cardinal points” mean?

Answer: The four cardinal points are zi, wu, mao and you; these four hours are all places of ablution, where one must merge and extract the Posterior Heaven martial fire so as to carry out natural steaming.

§38The Wind Palace, the Crimson Palace, the Purple Court
Original (Chinese)

问：风府、绛宫、紫庭在何处？

答：风府穴，在脑后。绛宫，紫庭二穴，在胸前。

Question: Where are the Wind Palace, the Crimson Palace and the Purple Court?

Answer: The Wind Palace point is at the back of the head. The Crimson Palace and Purple Court points are on the front of the chest.

§39"The breath surges through and the joints open; naturally the essence is full and the valley spirit remains"
Original (Chinese)

问：何谓气冲关节透，自然精满谷神存？

答：谷神者，元神也，言先天气满足，则冲关透节，从此元精足，则元神亦长存矣。

Question: What is “the breath surges through and the joints open; naturally the essence is full and the valley spirit remains”?

Answer: The valley spirit is the original spirit. It says that when the Prior Heaven breath is full, it breaks through the passes and opens the joints; from then on the original essence is sufficient, and the original spirit also endures.

§40The Great Medicine, the Root of Heaven, the intercalary remainder
Original (Chinese)

问：大药，天根，闰余何义？

答：大药、即金丹。天根，即乾顶泥丸宫处。闰余者，休息温养之义。

Question: What do the Great Medicine, the Root of Heaven and the intercalary remainder mean?

Answer: The Great Medicine is the Golden Elixir. The Root of Heaven is the Muddy Pellet palace at the Qian summit. The intercalary remainder means resting and nourishing warmly.

§41The Great Medicine is pure Qian and can be gathered
Original (Chinese)

问：止火说中，大药纯乾可采，何义？

答：乾卦纯阳，言金丹大药，已到纯粹之乾阳时，可以采取矣。

Question: In “On Stopping the Fire”, what does “the Great Medicine is pure Qian and can be gathered” mean?

Answer: The Qian hexagram is pure yang; it means that when the Great Medicine of the Golden Elixir has reached the stage of pure Qian yang, it can be gathered.

§42Breathing and blowing
Original (Chinese)

问：且道呼吸寄向谁之呼吸，与吹嘘是否相同？

答：呼吸及吹嘘义同。

Question: Is the breathing in “then to whom is the breathing entrusted?” the same as blowing?

Answer: Breathing and blowing have the same meaning.

§43The red sun and the bright moon
Original (Chinese)

问：《百句章》中，红日皓月如何解释？

答：日月者，即先天中阴阳二气之本象也。始而月现，阴也；继而日现，阳也。日月一合，即先天阴阳交媾也。

Question: How should the red sun and the bright moon in the Hundred-Line Chapter be explained?

Answer: The sun and moon are the original images of the two breaths of yin and yang within the Prior Heaven. First the moon appears — yin; then the sun appears — yang. When sun and moon join, that is the union of Prior Heaven yin and yang.

Key Terms

  • Lesser and Greater Celestial Circuits: the first turnings are lesser, the one turning when the elixir is ripe is greater; see Celestial Circuit.
  • The self well refined: the self (ji) belongs to earth, that is, the intention; the mind and intention refined to be constantly clear and still, without deluded thoughts.
  • Preserving and illuminating: preserving the mind and observing and illuminating without clinging to forms; see Reverting the Light.
  • Gathering, sealing, circulating: gathering the medicine, sealing and securing, running the Celestial Circuit.
  • Blowing in reverse into the Cavity of Breath, blowing in reverse faint and slow: turning the breath of inner and outer breathing back to blow toward the Cavity of Breath; the inner breathing blowing very faintly. See Wind of Xun.
  • Sealing, ablution, returning to the root: nourishing warmly with the civil fire; resting and nourishing warmly; returning to the Cavity of Breath. See ablution.
  • Muni: a most precious jewel-pearl, another name for the Golden Elixir; see Golden Flower.
  • The twelve methods of the Celestial Circuit, the twelve compasses: the six rising and six descending compasses on the diagram of the six stages of the Dharma Wheel.
  • The moment (jijian): the union of spirit and breath.
  • Nature and life smelted together: the spirit and the breath becoming one.
  • Handle of the Dipper: the handle of the Dipper in the body is the true intention; see Handle of the Dipper.
  • Four counts per hour, four counts per step: “hour” and “step” both refer to the “compass” on the Dharma Wheel diagram, a point on the route of circulation — six rising positions and six descending, twelve in all, each limited to four counts (four breaths); for how the four counts are counted, see Jingxuzi’s explanation under §3 of the third section of the ninth part.
  • Coming out of concentration in thousands of millions of transformation bodies: when the work of refining breath into spirit is complete, the Dharma body goes out through the gate at the top of the head; see spontaneous Dharma Body.
  • The five phases turned upside down: going along the ordinary course is the norm; running the Celestial Circuit goes in reverse; see the method of reversal.
  • Zi position and wu position: the Cavity of Breath is zi (Kun, Fu, belly, the rising of yang, the Governing vessel); the Muddy Pellet is wu (Qian, Gou, head, the descent of yin, the Conception vessel).
  • Inner and outer breathing: when the outer breath descends the inner rises; when the outer rises the inner descends.
  • Penetrating Vessel, Belt Vessel: the Penetrating Vessel is in the middle of the lower part, the Belt Vessel at the waist; see Penetrating Vessel and Belt Vessel.
  • Yang Pass: where the essence is emitted; see outer kidney.
  • Mysterious Pass: when the breath stirs, it becomes an opening; when the mechanism rests, it is vague and boundless; this opening must be sought within the body. See Mysterious Opening / Mysterious Pass.
  • Compass center: the Cavity of Breath. Xixinzi uses “compass center” for the Cavity of Breath, unlike other books that use it for the central palace.
  • Four cardinal points: zi, wu, mao and you, all places of ablution, where the Posterior Heaven martial fire is extracted and natural steaming is carried out.
  • Valley spirit: the original spirit.
  • Root of Heaven: the Muddy Pellet palace at the Qian summit; intercalary remainder: resting and nourishing warmly.
  • Red sun and bright moon: the original images of the two Prior Heaven breaths of yin and yang; when sun and moon join, that is the union of Prior Heaven yin and yang. See Union of sun and moon.

Putting It into Practice

  1. The numbers and diagrams are rules, not shackles. The diagram of the six stages of the Dharma Wheel only marks the rules along the path; “the number of breaths fixes the number of hours” gives the mind a rhythm.
  2. In running the Celestial Circuit, hold on to just two things: inner and outer breathing, one rising and one descending; and the true intention sitting guarding the Cavity of Breath, the spirit leading the breath.
  3. Ablution is resting and nourishing warmly. At the four cardinal points the Posterior Heaven martial fire is extracted and the breath is allowed to steam naturally.
  4. The Mysterious Pass must be sought within the body. When the breath stirs, it becomes an opening; when the mechanism rests, it is vague and boundless — do not look for it outside the body.

  2. §2: the original book prints “已熟何义？答：已属土” with 已 (“already”); from the meaning it should be 己 (ji, the self — the ji of the heavenly stems wu and ji, which the alchemical books take as earth and as the intention); we translate it as 己.
  3. §27, “enters from the Muddy Pellet into the Conception and Governing vessels and descends”: so in the original; from the context it enters from the Muddy Pellet into the Conception vessel and descends, and we translate accordingly.
  4. “The diagram of the six stages of the Dharma Wheel”, “the diagram of the Conception and Governing vessels”: attached to the ninth part in the original book; in line with our established principle, the diagrams are not reproduced for now.
