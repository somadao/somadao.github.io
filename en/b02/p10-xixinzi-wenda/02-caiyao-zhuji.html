Gathering the Medicine and Laying the Foundation

Xixinzi’s Answers to Students’ Questions, section 2 · Gathering the medicine and laying the foundation — the four-word formula, old and young, the yinqiao, the scenes of stillness, the exact place of the Cavity of Breath

About This Chapter

This section has thirty-three exchanges, mostly about the terms of “gathering the medicine” and “laying the foundation”: when to gather, what is gathered, where it is brought back from, and where it is stored. Two of them are longer and the most worth reading:

  • “Please show me the scenes of stillness and their effects” (§11): it describes the whole road from stillness to laying the foundation — only when all ties are put down can one speak of stillness; only when stillness reaches the dim and obscure does the true breath already depleted in the body have a source from which to be replenished; at the extreme of stillness yang is born, in time yang becomes strong, the elixir field grows warm, and only when it is sufficient can the River Chariot first move; “laying the foundation” and “repairing leaks” are both metaphors.
  • “Where exactly is the cavity?” (§18): it gives the exact location of the Cavity of Breath — “seven in front and three behind” is an ancient method, and “there is no need to cling to it”; do not be rigid about exact measurements; simply observing and illuminating the general area in the middle is best, otherwise one clings to forms. It is followed by the formula of the Method of Gathering the Mind: “The mind must not be too strict … the mind must not be too scattered … neither forgetting nor helping is just right. The mind empty, with the breath to rely on and follow.”

Reading tip: what this section teaches is the Wu-Liu school’s alchemy for men, often taking “the outer yang rising” as the sign that the yang breath is stirring; we translate according to the original meaning.

Original Text and Translation

§1When to use the four-word formula
Original (Chinese)

问：四字诀吸、舐、撮、闭当用于何时？

答：此诀系用在运周天之时，非采微阳时之法。

Question: When should the four-word formula — inhaling, pressing the tongue up, drawing in, closing — be used?

Answer: This formula is used when running the Celestial Circuit; it is not the method for gathering the faint yang.

§2Welcoming it back to the furnace
Original (Chinese)

问：从阴跻迎归炉，或十迎，或数十迎，外阳倒则止，是否采取送入丹田之法？

答：是。

Question: “Welcoming it back to the furnace from the yinqiao, ten times or several dozen times, stopping when the outer yang subsides” — is this the method of gathering it and sending it into the elixir field?

Answer: Yes.

§3The moment for gathering
Original (Chinese)

问：采取法，是否外阳初举时下功，抑壮旺时下功？

答：外阳举，即是气欲化精，以始举始采为嫩。若壮旺，则又老而将浊矣。当斟酌于不老不嫩之间为宜。

Question: In the method of gathering, does one begin work when the outer yang first rises, or when it is strong?

Answer: The outer yang rising means the breath is about to turn into essence; gathering it as soon as it begins to rise is “young”. If it is strong, it is already “old” and about to become turbid. It is best to judge it between not old and not young.

§4Original essence and turbid essence
Original (Chinese)

问：阳时举时是元精，抑浊精？

答：无念而举为元，淫念而举为浊。

Question: When the yang rises, is it original essence or turbid essence?

Answer: Rising without a thought is original; rising from a lustful thought is turbid.

§5The *zi* hour of the day and the Living Zi Hour
Original (Chinese)

问：日子时与活子时，如何分别？

答：日子时，是日夜钟表上有定之子时。人身中阳气生则阳举，为活动无定之子时，故名活子时。

Question: How does one distinguish the zi hour of the day from the Living Zi Hour?

Answer: The zi hour of the day is the fixed zi hour on the clock, day and night. When the yang breath is born in the human body, the yang rises; this is a living, unfixed zi hour, so it is called the Living Zi Hour.

§6"When the time comes, the spirit knows"; the yang light appearing once or twice
Original (Chinese)

问：时至神知，药产神知，光透帘帏，阳光一现二现作何解？

答：微阳与小药生时，如自心清净，元神即知，所以谓时至神知，药产神知。阳光一现二现，光透帘帏，是大药产生时之景象，非微阳生时所有。

Question: How should one understand “when the time comes, the spirit knows”, “when the medicine is produced, the spirit knows”, “the light shines through the curtains”, “the yang light appears once, twice”?

Answer: When the faint yang and the lesser medicine are born, if one’s mind is clear and pure, the original spirit knows it at once; that is why it is said “when the time comes, the spirit knows”, “when the medicine is produced, the spirit knows”. The yang light appearing once or twice, the light shining through the curtains, are scenes of the time when the Great Medicine is produced, not something that happens when the faint yang is born.

§7The saying "think of the Muddy Pellet in the morning, the Cavity of Breath in the evening"
Original (Chinese)

问：有云，早坐想泥丸为命交性，晚坐想气穴为性交命。此说是否？

答：非正道丹经之语，为妄造，不成问题。

Question: Some say that sitting in the morning and thinking of the Muddy Pellet is life uniting with nature, and sitting in the evening and thinking of the Cavity of Breath is nature uniting with life. Is this right?

Answer: These are not the words of the alchemical scriptures of the orthodox Way; they are fabrications and not worth discussing.

§8Where is the *yinqiao*
Original (Chinese)

问：阴跻为何？

答：阴跻者，阴精出由之路也。在下丹田下，阴囊后，谷道前之间。

Question: What is the yinqiao?

Answer: The yinqiao is the road by which yin essence goes out. It lies below the lower elixir field, between the back of the scrotum and the front of the anus.

§9Welcoming it back to the furnace from the *yinqiao*
Original (Chinese)

问：经云：从阴跻迎归炉，又云寻气以阴跻为先，元精者，在阴跻一脉等语何解？

答：皆言身内元阳发动，须行采取之功，从阴跻收回丹田，加以烹练之法也。

Question: The scriptures say “welcome it back to the furnace from the yinqiao”, and also “in seeking the breath, the yinqiao comes first” and “the original essence lies in the yinqiao vessel”. How should these be understood?

Answer: They all mean that when the original yang in the body stirs, one must do the work of gathering, bringing it back from the yinqiao to the elixir field and applying the method of cooking and refining.

§10Silently focusing on the *yinqiao*
Original (Chinese)

问：默注阴跻之法如何？

答：此是言调息之法，凡调息以引息者，只在凝神入气穴，神在气穴中，默注阴跻，不交而自交，不接而自接，那是神与气交也。调息一事，为行功之急务。

Question: What is the method of silently focusing on the yinqiao?

Answer: This speaks of the method of regulating the breath. Whenever one regulates the breath in order to draw out the breath, it lies only in concentrating the spirit in the Cavity of Breath; with the spirit in the Cavity of Breath, silently focused on the yinqiao, without uniting they unite of themselves, without joining they join of themselves — that is the spirit uniting with the breath. Regulating the breath is the urgent task of the work.

§11The scenes of stillness and their effects
Original (Chinese)

问：请示静之景象及其效果。

答：凡初下手行功，妄念最多，必万缘放下，割绝净尽，然后可以言静。静之景象，恍恍惚惚，杳杳冥冥，无天无地无人无我无众生，万象空空，一念不生也。功行不入恍惚杳冥，本人身中已耗之真气，从何处去产来补还？所谓静极阳生是也。如此勤行，久之阳旺，则有采炼之功。积而久之，阳气旺则丹田温暖，及到充足时，则河车始能初动，当行小周天之法也。我身中已耗之元气，既已从洪濛中产生，盗夺转来，补足还元，一切宿疾始能痊愈。筑基补漏之法即此是也，然筑基补漏两名词是喻言。筑基者，犹建亭台，先作地基，修士行功到河车初动，基已成矣。补漏者，犹物破当补，人身至十六岁阳关一破，精窍漏泄阳精，修士必使元阳不耗，漏眼闭塞，始能成丹。

Question: Please show me the scenes of stillness and their effects.

Answer: When one first begins the work, deluded thoughts are most numerous; one must put down all ties and cut them off completely, and only then can one speak of stillness. The scene of stillness is dim and indistinct, dark and obscure, without Heaven, without Earth, without others, without self, without beings; the myriad phenomena are empty, not a single thought arises. If the work does not enter the dim and obscure, from where could the true breath already depleted in one’s body be produced to replenish it? This is what is called “at the extreme of stillness, yang is born”. If one practices diligently like this, in time the yang becomes strong, and then there is the work of gathering and refining. When it has accumulated for a long time and the yang breath is strong, the elixir field grows warm; and when it becomes sufficient, the River Chariot can first move, and one should carry out the method of the Lesser Celestial Circuit. Only when the original breath already depleted in my body has been produced out of the primordial chaos, stolen back, replenished and restored to its origin, can all chronic ailments be healed. The method of laying the foundation and repairing leaks is just this; but the two terms “laying the foundation” and “repairing leaks” are metaphors. Laying the foundation is like building a pavilion: first one makes the foundation; when the practitioner’s work reaches the first movement of the River Chariot, the foundation is complete. “Repairing leaks” is like mending something broken: when a person reaches the age of sixteen and the Yang Pass is broken, the opening of the essence leaks yang essence; the practitioner must keep the original yang from being consumed and close the leaking eye before the elixir can form.

§12The mechanism of creation
Original (Chinese)

问：何谓造化之机？

答：一阳初动时，即造化之机也。

Question: What is the mechanism of creation?

Answer: The moment when one yang first stirs is the mechanism of creation.

§13The chamber of the spirit
Original (Chinese)

问：神室为何？

答：即丹田气穴也。

Question: What is the chamber of the spirit?

Answer: It is the elixir field, the Cavity of Breath.

§14The Great Void
Original (Chinese)

问：太虚为何？

答：无极之别名也。

Question: What is the Great Void?

Answer: Another name for the Limitless.

§15What is the Golden Elixir
Original (Chinese)

问：何谓金丹？

答：金者坚固之质，即金刚不坏，至尊至贵，长光明之义。丹者，丹药也。凡丹，以火炼金石而成丹。道则以身中真火煅炼坎中真水，凝结而成，借喻为丹。

Question: What is the Golden Elixir?

Answer: “Gold” is a firm substance, meaning indestructible as diamond, supremely honored and precious, ever bright. “Elixir” is an elixir medicine. Ordinary elixirs are made by refining metals and minerals with fire. In the Way, the true water within Kan is forged and refined with the true fire in the body and congeals; this is called an elixir by way of metaphor.

§16Having no mind when facing circumstances
Original (Chinese)

问：对境无心何意？

答：对任何境界，心中无此境界，即不动心也。

Question: What does “having no mind when facing circumstances” mean?

Answer: Facing any state whatever, the mind does not hold that state — that is, the mind does not move.

§17The six faculties in great concentration
Original (Chinese)

问：何谓六根大定？

答：六根者，眼、耳、鼻、舌、身、意也。又为六贼，窃人精神之义。大定者，不动之义。

Question: What is “the six faculties in great concentration”?

Answer: The six faculties are eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body and mind. They are also the six thieves, meaning that they steal a person’s essence and spirit. “Great concentration” means not moving.

§18Where exactly is the Cavity of Breath
Original (Chinese)

问：一，心止于脐下云云。二、其脉在脐后腰前云云。三、脐后腰前心下肾上云云，三处合参，不识何者为是？穴果何处？

答：脐与腰肾相对，其标准，距脐之后面，对过去七寸，距腰肾之前面，对过来三寸之中间，即气穴也。寸以本人中指中节为准量之。此古法也，不必执着。内肾是古称气穴之别名，非腰间肾子，肾子亦非藏精之所。论气穴一窍，非行功至当气机发动时，难知所在确处，外面难以指定确实。用《三车秘旨》所说，脐后腰前，心下肾上，中间一带，不可拘执之，大意观照着最妙，注重在中间一带，不可拘执尺寸上行功，始能活泼，否则着相。《收心法》文中有注重处，学者须知：停神安息以自然为主，心不可太严，当注意顺其自然之顺字，心不可太散，注意守其自然之守字，勿忘勿助，便是得当。心空虚，有息依随，重在依随二字，钻杳冥之钻字。此便是口诀，得者宝之。

Question: First, “the mind rests below the navel”, and so on. Second, “the vessel is behind the navel and in front of the waist”, and so on. Third, “behind the navel and in front of the waist, below the heart and above the kidneys”, and so on. Comparing these three, I do not know which is right. Where exactly is the cavity?

Answer: The navel faces the waist and kidneys. The standard is this: the point between seven cun back from the navel and three cun forward from the front of the waist and kidneys is the Cavity of Breath. The cun is measured by the middle joint of one’s own middle finger. This is an ancient method; there is no need to cling to it. “Inner kidney” is an old alternative name for the Cavity of Breath; it is not the kidneys at the waist, and the kidneys are not where essence is stored either. As for the single opening of the Cavity of Breath, unless the work has reached the point where the mechanism of the breath stirs, it is hard to know its exact location, and it is hard to point it out precisely from outside. Use what the Secret Principles of the Three Carts says — behind the navel and in front of the waist, below the heart and above the kidneys, the general area in the middle — and do not be rigid about it; simply observing and illuminating it in a general way is best, with attention on the general area in the middle. Do not be rigid about working with exact measurements; only then can it be lively — otherwise you cling to forms. The text of the Method of Gathering the Mind has points of emphasis that students should know: in settling the spirit and calming the breath, naturalness is the main thing; the mind must not be too strict — pay attention to the word “follow” in “follow what is natural”; the mind must not be too scattered — pay attention to the word “keep” in “keep to what is natural”; neither forgetting nor helping is just right. The mind empty, with the breath to rely on and follow — the emphasis is on the words “rely on and follow”, and on the word “bore” in “boring into the obscure”. This is the oral formula; those who obtain it should treasure it.

§19The foundation of the elixir
Original (Chinese)

问：何为丹基？

答：丹基者，丹之基础也。身内行功，将初乘做到七返还丹成就，结为丹母，则丹基凝矣。

Question: What is the foundation of the elixir?

Answer: The foundation of the elixir is the base of the elixir. In the work within the body, when the first vehicle is carried through to the completion of the reverted elixir of the seven returns and forms the mother of the elixir, the foundation of the elixir has congealed.

§20The true mechanism stirring of itself
Original (Chinese)

问：采微阳一段内，何为真机自动？

答：先无为真，发动为机，阳物勃然而举，即先天真一之气机动矣。

Question: In the passage on gathering the faint yang, what is “the true mechanism stirring of itself”?

Answer: The prior is true; stirring is the mechanism. When the male organ rises vigorously, the mechanism of the Prior Heaven true and single breath has moved.

§21Under pure yin
Original (Chinese)

问：何为纯阴之下？

答：纯阴是坤卦，先天坤卦变为后天坎卦，坎属水，亦纯阴也。

Question: What is “under pure yin”?

Answer: Pure yin is the Kun hexagram; the Prior Heaven Kun becomes the Posterior Heaven Kan; Kan belongs to water, which is also pure yin.

§22The fire in "forging and refining with fire"
Original (Chinese)

问：用火锻炼，何为火？

答：火之义不一，此处以神为火。

Question: In “forging and refining with fire”, what is the fire?

Answer: Fire has more than one meaning; here the spirit is the fire.

§23The yang breath arising
Original (Chinese)

问：何为阳气发生？

答：阳气者，先天一阳之气，自静极发生出来。

Question: What is “the yang breath arising”?

Answer: The yang breath is the breath of the one Prior Heaven yang, which arises out of the extreme of stillness.

§24The spirit's brightness comes of itself
Original (Chinese)

问：何为神明自来？

答：元神明知从来也。

Question: What is “the spirit’s brightness comes of itself”?

Answer: The original spirit clearly knows where it comes from.

§25The medicine in "middle-aged people have little medicine"
Original (Chinese)

问：中年，药少之药为何？

答：药者身中元精也。

Question: In “middle-aged people have little medicine”, what is the medicine?

Answer: The medicine is the original essence in the body.

§26What is gathering
Original (Chinese)

问：何为采取？

答：采取者，即从阴跻迎归炉，或十迎，或数十迎，外阳倒则止，即采取也。

Question: What is gathering?

Answer: Gathering is welcoming it back to the furnace from the yinqiao, ten times or several dozen times, stopping when the outer yang subsides — that is gathering.

§27"The move before the moment, to gather the faint yang"
Original (Chinese)

问：何为机先一着以采微阳也？

答：微阳者，稀微之阳气也。当真机未发动之先，权且采此微阳而积累之。

Question: What is “the move before the moment, to gather the faint yang”?

Answer: The faint yang is a scant, faint yang breath. Before the true mechanism has stirred, one provisionally gathers this faint yang and accumulates it.

§28Laying the foundation and regulating the medicine
Original (Chinese)

问：何为筑基、调药？

答：积累阳气到开关后，则基址筑成矣。勒转阳关之阳气，用风火以调和之，谓之调药。

纯阴之下云云，是谓虚耗之躯，纯是一片阴气，平时须要用元神不息而嘘，不存而照，勿忘勿助之文火锻炼之，方得阳气发生，则元神明明知所自来，此阳气即名为药，急采此阳气，以风火锻炼之，即为调药，积累久之，则有展窍开关之境，到此譬如建宅，先要筑固基址也。

Question: What are laying the foundation and regulating the medicine?

Answer: When yang breath has been accumulated until after the passes open, the foundation has been laid. Reining back the yang breath at the Yang Pass and harmonizing it with wind and fire is called regulating the medicine.

“Under pure yin” and so on refers to a depleted body, which is entirely yin breath. In ordinary times one must forge it with the civil fire of the original spirit — blowing without breathing, illuminating without preserving, neither forgetting nor helping — and only then does the yang breath arise, and the original spirit clearly knows where it comes from. This yang breath is called the medicine; quickly gather this yang breath and forge and refine it with wind and fire — that is regulating the medicine. When it has accumulated for a long time, there is the state of unfolding the openings and opening the passes; reaching this point is like building a house, where first one must lay a firm foundation.

§29The illusory elixir
Original (Chinese)

问：何为幻丹？

答：幻丹者，淫念而生之精，采炼而丹不成，终久要坏，故为幻丹。

Question: What is the illusory elixir?

Answer: The illusory elixir is essence born of lustful thoughts; when it is gathered and refined, no elixir forms, and in the end it is bound to spoil — so it is called an illusory elixir.

§30"When there is movement, apply the work; when there is stillness, sleep"
Original (Chinese)

问：动则施功静则眠，何义？

答：动则施功者，是谓睡浓时，阳气发生，外形（阳物）动了，即采回气穴，施武火烹炼之功，过后便静了，则睡眠。凡动一次，则施采炼功一次，时间长短，以气之旺微为准，在临机省察适当为要。

Question: What does “when there is movement, apply the work; when there is stillness, sleep” mean?

Answer: “When there is movement, apply the work” means that when, in deep sleep, the yang breath arises and the outer form (the male organ) moves, one gathers it back to the Cavity of Breath and applies the work of cooking and refining with the martial fire; afterward it becomes still, and one sleeps. Each time it moves, one applies the work of gathering and refining once; the length of time depends on whether the breath is strong or faint, and the key is to examine it appropriately at the moment.

§31"Steal leisure amid busyness to regulate the outer medicine"
Original (Chinese)

问：忙里偷闲调外药，无中生有产先天，何义？

答：元精生时，外形动了，我急忙趁此闲空时间，调回向外将出之阳气，即为外药。过后从无知无识之际，则有一阳来复，此即无中生有也，本人如梦初醒，外形动了，即先天元气生，正当采取之时，与微阳发动时异。

Question: What does “steal leisure amid busyness to regulate the outer medicine; from nothing produce something to give birth to the Prior Heaven” mean?

Answer: When the original essence is born, the outer form moves; I quickly take advantage of this spare moment to bring back the yang breath that is about to go out — this is the outer medicine. Afterward, out of a state without knowledge or awareness, one yang returns — this is “from nothing producing something”: one is like someone waking from a dream, the outer form moves, and the Prior Heaven original breath is born; it is just the time to gather it, and it is different from the time when the faint yang stirs.

§32The Xun wind and the Kun fire
Original (Chinese)

问：何为巽风坤火？

答：巽风者，呼吸也。坤火者，元气也。

Question: What are the Xun wind and the Kun fire?

Answer: The Wind of Xun is breathing. The Kun fire is the original breath.

§33The root in "every breath returning to the root"
Original (Chinese)

问：何为息息归根之根？

答：根，即气穴也。

Question: What is the root in “every breath returning to the root”?

Answer: The root is the Cavity of Breath.

Key Terms

  • Four-word formula: inhaling, pressing the tongue up, drawing in, closing; used when running the Celestial Circuit, not when gathering the faint yang. See four-word formula.
  • Welcoming it back to the furnace: welcoming the medicine back from the yinqiao to the elixir field, ten times or several dozen times, stopping when the outer yang subsides.
  • Old and young: gathering as soon as it rises is young, when strong it is old; between not old and not young is best. See Medicine.
  • Original and turbid: rising without a thought is original, rising from a lustful thought is turbid.
  • Yinqiao: between the back of the scrotum and the front of the anus, the road by which yin essence comes and goes; see perineum.
  • At the extreme of stillness yang is born: only when stillness enters the dim and obscure can the depleted true breath be produced and replenished.
  • Laying the foundation, repairing leaks: both metaphors — when the work reaches the first movement of the River Chariot, the foundation is complete; keeping the original yang from being consumed and closing the leaking eye is called repairing leaks. See laying the foundation.
  • Chamber of the spirit: the elixir field, the Cavity of Breath. Xixinzi uses this term for the lower elixir field, unlike other books that use it for the central palace.
  • Great Void: another name for the Limitless.
  • Golden Elixir: formed by forging and refining the true water within Kan with the true fire in the body until it congeals; see Golden Flower.
  • Having no mind when facing circumstances, the six faculties in great concentration: the mind not moving in any state; the six faculties not moving.
  • Seven in front and three behind: the Cavity of Breath lies between seven cun behind the navel and three cun in front of the waist (measured by the middle joint of one’s own middle finger); “this is an ancient method; there is no need to cling to it”.
  • Inner kidney: another name for the Cavity of Breath, not the kidneys at the waist.
  • The formula of the Method of Gathering the Mind: if the mind is too strict, follow; if too scattered, keep; the mind empty, with the breath to rely on and follow; bore into the obscure.
  • Foundation of the elixir, mother of the elixir: when the first vehicle is carried through to the reverted elixir of the seven returns and forms the mother of the elixir, the foundation of the elixir has congealed.
  • True mechanism: the prior is true, stirring is the mechanism.
  • Under pure yin: a depleted body, entirely yin breath.
  • Faint yang: the scant yang breath before the true mechanism stirs, gathered first and accumulated.
  • Regulating the medicine: reining back the yang breath at the Yang Pass and harmonizing it with wind and fire; see regulating the medicine.
  • Illusory elixir: essence born of lustful thoughts, which forms no elixir when gathered and refined; see illusory elixir.
  • When there is movement, apply the work; when there is stillness, sleep: when yang moves during sleep, gather and refine; when it is still, sleep.
  • Outer medicine, Prior Heaven: bringing back the yang breath about to go out is the outer medicine; one yang returning out of a state without knowledge or awareness is the Prior Heaven.
  • Xun wind and Kun fire: breathing and the original breath; see Wind of Xun.
  • Every breath returning to the root: the root is the Cavity of Breath; see concentrating the spirit in the Cavity of Breath.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Stillness is the precondition of everything. “If the work does not enter the dim and obscure, from where could the true breath already depleted in one’s body be produced to replenish it?” — without true stillness, there is no talk of gathering the medicine.
  2. Having a thought or having none is the dividing line between the original and the turbid. What is born of lustful thoughts becomes an illusory elixir even if refined.
  3. “There is no need to cling to” the location of the Cavity of Breath. It is enough to observe and illuminate, in a general way, the area in the middle between the navel and the waist; “do not be rigid about working with exact measurements; only then can it be lively — otherwise you cling to forms”.
  4. The right measure in gathering the mind: if too strict, follow; if too scattered, keep. Neither forgetting nor helping, the mind empty, with the breath to rely on and follow.
  5. Have no mind when facing circumstances. In any state, do not let the mind hold on to that state.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Yinqiao”: the original book prints it everywhere as 阴跻, which is the 阴跷 (the perineal area) commonly mentioned in alchemical books; the translation writes yinqiao throughout.
  2. §4, “when the yang rises” (yang shi ju shi 阳时举时): so in the original; from the context it should mean the time when the outer yang (the male organ) rises.
  3. §20, “the prior is true” (xian wu wei zhen 先无为真): so in the original; in light of what follows, “the mechanism of the Prior Heaven true and single breath has moved”, it should be understood as “the Prior Heaven is true”.
  4. §13, “chamber of the spirit”: Xixinzi says that the chamber of the spirit is the elixir field, the Cavity of Breath. Other alchemical books (such as The Secret of the Golden Flower) mostly use the term for the central palace or the Yellow Court; note this when reading.
  5. “The Method of Gathering the Mind”: the Method of Gathering the Mind placed before Li Hanxu’s Secret Principles of the Three Carts in the third part of this book.
  6. “Middle-aged people have little medicine”, “gathering the faint yang”, “on the order of fire phasing” and so on are all questions about the original words in the sayings selection in the ninth part of this book.
  7. This section is set against the background of the Wu-Liu school’s alchemy for men; “the outer yang rising”, “the outer form moving”, “the male organ rising vigorously” and so on are all translated according to the original meaning.
