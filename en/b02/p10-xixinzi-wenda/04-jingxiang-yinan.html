Xixinzi’s Answers to Students’ Questions · 静中景象与疑难

Scenes in Stillness and Difficulties

Xixinzi’s Answers to Students’ Questions, section 4 · Scenes in stillness and difficulties — headaches and dizziness, the dark and obscure, sleeping practice, illusory scenes and demons, methods for women

About This Chapter

This section has thirty-eight exchanges, mostly practitioners reporting what happened to them in quiet sitting and asking Xixinzi to judge: What causes headaches and dizziness? Does being half asleep count as the dark and obscure? If I keep observing and illuminating while asleep, is it the same as sitting practice? What should I do if I see light, a skeleton, a snake? Is it good or bad if my body feels as if insects were crawling, itches, breaks out in sores? Xixinzi’s standard of judgment is very consistent:

SituationXixinzi’s judgment
The mind settled, the breath faint, self and others both forgotten, half asleep, only the spirit knowing the faint breath movingThis is the scene of the dark and obscure, the first seeing of the nature, the first return to emptiness — “real evidence that must be there”
Body and mind in mid-air, as if illuminating the nine continents, with nothing to hold on toThe first return to emptiness, seeing the nature in true emptiness — “concentrating the spirit in emptiness”
The whole body empty, light and floating upward, with a faint movement withinThe first return to emptiness; the faint movement is the True Breath
In stillness, something like union in the bellyThe True Breath appearing; not yet the Mysterious Pass
Headaches and dizzinessClinging to forms too much, not understanding the formula
Something moving about in the head, a skeleton or a snake seen in the lightIllusions caused by habitually guarding the root of the nose, which lifts turbid yin breath into the head; drop the side methods and forget the images
White breath flying up and down without gatheringThe spirit not concentrated, the breath not gathered; there is a risk of falling into demonic states
Channels feeling as if insects were crawling, itching, sores, noises in the headQuite a bad sign; quickly seek someone enlightened to correct it
Wind roaring behind the headThe sound of wind, not the eagle’s cry; do not crave it

The overall principle is a single sentence: “In all circumstances, be still and settled, do not crave or cling; otherwise you will fall into demonic states.”

Original Text and Translation

§1Headaches and dizziness when first sitting
Original (Chinese)

问：初试静坐之后，有头痛昏迷之象，为何？

答：其弊是着相太过，功行不合玄妙，未明法诀耳。兹简浅一告，静坐之时，第一重要在克除心上一切妄念；第二重要在身心活泼，空松勿着力；第三，调匀呼吸，不急不缓，时将眼耳心一同收入丹田住定，及到心也清静，息也细微，心即不须死守丹田，但又非外驰，即勿忘勿助，似有似无，久之，入恍惚之境，人我两忘，神在虚空，极其愉快。待其自然清醒，则一身轻爽异常，断无致病之理。

Question: After my first attempts at quiet sitting, I have had headaches and dizziness. Why?

Answer: The fault is clinging to forms too much; the work does not match the mysterious wonder, and you have not understood the formula. Let me tell you briefly and simply. In quiet sitting, the first important thing is to overcome all deluded thoughts in the mind; the second is for body and mind to be lively, empty and loose, using no force; third, make the breathing even, neither hurried nor slow, and from time to time gather the eyes, the ears and the mind together into the elixir field and settle there. When the mind too has become clear and still and the breath fine and faint, the mind no longer needs to guard the elixir field rigidly — but neither does it rush outward; that is, neither forgetting nor helping, as if present and as if absent. After a while, you enter the dim and indistinct state, self and others both forgotten, the spirit in empty space, extremely pleasant. When you wake from it naturally, the whole body is unusually light and refreshed; there is no way it could cause illness.

§2Half asleep — is it the dark and obscure?
Original (Chinese)

问：现静坐到心定息微，人我两忘，似睡非睡，似觉非觉时，唯神知微息在其中动，是否杳冥景？

答：不错，此便是初见性，又即佛云真空，又即最初还虚。此初下手行功必须有之实据，否则不能补还我身中已耗之精气而成丹。此景常见，进一步到钻杳冥，则命蒂生，而阳气长，乃可开关运气矣。

Question: Now when I sit until the mind is settled and the breath faint, self and others both forgotten, half asleep and half awake, only the spirit knows the faint breath moving within. Is this the scene of the dark and obscure?

Answer: Correct. This is the first seeing of the nature, which the Buddhists call true emptiness, and also the very first return to emptiness. This is the real evidence that must be there when one first begins the work; otherwise one cannot replenish the essence and breath already depleted in one’s body and form the elixir. If this scene is seen often, and one goes a step further to “boring into the obscure”, the stalk of life is born and the yang breath grows, and then one can open the passes and move the breath.

§3Is sleeping practice the same as sitting?
Original (Chinese)

问：睡熟时，神亦知观照丹田行功，仍现杳冥景况，约一二十分钟，自然清醒，睡与坐，功夫同否？背谬否？

答：功同，不背谬。人以行功唯坐而已，殊不知，行、住、坐、卧皆当行功，不过行与住之功，是大意照顾，保守成绩，不如坐与卧，能静到极点。坐时达到杳冥景况，坐亦犹睡；睡仍照坐功行持，达到杳冥，睡亦是坐。惟睡之外式，须侧勿仰，足屈勿伸，里面与坐同功。如睡不依法，必入梦境，神必昏迷无主，或尘妄心重，亦致神昏。经云：自然时节，梦里也教知。即是言人在睡梦中，神亦灵觉不迷，自然时节到了，自会知得。

Question: When I am fast asleep, the spirit still knows to observe and illuminate the elixir field and work, and the dark and obscure state still appears; after about ten or twenty minutes I wake naturally. Is the work the same in sleep and in sitting? Is it wrong?

Answer: The work is the same; it is not wrong. People think the work is only sitting, not knowing that walking, standing, sitting and lying down are all times to work; it is only that the work of walking and standing is a general looking-after, preserving one’s gains, and cannot reach the extreme of stillness as sitting and lying can. When sitting reaches the dark and obscure state, sitting is like sleeping; when in sleep one still carries out the sitting practice and reaches the dark and obscure, sleeping is sitting too. Only the outward form of sleeping should be on the side, not on the back, with the legs bent, not stretched; inside it is the same work as sitting. If one does not sleep according to the method, one is bound to enter dreams, and the spirit becomes dazed with no master; or if worldly delusions weigh heavily on the mind, that too dazes the spirit. The scripture says: “When the natural time comes, even in dreams you will be made to know.” That is, even in sleep and dreams the spirit is numinously aware and not deluded, and when the natural time comes, one knows it of oneself.

§4Movement at the place of yin essence during sleep
Original (Chinese)

问：睡时神知观照丹田，觉阴精处有动象，是否精化气？

答：不合精化气之法，不过杳冥中有灵觉而已。阴精化气，非风火锻炼不为功。

Question: When I sleep, the spirit knows to observe and illuminate the elixir field, and I feel movement at the place of the yin essence. Is this essence transforming into breath?

Answer: It does not match the method of essence transforming into breath; it is only numinous awareness within the dark and obscure. Yin essence transforming into breath cannot be achieved without forging and refining with wind and fire.

§5Body and mind in mid-air
Original (Chinese)

问：近来一静即入恍惚杳冥，觉身心居空中，如照九洲万物，无有牵挂，此景好否？

答：此即前所谓初还虚，真空见性也。如心神气息皆入杳冥，即是钻杳冥也。如在空中照见九洲者，即经云：如坐高山而视众山众水，如燃天灯而照九幽九昧。所谓凝神于虚者，此也。

Question: Recently, as soon as I am still I enter the dim and obscure, and feel body and mind dwelling in mid-air, as if illuminating the nine continents and the myriad things, with nothing to hold on to. Is this scene good?

Answer: This is what I called earlier the first return to emptiness, seeing the nature in true emptiness. If mind, spirit, breath and breathing all enter the dark and obscure, that is “boring into the obscure”. As for illuminating the nine continents as if in mid-air, the scripture says: “Like sitting on a high mountain looking out over all the mountains and waters, like lighting a heavenly lamp to illuminate the darkest depths.” What is called “concentrating the spirit in emptiness” is this.

§6The difference between the dim and the obscure
Original (Chinese)

问：恍惚杳冥，有无分别，以多久为佳？

答：二者相似，亦微有差别。初则为恍惚，中有灵觉者为杳冥，此景纯系先天，久暂皆任天然造化，愈久愈佳，但非后天想念所能办到。

Question: Is there any difference between the dim and indistinct and the dark and obscure? How long is best?

Answer: The two are similar, with a slight difference. At first it is the dim and indistinct; when there is numinous awareness within it, it is the dark and obscure. This scene belongs purely to the Prior Heaven; how long or short it lasts is left entirely to natural creation — the longer the better, but it cannot be achieved by Posterior Heaven thinking.

§7Something like a round hole in the lower elixir field
Original (Chinese)

问：静极知下丹田如有二寸五一圆洞，是气穴否？幻景否？

答：即气穴确处，非幻。

Question: At the extreme of stillness I perceive something like a round hole two and a half cun across in the lower elixir field. Is it the Cavity of Breath? Is it an illusion?

Answer: It is the exact place of the Cavity of Breath; not an illusion.

§8Concentrating the spirit in emptiness and resting on one point
Original (Chinese)

问：凝神于虚，与住一点，两说同否？

答：同，不必东猜西疑，妄起分别，恐入魔景。

Question: Are “concentrating the spirit in emptiness” and “resting on one point” the same?

Answer: The same. Do not guess this way and that and make arbitrary distinctions, for fear of falling into demonic scenes.

§9Wind roaring behind the head
Original (Chinese)

问：脑后如风吼有声，是鹫鸣否？

答：风声也，非鹫鸣。凡身中天然发现之景，不可贪着，对境须忘情为要。

Question: Behind my head there is a sound like wind roaring. Is it the eagle’s cry?

Answer: It is the sound of wind, not the eagle’s cry. Do not crave any scene that appears naturally in the body; facing circumstances, forgetting feelings is the key.

§10Is there a fixed sitting posture?
Original (Chinese)

问：静坐身手足之外式，有一定否？

答：有定式，头端，身正，手握，足盘，垂帘，塞兑也，已详前说。全身以活泼不着力为要。

Question: Is there a fixed outward form for the body, hands and feet in quiet sitting?

Answer: There is a fixed form: head upright, body straight, hands clasped, legs crossed, lowering the curtains (of the eyes), closing the mouth — already explained in detail above. For the whole body, being lively and using no force is the key.

§11Must the work follow an order?
Original (Chinese)

问：行功是否一定要按程序？能否任随行去？

答：论正道，定限有序，初乘内有三阶段：积精累气为初段；展窍开关为二段；筑基炼已为三段。初下手，从格去物欲复还太虚始，即入杳冥也。涵虚真人称学钻杳冥，经七、八年专一之功，然后稍有把柄。君于人事忙碌中，顺带一行，已有把柄乎？大道必须于杳冥中，积累先天气，自混沌中，一阳来复，始能展窍开关，转运周天，是为筑基。筑基既久，积累益深，于丹田中突出一物，似有声有光为验，到此方是结丹之始。行功程序既无，意以丹经某语相似，以为已达某阶段，如是昏行，恐入魔境。论正道，河车运转，必阳气充足，玄关发现时，始可发动，且其中有升降度数，爻、时、位精微法诀，岂可以后天念想空转，而能成丹乎？

Question: Must the work follow a fixed order? Can one simply go along as one likes?

Answer: In the orthodox Way there are set limits and an order; within the first vehicle there are three stages: accumulating essence and building up breath is the first; unfolding the openings and opening the passes is the second; laying the foundation and refining the self is the third. One begins by investigating and removing the desire for things and returning to the Great Void — that is, entering the dark and obscure. The Perfected Hanxu said that he studied “boring into the obscure” with seven or eight years of single-minded work before he had some grip on it. You, practicing on the side amid the busyness of human affairs — do you have a grip on it already? In the great Way, one must accumulate Prior Heaven breath within the dark and obscure; only when one yang returns out of chaos can one unfold the openings, open the passes and turn the Celestial Circuit — this is laying the foundation. When the foundation has been laid for a long time and the accumulation is ever deeper, something bulges out in the elixir field, with what seems like sound and light as evidence; only at this point is it the beginning of forming the elixir. Having no order of work, and thinking that because something resembles some sentence in the alchemical scriptures one has reached a certain stage — to go on blindly like this risks falling into demonic states. In the orthodox Way, the River Chariot can be set turning only when the yang breath is sufficient and the Mysterious Pass appears; moreover, there are subtle formulas of degrees of rising and falling, lines, hours and positions — how could one form an elixir by turning it emptily with Posterior Heaven thoughts?

§12Warm breath going to the hip, the heart opening bright, sounds in the spine
Original (Chinese)

问：静坐中丹田动荡时，即有一股热气，由左斜走胯下。凝神一时，忽心中朗然开放，愉快非常，返观则见双义形、十字形，漩涡形。迨夫子午交，则脊骨谷谷有声，有似脱节，此现象好否？或为魔否？

答：景像有好有不好，于法有合有不合，惜无正传，混混行来，不符正宗程限。凡对境宜静定，勿贪着，否则入魔。

Question: In quiet sitting, when the elixir field stirs, a current of warm breath goes slantwise from the left down below the hip. After concentrating the spirit for a while, suddenly my heart opens bright, extremely joyful; looking back within, I see the shape of a pair of crossed strokes, a cross shape, a whirlpool shape. When zi and wu meet, the spine makes clicking sounds, as if it were coming out of joint. Is this phenomenon good? Or is it demonic?

Answer: Some of these scenes are good and some are not; some match the method and some do not. The pity is that without a true transmission, you muddle along and do not match the proper limits of the orthodox school. In all circumstances, be still and settled, do not crave or cling; otherwise you will fall into demonic states.

§13The twelve hours of the diagram of turning the Dharma Wheel
Original (Chinese)

问：阅周天转法轮图之十二时，是否以日间十二时为准则？子进阳火，午退阴符，余十时身中何地？

答：转运十二时，是身中之活时，非日间之时也，已详见前说。

Question: Reading the twelve hours on the diagram of the Celestial Circuit turning the Dharma Wheel — are they based on the twelve hours of the day? Advancing the yang fire at zi, withdrawing the yin tally at wu — where in the body are the other ten hours?

Answer: The twelve hours of turning are the living hours in the body, not the hours of the day; this has already been explained in detail above.

§14"When the time comes, the spirit knows; the mechanism moves and the pipes of nature sound"
Original (Chinese)

问：时至神知，机动籁鸣，何义？

答：时，即身中活子时发现，元神知得也。机，即玄关之机关发动，如天籁自鸣也。

Question: What does “when the time comes, the spirit knows; the mechanism moves and the pipes of nature sound” mean?

Answer: “The time” is the Living Zi Hour appearing in the body, which the original spirit knows. “The mechanism” is the mechanism of the Mysterious Pass stirring, like the pipes of nature sounding of themselves.

§15"Suddenly at midnight, a clap of thunder"
Original (Chinese)

问：忽然夜半一声雷，万户千门次第开，何义？

答：展窍开关也。

Question: What does “suddenly at midnight, a clap of thunder; ten thousand doors and a thousand gates open one after another” mean?

Answer: Unfolding the openings and opening the passes.

§16Playing the flute without holes
Original (Chinese)

问：吹无孔笛，何义？

答：调真息也。

Question: What does “playing the flute without holes” mean?

Answer: Regulating the True Breath.

§17Seeing only the scenes without knowing the fire phasing
Original (Chinese)

问：活子时采药交媾等象，次第呈现，但不知周天刻漏，升降度数，文火温养，武火烹炼，可否？

答：如此未必合正法程限。周天升降度数与文武火，详前说。不知火候，犹化铜铁矿，无柴炭风箱，终一顽石而已。

Question: The scenes of the Living Zi Hour, gathering the medicine, union and so on appear one after another, but I do not know the water clock of the Celestial Circuit, the degrees of rising and falling, warm nourishing with the civil fire, cooking and refining with the martial fire. Is that all right?

Answer: Then it does not necessarily match the limits of the right method. The degrees of rising and falling in the Celestial Circuit and the civil and martial fires are explained in detail above. Not knowing fire phasing is like smelting copper or iron ore without firewood, charcoal or bellows — in the end it is only a lump of stone.

§18White breath flying about without gathering
Original (Chinese)

问：静坐觉身中白气上下飞腾，但不凝聚，恐不合先天大道。

答：上下腾涌不停，神不凝，气不聚也，疑不合道，亦不错，并恐入魔。

Question: In quiet sitting I feel white breath in my body flying up and down, but it does not gather. I fear this does not match the Prior Heaven great Way.

Answer: Surging up and down without stopping means the spirit is not concentrated and the breath does not gather. Your doubt that it does not match the Way is not wrong, and there is a risk of falling into demonic states as well.

§19Channels feeling as if insects were crawling, itching and sores
Original (Chinese)

问：每行功身中经络如虫行其间，至处瘙痒，甚至发疮，或头部作响等象，好否？

答：此等象颇非好兆，须速求高明，将法诀纠正的确，节目澈底有据，以免发生危险。

Question: Every time I practice, the channels in my body feel as if insects were crawling in them, itching wherever they go, even breaking out in sores, or there are noises in the head and the like. Is this good?

Answer: Such signs are quite bad. You must quickly seek someone enlightened to correct your formula precisely, with every step thoroughly grounded, so as to avoid danger.

§20A line surging up from the belly
Original (Chinese)

问：静极时，觉腹中有长寸许之线一现，一次由腹中冲至心腑，一闪而罢，不知为何？

答：一线上冲心腑，有似展窍，一闪而罢，是阳气未充，故展窍未成。

Question: At the extreme of stillness, I felt something like a line about a cun long appear in the belly; once it surged from the belly up to the heart, flashed and was gone. I do not know what it was.

Answer: A line surging up to the heart is something like the unfolding of an opening; flashing and being gone means the yang breath is not yet sufficient, so the unfolding of the opening is not complete.

§21The whole body empty, light and floating upward
Original (Chinese)

问：坐至静极，觉全体皆空，轻而上浮，稍焉其中有微动象，不知何义？

答：此正达到初还虚之地，所谓凝神于虚者此也，又即轻清上升之初证，从中微动者，即真息也。

Question: When I sit to the extreme of stillness, I feel the whole body empty, light and floating upward, and after a while there is a faint movement within. I do not know what this means.

Answer: This is exactly reaching the place of the first return to emptiness — what is called “concentrating the spirit in emptiness” — and it is also the first evidence of the light and clear rising upward; the faint movement within is the True Breath.

§22The Cavity of Breath and the spirit focused on the *yinqiao*
Original (Chinese)

问：凝神于脐下之气穴，与收心法之神注肾根阴跻一脉，二说同否？

答：两地俱属邻近，各有作用，神注阴跻，不过片时，即提上气穴矣。

Question: Concentrating the spirit in the Cavity of Breath below the navel, and the Method of Gathering the Mind‘s focusing the spirit on the yinqiao vessel at the root of the kidneys — are the two the same?

Answer: The two places are close to each other, and each has its own function. Focusing the spirit on the yinqiao lasts only a moment, and then it is lifted up to the Cavity of Breath.

§23Something moving about in the head
Original (Chinese)

问：静坐时，觉有指大一股在头部，如物游动，不知为何？近阅《三车秘旨》第一河车有升上玉枕钻入泥丸之说，似是而非，不合法度道路，疑有错误，当如何补救？

答：头部游动，因习上守山根之法，将头上气机错乱，发生幻象。以收心法一段考之，尚未到命蒂生阳气长时，如何即到第一河车？疑不合法。今后当去旁法，并忘影像，以为补救。

Question: In quiet sitting I feel a finger-thick current in my head, moving about like a creature. I do not know why. Recently I read in the first River Chariot of the Secret Principles of the Three Carts about rising to the Jade Pillow and boring into the Muddy Pellet; it seems right but is not, it does not match the rules and the path, and I suspect I have made a mistake. How should I remedy it?

Answer: The movement in your head comes from habitually practicing the method of guarding the root of the nose above, which has disordered the mechanism of the breath in the head and produced illusions. Judging by the passage on the Method of Gathering the Mind, you have not yet reached the time when the stalk of life is born and the yang breath grows — how could you be at the first River Chariot already? I suspect it does not match the method. From now on, drop the side methods and forget the images; that is the remedy.

§24Something like union in the belly — is it the Mysterious Pass?
Original (Chinese)

问：性命双修，是否为神气交融？静时，腹中似有交媾象，是否玄关？平时亦有交媾之感觉，此象因何而有？又为何名？

答：能以神气交融，即性命双修也。静时腹中似有交媾，此真息发现时也，非玄关。须交融久之，从杳冥中神息相忘，于无知无识间，不觉恍然阳生，而人如醉，方是玄关发现，交媾景象。因心中之神，与肾中之气，融和而有，名曰心肾交也。

Question: Is dual cultivation of nature and life the merging of spirit and breath? In stillness there is something like union in my belly — is this the Mysterious Pass? I also have a sense of union in ordinary times. Why does this happen? And what is it called?

Answer: Being able to merge spirit and breath is dual cultivation of nature and life. Something like union in the belly in stillness is the time when the True Breath appears; it is not the Mysterious Pass. One must merge them for a long time, until, in the dark and obscure, spirit and breath forget each other, and in a state without knowledge or awareness, suddenly, before one knows it, yang is born and one is as if drunk — only that is the Mysterious Pass appearing, the scene of union. Because it comes from the spirit in the heart merging with the breath in the kidneys, it is called the union of heart and kidneys.

§25Saliva filling the mouth
Original (Chinese)

问：身中酥适美快，则口中津满，当如何收拾？是否津由舌尖出？

答：口津为气所化，来自舌下，非舌尖，津满则咽下为是。

Question: When my body feels relaxed, comfortable and delightful, saliva fills my mouth. What should I do with it? Does the saliva come from the tip of the tongue?

Answer: The saliva is transformed from breath; it comes from under the tongue, not the tip. When the mouth is full, swallowing it is right.

§26The formula of "boring in"
Original (Chinese)

问：钻字诀，是否静之又静，清而又清，一切放下，全体皆忘？

答：不错。须进而达到心神默默，气息绵绵，皆入于杳冥中，始备。

Question: Is the formula of “boring in” being stiller and stiller, clearer and clearer, putting everything down, forgetting the whole body?

Answer: Correct. One must go further, to where the mind and spirit are silent and the breath continuous, all entering the dark and obscure; only then is it complete.

§27A skeleton and a snake seen in the light
Original (Chinese)

问：往昔静坐，于光中见骷髅形，继而光中见蛇，被魔扰静二次，惧而停功，欲识现魔之所以，与遣魔之方法云云。

答：魔者，阴气也，有内外之分，内魔由身中阴气所幻化，而外魔应之。查功行素习上守山根之法，将浊阴之气升提，幻异无常，故二次现蛇，以后必化其他矣。遣魔之法，见幻象不着，元神镇静不动，则内魔息，而外魔亦遁矣。

Question: In the past, during quiet sitting, I saw the shape of a skeleton in the light, and then a snake in the light; twice my stillness was disturbed by demons, and in fear I stopped practicing. I would like to know why demons appear and how to dispel them.

Answer: Demons are yin breath; they are divided into inner and outer. Inner demons are illusions produced by the yin breath in the body, and outer demons respond to them. Looking at your practice, you have habitually practiced the method of guarding the root of the nose above, lifting turbid yin breath upward, so that the illusions are strange and changeable; so twice a snake appeared, and later it will surely turn into something else. The way to dispel demons: when you see illusions, do not cling to them; let the original spirit stay calm and unmoved, and the inner demons cease, and the outer demons flee too.

§28The exact places of the cavities of the Conception and Governing vessels
Original (Chinese)

问：请示任督脉、绛宫、紫庭、风府、玉枕、黄庭、气穴等确处。

答：督脉外附脊骨。任脉外附胸骨，不经心脏，不连胸部何穴。绛宫是心，紫庭相近。风府非玉枕。黄庭有上中下。中丹田在心。气穴是下丹田也。以气穴一窍而论，系虚悬一穴，假使剖视，亦难辨别，然又不诬，有苏君者，身中实验已到，静极知下丹田有二寸五分一圆洞；又许君，内见丹田一窍两口，此二人于穴已知确处矣。

Question: Please show me the exact places of the Conception and Governing vessels, the Crimson Palace, the Purple Court, the Wind Palace, the Jade Pillow, the Yellow Court, the Cavity of Breath and so on.

Answer: The Governing vessel lies along the outside of the spine. The Conception vessel lies along the outside of the breastbone; it does not pass through the heart and does not connect with any point on the chest. The Crimson Palace is the heart; the Purple Court is close by. The Wind Palace is not the Jade Pillow. The Yellow Court has an upper, middle and lower. The middle elixir field is at the heart. The Cavity of Breath is the lower elixir field. As for the single opening of the Cavity of Breath, it is a cavity suspended in emptiness; even if one were to dissect the body, it would be hard to make out — and yet it is not false. A Mr. Su, having reached real experience in his own body, at the extreme of stillness perceived a round hole two and a half cun across in the lower elixir field; and a Mr. Xu saw within one opening with two mouths in the elixir field. These two already know the exact place of the cavity.

§29The Mysterious Female
Original (Chinese)

问：何谓玄牝？

答：即气穴虚无一窍也。

Question: What is called the Mysterious Female?

Answer: It is the single empty opening of the Cavity of Breath.

§30The scene of union appearing in the dimness without the yang rising
Original (Chinese)

问：恍惚中现交媾象，阳不举，何义？

答：真息现也。

Question: In the dim and indistinct, a scene of union appears, but the yang does not rise. What does this mean?

Answer: The True Breath is appearing.

§31Warm breath rushing down toward the anus
Original (Chinese)

问：热气有时下奔谷道，当如何？

答：当收回气穴。下奔是神不凝，故气不聚也。

Question: Sometimes warm breath rushes down toward the anus. What should I do?

Answer: Bring it back to the Cavity of Breath. Rushing downward means the spirit is not concentrated, so the breath does not gather.

§32Carrying heavy loads and travelling far
Original (Chinese)

问：静修负重行远，宜否？

答：不宜，防散气。

Question: During quiet cultivation, is it all right to carry heavy loads and travel far?

Answer: Not advisable, to avoid scattering the breath.

§33The number of the Celestial Circuit's circulation
Original (Chinese)

问：周天运行数，是否同天之三百六十五度？子行卅六，午行廿四，是否子丑寅辰巳月，每月运卅六次，合阳爻；午未申戌亥月，运廿四次，合阴爻？

答：身中运转，虽是同天之度数，不同天之年月日时，自有活年月日时度数也。

Question: Is the number of the Celestial Circuit’s circulation the same as Heaven’s three hundred and sixty-five degrees? “Zi goes thirty-six, wu goes twenty-four” — does it mean that in the months zi, chou, yin, chen and si one circulates thirty-six times a month, matching the yang lines, and in the months wu, wei, shen, xu and hai twenty-four times, matching the yin lines?

Answer: Although the circulation in the body has the same degrees as Heaven, it does not have the same years, months, days and hours as Heaven; it has its own living years, months, days, hours and degrees.

§34The water clock
Original (Chinese)

问：何为刻漏？

答：古无钟表，以铜壶盛水漏滴，定时之刻数，故曰刻漏。

Question: What is the water clock (kelou)?

Answer: In ancient times there were no clocks; water was put in a bronze pot to drip out, fixing the quarters of the hours — so it is called the water clock.

§35The breathing of union
Original (Chinese)

问：交媾之息如何？

答：六根大定，片念不生也。

Question: What is the breathing of union like?

Answer: The six faculties in great concentration, not a single thought arising.

§36Regulating the medicine
Original (Chinese)

问：调药何义？

答：勒阳关也。

Question: What does regulating the medicine mean?

Answer: Reining in the Yang Pass.

§37How to avoid leaking the essence
Original (Chinese)

问：必如何而后可免漏精？

答：欲免此患，惟在炼性诚笃，则元神灵觉有主，醒睡如一。若睡去即昏，随梦中幻象转动，触色动淫，触财动贪等熟路者，是炼性之功不专、不诚、不精、不纯，故寐则心神昏迷，无灵明之正觉为主，或人事纷扰亦致神昏。经云，大道成始成终之功，不外炼性。倘初还虚之功熟，而初基炼精化气，又知用风用火，则可免漏精，否则精不化气，亦必漏泄。

Question: What must be done to avoid leaking the essence?

Answer: To avoid this trouble, it lies only in refining the nature sincerely and steadfastly; then the original spirit has a master in numinous awareness, and waking and sleeping are as one. If one becomes dazed as soon as one falls asleep and turns along with the illusions in dreams, following the well-worn paths of being stirred to lust on meeting beauty and to greed on meeting wealth, it is because the work of refining the nature has not been single-minded, sincere, refined or pure; so in sleep the mind and spirit are dazed, with no numinous, bright right awareness as master; or the bustle of human affairs also dazes the spirit. The scripture says: the work by which the great Way is begun and completed is nothing other than refining the nature. If the work of the first return to emptiness is well practiced, and in the initial foundation of refining essence into breath one also knows how to use wind and fire, then leaking the essence can be avoided; otherwise the essence does not transform into breath and is bound to leak.

§38Methods for women
Original (Chinese)

问：请言女子功法。

答：女子除斩赤龙外，亦不出此功法。或曰：“四大名山多女仙”，谓女迅于男也。但修炼下手功法，男固要分童壮老三种体质，女子亦然。女子童贞三宝全，一直用清静无为法，做到性命合一了手，与男子童贞同功。至壮年体破，亦须补筑，待赤龙一斩（即截断月经不来），丹基凝矣，再养性了手。若老年衰朽，赤龙绝，必须返到有时，从而斩之，则还元矣，仍到性命合一了手。虽云女子迅于男，成功者世不多闻，何也？吕祖云：仙才难得，真师难遇。

Question: Please speak about methods for women.

Answer: Apart from “beheading the red dragon”, women’s methods are no different from these. Some say “there are many female immortals on the four great mountains”, meaning women progress faster than men. But in the beginning methods of cultivation, just as men must be divided into three kinds of constitution — youthful, adult and old — so must women. A girl who is still a virgin, with the three treasures complete, uses the method of clarity, stillness and non-action all the way through to the completion of the union of nature and life, the same work as a male virgin. In adulthood, when the body has been broken, she too must repair and build; once the red dragon is beheaded (that is, the menses are cut off and no longer come), the foundation of the elixir has congealed, and then she nourishes the nature to completion. If she is old and declining and the red dragon has ceased, she must turn back to the point where it would come, and then behead it; then she returns to the origin, and again goes on to completion in the union of nature and life. Although it is said that women progress faster than men, few are heard of in the world who have succeeded. Why? Patriarch Lü says: “Talent for immortality is hard to find; a true teacher is hard to meet.”

Key Terms

  • Clinging to forms too much: the cause of headaches and dizziness; body and mind must be lively, empty and loose, and the mind need not guard the elixir field rigidly.
  • The scene of the dark and obscure, boring into the obscure, the first return to emptiness: the mind settled, the breath faint, self and others both forgotten, only the spirit knowing the faint breath moving; see the dim and obscure.
  • Stalk of life: born after boring into the obscure; see True Breath.
  • Sleeping practice: lying on the side with the legs bent, the same work within as sitting; “when the natural time comes, even in dreams you will be made to know”.
  • Concentrating the spirit in emptiness, resting on one point: the two are the same.
  • Eagle’s cry: a sound-scene behind the head mentioned in alchemical books; Xixinzi says the roaring of wind is not the eagle’s cry. See the shaking of the six roots.
  • Closing the mouth (saidui 塞兑): dui is the mouth.
  • The three stages of the first vehicle: accumulating essence and building up breath, unfolding the openings and opening the passes, laying the foundation and refining the self; see laying the foundation and refining the self.
  • Living hours: the twelve hours on the diagram of turning the Dharma Wheel are living hours in the body, not the hours of the day; see Living Zi Hour.
  • The mechanism moves and the pipes of nature sound: the mechanism of the Mysterious Pass stirring, like the pipes of nature sounding of themselves.
  • “Suddenly at midnight, a clap of thunder; ten thousand doors and a thousand gates open one after another”: unfolding the openings and opening the passes; see opening the passes.
  • Playing the flute without holes: regulating the True Breath; see True Breath.
  • Water clock: the ancient bronze dripping-water clock; the alchemical books use it for the counting of breaths. See fire phasing.
  • Demons: illusions produced by yin breath, divided into inner and outer; when one sees illusions without clinging and the original spirit stays calm, inner demons cease and outer demons flee. See realm of yin / realm of the aggregates.
  • Guarding the root of the nose: guarding upward between the eyes; Xixinzi holds that it lifts turbid yin breath into the head and produces illusions; see Root of the Mountain.
  • Union of heart and kidneys: the spirit in the heart merging with the breath in the kidneys; see conjunction of Kan and Li.
  • Saliva: it comes from under the tongue; when the mouth is full, swallow it. See Sweet Dew.
  • Mysterious Female: for Xixinzi, the single empty opening of the Cavity of Breath.
  • Beheading the red dragon: a term of women’s alchemy; see Beheading the Red Dragon.

Putting It into Practice

  1. If headaches or dizziness appear, first check whether you are using too much force. First, remove deluded thoughts; second, keep body and mind empty and loose, using no force; third, breathe neither hurried nor slow; once the mind is still and the breath faint, the mind need not guard the elixir field rigidly.
  2. “Do not crave or cling” to any scene. Light, sound, shapes, warm breath, lightness … do not crave the good ones, do not fear the strange ones; see illusions without clinging, and let the original spirit stay calm.
  3. Do not guard the upper part. Twice Xixinzi points out that disorder of the breath in the head and seeing skeletons and snakes in the light are connected with habitually guarding the root of the nose.
  4. Do not match yourself against sentences from the alchemical scriptures. “Thinking that because something resembles some sentence in the alchemical scriptures one has reached a certain stage — to go on blindly like this risks falling into demonic states.”
  5. Sleep too is practice. Lie on your side with legs bent; inside, it is the same work as sitting. Only if you refine the nature sincerely and steadfastly in daily life will you not be dazed in sleep.
  6. During a period of quiet cultivation, avoid carrying heavy loads and travelling far.

Translator's Notes

  1. §9, “the eagle’s cry”: one of the scenes behind the head often mentioned in alchemical books (as in “the eagle cries behind the head”); the original book prints 鹫 (“eagle”).
  2. §11, “laying the foundation and refining the self” (zhuji lianji 筑基炼已): the original book prints 已 (“already”); from the meaning it should be 己 (“self”), and we translate it as “refining the self”.
  3. §12, “the shape of a pair of crossed strokes” (shuangyi xing 双义形): so in the original; what it refers to is unclear, and we render it literally.
  4. §22, §23, “the Method of Gathering the Mind”: the Method of Gathering the Mind included in the third part of this book.
  5. §23, “the first River Chariot”: see the third part of this book, Secret Principles of the Three Carts, “The First River Chariot”.
  6. In §33 the questioner’s 卅六 and 廿四 are thirty-six and twenty-four; he understood “zi goes thirty-six, wu goes twenty-four” as counting by months, and Xixinzi answers that the years, months, days and hours in the body are “living”. For the actual calculation of the four counts, thirty-six and twenty-four, see Jingxuzi’s explanation under §3 of the third section of the ninth part.
  7. Safety note: Xixinzi considers the channels feeling as if insects were crawling, itching, sores and noises in the head mentioned in §19 to be “quite a bad sign”. Today, if this happens, besides stopping any questionable practice and consulting a reliable teacher, one should see a doctor if the physical symptoms persist or worsen.
  8. §38, “beheading the red dragon”, is an expression of ancient women’s alchemy; modern readers should see the note in the glossary entry for this term: no practice should take the cessation of menstruation as its goal, and any marked change in menstruation should first be checked by a doctor.
