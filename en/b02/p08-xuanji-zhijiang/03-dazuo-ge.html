Appendix: Song of Sitting in Meditation

Appendix · Zhang Sanfeng’s Song of Sitting in Meditation — when you first sit in meditation and learn to practice Chan, the news lies in the Mysterious Pass

About This Chapter

The Song of Sitting in Meditation is a popular verse formula in lines of three, three and seven characters, which reads like a folk song. It begins with first sitting in meditation: “the news lies in the Mysterious Pass”, “secretly and continuously regulate the breathing”, “clarity, stillness and non-action are the root”; it goes on to the signs within a hundred days, the numinous medicine passing through the three passes, one yang returning at the winter solstice, the River Chariot carrying it round; then it changes direction and speaks of the unity of the three teachings: “The immortal is the buddha, the buddha is the immortal; one nature, perfectly bright, is no different; the three teachings were originally one family — when hungry, eat; when tired, sleep”, and criticizes those who only burn incense and bow to the Buddha, keep vegetarian fasts and dream vainly of the Western Paradise. It ends with “practice one day and be firm one day; do not judge your cultivation by what is before your eyes”.

The original song runs on without a break; we have divided it into four sections according to meaning.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

初打坐，学参禅，这个消息在玄关。秘秘绵绵调呼吸，一阴一阳鼎内煎。性要悟，命要传，休将火候当等闲。闭目观，心守本命，清净无为是根源。

When you first sit in meditation and learn to practice Chan, the news lies in the Mysterious Pass. Secretly and continuously regulate the breathing; one yin and one yang simmer in the tripod. The nature must be awakened to, life must be transmitted; do not take fire phasing lightly. Close the eyes and look within, the mind guarding the root of life; clarity, stillness and non-action are the root.

§2
Original (Chinese)

百日内，见应验。坎中一点往上翻，黄婆其间为媒妁，婴儿姹女两团圆。美不尽，对谁言，浑身上下气冲天。这个消息谁知道，哑子做梦不能言。急下手，采先天，灵药一点透三关。丹田直上泥丸顶，降下重楼入中元。水火既济真铅汞，若非戊己不成丹。

Within a hundred days you will see the signs: the point within Kan turns upward, the Yellow Dame acts as matchmaker between them, and the infant and the lovely maiden are united. Its beauty is endless — to whom could you tell it? Through the whole body, above and below, the breath surges up to the sky. Who knows this news? Like a mute dreaming, you cannot speak it. Quickly set to work and gather the Prior Heaven; a single point of numinous medicine passes through the Three Passes: from the elixir field straight up to the summit of the Muddy Pellet, then down the Storied Tower into the middle origin. Water and fire in harmony are the true lead and mercury; without wu and ji (the True Intention), no elixir can form.

§3
Original (Chinese)

心要死，命要坚，神光照耀遍三千。无影树下金鸡叫，半夜三更现红莲。冬至一阳来复始，霹雳一声震动天。龙又叫，虎又欢，仙乐齐鸣非等闲。恍恍惚惚存有无，无穷造化在其间。玄中妙，妙中玄，河车搬运过三关。天地交泰万物生，日饮甘露似蜜甜。

The mind must die down, life must be firm; the light of the spirit shines through the three thousand worlds. Beneath the shadowless tree the golden rooster crows; in the middle of the night, at the third watch, the red lotus appears. At the winter solstice one yang begins to return; a single thunderclap shakes Heaven. The dragon calls, the tiger rejoices, immortal music sounds all together — nothing ordinary. Dim and indistinct, being and non-being are held; boundless creation lies in their midst. The wonder within the mystery, the mystery within the wonder: the River Chariot carries it through the three passes. Heaven and Earth meet in peace and the ten thousand things are born; every day one drinks sweet dew, sweet as honey.

§4
Original (Chinese)

仙是佛，佛是仙，一性圆明不二般。三教原来是一家，饥则吃饭困则眠。假烧香，拜参禅，岂知大道在目前。昏迷吃斋错过了，一失人身万劫难。愚迷妄想西天路，瞎汉夜走入深山。天机妙，非等闲，泄漏天机罪如山。四正理，着意参，打破玄关妙通玄。子午卯酉不断夜，早拜明师结成丹。有人识得真铅汞，便是长生不老丹。行一日，一日坚，莫把修行眼下观。三年九载功成就，炼成一粒紫金丹。要知此歌何人作，清虚道人三丰仙。

The immortal is the buddha, the buddha is the immortal; one nature, perfectly bright, is no different. The three teachings were originally one family: when hungry, eat; when tired, sleep. Falsely burning incense, bowing and practicing Chan — how could they know the great Way is right before their eyes? Keeping vegetarian fasts in a daze, they let the time slip by; once the human body is lost, it is hard to regain in ten thousand kalpas. Foolishly and deludedly dreaming of the road to the Western Paradise is like a blind man walking into deep mountains at night. The mechanism of Heaven is wondrous, nothing ordinary; to leak the mechanism of Heaven is a sin as heavy as a mountain. The principle of the four cardinal points — study it with all your mind; break open the Mysterious Pass and wondrously penetrate the mystery. Zi, wu, mao and you, without stopping day or night; seek out an enlightened teacher early and form the elixir. If someone recognizes the true lead and mercury, that is the elixir of long life without aging. Practice one day and be firm one day; do not judge your cultivation by what is before your eyes. In three years and nine years the work is accomplished, and a grain of purple-gold elixir is refined. If you want to know who wrote this song — the Daoist of Clear Emptiness, the immortal Sanfeng.

Key Terms

  • The news lies in the Mysterious Pass: see Mysterious Opening / Mysterious Pass.
  • The nature must be awakened to, life must be transmitted: the work on the nature relies on one’s own awakening, the work on life on a teacher’s transmission; see inner nature / life.
  • The mind guarding the root of life: the mind guarding the fundamental place.
  • The point within Kan turns upward: the true yang within Kan rises; see conjunction of Kan and Li.
  • Yellow Dame, infant and lovely maiden: the true intention, and lead and mercury; see True Intention and Lead and Mercury.
  • Wu and ji: the true intention.
  • Middle origin: the middle elixir field.
  • Beneath the shadowless tree the golden rooster crows; in the middle of the night the red lotus appears: images for the first stirring of one yang at the extreme of stillness; see Living Zi Hour.
  • The four cardinal points: zi, wu, mao and you; see “look at the four cardinal points” in the previous part, the Mirror for Compounding the Medicine.
  • Purple-gold elixir: a fine name for the Golden Elixir; see Golden Flower.
  • The Daoist of Clear Emptiness: one of Zhang Sanfeng’s Daoist names.

Putting It into Practice

  1. “Secretly and continuously regulate the breathing”, “clarity, stillness and non-action are the root” — the whole foundation of sitting in meditation is soft, long breathing and a clear, still mind.
  2. “When hungry, eat; when tired, sleep” — cultivation is not about outward forms; it is about keeping the nature perfectly bright in daily life.
  3. “Practice one day and be firm one day; do not judge your cultivation by what is before your eyes” — keep at it every day, do not rush for results, and do not measure it by immediate gains and losses.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original song runs on continuously in lines of three, three and seven characters; we have divided it into four sections according to content.
  2. “Within a hundred days you will see the signs”, “three years and nine years” and the like are round numbers typical of verse formulas.
  3. “A single thunderclap”, “immortal music sounds all together” and so on are the ancients’ descriptions of the stirring of the breath in stillness; they need not be taken as standards.
  4. “Do not judge your cultivation by what is before your eyes” (mo ba xiuxing yanxia guan 莫把修行眼下观): generally understood as not looking only at the immediate and not rushing for results.
