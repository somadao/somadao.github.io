The First Two Pieces of Zhang Sanfeng’s Straight Talk on the Mysterious Pivot · 炼丹火候说（一）

On Fire Phasing in Refining the Elixir (I)

Straight Talk on the Mysterious Pivot, On Fire Phasing in Refining the Elixir, part one — from sweeping away stray thoughts to the work of ten months

Note (translator): The second half of this piece speaks of the true movement of the River Chariot, the Greater Celestial Circuit and the work of ten months, which belong to progressively deeper stages. Please note the text’s own warning: “When the breath arrives but the spirit is not yet whole, it is not true movement; pay no attention to it” — if warm currents, pulsing and the like appear in the body, do not guide them with the mind; simply keep illuminating quietly.

About This Chapter

This piece covers the whole process, from the first steps to the ten months of nurturing the embryo, in a single passage:

  1. Beginning: do not cling to action, do not get attached to non-action. The first work lies in extinguishing emotional ties and sweeping away stray thoughts — removing stray thoughts is the first move, which is the work of laying the foundation and refining the self.
  2. Entering stillness: each day first be still for a double hour; when body and mind are settled and the breath is calm, only then close the eyes slightly, lower the curtains and observe “the space of one cun three fen below the heart and above the kidneys”, neither clinging nor leaving, neither forgetting nor helping, all thoughts extinguished, only the numinous awareness remaining — this is called “right thought”.
  3. Embryonic breathing: within this thought, lively and alive; within that breath, gentle and free; exhaling up, it does not rush against the heart; inhaling down, it does not rush against the kidneys; after practicing for one or two “sevens” (seven or fourteen days), the two kidneys gradually steam, the elixir field grows warm, the breathing regulates itself without being regulated, the breath refines itself without being refined — neither going out nor coming in; this is embryonic breathing, spirit breathing.
  4. The first movement of the River Chariot: the true breath steams up, rises from the Caudal Funnel to the Muddy Pellet, comes down the Magpie Bridge through the Storied Tower to the middle elixir field — but “when the breath arrives but the spirit is not yet whole, it is not true movement; pay no attention to it”; simply concentrate and illuminate gently and guard the central palace — this is called “nourishing the embryo and its outline”.
  5. The true movement of the River Chariot: after about a hundred days, the essence and spirit grow, the true breath gradually fills, Kan and Li unite, and a gust of returning wind surges up through the hundred vessels — only this is the true movement of the River Chariot; a point of numinous light in the elixir field is “the mysterious pearl at the bottom of the water”, “the yellow sprout within the earth”.
  6. The Greater Celestial Circuit and the ten months: water and fire unite, gathering at the second stage, obtaining the medicine at the fourth stage, the spirit dwelling within and the light of the elixir never departing — this is the Greater Celestial Circuit; a point of the essence of utmost yang congeals in the center, the breathing rests in the embryo, nourished warmly within and without — this is called the work of ten months.

Qiu Ling adds only one note: “This describes the work from the first steps to the Lesser and Greater Celestial Circuits and the ten months of nurturing the embryo very clearly, and the inner scenes are all there too.”

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

夫功夫下手，不可执于有为。有为都是后天，今之道门多流此弊，故世罕传真。亦不可着于无为，无为便落顽空，今之释门多中此弊，故天下少佛子。此道之不行由于道之不明也。初功在寂灭情缘，扫除杂念。除杂念是第一着，筑基炼己之功也。人心既除，则天心来复。人欲既净，则天理常存。

In beginning the work, one must not cling to action — action is all Posterior Heaven; most Daoist schools today have fallen into this fault, which is why the true teaching is rarely transmitted in the world. Nor must one get attached to non-action — non-action falls into dull emptiness; most Buddhist schools today have been struck by this fault, which is why there are few true children of the Buddha in the world. This Way is not practiced because this Way is not understood. The first work lies in extinguishing emotional ties and sweeping away stray thoughts. Removing stray thoughts is the first move; it is the work of laying the foundation and refining the self. Once the human mind is removed, the heavenly mind returns; once human desires are cleansed, the principle of Heaven is always present.

§2
Original (Chinese)

每日先静一时，待身心都安定了，气息都和平了，始将双目微闭，垂帘观照心下肾上一寸三分之间，不即不离，勿忘勿助，万念俱泯，一灵独存，谓之正念。斯时也，于此念中活活泼泼，于彼气中悠悠扬扬，呼之至上，上不冲心，吸之至下，下不冲肾，一阖一辟，一来一往，行之一七二七，自然渐渐两肾大蒸，丹田气暖，息不用调而自调，气不用炼而自炼。气息既和，自然于上中下，不出不入，无来无去，是为胎息，是为神息，是为真橐籥真鼎炉，是为归根复命，是为玄牝之门，天地之根。

Each day first be still for a double hour; when body and mind are all settled and the breath is all calm, only then close the eyes slightly, lower the curtains and observe the space of one cun three fen below the heart and above the kidneys, neither clinging nor leaving, neither forgetting nor helping, all thoughts extinguished, only the single numinous awareness remaining — this is called “right thought”. At this time, within this thought, lively and alive; within that breath, gentle and free; exhale up, and above it does not rush against the heart; inhale down, and below it does not rush against the kidneys; one closing and one opening, one coming and one going. Practice like this for one or two “sevens” (seven or fourteen days), and naturally the two kidneys gradually steam strongly, the elixir field grows warm, the breathing regulates itself without being regulated, the breath refines itself without being refined. Once the breath is harmonious, naturally, among the upper, middle and lower, it neither goes out nor comes in, neither comes nor goes — this is embryonic breathing, this is spirit breathing, this is the true bellows, the true tripod and furnace, this is returning to the root and restoring life, this is the gate of the Mysterious Female, the root of Heaven and Earth.

§3
Original (Chinese)

气到此时，如花方蕊，如胎方胞，息然真气熏蒸营卫，由尾闾穿夹脊升上泥丸，下鹊桥过重楼，至绛宫而落于中丹田，是为河车初动。但气至而神未全，非真动也，不可理他。我只微微凝照，守于中宫，自然无尽生机，所谓养鄞鄂者此也。行之一月二月，我神益静，静久则气益生，此为神生气、气生神之功也。

When the breath reaches this point, it is like a flower just in bud, like an embryo just forming its sac; naturally the true breath steams through the nutritive and defensive breaths, rises from the Caudal Funnel through the Spine-Straddling Pass up to the Muddy Pellet, comes down the Magpie Bridge through the Storied Tower to the Crimson Palace and settles in the middle elixir field — this is “the first movement of the River Chariot”. But when the breath arrives and the spirit is not yet whole, it is not true movement; pay no attention to it. I simply concentrate and illuminate gently and guard the central palace, and naturally there is inexhaustible vitality — what is called “nourishing the embryo and its outline” is this. Practice like this for a month or two, and my spirit becomes ever stiller; when stillness lasts, the breath is born ever more abundantly — this is the work of spirit giving birth to breath and breath giving birth to spirit.

§4
Original (Chinese)

或百日或百余日，精神益长，真气渐充，温温火候，血水有余，自然坎离交媾，乾坤会合，神融气畅。一霎时间真气混合，自有一阵回风，上冲百脉，是为河车真动。中间若有一点灵光，觉在丹田，是为水底玄珠、土内黄芽。尔时一阳来复，恍如红日初升，照于沧海之内，如雾如烟，若隐若见，则铅火生焉。

After a hundred days or somewhat more, the essence and spirit grow ever stronger, the true breath gradually fills, the fire phasing is warm and gentle, blood and water are more than sufficient; naturally Kan and Li unite, Qian and Kun meet, the spirit melts and the breath flows freely. In an instant the true breath merges, and of itself a gust of returning wind surges up through the hundred vessels — only this is “the true movement of the River Chariot”. If in the midst of this there is a point of numinous light, felt in the elixir field, this is “the mysterious pearl at the bottom of the water”, “the yellow sprout within the earth”. At that time one yang comes back, dimly like the red sun first rising and shining into the vast sea, like mist, like smoke, half hidden and half seen — and the lead fire is born.

§5
Original (Chinese)

方其乾坤坎离未交，虚无寂灭，神凝于中，功无间断，打成一团，是为五行配合。至若水火相交，二候采取，河车逆转，四候得药，神居于内，丹光不离，谓之大周天，谓之行九转大还也。此时一点至阳之精，凝结于中，隐藏于欲净情寂之时，而有象有形。到此地位，息住于胎，内外温养，顷刻无差，又谓之十月功夫也。

While Qian and Kun, Kan and Li have not yet united, in emptiness and silent extinction, the spirit concentrated in the center, the work uninterrupted and made into one mass — this is called “the pairing of the five phases”. As for water and fire uniting, gathering at the second stage, the River Chariot turning in reverse, obtaining the medicine at the fourth stage, the spirit dwelling within and the light of the elixir never departing — this is called the Greater Celestial Circuit, called “carrying out the great return of the nine cycles”. At this time a point of the essence of utmost yang congeals in the center, hidden at the time when desires are cleansed and feelings are silent, and it has image and form. On reaching this stage, the breathing rests in the embryo, nourished warmly within and without, without the slightest error for an instant — this is also called “the work of ten months”.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§5
Original note (Chinese)

此述下手功夫而至小、大周天、十月养胎甚明，内景亦备。

This piece describes the work from the first steps to the Lesser and Greater Celestial Circuits and the ten months of nurturing the embryo very clearly, and the inner scenes are all there too.

Key Terms

  • Clinging to action, getting attached to non-action: two faults — the first falls into the Posterior Heaven, the second into dull emptiness; see realm of yin / realm of the aggregates.
  • Extinguishing emotional ties, sweeping away stray thoughts: the first work, laying the foundation and refining the self.
  • Human mind, heavenly mind: once the human mind is removed, the heavenly mind returns.
  • The space of one cun three fen below the heart and above the kidneys: the place observed; see Central Yellow and Cave of Emptiness.
  • Right thought: all thoughts extinguished, only the single numinous awareness remaining; see True Intention.
  • Not rushing against the heart above or the kidneys below: soft breathing, neither pushing up nor pressing down.
  • One or two “sevens”: seven days, fourteen days.
  • Embryonic breathing, spirit breathing: neither going out nor coming in, neither coming nor going; see True Breath.
  • Bellows: a metaphor for the opening and closing of breathing.
  • The gate of the Mysterious Female, the root of Heaven and Earth: Laozi, chapter 6; see Mysterious Opening / Mysterious Pass.
  • The first movement and the true movement of the River Chariot: the breath arriving while the spirit is not yet whole is the first movement — “pay no attention to it”; Kan and Li uniting and the returning wind surging up is the true movement. See Celestial Circuit.
  • Nourishing the embryo and its outline: concentrating and illuminating gently, guarding the central palace; see spontaneous Dharma Body.
  • The mysterious pearl at the bottom of the water, the yellow sprout within the earth: a point of numinous light in the elixir field; see Golden Flower.
  • Lead fire: the breath of one yang coming back; see Lead and Mercury.
  • The pairing of the five phases: the spirit concentrated in the center, made into one mass.
  • Gathering at the second stage, obtaining the medicine at the fourth stage: see Medicine.
  • The great return of the nine cycles: the Greater Celestial Circuit; see Golden Flower (the nine returns).
  • The work of ten months: the breathing rests in the embryo, nourished warmly within and without; see Holy Embryo and the three transmutations.

Putting It into Practice

  1. First be still for a while, then close the eyes. Each time you sit, first let body and mind settle and the breathing become calm, then close the eyes slightly — do not force your eyes shut and force yourself into stillness the moment you sit down.
  2. “Above it does not rush against the heart; below it does not rush against the kidneys” — the breathing should be soft; the inhalation does not press down, the exhalation does not push up.
  3. “The breathing regulates itself without being regulated, the breath refines itself without being refined” — this is a result, not a method. The method is only to illuminate quietly.
  4. “When the breath arrives but the spirit is not yet whole, it is not true movement; pay no attention to it” — if warm currents or pulsing appear, pay no attention to them, do not guide them, and keep concentrating and illuminating gently. This is the most important sentence for avoiding deviations.
  5. “My spirit becomes ever stiller; when stillness lasts, the breath is born ever more abundantly” — the root of every good change lies in the single word “stillness”.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Be still for a double hour” (jing yi shi 静一时): one shi is one double hour (about two hours); today it can be understood as first being quiet for a while.
  2. “Naturally the true breath steams through the nutritive and defensive breaths” (xiran zhenqi xunzheng yingwei 息然真气熏蒸营卫): xiran means naturally, of itself; yingwei in Chinese medicine refers to the nutritive breath and the defensive breath, and here refers broadly to the whole body.
  3. “Blood and water are more than sufficient”: an expression of the ancients, meaning that the essence and blood in the body are abundant.
  4. “Gathering at the second stage, obtaining the medicine at the fourth stage”: the alchemical books divide the process of gathering the medicine into six stages; only the second and fourth are mentioned here.
  5. The original is one long paragraph, which we have divided into five shorter passages.
