The First Two Pieces of Zhang Sanfeng’s Straight Talk on the Mysterious Pivot · 炼丹火候说（二）

On Fire Phasing in Refining the Elixir (II)

Straight Talk on the Mysterious Pivot, On Fire Phasing in Refining the Elixir, part two — three stages of work within a quarter of an hour

About This Chapter

This piece explains a very practical principle: in quiet practice, every quarter of an hour contains all three stages of the work — refining essence into breath, refining breath into spirit, and refining spirit to return to emptiness — not only the ten months.

  • Sitting down, closing the eyes and preserving the spirit, making the mind still and the breath regulated — this is refining essence into breath;
  • Reverting the light to shine within, concentrating the spirit in the elixir cavity, letting the True Breath come and go, stillness reaching its extreme and turning to movement, movement reaching its extreme and turning to stillness — this is refining breath into spirit;
  • The true breath returning to the origin, yin and yang uniting back and forth for a while, then naturally the wind dying down and the waves calming, resting right thought in the elixir field — this is the fire phasing of sealing and securing.

Within a quarter of an hour, the first half is warm and advances the fire, the second half is cool and withdraws the tally — so “in one quarter of an hour of circulating, there is one quarter of an hour’s Celestial Circuit”, and “a quarter of an hour’s work seizes a year’s climate”.

The last passage is very plain: “Those with few stray thoughts obtain the elixir early; those with many stray thoughts obtain it late. This method is simple and easy; it is only that people are unwilling to be bold and vigorous.”

Qiu Ling notes: this is the teaching of “robbing Heaven and Earth, seizing creation” in Master Cui’s Mirror for Compounding the Medicine.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

夫静功在一刻一刻之中，亦有炼精化气、炼气化神、炼神还虚之功夫在内，不独十月然也。即一时一日一月一年皆然。坐下闭目存神，使心静息调，即是炼精化气之功也。回光返照，凝神丹穴，使真息往来，内中静极而动，动极而静，无限天机，即是炼气化神之功也。如此真气朝元，阴阳反复交媾一番，自然风恬浪静。我于此时，将正念止于丹田，即是封固火候。年月日时久久行此三部功夫，不但入圜十月也。

In quiet practice, every quarter of an hour also contains the work of refining essence into breath, refining breath into spirit, and refining spirit to return to emptiness — not only the ten months. The same holds for a double hour, a day, a month, a year. Sitting down, closing the eyes and preserving the spirit, making the mind still and the breath regulated — this is the work of refining essence into breath. Reverting the light to shine within, concentrating the spirit in the elixir cavity, letting the True Breath come and go, within stillness reaching its extreme and turning to movement, movement reaching its extreme and turning to stillness, the mechanism of Heaven without limit — this is the work of refining breath into spirit. When in this way the true breath returns to the origin and yin and yang unite back and forth for a while, naturally the wind dies down and the waves calm. At this time I rest right thought in the elixir field — this is the fire phasing of sealing and securing. Year, month, day and double hour, practice these three stages of work for a long time — not only during the “ten months of entering the enclosure”.

§2
Original (Chinese)

故曰：运之一刻，有一刻之周天。运之一时一日一月一年，即有一时一日一月一年之周天也。然一刻中，上半刻为温，为进火，为望，为上弦，为朝屯，为春夏。下半刻为凉，为退符，为晦，为下弦，为暮蒙，为秋冬。一时则有上四刻、下四刻之分。即一日一月一年皆同。此之谓攒簇阴阳五行，一刻之功夫夺一年之气候也。

So it is said: in one quarter of an hour of circulating, there is one quarter of an hour’s Celestial Circuit; in a double hour, a day, a month, a year of circulating, there is a double hour’s, a day’s, a month’s, a year’s Celestial Circuit. Within one quarter of an hour, the first half is warm, is advancing the fire, is the full moon, is the upper quarter, is “Zhun in the morning”, is spring and summer; the second half is cool, is withdrawing the tally, is the dark of the moon, is the lower quarter, is “Meng in the evening”, is autumn and winter. A double hour is divided into the upper four quarters and the lower four quarters; and so it is with a day, a month and a year. This is called “gathering together yin and yang and the five phases”: a quarter of an hour’s work seizes a year’s climate.

§3
Original (Chinese)

到此乃是真空真静，或一二年至十年百年，打破空虚，与太虚同体，此为炼神还虚之功也。前功十月既满，须时时照顾婴儿，十步百步千里万里，以渐而出。尚或放纵不禁，必致迷而不返。仙经曰：神入气成胎，气归神结丹，所谓一点落黄庭是也。

On reaching this point there is true emptiness and true stillness; whether in one or two years, or ten or a hundred years, one breaks through emptiness and becomes one body with the Great Void — this is the work of refining spirit to return to emptiness. When the earlier work of ten months is complete, one must constantly look after the infant, letting it go out gradually — ten steps, a hundred steps, a thousand li, ten thousand li. If one still lets it run free without restraint, it is bound to get lost and not return. The scripture of the immortals says: “The spirit enters the breath and the embryo forms; the breath returns to the spirit and the elixir forms.” What is called “a single point falls into the Yellow Court” is this.

§4
Original (Chinese)

但人杂念少者，得丹早。杂念多者，得丹迟。此法简易，奈人不肯勇猛耳。若能恒久行持，必然透金贯石，入水蹈火，通天达地，再行积行累功，服炼神丹大药，必然形神俱妙，此又在功德之浅深何如耳。

But those with few stray thoughts obtain the elixir early; those with many stray thoughts obtain it late. This method is simple and easy; it is only that people are unwilling to be bold and vigorous. If one can keep at it constantly and for a long time, one will surely pass through metal and pierce stone, enter water and tread on fire, reach through to Heaven and Earth; and if one further accumulates good deeds and merit and takes and refines the great medicine of the divine elixir, body and spirit will surely both become wondrous — and this again depends on how shallow or deep one’s merit and virtue are.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§4
Original note (Chinese)

此即《崔公入药镜》中“盗天地，夺造化”之说，即一刻功夫可夺一年气候，按程序做完三部功夫。

This is the teaching of “robbing Heaven and Earth, seizing creation” in Master Cui’s Mirror for Compounding the Medicine: a quarter of an hour’s work can seize a year’s climate, completing the three stages of work in proper order.

Key Terms

  • Three stages of work within a quarter of an hour: refining essence into breath, refining breath into spirit, refining spirit to return to emptiness; see the three transmutations.
  • Closing the eyes and preserving the spirit, the mind still and the breath regulated: refining essence into breath within a quarter of an hour.
  • Concentrating the spirit in the elixir cavity, the True Breath coming and going: refining breath into spirit within a quarter of an hour.
  • The true breath returning to the origin: see the five breaths returning to the origin.
  • The fire phasing of sealing and securing: resting right thought in the elixir field; see fire phasing.
  • Ten months of entering the enclosure: ten months of secluded retreat to nurture the embryo.
  • One quarter of an hour’s Celestial Circuit: advancing the fire in the first half, withdrawing the tally in the second; see Celestial Circuit.
  • Zhun in the morning, Meng in the evening: see the previous part, the Mirror for Compounding the Medicine.
  • Gathering together yin and yang and the five phases: a quarter of an hour’s work seizes a year’s climate.
  • Looking after the infant: the nurturing after sending out the spirit; see sending out the spirit.
  • “The spirit enters the breath and the embryo forms; the breath returns to the spirit and the elixir forms”: words from a scripture of the immortals; see Holy Embryo.
  • A single point falls into the Yellow Court: see Yellow Court.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Every sitting is a complete piece of work. Sit down, still the mind, regulate the breath; revert the light, concentrate the spirit, let the True Breath come and go; finally rest the attention lightly in the elixir field — this is one “lesser circuit”; there is no need to wait for “ten months” or “a hundred days”.
  2. At the end, “rest right thought in the elixir field” — when finishing a sitting, let the attention rest lightly on the lower belly, as “sealing and securing”.
  3. “Those with few stray thoughts obtain the elixir early” — how fast the work goes depends on how many stray thoughts there are.
  4. “This method is simple and easy; it is only that people are unwilling to be bold and vigorous” — the method is not hard; what is hard is keeping at it.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Quarter of an hour” (ke 刻): in ancient times a day and night were divided into a hundred ke, so one ke is a little over fourteen minutes today; a double hour (shichen) is eight ke (the original’s “upper four quarters and lower four quarters”).
  2. “Pass through metal and pierce stone, enter water and tread on fire, reach through to Heaven and Earth”: an idealized description by the ancients of what attainment brings; translated as in the original.
  3. The original is a single paragraph, which we have divided into four shorter passages.
