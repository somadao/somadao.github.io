The First Two Pieces of Zhang Sanfeng’s Straight Talk on the Mysterious Pivot · 篇首导读

Introduction to Part Eight

What this book is

Straight Talk on the Mysterious Pivot (Xuanji zhijiang 玄机直讲) is included in the Complete Works of Zhang Sanfeng. Qiu Ling says it is a work in which Zhang Sanfeng “revised the writings of earlier people in order to instruct others”; it covers fire phasing in refining the elixir, the signs of reversion, taking the great elixir, an explanation of the “grain of millet”, and more. Only the first two pieces, on fire phasing, are chosen here, with Zhang Sanfeng’s Song of Sitting in Meditation appended.

The two pieces are short but complete:

  • On Fire Phasing in Refining the Elixir (I) goes all the way from the first steps to the ten months of nurturing the embryo: first sweep away stray thoughts; each day first be still for a double hour; only when body and mind are settled and the breath is calm, close the eyes slightly, lower the curtains and observe “the space of one cun three fen below the heart and above the kidneys”; when the breath arrives but the spirit is not yet whole, “it is not true movement; pay no attention to it”, simply concentrate and illuminate gently and guard the central palace.
  • On Fire Phasing in Refining the Elixir (II) presents a very illuminating view: within a quarter of an hour of quiet sitting there are all three stages of the work — sitting down, closing the eyes, the mind still and the breath regulated, is refining essence into breath; reverting the light to shine within, the True Breath coming and going, is refining breath into spirit; resting right thought in the elixir field is sealing and securing. Qiu Ling notes that this is the Mirror for Compounding the Medicine‘s teaching of “robbing Heaven and Earth, seizing creation”.
  • The Song of Sitting in Meditation is a popular verse formula: “When you first sit in meditation and learn to practice Chan, the news lies in the Mysterious Pass”, “The immortal is the buddha, the buddha is the immortal; one nature, perfectly bright, is no different; the three teachings were originally one family — when hungry, eat; when tired, sleep.”

Qiu Ling's editorial note

Straight Talk on the Mysterious Pivot is a work in which Zhang Sanfeng revised the writings of earlier people in order to instruct later generations; it is included in the Complete Works of Zhang Sanfeng. Its contents discuss fire phasing in refining the elixir, the signs of reversion, taking the great elixir, an explanation of the grain of millet, and so on. Here I have selected only its two pieces on fire phasing in refining the elixir, with Zhang’s Song of Sitting in Meditation appended.

What this part is about (in one sentence)

Do not cling to action, do not get attached to non-action; first sweep away stray thoughts, be still for a double hour, then lower the curtains and illuminate the space below the heart and above the kidneys; when the breath comes but the spirit is not whole, pay no attention to it; within a quarter of an hour there are all three stages of the work — those with few stray thoughts obtain the elixir early, those with many stray thoughts obtain it late.

Reading tips

  1. The three opening sentences are the outline of the whole: “One must not cling to action; action is all Posterior Heaven”; “nor must one get attached to non-action; non-action falls into dull emptiness”; “this Way is not practiced because this Way is not understood”.
  2. “Each day first be still for a double hour; when body and mind are settled and the breath is calm, only then close the eyes slightly” — this is a very practical order for beginning: first become still, then close the eyes. It means the same as “when the mind is not yet clear, do not close the eyes at random” in the previous part.
  3. “When the breath arrives but the spirit is not yet whole, it is not true movement; pay no attention to it” — when something stirs in the body, do not hurry to guide it or manage it; simply concentrate and illuminate gently.
  4. “Those with few stray thoughts obtain the elixir early; those with many stray thoughts obtain it late. This method is simple and easy; it is only that people are unwilling to be bold and vigorous.” One sentence that says everything about why some are fast and others slow.
