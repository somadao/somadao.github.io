Appendix: Five Passages Collected by Sanfeng

Plain and Simple Words on the Dao, appendix · Five passages collected by Sanfeng — Bai Ziqing’s formula of the Mysterious Pass and formula of the furnace and tripod; Lu Qianxu’s three passages on regulating the breath

About This Chapter

These are five passages by earlier authors collected by Zhang Sanfeng:

  1. Bai Ziqing’s Formula of the Mysterious Pass: the Mysterious Pass has substance and function — its substance is “silent and unmoving”, its function “when stirred, it penetrates”. He divides the Mysterious Pass into one pass after another: the dense, misty mingling at the beginning of the union of spirit and breath is one pass; the dim and silent, dark and obscure is one pass; stillness reaching its extreme and giving rise to movement, the true breath arising from the compass center, is one pass; waking again from drunkenness with a numinous awareness is one pass (“when the time comes, the spirit knows”); the mechanism moving and the breath flowing, faintly passing through the Caudal Funnel, is one pass; ablution at the gate of mao, flying up to the Muddy Pellet, ablution at the door of you, and the great rest that returns to utmost stillness are each one more pass.
  2. Bai Ziqing’s Formula of the Furnace and Tripod: explains the poem “This opening is no ordinary opening” from the Four Hundred Characters on the Golden Elixir — Qian and Kun joined together make the outer opening, and spirit and breath stored within it make the inner opening.
  3. Lu Qianxu’s first passage on regulating the breath: not forgetting is keeping to the natural, not helping is following the natural; keeping is relying on the breath, following is leveling the breath; “it lies only in concentrating the spirit; never use intention. If you know you are using intention, it is not the true intention.”
  4. Lu Qianxu’s second passage on regulating the breath: concentrate the spirit in the Cavity of Breath, silently focused on the yinqiao, and without uniting they unite of themselves — “the man does not loosen his robe, the woman does not untie her sash … these are all only metaphors for concentrating the spirit and gathering the breath”.
  5. Lu Qianxu’s third passage on regulating the breath: water joins with water, fire with fire; regulating the breath is joining breath with breath — “why force it?”

Original Text and Translation

§1Bai Ziqing's *Formula of the Mysterious Pass*
Original (Chinese)

白紫清《玄关诀》云：玄关者，求玄之关道，玄妙之机关也。有体有用。何谓体？寂然不动。何谓用？感而遂通。不动有时候。神气交媾之初，氤氤氲氲，浑浑沦沦，是为一关，所谓四大五行不着处是也。神气交媾之际，昏昏默默，杳杳冥冥，是又一关，所谓无声无臭无内无外是也。及至静极生动，而用乃出焉。混混续续，兀兀腾腾，真气从规中起，是又一关，所谓念头起处为玄牝是也。念头起处，醉而复苏，有一个灵觉，当下觉悟，是又一关，所谓时至神知是也。此时以灵觉为用，如线抽傀儡，机动气流，微微通过尾闾，是又一关，所谓斡转魁罡运斗杓，正此时也。沐浴卯门又一关；飞上泥丸又一关；归根复命，沐浴酉户，又一关；大休歇大清静，空空忘忘，还于至静又一关。玄关之体用如此，千金万论，皆在是也。

Bai Ziqing’s Formula of the Mysterious Pass says: The Mysterious Pass is the gateway for seeking the mystery, the mechanism of the mysterious and wondrous. It has substance and function. What is its substance? Silent and unmoving. What is its function? When stirred, it penetrates. Its not moving also has its time. At the beginning of the union of spirit and breath, densely mingling and misty, whole and undivided — this is one pass; it is what is called “the place the four elements and five phases do not touch”. During the union of spirit and breath, dim and silent, dark and obscure — this is another pass; it is what is called “without sound or smell, without inside or outside”. When stillness reaches its extreme and gives rise to movement, the function then comes out: continuous and unbroken, rising and surging, the true breath arises from the compass center — this is another pass; it is what is called “where a thought arises is the Mysterious Female”. Where a thought arises, it is like waking again from drunkenness: there is a numinous awareness, an awakening right there — this is another pass; it is what is called “when the time comes, the spirit knows”. At this time, numinous awareness is used, like pulling a puppet by its strings; the mechanism moves and the breath flows, faintly passing through the Caudal Funnel — this is another pass; what is called “turning Kui and Gang to move the handle of the Dipper” is exactly this moment. Ablution at the gate of mao is another pass; flying up to the Muddy Pellet is another pass; returning to the root and restoring life, ablution at the door of you, is another pass; the great rest, the great clarity and stillness, empty and forgetting, returning to utmost stillness, is another pass. Such are the substance and function of the Mysterious Pass; a thousand pieces of gold and ten thousand treatises all lie here.

§2Old Master Ziqing's *Formula of the Furnace and Tripod*
Original (Chinese)

紫清翁《炉鼎诀》云：《金丹四百字》宜熟玩。此窍非凡窍一首，道尽鼎器之妙。此窍二字，分内外两个看。非凡者，赞词也。乾坤合之为外窍，神气藏于其中为内窍。神气交而坎离生，坎离交而元精产。此精不是寻常精，乃是至宝也。此就还丹言。若小丹所用，则试与人言曰：此窍非凡窍，阴阳共合成，名为二气穴，内有真阳生，可晓然矣。仙家有三秘：火候、药物、炉鼎。此诗尽炉鼎之妙矣！

Old Master Ziqing’s Formula of the Furnace and Tripod says: the Four Hundred Characters on the Golden Elixir should be read and savored thoroughly. The poem “This opening is no ordinary opening” says everything about the wonders of the tripod vessel. The two characters “this opening” must be seen as two, an inner one and an outer one; “no ordinary” is a word of praise. Qian and Kun joined together make the outer opening; spirit and breath stored within it make the inner opening. Spirit and breath unite and Kan and Li are born; Kan and Li unite and the original essence is produced. This essence is not ordinary essence; it is the supreme treasure. This is said with regard to the reverted elixir. As for what is used in the lesser elixir, one might try saying to people: “This opening is no ordinary opening; yin and yang together form it; it is called the cavity of the two breaths, and within it true yang is born.” Then it is clear. The immortals have three secrets: fire phasing, the medicinal ingredients, and the furnace and tripod. This poem says everything about the wonders of the furnace and tripod!

§3Lu Qianxu's first passage on regulating the breath
Original (Chinese)

陆潜虚论调息法云：如何是勿忘？曰守自然。如何是勿助？曰顺自然。如何守？曰依息。如何顺？曰平息。依息则息能通息矣，平息则息能匀息矣。问守与顺是二乎？是一乎？曰知所以守则知顺之矣，知所以顺则知守之矣。是二是一，原是不错，由博返约，唯在凝神，切勿用意。知用意则非真意。真意从静中生，鸿濛初判，无有染着，乃克用之。故要死过来乃知生，不知生亦不知死。生死是动静深机。

Lu Qianxu, discussing the method of regulating the breath, says: What is not forgetting? Answer: keeping to the natural. What is not helping? Answer: following the natural. How does one keep? Answer: by relying on the breath. How does one follow? Answer: by leveling the breath. Relying on the breath, the breath can flow through; leveling the breath, the breath can become even. Question: are keeping and following two things, or one? Answer: if you know how to keep, you know how to follow; if you know how to follow, you know how to keep. To say they are two or to say they are one — neither is wrong. Returning from the broad to the essential, it lies only in concentrating the spirit; never use intention — if you know you are “using intention”, it is not the True Intention. The true intention is born out of stillness, like the primordial chaos first dividing, without any taint or attachment; only then can it be used. So you must “die” before you know “life”; if you do not know life, you do not know death either. Life and death are the deep mechanism of movement and stillness.

§4Old Master Qianxu's second passage on regulating the breath
Original (Chinese)

潜虚翁又论调息法云：凡调息以引息者，只要凝神入气穴。神在气穴中，默注阴跷，不交而自交，不接而自接，所谓隔体神交理最详，古仙已言之确矣。所谓离形交气，别有口传也。所谓男不宽衣，女不解带，敬如神明，爱如父母，皆比凝神聚气而已。故曰道归自然。《参同》曰：自然之所为兮，非有邪伪道。此之谓也。

Old Master Qianxu, discussing the method of regulating the breath again, says: Whenever one regulates the breath in order to draw out the breath, one need only concentrate the spirit in the Cavity of Breath. With the spirit in the Cavity of Breath, silently focused on the yinqiao, without uniting they unite of themselves, without joining they join of themselves; the principle of what is called “the spiritual union across the body” is most detailed, and the ancient immortals have stated it precisely. What is called “uniting the breaths apart from the bodily form” has its own oral transmission. What is called “the man does not loosen his robe, the woman does not untie her sash; revere each other like gods, love each other like parents” — these are all only metaphors for concentrating the spirit and gathering the breath. That is why it is said: the Way returns to the natural. The Cantong qi says: “What the natural does has nothing deviant or false about it.” This is what it means.

§5Old Master Qianxu's third passage on regulating the breath
Original (Chinese)

潜虚翁三论调息法云：今夫水与水合，火与火合，风与风合，云与云合，常理也。调息者以气合气，何待强为。只要凝神入气穴，神光下照阴跷脉，不期而会者，一气之感通，自然而然也。屯卦曰：以贵下贱大得民也。咸卦曰：止而悦男下女，是以亨。《易》于交接之道，盖已言之矣。但调息之法，有法功，有器用，丹道始终不离。

Old Master Qianxu, discussing the method of regulating the breath a third time, says: Water joins with water, fire joins with fire, wind joins with wind, cloud joins with cloud — this is the ordinary principle. Regulating the breath is joining breath with breath; why force it? One need only concentrate the spirit in the Cavity of Breath, the light of the spirit shining down on the yinqiao vessel; they meet without expecting to — this is the resonance of the one breath, natural and spontaneous. The Zhun hexagram (in its Image commentary) says: “The noble placing himself below the humble greatly wins the people.” The Xian hexagram (in its Judgment commentary) says: “Stopping and delighting, the man places himself below the woman; therefore there is success.” The Book of Changes has probably already spoken of the way of union. But the method of regulating the breath has its method-work and its use of vessels; the alchemical Way never departs from it from beginning to end.

Key Terms

  • The substance and function of the Mysterious Pass: its substance is silent and unmoving, its function is “when stirred, it penetrates”; see Mysterious Opening / Mysterious Pass.
  • The place the four elements and five phases do not touch: the beginning of the union of spirit and breath.
  • “Where a thought arises is the Mysterious Female”: stillness reaching its extreme and giving rise to movement, where the true breath arises.
  • “When the time comes, the spirit knows”: waking again from drunkenness, numinous awareness awakening right there; see Living Zi Hour.
  • Like pulling a puppet by its strings: using numinous awareness to move the mechanism of the breath.
  • “Turning Kui and Gang to move the handle of the Dipper”: turning the Big Dipper, a metaphor for the true intention turning the pivot; see Handle of the Dipper.
  • Ablution at the gate of mao, ablution at the door of you: see ablution.
  • Outer opening, inner opening: Qian and Kun joined together make the outer opening; spirit and breath stored within it make the inner opening.
  • Cavity of the two breaths: the opening formed by the joining of the two breaths of yin and yang.
  • The three secrets of the immortals: fire phasing, the medicinal ingredients, the furnace and tripod; see fire phasing and Medicine.
  • Keeping to the natural, following the natural: not forgetting and not helping.
  • Relying on the breath, leveling the breath: the methods of keeping and following; see regulating the breath.
  • Never use intention: if you know you are using intention, it is not the true intention.
  • The spiritual union across the body, uniting the breaths apart from the bodily form: spirit and breath unite of themselves without uniting.
  • “The man does not loosen his robe, the woman does not untie her sash”: a metaphor for concentrating the spirit and gathering the breath.
  • Joining breath with breath: the essence of regulating the breath — why force it?
  • “The noble placing himself below the humble”, “the man places himself below the woman”: phrases from the Zhun and Xian hexagrams, metaphors for the spirit (noble, man) going down to meet the breath (humble, woman).

Putting It into Practice

  1. “Not forgetting is keeping to the natural, not helping is following the natural” — Lu Qianxu explains this pair of ideas most clearly: one is not letting go, the other is not pushing, and the two are really one thing.
  2. “Never use intention. If you know you are using intention, it is not the true intention” — if in quiet sitting you feel that you are “thinking hard” or “guarding hard”, you have already gone off course. The true intention is born naturally out of stillness.
  3. “Joining breath with breath — why force it?” — like water joining with water, it is natural and spontaneous and needs no forcing.
  4. Bai Ziqing explains the Mysterious Pass one pass at a time, which helps one understand that the Mysterious Pass is not a fixed point on the body but a series of states that appear naturally in the course of quiet sitting.

Translator's Notes

  1. Bai Ziqing, Old Master Ziqing: the Southern Song Daoist Bai Yuchan, whose style name was Ziqing.
  2. The poem “This opening is no ordinary opening”: from Zhang Boduan’s Four Hundred Characters on the Golden Elixir; the original poem reads “Qian and Kun together form it; it is called the cavity of spirit and breath; within it is the essence of Kan and Li”. Bai Ziqing changed it to “yin and yang together form it; it is called the cavity of the two breaths; within it true yang is born” to speak of the “lesser elixir”.
  3. “What the natural does has nothing deviant or false about it”: a line from the Zhouyi cantong qi.
  4. “Stopping and delighting, the man places himself below the woman”: the Judgment commentary on the Xian hexagram of the Book of Changes reads “止而说，男下女，是以亨”; 说 is the same as 悦 (“delight”).
  5. “The man does not loosen his robe, the woman does not untie her sash” and so on: the text says explicitly that “these are all only metaphors for concentrating the spirit and gathering the breath”; man and woman are used as metaphors for spirit and breath, and this has nothing to do with relations between men and women.
