The Jade-Liquid River Chariot, Refining the Self and Reverting the Elixir

Mirror for Compounding the Medicine, section 2 · The jade-liquid River Chariot, refining the self and reverting the elixir — it is nature and life, not spirit and breath; entrusting the Yellow Dame to court the lovely maiden

About This Chapter

This passage has three groups of twelve lines, on the jade-liquid River Chariot, refining the self and reverting the elixir:

  • Water can flow, fire can blaze: these are scenes that can be verified within the body — what the Secret Principles of the Three Carts describes when the jade liquid comes: suddenly it turns into warm breath flowing to the Bubbling Springs, suddenly into hot liquid passing out of the Caudal Funnel.
  • It is nature and life, not spirit and breath: what is meant here is neither the Posterior Heaven cognitive spirit and grain breath nor the original spirit and original breath as usually spoken of, but the more fundamental “root of nature and stalk of life” — the Prior Heaven within the Prior Heaven.
  • The opening of returning to the root, the pass of restoring life: this is the place of embryonic breathing; above it connects with the Muddy Pellet, below it runs through to the Caudal Funnel. But the Daoist Fool stresses that to obtain the true furnace and tripod “it still depends on the moment when the work arrives: when the mechanism moves, it is there; when the mechanism rests, it is not” — it cannot be reached by guessing and speculating.
  • Entrusting the Yellow Dame to court the lovely maiden: the Yellow Dame is the embryonic breathing in the central palace, the lovely maiden the original spirit of the jade-liquid inner elixir; move it lightly, raise it silently, it can be done at any time of the day — this is what the Secret Principles of the Three Carts calls “the jade liquid coming constantly”.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

水能流，火能焰。在身中，自可验。是性命，非神气。水乡铅，只一味。

Water can flow, fire can blaze; within the body, this can be verified for oneself. It is nature and life, not spirit and breath. The lead of the land of water is of one flavour only.

The Daoist Fool's explanationon §1
Original note (Chinese)

水流火焰，在身可验之景。即《三车秘旨》所云玉液来时，忽化热气，流至涌泉，忽化热汤，透出尾闾是也。神气有识神谷气、元神元气之分。识神谷气为后天，元神元气为先天。若性命，则性根元神，命蒂元气，乃先天中先天，为神气之本源，天命之性也，故曰：是性命，非神气。何为水乡？以产自坤宫，乃后天坎位，故曰水乡。何为一味？以铅乃乾中真阳，无他可比，故曰一味。

Water flowing and fire blazing are scenes that can be verified within the body. This is what the Secret Principles of the Three Carts says: when the jade liquid comes, suddenly it turns into warm breath and flows to the Bubbling Springs; suddenly it turns into hot liquid and passes out of the Caudal Funnel. Spirit and breath are divided into “cognitive spirit and grain breath” and “original spirit and original breath”: the cognitive spirit and grain breath are Posterior Heaven, the original spirit and original breath are Prior Heaven. As for nature and life, the root of nature is the original spirit and the stalk of life is the original breath — the Prior Heaven within the Prior Heaven, the source of spirit and breath, the nature that is Heaven’s mandate. That is why it says, “it is nature and life, not spirit and breath”. What is the “land of water”? Because it is produced in the Kun palace, which is the Posterior Heaven position of Kan, it is called the land of water. What is “one flavour”? Because the lead is the true yang within Qian and nothing else can compare with it, it is called one flavour.

§2
Original (Chinese)

归根窍，复命关。贯尾闾，通泥丸。真橐籥，真鼎炉。无中有，有中无。

The opening of returning to the root, the pass of restoring life; it runs through the Caudal Funnel and reaches the Muddy Pellet. The true bellows, the true tripod and furnace; existence within non-existence, non-existence within existence.

The Daoist Fool's explanationon §2
Original note (Chinese)

归根复命，乃神归气复之关窍。《玄机直讲》指为胎息处。合上之泥丸，下之尾闾，皆玉液能贯通之。六层炼心，由丹田分下涌泉一段，全身大路更明。究之要得橐天吁地、乾坤炉鼎之真，还在功夫到时，机动则有机息则无，有无中方见真相，非揣摩拟议可得也。

Returning to the root and restoring life is the pass and opening where the spirit returns and the breath is restored; Zhang Sanfeng’s Straight Talk on the Mysterious Pivot identifies it as the place of embryonic breathing. Together with the Muddy Pellet above and the Caudal Funnel below, all are places the jade liquid can run through. The passage at the sixth level of the Nine Levels of Refining the Mind, “divide it from the Cinnabar Field down to the Bubbling Springs”, makes the great route through the whole body clearer still. But in the end, to obtain the truth of “Heaven as bellows and Earth as pipe”, of Qian and Kun as furnace and tripod, still depends on the moment when the work arrives: when the mechanism moves, it is there; when the mechanism rests, it is not; only within existence and non-existence does one see the true appearance. It cannot be reached by guessing and speculating.

§3
Original (Chinese)

托黄婆，媒姹女。轻轻运，默默举。一日内，十二时。意所到，皆可为。

Entrust the Yellow Dame to court the lovely maiden; move it lightly, raise it silently. Within a single day, in all twelve double hours, wherever the intention reaches, it can be done.

The Daoist Fool's explanationon §3
Original note (Chinese)

姹女是玉液内丹之元神。黄婆之名，丹士皆知，但少有实指为谁者。兹直道破，即中宫胎息也。古仙云：“中宫胎息号黄婆，运转能教骨不枯”。可为明证。轻运默举，意到可为，皆黄婆媒娉之功。故一日内机动籁鸣，有不可遏抑之势。即《三车秘旨》所谓玉液长来也。要炼到骨气俱是金精，肌肤皆成玉质，登峰造极，方算炼己功纯。（以上玉液河车、炼己还丹）

The lovely maiden is the original spirit of the jade-liquid inner elixir. Every alchemist knows the name Yellow Dame, but few have ever said concretely what it is. Here I say it plainly: it is the embryonic breathing of the central palace. An immortal of old said: “The embryonic breathing of the central palace is called the Yellow Dame; its turning can keep the bones from withering.” This is clear proof. Moving it lightly, raising it silently, doing it wherever the intention reaches — all this is the Yellow Dame’s work as matchmaker. So within a single day the mechanism moves and all the pipes of nature sound, with a force that cannot be held back — this is what the Secret Principles of the Three Carts calls “the jade liquid coming constantly”. Only when one has refined until bones and breath have all become golden essence and flesh and skin have all become jade, reaching the very summit, does the work of refining the self count as pure. (The above is the jade-liquid River Chariot, refining the self and reverting the elixir.)

Key Terms

  • Water can flow, fire can blaze: the scenes within the body when the jade liquid comes.
  • It is nature and life, not spirit and breath: the root of nature is the original spirit, the stalk of life is the original breath — the Prior Heaven within the Prior Heaven. See inner nature / life.
  • Cognitive spirit and grain breath, original spirit and original breath: the Posterior Heaven and Prior Heaven spirit and breath. See Original Spirit and cognitive spirit.
  • Lead of the land of water: the true yang within Qian, produced in the Kun palace (the position of Kan). See One Breath of Prior Heaven.
  • The opening of returning to the root, the pass of restoring life: where the spirit returns and the breath is restored; Straight Talk on the Mysterious Pivot identifies it as the place of embryonic breathing. See concentrating Spirit and entering the Cavity of Breath.
  • Bellows (tuoyue 橐籥): a metaphor for the opening and closing of breathing (Daode jing: “Is not the space between Heaven and Earth like a bellows?”).
  • Yellow Dame: the Daoist Fool identifies it directly with the embryonic breathing of the central palace. See True Intention (the Yellow Dame usually stands for the true intention).
  • Lovely maiden: the original spirit of the jade-liquid inner elixir. See Lead and Mercury.
  • “The mechanism moves and all the pipes of nature sound”: the movement of breath starts, like all the sounds of nature ringing out together.
  • The work of refining the self is pure: bones and breath have all become golden essence, flesh and skin have all become jade. See refining the self.

Putting It into Practice

  1. “When the mechanism moves, it is there; when the mechanism rests, it is not” — scenes within the body are there when they come and gone when they go; do not chase them, and do not manufacture them.
  2. “It cannot be reached by guessing and speculating.” Understanding the principles from the alchemical books is enough; real experience has to appear naturally in stillness, not through imagination.
  3. “Move it lightly, raise it silently” — even in advanced work, the use of intention is light and silent.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Heaven as bellows and Earth as pipe”: the original book has 吁 where it should read 籥 (as in tuoyue, “bellows”); the translation follows the corrected reading.
  2. “Yellow Dame”: alchemical books generally use the Yellow Dame as a metaphor for the true intention (see the glossary entry “True Intention”); here the Daoist Fool identifies it directly with the embryonic breathing of the central palace. The two views can be read together — when the true intention rests in the central palace, embryonic breathing naturally becomes continuous and fine.
  3. “The embryonic breathing of the central palace is called the Yellow Dame; its turning can keep the bones from withering”: the words of an immortal of old; the source has not been traced.
  4. The original text of this section has three groups, each of four lines with one passage of explanation.
