General Discussion of the Lesser Celestial Circuit

Sayings selection, section 2 · General discussion of the Lesser Celestial Circuit — from beginning to end, the essentials of the River Chariot, the Conception and Governing vessels with diagram, the six stages of the Dharma Wheel with diagram

Note (translator): This section is about the Lesser Celestial Circuit, which the Wu-Liu school carries out by “blowing in reverse” with the breath and guiding with the intention. Please remember Liu Huayang’s own words in this section: “What moves is not the mind moving; it is the breath moving. Once the mechanism of the breath has stirred, one should respond to it with stillness”; “if one only guards clarity and stillness and does not recognize the moment of movement, or only seizes the moment of movement and does not return to clarity and stillness, neither is the right principle.” Without a natural movement of the breath, do not push the breath around the Celestial Circuit with the mind out of nothing.

About This Chapter

The four short discussions in this section are the Wu-Liu school’s overall account of the Lesser Celestial Circuit:

  • General discussion of the Lesser Celestial Circuit from beginning to end (Liu Huayang): “The Way of the Golden Elixir is entered through stillness and gathered at movement.” Stillness is the substance of the great Way; movement is not the mind moving but the breath moving; when the breath moves, respond to it with stillness; in each movement and each stillness, never missing the moment — this is regulating the medicine, this is union, this is dual cultivation of nature and life. One must understand whether the medicine produced is old or young: if old, the breath scatters and does not rise; if young, the breath is faint and does not rise; wait in stillness until the movement is strong before gathering. Only a depleted body needs long regulation; a youth’s intact body uses only the Greater Celestial Circuit and does not need the Lesser. The circulation “starts at the Xu-Wei cavity and ends at the Xu-Wei cavity”; it must use the breath, but “as if present and as if absent”; three hundred and sixty is only a framework — “not really three hundred and sixty-odd”.
  • On the essentials of the River Chariot of the Lesser Celestial Circuit (Yu Yuwu, Wu Chongxu, Liu Huayang, Patriarch Qiu and others): on inhaling, from below upward; on exhaling, from above downward; when inhaling, the emphasis is wholly on rising; when exhaling, wholly on descending; at the critical moment “one must make the spirit clear and bright and the will bold and vigorous, shutting out the ears and eyes outside and cutting off deliberation inside”; the intention guards the central palace, and the heart of Heaven never leaves its place. Most wonderful is Hunran’s question: the spirit follows the breath up and down, the intention guards the central palace, and the breathing too rises and falls — where, then, is the emphasis? Huayang answers: it is like weaving; at first one cannot attend to hands, feet, head and eyes all at once, but once it becomes familiar, one does not know who is directing it — “it is all governed by the natural mechanism of waxing and waning”.
  • The Conception and Governing vessels, with diagram (Liu Huayang): he criticizes the old view that “the Governing vessel lies outside the spine, and the Conception and Governing vessels end at the upper and lower lips” as “the false indications of common physicians”; when the immortals speak of the Conception and Governing vessels, it is “having personally travelled through the vessels as proof”.
  • The six stages of the Dharma Wheel, with diagram (Liu Huayang): of the wondrous functions of the Way, none surpasses the Dharma Wheel; of mysterious practice, none surpasses breathing; of not straying from the path, none surpasses the true intention. This opening “originally has no form; when the breath stirs, it becomes an opening; when the mechanism rests, it is vague and boundless” — that is why “the Mysterious Pass is hard to describe”.

Original Text and Translation

§1General discussion of the Lesser Celestial Circuit from beginning to end (1)
Original (Chinese)

柳华阳曰：夫金丹之道，从静而入，至动而取。静者大道之体，造化之根。若不静，则识性夹杂，神不灵而气亦不真，于此妄炼，即属后天，与先天虚无之道不相契也。故幻丹走泄而道不成者，皆由未静而夹于识之过也。夫静者静其性也，性能虚静，则尘念不生，真机自动。动者非心动，是气之动也。气机既然发动，则当以静应之。一动一静，不失机缄是谓调药，是谓交媾。行乎造化，性命双熔，即性命双修矣。苟或耑守清静而不识动机，或耑取动机而不复清静，皆非正理也。

Liu Huayang says: The Way of the Golden Elixir is entered through stillness and gathered at movement. Stillness is the substance of the great Way and the root of creation. Without stillness, the cognitive nature mixes in, the spirit is not numinous and the breath is not true either; refining recklessly in this state belongs to the Posterior Heaven and does not accord with the Prior Heaven way of emptiness. So those who produce an illusory elixir, leak, and do not achieve the Way all fail because they have not become still and have let the cognitive spirit mix in. Stillness is stilling one’s nature; when the nature can be empty and still, worldly thoughts do not arise and the true mechanism stirs of itself. What moves is not the mind moving; it is the breath moving. Once the mechanism of the breath has stirred, one should respond to it with stillness. In each movement and each stillness, never missing the moment — this is called regulating the medicine, this is called union. Moving with creation, nature and life smelted together — this is nature and life together, dual cultivation. If one only guards clarity and stillness and does not recognize the moment of movement, or only seizes the moment of movement and does not return to clarity and stillness, neither is the right principle.

§2General discussion of the Lesser Celestial Circuit from beginning to end (2)
Original (Chinese)

次当明其药产老嫩，老则气散不升，嫩则气微不升，务在静候动旺始采，是谓当令，故曰：时至神知。以顺行之时候，即逆行之时候也，故曰：药气驰外，则外别有景。如此调之日久，自有药产景到，然亦特为虚耗之躯言之耳。若壮旺之体，只候药产景到，调其老嫩，以运周天。若调之日久，不运周天，则阳极精满，满而又溢矣。故童真只用大周天，不必用小周天。是诀则一而法则活也。故调药者，元气一动，伺阳之长旺，即当采封运行周天。运而复静，动而复运，循环不已，是谓进退行火，是谓采取周天也。动行不惰，道有何难哉？故曰：丹田直至泥丸顶，自在河车已百遭。

Next one must understand whether the medicine produced is old or young: if old, the breath scatters and does not rise; if young, the breath is faint and does not rise. One must wait in stillness until the movement is strong before gathering; this is called “being in season”, so it is said “when the time comes, the spirit knows”. The time of going along the ordinary course is the time of going in reverse, so it is said “when the breath of the medicine rushes outward, there are other scenes outside”. After regulating like this for a long time, the scenes of the medicine being produced naturally arrive — though this is said specially for depleted bodies. For a strong and vigorous body, one only waits for the scenes of the medicine being produced to arrive, regulates whether it is old or young, and runs the Celestial Circuit; if one regulates for a long time and does not run the Celestial Circuit, yang reaches its extreme and the essence is full, and when full it overflows. That is why a youth’s intact body uses only the Greater Celestial Circuit and does not need the Lesser. The formula is one, but the method is alive. So those who regulate the medicine, as soon as the original breath stirs, wait for the yang to grow strong, and should then gather, seal and run the Celestial Circuit; after running it, return to stillness; when it moves again, run it again, round and round without end — this is called advancing and withdrawing the fire, this is called gathering for the Celestial Circuit. Moving and acting without slackness — what is hard about the Way? So it is said: “From the elixir field straight up to the summit of the Muddy Pellet, the River Chariot has freely gone round a hundred times.”

§3General discussion of the Lesser Celestial Circuit from beginning to end (3)
Original (Chinese)

又云：以虚危穴（注：即会阴穴）起，以虚危穴止。盖虚危穴即任督二脉之交处，立斗柄，运河车，皆由此而起止。故冲虚师曰：起于是，亦止于是。且运必假呼吸，若不以呼吸吹嘘，则神气不能如法。必似有似无，合乎自然相依之运行。盖行以神为之主宰，不见有气之形迹。元气乃无形之行，随元神之运行，听呼吸之催逼，故曰：夹脊尾闾空寄信。而呼吸乃采运元气之法则，逆吹微缓，谓之文火；紧重谓之武火。数息运元气者，为爻为时为度为位。而周天之造化，以此为规模，非真三百六十有余也，故曰每时四揲。所以然者，使其水火不致太过不及也。是范围元气而成度数，为造化之总序耳，故曰：以息数定时数也。或又问：鼎炉道路药物火候。曰：能此虚危任督运用，即鼎炉道路。明此阳动升降，即药物火候，而道即在是也。除此皆非正理，尽属筌蹄惑人矣。借筌蹄获鱼兔，谓筌蹄为鱼兔误也。去筌蹄专鱼兔，朝采暮炼，自然精化气足，丹成景至，再行向上功夫，炼气化神，超凡入圣。

He also says: start at the Xu-Wei cavity (editor’s note: the Huiyin point) and end at the Xu-Wei cavity. For the Xu-Wei cavity is where the Conception and Governing vessels meet; setting up the handle of the Dipper and running the River Chariot both start and end here. That is why Master Chongxu says: “It starts here and it ends here.” Moreover, the circulation must rely on the breath; without blowing with the breath, spirit and breath cannot follow the method. But it must be as if present and as if absent, in accord with the natural mutual reliance of the circulation. For the circulation has the spirit as its director, and no trace of the breath is seen; the original breath travels without form, following the movement of the original spirit and urged on by the breath — so it is said “between the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Caudal Funnel, it only sends word”. Breathing is the rule for gathering and moving the original breath: blowing in reverse, faint and slow, is called the civil fire; tight and heavy, the martial fire. Counting breaths to move the original breath fixes the lines, the hours, the degrees and the positions; the creation of the Celestial Circuit takes this as its framework, but there are not really three hundred and sixty-odd breaths — that is why it says “four counts per hour” (the “hour” here is a point on the route of the Dharma Wheel; at each point the breath takes only four breaths). The reason is to keep water and fire from going too far or falling short. It gives the original breath a boundary and makes it into degrees, as the overall order of creation; so it is said “the number of breaths fixes the number of hours”. Someone further asks: what are the tripod and furnace, the path, the medicinal ingredients and the fire phasing? Answer: being able to use the Xu-Wei cavity and the Conception and Governing vessels like this is the tripod and furnace and the path; understanding this stirring of yang and its rising and falling is the medicinal ingredients and the fire phasing; and the Way lies right here. Everything else is not the right principle; it is all “traps and snares” that mislead people. To use the trap and snare to catch the fish and the hare is right; to take the trap and snare for the fish and the hare is a mistake. Put aside the traps and snares and concentrate on the fish and the hare; gather in the morning and refine in the evening, and naturally the essence will be transformed and the breath will be full, the elixir will form and the scenes will arrive; then go on to the higher work, refining breath into spirit, transcending the ordinary and entering sagehood.

§4On the essentials of the River Chariot of the Lesser Celestial Circuit (1)
Original (Chinese)

俞玉吾曰：其法潜神于内，驭呼吸之往来，上至泥丸，下至命门，使五行颠倒运于其中，此即周天内外机动是也。冲虚曰：以意主中宫，以神驭气，其气至尾闾夹脊，上昆仑，复下丹田，周流运转。觅元子曰：乾坤阖辟阴阳运行之机，一吸则自下而上，子升。一呼则自上而下，午降。此一息之升降也。华阳曰：乾者首也，坤者腹也，阖辟者，乃内外呼吸之玄机。盖外面之气降，里面之气则过我而升。外面之气升，则里面之气过我而降。冲虚曰：当机在吸，则顺吸机而升乾。升不降非全不降，以灭阖辟，乃不重于降，而专重于升。只见升不见降也。当机在呼，则顺呼机而降坤，降不升非全不升，但轻其升若无，而专重于降，只见降不见升也，此乃周天先后二气消息之机也。

Yu Yuwu says: the method is to submerge the spirit within and drive the coming and going of the breath, up to the Muddy Pellet and down to the Gate of Life, making the five phases turn upside down and circulate within — this is the inner and outer working of the Celestial Circuit. Chongxu says: with the intention presiding over the central palace and the spirit driving the breath, the breath reaches the Caudal Funnel and the Spine-Straddling Pass, rises to Kunlun, comes back down to the elixir field, and circulates round and round. Miyuanzi says: the mechanism of the opening and closing of Qian and Kun and the circulation of yin and yang — one inhalation goes from below upward, called “rising at zi”; one exhalation goes from above downward, called “descending at wu”; this is the rising and falling of one breath. Huayang says: Qian is the head, Kun is the belly; opening and closing is the mysterious mechanism of inner and outer breathing. When the outer breath descends, the inner breath passes through me and rises; when the outer breath rises, the inner breath passes through me and descends. Chongxu says: when the moment is in the inhalation, follow the inhaling mechanism and rise to Qian; rising without descending is not not descending at all — without opening and closing it would not work — it is only not emphasizing descent and emphasizing rising wholly; one sees only rising and not descending. When the moment is in the exhalation, follow the exhaling mechanism and descend to Kun; descending without rising is not not rising at all, but treating the rising so lightly that it seems absent and emphasizing descent wholly; one sees only descending and not rising. This is the mechanism of the waxing and waning of the two breaths, Prior and Posterior Heaven, in the Celestial Circuit.

§5On the essentials of the River Chariot of the Lesser Celestial Circuit (2)
Original (Chinese)

华阳曰：大凡临机之时，必须畅明其神，勇猛其志，外除耳目，内绝思虑，一点真神，领气循环，切忌昏迷散乱。稍有他见，气则散于别络，空转无益。邱祖曰：采二气升降之际，若不以意守中宫，药物如何运得转？务宜立定天心之主宰，斗柄外移，而天心不离当处，六时进火，六时退符，而天心亦不以进退而离当处。天心真意也，当处中宫气穴也。浑然问曰：老师言意要主宰中宫，以为斗柄斡运之主，其神要随先天之气升降，又闻后天之气，要在息上升降，如此则三处皆有动静知觉之意，不知其神其意，重在何处？又如何分别用度？丹经曰：行则神气同行，住则神气同住，今如此分别何也？华阳答曰：内外皆主呼机也。云何为机？譬如世人安消息以制物，如若投机，一叩即应，无处不动。今既有先天之气，则我之经络，自能通应，况又有后天之气鼓舞，安有上下中间不应之理乎！又如世人织布然，临机之时，手足头目，上下左右，照顾接送。初学其法甚难，至于熟，则临机而上下左右，照顾接送，亦不知从何而主宰。大道亦然，可见先天后天，上下中间，皆主乎自然消息之机也。总之行火之时，心若不诚，则不灵，或昏迷十二之时，或迷失刻漏之数，或忘沐浴之候，或不知以何数周于天，或周已而犹不止，是皆失于火而药亦消。夫火所以炼药，古云：火药一处居。行火之法，愈久而愈密，愈密而愈精，则火药必成矣。

Huayang says: whenever one is at the critical moment, one must make the spirit clear and bright and the will bold and vigorous, shutting out the ears and eyes outside and cutting off deliberation inside; let one point of the true spirit lead the breath round, and above all avoid dullness, confusion and scattering. If there is the slightest other view, the breath scatters into other channels and turns in vain, to no benefit. Patriarch Qiu says: at the time of gathering the rising and falling of the two breaths, if the intention does not guard the central palace, how can the medicinal ingredients be turned? One must establish the director, the “heart of Heaven”: the handle of the Dipper moves outside, but the heart of Heaven does not leave its place; for six hours one advances the fire, for six hours one withdraws the tally, and the heart of Heaven still does not leave its place because of advancing or withdrawing. The heart of Heaven is the true intention; its place is the central palace, the Cavity of Breath. Hunran asks: Master, you say the intention must preside over the central palace as the director of the turning of the Dipper’s handle; the spirit must follow the Prior Heaven breath up and down; and I have also heard that the Posterior Heaven breath must rise and fall with the breathing — so in all three places there is an intention of movement, stillness and awareness. I do not know where the emphasis of the spirit and the intention lies, nor how to distinguish their use. The alchemical scriptures say: “When moving, spirit and breath move together; when resting, spirit and breath rest together.” Why are they now distinguished like this? Huayang answers: inside and outside, all are governed by the mechanism of breathing. What is a mechanism? It is like people setting up a trigger to catch something: once it is sprung, a single tap and everything responds; there is no place that does not move. Now that there is the Prior Heaven breath, my channels can naturally respond; and with the Posterior Heaven breath stirring them as well, how could the upper, lower and middle fail to respond! It is also like people weaving cloth: at the moment of working, hands and feet, head and eyes, up and down, left and right, all must attend, receive and send on. When first learning, it is very hard; once it becomes familiar, at the moment of working, up and down, left and right attend, receive and send on, and one does not know from where it is directed. The great Way is the same; one can see that Prior Heaven and Posterior Heaven, upper, lower and middle, are all governed by the natural mechanism of waxing and waning. In short, when applying the fire, if the mind is not sincere, it is not numinous: one may be dazed during the twelve hours, or lose count of the water clock, or forget the time for ablution, or not know by what count to complete the circuit, or not stop when the circuit is complete — in all these the fire is lost, and the medicine dissipates too. Fire is what refines the medicine; the ancients said “fire and medicine dwell in one place”. The method of applying the fire becomes closer the longer one practices, and finer the closer it becomes; then fire and medicine will surely be accomplished.

§6The Conception and Governing vessels, with diagram
Original (Chinese)

华阳曰：此图直泄元机，实愿得药之士，不失运行之路。丹道最秘，非余之敢妄泄也。古圣虽无图，却有言存留，奈何不全之过耳。又因旧说督脉在脊骨外，而任督止于上下唇，此二说皆俗医之妄指，岂知仙家说任督，实亲自在脉中行过以为证验。非但行一回也，金丹神气之玄妙，必要在脉中行过数百回方得成就。谬妄不但俗医乱指，今之修玄者亦妄指，愈加纷纷。苟不亲自领会境遇，妄意猜指，浅学信受，误丧厥志，岂不痛哉！故余将师所授之诀，以亲自领会之熟境，绘图以证其非。然而此图一出，游方之士与假道学则无容身之地矣！

Huayang says: this diagram reveals the original mechanism directly; I truly hope that those who obtain the medicine will not lose the path of circulation. The alchemical Way is most secret; it is not that I dare to reveal it recklessly. Although the ancient sages had no diagram, they left words behind — the trouble is only that they are incomplete. Also, the old view holds that the Governing vessel lies outside the spine and that the Conception and Governing vessels end at the upper and lower lips; both these views are the false indications of common physicians. How would they know that when the immortals speak of the Conception and Governing vessels, it is having personally travelled through the vessels as proof? And not only once: the mysterious wonder of the spirit and breath of the Golden Elixir requires travelling through the vessels hundreds of times before it can be accomplished. These absurd views are not only pointed out wrongly by common physicians; those who cultivate the mystery today also point wrongly, and the confusion grows ever greater. If one has not personally understood the experience but guesses and points at random, and shallow learners believe and accept it, they waste their aspiration — how painful! So I have taken the formula my teacher transmitted and, from the familiar experience I have personally understood, drawn a diagram to prove those views wrong. But once this diagram comes out, wandering adventurers and false Daoists will have no place to stand!

(At this point the original book has the “Diagram of the Conception and Governing Vessels”: a seated figure in side view, with the Governing vessel drawn along the spine at the back and the Conception vessel along the front of the body; they meet at the bottom in a circle (the perineum / lower elixir field) and at the top in a circle at the crown (the Muddy Pellet); at the throat, the two tubes of the pharynx and larynx are marked.)

§7The six stages of the Dharma Wheel, with diagram
Original (Chinese)

华阳曰：道之妙用，莫如法轮。玄妙行持，莫如呼吸。运行不蹊，莫如道路。不外道路，莫如真意。消息往来，莫如阖辟。有所起止，莫如界地。斯图也，窍妙全备，阳生在此，调药在此，鼓巽风在此，药产在此，采取在此，归炉在此，驾河车在此，还本复位在此，金丹造化之全功莫不在此。然其穴本无形，气发则成窍，机息则渺茫。其气之行，后通乎督脉，前通乎任脉；中通乎冲脉，横通乎带脉；上通乎心，下通乎阳关；上后通乎肾，上前通乎脐。静则集氤氲而栖真养息，宰化化生生之源；动则引精华而向外发散，每活子时二候之许，其窍旋发旋无，故曰玄关难言也。

Huayang says: of the wondrous functions of the Way, none surpasses the Dharma Wheel; of mysterious practice, none surpasses breathing; of circulating without straying, none surpasses the path; of not departing from the path, none surpasses the true intention; of waxing and waning, coming and going, none surpasses opening and closing; of having a start and an end, none surpasses the boundaries. This diagram is complete in all the wonders of the opening: the birth of yang is here, regulating the medicine is here, stirring the Xun wind is here, the production of the medicine is here, gathering is here, returning to the furnace is here, driving the River Chariot is here, returning to the origin and restoring the position is here; the whole work of the creation of the Golden Elixir is all here. Yet this cavity originally has no form; when the breath stirs, it becomes an opening; when the mechanism rests, it is vague and boundless. Its breath travels: to the back it connects with the Governing vessel, to the front with the Conception vessel; in the middle with the Penetrating Vessel, across with the Belt vessel; upward with the heart, downward with the Yang Pass; up and back with the kidneys, up and forward with the navel. When still, it gathers the dense mingling breath, lodging the true and nourishing the breath, governing the source of all transformation and generation; when moving, it draws the essential splendor outward and scatters it. At every Living Zi Hour, for about two stages, this opening suddenly arises and suddenly vanishes — that is why it is said “the Mysterious Pass is hard to describe”.

(At this point the original book has the “Diagram of the Six Stages of the Dharma Wheel”: an upright oval, with “Heaven” at the top and “Earth” at the bottom, “ablu-” in the middle of the right side and “-tion” in the middle of the left side; the right side is marked “inhale, advance, rise” and “first compass” to “sixth compass”, the left side “exhale, advance, descend” and “first compass” to “sixth compass”; the first to sixth stages are marked on the outside; inside the oval, “closing” is written in the upper half and “opening” in the lower half.)

Key Terms

  • Entered through stillness and gathered at movement: the overall principle of the Lesser Celestial Circuit.
  • The cognitive nature mixing in: not having become still, so that the cognitive spirit mixes in; see cognitive spirit.
  • What moves is not the mind moving but the breath moving: when the mechanism of the breath stirs, respond to it with stillness.
  • Moment (jijian 机缄): the mechanism, the critical point.
  • Nature and life smelted together: see inner nature / life.
  • Whether the medicine produced is old or young: if old, the breath scatters; if young, the breath is faint; wait in stillness until the movement is strong before gathering. See Medicine.
  • Being in season, “when the time comes, the spirit knows”: the right moment to gather the medicine.
  • A youth’s intact body uses only the Greater Celestial Circuit: a youth’s body does not need the Lesser Circuit to repair leaks.
  • Xu-Wei cavity: the perineum, where the Conception and Governing vessels meet, where the River Chariot starts and ends; see perineum.
  • Handle of the Dipper: see Handle of the Dipper.
  • As if present and as if absent: how the breath is used.
  • Blowing in reverse, faint and slow, is the civil fire; tight and heavy, the martial fire: see fire phasing.
  • “Four counts per hour”, “the number of breaths fixes the number of hours”: the “hour” is a point (compass) on the route of the Dharma Wheel, with four breaths per point; silent counting of breaths fixes the degrees; the numbers are only a framework.
  • Traps and snares: tools for catching fish and hares, a metaphor for expedient teachings.
  • Rising at zi, descending at wu: rising on the inhalation, descending on the exhalation.
  • Emphasis wholly on rising, wholly on descending: emphasis on rising when inhaling, on descending when exhaling.
  • The heart of Heaven, its place: the heart of Heaven is the true intention, its place is the central palace, the Cavity of Breath; see True Intention and Central Yellow.
  • The weaving metaphor: once it becomes familiar, one does not know from where it is directed; all is governed by the natural mechanism of waxing and waning.
  • Fire and medicine dwell in one place: fire and medicine are together.
  • The diagram of the Conception and Governing vessels: Huayang’s criticism of the common physicians’ views.
  • Dharma Wheel: a metaphor for the Celestial Circuit.
  • Belt vessel, Yang Pass: the Belt vessel circles the waist; the Yang Pass refers to the genitals.
  • The Mysterious Pass is hard to describe: the opening suddenly arises and suddenly vanishes; see Mysterious Opening / Mysterious Pass.

Putting It into Practice

  1. “Entered through stillness and gathered at movement” — everything begins from stillness. Without stillness, nothing that follows exists.
  2. “What moves is not the mind moving; it is the breath moving. Once the mechanism of the breath has stirred, one should respond to it with stillness.” When something stirs in the body, the mind should not move with it; respond to it with stillness.
  3. “It must be as if present and as if absent, in accord with the natural mutual reliance of the circulation” — even when the breath is used, it is used as if present and as if absent.
  4. The weaving metaphor is worth remembering: at first you have to attend to everything, and it is hard; once it becomes familiar, it happens naturally. Do not get anxious because you cannot manage everything at the start.
  5. The numbers are all frameworks; they are not meant to be really counted. “Not really three hundred and sixty-odd.”

Translator's Notes

  1. 耑 is the same as 专 (“solely”, “wholly”).
  2. “Between the Spine-Straddling Pass and the Caudal Funnel, it only sends word”: meaning that the original breath is formless and travels between the Caudal Funnel and the Spine-Straddling Pass as if merely “sending word”.
  3. “Four counts per hour” (mei shi si she 每时四揲): she is the counting of yarrow stalks in groups of four in ancient divination. The “hour” here is not a clock hour but a point (compass) on the route of the Dharma Wheel; at each point the breath takes four breaths. In the six rising positions each breath is silently counted from one to nine, in the six descending positions from one to six; the Qian count of two hundred and sixteen and the Kun count of one hundred and forty-four together make three hundred and sixty, and since the ablutions at mao and you are not counted, the real number is three hundred. For the complete calculation see Jingxuzi’s explanation in §3 of “On the Counts of Breathing” in the next section.
  4. “以灭阖辟”: 灭 (“extinguish”) probably has the sense of 失 (“lose”) or 无 (“be without”), meaning that the opening and closing cannot be entirely absent; we translate accordingly.
  5. “Patriarch Qiu”: Qiu Chuji (the Perfected Changchun).
  6. In line with the project’s principle, the two diagrams are not reproduced for now; their content is described in words in the text.
