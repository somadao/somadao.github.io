Li Hanxu’s Secret Principles of the Three Carts, Annotated · 第三件河车

The Third River Chariot

The Third River Chariot — moving the essence and breath of the Prior Heaven: meeting the lead with the mercury; the great elixir of golden liquid of the seven returns and nine reversions

Note (translator): The third River Chariot belongs to the advanced stages of the Greater Celestial Circuit and of refining the spirit to return to emptiness. The original text is written almost entirely in veiled language (Qian and Kun, Kan and Li, the infant and the lovely maiden, “first menses”, “heavenly water” and so on). Qiu Ling says plainly that these metaphors “can be said to mean heart and kidneys, or spirit and breath, or even man and woman”; following the pure-and-quiet principle he laid down in his preface to this book, we translate them all as heart and kidneys, spirit and breath within one body. This section is for understanding only.

About This Chapter

Only when refining the self has become thoroughly practiced in the first two carts, and the nature of the mercury has become spiritually responsive, advancing and withdrawing at will, can one undertake “the great work of return” — the third River Chariot, moving the essence and breath of the Prior Heaven, which alchemists call “the mercury meets the lead and lets it in; the emotions come and return to the nature”.

  • The reverted elixir of the seven returns: hide the mercurial nature already formed (the inner elixir) in the empty cavity, above like Qian, below like Kun; first “enter the non-existent from the existent”, then “give birth to the existent from the non-existent”. While entering the non-existent, all is silent and still, the mind dead and the spirit preserved. At this time one’s own cognitive spirit turns into things that frighten or attract, and demon generals of the heavens turn into good or wicked people, to “test” you; do not be moved by any of them, keep the original spirit clear, and after a while a stream of yang breath arises (the return of one yang). I then breathe out the one yin of the Qian palace to meet it — this is called “meeting the lead with the mercury”, “the great conjunction of Kan and Li”. Plucking it and establishing it as the root of the elixir is the reverted elixir of the seven returns.
  • The great elixir of the nine reversions: once the root of the elixir is established, the one yang gradually grows into two yang (Dui) — what alchemists call “first menses”, “heavenly water”, “the moon in the geng quarter”, “the moon over the western river”, “the moth-eyebrow moon”. Gathering this is called “drawing in the emotions and returning them to the nature”. When, after five thousand and forty-eight days, it returns to the Yellow Path, it is like the bright moon on the fifteenth night, metal and water fully round; the essence and breath of the Prior Heaven are surging and at their fullest. Quickly start the great River Chariot and move it up to the Muddy Pellet; a fine liquid drops onto the palate, as big as a sparrow’s egg or a grape, strangely sweet and fragrant — this is the great elixir of golden liquid of the nine reversions.
  • The infant: take this golden liquid, the infant and the lovely maiden meet, nourish it morning and evening, within and without all is bright, and in the middle there is one true thing that looks just like me — this is the infant. Nourish it silently, move it from the Spiritual Valley up to the Valley of Heaven, and then send out the spirit and enter into transformation.

Qiu Ling’s notes mainly “decode” the text: the way of refining the spirit has three passes — refining the spirit to complete the nature (refining the self with the jade liquid), refining the spirit to complete life (refining the body with the golden liquid) and refining the spirit to return to emptiness. He uses the trigrams Qian, Kun, Kan and Li to explain the “seven returns” and “nine reversions”, and explains that “a girl of fourteen whose menses first come”, “five thousand and forty-eight days” and “the full moon on the fifteenth” are all merely metaphors, “only to explain the return from the Posterior Heaven to the Prior Heaven”.

Original Text and Translation

§1
Original (Chinese)

运先天精气，丹家名汞迎铅入，情来归性，七返九还之事也。前此炼己纯熟，汞性通灵，进退自如，雌雄应变。功夫至此，乃可行返还大事。

Moving the essence and breath of the Prior Heaven is what alchemists call “the mercury meets the lead and lets it in; the emotions come and return to the nature” — the business of the seven returns and nine reversions. Earlier, refining the self became thoroughly practiced; the nature of the mercury became spiritually responsive, advancing and withdrawing at will, female and male responding to change. When the work has reached this point, one can undertake the great work of return.

§2
Original (Chinese)

七返还丹者，先将已成之汞性，呼为内丹，于是入室坐圜，把内丹藏于空洞之中，上边如乾，下边如坤，性边属有，命边属无。先要以有入无，然后从无生有。其象如乾精播于坤母，坤乃实腹而为坎。坤精感自乾父，乾乃虚心而为离。乾坤既列，名为鼎器（即有无妙窍也）。离坎二用，借此现形。

As for the reverted elixir of the seven returns: first take the mercurial nature already formed, which is called the “inner elixir”; then enter the chamber and sit in the retreat hut, and hide the inner elixir in the empty cavity — above like Qian, below like Kun; the side of nature belongs to “existence”, the side of life belongs to “non-existence”. First one must enter the non-existent from the existent, and then give birth to the existent from the non-existent. Its image is like the essence of Qian sown in the mother Kun, so that Kun fills its belly and becomes Kan; and the essence of Kun stirred by the father Qian, so that Qian empties its heart and becomes Li. Once Qian and Kun are set out, they are called the “tripod and vessel” (that is, the wondrous opening of existence and non-existence). The two functions of Li and Kan take visible form through it.

§3
Original (Chinese)

原夫以有入无之时也，寂寂静静，心死神存，稍焉，有自己识神，化为惊人爱人之物，试尔内神；又有诸天魔将，化为好人恶人之物，试尔内神。诸般不动，元神湛然，乃更一时焉。

Now, at the time of entering the non-existent from the existent, all is silent and still, the mind dead and the spirit preserved. Before long, one’s own cognitive spirit turns into things that frighten or attract, to test your inner spirit; and demon generals of the heavens turn into good people or wicked people, to test your inner spirit. Be moved by none of them; the original spirit remains clear, and so another while passes.

§4
Original (Chinese)

有一支阳气发生，譬如坤阴之下，一阳来复，我即吐乾宫一阴以迎之（肾气上升，心液下降，本乎自然），名曰以汞迎铅，又曰大坎离交，又曰内外阴阳消息。消息既通，于是摘而取之，立为丹本，此即七返还丹也。

Then a stream of yang breath arises, like the return of one yang beneath the yin of Kun. I at once breathe out the one yin of the Qian palace to meet it (the breath of the kidneys rises, the liquid of the heart descends, according to nature). This is called “meeting the lead with the mercury”, also “the great conjunction of Kan and Li”, also “the waxing and waning of inner and outer yin and yang”. Once the waxing and waning are in communication, pluck it and take it, and establish it as the root of the elixir. This is the reverted elixir of the seven returns.

§5
Original (Chinese)

丹本既立，神气融和，由是一阳渐长而为兑，坎男变为兑女矣（此即庚方月、西江月、蛾眉月诸喻时也）。因此，兑女二字，故丹家名曰首经，又曰天癸（因类而言耳，愚人不知，盲修瞎炼，未遇真师之故也）。丹士采此首经，名曰摄情归性，五千四十八日归黄道之时，有如十五明月，金水圆满，在人身中总一先天精气，腾腾壮盛之时也。

Once the root of the elixir is established, spirit and breath blend in harmony; from then on the one yang gradually grows and becomes Dui, and the Kan male turns into the Dui maiden (this is the time referred to by the various metaphors “the moon in the geng quarter”, “the moon over the western river”, “the moth-eyebrow moon”). Because of the two words “Dui maiden”, alchemists call it “first menses”, also “heavenly water” (this is only speaking by analogy; ignorant people do not understand, and cultivate blindly and practice wildly, because they have not met a true teacher). The alchemist gathers this “first menses”, which is called “drawing in the emotions and returning them to the nature”. When, after five thousand and forty-eight days, it returns to the Yellow Path, it is like the bright moon on the fifteenth night, metal and water fully round — the time when the one unified essence and breath of the Prior Heaven in the body is surging and at its fullest.

§6
Original (Chinese)

学人到此，急起大河车，运上泥丸。稍焉，有美液坠于腭中，大如雀卵葡萄，非麝非蜜，异样甘香，此乃九还金液大丹也。道人服此金液，然后名之曰铅投汞、金并木、后天返先天、婴儿会姹女。婴姹相逢，朝夕涵养，久之洞见脏腑，内外光明，中有一真，宛然似我。此婴姹复生婴儿矣。得此婴儿者，必须默默调养，刻刻温存，由灵谷移上天谷，然后出神入化。

When the student reaches this point, he must quickly start the great River Chariot and move it up to the Muddy Pellet. Before long a fine liquid drops onto the palate, as big as a sparrow’s egg or a grape — not musk, not honey, yet strangely sweet and fragrant. This is the great elixir of golden liquid of the nine reversions. When the Daoist has taken this golden liquid, it is then called “lead cast into mercury”, “metal joined with wood”, “the Posterior Heaven returning to the Prior Heaven”, “the infant meeting the lovely maiden”. When the infant and the lovely maiden meet and are nourished morning and evening, after a long time one can see clearly into the organs, within and without all is bright, and in the middle there is one true thing that looks just like me — this is the infant and the lovely maiden giving birth again to an infant. Whoever obtains this infant must nourish it silently and cherish it moment by moment, move it from the Spiritual Valley up to the Valley of Heaven, and then send out the spirit and enter into transformation.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§6
Original note (Chinese)

第三件河车，即大周天，运先天精气，七返还丹，九还金液大丹，炼神还虚事也。前此第一、二件河车，已完成开关、筑基、得药、炼己等后天次序，达到宝精裕气，对境忘情，元神（汞性）通灵，内丹已成地步，从此进入炼神的先天之道。炼神之道则有三关，即炼神了性（玉液炼己）；炼神了命（金液炼形）；炼神还虚（与太虚同体）。第三件河车重在炼神了命及还虚。

此段多用喻言，如离坎汞铅等，不易理解。此等喻言皆代名词，说心肾亦可，说神气亦可，说男女亦无不可。

何谓七返还丹？七乃火之成数，心属火，降心火于下丹田，养得肾中真气上升，复返于心田，即为七返之功。此可以用《易经》的卦象来说明。乾坤二卦之交而生坎离。乾（☰）坤（☷）交媾时，坤受乾中爻之一阳，变成坎（☵），在丹经中又喻之曰婴儿；乾受坤中爻之一阴，变而成离（☲），在丹经中又喻之曰姹女。坎为水，水性寒；离为火，火性热。坎中之一点真阳，又名乾金，又名水中金，异名真铅。离中之一点纯阴，又名真阴，又名火里汞，异名真汞。乾坤者，阴阳之体也；坎离者，阴阳之用也。真铅真汞，实即真阴真阳。七返还丹实行大坎离交，即先天坎离交，静定中，坎宫一阳来复，离宫一阴相交接，亦取坎填离，以汞迎铅之义，实是真阴真阳相配合。待到九还金液大丹成，离男仍还为纯阳之乾体，坎女仍返为纯阴之坤体，各复返还先天本来面目，此即为返本还元之道，移炉换鼎采大药之法。李涵虚于《道窍谈》中说：“后天坎离者，元神元气交媾而筑丹基，生小药也。先天坎离者，真阴真阳交媾而立丹基（即丹母），生大药也。”

何谓九转还丹？九为金之成数，情属金，摄情归性，去识归真，养得性光圆明，以还先天之性，即为九还之功。坎中一阳逐渐壮盛，古人因兑卦（☱）近居西邻，在八卦中所属人物为少女，故以十四岁女子潮信初来，比九转金液大丹功成定候，喻时为蛾眉月、西江月等，又将兑女喻为首经、天癸、摄情归性，又以五千四十八日喻一部《大藏真经》之数，表示功成之时，再以十五月圆喻人身先天精气之壮盛，不过为了说明后天返回先天，外丹圆满结胎婴。然后再行温养及移神天谷（泥丸）之功。

The third River Chariot is the Greater Celestial Circuit, moving the essence and breath of the Prior Heaven — the reverted elixir of the seven returns and the great elixir of golden liquid of the nine reversions; it is the business of refining the spirit to return to emptiness. The earlier first and second River Chariots have completed the Posterior Heaven sequence of opening the passes, laying the foundation, obtaining the medicine and refining the self, reaching the stage where the essence is treasured and the breath abundant, feelings are forgotten in the face of circumstances, the original spirit (the mercurial nature) is spiritually responsive and the inner elixir is formed. From here one enters the Prior Heaven way of refining the spirit. The way of refining the spirit has three passes: refining the spirit to complete the nature (refining the self with the jade liquid); refining the spirit to complete life (refining the body with the golden liquid); and refining the spirit to return to emptiness (becoming one body with the Great Void). The third River Chariot focuses on refining the spirit to complete life and on returning to emptiness.

This passage uses many metaphors, such as Li and Kan, mercury and lead, which are not easy to understand. All these metaphors are code words; they can be said to mean heart and kidneys, or spirit and breath, or even man and woman.

What is the reverted elixir of the seven returns? Seven is the completing number of fire, and the heart belongs to fire. Lowering the fire of the heart into the lower Cinnabar Field, nourishing the true breath in the kidneys so that it rises and returns again to the field of the heart — this is the work of the seven returns. It can be explained with the trigrams of the Book of Changes. The two trigrams Qian and Kun intersect and give birth to Kan and Li. When Qian (☰) and Kun (☷) unite, Kun receives the one yang of Qian’s middle line and becomes Kan (☵), which the alchemical scriptures also call the “infant”; Qian receives the one yin of Kun’s middle line and becomes Li (☲), which the alchemical scriptures also call the “lovely maiden”. Kan is water, cold by nature; Li is fire, hot by nature. The point of true yang within Kan is also called “Qian metal”, also “metal within water”, and by another name “true lead”. The point of pure yin within Li is also called “true yin”, also “mercury within fire”, and by another name “true mercury”. Qian and Kun are the substance of yin and yang; Kan and Li are the function of yin and yang. True lead and true mercury are in fact true yin and true yang. The reverted elixir of the seven returns carries out the great conjunction of Kan and Li, that is, the Prior Heaven conjunction of Kan and Li: in still concentration, one yang returns in the Kan palace, and the one yin of the Li palace joins with it — which is also the meaning of “taking from Kan to fill Li” and “meeting the lead with the mercury”; in reality it is the pairing of true yin and true yang. When the great elixir of golden liquid of the nine reversions is accomplished, the Li male is restored to the pure yang body of Qian and the Kan female returns to the pure yin body of Kun; each returns to its original Prior Heaven face. This is the way of returning to the root and reverting to the origin, and the method of moving the furnace, changing the tripod and gathering the Great Medicine. Li Hanxu says in Talks on the Openings of the Dao: “The Posterior Heaven Kan and Li are the union of the original spirit and the original breath, which lays the foundation of the elixir and gives birth to the lesser medicine. The Prior Heaven Kan and Li are the union of true yin and true yang, which establishes the foundation of the elixir (the mother of the elixir) and gives birth to the Great Medicine.”

What is the reverted elixir of the nine turnings? Nine is the completing number of metal, and the emotions belong to metal. Drawing in the emotions and returning them to the nature, leaving cognition behind and returning to the true, nourishing the light of the nature until it is round and bright, so as to restore the nature of the Prior Heaven — this is the work of the nine reversions. As the one yang within Kan gradually grows strong — because the trigram Dui (☱) lies close to the west and, among the figures assigned to the eight trigrams, stands for the youngest daughter — the ancients compared the moment when the great elixir of golden liquid of the nine turnings is accomplished to a girl of fourteen whose menses first come, and used the moth-eyebrow moon, the moon over the western river and so on as metaphors for the timing. They also likened the “Dui maiden” to “first menses”, “heavenly water” and “drawing in the emotions and returning them to the nature”; they used five thousand and forty-eight days as a metaphor for the number of scrolls in the Buddhist Canon, to indicate the time of accomplishment; and they used the full moon on the fifteenth as a metaphor for the fullness of the Prior Heaven essence and breath in the body — all only to explain the return from the Posterior Heaven to the Prior Heaven, and the outer elixir becoming complete and forming the embryo. After that one does the work of warm nourishing and moving the spirit to the Valley of Heaven (the Muddy Pellet).

Key Terms

  • “The mercury meets the lead and lets it in; the emotions come and return to the nature”: meeting life (lead) with nature (mercury). See Lead and Mercury and inner nature / life.
  • Seven returns and nine reversions: seven is the completing number of fire (the heart), nine the completing number of metal (the emotions). See Golden Flower.
  • Entering the chamber and sitting in the retreat hut: going into a quiet room for a closed retreat of sitting meditation.
  • Qian and Kun, Kan and Li: the substance of yin and yang and the function of yin and yang. See Qian and Kun and conjunction of Kan and Li.
  • Tripod and vessel, the wondrous opening of existence and non-existence: the place where Qian and Kun are set out.
  • The cognitive spirit turning into things, demon generals testing the spirit: the various illusions that appear in concentration. See realm of yin / realm of the aggregates.
  • Meeting the lead with the mercury, the great conjunction of Kan and Li, the waxing and waning of inner and outer yin and yang: the Prior Heaven conjunction of Kan and Li, the pairing of true yin and true yang.
  • Root of the elixir, mother of the elixir: the basis on which the foundation of the elixir is established.
  • Dui; the moon in the geng quarter, the moon over the western river, the moth-eyebrow moon: images of one yang growing into two, metaphors for the timing.
  • “First menses”, “heavenly water”: metaphors in this section (Li Hanxu: “only speaking by analogy”); in the pure-and-quiet reading, the moment when the Prior Heaven essence and breath in the body first become full.
  • Drawing in the emotions and returning them to the nature: the work of the nine reversions.
  • Five thousand and forty-eight days: a metaphor taken from the number of scrolls in the Buddhist Canon, indicating accomplishment.
  • Yellow Path: see the glossary.
  • The infant and the lovely maiden: the true yang within Kan and the true yin within Li. See Lead and Mercury.
  • Spiritual Valley, Valley of Heaven: the lower Cinnabar Field and the Muddy Pellet.
  • The three passes of refining the spirit: refining the spirit to complete the nature, to complete life and to return to emptiness. See the three transmutations.

Putting It into Practice

  1. This section is for understanding only. The one thing that can be borrowed directly is §3: when, in stillness, all kinds of scenes appear — frightening, attractive, good people, wicked people — “be moved by none of them; the original spirit remains clear”: do not be frightened, do not be attracted, do not chase them.
  2. Qiu Ling’s way of “decoding” is worth remembering: words in the alchemical books such as man and woman, the infant and the lovely maiden, “first menses” and so on are all code words. When reading an alchemical book, first find out what the word refers to within one body; do not take the words at face value. The original text itself says: “This is only speaking by analogy; ignorant people do not understand, and cultivate blindly and practice wildly, because they have not met a true teacher.”

Translator's Notes

  1. “Retreat hut” (huan 圜): read as 环; in Daoist usage, the small hut in which one sits in closed retreat.
  2. “First menses”, “heavenly water”: in some alchemical books (especially those of the “grafting” tradition) these terms have a literal reference. Li Hanxu himself notes here that he is “only speaking by analogy”, and Qiu Ling explains them, according to the pure-and-quiet method, as the Prior Heaven essence and breath within the body. We translate according to Qiu Ling’s principle and make no other conjectures.
  3. “Five thousand and forty-eight days”: the Kaiyuan Catalogue of Buddhist Teachings (Kaiyuan shijiao lu 开元释教录) lists 5,048 scrolls of Buddhist scriptures; the alchemists borrowed the number.
  4. “A girl of fourteen whose menses first come”: this is Qiu Ling’s explanation of where the ancient metaphor comes from (Dui stands for the youngest daughter); it only explains the origin of the metaphor.
  5. The original text is one passage; we have divided it into six short sections.
