Li Hanxu’s Secret Principles of the Three Carts, Annotated · 第一件河车

The First River Chariot

The First River Chariot — the work of moving the breath: the return of one yang in the obscure; advancing the yang fire at the zi hour

Safety note (translator): This section speaks of moving the “newly awakened mind” past the lower Magpie Bridge (the perineum), keeping it at the Caudal Funnel, and the true breath passing up the lumbar spine to rise to the Muddy Pellet. Qiu Ling quotes Talks on the Openings of the Dao, which says it clearly: the River Chariot “is not a method of imagining and transporting”, and “without using force to open them, the passes open by themselves”. Please do not use the mind to push the breath up along the spine; if there is no natural movement of breath, just do the work of stillness. The original phrases “one may only lead the breath into the throat … send it into the windpipe” are the ancients’ way of speaking; on no account take them to mean swallowing saliva or anything else into the windpipe.

About This Chapter

First comes the overview of the three carts: the first moves the breath (the zi–wu Lesser Celestial Circuit), the second moves the essence (the jade-liquid River Chariot), the third moves essence and breath together (the Greater Celestial Circuit); all three carts are directed by the true spirit and the true intention.

The order of the first River Chariot is very clear:

  1. It begins with nurturing the true breath in emptiness: gather the mind and regulate the breath, close the eyes and keep the spirit, stillness upon stillness, let everything go, undifferentiated and obscure — like winter, when all things return to their roots, or night, when everyone’s mind comes to rest.
  2. The return of one yang: just when one is without knowledge or awareness, something in the Cinnabar Field seems to give a start — like smoke and yet not smoke, like breath and yet not breath — rising from the lower Cinnabar Field to the pit of the heart, and one is like someone just waking from a dream. This is called the “Living Zi Hour”.
  3. Advancing the yang fire at the zi hour: abruptly turn this “newly awakened mind” around, move it past the lower Magpie Bridge (the perineum) and keep it steadily at the Caudal Funnel; the true breath then passes up from the Caudal Funnel through the lumbar spine, rises to the Jade Pillow and bores into the Muddy Pellet.
  4. Turning into liquid and laying the foundation: at the Mysterious Breast the breath turns into sweet dew, which washes the palace of the heart and falls back into the opening of emptiness; treasuring it and letting it grow abundant is laying the foundation. After a long time something bursts out of the Cinnabar Field; move it along the route of the first cart, it turns into liquid, and one obtains the “jade-liquid elixir head”.

Qiu Ling’s notes have three main points: this passage is the work of “establishing life through nature”; the so-called “true formula sworn never to be transmitted” is turning the newly awakened mind (the true intention) around and guiding the breath past the perineum; and most important of all, he quotes Talks on the Openings of the Dao — the River Chariot is not imagining and transporting; it is “following it as it moves and leading it; without using force to open them, the passes open by themselves”.

Original Text and Translation

§1Overview of the three carts
Original (Chinese)

三车者、三件河车也。第一件运气，即小周天子午运火也。第二件运精，即玉液河车，运水温养也。第三件精气兼运，即大周天运先天金汞，七返还丹，九还大丹也。此三车者，皆以真神真意斡乎其中。人能知三车秘谛，则精气神三品圆全，天地人三仙成就。

The three carts are three River Chariots. The first moves the breath — that is, the Lesser Celestial Circuit, moving the fire by zi and wu. The second moves the essence — that is, the jade-liquid River Chariot, moving the water and nourishing warmly. The third moves essence and breath together — that is, the Greater Celestial Circuit, moving the golden mercury of the Prior Heaven: the reverted elixir of the seven returns and the great elixir of the nine reversions. In all three carts it is the true spirit and the true intention that direct the turning. If one knows the secret truth of the three carts, the three grades of essence, breath and spirit will be complete, and one will become the three immortals of Heaven, Earth and Humanity.

§2
Original (Chinese)

运气功夫，所以开关筑基，得药结丹也。其中次叙，从虚空中涵养真息为始。收心调息，闭目存神，静之又静，清而又清，一切放下，全体皆忘，混混沌沌，杳杳冥冥。功夫到此，如天之有冬，万物芸芸，各返其根。如日之有夜，亥漏沉沉，各息其心。

The work of moving the breath is for opening the passes and laying the foundation, obtaining the medicine and forming the elixir. Its order begins with nurturing the true breath in emptiness. Gather the mind and regulate the breath, close the eyes and keep the spirit, stillness upon stillness, clarity upon clarity; let everything go, forget the whole body, undifferentiated and chaotic, dim and obscure. When the work reaches this point, it is like winter in the year, when all the teeming things return each to its root; like night in the day, when the water clock drips heavily at the hai hour and everyone’s mind comes to rest.

§3
Original (Chinese)

此无知无识时也，谁晓得无知无识之际，才有一阳来复，恰如冬之生春，夜之向曙，蓦地一惊，无烟似有烟，无气似有气，由下丹田熏至心阙，使人如梦初醒。初醒之候，名曰活子时，急起第一河车，采此运行，迟则无形之气变为有形。此气也，名壬铅，名后天，又名阳火。故曰子时进阳火。

This is the time of no knowledge and no awareness. Who would know that it is precisely at this moment of no knowledge and no awareness that one yang returns — just as winter gives birth to spring and night turns towards dawn? Suddenly there is a start: no smoke, yet as if there were smoke; no breath, yet as if there were breath, rising from the lower Cinnabar Field to the pit of the heart, so that one is like someone just waking from a dream. This moment of first waking is called the “Living Zi Hour”. Quickly start the first River Chariot, gather it and set it moving; if you are late, the formless breath turns into something with form. This breath is called the ren lead, called the Posterior Heaven, and also called the yang fire. Hence the saying, “advance the yang fire at the zi hour”.

§4
Original (Chinese)

何为进阳火？学人把初醒之心，陡地拨转，移过下鹊桥，即天罡前一位，誓愿不传之真诀也。此心名曰天地之心，又名妙心，又名元神，又名真意，又名玄关发见。移至尾闾守而不乱，霎时间真气温温，从尾闾骨尖两孔中，透过腰脊，升至玉枕，钻入泥丸。古仙云：“夹脊双关透顶门，修行路径此为尊。”即指此也。

What is advancing the yang fire? The student abruptly turns this newly awakened mind around and moves it past the lower Magpie Bridge — that is, “one position before Tiangang”, the true formula sworn never to be transmitted. This mind is called the heart of Heaven and Earth, also the wondrous mind, also the original spirit, also the True Intention, also “the manifestation of the Mysterious Pass”. Move it to the Caudal Funnel and keep it there without confusion; in an instant the true breath grows warm and, from the two holes at the tip of the tailbone, passes through the lumbar spine, rises to the Jade Pillow and bores into the Muddy Pellet. An immortal of old said: “Through the twin passes of the spine to the crown of the head — of all the paths of cultivation this is the most honoured.” That is what it refers to.

§5
Original (Chinese)

愚人不知运气，便要舌舐上腭，以承甘露，吁！可笑亦可怜也。皆不得师之过也。须知运气一道，只可引气入喉。《黄庭经》曰：“服食玄气以长生。”因此阳火之气紫黑色，名曰玄气耳。服食之法，须要口诀，乃能送入气管。否则，走入食喉，从何处立得丹基？须把这阳气送下气喉，至于玄膺，乃化为甘露之水。《黄庭》曰：“玄膺气管受精符”。此之谓也。玄膺名玄雝，又名玄壅，言人之气到此壅塞也。

Ignorant people do not know how to move the breath; at once they want to lick the upper palate with the tongue to receive the sweet dew — alas, it is both laughable and pitiful! It is all the fault of not having a teacher. You must know that in the way of moving the breath one may only lead “breath” into the throat. The Yellow Court Scripture says: “Take the mysterious breath as food and live long.” Because the breath of this yang fire is purplish black, it is called the “mysterious breath”. The method of taking it requires an oral formula; only then can it be sent into the windpipe. Otherwise it goes into the gullet — and where then would you establish the foundation of the elixir? You must send this yang breath down the windpipe to the Mysterious Breast; only there does it turn into the water of sweet dew. The Yellow Court says, “The Mysterious Breast and the windpipe receive the talisman of essence” — that is what it means. The Mysterious Breast is also called xuanyong 玄雝 or xuanyong 玄壅, meaning that a person’s breath becomes blocked here.

§6
Original (Chinese)

俗人不知玄妙，气至泥丸，就想他化为神水，如吞茶汤一般，吾恐气管一滴，便叫汝咳而不休矣。盖水者有形之物，安能入得气管？故《黄庭》曰：“出清入玄二气焕，子若遇之升天汉。”犹言清气出于丹田，玄气入于玄膺，二气转换云尔。气化为水，洒濯心宫，仍落于虚无窍内，宝之裕之，是为筑基。

Ordinary people do not know the mystery: as soon as the breath reaches the Muddy Pellet they want it to turn into divine water and swallow it like tea — I fear that a single drop in the windpipe would set you coughing without end! For water is a thing with form; how could it enter the windpipe? That is why the Yellow Court says: “The clear goes out and the mysterious comes in, the two breaths shining; if you meet them, you will rise to the Milky Way.” That is to say, the clear breath goes out of the Cinnabar Field and the mysterious breath comes into the Mysterious Breast, the two breaths changing into each other. The breath turns into water, washes the palace of the heart and falls back into the opening of emptiness; treasuring it and letting it grow abundant is laying the foundation.

§7
Original (Chinese)

筑基既久，积累益深，乃有一个时候，照常静坐，忽于丹田中突出一物，有声如风雷之响，有色如星电之光，是为后天中先天药，即按第一车运之，至于泥丸，始化为液，饵而服之，方得玉液丹头，此得药结丹之始也。以后功夫，须要绵绵不绝，固蒂深根，乃尽养丹之妙。请看下文分解。

When the foundation has been laid for a long time and the accumulation grows deeper, there comes a time when, sitting quietly as usual, suddenly something bursts out of the Cinnabar Field, with a sound like wind and thunder and a colour like the light of stars and lightning. This is “the medicine of the Prior Heaven within the Posterior Heaven”. Move it along the route of the first cart; at the Muddy Pellet it begins to turn into liquid; take it and swallow it, and only then do you obtain the “jade-liquid elixir head”. This is the beginning of obtaining the medicine and forming the elixir. The work after that must be continuous and unbroken, making the stalk firm and the root deep, so as to realise fully the wonder of nourishing the elixir. Please see the explanation below.

Qiu Ling's noteon §1–§7
Original note (Chinese)

第一件河车，即子午小周天，运气功夫也。下手请参阅本文后面《收心法下手功夫》一节及前载《后天串述文终经》和《九层炼心循途录》前五层，其义相同。

此段功夫乃以性立命之功。收心调息，使心神气息皆入杳冥之中，此性功也；杳冥中有气，惟神独觉，是为真息，阴精非真息不能化，阳气非杳冥不能生，故有一阳来复，此命功也。

无知无识之际，才有一阳来复，有热气从下丹田上熏心府，名为活子时，又叫展窍，亦即玄关现象。这是气功态中从无到有、从量变到质变的关键时刻。本人如梦初醒，此时外阳兴起，急须采取运行，起第一河车。所谓誓愿不传之真诀者，即把初醒之心陡地拨转，引导阳气过下鹊桥，即天罡前一位。初醒之心即真意，天罡前一位、天地之心、妙心、元神，亦均指真意。下鹊桥在会阴穴。用真意导气过会阴，由尾闾升夹脊，到玉枕，入泥丸，过上鹊桥（舌），降下气管，入心宫，落于虚无窍（下丹田）内，宝之裕之，到此方算筑基，以后才有得药之境，而至结丹。

李涵虚《道窍谈》云：“河车者，得药运行之要旨，非存想搬运之法，乃子午进退、阴阳阖辟、内外升降，天地自然之火候也。”他又说：“惜后人不得真传，多落存想搬运，空空往来，有何益哉？”这就是说，当“神息相依，风火交合，忽然而灵芽吐萌，气机生动”，起河车以炼之，这是丹田气满，有物质基础当行之事；而存想搬运，空空往来，乃无物质基础不当行之事，二者有本质之不同。这正是《悟真篇》所说：“咽津纳气是人行，有药方能造化生，鼎内若无真种子，犹将水火煮空铛”。

《道窍谈》又认为，得药运引河车，“妙在意守于内，神驭于外”，“抑知神守内庭，只贵凝而不贵运，运则必用乎意也；周天之妙，外运逸而内掌劳，故内掌必以意当之”。意者指真神、真意。意筹其内，而神自运于外，实二仍是一。伍冲虚曰，“有两相知之微意”，即指此也。

《道窍谈》又云：“中年学道者，只要凝神有法，调息有度，阴跷气萌，摄入鼎（下丹田）内，忽忘忽助，后天气生，再调再烹，真机自动，乘其动而引，不必着力开而关自开，不必着力展而窍自展。真气一升于泥丸，于是河车之路可通。要皆自然而然，乘乍动而又静之际，微微起火，逼过尾闾，逆流天谷（泥丸），自然炼精化气，灌注三宫”。此段为开关妙法，全在待其机动而引，不必着力，自然而然。

服食神水之口诀，在本文第二河车一节内，即舌倒舐上腭云云一段，学者可参阅。玄膺穴在舌下。

The first River Chariot is the zi–wu Lesser Celestial Circuit, the work of moving the breath. For how to begin, please refer to the section “The method of beginning by gathering the mind” at the end of this text, and to the Classic of Ending through a Serial Account of the Posterior Heaven and the first five levels of the Record of Following the Path through the Nine Levels of Refining the Mind given earlier; the meaning is the same.

The work in this passage is the work of “establishing life through nature”. Gathering the mind and regulating the breath so that mind, spirit and breath all enter the obscure — this is the work on nature. Within the obscure there is breath, of which the spirit alone is aware: this is the true breath. Yin essence cannot be transformed without the true breath, and yang breath cannot be born without the obscure; hence the return of one yang — this is the work on life.

At the moment of no knowledge and no awareness, one yang returns: a warm breath rises from the lower Cinnabar Field to the heart. This is called the Living Zi Hour, also “unfolding the opening”, which is the phenomenon of the Mysterious Pass. It is the critical moment in the qigong state when something comes from nothing and quantitative change becomes qualitative change. One is like someone just waking from a dream; at this moment the outer yang rises, and one must quickly gather and move it, starting the first River Chariot. The “true formula sworn never to be transmitted” is to turn the newly awakened mind around abruptly and guide the yang breath past the lower Magpie Bridge, that is, “one position before Tiangang”. The newly awakened mind is the true intention; “one position before Tiangang”, “the heart of Heaven and Earth”, “the wondrous mind” and “the original spirit” all refer to the true intention as well. The lower Magpie Bridge is at the huiyin point. Use the true intention to guide the breath past the perineum, up from the Caudal Funnel to the Spine-Straddling Pass, to the Jade Pillow and into the Muddy Pellet; past the upper Magpie Bridge (the tongue), down the windpipe, into the palace of the heart, and falling into the opening of emptiness (the lower Cinnabar Field). Treasure it and let it grow abundant — only at this point does it count as laying the foundation; only after that is there the state of obtaining the medicine, leading on to forming the elixir.

Li Hanxu’s Talks on the Openings of the Dao says: “The River Chariot is the key to moving the medicine once it has been obtained. It is not a method of imagining and transporting; it is the advancing and withdrawing of zi and wu, the opening and closing of yin and yang, the rising and falling of inner and outer — the natural fire phasing of Heaven and Earth.” He also says: “Unfortunately later people did not receive the true transmission and mostly fell into imagining and transporting, coming and going in emptiness — what good is that?” That is to say, when “spirit and breath rely on each other, wind and fire unite, and suddenly the spiritual sprout puts forth a bud and the movement of breath comes alive”, one starts the River Chariot to refine it; this is what should be done when the Cinnabar Field is full of breath and there is a material basis. Imagining and transporting, coming and going in emptiness, is what should not be done when there is no material basis; the two are fundamentally different. This is exactly what the Wuzhen pian says: “Swallowing saliva and taking in breath are human doings; only with the medicine can creation give birth. If there is no true seed in the tripod, it is like boiling an empty pot with water and fire.”

Talks on the Openings of the Dao also holds that, in moving the River Chariot once the medicine has been obtained, “the wonder lies in the intention guarding within and the spirit driving without”, and that “the spirit guarding the inner court values only congealing, not moving; moving must use the intention. The wonder of the circuit is that the outer moving is at ease while the inner control is laborious, so the inner control must be undertaken by the intention.” “Intention” means the true spirit, the true intention. The intention plans within and the spirit moves of itself without; in reality they are two and yet one. Wu Chongxu’s “a subtle intention by which the two know each other” refers to this.

Talks on the Openings of the Dao also says: “Those who study the Way in middle age need only concentrate the spirit with method and regulate the breath with measure. When the breath of the yinqiao sprouts, draw it into the tripod (the lower Cinnabar Field); neither forgetting nor helping, the Posterior Heaven breath is born. Regulate it again and cook it again, and the true mechanism moves of itself. Follow it as it moves and lead it: without using force to open them, the passes open by themselves; without using force to unfold it, the opening unfolds by itself. Once the true breath has risen to the Muddy Pellet, the route of the River Chariot can be opened. It is all spontaneous: at the moment when it has just moved and is still again, raise the fire very slightly, press it past the Caudal Funnel and against the current to the Valley of Heaven (the Muddy Pellet); naturally essence is refined into breath and pours into the three palaces.” This passage is the wonderful method of opening the passes; it lies entirely in waiting for the mechanism to move before leading it, without using force, naturally.

The oral formula for taking the divine water is in the section on the second River Chariot of this text — the passage beginning “turn the tongue back to lick the upper palate”; students may refer to it. The Mysterious Breast point is under the tongue.

Key Terms

  • Three carts: the three River Chariots, that is, three stages of work; there is only one route. See Celestial Circuit.
  • The three immortals of Heaven, Earth and Humanity: the human immortal, the earthly immortal and the heavenly immortal.
  • True breath, the obscure: see True Breath and the dim and obscure.
  • “The water clock drips heavily at the hai hour”: late at night, the sound of the water clock is heavy — a metaphor for complete silence.
  • Living Zi Hour: the return of one yang at the moment of no knowledge and no awareness, like waking from a dream.
  • Ren lead, yang fire: other names for this newly born breath.
  • Advancing the yang fire at the zi hour: see fire phasing.
  • Newly awakened mind, heart of Heaven and Earth, wondrous mind, “one position before Tiangang”, manifestation of the Mysterious Pass: Qiu Ling says they all refer to the True Intention.
  • Lower Magpie Bridge: Qiu Ling places it at the huiyin point; upper Magpie Bridge: the tongue. See Magpie Bridge.
  • The two holes at the tip of the tailbone: openings at the lower end of the sacrum.
  • Mysterious breath: the breath of the yang fire; the Yellow Court Scripture: “Take the mysterious breath as food and live long.”
  • Mysterious Breast: under the tongue; also called xuanyong.
  • “The clear goes out and the mysterious comes in”: the clear breath goes out of the Cinnabar Field and the mysterious breath comes into the Mysterious Breast; the two breaths change into each other.
  • Medicine of the Prior Heaven within the Posterior Heaven, jade-liquid elixir head: what bursts out of the Cinnabar Field after the foundation is laid; it turns into liquid and is swallowed.
  • Establishing life through nature: the work on nature (entering the obscure) gives rise to the work on life (the return of one yang).
  • Imagining and transporting: using imagination to push the breath around the circuit; Talks on the Openings of the Dao rejects it explicitly.
  • “The intention guarding within, the spirit driving without”: the true intention plans within and the spirit moves of itself without.
  • “A subtle intention by which the two know each other”: Wu Chongxu’s words; see the notes to the Concise Essentials of Great Accomplishment in the first book.

Putting It into Practice

  1. “It begins with nurturing the true breath in emptiness” — most of the work of the first River Chariot is in fact stillness: let everything go, undifferentiated and chaotic, dim and obscure.
  2. Do not imagine and transport. This is the most important point of this section: “If there is no true seed in the tripod, it is like boiling an empty pot with water and fire.” If there is no real movement of breath, do not imagine the breath travelling around the circuit.
  3. “Follow it as it moves and lead it; do not use force.” Even when a natural warm current appears, simply follow it lightly, without pushing or pulling.
  4. Do not rush to lick the upper palate to “receive the sweet dew”. Li Hanxu criticises this as putting the cart before the horse. Resting the tongue lightly against the palate is fine, but there is no need to do it deliberately in order to wait for saliva.

Translator's Notes

  1. The original phrases “one may only lead breath into the throat”, “send it into the windpipe” and “a single drop in the windpipe would set you coughing without end”: here Li Hanxu is stressing the difference between “breath” and “water” — what rises into the throat is formless breath, which only turns into saliva at the Mysterious Breast; if one tried to send something with form, such as water, into the windpipe, one would choke. This is the ancients’ description of inner sensations, not the “windpipe” of anatomy; on no account attempt anything with swallowing on the basis of it.
  2. “One position before Tiangang”: Tiangang is the handle of the Northern Dipper, which alchemists use as a metaphor for the true intention; what exactly “one position before” means has been explained in various ways, and Qiu Ling takes it to mean the true intention as well.
  3. “The outer yang rises”: see the entry “outer kidney” in the Concise Essentials of Great Accomplishment in the first book; in men’s alchemy this is the bodily signal of the Living Zi Hour.
  4. Xuanyong 玄雝 and xuanyong 玄壅: 雝 is read as 壅, “to block”.
  5. “Swallowing saliva and taking in breath are human doings … it is like boiling an empty pot with water and fire”: lines from Zhang Boduan’s Wuzhen pian.
  6. In the original, the first River Chariot is one long passage; we have divided it into short sections. Qiu Ling’s notes originally came at the end of the passage; we have placed them after the whole of the original text.
