A Brief Account of the Essentials of the Schools of the Way of the Elixir

A brief account of the essentials of the schools of the Way of the Elixir — dual cultivation of nature and life, the Southern and Northern lineages, the schools and their transmissions, the oral formulas must be taught by a teacher

About This Chapter

Original Text and Translation

§1Dual cultivation of nature and life is the first principle
Original (Chinese)

道家丹道一门，世称丹鼎派，亦即神仙家者是。其修证法，无论何宗何派，总以炼养阴阳，返朴还淳，性命双修，性命双了，为第一要义。《吕祖指玄篇》有云：“伏羲传道至于今，穷理尽性至于命。”又曰：“修性不修命，此为修行第一病。只修祖性不修丹，万劫阴灵难入圣。”即可证性命二者，不可单修其一而舍弃其一。命功旨在形神俱妙而同化，性功旨在超凡入圣而登真。双修双了，要在燮理阴阳，参天地而同造化也。儒家以“天人合一”为最高境界，道家对“天人合一”与“天人同化”之功，确有其玄义无穷之修证方法。如道德上之修炼，心性上之修炼，与乎生理上、精气神之炼化，自初步入手以至最后了手，工程次第，步步上修，井然不紊；而其步步妙化，如人饮水，冷暖自知，不到境地，难以与言也。

The alchemical school of the Daoists is known in the world as the “tripod-and-cauldron school”, that is, the school of the immortals. Its methods of cultivation and realization, whatever the lineage or school, all take as their first principle refining and nourishing yin and yang, returning to simplicity and purity, the dual cultivation of nature and life and the dual completion of nature and life. Patriarch Lü’s Pointing to the Mystery says: “From Fuxi’s transmission of the Way until now: exhaust the principles, fulfill the nature, and so arrive at life.” It also says: “To cultivate the nature without cultivating life — this is the first sickness of practice. To cultivate only the ancestral nature without cultivating the elixir, a yin spirit will not enter sagehood in ten thousand kalpas.” This proves that of nature and life, one cannot cultivate only one and abandon the other. The work on life aims at body and spirit both becoming wondrous and transformed together; the work on the nature aims at transcending the ordinary, entering sagehood and ascending to perfection. Cultivating both and completing both lies in harmonizing yin and yang, joining Heaven and Earth and sharing in their creation. The Confucians take “the unity of Heaven and man” as the highest state; the Daoists truly have methods of cultivation and realization, of endless mysterious meaning, for the work of “the unity of Heaven and man” and “the co-transformation of Heaven and man”. Such as cultivation in morality, cultivation in mind and nature, and the refining and transformation of the physiological essence, breath and spirit — from the first steps to the final completion, the stages of the work rise step by step in good order; and each step’s wondrous transformation is like drinking water, where only the drinker knows whether it is cold or warm; until one reaches that state, it is hard to speak of it.

§2One part of work, one part of results
Original (Chinese)

夫阴阳炼养，自可得阴阳炼养之变化，与阴阳炼养之成就；性命双修，自可得性命双修之变化，与性命双修之成就。如栽培花木然，有一分培养，即有一分效验；多一分功夫，即多一分成就；世未有只有耕耘而无收获者也。丹家“九转还丹”之事，全是功夫次第；有一步功夫，即有一步效验可见，有一步境界可证。过来人，自知全非虚语！

Refining and nourishing yin and yang naturally brings the transformations and the achievements of refining and nourishing yin and yang; the dual cultivation of nature and life naturally brings the transformations and the achievements of the dual cultivation of nature and life. It is like growing flowers and trees: one part of cultivation brings one part of results; one more part of work brings one more part of achievement; there has never been anyone in the world who ploughed and weeded and reaped no harvest. The alchemists’ matter of the “reverted elixir of the nine cycles” is entirely a sequence of work; each step of work has a step of results to be seen and a step of states to be verified. Those who have been through it know that none of this is empty talk!

§3Beginning with exhausting the principles
Original (Chinese)

由穷理尽性至命一语，可证无论性功命功，均应从穷理始。由穷理中尽性，由穷理中至命，方可彻达上乘。否则只能做个小乘道。穷理属于“明道”边事，属于“悟道”边事，也就是“道学问”边事。故古真谓：“无超人学问，即无从直透人天境地。”终日面壁枯坐，暗中探索，何能证“入圣登真”之果。尽性属于圣功边事，属于心性边事，也就是属于“尊德性”边事。及乎至命，则是属于成道边事，属于证道边事，也就是属于神化边事。到这里便是性命双成，性命双了矣。这是道门中各宗各派的一个共同宗旨。

From the saying “exhaust the principles, fulfill the nature, and so arrive at life”, one can see that whether in the work on the nature or the work on life, one should begin with exhausting the principles. Only by fulfilling the nature through exhausting the principles, and arriving at life through exhausting the principles, can one reach the highest vehicle; otherwise one can only practice a lesser-vehicle Way. Exhausting the principles belongs to the side of “understanding the Way”, the side of “awakening to the Way”, that is, the side of “following the path of inquiry and learning”. So the perfected of old said: “Without learning beyond the ordinary, there is no way to pierce straight through to the realm of Heaven and man.” Sitting all day like a log facing a wall, groping in the dark — how could one realize the fruit of “entering sagehood and ascending to perfection”? Fulfilling the nature belongs to the side of the sage’s work, the side of mind and nature, that is, the side of “honoring the virtuous nature”. As for arriving at life, it belongs to the side of accomplishing the Way, the side of realizing the Way, that is, the side of spiritual transformation. On reaching this point, nature and life are both accomplished, nature and life are both completed. This is an aim shared by all the lineages and schools of the Daoist gate.

§4The three-origin elixir methods; Wenshi and Shaoyang; the Southern and Northern lineages
Original (Chinese)

道家修真之法，总不外三元丹法，即天元、地元、人元是也。天元神丹，百世不一遇。地元灵丹，点化黄白，世罕真传。人元大丹，性命双修，阴阳锻炼，超凡入圣，莫过于此。前二者为外金丹法，后者为内金丹法。丹经中亦有以修清静者为天元丹法，修服食者为地元丹法，修阴阳者为人元丹法。要以前述之分法为普遍。人元丹内，以文始派最高，以少阳派最大。文始派直修虚无大道，以无为法而兼举有为法，顿超直入，了性而自了命者也。少阳派则以阴阳大道为本修，自有为法而上修无为法，积功渐晋，了命而性亦因得了也。文始派曲高和寡，修者日稀。少阳派则平易近人，尽人可修，尽人可证，故得大行于世。其最著者为南北两派，其后又有东派、西派、中派、青城派、崆峒派、三丰派、五柳派，要皆可以南北两宗范畴之。余则不足道矣，且亦皆可并入两宗之内也。

The Daoist methods of cultivating perfection all come down to the three-origin elixir methods: the heavenly origin, the earthly origin and the human origin. The divine elixir of the heavenly origin is not met once in a hundred generations. The numinous elixir of the earthly origin, which transmutes into gold and silver, rarely has a true transmission in the world. The great elixir of the human origin — dual cultivation of nature and life, tempering yin and yang, transcending the ordinary and entering sagehood — nothing surpasses it. The first two are methods of the outer Golden Elixir; the last is the method of the inner Golden Elixir. Some alchemical scriptures also call the cultivation of clarity and stillness the heavenly-origin method, the cultivation of ingested substances the earthly-origin method, and the cultivation of yin and yang the human-origin method; but the division given above is the more common one. Within the human-origin elixir, the Wenshi school is the highest and the Shaoyang school the largest. The Wenshi school cultivates the great Way of emptiness directly, taking the method of non-action while also employing the method of action, transcending suddenly and entering directly; completing the nature, it completes life of itself. The Shaoyang school takes the great Way of yin and yang as its basic cultivation, rising from the method of action to cultivate the method of non-action, accumulating merit and advancing gradually; completing life, the nature too is thereby completed. The Wenshi school is too lofty to find many followers, and those who cultivate it grow fewer by the day. The Shaoyang school is easy and accessible; anyone can cultivate it, anyone can realize it, and so it has spread widely in the world. Its best known branches are the Southern and Northern schools; after them came the Eastern school, the Western school, the Central school, the Qingcheng school, the Kongtong school, the Sanfeng school and the Wu-Liu school, all of which can be comprised within the scope of the Southern and Northern lineages. The rest are not worth mentioning, and they too can all be merged into the two lineages.

§5General lineage chart
Original (Chinese)

大道渊源于黄帝，而集大成于老子。一传尹文始，即关尹子。尹传麻衣（即李名和），李传陈希夷，陈传火龙真人，是为文始一派。一传王少阳，开全真教一派。王传钟离权，钟传吕纯阳。吕祖首传王重阳，重阳传邱长春，开北宗，又称龙门派。吕祖嗣又传刘海蟾，海蟾传张紫阳，开南宗，是为紫阳一派（按海蟾亦曾以丹法传希夷，递传至张三丰，开一新脉。至若晋之抱朴子葛洪，梁之陶隐居宏景，均抱高世之学，怀王佐之才，对于丹道学术，多所述著，传世不绝，唯非此脉脉相传承内者也）。

The great Way originates with the Yellow Emperor and was brought to completion by Laozi. One line of transmission went to Yin Wenshi, that is, Guanyinzi. Yin transmitted to Mayi (that is, Li He), Li transmitted to Chen Xiyi, and Chen transmitted to the Perfected Huolong — this is the Wenshi school. Another line went to Wang Shaoyang, who opened the Quanzhen teaching. Wang transmitted to Zhongli Quan, and Zhongli to Lü Chunyang. Patriarch Lü first transmitted to Wang Chongyang, and Chongyang transmitted to Qiu Changchun, opening the Northern lineage, also called the Longmen school. Patriarch Lü later also transmitted to Liu Haichan, and Haichan transmitted to Zhang Ziyang, opening the Southern lineage — this is the Ziyang school (note: Haichan also transmitted the alchemical method to Xiyi, and it was passed on down to Zhang Sanfeng, opening a new line. As for Ge Hong, Baopuzi of the Jin, and Tao Hongjing, the Recluse Tao of the Liang, both held learning above their age and the talent to assist kings, and wrote much on the alchemical Way that has been handed down without break; but they are not within this line of transmission from master to disciple).

§6The Seven Perfected of the North
Original (Chinese)

世常称北七真者，即邱长春处机，刘长生处传，谭长真处端，马丹阳处钰，郝广宁太古，王玉阳处一，孙清静不二（丹阳之妻）。七真又称七祖，皆重阳弟子。七祖中广开法派，奠定北宗之基者为长春真人。此派上采易理，旁涉禅宗，而一归之于道。微妙玄通，广大悉备，要在以清静单修为主旨。北宗始祖，则均尊称重阳真人。此派经典之最著者，除七真之著述语录外，即为金盖山刊行之《古书隐楼藏书》。

Those commonly called the Seven Perfected of the North are Qiu Changchun (Chuji), Liu Changsheng (Chuxuan), Tan Changzhen (Chuduan), Ma Danyang (Yu), Hao Guangning (Datong), Wang Yuyang (Chuyi) and Sun Qingjing (Bu’er, Danyang’s wife). The Seven Perfected are also called the Seven Patriarchs; all were disciples of Chongyang. Among the Seven Patriarchs, the one who widely opened lineages and laid the foundation of the Northern lineage was the Perfected Changchun. This school draws above on the principles of the Changes and alongside on Chan, and unifies them all in the Way; subtle, wondrous, mysterious and penetrating, vast and complete, its essence is to take solitary pure and quiet cultivation as its main aim. As the founding patriarch of the Northern lineage, all honor the Perfected Chongyang. Among the scriptures of this school, the best known, besides the writings and sayings of the Seven Perfected, are the Collected Books of the Hidden Tower of Ancient Writings, printed at Mount Jingai.

§7The Five Patriarchs of the South; the Seven Perfected of the South
Original (Chinese)

世常称南五祖者，系由张紫阳开其法派，张传石杏林，石传薛道光，薛传陈泥丸，陈传白玉蟾。总是南方人，是称南五祖。张祖又传刘永年，玉蟾又传彭鹤林，均为不世之杰出人物，故又合为南七真。此派修为，以阴阳双修，亦即男女双修为主，故其丹法重在命功，回阳换骨，采铅接命，太乙归真，片晌工夫可成。中老年人破体之身，修此较宜。又北七真中，马真究为阴阳所成，故另开一支，马传宋德芳，宋传李双玉，李传张紫阳，张传赵友钦（缘督子），赵传陈观吾（上阳子）。至南宗始祖，则均尊海蟾真人刘操。大张法门则始于紫阳真人。此派以张紫阳之《悟真篇》为圣经。而《白玉蟾全集》，亦名重道林。白著全近禅理，尤切清修，为世所尊。

Those commonly called the Five Patriarchs of the South are the line opened by Zhang Ziyang: Zhang transmitted to Shi Xinglin, Shi to Xue Daoguang, Xue to Chen Niwan, and Chen to Bai Yuchan. All were southerners, so they are called the Five Patriarchs of the South. Patriarch Zhang also transmitted to Liu Yongnian, and Yuchan to Peng Helin, both outstanding figures rare in any age, so together they are called the Seven Perfected of the South. The cultivation of this school takes dual cultivation of yin and yang, that is, dual cultivation of man and woman, as its main thing; so its alchemical method emphasizes the work on life — restoring yang and changing the bones, gathering lead to prolong life, the Great One returning to the true — which can be accomplished in a short time. For middle-aged and older people whose bodies are already broken, this is more suitable. Also, among the Seven Perfected of the North, the Perfected Ma was in the end accomplished through yin and yang, and so opened a separate branch: Ma transmitted to Song Defang, Song to Li Shuangyu, Li to Zhang Ziyang, Zhang to Zhao Youqin (Yuanduzi), and Zhao to Chen Guanwu (Shangyangzi). As the founding patriarch of the Southern lineage, all honor the Perfected Haichan, Liu Cao; the great expansion of its teaching began with the Perfected Ziyang. This school takes Zhang Ziyang’s Wuzhen pian as its sacred scripture. And the Complete Works of Bai Yuchan is also renowned among Daoists. Bai’s writings are wholly close to Chan principles and especially suited to pure cultivation, and are honored in the world.

§8The Eastern school
Original (Chinese)

东派则为陆潜虚所创始，亦称为吕祖亲传。著有《方壶外史丛编》，传其法乳。嗣后傅真铨又大张门庭，并编著有证道秘书十七种广行于世。纯主阴阳双修丹法，要可属于南宗。综考东派道脉与双修诀法，深有过于密宗无上瑜伽双身修法之欲乐大定，实为上乘大法。唯后世借附其说，下者流于房术采战，亦深深可惜也。

The Eastern school was founded by Lu Qianxu and is also said to have been transmitted by Patriarch Lü in person. He wrote the Collected Works of the Outer History of Fanghu, handing down its teaching. Later Fu Jinquan greatly expanded the school and compiled the Seventeen Secret Books for Realizing the Dao, which circulated widely. It advocates purely the alchemical method of dual cultivation of yin and yang and can be assigned to the Southern lineage. Examining the Eastern school’s lineage and its formulas of dual cultivation as a whole, they go deeper than the blissful great concentration of the dual-body practice of supreme yoga in Tantric Buddhism; it is truly a supreme great method. But later generations attached themselves to its teachings, and the lower ones fell into the bedchamber arts of “battle-gathering” — a great pity.

§9The Western school
Original (Chinese)

西派则为李涵虚所开，亦称为吕祖直传。曾著有《道窍谈》、《三车秘旨》、《太上十三经注释》等行于世。虽亦为阴阳二品大丹，然较之东派，则又略有逊焉，以其丹法较为严繁也。惟仍可统属于南宗。其所分全在用鼎成丹之工程法，及其接命之术，略有异耳。

The Western school was opened by Li Hanxu and is also said to have been transmitted directly by Patriarch Lü. He wrote Talks on the Openings of the Dao, the Secret Principles of the Three Carts, the Annotations on the Thirteen Scriptures of the Most High and others, which circulate in the world. Although it too is the great elixir of the two grades of yin and yang, compared with the Eastern school it falls slightly short, because its alchemical method is stricter and more elaborate. It can still be comprised within the Southern lineage. The differences lie entirely in the method of using the tripod to form the elixir and in its art of prolonging life, which differ slightly.

§10The Sanfeng school
Original (Chinese)

其次，世又有三丰派丹法，为张三丰所开，盛行于世。此又称丹家之新派，以其介于文始与少阳之间也。由其《玄谭全集》与《大道指要》等可见其要旨，系主清静阴阳双修者。坊间流行之《张三丰全集》，复渗杂另一张三峰之房中法要入之，致失其本来面目，唯明眼人，则一见可别真伪。

Next, there is also in the world the alchemical method of the Sanfeng school, opened by Zhang Sanfeng, which is widely practiced. It is also called the new school of the alchemists, because it lies between Wenshi and Shaoyang. Its essentials can be seen from his Complete Mysterious Talks and Essential Pointers to the Great Way; it advocates both the pure and quiet and the dual cultivation of yin and yang. The Complete Works of Zhang Sanfeng circulating in bookshops has had the essentials of the bedchamber methods of another Zhang Sanfeng (三峰) mixed into it, so that it has lost its original face; but a discerning eye can tell the true from the false at a glance.

§11The Central school (with the Kongtong school)
Original (Chinese)

至若世称中派者，则为尹真人师弟一派，有《性命圭旨》一书为其修持准绳，主三教合参。次如李道纯、黄元吉，亦为此派翘楚，此二巨匠，类多以儒说道（毅一子杨毅廷亦然），彻入精微，而不落窠臼也。惟究其实际，亦可归入北派之清静丹法。崆峒派丹法亦如之。

As for what is called the Central school, it is the school of the disciples of the Perfected Yin, with the book Pointers on Nature and Life (Xingming guizhi) as its standard of practice, advocating the combined study of the three teachings. Next, Li Daochun and Huang Yuanji are also outstanding figures of this school; these two great masters mostly speak of the Way in Confucian terms (as does Yang Yiting, Yiyizi), penetrating into the subtle without falling into stereotypes. But looking at its reality, it too can be assigned to the pure and quiet alchemical method of the Northern school. The alchemical method of the Kongtong school is the same.

§12The Wu-Liu school
Original (Chinese)

复有伍柳派，系明万历时伍冲虚、柳华阳师弟所创。理论以道佛为基，方法以修气修脉，锻炼精神为主。虽非上乘丹法，浅近而指点明白，故世之奉修者极众。伍之《天仙正理》与《仙佛合宗》，柳之《金仙证论》与《慧命经》等书，几与北派经典，同等畅行。二人均系由禅转道者，实则二门均未得究竟。其法且极繁琐，防走丹一事，上夹下塞，尤为奇谈。惟以其纯主清静修为，故尚不失正途，其丹法则可列入北派之范畴。

Then there is the Wu-Liu school, founded in the Wanli reign of the Ming by the master and disciple Wu Chongxu and Liu Huayang. Its theory is based on Daoism and Buddhism, and its method consists mainly of cultivating the breath and the vessels and tempering essence and spirit. Though not the highest alchemical method, it is plain and clear in its instructions, and so those in the world who follow it are extremely numerous. Wu’s The Correct Principles of Celestial Immortality and The Union of Immortals and Buddhas, and Liu’s Treatise on the Realization of Golden Immortality and Scripture of Wisdom and Life, circulate almost as widely as the scriptures of the Northern school. Both men turned from Chan to the Way, but in fact neither reached the ultimate in either gate. Their method is moreover extremely cumbersome; in guarding against the elixir running off, “clamping above and blocking below” is especially strange. But because it advocates purely pure and quiet cultivation, it has not lost the right path, and its alchemical method can be listed within the scope of the Northern school.

§13The Qingcheng school (with the Jiangxi and Huxiang schools)
Original (Chinese)

次如青城派，则系以南北双修为主，与三丰派近似，惟更得有文始派之精髓。创始者为青城丈人，继起者为李八百等。丹法系先北修以奠其基，而后再南参以宏其功，双证以张其用。旨奥而诀幽，法简而功速。惟门法甚严，向不轻传。方内散人之江西派，衡岳真人之湖湘派可入之。青城派有《青城秘录》、《大道玄指》等秘笈，江西派有《通一斋道书》四种，湖湘派有《崆峒微传》、《文始秘要》等经籍，均系以南北两宗双修同运为主者。

Next, the Qingcheng school takes dual Southern and Northern cultivation as its main thing, similar to the Sanfeng school, but it has also obtained the marrow of the Wenshi school. Its founder was the Elder of Qingcheng, and its successors include Li Babai and others. Its alchemical method first cultivates in the Northern way to lay the foundation, then studies the Southern way to expand the work, and with both realized, extends their use. Its purport is profound and its formulas hidden; its method is simple and its work quick. But its rules are very strict, and it has never been transmitted lightly. The Jiangxi school of the Recluse Fangnei and the Huxiang school of the Perfected Hengyue can be included in it. The Qingcheng school has secret books such as the Secret Record of Qingcheng and Mysterious Pointers to the Great Way; the Jiangxi school has the Four Daoist Books of the Studio of Penetrating the One; the Huxiang school has scriptures such as Subtle Transmissions of Kongtong and Secret Essentials of Wenshi; all take the combined operation of the dual cultivation of the Southern and Northern lineages as their main thing.

§14The Three Peaks school
Original (Chinese)

至若三峰派，乃朴阳子张三峰所创，属东派旁枝，世人常将其与张三丰混淆，实则非一人。其丹法则纯以“泥水丹法”为主，事“阴阳采战”，即世所谓左道旁门者是也。惟“正人行邪道，邪道亦归正；邪人行正道，正道亦归邪”。故余常谓：左道，道也；旁门，门也。只要能心正意诚，并不止于入门，而复能升堂入室，则又未始不可一述以存之也。

As for the Three Peaks school, it was founded by Zhang Sanfeng (三峰), Puyangzi, and is a side branch of the Eastern school; people often confuse him with Zhang Sanfeng (三丰), but in fact they are not the same person. Its alchemical method relies purely on the “mud-and-water alchemical method” and practices “yin-yang battle-gathering” — what the world calls a heterodox side door. Yet “when a righteous person practices a heterodox way, the heterodox way too returns to the right; when a heterodox person practices the right way, the right way too returns to the heterodox”. So I often say: the heterodox way is still a way; the side door is still a door. As long as one’s mind is upright and one’s intention sincere, and one does not stop at entering the door but can go on to ascend the hall and enter the inner chamber, then it is not without reason to give an account of it here to preserve it.

§15The Qing masters and the Liu and Luo schools of recent times
Original (Chinese)

迄乎清代，道学鼎盛。如刘悟元、朱云阳，出入佛老，栖心禅寂，尽群经微旨，拔迷归宗；彻诸家指要，循流达源，故又均能以学术鸣于世。朱之《参同契阐幽》与《悟真篇阐幽》二著，会心别出直彻真源。刘之《道书十二种》，显露玄微，博大精深；其《修真辨难参证》一书，尤可视为丹道指南。二真享誉极盛，历久不衰。迨黄元吉出，则又综罗儒道，会归一致；独标正鹄，体用不遗，有如揭灯去暗，拨云见日者。其《乐育堂语录》与《老子道德经精义》二书，说理谈经，无不彻达上乘。极高明而道中庸，极博大而尽精微，实当之无愧。惟以其修炼授受，概主清静，故亦可列入北宗中也。近世四川尚有刘门，为刘止唐所创，又有罗门，为罗春浦所传，均为盛极一时者。

In the Qing dynasty, Daoist learning flourished. Liu Wuyuan and Zhu Yunyang, for example, moved between Buddhism and Laozi, lodging their minds in the stillness of Chan, exhausting the subtle purport of all the scriptures and leading the lost back to the source; penetrating the essentials of all the schools and tracing the streams back to their source, so both became renowned in the world for their learning. Zhu’s two works, Elucidation of the Hidden in the Cantong qi and Elucidation of the Hidden in the Wuzhen pian, are original in their understanding and penetrate straight to the true source. Liu’s Twelve Daoist Books reveals the mysterious and subtle and is broad and profound; his Discussions and Difficulties in Cultivating Perfection in particular can be regarded as a guide to the alchemical Way. The reputation of these two perfected ones was very great and has lasted long. When Huang Yuanji appeared, he brought together Confucianism and Daoism and unified them; he set up the right target alone, neglecting neither substance nor function, like lifting a lamp to dispel darkness or parting the clouds to see the sun. His two books, Sayings from the Hall of Joyful Nurture and The Essential Meaning of Laozi’s Daode jing, explain principles and expound scripture, all reaching the highest vehicle. “Reaching the utmost height and brightness while following the path of the mean, reaching the utmost breadth while exhausting the subtle” — he truly deserves it. But because his cultivation and transmission were wholly pure and quiet, he too can be listed in the Northern lineage. In recent times in Sichuan there were also the Liu school, founded by Liu Zhitang, and the Luo school, transmitted by Luo Chunpu, both of which flourished greatly for a time.

§16Conclusion: the oral formulas must be taught by a teacher
Original (Chinese)

总之，丹道中千门百派，诀法实难尽述，口诀尤不易得全。然无论其言乾坤鼎器，坎离男女，日月魂魄，庚甲虎龙，水银朱砂，白金黑锡，药物斤两，四象五行，以及种种喻词与妙用，若不明真铅真汞（真阴真阳）之妙用，火候法度之进退，采取追摄之真机，温养生化之指归，无益也。故曰：“丹经万卷，重在心传；天机口诀，常须师授。”其次，若“南宫”与“正一”及其他各门派，以其非以参玄修丹为专务者，故不具论矣！

In sum, the alchemical Way has a thousand gates and a hundred schools; their formulas and methods can hardly all be described, and the oral formulas are especially hard to obtain complete. But whatever they speak of — the tripod vessels of Qian and Kun, Kan and Li as man and woman, the sun and moon as hun and po souls, geng and jia as tiger and dragon, quicksilver and cinnabar, white gold and black lead, the weights of the medicinal ingredients, the four images and five phases, and all kinds of metaphors and wondrous functions — if one does not understand the wondrous function of true lead and true mercury (true yin and true yang), the advance and withdrawal of the rules of fire phasing, the true moment of gathering and pursuing, and the aim of warm nourishing and generation, it is useless. That is why it is said: “Ten thousand scrolls of alchemical scriptures — what matters is transmission from heart to heart; the oral formulas of Heaven’s mechanism must always be taught by a teacher.” Further, schools such as “Nangong” and “Zhengyi” and others, which do not make investigating the mystery and cultivating the elixir their special task, are not discussed here!

Key Terms

  • Tripod-and-cauldron school, school of the immortals: the branch of Daoism devoted to the alchemical Way.
  • Dual cultivation and dual completion of nature and life: the common aim of all the schools; see inner nature / life.
  • Exhausting the principles, fulfilling the nature, arriving at life: from the Book of Changes, “Discussion of the Trigrams”, “exhaust the principles, fulfill the nature, and so arrive at life”; Xiao takes exhausting the principles as understanding the Way, fulfilling the nature as the sage’s work, and arriving at life as accomplishing the Way.
  • Three-origin elixir methods: the divine elixir of the heavenly origin, the numinous elixir of the earthly origin (outer alchemy), the great elixir of the human origin (inner alchemy).
  • Wenshi school, Shaoyang school: the highest and the largest schools within the human-elixir method.
  • Southern and Northern lineages: the Northern lineage (Quanzhen, Longmen) advocates solitary pure and quiet cultivation; the Southern lineage (Ziyang school), according to Xiao, mainly dual cultivation of yin and yang. See Southern and Northern Schools.
  • Seven Perfected of the North, Five Patriarchs of the South, Seven Perfected of the South: see paragraphs 6 and 7.
  • Eastern, Western, Central, Sanfeng, Wu-Liu and Qingcheng schools: see paragraphs 8–13.
  • Mud-and-water alchemical method, yin-yang battle-gathering: the side-door arts condemned by Xiao.
  • True lead and true mercury: true yin and true yang; see Lead and Mercury.
  • Reverted elixir of the nine cycles: a general name for the sequence of the alchemists’ work; see Golden Flower.

Putting It into Practice

  1. Neither nature nor life may be neglected. “To cultivate the nature without cultivating life — this is the first sickness of practice”; cultivating only life without the nature is equally incomplete.
  2. Begin with exhausting the principles. First understand the principles clearly, then put in the work; “sitting all day like a log facing a wall, groping in the dark — how could one realize the fruit of entering sagehood and ascending to perfection?”
  3. One part of work, one part of results. Do not be impatient, and do not doubt.
  4. However many metaphors there are, without understanding true lead and true mercury, fire phasing, gathering and warm nourishing, it is all useless.
  5. The oral formulas must be taught by a teacher. Books can only point the way; they cannot replace a reliable transmission.

Translator's Notes

  1. “Xiao Tianshi” (肖天石): so in the original (肖 is a popular form of 萧); this is the Taiwanese scholar Xiao Tianshi 萧天石 (1909–1986).
  2. §4, “Wu-Liu school” (五柳派): so in the original; it is the Wu-Liu school (伍柳派).
  3. §5, “Li Minghe” (李名和): so in the original. The Daoist Mayi is said by some to have been named Li He; the translation writes “Li He”.
  4. §6, “Liu Changsheng (Chuchuan)” (刘长生处传): so in the original. Liu Changsheng’s name is usually given as Chuxuan 处玄; the translation follows “Chuxuan”.
  5. §8, “Fu Zhenquan” (傅真铨): so in the original. The compiler of the Seventeen Secret Books for Realizing the Dao was Fu Jinquan 傅金铨 of the Qing; the translation follows “Fu Jinquan”.
  6. §7, “the Complete Works of Bai Yuchan is also renowned among Daoists” (ming zhong daolin 名重道林): meaning that it enjoys a great reputation in the Daoist world.
  7. §7, “the Perfected Ma was in the end accomplished through yin and yang, and so opened a separate branch”, “Li to Zhang Ziyang”: this is Xiao’s account; whether this “Zhang Ziyang” is the same person as Zhang Ziyang, founder of the Southern lineage, the original does not say, and we record it as it stands.
  8. The judgments on the schools in this text (such as the Wu-Liu school “though not the highest alchemical method”, “neither reached the ultimate in either gate”, and the Eastern school “truly a supreme great method”) are all Xiao’s own opinions; when including the text, Qiu Ling said “Mr. Xiao’s views may serve as a reference”. The methods in all the parts of this book are understood by Qiu Ling according to the pure and quiet method.
  9. The original is a single continuous discussion without sections; the paragraph headings are ours.
