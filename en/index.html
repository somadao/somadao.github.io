Immortality, Grounded

Classics of Daoist inner alchemy, translated so that they can be understood, read aloud and put into practice.

Every chapter gives the classical Chinese original, a passage-by-passage translation, the commentators’ notes, explanations of difficult terms, and practical pointers. The Chinese edition has narration recorded by the author; English narration is in preparation. The first two books, Qiu Ling’s Selected Ancient Chinese Qigong Texts and Essentials for Entering Alchemical Practice, are now complete in English.
